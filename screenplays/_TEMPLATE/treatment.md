# Treatment

## Story promise

What dramatic experience is this project trying to deliver? Name the pressure, not the theme slogan.

## Beginning

Describe the initial situation, protagonist objective, disrupting event, and the decision that commits the story.

## Development

Track major reversals, discoveries, costs, alliances, failures, and changes in strategy. Prefer causality over a list of scenes.

## Ending

Describe the final sequence, decisive choice, consequence, and resulting world state.

## Open problems

Keep unresolved structural questions here rather than pretending the treatment is settled.

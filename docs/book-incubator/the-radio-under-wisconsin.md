# The Radio Under Wisconsin

Working subtitle: **Project Sanguine, Extremely Low Frequency, and the Attempt to Turn Bedrock Into an Antenna**

A normal radio antenna can fit on a tower. An antenna for extremely low frequency communication wants a continent.

Project Sanguine emerged from the Navy's need to signal ballistic-missile submarines while they remained deeply submerged. Very low frequencies penetrate seawater better than ordinary radio, but the wavelengths become enormous and the usable bandwidth collapses. The resulting engineering logic pushed the transmitter outward into geography itself: long conductors, buried or grounded systems, carefully chosen rock, immense power, and messages so slow they functioned more like a bell than a conversation.

The most ambitious Sanguine concepts would have occupied a vast portion of Wisconsin with a grid-like antenna system. Political resistance, environmental concern, cost, and changing technical requirements repeatedly shrank the idea through later variants. A much smaller Project ELF system was eventually built in Wisconsin and Michigan and operated for years before the Navy shut it down in 2004.

The book is about a communications system whose components include geology. Bedrock conductivity matters. Seawater matters. Planetary scale matters. The Earth is not scenery around the machine; it is part of the circuit. That is an unusually fertile way into submarine command-and-control, nuclear deterrence, environmental politics, and the odd asymmetry of one-way communication to hidden machines.

A possible spine: why submarines disappear electromagnetically; seawater as shield; wavelength and antenna size; early ELF experiments; Project Sanguine; Wisconsin geology; the proposed continental grid; public opposition; biological-effects controversy; Seafarer; Austere ELF; the smaller system that finally got built; coded low-bandwidth messages; strategic command and control; the impossibility of easy submarine replies; protest and sabotage; improved alternatives; shutdown; and what it means to build infrastructure at a scale where citizens live inside the antenna.

Evidence boundary: Sanguine, its mission, contractors, proposed scale, and later ELF descendants are documented. Claims about health effects need careful treatment by study quality and period knowledge rather than activism or Navy reassurance alone.

# The Air That Becomes a Circuit

Working subtitle: **Plasma Aerodynamics, Magnetohydrodynamics, and the Dream of Controlling Hypersonic Flow With Fields**

At sufficiently high temperature, air stops behaving only like a neutral gas. Ionization creates charged species. Once the flow conducts, electric and magnetic fields can exchange momentum and energy with it. The atmosphere becomes, partially and inconveniently, an electrical working fluid.

DIA commissioned *MHD Air-Breathing Propulsion and Power for Aerospace Applications*, a survey that discusses plasma/MHD flow control, power extraction, hypersonic propulsion and the heavily publicized Russian AJAX concept. The report is unusually useful because it contains both enthusiasm and a sharp negative result: it argues that the celebrated MHD “energy bypass” story becomes meaningless below very high Mach number because artificial ionization costs too much energy.

That is the ideal Sven book. The exotic mechanism is real. The hype contains a genuine thermodynamic trap. The interesting work happens in the ledger between them.

A possible spine: ionization and conductivity; Lorentz force in a flowing plasma; Hall effects; MHD generators; classic power-generation experiments; hypersonic boundary layers; shock control; plasma actuators; energy addition versus extraction; why cold air is a terrible conductor; seeding and ionization costs; AJAX; the DIA critique; “virtual cowl” ideas; reentry plasma; blackout mitigation; magnetoplasmadynamic propulsion in space; nuclear-electric architectures; modern high-power electronics; computational MHD; and the difference between demonstrating force on a plasma and closing the full vehicle energy budget.

The book should also untangle several concepts that get casually blended online. Electrohydrodynamic lifters in air are not the same as MHD. Plasma actuators at atmospheric pressure are not proof of field-controlled hypersonic craft. Space electric propulsion does not automatically scale to dense-atmosphere propulsion. A magnetic field can strongly affect a plasma while doing almost nothing to neutral air until the air has been made conductive, and making it conductive may cost more than the benefit.

Evidence boundary: MHD, plasma propulsion, plasma actuators and electromagnetic flow interaction are established physics. The performance of specific hypersonic MHD architectures is an engineering question with severe power-density and ionization constraints. DIA study does not establish operational “plasma stealth” or field-propelled aircraft.

Starting points:

- DIA, *MHD Air-Breathing Propulsion and Power for Aerospace Applications*: https://www.dia.mil/FOIA/FOIA-Electronic-Reading-Room/FileId/237629/
- Standard magnetohydrodynamics and plasma-physics texts.
- Peer-reviewed work on MHD power extraction, plasma-assisted combustion, hypersonic flow control, reentry plasma and electric spacecraft propulsion.

This one should have equations early. Otherwise the word plasma starts doing magic tricks.

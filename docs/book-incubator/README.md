# Book Incubator

These are book seeds, not active Desk books. They are deliberately kept outside `books/` so the Reader catalog and feedback surfaces do not fill with titles that have not earned manuscript status yet.

Each seed has a working title, a hard factual core, an evidence boundary, and a possible chapter spine. The test is simple: the premise should sound invented at dinner and become harder to dismiss the longer the primary record stays open.

## DIA advanced-aerospace physics seeds

The next ten are domain-first rather than incident-first. They begin from the publicly released Defense Intelligence Reference Documents produced under DIA's Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications program and then move outward into the underlying mathematics, peer-reviewed literature, failed experiments, replication history, and engineering constraints.

Important classification boundary: Senator Harry Reid requested restricted Special Access Program protection for the program in 2009. DIA reviewed that request and said the existing work did not justify a restricted SAP. The agency's memo says FY2009 deliverables were unclassified, most FY2010 research was expected to remain unclassified, and a small number of reports might incorporate Secret-level foreign-research material. These seeds therefore say **DIA-funded and officially studied**, not “secretly proven” or “highest-compartmented.”

11. [The Shape of Escape](the-shape-of-escape.md) — warp metrics, spacetime engineering, dark energy, and the geometry of faster-than-light-looking motion.
12. [The Hole That Stays Open](the-hole-that-stays-open.md) — traversable wormholes, topology, energy conditions, and the cost of keeping a shortcut open.
13. [The Debt of Empty Space](the-debt-of-empty-space.md) — Casimir physics, squeezed states, negative energy, quantum inequalities, and whether vacuum structure can ever become engineering.
14. [The Weight of a Superconductor](the-weight-of-a-superconductor.md) — superconductors, gravitomagnetism, claimed gravity anomalies, and the difference between macroscopic quantum coherence and gravitational control.
15. [The Geometry That Hides](the-geometry-that-hides.md) — metamaterials, transformation optics, subwavelength structure, cloaking, and why “invisibility” became respectable mathematics before it became good hardware.
16. [The Whisper Through Stone](the-whisper-through-stone.md) — high-frequency gravitational waves, laboratory generation/detection proposals, and the fantasy of a signal that ignores the planet in between.
17. [The Air That Becomes a Circuit](the-air-that-becomes-a-circuit.md) — plasma aerodynamics, magnetohydrodynamics, flow control, power extraction, and propulsion at hypersonic conditions.
18. [The Antimatter Engine](the-antimatter-engine.md) — positron production, storage, annihilation energy, and the brutal arithmetic between exquisite specific energy and microscopic fuel inventories.
19. [The Fire Without the Neutrons](the-fire-without-the-neutrons.md) — aneutronic fusion, charged-particle exhaust, advanced fuels, and why a cleaner fusion reaction is often a harder fusion reaction.
20. [The Forbidden Telephone](the-forbidden-telephone.md) — entanglement, nonlocality, no-signaling theorems, nonlinear quantum mechanics, and repeated attempts to ask whether correlation can ever become communication.

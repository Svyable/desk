# The Antimatter Engine

Working subtitle: **Positrons, Annihilation, Storage, and the Most Energy-Dense Fuel We Barely Know How to Keep**

Antimatter propulsion is often placed in the same cultural bucket as warp drive. Physically it belongs in a different drawer. Antimatter exists. Positrons are manufactured routinely. Matter-antimatter annihilation is measured physics. The fantasy begins when a microscopic laboratory inventory is mentally converted into a spacecraft fuel tank.

DIA commissioned *Positron Aerospace Propulsion*, drawing on work sponsored by the Air Force Research Laboratory and NASA's advanced-concepts ecosystem. The report reviews positron production, storage and propulsion concepts. That makes this seed less about forbidden laws and more about scale: a fuel can have magnificent specific energy and still be useless if producing, trapping and handling enough of it is catastrophically difficult.

The positron is especially interesting because it is easier to make than an antiproton and annihilates differently. Positron-electron annihilation produces gamma rays whose directionality, penetration and energy deposition create both propulsion possibilities and shielding problems. A concept that uses annihilation to heat a working fluid differs radically from one trying to collimate annihilation products directly.

A possible spine: Dirac's prediction; Anderson's discovery; antimatter in accelerators; pair production; positron sources; Penning traps; plasma confinement; annihilation physics; specific energy versus specific power; gamma transport; positron-catalyzed concepts; thermal rockets; beamed-core dreams; production efficiency; storage lifetime; trap density; charge and space-charge limits; radiation protection; mission architectures; the DIA report; AFRL/NASA work; how many positrons a real maneuver would require; and why “grams of antimatter” is an industrial statement, not merely a mass unit.

The book's governing arithmetic should be cruel. Start with a mission impulse or energy requirement. Convert it into annihilation energy. Convert that into positron number. Then compare with demonstrated production rates, stored populations, efficiency and containment. Do not let the reader remain at the level of “a little antimatter contains a lot of energy.”

Evidence boundary: positrons, annihilation, trapping and antimatter production are established. No crewed or uncrewed vehicle has flown on positron propulsion. Government sponsorship of propulsion studies is evidence that the concept was technically assessed, not evidence of a secret antimatter engine or stockpile.

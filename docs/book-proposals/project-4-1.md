# Project 4.1

Working subtitle: Fallout, Medicine, and the Marshall Islanders After Castle Bravo

## Thesis

After Castle Bravo contaminated inhabited Marshall Islands, U.S. medical teams studied exposed populations for decades. The difficult historical question is not whether fallout injury was real; it is how treatment, longitudinal observation, strategic responsibility, displacement, and research became entangled in a population that had little control over the event that made it medically valuable.

## Evidence posture

Treat claims that the exposure itself was intentional as disputed unless supported by documentary evidence. Separate the documented medical program from allegations about experimental intent. Center Marshallese testimony alongside U.S. technical records without pretending either source class answers every question.

## Narrative engine

Start with Bravo's yield and fallout, then follow Rongelap and Utirik through evacuation, return, thyroid disease, medical missions, Project 4.1 documentation, political struggle, resettlement, compensation, and contemporary memory.

## Research targets

DOE and AEC reports; Castle Bravo technical records; ACHRE; Marshall Islands Nuclear Claims Tribunal material; U.S. congressional records; peer-reviewed epidemiology; Marshallese oral histories and scholarship.

## Defeat conditions

Do not state that Bravo deliberately targeted civilians without evidence. Preserve uncertainty around dose reconstruction and competing epidemiological interpretations.

## Evidence classification

**Documented:** McNeill made the photograph; the Library dates it to 1962; the title comes from the negative envelope; Robinson and Campanella appear with African American State Department staff; the Library's item-level rights advisory says no known restrictions on publication.

**Corroborated context, not event evidence:** Carl T. Rowan served in State Public Affairs during 1962 and had prior documented relationships to both Robinson and Campanella. This makes him a sensible archival lead, not a proven participant in this visit.

**Unresolved:** exact date; employee identities; purpose; invitation chain; organizer; Rowan participation; policy discussion; overseas-information or formal public-diplomacy function.

**Interpretation:** the photograph can illustrate an earlier physical convergence of baseball celebrity, Black federal employment, and the diplomatic institution central to the book. It cannot establish a lineage to Burns or prove that State was deliberately deploying Robinson and Campanella as public-diplomacy instruments.

## New contextual research lead

A State Department Diplomatic Security oral-history feature records William DeFossett recalling that the Department sought a Black security agent in 1961 or early 1962 and that he joined the Office of Security in 1963. This is valuable institutional context for the racial composition of State in the same period, but it postdates the photograph as employment evidence and does **not** identify anyone in McNeill's frame. Do not use it to name a pictured employee or date the visit.

# NOISY CHANNEL — evidence ledger

## Working thesis

Remote viewing is a unusually useful epistemic case because the public record permits a conclusion more interesting than belief/disbelief: some evaluators regarded laboratory performance as statistically anomalous while the 1995 program review concluded that the information had not demonstrated adequate value for intelligence operations. The book investigates the distance between statistical evidence, mechanism, replication, interpretation, and decision utility.

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- CIA acknowledges that it investigated remote viewing, transferred work to defense organizations, received the program back in the mid-1990s, commissioned an independent review, and did not restore the program after that review. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-did-cia-really-study-psychic-powers/
- Declassified GRILL FLAME records document operational remote-viewing tasking connected to the 1980 Iran hostage crisis. Example: CIA-RDP96-00788R000800760001-1, 5 March 1980. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r000800760001-1
- Session reports explicitly described viewer impressions as raw intelligence data whose interpretation and use were the requester's responsibility. Example: CIA-RDP96-00788R000700070001-8. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r000700070001-8
- Training records include poor-correlation outcomes, not only apparent successes. Session 876 states that post-session analysis found “very little correlation” and points readers to another session on the same target. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r001400250001-0
- SRI's 1970s contractor reporting asserted successful controlled and operational performance. That assertion is documented as an assertion by the investigators; its existence does not settle its validity. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00791r000300030003-0

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete / contested interpretation

- The 1995 review produced a famous disagreement in emphasis. Statistician Jessica Utts argued that the experimental evidence supported anomalous cognition; psychologist Ray Hyman disputed whether the evidence justified a paranormal conclusion and emphasized methodological and replication issues. Their disagreement is itself central evidence for the book: a statistical residual and an established operational channel are not identical claims.
- CIA's retrospective summary says the review found enough accurate experiences to defy randomness but judged the phenomenon unreliable, inconsistent, and sporadic for intelligence use. This is a useful institutional summary, but the manuscript should quote and reconstruct the underlying AIR material directly before treating that wording as the definitive technical conclusion.

## Class 3 — disputed / not independently established

- Famous individual operational “hits” are not to be presented as proof unless reconstructed prospectively from tasking, timestamped response, target definition, judging procedure, feedback path, and outcome.
- Claims that remote viewing located specific hostages, submarines, aircraft, people, planets, ancient sites, or future events remain case-specific allegations until the full evidentiary chain is checked.
- Claims that Soviet or Russian programs demonstrated operational psychotronic capability are not established merely because U.S. intelligence collected reports about Soviet interest.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

If a robust residual survives leakage controls, preregistered judging, independent replication, fraud controls, and appropriate multiple-testing corrections, candidate explanations could include an unrecognized ordinary information channel, an experimental artifact not yet isolated, or a genuinely anomalous information process. No mechanism is assumed here.

## Contradictions and incentives to track

1. **Threat inflation.** Intelligence organizations have incentives to investigate a low-probability capability if an adversary might possess it. Research expenditure therefore does not map cleanly to prior belief.
2. **Program survival.** Investigators and operational units can have incentives to emphasize successes, especially when continuation depends on sponsor interest.
3. **Skeptical compression.** Critics can erase real methodological puzzles by treating the mere weirdness of the hypothesis as sufficient refutation.
4. **Classification selection.** The public corpus may not be a random sample of the operational record. This cannot be used as an all-purpose rescue device; missing records are missing evidence.
5. **Retrospective matching.** Rich verbal descriptions create many possible correspondences after the target is known. Prospective scoring matters.
6. **Customer utility.** Intelligence value depends on whether information changes a decision relative to alternatives, not whether a passage sounds striking after feedback.

## Publication holds

- Exact quantitative characterization of the AIR/Utts/Hyman disagreement until the original 1995 reports are directly checked.
- Any claimed operational success rate.
- Any claim that STARGATE was terminated because the phenomenon was disproven. The public record supports a narrower operational-utility conclusion.
- Any claim that statistical anomaly establishes psi, telepathy, clairvoyance, precognition, or a physical mechanism.
- Any claim that Soviet research independently validated U.S. results.

## Defeat condition

The book's central distinction fails if high-quality prospective evidence shows that remote-viewing output, under robust blinding and independent replication, reliably improves real decisions over specified baselines at a practically useful rate. In that case the channel is not merely statistically interesting; it is operational. Conversely, if the apparent laboratory anomaly disappears under stronger independent protocols, the operational gap becomes less mysterious: there may be no channel to operationalize.

- Build a prospective anatomy of several operational cases, including failures and ambiguous cases rather than famous hits only.
- Trace program names and organizational transfers carefully; do not flatten SCANATE, GRILL FLAME, CENTER LANE, SUN STREAK and STARGATE into one timeless program.

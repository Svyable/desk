## Chapter 1 — The Country That Thinks

### U.S. data-center electricity demand

Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, **2024 United States Data Center Energy Usage Report** (published December 19, 2024):

- U.S. data centers consumed an estimated 176 TWh in 2023, about 4.4% of total U.S. electricity consumption.
- Scenario range for 2028: roughly 325–580 TWh, or approximately 6.7–12% of total U.S. electricity consumption.

Source: https://energyanalysis.lbl.gov/publications/2024-lbnl-data-center-energy-usage-report

DOE summary: https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-releases-new-report-evaluating-increase-electricity-demand-data-centers

### Global and U.S. data-center demand

International Energy Agency, **Energy and AI** (2025):

- Global data-center electricity consumption is projected to more than double to about 945 TWh by 2030 in the base case.
- The United States accounts for the largest projected increase; data centers account for nearly half of U.S. electricity-demand growth to 2030 in the IEA base case.
- U.S. data-center electricity consumption is projected to increase by around 240 TWh between 2024 and 2030.

Source: https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/executive-summary

Regional detail: https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai/energy-demand-from-ai

### U.S. AI investment

Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence, **2026 AI Index Report**:

- U.S. private AI investment reached $285.9 billion in 2025.
- The United States recorded 1,953 newly funded AI companies in 2025.
- Industry produced more than 90% of notable frontier models in 2025.

Source: https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report

Economy chapter: https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report/economy

### Latin American and Caribbean adoption versus impact

Inter-American Development Bank, **Will AI Boost Productivity—or Widen Inequality?** (July 27, 2026):

- Around 80% of firms in Latin America and the Caribbean use AI tools.
- 23% report economic benefits.
- 6% report significant impact.
- The analysis emphasizes workflow redesign, skills, digital infrastructure, investment, and governance as necessary to convert experimentation into productivity.

Source: https://www.iadb.org/en/blog/economic-analysis/will-ai-boost-productivity-or-widen-inequality

### Regional public use and readiness

ECLAC / CENIA, **Latin American Artificial Intelligence Index (ILIA) 2025**:

- The region accounts for about 14% of global visits to AI web solutions, exceeding its share of global internet users.
- ILIA assesses 19 countries using more than 100 sub-indicators covering enabling factors; research, development and adoption; and governance.
- Chile, Brazil, and Uruguay are classified as regional pioneers in the 2025 edition.

Source: https://desarrollodigital.cepal.org/en/data-and-facts/latin-american-artificial-intelligence-index-ilia-2025

ECLAC publication record: https://www.cepal.org/en/publications/86008-latin-american-artificial-intelligence-index-ilia-2025-key-findings-applied

## Chapter 2 — The Five Currencies of Sovereignty

### Canadian sovereign compute strategy

Government of Canada, **Canada's National Artificial Intelligence Strategy: AI for All** (June 2026):

- Canada plans a public AI supercomputer.
- The strategy calls for expanded sovereign compute, cloud, connectivity, data, and talent.
- Proposed large-scale data-center partnerships target at least 100 MW facilities and, collectively, proposed 850 MW of capacity by 2030 with potential scaling to 2.3 GW.
- The strategy frames procurement through a build-partner-buy approach and treats sovereign capability as compatible with deep allied cooperation.

Source: https://ised-isde.canada.ca/site/ised/en/canadas-national-artificial-intelligence-strategy-ai-all

Prime Minister announcement: https://www.pm.gc.ca/en/news/news-releases/2026/06/04/prime-minister-carney-launches-ai-all-canadas-new-national-artificial

Sovereign AI Compute Infrastructure Program: https://www.canada.ca/en/innovation-science-economic-development/news/2026/04/canada-launches-national-initiative-to-build-large-scale-ai-supercomputing-capacity.html

## Chapter 7 — Chile, Uruguay, and the Competent State

### Chile national AI policy and action plan

Chile Ministry of Science, **Política Nacional de Inteligencia Artificial / Plan de Acción**:

- Chile's first National AI Policy was published in 2021.
- The policy is organized around enabling factors; development and adoption; and governance and ethics.
- The current action plan contains 177 initiatives coordinated by 14 ministries.
- Chile's Ministry of Science describes CENIA, 5G deployment, AI doctoral training, and ethical-algorithm work among the capabilities developed since the original policy.

Sources:
- https://www.minciencia.gob.cl/areas/inteligencia-artificial/politica-nacional-de-inteligencia-artificial/
- https://minciencia.gob.cl/areas/inteligencia-artificial/Inteligencia-Artificial/politica-Nacional-Inteligencia-Artificial-Plan-Accion/

### Uruguay national AI strategy and 2026 digital strategy

Uruguay Agesic / Presidency:

- Uruguay approved its National Artificial Intelligence Strategy 2024–2030 in November 2024 after a participatory process involving more than 300 people and multiple public, private, academic, civil-society, and stakeholder organizations.
- The strategy explicitly covers governance, capabilities and infrastructure, talent, data, adoption, sustainable development, inclusive growth, sovereignty, and public-management improvement.
- In May 2026, the government presented a broader digital strategy intended to make the state more efficient, accelerate procedures, consolidate public information, and use data and AI to improve decision-making.
- In May 2026, Agesic also established a public-sector strategic committee for AI and data to support implementation and strategy development.

## Chapter 8 — Brazil: The Green Compute Republic

### Brazilian Artificial Intelligence Plan

Brazil Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation, **Plano Brasileiro de Inteligência Artificial (PBIA) 2024–2028**:

- The plan carries approximately R$23 billion in planned investment over four years.
- It includes infrastructure, research, innovation, public-sector adoption, business adoption, skills, and governance.
- The infrastructure program includes expansion of national high-performance computing and a goal of placing a Brazilian AI-oriented supercomputer among the world's top systems.
- The PBIA's published structural actions include R$1.8 billion for the flagship AI supercomputer initiative and expanded regional high-performance computing centers.

### Brazilian electricity mix and data-center planning

Brazil Ministry of Mines and Energy / Energy Research Office (EPE):

- Renewable sources supplied 88.2% of Brazil's electricity mix in 2024.
- Wind and solar together supplied about 24% of total generation in 2024.
- By 2025–2026, EPE was explicitly integrating prospective data-center demand into energy and transmission planning and created a data-collection portal for data-center demand.
- Brazil approved its Ten-Year Energy Expansion Plan 2035 in July 2026, maintaining a planning framework centered on a predominantly renewable electricity system, resilience, and new demand categories.

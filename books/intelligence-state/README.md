Artificial intelligence is becoming a form of state capacity.

*Intelligence State* follows that transformation across the Americas and asks a harder question than who builds the best model: **who can make intelligence abundant without making sovereignty scarce?** The book treats sovereignty not as autarky but as meaningful choice under pressure across five currencies—compute, energy, data, talent, and trust.

## Core argument

The first AI race was a race among laboratories. The second is a race among organizations redesigning work. The third is a race among political economies.

The book's recurring concepts include **meaningful choice under pressure**, the **sovereignty premium**, **minimum viable sovereignty**, the **competence premium**, **maximum learning per installation**, **resource curse 2.0**, **diffusion infrastructure**, **machine speed with human contestability**, **high-quality friction**, and **capability residue**.

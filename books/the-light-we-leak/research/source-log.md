# Source log

## Historical and negative evidence

- Lorenz E. “Search for Mitogenetic Radiation by Means of the Photoelectric Method.” *Journal of General Physiology* 17(6), 1934. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1085/jgp.17.6.843 — physical detector study reporting no detectable mitogenetic radiation from tested biological materials; essential counterevidence.
- Gurwitsch A, Gurwitsch L. 1920s papers on mitogenetic radiation and induction, indexed in PubMed, including https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28354955/ and related records. These establish historical claims, not modern validation.
- Gurwitsch AA. “A historical review of the problem of mitogenetic radiation.” *Experientia* 44, 1988. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01953301 — retrospective from within the Gurwitsch research tradition; useful but interested source.

## Modern reviews and mechanism

- Mould RR et al. “Ultra weak photon emission—a brief review.” *Frontiers in Physiology* 15, 2024. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2024.1348915 — recent overview of mechanism, detection and proposed applications.
- Kučera O, Cifra M. “Cell-to-cell signaling through light: just a ghost of chance?” *Cell Communication and Signaling* 11, 2013. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1186/1478-811X-11-87 — key physical critique of endogenous optical communication, ambient noise and photon budgets.
- Volodyaev I, Beloussov LV. “Revisiting the mitogenetic effect of ultra-weak photon emission.” *Frontiers in Physiology* 6, 2015. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2015.00241 — historical/experimental review sympathetic to renewed testing; treat signaling interpretation cautiously.

## Human and skin measurements

- Kobayashi M, Kikuchi D, Okamura H. “Imaging of Ultraweak Spontaneous Photon Emission from Human Body Displaying Diurnal Rhythm.” *PLOS ONE* 4(7), 2009. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0006256 — cryogenic CCD imaging, diurnal variation, intensity far below visual threshold.
- Tsuchida K, Kobayashi M. “Ultraviolet A irradiation induces ultraweak photon emission with characteristic spectral patterns from biomolecules present in human skin.” *Scientific Reports* 10, 2020. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-78884-0 — spectra from skin-relevant biomolecules and tissue.
- “Imaging of ultraweak photon emission for evaluating the oxidative stress of human skin.” *Journal of Photochemistry and Photobiology B*, 2019. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31349151/ — UV dose response and antioxidant suppression.

## Plants

- Yoshinaga N et al. “Ultraweak photon emission from herbivory-injured maize plants.” *Naturwissenschaften* 93, 2006. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00114-005-0059-9
- “Biophoton imaging: a nondestructive method for assaying R gene responses.” PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15720077/ — plant hypersensitive response imaging.
- Oros CL, Alves F. “Leaf wound induced ultraweak photon emission is suppressed under anoxic stress.” *PLOS ONE* 13, 2018. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0198962

## Neural emission and speculative extensions

- Kataoka Y et al. “Activity-dependent neural tissue oxidation emits intrinsic ultraweak photons.” *Biochemical and Biophysical Research Communications* 285, 2001. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1006/bbrc.2001.5285 — supports activity-linked neural emission, not optical cognition.
- Tang R, Dai J. “Biophoton signal transmission and processing in the brain.” *Journal of Photochemistry and Photobiology B* 139, 2014. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jphotobiol.2013.12.008 — hypothesis/review; use as evidence of proposal, not established mechanism.

## Coherence and DNA claims

- Popp FA et al. “Biophoton emission. New evidence for coherence and DNA as source.” *Cell Biophysics* 6, 1984. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02788579 — historically influential coherence/DNA interpretation requiring independent modern validation.
- “Ultra-weak photon emission from DNA.” *Scientific Reports*, 2024. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-80469-0 — recent physical experiment with strong interpretive claims; evidence of measured setup results, not proof of an endogenous cellular DNA laser.

## Evidence-use rule

A paper showing emission is evidence for emission. A paper showing light sensitivity is evidence for light sensitivity. Neither alone establishes endogenous photonic communication. Patents, theoretical waveguide papers and photobiomodulation studies should not be upgraded into evidence of a naturally used signaling channel without matched endogenous photon budgets.

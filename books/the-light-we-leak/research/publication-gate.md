## Chapter-specific expansion work

### Ch 1 — The Onion Root
- Recover and page-index the original 1923 Gurwitsch onion-root publication or the closest primary archival copy.
- Reconstruct source/receiver geometry, mitotic scoring, sample size, exposure timing, and quartz/glass conditions.
- Distinguish what Gurwitsch himself claimed from later summaries.

### Ch 2 — A Discovery Too Easy to Love
- Build a denominator table of early positive, negative, and ambiguous replications by laboratory and method rather than repeating aggregate “600/700 papers” counts.
- Trace early cancer/diagnostic proposals to primary publications.

### Ch 3 — The Quartz Window
- Quantify transmission spectra of historically relevant quartz and glass thicknesses.
- Model alternative barrier effects: UV transmission, thermal transfer, airflow, volatile exchange, static fields, and handling.

### Ch 4 — When the Effect Vanished
- Obtain the National Research Council Bulletin No. 100 by Hollaender and Claus and reconstruct protocols and negative results directly.
- Build the clearest historical timeline of how the field lost mainstream support rather than implying one decisive paper killed it.

### Ch 5 — The Light Was Real
- Add detector-history chronology from photomultipliers through cooled CCD/EMCCD/SPAD systems.
- Normalize reported UPE intensities across representative organisms and preparations with detector sensitivity and spectral ranges.

### Ch 6 — Chemistry That Glows
- Add reaction-level treatment of lipid peroxidation, triplet carbonyls, singlet oxygen and other emissive species.
- Include representative spectra and quantum yields where primary data support them.

### Ch 7 — How to Count Almost Nothing
- Add an explicit photon-counting noise budget: detector dark counts, shot noise, cosmic/environmental contributions, afterglow/delayed luminescence, calibration and integration-time tradeoffs.
- Compare PMT, cooled CCD/EMCCD and modern SPAD/superconducting detector strengths for UPE.

### Ch 8 — The Human Body Glimmers
- Reconstruct the 2009 human study at protocol level, including n, timing, dark adaptation, camera integration and anatomical pattern.
- Search for independent human circadian-UPE replication and null results through 2026.

### Ch 9 — The Brain in the Dark
- Reconstruct major rat/brain preparations, anesthesia and exposure geometry.
- Separate scalp/skull transmission from ex-vivo or exposed-cortex measurement.
- Quantify plausible photon budgets for proposed neural functions.

### Ch 10 — Signal Is a Much Bigger Word
- Formalize the emission → cue → signal → communication → code ladder using biological-signaling literature without turning the chapter into an information-theory abstraction.
- Add at least one worked falsification example for timing and one for spectrum.

### Ch 11 — Two Cuvettes
- Reproduce the Mould et al. 2023 apparatus and statistics from the full text, including exact cuvette geometry, illumination conditions, antimycin protocol, effect sizes and controls.
- Search for independent replication or criticism through September 2026.

### Ch 12 — The Receiver Problem
- Build a receptor table: chromophore, absorption band, natural biological role, demonstrated sensitivity, and estimated natural UPE photon availability.
- Separate photobiomodulation intensities from endogenous UPE by explicit orders of magnitude.

### Ch 13 — Noise That Biology Could Steal

- Calculate whether any plausible amplification regime survives known optical losses and reaction noise.

### Ch 14 — The Popp Problem

### Ch 15 — Medicine Loves a Noninvasive Signal

### Ch 16 — Quantum Is Not a Permission Slip

## Research gaps

- Primary Soviet/Russian-language Gurwitsch papers and institutional history need deeper archival sourcing.
- The early-replication denominator needs primary-paper verification.
- The Popp/coherence controversy needs technical counterpapers, not generic skeptical summaries.
- Post-2023 non-chemical mitochondrial/cell-culture replication literature needs a systematic search through 2026.
- Candidate-receptor thresholds need explicit photon-flux comparison at endogenous intensity.
- Any consciousness-related biophoton material should remain outside the manuscript unless a later evidence audit identifies a concrete experiment with a falsifiable photonic mechanism.

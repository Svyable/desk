# Contradictions and incentives

The field contains several contradictions that should stay visible rather than be harmonized away.

1. **Historical biological positives vs physical nulls.** Gurwitsch-era induction reports cannot simply be treated as validated because modern UPE exists. Lorenz's 1934 physical null is part of the record.
2. **Robust emission vs fragile function.** Photon emission is reproducible across many systems; endogenous communication is not established to the same standard.
3. **Useful correlation vs causal signaling.** UPE can track real signaling pathways such as oxidative bursts without itself carrying the biological signal.
4. **Optical sensitivity vs endogenous relevance.** Cells respond to experimentally applied light at many wavelengths and intensities; those intensities may dwarf spontaneous emission.
5. **Dark-tissue advantage vs transport loss.** Internal tissue can shield endogenous photons from ambient light while simultaneously absorbing the photons before they reach a receiver.
6. **Coherence claims vs heterogeneous chemistry.** Complex decay/statistical structure may be real while still admitting classical chemical explanations.

## Incentives that can distort interpretation

- An anomalous or consciousness-adjacent framing attracts attention beyond what a weak physiological reporter does.
- Diagnostic/commercial applications reward claims of unique disease signatures before transport and confound studies are mature.
- Skeptics can overcorrect by treating “biophoton” as inherently pseudoscientific despite well-established UPE measurements.
- Enthusiasts can retroactively vindicate historical claims whenever any nearby optical phenomenon is confirmed.
- Review articles can flatten evidence classes by citing hypothesis papers alongside direct measurements without marking the difference.

# Evidentiary Matrix

This matrix keeps four evidence classes separate. A source may establish that an investigator made a claim without establishing that the claim is true.

| Claim | Class | Current posture | Key evidence / counterevidence | Upgrade or defeat condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Living systems spontaneously emit ultraweak photons | 1 — verified/documented | Strong | Physical photon-counting and imaging across microbial, plant, animal, human skin and neural preparations; modern reviews | Would require systematic demonstration that accepted measurements are dominated by unrecognized instrument artifact; not presently plausible |
| Much spontaneous UPE arises from oxidative metabolic/stress chemistry | 1 — verified/documented | Strong, mechanism plural | ROS-linked experiments; lipid/protein/nucleic-acid oxidation; excited carbonyl and singlet-oxygen pathways; antioxidant and oxygen perturbations | Narrow individual pathways as spectroscopy improves; do not replace plural chemistry with one universal emitter |
| Human body UPE varies over the day | 1 — documented for studied protocol | Moderate-to-strong, small original human sample | Kobayashi et al. 2009 | Larger independent replication with controlled light history, pigmentation, diet, activity, and time would strengthen generality |
| Brain UPE tracks metabolic/oxidative state | 1/2 boundary | Supported in animal/preclinical work | Rat-brain imaging and later review literature | Independent multimodal replication with source localization and tissue-path modeling |
| Gurwitsch actually reported a mitogenetic radiation program in the 1920s–30s | 1 — verified historical fact | Strong | Primary/contemporary publications and reviews | Historical archival refinement only |
| Gurwitsch’s original onion-root mitogenetic effect was a robust photon-mediated biological effect | 3 — disputed/not established | Weak | Historical positive literature versus major negative replication programs and unstable biological detection | Modern blinded preregistered replication with measured source flux, optical-path control, artificial replay, and automated mitosis scoring |
| Quartz-versus-glass selectivity established UV as the original carrier | 3 — disputed/not established | Suggestive historical clue, not decisive | Historical barrier logic; incomplete control of thermal, volatile, handling and biological-detector variables | Reconstruct with spectrally measured matched barriers and direct photon detection |
| Natural UPE can causally alter neighboring cells/organelles | 2 — credible but incomplete | Open | Reports of non-contact biological effects, including mitochondria work; optical/light dependence in some studies | Independent replication with optical-only switching, direct flux measurement, matched replay and channel controls |
| Mitochondria communicate via biophotons | 2/3 boundary | Possible carrier, not established | 2023 separated-mitochondria respiration study suggests non-chemical/light-linked effect but does not uniquely identify photons | Measure transmitted photons during effect; opaque block abolishes; matched natural-flux replay restores; thermal/volatile/EM channels remain flat |
| Cells contain molecules capable of absorbing wavelengths overlapping UPE | 1 — verified chemistry | Strong | Flavins, porphyrins, cytochromes, opsins, cryptochromes and other chromophores | Functional significance remains separate |
| Those chromophores act as dedicated endogenous UPE receptors | 3 — not established generally | Weak | Candidate mechanism literature, mostly extrapolated from stronger external illumination | Genetic/pharmacological receptor perturbation tied to natural-flux source-receiver effect and matching action spectrum |
| Natural UPE carries statistically usable information about source state | 1/2 boundary | Strong for some biomarker contexts; open for general coding | Intensity/spectral changes with oxidative state and physiology | Prospective out-of-sample state decoding with predeclared features |
| Cells encode and decode messages in UPE timing or wavelength | 3 — not established | Weak | Hypotheses and pattern reports | Preserve total flux while shuffling timing/spectrum; receiver responds specifically to intact pattern at natural levels |
| Biological UPE forms a coherent organism-wide optical field | 3 — disputed | Weak | Popp-school coherence claims; limited modern independent confirmation under current quantum-optical standards | Independent first/higher-order coherence measurements with matched chemiluminescent controls and physiological causality |
| DNA is a primary coherent biophoton source | 3 — disputed | Weak | Popp et al. reported changes associated with DNA perturbation; modern oxidative-source literature is plural | Direct source-resolved spectroscopy/quantum statistics isolating DNA contribution without broad metabolic confounding |
| UPE can serve as a noninvasive oxidative-stress biomarker | 1/2 boundary | Supported research application | Skin oxidative-stress studies, human imaging, animal neural work | Prospective reproducibility, calibration, confound control and incremental decision value |
| UPE can diagnose cancer or neurological disease clinically | 2/3 boundary | Research-stage, not clinically established | Cell/tissue differences and emerging reviews | Large prospective validation with sensitivity, specificity, external replication, workflow and comparator performance |
| Endogenous UPE participates in neural information processing | 3 — not established | Speculative | Neural emission exists; candidate theories/reviews; no established receiver/channel chain | Natural-flux causal experiment plus receptor/channel identification and information-rate plausibility |
| Quantum effects beyond ordinary photochemistry are required to explain UPE | 3 — not established | Unsupported as general claim | Every photon is quantum; current bulk UPE largely consistent with chemiluminescent mechanisms | Demonstrate nonclassical statistics/coherence not reproduced by matched chemical controls and show biological consequence |
| Evolution may exploit metabolic photon leakage as a local cue in some systems | 4 — speculative synthesis | Plausible and intentionally narrow | UPE covaries with physiology; biology often exploits by-products; some non-contact effects reported | Strengthens with natural-flux receiver/receptor experiments; weakens if thresholds are orders above natural flux or optical blockade has no effect |

## Defeat conditions for the book’s strongest open synthesis

The “metabolic leakage recruited as cue” synthesis should be narrowed or abandoned if careful photon-budget calculations and experiments show natural source fluxes consistently too small to affect any plausible receptor in relevant geometry; if source-receiver effects do not replicate under blinded preregistered protocols; or if effects survive elimination of the optical path and instead follow another carrier.

It would strengthen if at least two independent laboratories observe the same natural-flux effect, directly measure photons reaching the receiver, abolish the response through spectrally appropriate optical blockade, restore it with matched artificial replay, and identify a receiver mechanism whose action spectrum and dose-response agree with the natural source.

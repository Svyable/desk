## Defeat condition for the book's central thesis

The book's central thesis is intentionally conditional: UPE is established, signaling is not, but exaptation is empirically plausible. It should narrow toward “reporter only” if calibrated source-to-receiver photon budgets fail across plausible local geometries, matched endogenous replay repeatedly produces no effect, and candidate receptor manipulations do not alter reported distant interactions.

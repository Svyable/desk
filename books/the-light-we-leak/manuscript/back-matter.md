# Back Matter

This draft uses “ultraweak photon emission” (UPE) for spontaneous low-intensity optical emission associated with biological processes and uses “biophoton” only when discussing the literature or a historical term. The photon itself is ordinary; the biological source and possible function are what require explanation.

The manuscript deliberately separates four evidence classes. Verified or documented facts include measured UPE, modern oxidative-generation mechanisms, and specific published experimental results. Credible but incomplete evidence includes some distant-interaction reports, proposed receptor pathways and tissue-optics models that are plausible but not yet sufficient for a functional signaling claim. Disputed allegations include broad claims of coherent organism-wide optical control, human aura mechanisms and strong DNA-laser interpretations lacking independent mechanistic confirmation. Speculative synthesis includes the evolutionary proposal that unavoidable oxidative emission could be exapted first as a cue and later, in some contexts, as a signal.

No unpublished classified, proprietary or private scientific material was used. All sources in the research trail are public.

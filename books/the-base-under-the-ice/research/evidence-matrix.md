## Four evidence classes

1. **Verified/declassified/documented:** primary or authoritative public records and direct measurements.
2. **Credible but incomplete:** strong reporting, archival reconstruction or scientific inference with missing records, uncertain attribution or unresolved parameters.
3. **Disputed/unconfirmed:** claims lacking independent confirmation, contradicted by stronger evidence, or frequently repeated beyond what the source establishes.
4. **Speculative synthesis/open conjecture:** the book's proposed interpretation. These claims are useful only insofar as they remain defeasible.

## Distinct collision

The neglected collision is not merely “secret base + climate change.” It is that the same physical property defeated and preserved the project. Ice flow made a vast stable missile railway unattractive; accumulation and flow then buried the abandoned installation; the camp's drilling program produced an archive demonstrating that the ice sheet itself has not been permanent on geological timescales; modern warming turned a disposal assumption into a governance question; later site measurements then narrowed the alarm. The physical system keeps changing the meaning of the original military act.

## Why this is not another secrecy book

Secrecy is one evidence stream, not the thesis. The book would still work if every Iceworm file had been declassified in 1963 because its main object is the mismatch between administrative closure and physical persistence. The decisive evidence comes from engineering, glaciology, waste transport, ice cores, sovereignty, and remote sensing rather than from classification alone.

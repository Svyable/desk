# Back Matter

## Evidence posture

This manuscript is **declassified-engineering-forward / climate-model-corrective / sovereignty-aware / waste-pathway-cautious / prototype-not-deployment / ice-core-science-strong / physical-afterlife-synthesis-explicit**.

It treats Camp Century's construction, PM-2A operation, deep drilling, Project Iceworm studies, abandonment, later radar mapping and climate monitoring as documented. It treats the deployment of hundreds of nuclear missiles beneath Greenland as a plan that was studied and rejected, not a capability that was fielded. It treats the presence of residual radiological waste as real while rejecting the common claim that the reactor itself remains under the ice. It treats the 2016 high-emissions remobilization study as a serious risk analysis and the later GEUS finding of no expected meltwater interaction through 2100 under assessed scenarios as equally important evidence.

## Claim boundary

The manuscript does not turn Iceworm planning into deployment, does not turn Camp Century's real reactor history into a claim that a reactor remains buried, does not turn a high-emissions model projection into a scheduled 2090 exposure event, and does not turn modern radar imagery into evidence of an intact underground city. Where the public record is incomplete about political knowledge or intent, the prose remains attributed and conditional.

# Source Log

## Primary / official

1. **Klebesadel, Strong & Olson (1973), “Observations of Gamma-Ray Bursts of Cosmic Origin.”** First discovery paper; 16 events, 1969–72; directional exclusion of Earth and Sun. https://svs.gsfc.nasa.gov/vis/a010000/a014300/a014355/1973ApJ...182L..85K.pdf
2. **NASA NTRS: Cline et al. (1973), “Energy Spectra of Cosmic Gamma-Ray Bursts.”** Independent IMP-6 confirmation. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19730060251
3. **NASA HEASARC Vela 5B archive.** Mission characteristics, payload, operational lifetime and scientific outputs. https://heasarc.gsfc.nasa.gov/docs/vela5b/vela5b.html
4. **NASA 50-year GRB history.** Program-to-astronomy overview and discovery chronology. https://www.nasa.gov/universe/nasa-looks-back-at-50-years-of-gamma-ray-burst-science/
5. **National Security Archive Vela collection (2016).** Declassified White House, State, intelligence and technical documents on the 1979 event. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/nuclear-vault/2016-12-06/vela-incident-south-atlantic-mystery-flash-september-1979-raised-questions-about-nuclear-test
6. **Nuclear Explosion Panel charter, circa 1 Nov 1979.** Establishes panel mandate to assess nuclear, natural and instrumental hypotheses. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/media/22326/ocr
7. **White House Science and Technology staff report, 9 Nov 1979.** Records early Ruina-panel skepticism and Arecibo lead. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/19604-national-security-archive-doc-3-white-house
8. **Owen/Oplinger to Brzezinski, 9 Jan 1980.** Records policy consensus that government did not know what happened. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/19605-national-security-archive-doc-4-henry-owen-jerry
9. **Frank Press to Stansfield Turner, 6 Jun 1980.** Explains panel’s “probably not nuclear” conclusion while acknowledging DIA/CIA NIP/DOE-lab dissent. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/19607-national-security-archive-doc-6-frank-press
10. **Director of Central Intelligence, NI IIM 79-10028, “The 22 September 1979 Event.”** Conditional perpetrator analysis; does not settle event identity. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/29884-document-6b-director-central-intelligence-ni-iim-79-10028-22-september-1979-event
11. **CIA/DOE PDF release of same study.** https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/DOC_0000037625.pdf

## Secondary orientation only

15. NASA historical GRB pages and Air Force space-history sources may be used for orientation but claims should migrate to primary/official sources in the expansion pass.

## Source handling rules

- Patents, memoirs and later recollections do not establish event efficacy or occurrence by themselves.
- A document analyzing actors under a nuclear-event assumption is not evidence that the assumption was proven.
- National Security Archive descriptions are valuable finding aids; manuscript-critical claims should be page-indexed back to underlying documents where available.
- Retrospective scientific reanalysis can update the evidentiary balance but must not be presented as official government closure.

# Back Matter

## Note on evidence

This manuscript treats program existence, sensor detection, event identification and actor attribution as separate burdens. It does not use the reality of South Africa’s later-acknowledged nuclear arsenal or Israel’s widely assessed nuclear capability as proof of the 22 September 1979 event. It does not treat the Ruina panel’s preferred natural explanation as a demonstrated meteoroid impact. It does not convert later hydroacoustic or iodine-131 analyses into a government confession.

## Acknowledgment of uncertainty

The strongest version of this book is one that could survive a future declassification proving the event nuclear, proving it non-nuclear, or naming an actor different from the most discussed candidates. The core thesis concerns how evidence moves through institutions under different error costs. It should not depend on the mystery remaining mysterious.

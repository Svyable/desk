## Release use

Before first Shelf publication, the final claim-level pass should use this map in both directions:

1. for every major factual claim in a chapter, identify the source packet that supports or constrains it;
2. for every source packet, verify the manuscript has not turned a narrow fact into a broader causal or “first” claim the evidence does not support.

The highest-risk wording remains the book’s deliberate superlative. “Most important school of the decade” is the author’s selection argument, not a sourced objective ranking. Historical “first” language inside chapters is narrower and must remain tied to the metric actually supported by the record.

# BURN THE GAS — evidence ledger

## Working thesis

Project Plowshare is a positive control for extraordinary state technology that genuinely crossed from classified/nuclear capability into attempted civilian infrastructure. Nuclear gas stimulation shows that secrecy can protect device details and operations while the claimed *useful output* creates interfaces that cannot simply be classified away. The governing question for modern hidden-technology claims is therefore not "could a program be secret?" but "what matter, energy, product, labor, measurement or downstream consequence must cross the compartment boundary if the claimed capability is real?"

This is a proposed analytical framework, not an established law. It must be normalized by scale, frequency, infrastructure reuse, output type and signature specificity.

## Evidence classes

### 1. Verified / declassified / documented

- DOE Office of Legacy Management identifies Gasbuggy as a Plowshare site and states that the AEC detonated a nuclear device in 1967 at 4,227 feet below ground to fracture rock and increase natural-gas flow. DOE says most radionuclides remained in solidified molten rock in the cavity and reports no surface radiation release from the detonation. Source: https://www.energy.gov/lm/tribal-collaboration
- DOE maintains dedicated legacy-management pages for Gasbuggy, Rulison and Rio Blanco. Rulison and Rio Blanco require monitoring of nearby natural-gas and groundwater wells plus continuing records/stakeholder work. Sources: https://www.energy.gov/lm/gasbuggy-new-mexico-site ; https://www.energy.gov/lm/rulison-colorado-site ; https://www.energy.gov/lm/rio-blanco-colorado-site
- DOE/OSTI Plowshare history reports a twenty-year program with ten nuclear field experiments: five cratering events and five contained explosions. It identifies three off-Nevada-Test-Site experiments as nuclear gas stimulation. Source: https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/514386-g3aMbc/webviewable/514386.pdf
- DOE OpenNet's Plowshare history describes the program's broad proposed uses, including harbors, canals, tar sands/oil shale, aquifers, leaching/mining and other excavation/resource applications. Source: https://www.osti.gov/opennet/reports/plowshar.pdf
- DOE legacy documentation of Gasbuggy sampling reports tritium, krypton-85, iodine-131 and argon-37 in post-test gas samples and says gas directly affected by the detonation (including tritiated methane) was flared during post-detonation production testing. Source surfaced through OSTI: https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/1023213
- DOE states Plowshare was established in 1957 to explore technical and economic feasibility of peaceful nuclear-explosive uses. A 2026 Legacy Management note on the Bronco site also documents participation by the AEC, Bureau of Mines and CER Geonuclear, which represented 18 oil companies, illustrating the government/industry interface around prospective applications. Source: https://www.energy.gov/lm/articles/bronco-site-reclamation-project-successfully-completed

### 2. Credible but incomplete

- Exact production improvement at Gasbuggy, Rulison and Rio Blanco relative to pre-shot reservoir models requires primary production tables and uncertainty reconstruction before quantitative claims enter manuscript prose.
- Exact radionuclide concentrations in produced gas over time, gas-processing experiments, proposed customer-dose calculations and regulatory/economic thresholds require event-level technical reports rather than later summaries.
- Private-industry decision making and the reasons specific commercialization pathways died need contemporary company/AEC records. Avoid compressing technical, regulatory, economic and public-acceptance causes into one slogan.
- Soviet peaceful nuclear-explosion comparisons are potentially powerful but require program-by-program primary sourcing and must not be used as evidence that the U.S. applications were secretly more successful than public records show.

### 3. Disputed / unconfirmed allegations

- Claims that Plowshare served as a cover for unrelated exotic weapons, UAP programs, underground bases or non-human technology are not established by the sources above.
- Claims that current governments secretly use nuclear explosives for routine resource extraction are not established here.
- The existence of classified nuclear-device design information does not establish hidden successful commercialization.
- Patents or proposals for nuclear excavation/resource extraction are evidence of filing/planning, not efficacy.

### 4. Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

**Interface leakage:** a physical capability that must deliver useful output outside its compartment tends to create externally coupled traces. Candidate trace classes: product, waste, energy, transport, specialized labor, metrology, regulation, procurement, maintenance, environmental signature and downstream technical consequence.

**Product-crossing test:** identify the claimed useful output and ask where it first must interact with ordinary infrastructure. A hidden capability with no required external product has a lower trace burden than one claimed to reshape an industry.

**Legacy duration:** for physical technologies, program duration should include stewardship/monitoring/cleanup when those are causally created by the intervention. This is an accounting choice and should be labeled as such.

**Boring-technology race:** an exotic application competes not with the incumbent at the moment of proposal but with the incumbent's improvement curve over the exotic system's development time. This may explain why technically real extraordinary approaches can lose without having been fraudulent or physically impossible.

## Quantitative extraction burden

For each nuclear gas-stimulation event extract: device count; stated yield; emplacement depth; formation; pre-shot permeability/production; predicted fractured volume; measured cavity/fracture estimates; shut-in period; production-test dates; gas volume; radionuclide species and concentration by date; treatment/dilution assumptions; flared volume; modeled dose pathways; nearby groundwater/gas monitoring geometry; economic assumptions; comparison with conventional stimulation; and explicit AEC/industry go/no-go criteria.

Do not use a single number such as yield or gas volume as a proxy for system success.

## Contradictions / incentives to track

- AEC/Livermore institutional incentive to find civilian applications for weapons-laboratory expertise versus industry incentive to obtain cheap stimulation technology.
- "Contained" can mean no prompt surface release while still allowing radionuclides in produced gas; never let those statements appear contradictory when they answer different pathways.
- Later DOE legacy summaries are authoritative for stewardship status but can compress the technical controversy; reconstruct original reports where possible.
- Anti-nuclear advocacy sources can preserve valuable documents and local testimony while carrying political incentives; use them as discovery aids unless primary evidence is independently recovered.
- Program advocates could define technical success more narrowly than commercial success. Preserve both definitions instead of retroactively choosing one.

## Strongest countermodel

Interface leakage is not a universal detector of secrecy. A small program can reuse secure laboratories, contractors, ranges and logistics already built for other purposes. Physical traces can be real but nonspecific. Commercial programs generate far more interfaces than intelligence collection or one-off prototypes. Plowshare therefore cannot establish a minimum footprint for unrelated alleged programs. The model earns value only if it predicts *which* interfaces scale with a specific claim.

## Defeat condition

The book's framework weakens substantially if, after scale-normalized historical comparison, successful consequential classified physical technologies routinely show no greater externally coupled trace burden than failed or nonexistent programs, or if the proposed interface categories cannot discriminate real programs from rumor after controlling for scale and duration.

## Publication holds

- Do not call Gasbuggy commercially successful.
- Do not claim the gas was "too radioactive to sell" until contemporaneous concentration, treatment, regulatory and market records are reconstructed. Current prose states the narrower documented fact: radionuclides were detected and directly affected gas was flared during production tests.
- Do not infer surface release from radionuclides measured in produced gas.
- Do not use the phrase "nuclear fracking" as a historical program name; it is a modern explanatory shorthand.
- Do not imply all Plowshare concepts were field-tested.
- Do not infer motive from the program's civilian label.
- Do not connect Plowshare to UAP/retrieval claims except as an explicitly bounded positive-control comparison.

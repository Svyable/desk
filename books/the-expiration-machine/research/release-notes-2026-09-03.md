## First-edition thesis

Civilization repeatedly solves the same problem in different forms: yesterday's judgment must remain reliable long enough to coordinate action, but not so long that stale trust becomes permanent authority. Expiration is the mechanism that gives the future another vote.

## Final-section addition

Chapter 21, **The Dead Man's Switch**, extends the framework to autonomous software. It argues for a layered authority model:

**durable principal → temporary delegation → independent renewal → automatic expiration on silence**

The chapter also preserves the counterexample: fail-closed behavior is appropriate only when unattended continuation is more dangerous than interruption.

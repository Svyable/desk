# Evidence Matrix

| Claim | Class | Public support | Boundary / defeat condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| U.S. personnel secretly evaluated an Israeli-provided MiG-21 at Groom Lake under HAVE DOUGHNUT in 1968. | 1 — verified/documented | National Security Archive collection; DIA/FTD report metadata; NASIC retrospective briefings. | Defeated only by a major archival authenticity failure; current record is convergent and official/declassified. |
| HAVE DRILL and HAVE FERRY evaluated MiG-17 aircraft at Groom Lake in 1969. | 1 | National Security Archive; NASIC briefings. | Same archival standard. |
| CONSTANT PEG/4477th flew MiG-17, MiG-21 and MiG-23 aircraft to train U.S. aircrew. | 1 | USAF declassification announcement; National Museum USAF history. | Established program fact. |
| Constant Peg flew >15,000 sorties and trained/exposed roughly 6,000 aircrew. | 1 | USAF Museum; Air University review gives 15,264 sorties/5,930 crews. | Exact totals should be reconciled against primary squadron history in expansion pass. |
| Secret foreign aircraft materially improved U.S. fighter training. | 1/2 | Program purpose documented; participant and official assessments strongly positive. | Magnitude of causal contribution to later combat outcomes is not isolated experimentally. |
| Constant Peg significantly caused Desert Storm’s favorable air-to-air exchange ratio. | 2 — credible but incomplete | Official retrospective credits contribution; graduates participated. | Competing causes include Red Flag, Topgun, platform/sensor/missile advantages, Iraqi training/doctrine and coalition conditions. Treat as contributory, not monocausal. |
| Later MiGs were acquired through specific covert pathways commonly described in popular histories. | 2/3 depending case | Some participant/secondary accounts; public official detail incomplete. | Do not state a specific pathway without documentary corroboration. |
| Historical foreign-materiel exploitation predicts an organizational/logistical “wake” around real retrieval programs. | 4 — speculative synthesis | Inference from documented programs. | Weakened if comparably large real black programs can be shown to leave no later documentary, personnel, infrastructure or capability residue at all. |
| Modern U.S. programs possess recovered non-human craft. | 3 — disputed/unconfirmed in public record | Public testimony and allegations exist, but no publicly inspectable chain-of-custody hardware demonstrating non-human origin is established here. | Move upward only with authenticated records, inspectable material, independently reproducible properties, or equivalent evidence. |
| A recovered anomalous object has been successfully reverse engineered into deployable breakthrough propulsion/energy technology. | 3 | Public claims exist in the broader discourse; no independently verifiable performance record established in this book. | Require reproducible effect, scaling, integration and operational consequence. |

## Rule against category leakage

A Class 1 fact about historical secrecy does not upgrade a Class 3 modern allegation. “Governments really hid MiGs at Area 51” establishes that governments can hide MiGs at Area 51. It does not establish that they hid non-human vehicles there.

Likewise, a Class 3 claim should not contaminate the documented history. The MiG programs remain worth studying even if every modern crash-retrieval allegation were false.

## Deliberate claim boundaries

- The book treats the existence, broad purpose and documented test/training histories of HAVE DOUGHNUT, HAVE DRILL, HAVE FERRY and CONSTANT PEG as established.
- Specific acquisition pathways for later MiGs are not generalized beyond public evidence.
- Operation Diamond detail beyond the documented Redfa defection and Israeli custody is treated cautiously because much public narrative depends on later intelligence histories and memoir.
- The book does not infer that all alleged UAP retrieval claims are false from the historical MiG record. It uses the MiG record as a control case for expected program structure and evidentiary residue.
- Patents, if discussed in later expansion, will be treated as evidence of filing, not efficacy.
- Classification is never treated as evidence that a technical claim is true.

# Back Matter

## A Note on Evidence

This book makes a narrow claim from a strong historical base and then uses that base to discipline a weaker modern evidentiary field.

The existence of HAVE DOUGHNUT, HAVE DRILL, HAVE FERRY and CONSTANT PEG is not controversial in the same way that modern crash-retrieval allegations are controversial. The historical programs have official acknowledgments, declassified reports, named units, surviving aircraft, test objectives, dates, facilities and personnel histories.

The book therefore refuses a common rhetorical move: using the reality of one secret to certify another.

“Governments hid MiGs” is evidence that governments can hide MiGs.

It is not evidence that governments recovered non-human craft.

The legitimate inference is methodological. Documented foreign-materiel exploitation shows what acquisition, custody, testing, maintenance, dissemination and operationalization look like when a secret hardware program is real. That pattern can be used to generate questions and predictions for less-established claims.

## Source hierarchy

Priority order in revision:

1. authenticated declassified/official records;
2. contemporaneous technical reports and official histories;
3. peer-reviewed or professional technical literature;
4. primary participant interviews and memoir, attributed and checked;
5. serious investigative reporting;
6. popular secondary summaries only as leads.

No anonymously sourced modern allegation should carry a major technical claim without independent corroboration.

## Terms

**Foreign materiel exploitation (FME):** technical and operational study of adversary equipment acquired through capture, defection, purchase, allied transfer or other means.

**Characterization:** measurement of properties without necessarily establishing full mechanism.

**Reverse engineering:** reconstruction of design logic or function from an existing object. In this book the term does not imply successful reproduction.

**Replication:** independent reproduction of a claimed effect or component behavior.

**Fielding:** conversion of a replicated effect into a reliable operational system.

**Retrieval claim:** an allegation that a government or contractor recovered a vehicle, debris, material or biological evidence associated with anomalous aerospace. “Retrieval” alone does not establish origin.

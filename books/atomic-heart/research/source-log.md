## Source boundaries

The published papers establish performance in reported cohorts, not all implanted nuclear pacemakers worldwide. NRC enforcement pages establish custody violations, not resulting public exposure. Museum estimates of surviving patients are dated secondary counts pending current regulator/manufacturer reconciliation. Claims about first implant, total worldwide implants, exact source quantities and national recovery rates remain model- and jurisdiction-specific until terminal records are recovered.

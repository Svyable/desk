## Falsification / alternative explanations

The book's systems thesis should be tested against simpler explanations: manufacturing cost alone, regulator prohibition, public fear, poor thermoelectric efficiency, device size, isotope supply, or clinical safety. The final manuscript should rank these explanations only after decision records and market data are recovered.

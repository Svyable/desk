# Back Matter

## Evidence note

This manuscript treats the atomic pacemaker as a lifecycle-engineering history. It does not infer clinical safety merely from the absence of dramatic published harm, nor does it infer danger merely from curie-scale source activity. Claims about patient dose, source containment, generator survival, lost devices and long-term outcomes must remain attached to specific models, studies and regulatory records.

The strongest next archival task is a device-level registry linking manufacturer, model, serial number, source activity, implant date, patient age/indication, generator replacement reason, patient death, source recovery and final disposition. Without that denominator, striking long-lived cases and striking lost-source cases are both easy to overread.

## Author

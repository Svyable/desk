## Thesis

Plutonium-238 pacemakers were not an atomic-age gimmick that disappeared because the battery failed. The generators often performed exceptionally well. They disappeared because lithium batteries, low-power electronics and changing pacing modes moved the system bottleneck while radioisotope power retained a lifelong burden of licensing, patient tracking, recovery and disposal. The device is a historical experiment in what happens when one component is engineered to outlive the medicine, institutions and sometimes the patient around it.

## Author's Note

This book begins with a drawing.

It does not begin with the assumption that the drawing is sacred, supernatural, scientifically prophetic, or even correctly interpreted.

That distinction matters.

The historical symbols in these pages belong to traditions older and more complicated than any modern theory I could lay over them. Where I discuss those traditions, I will try to say what the sources support. Where I borrow the geometry as a modern conceptual device, I will say that too.

The point is not to prove chakras.

The point is to notice a structure.

A square. A crescent. A triangle. Two triangles occupying one center. A circle. An eye that turns back toward itself. A line looping through all of it.

Taken as geometry, the sequence offers a surprisingly good set of questions about how bounded matter becomes dynamic, how dynamics become directed, how directed systems integrate information, how integrated systems build models, and how some models eventually include the system doing the modeling.

That final turn is the problem this book is about.

Not consciousness as magic.

Not consciousness as a word we paste over ignorance.

Consciousness as the place where reality appears to bend back and acquire some form of access to itself.

That may be wrong.

But if it is wrong, it should be wrong in an interesting and testable way.

## Working closing note

The manuscript's ending will return to the source drawing after the scientific, philosophical, and historical arguments have had time to complicate it.

The final claim should remain narrower than a metaphysical proof:

Matter can be organized into systems that sense, model, model themselves, evaluate aspects of their own modeling, and act on the world using those representations.

Whether that architecture is identical to consciousness, necessary for consciousness, merely correlated with consciousness, or still missing the decisive ingredient is the question the completed book must leave sharper than it found it.

# Evidence ledger

Working research notes. This file is deliberately stricter than the prose. Claims do not move upward between evidence classes because they are narratively attractive.

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

### The Moscow electromagnetic signal existed

The National Security Archive’s 2022 document collections reproduce and index State Department, ARPA and related records. A 25 June 1967 memorandum records Secretary of State Dean Rusk raising an “electro-magnetic signal” aimed at the U.S. Embassy chancery with Soviet Foreign Minister Andrei Gromyko and explicitly saying the U.S. did not know its purpose.

Source: National Security Archive, *The Moscow Signals Declassified: Microwave Diplomacy, 1967–1977* (15 Sep 2022):
https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence-russia-programs/2022-09-15/moscow-signals-declassified-microwave

### PANDORA/BIZARRE studied possible microwave bioeffects

The National Security Archive’s companion collection indexes declassified program records and reports that PANDORA/BIZARRE investigated biological/behavioral effects of microwave exposure. It also documents a crucial parameter correction: an early primate experiment used CIA exposure readings later found too high; a 1967 ARPA update reported Moscow power density no greater than about 0.05 mW/cm² and said exposure at the corrected level did not degrade the primate’s work-task performance.

Source: National Security Archive, *The Moscow Signals Declassified: Microwave Mysteries: Projects PANDORA and BIZARRE* (13 Sep 2022):
https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence-russia-programs/2022-09-13/moscow-signals-declassified-microwave

Publication hold: recover the underlying ARPA/CIA documents page-by-page before manuscript gives exact experimental timing, waveform, animal count, or stronger causal interpretation.

### Later epidemiology did not establish a clear broad mortality excess

The 1978 Foreign Service health-status study is the primary object. A 2019 peer-reviewed review describes the official study as showing no apparent evidence of increased mortality and limited evidence regarding general health status, while arguing that methodological loose ends remain.

Source: José A. Martínez, “The ‘Moscow signal’ epidemiological study, 40 years on,” *Reviews on Environmental Health* 34(1), 2019, DOI 10.1515/reveh-2018-0061; PubMed:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/30676008/

Publication hold: obtain and extract the 1978 Lilienfeld report itself before chapter-level claims about sample size, person-years, outcome categories, exposure grouping, losses to follow-up, significance, or cancer subgroups.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Exact exposure history by floor, room, employee, year, frequency, waveform and duty cycle.
- Soviet operational purpose. Espionage/electronic explanations are plausible and have historical comparators, but purpose must not be promoted beyond the documentary record.
- Degree to which particular U.S. officials believed biological, behavioral, jamming, bug-activation, or collection hypotheses at specific dates.
- Long-latency health implications. Later methodological criticism deserves treatment but cannot be converted into proof of harm.
- Extent and rationale of undisclosed medical sampling. Reconstruct from primary memoranda and contracts before assigning motive.

## Class 3 — disputed allegations / unconfirmed claims

Do not state as fact:

- Moscow Signal was designed as a mind-control, incapacitation, or cancer weapon.
- PANDORA demonstrated an operational microwave behavioral-control capability.
- Ambassador Walter Stoessel’s leukemia or another named person’s illness was caused by the signal.
- A direct technical/programmatic line runs from Moscow Signal to later anomalous-health incidents.
- A specific Soviet passive bugging system explains all measured Moscow transmissions.
- U.S. officials proved harmful nonthermal effects and suppressed that proof.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

### Signal/effect/intent triangle

A reasoning tool: detection of a physical signal, demonstration of a biological effect, and inference of operator intent are separate evidentiary propositions. Support for one does not automatically populate the others.

### Causal overhang

Hypothesis: when a hidden event is later verified but its mechanism/purpose remain partly secret or unresolved, the verified core gives unsupported explanations unusually durable narrative support.

Defeat test: blind coders to classification status and compare explanatory proliferation around matched secret and public technical controversies. If secrecy status does not predict later unsupported causal accretion after controlling for salience and uncertainty, narrow or discard the concept.

### Secret-exposure debt

Hypothesis: classification and delayed disclosure degrade the joins required for later causal health inference—person × location × time × field × protection × outcome—beyond ordinary archival decay.

Defeat test: compare matched nonclassified occupational/environmental exposure cohorts from the same period. If reconstruction quality is no worse in classified cases, secrecy is not the causal variable.

## Contradiction ledger

1. **Concern vs result:** senior U.S. concern and research investment coexist with experimental/epidemiological evidence that did not cleanly establish the feared effect. Do not resolve this by choosing one side; it is the book.
2. **Early exposure estimate vs corrected estimate:** an early primate experiment reportedly used an exposure estimate materially above the later CIA estimate. Any positive result tied to the earlier setting cannot be silently mapped onto the embassy exposure.
3. **Secrecy vs informed health inference:** concealing the real purpose of sampling may have served counterintelligence/diplomatic aims while simultaneously damaging trust and later causal reconstruction.
4. **Standards vs demonstrated harm:** Soviet exposure standards reportedly differed from U.S. limits. A stricter standard is evidence about policy/precaution, not itself evidence of injury at the embassy dose.

## Incentive audit

- **Soviet state:** conceal collection/harassment technology and deny or normalize activity.
- **U.S. intelligence/defense:** understand purpose without revealing detection capability; avoid missing a novel weapon; protect sources/methods.
- **State Department:** protect staff while managing diplomacy, arms-control politics, press consequences and morale.
- **Researchers:** obtain funding and publish meaningful effects; also face pressure from sparse, classified, parameter-uncertain data.
- **Later advocates:** Moscow offers a documented precedent for directed electromagnetic exposure and therefore strong rhetorical leverage.
- **Later skeptics:** may overcorrect by treating weak evidence of harm as if it negated the documented signal itself.

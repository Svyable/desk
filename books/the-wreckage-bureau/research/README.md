# Research trail and evidentiary matrix

## Selected thesis

**The Wreckage Bureau: What real reverse engineering teaches us about crash-retrieval claims**

Thesis: documented foreign-materiel exploitation proves that states can secretly acquire, transport, test, disassemble, and operationally exploit adversary technology. Those same programs establish an empirical control group for modern allegations of recovered non-human technology. A long-running retrieval program should create an institutional metabolism—custody, logistics, technical work, records, contractor interfaces, security succession, and downstream consequences—whose expected footprint grows with the number of objects, years, bodies, facilities, and claimed technical successes.

## Evidence classes used in the manuscript

### Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- The U.S. acquired and flight-tested No Kum-Sok’s MiG-15bis after his September 21, 1953 defection.
- HAVE DOUGHNUT, HAVE DRILL, and HAVE FERRY were real classified exploitation programs involving Soviet-designed MiG aircraft.
- CONSTANT PEG operated a secret fleet of Soviet-designed fighters and trained U.S. aircrew against them.
- Project AZORIAN was a real CIA/DoD covert effort using the Hughes Glomar Explorer under a deep-sea-mining cover to retrieve part of Soviet submarine K-129.
- Army TECHINT doctrine and the 203rd MI Battalion explicitly cover collection/exploitation of foreign and captured enemy materiel.
- Technical-intelligence personnel deployed to Grenada in 1983 and shipped selected Soviet-origin materiel for deeper exploitation.
- The July 26, 2023 House UAP hearing occurred; David Grusch publicly made crash-retrieval and biologics allegations under oath.
- AARO published its 2024 historical report and states it found no empirical evidence of off-world technology exploitation.
- KONA BLUE existed as a proposal / prospective SAP effort; AARO says it was never approved, funded, or supplied with recovered material.
- ORNL analyzed the publicly disputed magnesium/zinc/bismuth specimen under AARO sponsorship and reported terrestrial origin and no support for the claimed terahertz-waveguide function.

### Class 2 — credible but incomplete reporting / testimony / assessment

- Grusch’s account that people he interviewed had direct knowledge of a crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering program. Publicly, the underlying firsthand evidence is incomplete.
- Grusch’s attribution of “non-human” biological assessment to people with direct knowledge. The public record does not include the biological material or a test corpus establishing origin.
- AARO’s negative conclusion regarding all named alleged hidden programs. AARO reports broad classified access and specific resolutions, but the public cannot independently inspect the full search space or all underlying sensitive records.
- Claims that specific contractors/facilities were involved where details may have been supplied in classified settings but are not established in open evidence.

### Class 3 — disputed allegations without independent confirmation

- U.S. possession of intact non-human craft.
- U.S. possession of non-terrestrial intelligent biological remains.
- An eight-decade illegal program concealed from lawful congressional oversight.
- Successful reverse engineering of non-human propulsion, energy, materials, or control systems.
- Specific aerospace companies possessing off-world hardware.
- Claims that familiar postwar technologies derive from recovered non-human technology absent a documented transfer chain.

### Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- A multi-object, multi-decade exploitation program should create “surface area” measurable through physical and administrative interfaces even when the core mission is classified.
- Technical memory creates a succession problem that makes an eighty-year program qualitatively different from a short covert operation.
- A genuinely exotic program might itself inherit a mistaken provenance narrative if later generations trust early classified conclusions without revalidating chain of custody.
- Sanitized technology transfer could obscure a source while still creating downstream research discontinuities; this is a testable historical hypothesis, not evidence that such transfer occurred.
- A dedicated “wreckage bureau” function could reduce both false-positive and false-negative errors by applying TECHINT, forensic custody, adversarial science, source genealogy, and secure oversight to anomalous material claims.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. **Real spectacular secrecy vs evidentiary restraint.** AZORIAN demonstrates that the U.S. can hide an enormous retrieval project. It does not increase the empirical probability of a non-human retrieval enough to substitute for evidence.

2. **Authentic black programs vs mistaken origin.** DIA/USAF history confirms secret foreign aircraft at Groom Lake. That strengthens the plausibility of restricted aerospace observations while also providing a mechanism for sincere UAP misidentification.

3. **Credible insider allegations vs public physical gap.** Grusch’s background and sworn testimony make the allegations investigatively important; publicly available evidence still does not establish craft, bodies, or non-human origin.

4. **AARO access claims vs trust/access disputes.** AARO says it had secure access to pertinent sensitive programs and resolved many allegations. Critics dispute adequacy and confidence in the process. Treat the report as strong counterevidence with auditable methodological questions, not as absolute proof of absence.

5. **Real KONA BLUE paperwork vs absent material.** The proposal contemplated reverse engineering hoped-for off-world craft. AARO says no material ever arrived. The case demonstrates that authentic classified planning language cannot be treated as evidence of possession.

6. **Material testability vs provenance weakness.** ORNL can characterize the specimen it received. AARO/ORNL explicitly note the long chain of custody cannot be verified. A mundane result applies to that specimen; it does not settle every debris claim.

7. **Contractor capability vs contractor-as-black-hole mythology.** Private firms can perform deeply classified work and served essential roles in AZORIAN. They also generate contracts, security relationships, facilities, personnel records, procurement, succession issues, and other interfaces.

## Falsification / confidence movers

Evidence that would materially raise confidence in a hidden anomalous retrieval program:

- firsthand technical witnesses whose access can be independently verified and whose accounts converge on specific physical facts without a shared source network;
- contemporaneous program records establishing actual receipt and custody rather than proposed/anticipated receipt;
- a specimen with continuous official chain of custody from a documented anomalous recovery;
- repeatable material or functional properties surviving adversarial independent laboratories and terrestrial controls;
- secure congressional or inspector-general verification of a specific hidden program’s mission and physical inventory;
- technical reports showing a progression from unknown object to reproducible principle with authentic provenance.

Evidence that would materially lower confidence:

- source genealogy showing major claims collapse onto a small circular reporting network;
- records resolving named programs to unrelated sensitive projects with strong time/place/person matching;
- key firsthand witnesses denying the attributed stories;
- alleged specimens failing provenance or adversarial testing;
- evidence that documents offered as proof describe proposals, simulations, or contingencies rather than operational possession;
- absence of expected custody/logistics/technical records after authorized investigators obtain genuinely comprehensive access.

## Research discipline

Patents are evidence of filing, not efficacy. Technical concepts are not operational systems. Testimony is attributed testimony, not proof. “Non-human” is not treated as synonymous with “extraterrestrial.” AARO statements are attributed to AARO. Grusch statements are attributed to Grusch. No program, clearance, craft, biological specimen, technical capability, or classified access is invented.

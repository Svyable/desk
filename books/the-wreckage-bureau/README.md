# The Wreckage Bureau

*What Real Reverse Engineering Teaches Us About Crash-Retrieval Claims*

The United States has unquestionably run secret programs to acquire, recover, hide, test, disassemble, and exploit foreign technology. It hid Soviet MiGs at Groom Lake. It operated a clandestine adversary-aircraft school. It built the Hughes Glomar Explorer behind a deep-sea-mining cover story to recover part of a Soviet ballistic-missile submarine.

*The Wreckage Bureau* uses those documented programs as a control group for modern allegations that the government recovered and reverse-engineered non-human technology. The thesis is neither “the government could never hide it” nor “AZORIAN proves anything is possible.” Real exploitation has a physical and administrative metabolism: acquisition path, custody, transport, measurement, security, contractors, records, succession, failures, and downstream consequences. The larger and older the alleged program, the stronger the expected footprint.

The book treats David Grusch’s 2023 testimony as serious attributed testimony, AARO’s 2024 historical report as serious official counterevidence, KONA BLUE as a particularly revealing case where authentic special-access planning did not establish possession, and materials claims as inseparable from chain of custody.

## Evidence posture

**Declassified-heavy / foreign-materiel-history-heavy / testimony-aware / skeptical of provenance inflation.**

The working evidence classes are: (1) verified/declassified/documented fact, (2) credible but incomplete reporting or testimony, (3) disputed allegation without independent confirmation, and (4) speculative synthesis/open conjecture.

See [`research/README.md`](research/README.md) for the source trail, claim matrix, contradictions, and explicit confidence movers.

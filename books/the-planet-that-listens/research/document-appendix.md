# Document Appendix

This appendix is a working map of documents that should be reconstructed directly in later passes. It deliberately distinguishes documents already inspected through authoritative summaries/OCR from records still needing full-page review.

## Vela 1979 core record

### Nuclear Explosion Panel charter, circa 1 November 1979

Status: public/declassified; OCR inspected through National Security Archive.

Use: establishes the White House scientific panel’s actual mandate: evaluate nuclear vs natural origin, technical malfunction/interference, corroborating classified/unclassified data and possible additional sources.

Link: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/media/22326/ocr

### White House Science and Technology staff, “Evening Report,” 9 November 1979

Status: public/declassified; description/OCR inspected.

Use: contemporaneous evidence that Ruina panel was leaning skeptical; records non-nuclear bhangmeter comparisons, absence of corroborative sampling and Arecibo ionospheric disturbance as strongest new lead.

Link: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/19604-national-security-archive-doc-3-white-house

### White House Situation Room, “Reflections of South African Nuclear Event,” 25 October 1979

Status: public/declassified; archive description inspected.

Use: traces debris search and reports Australian sheep-thyroid I-131 finding. Must inspect full PDF before any quantitative claim.

Link: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/19602-national-security-archive-doc-1-white-house

### DCI NI IIM 79-10028, “The 22 September 1979 Event,” December 1979

Status: declassified in multiple versions with inconsistent redactions.

Use: intelligence hypothesis space for actors and event interpretation. Publication pass must compare releases side by side before quoting or summarizing withheld sections.

Link: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/29884-document-6b-director-central-intelligence-ni-iim-79-10028-22-september-1979-event

### INR/PMA memorandum on Naval Research Laboratory analysis, 17 June 1980

Status: public/declassified; archive description inspected.

Use: documents Jack Varona/DIA criticism of Ruina panel and claimed hydroacoustic evidence. Must not be described as raw NRL proof without underlying record.

Link: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/22351-39-robert-martin-inr-pma-political-military

### Department of State Telegram 050226, 25 February 1980

Status: public/declassified.

Use: shows continuing sensitive discussion of possible Israeli involvement and Ruina’s personal contacts; evidence of concern, not proof of actor.

Link: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/22350-38a-department-state-telegram-050226-u-s

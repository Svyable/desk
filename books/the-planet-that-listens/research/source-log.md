## Source-boundary rules for this book

1. A declassified assessment proves an agency held an assessment; it does not prove the underlying event.
2. A program budget proves authorized work and priorities; it does not prove an adversary is performing the modeled activity.
3. A patent or proposed sensor concept is evidence of filing or inquiry, not efficacy.
4. “Nuclear-test-like” waveform evidence is not automatically proof of fission.
5. Radionuclide non-detection is negative evidence only in proportion to expected release, transport and station opportunity.
6. Claims that the 1979 Vela event was specifically an Israeli-South African joint test remain disputed unless a publication pass finds independently verifiable operational evidence beyond the current public record.
7. Scientific secondary applications of IMS data do not imply the IMS was designed for those applications.

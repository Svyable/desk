# Source Ledger

This ledger is the research trail for *Right To Try*. It favors primary law, regulator guidance, approval records, trial registries and original clinical reports. Secondary sources are included where they help identify contested legal interpretations, commercialization patterns or historical context; they are not used as substitutes for the underlying rule.

## Working rules for this ledger

- A source showing that a therapy was administered does **not** establish efficacy.
- A Phase 1 study establishes only the evidence actually measured in that study; it is not shorthand for “safe.”
- A press release can establish that an institution announced an event, but clinical claims should be checked against the study report, regulator record or peer-reviewed paper.
- A state statute can establish what the state authorizes; it does not establish that federal product law is displaced.
- COFEPRIS enforcement actions establish that Mexican health law exists and can be enforced; they do not establish how consistently every clinic is policed.
- A clinic’s statement that a treatment is “experimental,” “investigational,” “personalized” or “regenerative” is not accepted as a regulatory classification without corroboration.
- When a chapter uses an anecdote of recovery, it should state the evidentiary limits around causation, concurrent treatment and follow-up.
- Legal and regulatory claims are dated to the source vintage. This draft’s current-law cutoff is **August 30, 2026**.

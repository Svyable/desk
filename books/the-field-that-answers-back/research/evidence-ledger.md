# Evidence ledger — The Field That Answers Back

## Class 1 — verified / documented

General relativity's weak-field gravitomagnetic predictions; experimental frame-dragging measurements; gravitational redshift; equivalence-principle tests; established electromagnetic and condensed-matter cross-responses including Hall, piezoelectric, magnetostrictive, Barnett, Einstein–de Haas, London-moment, Josephson and thermoelectric effects; plasma electrodynamics; fusion energy and momentum bookkeeping.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

The methodological claim that a generalized response-tensor language may expose useful experimental commonalities across otherwise separate physical domains. The proposed use of clocks, resonant nuclei and phase-sensitive systems as especially clean monitors of hypothetical inertial/gravitational cross-response. These are research-program proposals, not established new laws.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

Claims of anomalous weight changes in superconductors, anomalous gravitomagnetic fields from rotating cryogenic matter, reactionless thrust, gravity shielding, craft-scale inertial suppression, or recovered non-human propulsion systems. None is treated here as independently established.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

Universal Response Dynamics itself: the conjecture that a compact generalized response matrix, constrained by symmetry, conservation, reciprocity and astronomy, could organize known couplings and expose previously unmeasured off-diagonal terms. The framework does not assert that any such new term is nonzero.

## Defeat conditions

URD becomes scientifically uninteresting if its notation only redescribes already-known constitutive relations without generating independent predictions; if proposed cross-response terms violate established symmetry or equivalence bounds; if all candidate anomalous sectors vanish under controlled replication; or if no parameter region survives existing laboratory and astrophysical constraints.

## Epistemic rule

No UAP or retrieval testimony is permitted to set the physics. A physical coupling must stand on reproducible measurement whether or not any anomalous craft claim is ever substantiated.

# Zoonosis: Nature’s Deadliest Diseases

**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson
**Status:** Drafting
**Chapters:** 18

## Thesis

Humanity keeps describing zoonotic epidemics as invasions from nature. The deeper record shows something more uncomfortable: pathogens cross species through ecological doors, but human systems decide how many doors exist, how wide they stand, and whether anyone is watching them.

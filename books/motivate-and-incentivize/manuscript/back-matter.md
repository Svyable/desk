# Back Matter

This back matter will hold source notes, methodological notes, and practical design questions as the manuscript develops.

The final edition should make it easy to distinguish documented cases, experimental findings, economic models, organizational interpretation, and Sven Hardy Benson's conclusions. Incentive effects are highly dependent on task, context, measurement, baseline motivation, and the design of the reward itself; the book should preserve those boundary conditions rather than turning a conditional literature into a universal rule.

The working source trail is maintained in [../research/README.md](../research/README.md).

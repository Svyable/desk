## Acknowledgments

This Desk draft draws on archival records of Cold War crisis communications, official strategy documents on diplomatic and alliance uses of artificial intelligence, technical provenance standards, and policy research on mediation, strategic stability, and information integrity. Errors of interpretation remain the author's.

## Note on sources and method

Chapter arguments prefer primary documents, official strategies, and named research publications over anonymous anecdote. American and Soviet records of the Cuban Missile Crisis are preserved in their disagreement rather than harmonized. Archived government webpages are treated as evidence of claims made at the time, not as proof of current policy without separate confirmation. Think-tank simulations and Track II dialogues are cited as research or dialogue evidence, not as descriptions of classified operational practice.

Invented dialogue, invented statistics, and unverified quotations are excluded by design. Where the manuscript discusses plausible future failure modes, it labels them as scenarios rather than as reported events.

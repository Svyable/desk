# The Last Human Channel

## Diplomacy at Machine Speed

Sven Hardy Benson

Working manuscript — Sven Hardy Benson’s Desk

© 2026 Sven Hardy Benson. All Rights Reserved.

Artificial intelligence can make analysis, translation, surveillance, drafting, simulation, and persuasion abundant. It cannot make authorized intent, political responsibility, or trusted relationships abundant on demand.

*The Last Human Channel* asks what happens to diplomacy when governments can see more, generate more options, communicate faster, and fabricate more convincingly than any foreign ministry in history. Its argument is not that machines will replace diplomats. It is that machine-speed statecraft raises the value of the institutions that tell governments when to slow down, how to authenticate a message, what a counterpart actually has authority to promise, and which human relationship can still carry meaning when every public channel is noisy.

The manuscript is organized around a simple shift in scarcity: from information to judgment, provenance, attention, and trust.

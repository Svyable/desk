# The Woodpecker — evidence ledger

## Working rule

No claim graduates because it is famous. For every technical or historical assertion, distinguish program existence, intended capability, demonstrated effect, operational reliability, and later interpretation.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- CIA public historical material documents U.S. awareness of Soviet strategic technical programs generally; the next pass must recover item-level CIA records specifically addressing Soviet OTH radar rather than relying on retrospective secondary summaries.
- The Russian Woodpecker was a repeatedly observed HF interference signal beginning in the 1970s, widely characterized by a roughly 10 Hz pulse repetition mode.
- Over-the-horizon HF radar is an established radar architecture that exploits ionospheric propagation to reach beyond geometric line of sight.
- Large Duga-associated antenna infrastructure survives near Chernobyl in Ukraine; Soviet/Russian technical history identifies the 5N32/Duga family with over-the-horizon missile-warning work.
- Contemporary Western engineering and amateur-radio communities identified radar as a serious explanation before the Soviet system became openly documented.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Exact transmitter power and EIRP. Published figures are frequently repeated without clean primary provenance and often confuse transmitter output with effective radiated power.
- Exact waveform modes across years and sites. A late-1980s FCC technical characterization is repeatedly cited in secondary literature; obtain the original study before using exact bandwidth/PRI numbers in publication prose.
- Detection performance against individual missile/space launches. Russian technical histories report successful prototype detections, but launch-by-launch denominators and operational false-alarm data are not yet assembled.
- The relationship among Mykolaiv prototypes, Chernobyl-area 5N32, Far East sites, and NATO reporting names. Terminology is inconsistent in open literature.
- Causes of shutdown: Oko satellite maturation, system limitations, Chernobyl effects, economics, political change, and Soviet collapse may all matter. Do not write a single-cause ending without records.

## Class 3 — disputed / unsupported at present

- Duga as a principal weather-control system.
- Duga as a population-scale mind-control or behavior-modification transmitter.
- Duga as an earthquake-generation system.
- Duga as a deliberate global jammer whose primary mission was interference rather than radar.
- Claims that proximity to the Chernobyl plant proves a causal or operational connection beyond power/logistics and geography.

These claims remain research objects because their provenance teaches us how secrecy and technical ambiguity generate folklore. They are not treated as hidden truths awaiting rhetorical rescue.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- **Audible secret:** a classified capability may be unable to conceal its physical exhaust while still concealing purpose, performance, and institutional context.
- **Dependency-to-agency inversion:** when a system depends on an environmental phenomenon (ionosphere), rumor may reinterpret it as a system designed to control that phenomenon (weather).
- **Frequency resemblance trap:** numerical similarity between a transmitter pulse rate and a biological rhythm can generate causal narratives without a demonstrated coupling chain.
- **Parallel inference:** civilian technical communities and state intelligence services can independently infer the same hidden system from different sensors and with different confidence.

## Contradiction ledger

1. **Secret vs globally audible.** Resolution: secrecy can attach to purpose/performance rather than existence.
2. **Huge infrastructure vs uncertain effectiveness.** Resolution: scale demonstrates investment, not achieved performance.
3. **Radar identification vs persistent exotic theories.** Research question: which theories persisted after radar attribution, and why?
4. **Powerful transmitter vs biological-effect claims.** Required bridge: source power → antenna gain → propagation → field at subject → absorption → mechanism → controlled effect. Missing links cannot be replaced by adjectives.
5. **Chernobyl proximity vs shutdown chronology.** Need site-specific operational records before claiming the 1986 accident ended Duga.

## Incentive / disinformation audit

- Soviet state: incentive to conceal strategic-warning architecture and performance weaknesses.
- Western intelligence: incentive to conceal collection capabilities and knowledge state.
- Amateur operators: incentive to identify and stop interference; technically useful but not institutionally neutral.
- Journalists/documentarians: incentive toward dramatic explanation and visually powerful Chernobyl linkage.
- Post-Soviet memoirists/institutions: incentives around prestige, blame, nostalgia, tourism, and selective disclosure.
- Conspiracy entrepreneurs: incentive to preserve unfalsifiable exotic claims after conventional attribution.

Do not call a claim “disinformation” without evidence of actor, dissemination channel, timing and intended target.

## Primary / authoritative source spine

### Immediate targets

- CIA Reading Room / CREST: Soviet over-the-horizon radar, ionospheric research, STEEL WORK/STEEL YARD terminology, 1960s–1980s technical intelligence.
- U.S. FCC: original 1988 Woodpecker technical study or associated interference proceedings.
- ITU / IFRB records: complaints, frequency coordination and Soviet responses.
- IEEE Proceedings / Spectrum: contemporary OTH radar engineering, especially Headrick and Skolnik; distinguish generic OTH physics from Duga-specific claims.
- Soviet/Russian NIIDAR histories and technical memoirs: 5N77/5N32 development, designers, site chronology, prototype tests, performance limitations.
- Amateur Radio Relay League and contemporary monitoring publications: dated signal measurements and interference reports.
- U.S. early-warning intelligence histories: what was known before public Woodpecker reporting.
- Oko satellite program histories: replacement/complementarity chronology.

### Web sources used only to establish the first-pass research direction

- CIA public history pages are useful for methodological comparators on classified technical programs, but no CIA page located in the first web pass was sufficient by itself to establish Duga performance.
- Current secondary Duga summaries are discovery aids, not final evidence. Exact power, dimensions, NATO names, launch detections, and shutdown causes must be upgraded before publication.

2. Recover original FCC 1988 signal characterization; reproduce waveform table with units and measurement dates.
3. Build a year-by-year frequency/pulse-mode ledger from contemporary monitoring reports.
4. Build a site ledger: receiver, transmitter, coordinates, construction, activation, modification, shutdown, source confidence.
5. Build a launch-detection denominator from Soviet/Russian records: target launch, date, expected geometry, detected/not detected, source.
6. Recover false-alarm and auroral/polar-propagation evidence.
7. Quantify generic OTH radar physics with a worked example and explicit assumptions.
8. Trace weather-control, mind-control and earthquake claims to their earliest identifiable sources.
9. Compare Duga mythology with later HAARP narratives without implying equivalence.
10. Reconstruct Chernobyl-accident effects on staffing, contamination, access, power and operational status.
11. Reconstruct Oko maturation and test whether it actually displaced Duga operationally.
12. Interview/archive route: NIIDAR engineers, Soviet radar veterans, amateur monitoring groups, spectrum regulators.

## Publication gate

The present manuscript is structural, not complete. Before publication: all 18 chapters must be expanded through evidence; exact technical numbers need primary provenance; Soviet-side sources need translation and cross-checking; the exotic-claim chapters need provenance rather than ridicule; and the final argument must survive a falsification pass asking whether the “observable but not legible” distinction adds explanatory value beyond a generic secrecy story.

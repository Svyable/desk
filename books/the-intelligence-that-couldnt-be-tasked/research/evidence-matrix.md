## Defeat condition

The manuscript's central synthesis should be abandoned or substantially rewritten if either of two evidentiary developments occurs:

- a full, protocol-level reconstruction demonstrates that the apparently positive controlled remote-viewing database is adequately explained by identifiable sensory leakage, target dependencies, selection/publication effects, analytic flexibility, non-independence, or other ordinary mechanisms; or
- independent preregistered laboratories demonstrate reproducible target-specific information under strong information-security controls **and** a prospective operational trial shows calibrated decision advantage after false leads, verification cost and opportunity cost are counted.

The first result would collapse the “possible weak anomaly” side of the thesis. The second would collapse the “couldn't become a capability” side.

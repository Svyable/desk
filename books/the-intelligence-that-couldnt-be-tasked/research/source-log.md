# Source Log

Checked for this working draft on 2026-09-13. Links below are public and lawfully available. A source's inclusion records provenance; it does not endorse every claim inside it.

## Primary / official program record

- CIA, “Ask Molly: Did CIA Really Study Psychic Powers?” — useful current agency summary of the CIA→DIA→CIA history and the 1995 independent evaluation. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-did-cia-really-study-psychic-powers/
- CIA FOIA, *Transcript Remote Viewing Session 836*, 17 Dec. 1981 — example of the operational-report format and explicit “raw intelligence” handling language. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r000700070001-8
- CIA FOIA, *INSCOM GRILL FLAME Program Session Report*, session DC-93 — another explicit raw-intelligence disclaimer and protocol reference. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r000600030001-3
- CIA FOIA, *Summary Remote Viewing Session XCI* — useful positive training example complicated by erroneous coordinates: the report says the viewer described the geography associated with the coordinates rather than the intended Offutt AFB target. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r000900970001-7
- CIA FOIA, *Summary Remote Viewing Session LIII* — official training record stating “absolutely no target correlation.” https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r000900390001-1
- CIA FOIA, *Perceptual Augmentation Techniques, Part Two—Research Report*, Puthoff & Targ, 1 Dec. 1975. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00791r000300030003-0
- CIA FOIA STAR GATE collection landing/search surface. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/collection/stargate
- CIA FOIA, CENTER LANE program records and transfer material. Search/document set begins at: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/collection/stargate
- Defense Intelligence Agency, 1984 psychoenergetics/GRILL FLAME summary report, available through the CIA STAR GATE collection. Treat its statements that remote viewing is “real,” trainable, shield-independent, and potentially useful as the program authors' conclusions, not independent establishment of those propositions.
- SRI, *Final Report: Review of the Psychoenergetic Research Conducted at SRI International (1973–1988)*, 1989, available in CIA STAR GATE collection. Important because it reports a large aggregate positive database while also stating that quantitative evidence did not support the training hypothesis.
- American Institutes for Research, Michael D. Mumford, Andrew M. Rose, David A. Goslin et al., *An Evaluation of Remote Viewing: Research and Applications*, 29 Sept. 1995, CIA FOIA PDF: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00791R000200180006-4.pdf
- Edwin C. May, Jessica M. Utts & S. James P. Spottiswoode, *Decision Augmentation Theory: Toward a Model of Anomalous Mental Phenomena*, 16 May 1995, CIA FOIA: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00791r000200280002-5

## Source-use rules for expansion

1. Page-index every operational or evaluative claim against the primary report before publication.
2. Keep program-authored conclusions distinct from independent findings.
3. Do not use the CIA's current popular summary as a substitute for the 1995 AIR report where exact methodology or counts matter.
4. Reconstruct celebrated operational cases from contemporaneous tasking/session/feedback records before using later memoirs.
5. For Soviet/Russian/Chinese claims, locate the originating foreign-language or intelligence record rather than citing U.S. threat summaries recursively.
6. Patents, if later added, establish filing only, never efficacy.
7. Preserve nulls and failed sessions with the same specificity used for positive examples.

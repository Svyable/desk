# Quantitative Budget — What Must Be Multiplied

A useful bioprecipitation budget cannot begin with the phrase “bacteria are efficient ice nuclei.” It needs at least the following factors:

1. **Source flux** — active biological particles emitted per area per time from vegetation, soil, freshwater, ocean, rain splash, agricultural activity and other sources.
2. **Survival/activity fraction** — fraction retaining relevant ice-nucleating activity after UV, desiccation, oxidation, pollutants and transport.
3. **Vertical delivery** — fraction reaching cloud-relevant altitudes before deposition or scavenging.
4. **Droplet incorporation** — fraction entering droplets rather than remaining interstitial aerosol.
5. **Temperature activation spectrum** — cumulative active sites per particle or per volume over cloud temperature history.
6. **Competitor field** — mineral dust, soot, fungal, pollen, marine and other INPs active in the same droplets/temperature range.
7. **Microphysical leverage** — change in ice number, glaciation timing, riming, aggregation or phase partitioning caused by the biological increment.
8. **Dynamical conversion** — fraction of microphysical perturbation that survives cloud dynamics to affect precipitation amount, timing or location.
9. **Biological return** — viable cells or lineage-benefiting material deposited into habitats where reproduction occurs.
10. **Reloading gain** — additional active material emitted because of the precipitation/deposition event.

The strong feedback requires the product of these factors to be non-negligible. A spectacular value for factor 5 cannot compensate automatically for near-zero factors elsewhere.

## Empirical anchors

- The 2017 Virginia precipitation survey reported confirmed ice-active bacterial concentrations varying widely across events, including values up to the thousands of culturable units per liter of precipitation.
- The 2019 Louisiana study reported biological INP concentrations in some snow/sleet samples approaching ~16,000 INPs per liter of precipitation active at temperatures warmer than or equal to −10 °C.
- Arctic PBAP observations in 2023 reported roughly 10^-3 to 10^-1 particles per liter of air at the studied mountain site, with strong summertime/source correlations.
- Aircraft sampling over the British Isles found that mineral dust alone could not explain all measured INPs, supporting a biological contribution aloft without assigning a universal percentage.

These units are not interchangeable. INPs per liter of precipitation, culturable cells per liter, and particles per liter of air answer different questions. The next manuscript pass should resist normalizing them into one table until sampling efficiencies and activation temperatures are matched.

## Prospective calculation

For each field campaign, compute an upper and lower bound on biological contribution to initial ice using measured INP spectra and cloud liquid water. Then perturb only the biological fraction in a constrained cloud model. Report the concentration regime where cloud response first becomes distinguishable from meteorological ensemble spread.

That threshold is more useful than a generic statement that biological INPs “may affect precipitation.” It says what abundance nature must actually supply.

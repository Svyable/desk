## Claim discipline

- Patents or commercial claims are evidence of proposed use, not atmospheric efficacy.
- Cloud-seeding operational reports require controlled/statistical attribution before being used as causal precipitation evidence.
- Detection of an organism in precipitation is evidence of presence and transport, not proof that it initiated precipitation.
- Model sensitivity to biological INPs is conditional on source and activation parameterization unless field-validated.

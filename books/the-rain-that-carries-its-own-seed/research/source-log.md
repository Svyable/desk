## Ice-minus biotechnology and regulation

- U.S. EPA, 14 Nov 1985. “EPA Approves First Use in Environment of Genetically Altered Bacteria.” Archived official press release: https://www.epa.gov/archive/epa/aboutepa/epa-approves-first-use-environment-genetically-altered-bacteria.html — AGS experimental-use permits and the intended strawberry mechanism; this is approval, not the date of the eventual 1987 release.
- U.S. EPA, *Federal Register* 50(233), 4 Dec 1985, pp. 49760–49762. Experimental-use permit notice: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1985-12-04/pdf/FR-1985-12-04.pdf — Specifies separate AGS Ice− *P. syringae* and *P. fluorescens* permits, viable-cell limits, 0.1-acre treated areas and research-only crop restrictions.
- *Foundation on Economic Trends v. Heckler*, 756 F.2d 143 (D.C. Cir. 1985): https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/F2/756/143/162040/ — Concerns the separate UC Berkeley Lindow–Panopoulos deliberate-release proposal, not the AGS Frostban strawberry trial. The court affirmed an injunction until NIH completed an adequate environmental assessment, emphasizing inadequate treatment of dispersion and possible ecological consequences. It did not find that Ice− bacteria had been proved environmentally harmful.
- U.S. EPA, *EPA Journal* 12(8), Oct 1986: https://nepis.epa.gov/Exe/ZyPURL.cgi?Dockey=93000ELG.TXT — EPA distinguishes the UC potato and AGS strawberry experiments and records that AGS had conducted outdoor work before receiving appropriate permission; EPA described the conduct as improper and illegal and fined the company. Use as regulatory history, not evidence that the release caused environmental harm.
- U.S. Congress, Office of Technology Assessment, 1988. *New Developments in Biotechnology, 3: Field-Testing Engineered Organisms: Genetic and Ecological Issues*, OTA-BA-350. Digital government-document copy: https://digital.library.unt.edu/ark:/67531/metadc39908/ ; Appendix A scan: https://www.princeton.edu/~ota/disk2/1988/8816/881609.PDF — Separates the two 1987 California events: AGS began its Brentwood strawberry field test on 24 April; the UC Lindow–Panopoulos potato test commenced near Tulelake on 29 April with 2,000 inoculated tubers and 2,000 controls. ## Claim discipline

- Patents or commercial claims are evidence of proposed use, not atmospheric efficacy.
- Cloud-seeding operational reports require controlled/statistical attribution before being used as causal precipitation evidence.
- Detection of an organism in precipitation is evidence of presence and transport, not proof that it initiated precipitation.
- Model sensitivity to biological INPs is conditional on source and activation parameterization unless field-validated.
- Keep the AGS Frostban strawberry trial, the UC Lindow–Panopoulos potato trial and Lindow's 1987 chemical-mutant competitive-exclusion paper distinct; they answer related but non-identical questions.

## Research

- [Research trail, chapter source map, and falsification notes](research/README.md)

This is a book about calibrated self-recognition: how to notice real evidence of progress, competence, trust, resilience, and belonging without confusing those signals with vanity, certainty, or permanent arrival. It treats self-assessment as an evidence problem rather than a confidence performance.

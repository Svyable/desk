**Checked:** September 3, 2026

## Claim discipline

No web search can prove that a human idea has literally never been expressed anywhere. ## What surfaced

The scan surfaced scattered essays and familiar phrases about life having no undo button, gaming and failure tolerance, AI and rough drafts, and software retry functionality. Those are adjacent motifs, not the same cross-domain book architecture.

A separate candidate idea — **The Waiting Class**, about inequality in who is forced to wait — was deliberately discarded after the scan surfaced Stephen R. Christ's June 2026 *Theory and Society* article, **“Institutional time inequality: speed capital and the social distribution of waiting.”** That article already develops a close version of the proposed conceptual center. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s11186-026-09730-x

The collision is documented here to show that the originality screen changed the project rather than being used as marketing theater.

## Distinctive combination in this manuscript

The draft's originality rests less on any single observation than on the combination and vocabulary:

1. **The retry as a cultural condition** rather than merely a software feature or gaming mechanic.
2. **Reversible thinking** as a learned cognitive posture.
3. **Sandbox / workshop / cliff** as a general consequence-literacy model.
4. **Retry inflation** as an abundance problem created when generating alternatives becomes nearly free.
5. **Retry infrastructure** as a way to think about social second chances without pretending consequences vanish.
6. **Retry asymmetry** as a test for high-stakes AI: the system may be easy to rerun while the human consequence is not.
7. **Option obedience** as the point where preserving optionality begins to prevent a life from compounding.
8. **Integration** as the alternative to both permanent shame and fictional reset.
9. **Mercy for the reversible; reverence for the irreversible** as the book's governing ethic.

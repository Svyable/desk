# Chapter 17 — The Event Physics Standard

The phrase *unidentified anomalous phenomenon* is bureaucratically useful and scientifically unfinished.

A phenomenon becomes researchable when the report can be translated into measured variables.

Time with uncertainty.

Observer position.

Sensor position and attitude.

Line of sight.

Range or explicit absence of range.

Angular size at native resolution.

Radiance by wavelength.

Thermal behavior.

Radar parameters.

RF emissions.

Weather.

Lightning.

Ionospheric and geomagnetic context.

Known aircraft, balloon and satellite traffic.

Processing history.

Witness cueing.

Only then should the case receive a physical model.

This is the event-physics standard.

It sounds obvious because every mature experimental field already works this way. The anomaly domain often cannot because the event is discovered after it is over. That is precisely why retrospective case accumulation eventually hits diminishing returns.

There will always be another story with missing range.

The frontier lies in prospective collection.

A useful future database should therefore store claims separately from measurements. "Object accelerated" belongs in the claim layer until range and timing support a physical acceleration. "Sphere" belongs in the morphology layer until a boundary is resolved. "Radar confirmed" belongs in the provenance layer until the radar return and processing are available.

This structure would prevent a common contamination: conclusions migrating into raw fields.

It would also allow explicit evidence classes.

**Class 1: verified/documented.** Raw or calibrated measurements, official records, reproducible natural phenomena, established sensor behavior.

**Class 2: credible but incomplete.** Skilled witness reports, partial multi-sensor records, official assessments lacking releasable raw data, plausible but not decisive correlations.

**Class 3: disputed/unconfirmed.** Claims of extraordinary performance without adequate range, alleged classified conclusions without documents, attribution based mainly on testimony.

**Class 4: speculative synthesis.** Plasma models for particular residual cases, foreign breakthrough technology, non-human intelligence, novel atmospheric mechanisms.

The classes are not moral rankings.

They are distances from direct evidence.

A class-four hypothesis can become class one if the world cooperates.

A class-one document can contain a class-four claim.

That last distinction is crucial. A declassified memo can verify that an analyst believed something. It does not verify the thing believed.

This is how the field grows up without becoming dull.

It gives wonder somewhere to go.

# The Electric Sky

## What Atmospheric Plasma Can — and Cannot — Explain About Unidentified Aerial Phenomena

**Sven Hardy Benson**

---

## Author's note

This is a book about an error made in both directions.

One side looks at an unexplained light and promotes it too quickly into technology. The other looks at the same light and demotes it too quickly into weather.

The atmosphere deserves more respect than either move gives it.

Within living scientific memory, transient luminous events above thunderstorms moved from scattered pilot reports into a measured zoology of sprites, elves, blue jets and gigantic jets. Ball lightning remains a serious but incompletely resolved physical problem. Hessdalen has produced recurring luminous events under long-running observation. Radar propagation, infrared imaging and computational processing can each manufacture apparent behavior that looks mechanical before the underlying event has been physically bounded.

None of those facts proves that atmospheric plasma explains the hard UAP cases.

They establish a control group.

The question pursued here is narrower: **what observable features should a natural atmospheric-electrical explanation produce, what should make that explanation fail, and what measurement architecture would finally separate luminous weather from vehicles, sensor artifacts and genuinely unknown phenomena?**

I use four evidence classes throughout:

1. **Verified / documented:** official records, reproducible laboratory results, established atmospheric phenomena, direct public measurements.
2. **Credible but incomplete:** serious field reports, partial instrument correlations, official assessments whose underlying data are incomplete or unavailable.
3. **Disputed / unconfirmed:** specific extraordinary attributions, alleged capabilities, mechanism claims without independent confirmation.
4. **Speculative synthesis / open conjecture:** proposed bridges between evidence streams that should be treated as hypotheses, not findings.

A document can be class one evidence that a class-four hypothesis was considered.

That sentence will do more work than any disclosure hearing.

No non-public classified material was used or implied. Project Condign is treated as a released intelligence assessment, not as experimental proof of plasma UAP. Hessdalen is treated as a recurring instrumented anomaly site, not evidence of non-human craft. AARO and NASA material is used according to the claims those institutions actually make.

The book is deliberately willing to let plasma lose.

If a case establishes a rigid body, range, three-dimensional trajectory and dynamics incompatible with an atmospheric process, the natural hypothesis should be discarded.

If the data never establish those things, extraordinary kinematics should not be smuggled in through adjectives.

That is the bargain.

# IRON RADIO

## How a Protein Became a Remote Control

**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson

## Thesis

Magnetogenetics is a better anomaly story than most anomalies because the disputed thing can be built, measured, broken, and rebuilt. Reports that weak magnetic or radiofrequency fields can modulate ferritin-coupled ion channels collided with a brutal physical objection: ordinary magnetothermal and magnetomechanical forces from ferritin appear far too small. Yet later work proposed and experimentally supported a slower biochemical route through iron redistribution, reactive oxygen species, oxidized lipids, and TRP-channel activation. The useful question is not whether magnetism secretly controls minds. It is what science should do when a reported biological effect survives longer than the first mechanism invented to explain it.

## Evidence posture

1. **Verified/documented:** peer-reviewed ferritin magnetogenetics experiments exist; major physical critiques exist; failed independent replications exist; FeRIC studies report RF-linked biochemical and electrophysiological effects.
2. **Credible but incomplete:** some ferritin-coupled systems may respond reproducibly under tightly specified biological and RF conditions. Generality, effect size, parameter dependence, and independent multisite reproduction remain incomplete.
3. **Disputed/unconfirmed:** broad claims that Magneto/ferritin constructs provide simple, robust, noninvasive magnetic neuronal remote control; claims that ordinary ferritin heating or force explains early results.
4. **Speculative synthesis:** `mechanism migration`, `energy-route substitution`, and the `remote-control tax` developed in this book. These are analytical devices, not established scientific terms.

No result in this book establishes covert mind control, natural telepathy, paranormal signaling, or operational intelligence use.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- Brooks et al., *Magnetic Resonance in Medicine* (1998), ferritin relaxometry/magnetometry: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/9702704/
- Hernández-Morales et al., *Cell Reports* (2020), reports RF/ferritin-associated iron redistribution, ROS/lipid oxidation and activation of ferritin-tagged TRP channels: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32160534/
- Hernández-Morales et al., *J Neurosci* (2024), reports electrophysiological validation in cultured neurons expressing FeRIC channels, including membrane-resistance/potential and spiking changes: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38777598/
- The 2024 paper itself states the field has faced mechanism controversy and reproducibility deficits.
- Review literature records independent replication failures for several early magnetogenetic constructs and substantial skepticism over ferritin heating/force mechanisms: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8830762/ and https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5470037/
- Meister 2016 physical critique and subsequent theory papers must be extracted directly before quantitative publication claims are frozen.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- FeRIC may constitute a reproducible RF-sensitive engineered biological system under bounded conditions.
- The biochemical iron/ROS/lipid pathway may explain some FeRIC observations better than simple magnetothermal or magnetomechanical stories.
- Protocol sensitivity—iron loading, ferritin expression, channel expression, redox state—may account for some interlaboratory variation. This is a testable explanation, not a blanket excuse for failed replication.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Robust general replication of early Magneto / ferritin remote-neural-control claims.
- Simple ferritin nanoparticle heating or force as sufficient mechanism for early effects.
- Translation from cultured-cell or selected animal experiments to arbitrary deep-brain human control.
- Any operational intelligence, military, covert behavioral-control or paranormal application.
- Any inference that magnetogenetics explains Havana syndrome, telepathy, psychotronics, UAP-associated effects, or historical mind-control allegations.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

### Mechanism migration
An observed effect can outlive its original mechanism. A replacement mechanism earns attention only if it predicts interventions that distinguish it from artifact and from the failed mechanism.

### Energy-route substitution
In a powered biological system, the external field may act as a perturbation rather than supply the full downstream work. This is not free energy: the endogenous chemical gradients and reactions must be explicitly included in the system boundary.

### Remote-control tax
Every remote-control claim owes transmitter, receiver, coupling, delivered dose and discriminating output. Neural/behavioral claims owe additional causal rungs.

## Contradictions / incentives

- Positive groups may have intellectual and patent incentives; note the patent-interest disclosure in the 2024 J Neurosci paper.
- Negative replications may mismatch construct, expression, iron state, RF apparatus or endpoint; mismatch must be quantified rather than invoked rhetorically.
- Protocol complexity can become an unfalsifiable escape hatch if every null is retrospectively attributed to an uncontrolled biological variable.
- A plausible new mechanism does not retroactively validate old behavioral data.
- A physically implausible proposed mechanism does not logically prove the reported observation false; it changes the replication burden.

## Quantitative extraction burden

For each key experiment extract: construct; cell/animal type; sample size and exclusions; field type; frequency; amplitude; gradient; coil geometry; distance; exposure duration/duty cycle; bulk and local temperature controls; sham/blinding; ferritin iron load; channel expression; ROS/lipid assay; latency; Ca2+ effect; membrane resistance; membrane potential; spike rate; behavioral endpoint; effect size; replication site; preregistration; raw-data availability.

## Publication holds

Do not publish numerical force/heating gaps until the original physical critiques and exact assumptions are page-checked. Do not call FeRIC independently replicated until laboratory independence and construct/protocol equivalence are established. Do not use `nonthermal` as shorthand for `not involving energy`; specify the measured thermal controls and proposed biochemical route. Do not describe behavior as mind control.

## Defeat condition

The book's central `mechanism migration` case weakens sharply if blinded, multisite experiments using frozen constructs and calibrated RF exposures fail to reproduce FeRIC electrophysiology, or if construct-positive and construct-negative preparations respond equivalently after thermal/electromagnetic artifact controls. The biochemical model specifically fails if predicted pathway interventions—iron dependence, ROS/lipid perturbation and channel dependence—do not alter RF-linked electrophysiological effects in the preregistered direction.

A strong positive result must survive hostile replication. A strong null must demonstrate parameter closure rather than merely use the same nouns.

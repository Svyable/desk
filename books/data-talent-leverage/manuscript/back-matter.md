# Back Matter

This back matter will hold source notes, case-study references, data definitions, and the practical operating tools that emerge from the manuscript.

The final edition should distinguish company statements from independent reporting, empirical research from interpretation, measured productivity effects from projections, and legitimate data advantage from access that depends on unclear rights or surveillance. It should also maintain the book's central counterfactual: if the same result can be explained by capital scale, market power, regulation, luck, or ordinary management, the manuscript should not credit a data-talent-leverage loop without evidence.

The working source trail is maintained in [../research/README.md](../research/README.md).

# Back Matter

## Evidence posture

This manuscript uses four evidence classes.

**Class 1 — verified/documented.** SG-3's drilling history and 12,262 m maximum depth; published Kola lithology, temperature, seismic, fluid and mineralogical results; the Kola-92 seismic work; the 2020 deep-fluid gold-nanoparticle study; documented modern Chinese ultradeep drilling.

**Class 2 — credible but incomplete.** Interpretations of specific deep reflectors as fracture/fluid structures; reconstruction of institutional motives and operational history from later interviews and historical scholarship; the degree to which particular Kola results generalize to other continental crust.

**Class 3 — disputed/unconfirmed.** Claims that the project was terminated because it encountered a forbidden discovery; authenticated 'sounds from Hell'; supernatural cavities; claims that public scientific accounts are cover stories for an undisclosed event.

**Class 4 — speculative synthesis.** Kola as a general model for the epistemology of direct access; the proposition that comparative >10 km drilling networks will systematically expose classes of inversion error; the idea that opacity plus real technical extremity creates a predictable ecology for anomaly folklore.

## Defeat conditions

The book's synthesis should be revised if primary Soviet records establish a materially different project history than the public scientific and engineering record; if authenticated contemporary Kola documentation supports any extraordinary termination event; if reanalysis shows that the major seismic interpretations described here were not actually revised by drilling; or if modern comparative ultradeep programs demonstrate that Kola's fluid, thermal and reflector lessons were unusually local and poor guides to other crustal settings.

## Research priority

The strongest next version of this book should recover and translate the 1984 Kozlovsky volume and Soviet drilling proceedings; build a depth-indexed Kola data table linking lithology, core recovery, temperature, porosity, fluids, seismic velocity and branch history; reconstruct the complete Kola-92 experiment; trace the Well-to-Hell transmission chain through original Finnish/Norwegian/U.S. media records; and compare Kola quantitatively with KTB, SAFOD, Chinese >10 km wells and other ICDP/IODP direct-access programs.

## Author's Note

Every company name, executive quotation, dollar figure, court filing, regulatory action, and dataset in this book is drawn from public reporting, company and regulatory filings, court dockets, central bank and supervisory publications, and academic research current as of September 2026, cited in the research trail that accompanies this manuscript. Where the evidence is contested or the outcome is still unfolding, the text says so. Private credit is a live story; some of the funds, ratios, and even bankruptcies described here will have moved by the time you read this. The mechanism the book describes — how a market prices, or declines to price, debt that trades by phone call instead of by ticker — should still hold.

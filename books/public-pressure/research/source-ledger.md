## Release-pass checklist

For every chapter before release:

- confirm names, titles, dates, monetary figures, procedural posture, and current regulatory status;
- replace secondary descriptions with primary sources wherever practical;
- preserve meaningful company/defendant responses to allegations;
- identify the exact source supporting every direct quotation;
- label reported-loss figures as reported losses rather than total incidence;
- distinguish AI, machine learning, rules-based automation, statistical models, and generative AI when the technical distinction affects the claim;
- re-check U.S. federal policy, EU AI Act implementation, pending copyright litigation, Medicare Advantage investigations, OpenAI governance, and any named executive role because those facts can change rapidly;
- record **What would make this false?** beside every major causal claim.

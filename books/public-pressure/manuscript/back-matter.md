# Back Matter

*Public Pressure* is a working nonfiction draft. The manuscript deliberately separates allegations from findings, regulatory allegations from final orders, filed lawsuits from adjudicated claims, and public reaction from evidence about institutional conduct.

For a reader-facing guide to the principal cases, rules, agreements, enforcement matters, and evidentiary cautions used throughout the book, see [Notes on Cases and Sources](notes-on-cases-and-sources.md). The complete working research trail lives in `../research/`.

Before a Shelf release, every fast-moving factual claim should receive a final date-and-status check, especially pending litigation, AI regulation, executive leadership, federal policy, and enforcement matters that may have changed since this August 2026 draft.

The book’s governing standard is simple: criticism should become more precise as the stakes rise. A person should not be treated as a corporation, a model should not be treated as a person, a complaint should not be treated as a verdict, and institutional complexity should not be used to make responsibility disappear.

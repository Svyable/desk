# The Vacuum Engineers

**Subtitle:** Hal Puthoff, Zero-Point Energy, and the Physics at the Edge of Empty Space

**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson

## Thesis

Real quantum-vacuum phenomena, speculative models of inertia and gravity, NASA breakthrough-propulsion research, and DIA-funded metric-engineering studies have been repeatedly collapsed into one story about hidden propulsion technology. This book pulls them apart and asks what, if anything, remains experimentally actionable.

## Evidence posture

Primary/peer-reviewed-heavy where the physics permits; government-document-heavy for institutional history; explicitly skeptical of technology-by-association. Four classes are maintained: documented facts; credible but incomplete evidence; disputed allegations; speculative synthesis.

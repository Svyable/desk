# Evidence Matrix and Source Log

## Class 1 — verified / documented / directly established

- Quantum electrodynamics contains measurable vacuum-related phenomena; Casimir forces have an extensive experimental literature.
- Puthoff, Haisch and Rueda published peer-reviewed work proposing a zero-point-field contribution to inertia. Publication establishes the proposal, not its correctness.
- A later peer-reviewed critical analysis by Yefim S. Levin concluded that the Lorentz-force contribution used in the Haisch–Rueda–Puthoff inertia model vanishes under the relativistically consistent treatment he analyzed: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.79.012114
- Puthoff published a 1989 proposal for a source of electromagnetic zero-point energy, then a 1990 erratum; Wesson and Santos published mathematical/physical criticisms in 1991, and Puthoff replied. The correction history is part of the evidence, not a footnote: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.41.2902 ; https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.44.3379 ; https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.44.3383
- Puthoff published a polarizable-vacuum gravity model with weak-field correspondences and strong-field differences from standard general relativity.
- Alcubierre's 1994 metric is a published solution of Einstein's equations for a specified exotic stress-energy distribution: https://doi.org/10.1088/0264-9381/11/5/001
- Pfenning and Ford applied quantum-inequality constraints to the original Alcubierre geometry and found physically extreme wall-thickness and energy requirements under their assumptions: https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/9702026
- Ford/Roman/Pfenning quantum-inequality work constrains negative-energy distributions relevant to wormholes and warp concepts: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.57.4839
- Bobrick and Martire published a 2021 generalized treatment including subluminal positive-energy warp shells and emphasizing that a warp shell still requires propulsion: https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6382/abdf6e
- Erik Lentz published a 2021 positive-energy hyper-fast soliton construction in an Einstein–Maxwell–plasma framework: https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6382/abe692
- Santiago, Schuster and Visser published a 2022 analysis arguing that generic physically reasonable warp drives in the class they studied still violate standard energy conditions, directly challenging broad positive-energy claims: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.105.064038
- Wilson et al. experimentally observed the dynamical Casimir effect in a superconducting circuit in 2011; the time-dependent boundary condition was externally driven: https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10561
- NASA operated the Breakthrough Propulsion Physics project and publicly assessed speculative propulsion concepts: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20040070788
- DIA commissioned *Advanced Space Propulsion Based on Vacuum (Spacetime Metric) Engineering* under AAWSAP. The released document names Puthoff as author: https://documents.theblackvault.com/documents/Advanced_Space_Propulsion_1525479713208_41686961_ver1.0.pdf
- The AAWSAP contract produced multiple DIRDs on exotic advanced-technology topics.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete / unresolved

- Stochastic-electrodynamics approaches can reproduce or reinterpret selected quantum phenomena, but they are not the accepted replacement for quantum field theory.
- Vacuum-boundary engineering can yield measurable forces, state changes and driven photon production in constrained systems; extrapolation to net spacecraft propulsion or closed-cycle vacuum power is unestablished.
- Analog-gravity and transformation-optics systems can emulate aspects of curved-spacetime propagation without establishing real gravitational metric control.
- Modern warp-drive theory remains active and technically contested. Positive-energy constructions exist in the literature, while later analyses dispute whether physically reasonable superluminal warp configurations avoid energy-condition violations.
- Semiclassical quantum field theory permits local negative renormalized energy densities in constrained states; quantum inequalities and related results restrict treating this as a freely scalable macroscopic resource.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed claims

- Controllable inertial-mass reduction through zero-point-field manipulation.
- Net extraction of ambient zero-point energy as a closed-cycle power source.
- Reactionless propulsion by pushing against the quantum vacuum.
- Existing classified craft using metric engineering, warp physics or engineered vacuum states.
- Claims that UAP performance demonstrates spacetime engineering.
- Claims that government commissioning of DIRDs reflects possession of recovered or operational exotic technology.
- Claims that the existence of Casimir or dynamical-Casimir effects establishes a usable negative-energy material or propulsion medium.
- Claims that a metamaterial or analog effective metric is itself an externally measurable gravitational metric.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- Precision metamaterial/vacuum-boundary experiments might place new limits on proposed gravity-vacuum couplings.
- A multi-laboratory force-metrology program could decisively eliminate broad classes of claimed vacuum thrusters or reveal a genuine residual.
- If a reproducible residual were found, scaling against vacuum-boundary geometry, gravitational potential and material properties could discriminate competing mechanisms.
- A staged metric-engineering experiment could place independent neutral clocks, atom interferometers or force sensors outside an engineered electromagnetic/vacuum structure to distinguish an effective optical metric from genuine external spacetime curvature.
- A clean inertia experiment would apply a calibrated external force to the same test mass under active and sham vacuum-state conditions and measure acceleration directly, then separately compare gravitational response.

## Contradictions and incentives

- Exotic-propulsion contractors benefit from maintaining fundable possibility spaces; this does not imply bad faith but is a real incentive.
- Intelligence agencies benefit from surveying low-probability/high-consequence technologies; commissioning therefore cannot be treated as endorsement.
- Secrecy prevents independent inspection and can amplify folklore by making absent evidence interpretable as hidden evidence.
- Academic stigma can discourage replication of embarrassing topics, while fringe communities can overvalue positive anomalies and under-report nulls.
- Government letterhead, patents, peer review and classification markings each establish different things; none is a substitute for reproducible performance.
- Peer review also preserves correction: Puthoff’s 1989 ZPE paper has an erratum and published critical exchanges, and the HRP inertia model has a later detailed *Physical Review A* critique. Citing only the original papers creates false evidentiary asymmetry.
- Theoretical permissibility is layered. A valid metric is not a physical source; a physical source is not a creation protocol; a creation protocol is not a propulsion system.
- Quantum violations of classical pointwise energy conditions do not imply arbitrary stress-energy engineering; constraints on magnitude, duration, observer dependence and full-system energy accounting remain central.

# Back Matter — Chronology and Selected Bibliography

## Chronology

- **1948:** Hendrik Casimir predicts the force between conducting plates associated with quantum electromagnetic boundary conditions.
- **1957:** Robert H. Dicke publishes work treating gravitation through variable electromagnetic properties in a way later relevant to polarizable-vacuum approaches.
- **1968:** Andrei Sakharov proposes induced gravity as an emergent vacuum phenomenon.
- **1984:** Robert L. Forward publishes on extracting electrical energy from the vacuum through Casimir-force systems, emphasizing full energy accounting.
- **1989–1994:** Puthoff, Bernard Haisch and Alfonso Rueda publish zero-point-field/inertia work; the program receives substantive technical criticism as well as attention.
- **1994:** Miguel Alcubierre publishes the warp-metric solution in *Classical and Quantum Gravity*.
- **1990s:** Ford and Roman develop quantum-inequality constraints relevant to negative-energy requirements.
- **1996–2002:** NASA runs the Breakthrough Propulsion Physics project to assess far-reaching propulsion concepts and experimental claims.
- **2002:** Puthoff publishes a polarizable-vacuum approach to general relativity in *Foundations of Physics* and related conference literature.
- **2009–2010:** DIA's AAWSAP contract produces Defense Intelligence Reference Documents on speculative advanced technologies.
- **2010:** Puthoff's *Advanced Space Propulsion Based on Vacuum (Spacetime Metric) Engineering* appears in *Journal of the British Interplanetary Society* and as a DIA reference document.
- **2012:** A public arXiv version of Puthoff's metric-engineering paper appears.

## Research note

Patents, government commissioning, security markings and theoretical permissibility are not treated as evidence of efficacy. The manuscript deliberately retains negative constraints, criticism and null-test requirements.

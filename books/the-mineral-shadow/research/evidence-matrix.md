# Evidence Matrix and Source Log

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- Mineral evolution is a published historical framework for increasing mineral diversity through planetary change. Source: https://doi.org/10.2138/am.2008.2955
- Earth's oxygenation enabled extensive new oxidation-state-dependent mineral pathways. See Hazen et al. 2008 and subsequent mineral-evolution literature.
- Humans synthesize many crystalline compounds and generate novel mineral-like phases and assemblages. Source: https://doi.org/10.2138/am-2017-5875
- IMA nomenclature distinguishes minerals from synthetic compounds; terminology must not be blurred. Source: https://cnmnc.units.it/

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Whole-planet mineral diversity may contain statistically useful information about biological history, but cross-planet calibration is extremely sparse.
- Industrial waste assemblages may preserve technological process information over geological intervals; survival is environment-specific.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- No known extraterrestrial mineral or meteorite is established evidence of non-human technology.
- Claims that unusual purity, morphology or composition alone demonstrate manufacture are insufficient without natural-pathway exclusion and custody.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- The book's “mineral shadow” is a proposed construct: the distribution of durable mineral/material states made accessible by a planet's contingent history.
- A blinded Earth calibration program could train a mineral-technosignature pipeline before application to returned extraterrestrial samples.

## Contradictions and incentives

Mineralogists gain professional value from novelty, but formal naming rules constrain overclaiming. Astrobiology has incentives for compelling biosignatures and equally strong reputational incentives against false positives. Sample-return programs face contamination and custody pressures. Technosignature framing can inflate ordinary anomaly detection into origin claims; this manuscript explicitly forbids that move.

## Defeat conditions

The technosignature proposal weakens if supposedly diagnostic industrial assemblages are routinely reproduced by natural processes, if alteration erases discriminating features on relevant timescales, or if blinded classifiers cannot distinguish known technological residues from natural controls at useful false-positive rates.

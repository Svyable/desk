# The Search Image

You cannot search for an anomalous mineral without deciding what normal means.

That sounds trivial until machine learning enters the room. A classifier trained on terrestrial mineral databases will be superb at recognizing the distribution we have sampled. It may flag rare phases, strange associations or spectra unlike its training set. None of those outputs means artificial.

Anomaly detection is a triage instrument.

A serious search image should combine mineral identity with context: host rock, grain morphology, trace elements, isotopes, spatial association, age and alteration state. It should be tested on known natural oddities and known human industrial residues. Better still, investigators should blind the pipeline to a mixture of natural and manufactured samples and ask whether it can rank technological process signatures without being told which are which.

This creates a calibration set for mineral SETI using Earth.

If the method cannot distinguish a slag heap from a volcanic deposit under controlled conditions, it has no business diagnosing a grain from Mars.

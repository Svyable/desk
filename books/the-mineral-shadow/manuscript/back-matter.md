# Back Matter

## Working chronology

- c. 4.56 Ga — Solar System mineral inventory develops through condensation, alteration and parent-body processing.
- c. 4.4 Ga — ancient terrestrial zircons preserve evidence from early crustal history.
- c. 2.4 Ga — Great Oxidation Event begins major reorganization of surface redox chemistry.
- 20th century — electron microscopy, diffraction and microanalysis radically expand mineral identification.
- 2008 — Hazen and colleagues publish a major mineral-evolution synthesis in *American Mineralogist*.
- 2017 — Hazen and colleagues publish work emphasizing rarity and diversity in Earth's mineral kingdom.
- 21st century — anthropogenic mineral-like compounds and technosphere materials become an explicit research subject.

# False Biosignatures

Nature is an accomplished counterfeiter because the word counterfeit assumes an intention nature does not need.

Abiotic chemistry can generate organics. Crystals can grow cell-like forms. Hydrothermal systems can fractionate isotopes. Meteorites can deliver compounds later associated with life. Serpentinization can generate hydrogen and methane. None of this makes biosignature research hopeless. It makes controls the center of the work.

The most dangerous false positive is not a known abiotic pathway. It is an unknown one.

That asymmetry should make extraordinary claims conservative without making them impossible. A proposed biosignature should specify the competing formation mechanisms examined, the environmental parameters required by each, and what additional observation would discriminate among them.

Mineralogy is particularly suited to this because phase equilibria constrain possibilities. But natural systems are open, kinetic and messy. Metastable phases survive. Fluids move. Impurities matter. Laboratory equilibrium diagrams are maps, not the territory.

A mineral shadow becomes evidential only when we can say what other histories could cast it.

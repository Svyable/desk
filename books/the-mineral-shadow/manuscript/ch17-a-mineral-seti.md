# A Mineral SETI

A mineral SETI would not begin by asking curators to hunt alien alloys. It would begin by building adversarial controls.

First, assemble blinded suites of natural minerals, meteorites, industrial slags, high-purity semiconductor materials, ceramics, nuclear-industry residues and deliberately synthesized exotic phases. Second, degrade some samples through heat, radiation, aqueous alteration and mechanical fragmentation. Third, ask independent laboratories to rank which samples require technological processing and to state the natural alternatives.

The point is not classification accuracy alone. It is calibrated uncertainty.

From that exercise, derive features that survive alteration: unnatural purity, correlated dopant patterns, phase associations, isotopic anomalies, microstructural regularity, or combinations not reproduced by known natural pathways. Then apply the method prospectively to extraterrestrial samples under strict contamination controls.

A positive candidate would move through escalation gates. Replicate composition. Verify custody. Exclude spacecraft contamination. Model natural formation. Search adjacent grains. Test isotopes. Invite hostile replication. Publish the failure conditions before origin claims.

This is speculative synthesis, evidence class four. No known extraterrestrial sample currently satisfies such a technological inference.

But the experiment can be built before the anomaly arrives. That is the difference between a research program and a reaction.

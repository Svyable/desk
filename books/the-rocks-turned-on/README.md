# The Rocks Turned On

## Earthquake Lights, Electric Stone, and the Prediction Signal That Refuses to Become a Forecast

**Sven Hardy Benson**

## Thesis

Earthquakes unquestionably disturb the electromagnetic and atmospheric environment after rupture, and a small but persistent literature reports luminous phenomena and electrical anomalies before or during some earthquakes. Laboratory work also shows that stressed igneous rocks can activate mobile charge carriers. The unresolved question is not whether rocks can produce electrical effects. It is whether any pre-rupture effect escapes the crust with enough specificity, reproducibility, and lead time to become a useful earthquake precursor. The collision is unusually instructive: a plausible mechanism, centuries of eyewitness reports, modern instruments, and repeated null or confounded precursor results can all be true at once.

The book’s bounded claim is that **earthquake electromagnetism is a case study in the difference between a physical effect and a predictive signal**. Coseismic coupling is real. Some earthquake-light reports may be real. Stress-activated charge transport is experimentally interesting. None of those propositions establishes reliable short-term earthquake prediction.

## Evidence classes

1. **Verified / documented facts.** Earthquakes produce measurable coseismic electromagnetic and ionospheric disturbances; laboratory experiments have measured charge generation and propagation in stressed igneous rocks; peer-reviewed literature documents historical earthquake-light reports and some instrumented luminous events.
2. **Credible but incomplete evidence.** Carefully timed eyewitness reports, security-camera observations, site-specific electromagnetic anomalies, and correlations with tectonic setting may describe genuine phenomena but often lack complete controls, denominators, or independent replication.
3. **Disputed allegations.** Claims that particular ULF, ionospheric, infrared, radon, animal-behavior, or luminous anomalies reliably predicted specific earthquakes are not treated as established. Post-hoc threshold selection, geomagnetic contamination, infrastructure noise, incomplete baselines, and publication bias remain live problems.
4. **Speculative synthesis / open conjecture.** Stress-activated electronic charge carriers may contribute to some pre-rupture surface, atmospheric, or luminous phenomena, but the end-to-end chain from stressed source volume to reproducible forecast observable remains unproven.

## Defeat condition

The book fails if it turns a laboratory mechanism into a field prediction system by rhetoric, treats every strange light near an earthquake as tectonic, ignores the enormous denominator of earthquakes without reported lights, or dismisses all precursor work merely because reliable prediction has not been achieved. It must keep mechanism, observation, attribution, and forecast utility separate.

# The Lost Compass

## Front matter

**The Lost Compass: Magnetoreception, Quantum Biology, and the Sense Humans May Have Misplaced**

Sven Hardy Benson

This book begins from an asymmetry. Magnetoreception is established behaviorally across multiple animal lineages, yet the receptor mechanisms remain incompletely resolved. The avian light-dependent compass has strong evidence consistent with radical-pair chemistry; magnetite-based mechanisms remain plausible for other magnetic tasks; sea-turtle work now supports mechanistic separation between magnetic map and compass behavior. Human magnetoreception is not established. A 2019 EEG study reported cortical responses to Earth-strength field rotations, but independent multi-site replication and receptor identification remain necessary.

The manuscript uses four evidence classes throughout:

1. documented or directly established facts;
2. credible but incomplete evidence or inference;
3. disputed or unconfirmed claims;
4. speculative synthesis and open conjecture.

“Quantum” is used here only where quantum spin chemistry is actually part of the proposed mechanism. It is not used as a synonym for mystery, consciousness, nonlocality, healing, or paranormal ability.

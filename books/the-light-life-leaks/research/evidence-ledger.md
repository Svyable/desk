# Evidence Ledger

This ledger is deliberately stricter than the manuscript's rhetoric. A claim moves class only when the evidence changes.

## Class 1 — verified / documented / reproducibly measured

- Biological tissues, cells, plants, animals, and humans can emit ultraweak photons detectable with sufficiently sensitive instruments.
- Much measured UPE is associated with oxidative chemistry, including pathways involving lipid peroxidation, triplet excited carbonyls, singlet oxygen, and reactive oxygen species.
- Rat liver nuclei were reported in 1991 to show oxygen-dependent ultraweak emission linked experimentally to oxidative/peroxidative processes.
- Human spontaneous UPE was imaged in 2009 and showed a diurnal pattern under the study conditions.
- Wounded plants show localized changes in UPE; herbivory-associated and chemical treatments can alter emission patterns.
- Salari et al. 2025 reported a strong difference in UPE between four living hairless mice and the same animals after euthanasia under their protocol; heat, injury, and chemical treatments altered plant UPE.
- UVA-driven oxidative chemistry in skin-related biomolecules produces distinguishable weak-emission spectra; human skin emission is consistent with a composite of multiple molecular sources.
- The Japanese ERATO INABA Biophoton Project existed from 1986–1991 and developed sensitive photon-counting, near-infrared, spectral, and imaging methods for biological weak light.
- E. Lorenz published a 1934 photoelectric search that failed to detect the claimed mitogenetic ultraviolet radiation from tested biological materials at his apparatus's stated sensitivity.
- Popp and collaborators published coherence/DNA interpretations in the 1980s; this is documented publication history, not Class 1 evidence that the interpretations are correct.
- Pietruszka and Marzec published a 2024 Scientific Reports paper reporting ultraweak emission from barley genomic DNA and interpreting associated observations in terms of coherent/non-equilibrium phenomena; publication and reported observations are documented, stronger interpretation remains separate.
- A 2023 paper reported a non-contact physiological effect between adjacent isolated mitochondrial preparations under its experimental geometry; the published observation is Class 1 as a reported result, not as an independently replicated phenomenon or photon-mediated mechanism.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- UPE can become a useful label-free biomarker of oxidative stress, tissue viability, plant stress, or metabolic state in selected applications.
- Specific UPE spectral/temporal patterns may distinguish classes of biochemical oxidative processes beyond total intensity.
- Neural UPE may provide information about metabolic state, injury, or disease in experimental models.
- Some non-contact biological effects between physically separated preparations may reproduce under better isolation.
- The 2023 mitochondrial inter-cuvette effect may involve an optical component because source and receiver are both plausible photochemical systems and reported effects depended in part on illumination context; the channel has not been isolated.
- Local endogenous photon flux inside tissue may exceed externally measured escape flux because most photons are absorbed or scattered before reaching a camera.
- Some biological chromophores could, in principle, respond to very low photon counts if strong molecular amplification exists; specific endogenous-flux receiver mechanisms remain to be demonstrated.
- UPE imaging may have clinical or agricultural value as a dynamic stress-response measurement even if it lacks disease specificity.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed / not independently established

- Gurwitsch's original mitogenetic radiation functioned as a reproducible ultraviolet communication channel that increased mitosis in neighboring tissue.
- Biological UPE generally has laser-like optical coherence that cannot be explained by ordinary chemiluminescent sources and detector/statistical effects.
- DNA is a major coherent biological photon source or functions as an in-vivo exciplex laser regulating cells.
- Endogenous ultraweak photons provide a general long-range communication system between cells, organs, or organisms.
- The human brain uses endogenous photons as a major information-processing channel or as a substrate of consciousness.
- Transcranial UPE can currently diagnose specific neurological disease or cognitive state with validated clinical performance.
- Human observers can consciously see ordinary UPE as an aura.
- Kirlian or gas-discharge photography images spontaneous biological UPE.
- Commercial “biophoton” or “biofield” devices can infer generalized vitality or disease without calibrated photon metrology and prospective validation.
- The 2024 DNA-emission paper's strongest coherence and phase-transition interpretation has independent experimental confirmation.
- Photobiomodulation results at therapeutic irradiances show that endogenous UPE-level photon flux has equivalent biological effects.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- Evolution may have recruited a subset of metabolic photon emission as a local intracellular or intercellular cue.
- Some organisms may eavesdrop on stress-related UPE generated incidentally by another organism rather than receiving an evolved signal.
- Intracellular photons may produce local photochemical feedback even when no communication code exists.
- A small coherent or spectrally narrow photon component may exist inside a much larger incoherent oxidative background and therefore be difficult to detect in whole-organism measurements.
- Guided or near-field optical coupling could raise local source-to-receptor photon delivery above estimates based on isotropic whole-body emission; no general biological waveguide mechanism is assumed.
- Dynamic UPE responses to controlled metabolic challenges may contribute one useful line of evidence in astrobiology, but UPE alone is not a biosignature.
- UPE could eventually serve as a contactless optical readout for transplant viability, wound progression, plant stress, or other bounded applications if calibration and specificity barriers are solved.

## Promotion rules

A communication claim moves toward Class 2/1 only after the proposed optical channel is isolated from chemical, volatile, thermal, mechanical, acoustic, and electromagnetic alternatives, and after blocking/attenuating/substituting the photon path changes the receiver predictably.

A coherence claim moves only after direct optical coherence or photon-correlation measurements reproduce across laboratories against calibrated chemiluminescent and instrument controls.

A DNA claim moves only after blinded independent replication with purity controls, synthetic nucleic-acid comparators, orthogonal spectroscopy, and a demonstrated source mechanism.

A medical claim moves only after prospective validation against existing clinical comparators in the intended population.

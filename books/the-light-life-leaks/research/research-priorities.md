# Research Priorities and Defeat Conditions

## Highest-value experiments

1. **Optical-only mitochondrial channel replication.** Repeat the 2023 adjacent-mitochondria effect using independent sealed chambers linked only by a controllable optical path. Measure UPE simultaneously. Include full-pass, wavelength-filtered, neutral-density attenuated, opaque, and fiber-linked conditions.
2. **Emitter substitution.** Replace a biological emitter with a calibrated source reproducing its measured spectrum, temporal pattern, and photon flux. A causal optical hypothesis should survive substitution.
3. **Endogenous-flux dose response.** Test candidate mitochondrial/cellular photoreceptors across photon fluxes spanning below UPE through standard photobiomodulation levels. The endogenous range must be included explicitly.
4. **Modern coherence test.** Use calibrated HBT-style second-order correlation measurements and appropriate chemical-luminescence controls. Pre-register which photon statistics would count as evidence beyond known oxidative emission and detector artifacts.
5. **DNA replication consortium.** Blind multiple labs to samples containing genomic DNA, synthetic oligos, RNA, proteins, buffers, oxidized DNA, and contamination controls. Measure emission and any coherence claims with different detector platforms.
6. **Human circadian replication.** Multi-day within-person UPE imaging with controlled light exposure, food, exercise, and sleep, plus simultaneous temperature, melatonin/cortisol, oxygen-consumption, skin-reflectance, and oxidative markers.
7. **Postmortem kinetics.** Dense live-to-postmortem UPE time series with organ-level mapping, controlled temperature, oxygen, antioxidants, mitochondrial inhibitors, and independent metabolic measurements.
8. **Neural depth validation.** Compare scalp, exposed-cortex, and intracranial/ex-vivo UPE in animal models; build tissue transport models and superficial-tissue controls before assigning transcranial signals to brain metabolism.
9. **Clinical narrow-use trial.** Pick one bounded application such as transplant-organ viability or wound oxidative progression and prospectively test whether UPE adds predictive value beyond established measurements.
10. **Evolutionary receiver search.** Screen dark-adapted or close-contact organisms for behavioral/physiological responses to UPE-level calibrated stimuli; follow any positive result with receptor identification.

## Defeat conditions

### General communication hypothesis

The broad claim that endogenous UPE serves as a common biological communication channel should be substantially downgraded if well-powered, independently replicated optical-only channel experiments fail while reproducing ordinary UPE emission and biochemical state dependence.

### Mitochondrial optical-coupling hypothesis

Downgrade if adjacent-preparation effects disappear when volatile/thermal/mechanical/electromagnetic coupling is removed, or if effects persist unchanged under complete optical blocking. Also downgrade if calibrated artificial light at measured endogenous flux cannot reproduce effects across a broad physiologically relevant parameter range.

### Coherence hypothesis

Downgrade if modern photon-correlation measurements across multiple biological sources are statistically indistinguishable from matched oxidative chemiluminescence after instrument artifacts and nonstationarity are modeled.

### DNA coherent-source hypothesis

Downgrade if blinded independent labs cannot reproduce emission above matched controls, if signal tracks contamination/oxidative products rather than DNA mass/state, or if direct coherence measurements fail.

### Neural optical-signaling hypothesis

Downgrade if realistic photon budgets through neural tissue fall well below candidate receptor thresholds with no demonstrated waveguide/amplification mechanism, and if optical blocking does not alter the proposed functional effect.

### Diagnostic platform hypothesis

Downgrade broad clinical claims if multi-center prospective studies show UPE adds no meaningful predictive information beyond temperature, perfusion, conventional oxidative markers, or existing imaging.

### Aura / conscious perception hypothesis

Downgrade if blinded observers cannot detect realistic UPE or physiological-state differences when ordinary visual, thermal, auditory, olfactory, and social cues are controlled.

## Conditions that would materially upgrade the field

- Independent reproduction of a receiver response that follows a wavelength-specific endogenous optical channel and can be replicated by a matched artificial photon source.
- Identification of a receptor molecule whose knockout abolishes the response at measured endogenous flux.
- Direct reproducible optical coherence evidence not explained by known chemical luminescence or detector effects.
- Multi-lab DNA emission/coherence replication with contamination controls.
- Prospective clinical utility in a defined indication with external validation.

# Back Matter

The book ends without deciding that cells speak in light.

That is intentional.

The verified phenomenon is already larger than the caricature. Living systems produce ultraweak photons. Oxidative chemistry explains substantial portions of the emission. Modern single-photon instruments can image changes associated with wounds, stress, metabolism, circadian state, and organismal viability. Japan once funded a five-year national research effort around the measurement problem and produced serious instrumentation and mechanistic work.

The strongest functional claims remain open.

The decisive next step is not another review arguing that communication is plausible or implausible. It is a channel experiment: isolate emitter and receiver, characterize the photon flux and spectrum, eliminate non-optical coupling, attenuate and filter the light, then replace the living emitter with a calibrated source at the same intensity. If the receiver follows the optical channel, the evidence class changes.

The same principle applies to coherence, DNA emission, neural UPE, and clinical translation. Build tests that can remove the favored explanation rather than merely produce another compatible observation.

The title is a boundary as much as a thesis.

Life leaks light.

We know that.

Whether life listens is still an experiment.

## Research priorities

The highest-value next work is a multi-laboratory replication of non-contact mitochondrial effects with physically isolated chambers and a controlled optical-only path; direct endogenous-flux dose-response testing of candidate photoreceptors; second-order coherence measurements against matched oxidative chemiluminescence controls; blinded multi-lab replication of purified-DNA UPE; full postmortem time-series imaging after controlled euthanasia; standardized human circadian UPE replication across pigmentation and chronotype; and calibrated disease or organ-viability studies that compare UPE against existing clinical measurements.

## Claim boundary

Nothing in this manuscript establishes a visible human aura, a soul or consciousness leaving the body as photons, a coherent organism-wide biophoton field, DNA functioning as a biological laser in vivo, or a universal optical language between cells. Those ideas are treated only at the evidence level their published support warrants.

## Source practice

The research directory records the primary and serious sources used for concrete claims, along with contradictions, known limitations, and explicit defeat conditions. Patents, wellness marketing, founder claims, and retrospective narratives are not treated as evidence of efficacy.

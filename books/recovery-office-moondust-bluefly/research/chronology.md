# Chronology

- **1917** — Institutional lineage later claimed by NASIC begins with foreign-data evaluation in Army aviation engineering.
- **1947** — Roswell incident; later becomes central to crash-retrieval claims.
- **1947–1969** — Air Force UFO investigations under Sign, Grudge, and Blue Book.
- **October 4, 1957** — Sputnik 1 launch makes foreign orbital hardware an immediate strategic-intelligence object.
- **Late 1950s / early 1960s** — Moon Dust and Blue Fly appear in Air Force intelligence recovery/exploitation tasking.
- **1961** — Air Force intelligence memorandum links Moon Dust, Blue Fly, UFO investigation, and quick-reaction field personnel in technical-intelligence tasking; exact archival locator to be hardened in next pass.
- **1967–1972** — Declassified State/Defense traffic documents descended-space-object cases and diplomatic coordination under Moon Dust-related channels.
- **1968** — U.S. team examines fragments in Nepal; possible Soviet/Cosmos provenance considered.
- **1968** — HAVE DOUGHNUT clandestine MiG-21 evaluation led by Foreign Technology Division/DoD team.
- **1970** — Chad metal-sphere case appears in Moon Dust traffic.
- **1977–1988** — CONSTANT PEG expands operational exploitation/training with acquired Soviet MiGs.
- **1969** — Project Blue Book terminates; official conclusions state no evidence of extraterrestrial vehicles or technology beyond known scientific principles.
- **1980s–1990s** — Moon Dust/Blue Fly and Roswell retrieval claims gain increasing prominence in UFO literature and FOIA research.
- **July 28, 1995** — GAO publishes Roswell records search and comments on MJ-12 material.
- **2022** — DoD establishes AARO under congressional direction.
- **July 26, 2023** — David Grusch, Ryan Graves, and David Fravor testify before House UAP hearing; Grusch publicly alleges concealed retrieval/reverse-engineering activity based substantially on information reported to him.
- **March 8, 2024** — DoD releases AARO Historical Record Report Volume I; AARO says it found no verifiable evidence of extraterrestrial technology or a hidden reverse-engineering program.
- **2026** — Historical argument remains unresolved at the payload level: recovery/exploitation infrastructure is documented; non-human recovered technology remains publicly unverified.

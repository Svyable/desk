## Source handling rules

1. Official program descriptions establish what an agency says the program did; they do not automatically establish every claimed case outcome.
2. Later institutional histories are authoritative for broad lineage but can simplify operational detail.
3. FOIA mirrors are discovery aids. Where possible, final citations should point to originating agency or NARA identifiers.
4. Modern testimony is attributed as testimony. It is never silently upgraded to physical proof.
5. AARO’s negative findings are recorded as official investigative conclusions, not as logical proof that no concealed program can exist.

# The Small Signal — research ledger

## Thesis

The most consequential unresolved psi question may not be whether spectacular paranormal ability exists, but whether a small anomalous information effect survives modern controls while remaining too weak and unstable for intelligence use. This book treats statistical existence, mechanism, controllability and operational utility as separate claims.

## Evidence classes

### 1 — Verified / declassified / documented

- CIA acknowledges U.S. government remote-viewing research and the later independent 1995 evaluation; CIA says it did not restore the program because results were unreliable and inconsistent for intelligence use. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-did-cia-really-study-psychic-powers/
- Declassified GRILL FLAME/STARGATE session reports document operational tasking, including Tehran-hostage-related sessions. Example: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r000800580001-1
- CIA FOIA holds the 1973 SRI parapsychology report trail. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00787r000100070001-5
- Tressoldi & Storm 2024, Stage 2 Registered Report meta-analysis of ganzfeld research, reports a small positive aggregate effect; peer review is mixed rather than unanimous. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.51746.4
- Mossbridge, Tressoldi & Utts 2012 meta-analyzed 26 reports on pre-stimulus physiological anticipation and reported an unexplained anticipatory effect. This establishes the publication and its analysis, not precognition as mechanism. https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2012.00390

### 2 — Credible but incomplete

- AIR 1995's statistical and methodological conclusions need document-level reconstruction, including the separate positions of Jessica Utts and Ray Hyman, operational case sampling, evaluator access and what “useful” meant to intelligence consumers.
- Soviet/Russian psychotronics and parapsychology programs are documented in U.S. intelligence reporting, but claims about Soviet efficacy require primary-language and provenance work rather than repetition of threat estimates.
- Some laboratory literatures report persistent small effects. Whether those estimates survive publication-bias, heterogeneity, dependence and protocol-quality corrections remains an active methodological dispute.

### 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

- Telepathy, clairvoyance or precognition as the mechanism behind aggregate experimental effects.
- Claims that remote viewing produced decisive intelligence unavailable by conventional means in famous operational cases.
- Claims of operational Soviet/Russian psychic weapons or reliable psychotronic capabilities.
- Anecdotal “hits” lacking precommitted scoring, complete session denominators and independent target records.

### 4 — Speculative synthesis

- Psi may be best investigated as a weak-signal problem whose scientific detectability and practical utility diverge.
- Intelligence secrecy may selectively preserve memorable hits while hiding the denominator required to estimate operational value, without any deliberate deception.
- Rival-state research may create a reflexive funding loop in which investigation is misread as evidence of capability.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. A statistical anomaly can coexist with operational failure; neither implies the other.
2. Government persistence can reflect threat hedging rather than belief or success.
3. Program cancellation can reflect poor utility rather than proof that every laboratory effect is zero.
4. Stronger experimental controls can reduce effect size without deciding whether the residual is artifact or anomaly.
5. Meta-analysis can increase power while also aggregating correlated biases.

## Quantitative burden

For each experimental family extract: preregistration status; randomization method; target pool; sensory-leakage controls; independent judging; number of trials; raw hit rate; chance rate; effect size and uncertainty; exclusions; stopping rules; study dependence; publication status; heterogeneity; prospective replication outcome.

For each operational case extract: task date; target definition before session; viewer cueing; transcript timestamp; number of sessions; all scored claims rather than selected correspondences; scoring performed before feedback where recoverable; alternative-target discriminability; consumer action; independent corroboration; conventional collection available; whether the product changed a decision.

## Strongest conventional model

There is no anomalous information channel. Small positive aggregates arise from some mixture of publication bias, flexible analysis, imperfect randomization, sensory leakage, non-independence, selective reporting, retrospective matching and ordinary sampling variation. Government programs persisted because Cold War threat hedging, bureaucratic momentum and memorable anecdotes can sustain research into low-probability capabilities.

This model wins if prospectively registered, independently run, high-powered experiments with locked analysis and leakage controls converge on chance, especially if historical effect sizes track methodological weakness.

## Strongest extraordinary model

A real anomalous information effect exists but has low bandwidth, poor controllability and unknown mechanism. Laboratory aggregation can detect it while individual operational trials remain unreliable. This model must predict prospective outcomes better than artifact models; invoking “elusiveness” after failures is not a prediction.

## Defeat condition

The book's small-signal thesis should be abandoned if either side wins cleanly. Robust prospective null results under strong controls remove the empirical residue. Conversely, reproducible high-information performance under blinded operational scoring would make “statistically real but practically useless” the wrong book.

## Publication holds

- Do not say CIA concluded psychic functioning was proved. Attribute evaluator positions precisely.
- Do not say STARGATE “worked” because it was funded or tasked.
- Do not call Soviet intelligence reports evidence of Soviet efficacy.
- Do not convert a ganzfeld aggregate effect into telepathy without mechanism-level evidence.
- Do not use famous operational hits without the session denominator and contemporaneous target/scoring record.
- Recover the AIR 1995 report and original Utts/Hyman text before drafting Chapters 10–11.
- Recover primary Soviet/Russian sources or mark the international chapter as intelligence-reporting history, not capability history.

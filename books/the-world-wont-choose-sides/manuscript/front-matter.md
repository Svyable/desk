# The World Won’t Choose Sides

*Middle Powers and the New Balance of Power*

Sven Hardy Benson

First field edition candidate, September 2026.

This book is about the bargaining power created by having more than one useful relationship. It is not a ranking of countries and it does not assume that every state discussed here can remain equally autonomous in every crisis. The argument is narrower: a world organized through overlapping security, trade, energy, technology, financial, and diplomatic networks gives pivotal states more ways to align selectively than a two-bloc map suggests.

# The World Won’t Choose Sides

*Middle Powers and the New Balance of Power*

Sven Hardy Benson

The argument of this book is not that alliances are disappearing or that every government can indefinitely avoid hard choices. It is that great-power competition is filtered through states with their own histories, geographies, domestic politics, economic dependencies, and ambitions. Treating them as pieces waiting to be assigned to one camp or another makes the world easier to draw on a map and harder to understand.

This is a book about the strategy inside the refusal.

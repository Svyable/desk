# The World Won’t Choose Sides

*Middle Powers and the New Balance of Power*

The working thesis is that the defining geopolitical fact of the next decade is not a clean division between American and Chinese blocs but the growing leverage of states wealthy, populous, strategic, or connected enough to refuse permanent alignment and bargain issue by issue.

This is a book about selective alignment rather than neutrality. India can cooperate deeply with the United States without accepting permanent strategic dependence. Turkey can remain in NATO while cultivating relationships with institutions led by China and Russia. Gulf states can combine American security ties with Asian trade, capital, technology, and diplomatic initiatives. Indonesia, Brazil, South Africa, and other pivotal states can support one coalition on one problem and resist it on the next.

The first field edition publishes the argument and opening research path while the full trade manuscript remains in development on Desk.

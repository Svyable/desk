# Publication Gate

This first pass completes the 18-chapter argument but does **not** satisfy Desk's requested one-shot target of at least 3,000 substantive words per chapter / 54,000+ manuscript words. The current chapters are intentionally evidence-dense but materially shorter. No line-count or additions proxy should be represented as a word count.

Before publication-candidate status, complete all of the following:

- Recover and page-index the original Robin Baker human orientation/homing papers plus direct replication failures and methodological critiques.
- Reconstruct Wang et al. 2019 at protocol level: participant counts, responder distribution, all stimulus geometries, field strengths, coil design, sham-current configuration, EEG preprocessing, statistical tests, exact effect sizes, data exclusions and open-data reanalysis.
- Search exhaustively for independent replication, attempted replication, critiques and null results of the 2019 alpha-ERD finding through publication date.
- Reconstruct Chae et al. 2022 at protocol level: forced-choice task, lighting spectra/intensity, participant grouping, RF frequencies/amplitudes/orientation, sham conditions, exclusions, effect sizes and multiplicity handling.
- Search exhaustively for independent replication, critique and null results of the 2022 light/RF behavioral effect.
- Build a quantitative comparison table of magnetite versus radical-pair predictions: polarity, inclination, light dependence, RF dependence, frequency sensitivity, field-strength dependence, expected latency and anatomical requirements.
- Expand the magnetite section with receptor-anatomy history across animals, including false starts, contamination problems and claims that failed anatomical replication.
- Expand human brain-magnetite provenance: endogenous crystal morphology, combustion-derived particles, tissue distribution and limits of inferring sensory function from bulk measurements.
- Expand radical-pair physics quantitatively enough to explain electron Larmor frequency, hyperfine interactions, coherence/lifetime requirements and RF-disruption predictions without mystical language.
- Reconstruct the bird RF-disruption literature including replication controversies and environmental electromagnetic-noise experiments.
- Expand cryptochrome biology: human CRY1/CRY2 localization, mammalian circadian roles, differences from avian CRY4 and whether human retinal photochemistry supports the proposed mechanism.
- Add comparative evidence from sea turtles, fish, insects, mammals and, where available, nonhuman primates to separate compass and map functions.
- Build a preregistrable two-laboratory protocol for the factorial cross-mechanism experiment proposed in Chapter 16, including power analysis and explicit defeat thresholds.
- Expand every numbered chapter to at least 3,000 substantive words without repeated framework padding.
- Run a whole-book anti-slop and paragraph pass against `docs/prose-authoring-standard.md`.
- Run book metadata checks and `scripts/check-desk.py` locally on a checked-out branch before any inventory/catalog integration.

# Source Log — The Reactor That Ate the Air

## Primary / official sources

1. Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, **1955 Nuclear Propulsion** institutional history PDF. Documents the Laboratory's shift to Project Pluto, Tory II-A and II-C roles, 45-second 1961 test, ~500 MW 1964 test, six tests without failure, and cancellation after loss of firm military commitment. https://www.llnl.gov/sites/www/files/1955.pdf

2. Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, **A Short History of the Laboratory**, *Science & Technology Review*, September 1998. Describes Tory II-A and Tory II-C and states that DOD concluded it had no need for nuclear ramjets. https://str.llnl.gov/sites/str/files/2024-04/1998.09.pdf

3. Charles Barnett, **Tory II-C Test Operations**, Lawrence Livermore Laboratory, UCRL-12263, March 12, 1965. Period technical report; OSTI DOI 10.2172/4356209. Priority source for detailed test chronology and operating conditions. https://www.osti.gov/biblio/4356209

4. E. Goldberg, **Tory II-C Program: Introduction and General Description**, Lawrence Radiation Laboratory, 1962. Priority period source for system architecture and program framing.

5. C. E. Walter, **Structure of Reactor — Part 2 of the Tory II-C Program**, UCRL-7036 Pt. 2, September 27, 1962. Period reactor-structure report. https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4352707

6. Nevada National Security Site, tour/history material describing Tory II-A as the world's first nuclear ramjet test and Tory II-C's five-minute 1964 test, with July 1, 1964 cancellation. https://www.nnss.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/NSSAB_Tour_Booklet_FINAL_10-26-16_RED.pdf

7. Mark Burmeister, **Closure Report for Corrective Action Unit 117: Area 26 Pluto Disassembly Facility, Nevada Test Site, Nevada**, 2009, OSTI 963423. Primary environmental-restoration source for the physical afterlife and disposition of Pluto facilities. https://www.osti.gov/biblio/963423

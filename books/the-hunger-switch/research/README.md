# Research brief — The Hunger Switch

This book is narrative nonfiction about the GLP-1 obesity-drug era: the 1980s-1990s laboratory discovery of exendin-4 in Gila monster venom, Novo Nordisk's decades-long incretin research program, the 2017-2026 commercial and cultural explosion of semaglutide and tirzepatide, and the industries — food, insurance, bariatric surgery, addiction medicine, pharmacy economics — the drugs are reshaping.

## Sourcing approach

Every dated fact, trial result, dollar figure, and direct quotation traceable to a named person is backed by a real, checkable source in [`source-ledger.csv`](source-ledger.csv). Sources are weighted toward primary material where available (FDA approval records, company clinical-trial disclosures, peer-reviewed trial publications in NEJM and comparable journals, federal research-institute releases) and toward named, dated journalism where primary material is not directly public (Walmart executive comments, market-cap reporting, patent-timeline analysis).

No invented statistics, no invented quotations, and no composite scenes involving real, identifiable people appear in the manuscript. Where a claim is contested, uncertain, or still evolving as of the drafting date (September 2026) — long-run mortality effects, generic pricing before any generic exists, the durability of addiction-related findings — the chapter says so rather than presenting a projection as settled fact.

## Known open questions for a future editorial pass

- Long-term (10+ year) cardiovascular, cancer, and mortality data are still accumulating; SELECT and similar trials cover a matter of years, not decades.
- Manufacturing-cost estimates (Yale, University of Liverpool) are academic modeling exercises, not disclosed company cost accounting; they are presented in the text as estimates, not as Novo Nordisk's or Eli Lilly's actual unit economics.
- Some 2026 developments (the oral Wegovy launch price, the FDA's April 2026 503B Bulks List proposal) were current at drafting time and may have moved by the time of any release edit; a pre-release pass should re-check anything dated within the final drafting quarter.
- Chapter 20's synthesis about cultural and moral reframing draws on published journalism and clinical commentary about body image and "Ozempic face"; it deliberately avoids naming or diagnosing specific private individuals.

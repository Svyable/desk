# Research brief — The Last Witnesses

## Thesis

The book asks whether intelligent observers can become custodians of scientific evidence that is naturally time-dependent. The strongest cosmological case comes from the standard far-future ΛCDM scenario: persistent accelerated expansion eventually isolates gravitationally bound regions, removes external galaxies from future causal access, redshifts the cosmic microwave background beyond useful local observation, and makes clean primordial-abundance evidence harder to recover after extensive stellar processing.

The manuscript treats that scenario as a conditional projection, not a measured future fact. The nature of dark energy remains an open empirical problem. DESI results have made time-varying dark energy more interesting, so every far-future claim should be rechecked as the full survey analyses mature.

## Claim classes

### Established observational ground

- The universe is expanding, with large-scale recession measured through galaxy redshifts and distance indicators.
- Late-time expansion is accelerating under the standard interpretation of multiple independent cosmological probes.
- The cosmic microwave background is a near-blackbody relic radiation field containing early-universe anisotropy information.
- Primordial nucleosynthesis predicts light-element abundances that can be compared with relatively unprocessed astronomical environments; deuterium is especially useful and the lithium discrepancy remains unresolved.
- Large-scale structure and baryon acoustic oscillations retain early-universe information and constrain expansion history.

### Model-dependent projection

- A positive cosmological constant that remains dominant indefinitely produces an asymptotically de Sitter-like future with a cosmological event horizon.
- In that scenario, gravitationally unbound external galaxies become observationally inaccessible to sufficiently late local observers.
- The CMB continues to redshift and eventually ceases to function as a practical local cosmological relic; the classic Krauss–Scherrer argument discusses plasma-frequency and de Sitter-temperature limits.
- Stellar processing reduces the availability of pristine primordial chemical evidence, especially deuterium.

### Live uncertainty

- Whether dark energy is exactly a cosmological constant.
- Whether DESI’s preference for evolving dark energy in combinations with other datasets survives later data, alternate supernova compilations, systematics work, and model comparison.
- The origin of the Hubble tension.
- The cosmological lithium problem.
- The identity of dark matter, the mechanism of inflation, and the correct quantum theory of gravity.
- Which currently unknown relics or future measurement techniques could remain available to remote future observers.

### Speculative synthesis

- Intelligence can function as a transport mechanism for scientific evidence across epochs by producing records that outlast access to their original sources.
- A future scientific archive should optimize for provenance, independent cross-checks, graceful degradation, distrust, and repeated copying rather than one supposedly immortal vault.
- Humanity has no demonstrated cosmic appointment; any custodial role is a chosen consequence of valuing knowledge and future knowers.

## Falsification and revision notes

1. If observations establish dark-energy dynamics that eliminate persistent acceleration and the relevant future event horizon, revise or remove the specific “island universe” future rather than preserving it rhetorically.
2. If a robust future observable is identified that independently recovers expansion, the hot early universe, and primordial composition after the familiar probes vanish, narrow claims about an “end of cosmology.”
3. If the CMB-plasma-frequency or other specific far-future detectability arguments are superseded by better calculations, follow the better physics.
4. Keep the practical archival thesis separate from the cosmological forecast. Unique transients, changing samples, long baselines, and ordinary data decay already establish time-dependent evidence without ΛCDM extrapolation.
5. Do not turn DESI’s current hints into a discovery claim. Record the date and dataset combination whenever discussing evolving dark energy.

## Source discipline

Prefer primary papers, collaboration releases, observatory documentation, and technical reviews. No quotation should appear in the manuscript unless checked against the source. Numerical claims that are not stable reference facts should carry a date or source in research notes. Preserve disagreements rather than resolving them for narrative neatness.

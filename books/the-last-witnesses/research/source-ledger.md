# Source ledger — The Last Witnesses

Working sources for factual claims and revision. Links are intended to be directly usable rather than ornamental.

## Core far-future cosmology

- Lawrence M. Krauss and Robert J. Scherrer, “The Return of a Static Universe and the End of Cosmology,” *General Relativity and Gravitation* 39 (2007). Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/0704.0221 · DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s10714-007-0472-9
  - Primary source for the late-time Λ-dominated island-universe argument: loss of observable Hubble flow, CMB detectability, and primordial-abundance evidence.
- Fred C. Adams and Gregory Laughlin, “A Dying Universe: The Long-Term Fate and Evolution of Astrophysical Objects,” *Reviews of Modern Physics* 69 (1997). https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9701131
  - Broad reference for long-term astrophysical evolution and the enormous separation between human, stellar, galactic, and black-hole timescales.

## Accelerating expansion

- Adam G. Riess et al., “Observational Evidence from Supernovae for an Accelerating Universe and a Cosmological Constant,” *Astronomical Journal* 116 (1998). https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9805201
  - High-Z Supernova Search Team discovery paper.
- Saul Perlmutter et al., “Measurements of Ω and Λ from 42 High-Redshift Supernovae,” *Astrophysical Journal* 517 (1999). https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/9812133
  - Supernova Cosmology Project result supporting accelerated expansion.
- Nobel Prize in Physics 2011 background, Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/physics/2011/summary/
  - Authoritative historical reference for the acceleration discovery and award.

## ΛCDM baseline and the CMB

- Planck Collaboration, “Planck 2018 results. VI. Cosmological parameters,” *Astronomy & Astrophysics* 641, A6 (2020). https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201833910 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.06209
  - Precision reference for the standard six-parameter spatially flat ΛCDM baseline and CMB-derived cosmological constraints.
- A. A. Penzias and R. W. Wilson, “A Measurement of Excess Antenna Temperature at 4080 Mc/s,” *Astrophysical Journal* 142 (1965). https://doi.org/10.1086/148307
  - Discovery observation of the cosmic microwave background.
- R. H. Dicke, P. J. E. Peebles, P. G. Roll, and D. T. Wilkinson, “Cosmic Black-Body Radiation,” *Astrophysical Journal* 142 (1965). https://doi.org/10.1086/148306
  - Immediate theoretical interpretation of relic blackbody radiation.
- NASA LAMBDA CMB resources: https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/
  - Authoritative archive and explanatory material for COBE, WMAP, Planck-linked CMB products and cosmological background.

## Primordial nucleosynthesis

- Cyril Pitrou, Alain Coc, Jean-Philippe Uzan, and Elisabeth Vangioni, “Precision Big Bang Nucleosynthesis with Improved Helium-4 Predictions,” *Physics Reports* 754 (2018). https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.08023
  - Technical review for primordial light-element production and observational comparisons.
- Particle Data Group, review materials: https://pdg.lbl.gov/
  - Living technical reference for cosmological parameters, particle properties, and review context; verify the current edition when revising numerical claims.

## DESI and live dark-energy uncertainty

- Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument official project: https://www.desi.lbl.gov/
- DESI collaboration papers and public data documentation: https://data.desi.lbl.gov/doc/papers/
- DESI Collaboration, “DESI 2024 VI: Cosmological Constraints from the Measurements of Baryon Acoustic Oscillations,” *Journal of Cosmology and Astroparticle Physics* 02 (2025) 021. https://doi.org/10.1088/1475-7516/2025/02/021
  - Primary collaboration analysis for early DESI BAO cosmological constraints and combined-data dark-energy fits.
- DESI project update, April 15, 2026, announcing completion of observations for the originally planned five-year survey and more than 47 million observed galaxies and quasars: https://www.desi.lbl.gov/
  - Recheck the exact release URL and full-survey analysis status during the next fact pass; the project home is retained here because the result stream is still moving.

## Large-scale structure and standard rulers

- Daniel J. Eisenstein et al., “Detection of the Baryon Acoustic Peak in the Large-Scale Correlation Function of SDSS Luminous Red Galaxies,” *Astrophysical Journal* 633 (2005). https://arxiv.org/abs/astro-ph/0501171
  - Classic empirical BAO standard-ruler reference.
- Sloan Digital Sky Survey: https://www.sdss.org/
  - Survey documentation and public data lineage relevant to the manuscript’s discussion of sky surveys as historical records.

## Gravitational-wave evidence and singular events

- B. P. Abbott et al. (LIGO Scientific Collaboration and Virgo Collaboration), “Observation of Gravitational Waves from a Binary Black Hole Merger,” *Physical Review Letters* 116, 061102 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.116.061102 · https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.03837
  - Primary source for GW150914 and the chapter’s distinction between repeating a class of phenomenon and repeating a singular event.

## Deep-time communication and archive precedents

- NASA, Voyager Golden Record: https://science.nasa.gov/mission/voyager/golden-record-overview/
  - Authoritative background for a deliberately cross-cultural/cross-species communication artifact and its decoding problem.
- NASA, Apollo Next Generation Sample Analysis program: https://science.nasa.gov/lunar-science/lunar-samples/
  - Starting point for the practice of preserving returned lunar material for later analytical technologies; verify specific sample-opening claims against mission/sample documentation in a publication edit.

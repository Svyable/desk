# Source Ledger

Reporting cutoff: 2026-09-01. This ledger records major sources used to ground the release-candidate manuscript. Primary institutional sources are preferred for current trial, regulatory and funding status. Company and clinic pages establish what those organizations publicly state or advertise; they are not treated as independent evidence of efficacy.

## Source discipline for future editions

The release candidate is frozen to a September 1, 2026 reporting cutoff. Later editions should refresh trial status, regulatory decisions, full INTerpath-001 effect sizes when publicly presented, overall-survival follow-up, commercial manufacturing performance, direct-pay offers and clinic pricing, and any FDA finalization or revision of the August 2026 potency guidance. Mutable clinic and company pages should be treated as dated claims rather than timeless facts. Company or provider materials must never be used as independent proof that a therapy improves survival.

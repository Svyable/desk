# Evidence ledger — When the Forest Reports Back

## Governing thesis

Defense sensing is crossing a boundary from instruments placed in environments toward systems in which living organisms and ecological processes are functional components. The surprising collision is synthetic biology + persistent surveillance + sensory ecology: metabolism and self-maintenance can become infrastructure, but they import evolution, ecological dependence and biological variability as engineering constraints.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- DARPA Persistent Aquatic Living Sensors (PALS) explicitly sought to use marine organisms' responses as the first stage of systems detecting and characterizing underwater vehicles. Official program page: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/persistent-aquatic-living-sensors
- DARPA Advanced Plant Technologies sought engineered plants as persistent ground-based sensors for CBRNE threats, with remotely monitored responses and explicit fitness/biosafety requirements. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/advanced-plant-technologies
- DARPA Quantum Effects in Biological Environments investigated whether nontrivial quantum effects contribute to biological sensing and could inform military sensor design. This documents a research question, not a successful quantum sensor. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/quantum-effects-in-biological-environments
- DARPA Tellus pursued a platform methodology for microbe-based sense-and-respond devices. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/tellus
- DARPA Convergence describes engineered non-pathogenic microbial consortia for environmental hazard sensing and an explicit regulatory/testing path. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/convergence
- DARPA BRICS pursued robustness of engineered microbial biosystems in complex environments, including sensing applications, while keeping program work in controlled laboratory settings. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/biological-robustness-in-complex-settings

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Individual performer papers and program reports may establish laboratory sensitivity, organism response, classifier performance or prototype integration. They must be reconstructed experiment-by-experiment before the manuscript assigns field performance to a program.
- Public DARPA program descriptions are authoritative for goals and program architecture but are not independent validation that target performance was achieved.
- Claims that living sensors can outperform conventional sensors in persistence, energy burden or sensitivity are plausible and sometimes measurable, but must be compared task-by-task with equivalent hardware baselines.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- No claim of covert operational deployment follows from the public research programs above.
- No public evidence reviewed here establishes an autonomous living surveillance network at strategic scale.
- No broad claim that quantum coherence explains biological sensory superiority is established by QuBE's existence.
- Anecdotes about animals predicting earthquakes, psychic sensing, or unexplained military biological detectors remain outside the evidentiary spine unless primary controlled evidence earns inclusion.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- The central synthesis: once growth, metabolism, reproduction and ecological persistence become engineering resources, sensor infrastructure acquires evolutionary and ecological failure modes that conventional hardware lacks.
- A sufficiently mature living sensor network could shift maintenance from replacement logistics toward habitat management. This is an engineering conjecture, not a documented deployment doctrine.
- Biological sensing may make concealment structurally different because the sensing element can be visually ordinary and environmentally native. Whether that advantage survives telemetry and interrogation requirements is an open systems question.

## Contradictions and incentives

Program offices have incentives to describe ambitious capability targets. Contractor teams have incentives to emphasize performance. Critics may collapse all defense synthetic biology into speculative weaponization. The manuscript must resist both directions.

A living detector's apparent energy independence can hide ecological subsidy: sunlight, nutrients, water and habitat are power infrastructure supplied by the environment. "Self-sustaining" must never mean energy-free.

Self-repair and reproduction can reduce maintenance while increasing control problems. The same evolutionary machinery that makes biology robust can select against engineered burdens.

## Strongest conventional countermodel

Living sensors remain niche research because ordinary hardware wins once specificity, calibration, communications, reproducibility, regulation and total-system logistics are counted. Biology's apparent advantages may disappear at the full-system boundary.

## Defeat condition

The book's strong infrastructure thesis should be weakened if program-by-program reconstruction shows that biological sensing repeatedly fails to progress beyond laboratory demonstrations because organism variability, environmental confounds, fitness costs and readout complexity erase persistence or sensitivity advantages.

## Publication hold

Do not describe any program as operationally deployed unless an official record or equivalent primary evidence says so. Do not use "quantum sensor" for an organism merely because QuBE studied it. Do not call living systems self-powered without naming their environmental energy source. Do not imply engineered organisms were released outside contained/regulatory settings without evidence.

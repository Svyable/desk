# Research queue — Bring Me Their Machine

## Immediate primary-source reconstruction

1. **AZORIAN at engineering resolution.** Recover the full declassified Studies in Intelligence history and engineering article. Build a dated chain from locating K-129 through feasibility, ship/capture-vehicle construction, cover, recovery, partial structural failure, exploitation, exposure, and cancellation of the second attempt. Record what remained secret for how long and what eventually leaked.
2. **HAVE DOUGHNUT / HAVE DRILL.** Recover Air Force historical studies and test reports where public. Record acquisition route, custody, range security, instrumentation, test objectives, technical findings, safety problems, dissemination restrictions, and tactical changes.
3. **CONSTANT PEG.** Reconstruct aircraft count/types, maintenance ecosystem, pilot access, Tonopah/Groom geography, security practice, training throughput, and measurable doctrinal effect. Distinguish museum retrospective from contemporaneous records.
4. **Foreign Technology Division / National Air and Space Intelligence Center lineage.** Establish the institutional history of foreign materiel exploitation and where captured hardware, documents, imagery, telemetry, and scientific intelligence enter the system.
5. **Other controls.** Soviet radar/air-defense hardware, missiles, torpedoes, submarines, electronics, captured equipment in Korea/Vietnam/Middle East conflicts, defections with aircraft, and foreign materiel acquired through allies. Prefer cases with eventual primary documentation.

## Modern allegation reconstruction

Build a claim table, not a personalities chapter. Each row should include: exact claim; speaker; date; venue; oath status; claimed access; firsthand/secondhand; named program; named custodian; alleged location; alleged material; documentary support; chain of custody; independent corroborator; contradiction; official response; unresolved question.

Priority public records:
- 26 July 2023 House Oversight UAP hearing and written statements.
- Subsequent House/Senate hearings with firsthand military witnesses and program allegations.
- Public inspector-general statements or correspondence where the document itself can be authenticated.
- AARO historical reports, methodology, named interview totals, case appendices, and official corrections.
- ODNI/DOD annual UAP reports for the boundary between anomalous observations and retrieval allegations.
- Congressional statutory language concerning UAP records, controlled disclosure, eminent domain, legacy programs, and definitions. Treat legislative language as evidence of legislative concern or proposed process, not proof that defined objects exist.

## Quantitative comparison matrix

For each historical control and modern allegation, score only observable evidence, never “plausibility”:

- physical artifact publicly acknowledged;
- acquisition/retrieval chain;
- engineering program footprint;
- specialized facilities;
- named organizations;
- contractor role;
- budget/procurement trace;
- safety/accident trace;
- oversight/document trace;
- independent firsthand witnesses;
- technical publications or derivative requirements;
- downstream doctrine/capability change;
- cover/deception evidence;
- declassification/leak chronology.

Do not sum this into a fake scientific probability. The matrix is for pattern comparison and missing-data visibility.

## Chapters that require calculations

- **How many people does a machine require?** Estimate staffing ranges from historical exploitation programs without assuming linear scaling.
- **The building around the object.** Storage, security, hazardous materials, metrology, power, cryogenics/vacuum if claimed, test stands, transport and waste streams. Separate generic classified-lab infrastructure from claim-specific requirements.
- **Reverse engineering has a clock.** Compare time from acquisition to useful tactical/technical output in documented cases. Do not assume alien technology would be tractable.
- **Leakage without revelation.** Model why personnel count alone is a poor secrecy argument; compartmentation reduces knowledge but increases interfaces.

## Falsification / strongest countermodel

The strongest conventional model is not “everyone is lying.” It is a mixture: genuine classified aerospace and foreign-materiel work; real UAP observations with heterogeneous causes; compartmentation and access denial; sincere but secondhand testimony; legacy folklore; deliberate counterintelligence ambiguity; and social network effects that make reused stories appear independent.

The strongest extraordinary model is not “all UAP are alien.” It is narrower: at least one state or contractor possesses recovered material whose properties or origin cannot be explained as ordinary human technology, and the public evidence is weak because custody and exploitation are deeply compartmented.

The book earns its ending only by identifying evidence that separates these models.

## Publication gates

Before a full draft can be called complete:
- every modern allegation must have a primary locator or be labeled secondary;
- firsthand and hearsay must be machine-auditable in the notes;
- no “crash retrieval” wording may silently imply non-human origin;
- historical controls must include failures, leaks, and mundane acquisition routes, not just spectacular successes;
- AARO claims must be quoted only from retrieved official text and subjected to access/method limits;
- whistleblower claims must be attributed precisely and subjected to the same access/method limits;
- contractor names must not be attached to possession allegations without a public attributable source;
- no patent may be described as demonstrated technology without independent evidence;
- final chapter must specify what evidence would materially update both skeptics and proponents.

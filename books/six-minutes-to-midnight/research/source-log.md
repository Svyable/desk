# Source log

Checked 2026-09-10. Public/lawfully available material only.

## Primary and official

- U.S. Department of State, FRUS 1977–1980, Vol. IV, preface. Notes that November 1979 and June 1980 nuclear-attack false alarms intensified Carter administration concern about C3I: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v04/preface
- Secretary of Defense Harold Brown to President Carter, **NORAD False Alarm**, 20 Nov 1979, FRUS doc. 167: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v04/d167
- State Department telegram, **TNF: Soviets and the False Missile Alert**, 28 Nov 1979, FRUS 1977–1980 Vol. VI doc. 234. Records the test scenario, under-six-minute rejection, no advanced strategic alert, and Brezhnev concern: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v06/d234
- Department of Defense, **Fact Sheet**, 4 Jun 1980, FRUS doc. 188. Describes erroneous computer-generated attack indications and contradictory direct sensor evidence: https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1977-80v04/d188
- KGB Headquarters Moscow, **Permanent operational assignment to uncover NATO preparations for a nuclear missile attack on the USSR**, 17 Feb 1983, reproduced by National Security Archive: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/17309-document-09-kgb-headquarters-moscow
- President's Foreign Intelligence Advisory Board, **The Soviet War Scare**, 15 Feb 1990, declassified extracts and context via National Security Archive Able Archer sourcebook: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/project/able-archer-83-sourcebook
- Lt. Gen. Leonard H. Perroots, **End of Tour Report Addendum**, Jan 1989, reproduced with archival context: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/33585-document-1-censored-frus-appendix
- National Security Archive, **The Soviet Side of the 1983 War Scare**, collection of former Soviet/Warsaw Pact records: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/aa83/2018-11-05/soviet-side-1983-war-scare
- U.S. Department of Defense, **Stratcom Commander Discusses Nuclear System Modernization**, 21 Nov 2024. Public statement on NC3 modernization, cyber/space vulnerability, AI support and human control: https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3973074/stratcom-commander-discusses-nuclear-system-modernization/

## Secondary / archival synthesis

- National Security Archive, **Able Archer War Scare 'Potentially Disastrous'**, 17 Feb 2021: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/aa83/2021-02-17/able-archer-war-scare-potentially-disastrous
- National Security Archive, **The Censored History of Able Archer 83**, 14 Nov 2025. Important also as a case study in reclassification/censorship of previously public historical material: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/able-archer-83/2025-11-14/censored-history-able-archer-83

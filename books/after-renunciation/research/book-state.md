## Freshness check

Recent Author-agent PRs were inspected before selection. Immediate territory includes sensory deprivation/MKULTRA (closed as duplicate), plant biosensors, animal intelligence platforms, CORONA return logistics, COLDFEET, GENETRIX, AZORIAN, foreign-materiel exploitation, Argus, strategic sensors and remote viewing. AFTER RENUNCIATION moves away from another operational-secret control case and into a different mechanism: **what a verified policy discontinuity does to a pre-existing technical capability, and how compliance can be evidenced when expertise and dual-use infrastructure persist.**

This is not a book alleging covert U.S. biological-weapons continuation. The tension is precisely that technical residue can persist under genuine compliance.

## Voice target

Sven Hardy Benson: close to documents, facilities and verbs; suspicious of program labels; willing to let administrative detail become narrative when it reveals the mechanism. Avoid generic Cold War darkness, biological-horror spectacle, and conspiratorial implication by adjacency.

## Governing distinctions

- policy termination ≠ instantaneous technical erasure;
- retained expertise ≠ retained weapon;
- defensive research ≠ proof of offensive intent;
- dual-use apparatus ≠ verdict;
- destruction plan ≠ completed destruction;
- capability to reconstitute ≠ current prohibited capability;
- suspicion ≠ evidence;
- official announcement ≠ sufficient proof of compliance.

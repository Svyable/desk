# Back Matter

The Apollo seismic archive is a public scientific record, not evidence of access to non-public classified material. The manuscript distinguishes contemporary mission documentation from later model-based reanalysis and from folklore.

The strongest unresolved experiments are geometric: farside and polar stations, a global broadband network, independent active-source calibration, and joint seismic/orbital/geodetic observation of shallow faulting. The strongest defeat condition for the book's central synthesis would be a new lunar dataset showing that ordinary heterogeneous-solid models cannot reproduce the long codas while a quantitatively specified large-cavity or shell model does so and simultaneously satisfies lunar mass, moment-of-inertia, gravity and other geophysical constraints.

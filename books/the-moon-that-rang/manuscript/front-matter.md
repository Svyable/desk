# The Moon That Rang

## Apollo Seismology, Moonquakes, and the Hollow-Moon Mistake

Sven Hardy Benson

The Moon did ring after Apollo deliberately struck it. That observation is real. The planetary-shell conclusion built from it is not.

This book follows the stranger story that survived measurement: a battered, low-attenuation world whose fractured megaregolith scatters seismic energy into extraordinary codas; deep quake nests whose timing follows tides; rare shallow quakes that may still move young thrust faults; artificial impacts used as controlled sources; and a near-side Apollo network that left consequential blind spots for the next generation of lunar instruments.

The evidentiary rule is simple. Observation, inference, allegation and conjecture do not get to borrow each other's certainty.

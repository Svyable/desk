## Thesis

Camp Century was not merely a strange Cold War base under Greenland ice. It was a full-scale experiment in treating a moving ice sheet as infrastructure: habitat, concealment, foundation, heat sink, waste repository, and potentially the geography for a dispersed nuclear missile force. Project Iceworm failed because the ice refused to behave like ground. Six decades later the same site has become a second experiment, this time involuntary, in what happens when strategic secrecy, engineering assumptions, abandoned waste, climate change, and sovereignty outlive the people who made them.

The book uses Camp Century as a bounded case study in technological surprise. It does not imply that every publicly described research installation concealed a weapons program, or that Iceworm was operationally deployed. The useful fact is narrower and stranger: the Army seriously studied a vast missile network beneath Greenland, built a nuclear-powered subsurface base that generated engineering data relevant to that ambition, and learned that ice is a material with memory and motion.

## Evidence classes

1. **Verified/declassified/documented:** Camp Century construction and operation; PM-2A nuclear power; Army cold-regions engineering; Project Iceworm planning; deformation problems; abandonment; documented waste inventories; later scientific recovery of the Camp Century ice core and subglacial material.
2. **Credible but incomplete:** exact boundaries between Camp Century's publicly presented research mission and Iceworm-specific experimentation; Danish knowledge at different institutional levels; some operational and engineering interpretations reconstructed from later archival scholarship.
3. **Disputed/unconfirmed:** broad claims that Denmark knowingly approved the full Iceworm missile concept, claims that an operational missile network was secretly emplaced, and retrospective anecdotes not anchored to contemporaneous records.
4. **Speculative synthesis/open conjecture:** Camp Century as a model for how physical environments can function as security compartments; the proposition that climate change acts as a delayed declassifier by exposing material residues and reopening political liabilities.

## Defeat condition

The central argument weakens if primary records show that Camp Century produced no material engineering knowledge relevant to Iceworm, or that the missile concept was merely a rhetorical proposal without serious technical, logistical, or command consideration. The climate-afterlife argument weakens if updated field measurements show the abandoned wastes pose no plausible future remobilization pathway under realistic Greenland surface-mass-balance scenarios.

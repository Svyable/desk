# Meteors Announce Themselves

A monitoring network built around human suspicion spends much of its life listening to things no government caused. Meteoroids entering the atmosphere can generate infrasound over enormous distances. Their signals help scientists estimate trajectories and energetics, and they provide naturally occurring tests of propagation models.

This is not mission creep so much as epistemic necessity. To identify forbidden explosions, analysts must understand legitimate explosions and natural ones. The catalogue of false positives becomes a scientific archive.

The planet trains the detector. Every bolide is a calibration opportunity nobody scheduled.

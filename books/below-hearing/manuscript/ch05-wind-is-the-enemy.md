# Wind Is the Enemy

The phrase sounds trivial until one builds an array. At infrasonic frequencies, ordinary atmospheric turbulence can dominate the measurement. A detector designed for tiny pressure oscillations is exquisitely capable of detecting the fact that Earth has weather.

Noise reduction is therefore not cosmetic engineering. Pipe arrays, porous hoses, spatial averaging and protected sites exist because the signal of interest must be separated from pressure variations generated close to the sensor. Analysts also need meteorological data because the medium changes while they listen.

This is where many popular accounts of exotic acoustic detection become unserious. They quote a theoretical propagation distance without carrying the noise floor along with it. Range without detectability is a geography lesson, not a sensor specification.

The same warning applies to alleged acoustic weapons. A frequency can propagate; that does not tell us what pressure level arrives at a target, what source power was required, how directional the field is, or whether the claimed biological effect survives controlled exposure. The atmosphere charges rent at every step.

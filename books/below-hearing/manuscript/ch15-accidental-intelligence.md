# Accidental Intelligence

Once a sensitive global network exists, it hears events outside its founding mission. That creates accidental intelligence: observations valuable to volcanology, meteor science, bolide studies, disaster analysis and atmospheric dynamics.

The political design matters. Treaty monitoring data sit at an unusual boundary between national-security purpose and international scientific utility. The network demonstrates that verification infrastructure can produce a commons without becoming naïve about adversaries.

There is a broader proposition here. Expensive sensors are often justified by one feared event and scientifically redeemed by the thousands of ordinary events they record instead.

# Seven Hertz

Seven hertz has the numerological charisma of a forbidden frequency. The 1972 assessment reproduced alarming reports involving animals and low-frequency exposure, including material whose provenance was weak enough that a newspaper anecdote appeared in the evidentiary stream.

This is precisely where an evidence ledger earns its keep. A claim can appear in a genuine declassified document and still be poor evidence for the underlying phenomenon. Authentic paper does not authenticate every sentence printed on it.

Biological response to low-frequency sound depends on pressure level, duration, coupling, body geometry, environment and endpoint. Frequency alone is not a dose. A story that says only '7 Hz' has omitted most of the experiment.

The weapon folklore thrives by stripping away those variables. A magic frequency is portable. A pressure field is engineering.

# The Problem of Attribution

Detection answers 'did something happen?' Attribution asks 'what was it, who did it, and why?' The distance between those questions is where intelligence failures breed.

Infrasound can contribute location and source characterization. Seismic evidence may add another geometry. Radionuclides can supply material signatures. Open-source reports may identify an industrial accident. Satellite imagery may show a launch site. Attribution strengthens when evidence streams are causally independent.

The worst practice is to count correlated traces as independent witnesses. Five sensors fed by the same atmospheric model are not necessarily five votes. A serious architecture maps dependencies before assigning confidence.

Anomalous-event research rarely does this well. It should.

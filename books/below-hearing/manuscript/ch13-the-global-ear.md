# The Global Ear

The International Monitoring System is the mature counterexample to vague stories about secret listening. It specifies facilities, sensor types, data flows and a mission. Its planned architecture includes sixty infrasound stations alongside seismic, hydroacoustic and radionuclide components. Data move to an international centre for processing and analysis.

The network has detected declared North Korean nuclear tests, while its sensors also record earthquakes, volcanoes and other natural phenomena. That coexistence is not a defect. It is what a real planetary sensor network looks like.

The phrase global ear is irresistible and slightly wrong. The system does not hear as a person hears. It samples physical fields and assembles evidence through computation, geometry and expert interpretation. That difference is why it works.

# How to Test the Weapon Claims

An infrasonic weapon claim should be made expensive in the right way. Specify frequency spectrum, sound-pressure level at the target, duration, source geometry, distance, atmospheric conditions and biological endpoint before exposure. Register the analysis. Blind subjects and evaluators where possible. Include sham exposures and audible/vibrational controls. Measure the field at the body rather than inferring it from the generator setting.

For remote-capability claims, measure power and propagation. Require the source to achieve the alleged target exposure at operational distance under realistic environmental noise. If concealment is part of the claim, test that too.

For historical claims, separate authentic program records from efficacy evidence. A budget line can establish funding. A test report can establish that a test occurred. Neither establishes success unless the result supports it and survives scrutiny.

The experiment that can kill the thesis is more valuable than another suggestive anecdote.

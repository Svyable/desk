# A Planet Full of False Positives

A useful detector must live with boredom and embarrassment. Storms, surf, quarry blasts, volcanoes, meteors, aircraft, rockets and industrial explosions populate the same world as prohibited tests. The analyst's job is not to eliminate false positives by decree but to model the population that produces them.

This is why secrecy can distort frontier subjects. If analysts reveal only the exceptional event, outsiders never see the denominator. A handful of dramatic traces float free of the millions of mundane signals against which they were judged.

The cure is a background catalogue. How often does the instrument produce this morphology? Under what weather? Near what infrastructure? What happens when analysts are blinded to the event label? The boring distribution is the foundation of extraordinary inference.

# Volcanoes in the Treaty Network

Volcanoes are inconvenient for a treaty system and generous to science. Explosive eruptions radiate infrasound that arrays can detect and characterize. Networks intended to enforce human agreements therefore become instruments for watching mountains.

The transfer goes both ways. Volcanology provides rich examples of source processes, repeated events and ground truth. Those examples improve methods for detection and discrimination. A sensor architecture designed around adversaries gets better by studying nature honestly.

There is something institutionally beautiful about this. The infrastructure of suspicion becomes infrastructure of observation. It is one of the few recurring ways classified or security-driven science repays the public without needing to reveal every original motivation.

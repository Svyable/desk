# Below Hearing

**Subtitle:** Infrasound, Secret Listening, and the Planetary Signals We Cannot Hear

**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson

**Status:** Drafting

## Thesis

The atmosphere is an acoustic intelligence medium. Long-period pressure waves from explosions, rockets, meteors, volcanoes, storms, aircraft, and industrial events can travel extraordinary distances below human hearing. The same physics that made infrasound attractive to Cold War weapons intelligence now underwrites a global treaty-verification network—and also generated a parallel folklore of infrasonic mind control and secret sonic weapons that must be separated from measured bioeffects.

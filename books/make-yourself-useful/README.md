How Ordinary People Build Trust, Opportunity, and a Life of Their Own.

Most people wait to be chosen. Useful people start making the situation better before anyone asks them to. Over time, usefulness becomes reputation; reputation becomes access; access becomes opportunity; and opportunity becomes the ability to choose your own life.

The book follows ordinary people who notice the unclaimed problem, carry more than their official job requires, and turn small acts of usefulness into trust, access, and eventually freedom. AI is an important thread because it makes basic production cheaper and raises the value of context, judgment, responsibility, and trust, but it is not the book's identity. The argument should still feel useful in 2037.

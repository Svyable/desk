What Remains Valuable When Intelligence Is Everywhere.

*Ubiquitous* asks what happens to work when competent digital execution becomes an ordinary property of software. AI raises the floor for people who begin without a marketable specialist skill, while putting pressure on workers whose scarcity comes mainly from routine digital production, interface navigation, standardized synthesis, or stable symbolic procedures.

The book argues against both easy refuges: one narrow vertical expertise is increasingly callable inside model-and-tool harnesses, but “work with your hands” is not a blanket defense either. Durable advantage migrates toward diagnosis, verification, system integration, skilled intervention in messy physical environments, local trust, ownership of outcomes, and judgment where several real systems disagree. That makes the next decade unusually favorable to people who combine a genuine craft with persistent cross-domain curiosity—the renaissance worker with connected depths rather than shallow generalism.

## Central thesis defeat condition

The book's central synthesis would be weakened if comparative analysis showed that long-duration, hazardous, hardware-intensive frontier programs routinely reach operational scale without generating any meaningful physical, administrative, test, accident, training, supply-chain or archival residue even after decades. The thesis is probabilistic, not absolute: residue can be hidden, ambiguous or delayed.

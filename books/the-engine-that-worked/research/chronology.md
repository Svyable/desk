# Chronology

- **1955:** NACA publishes analysis of nuclear-powered ramjet missile concepts; broader U.S. nuclear-propulsion work is active.
- **1957:** AEC/Air Force place nuclear ramjet work at Lawrence Radiation Laboratory; Project Pluto begins in its mature institutional form.
- **1959:** Theodore Merkle's *The Nuclear Ramjet Propulsion System* and Hadley's Tory II-A design report document the architecture and test plan.
- **1960:** Tory II-A instrumentation documentation describes remote measurement/control arrangements.
- **May 14, 1961:** Tory II-A conducts a major powered test at Nevada Test Site after low-power measurements and facility shakedown.
- **1961–1963:** Follow-on testing and Tory II-C design/materials work continue; extensive technical documentation is produced.
- **1963:** Tory II-C data book compiles design, neutronics, materials and intended test performance.
- **Spring 1964:** Tory II-C reaches successful high-power ground testing; institutional histories describe roughly 500 MW and a five-minute test.
- **July 1, 1964:** Project Pluto is cancelled by AEC/Air Force after the reactor technology has demonstrated substantial technical success but before full vehicle flight test.
- **November 1964:** Engineering-design report for Tory II-C records culmination of the 1957–64 development effort.
- **1973:** Major Tory documentation is declassified, creating a large technical archival seam.
- **March 1, 2018:** Vladimir Putin publicly announces a nuclear-powered cruise missile later identified as Burevestnik/9M730.
- **August 8, 2019:** Accident near Nenoksa kills personnel and produces radiological concern; U.S. officials later associate event with recovery of a Burevestnik test unit and a nuclear reaction. Exact public reconstruction remains incomplete.
- **October 21, 2025:** Russian officials later claim Burevestnik completed a roughly 14,000-km, 15-hour flight. Treat figures as official claims pending independent corroboration.
- **October 26, 2025:** Putin/Gerasimov discuss the claimed test publicly; Putin says employment and infrastructure work remain.
- **September 2026:** This manuscript freezes its modern evidence boundary at publicly available sources and does not infer operational status beyond them.

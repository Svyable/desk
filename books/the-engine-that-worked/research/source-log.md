## Institutional histories

10. Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, **1955 Nuclear Propulsion**, 65th anniversary history. Records shift to Pluto, Tory II-A / II-C testing, roughly 500 MW II-C output, six tests without failure, and termination for lack of firm military commitment. https://www.llnl.gov/sites/www/files/llnl_65th_anniversary_book.pdf
11. U.S. Department of Energy / Nevada National Security Site, **Battlefield of the Cold War: The Nevada Test Site**. Pluto facility, heated-air plant, test history and cancellation context. https://www.osti.gov/opennet/manhattan-project-history/publications/DOENTSAtmospheric.pdf
12. Nevada National Security Site, **NNSS History / Project Pluto material**, including Site 401 and Tory test summaries. https://nnss.gov/publication-library/anniversary-publications/

## Contemporary strategic/program context

13. J. S. Butz Jr., **Pluto: A New Strategic System or Just Another Test Program?**, Air Force Magazine, July 1964. Contemporary record of the fight over moving from successful reactor work to costly flight/system development. https://www.airandspaceforces.com/article/0764pluto/

## Modern nuclear-powered cruise-missile context

14. U.S. Department of State archive, **Report Relating to Ongoing Implementation of the New START Treaty**. Public U.S. description of Burevestnik/Skyfall and the 2019 accident assessment. https://2017-2021.state.gov/report-relating-to-ongoing-implementation-of-the-new-start-treaty/
15. Arms Control Association, **U.S. Reveals Assessment of Russian Explosion**, Nov. 2019. Attributes U.S. official assessment concerning the Nenoksa recovery accident. https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2019-11/news/us-reveals-assessment-russian-explosion
16. Arms Control Association, **Russia Tests Nuclear-Powered Cruise Missile, Torpedo**, Nov. 2025. Reports Russian official claim that Burevestnik flew 14,000 km for 15 hours on Oct. 21, 2025, and Putin's statement that employment/infrastructure work remained. https://www.armscontrol.org/act/2025-11/news-briefs/russia-tests-nuclear-powered-cruise-missile-torpedo
17. Defense Intelligence Agency, **Golden Dome for America: Current and Future Missile Threats to the U.S. Homeland**, released May 13, 2025. Current unclassified missile-threat context. https://www.dia.mil/articles/press-release/article/4182231/dia-releases-golden-dome-missile-threat-assessment/

## Use notes

- Patents, where encountered, are evidence of filing and concept disclosure only, not efficacy.
- Russian official performance claims are attributed, not silently promoted to independently verified fact.
- Later popular descriptions of SLAM as intentionally using reactor exhaust or shock effects as primary weapons require source-level checking before use; the manuscript does not rely on those claims.
- Anniversary histories are useful but secondary to technical reports for exact performance numbers.

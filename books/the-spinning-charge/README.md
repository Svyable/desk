## Thesis

General relativity really does contain a weak-field gravitoelectromagnetic analogy, rotating matter really does drag inertial frames, and precision nuclear/clock experiments really can measure tiny relativistic shifts. Those facts make “gravitational-charge dualism” tempting—and dangerous. This book asks whether any laboratory evidence justifies promoting the analogy into a new charge-like sector that could be engineered, with superconductors, rotating masses and UAP propulsion claims serving as stress tests rather than premises.

# The AI Value Stack

1. **Energy and land** — generation, transmission, substations, interconnections, powered sites.
2. **Data-center infrastructure** — buildings, cooling, electrical systems, backup generation, racks, fiber.
3. **Compute hardware** — accelerators, CPUs, memory, networking, storage, packaging.
4. **Cloud and capacity markets** — pooled compute, reservations, scheduling, managed infrastructure.
5. **Models** — frontier and specialized systems, proprietary and open-weight.
6. **Tools and agent infrastructure** — orchestration, evaluation, memory, routing, security, observability.
7. **Enterprise workflows** — software embedded in valuable processes.
8. **Distribution and customer trust** — access, brand, regulated relationships, marketplaces.
9. **End-user economic outcomes** — revenue, savings, risk reduction, throughput, quality.

**Core rule:** Technical importance does not guarantee economic capture. Ask where switching is difficult, supply is disciplined, learning compounds, and customer willingness to pay is tied to outcomes.

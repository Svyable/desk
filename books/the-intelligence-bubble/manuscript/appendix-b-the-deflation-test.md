# The Deflation Test

Ask:

> If the cost of intelligence falls by 90 percent, does the company earn more, earn less, or become irrelevant?

**Earns more**

- Workflow owners whose cost of service falls.
- Companies that use AI to improve a larger transaction or product.
- Distributors able to embed cheap intelligence across an installed base.
- Businesses selling outcomes rather than raw compute.

**Earns less**

- Undifferentiated model resellers.
- Capacity owners with high fixed costs and weak utilization.
- Providers whose pricing depends on persistent technical scarcity.

**Becomes irrelevant**

- Thin wrappers around temporary model access.
- Products whose feature is absorbed by a platform.
- Intermediaries whose only function was navigating a scarcity that disappears.

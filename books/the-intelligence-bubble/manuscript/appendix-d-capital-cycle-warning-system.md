# Capital-Cycle Warning System

Watch for clusters of the following signals:

- Debt and lease obligations rising faster than operating cash flow.
- Capex accelerating faster than monetized demand.
- Less precise disclosure around utilization.
- Unit prices falling despite strong volume growth.
- Longer contracts compensating for weaker spot demand.
- Customer concentration rising.
- Rapid changes in depreciation assumptions or equipment useful lives becoming central to earnings.
- Increasing reliance on adjusted earnings while free cash flow deteriorates.
- Vendor financing or cross-investments that blur independent end demand.
- Large future lease commitments for capacity not yet in service.
- Refinancing requirements colliding with a higher cost of capital.
- A rapid expansion in AI IPO supply.
- Valuations dependent on distant margins far above competitive analogues.

No single item proves a bubble. The pattern matters.

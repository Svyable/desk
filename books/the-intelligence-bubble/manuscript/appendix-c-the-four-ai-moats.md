# The Four AI Moats

**Data moat** — proprietary, rights-cleared, high-quality data connected to a compounding feedback loop.

**Workflow moat** — deep integration into a difficult-to-replace process with accumulated operational context.

**Distribution moat** — trusted access to customers, users, or regulated markets.

**Cost moat** — a structural ability to deliver equivalent outcomes more cheaply.

A fifth advantage often appears in high-consequence markets: **responsibility**—the ability to stand behind the outcome with auditability, insurance, compliance, and a balance sheet.

4. **Frontier model economics:** obtain public or attributable commentary on inference margins, price cuts, capacity commitments, and the mix of training versus inference demand.
5. **Post-bubble historical analogue:** reconstruct one telecom/fiber asset from boom financing through restructuring to later productive use.
6. **Workflow winner:** profile a company whose AI value comes from measurable outcomes and integrations rather than model ownership.

## Editorial rule for source use

The book's strongest claim is a mechanism, not a prediction. Sources should therefore be used to demonstrate:

- the scale and financing of committed capacity;
- the rate of technical and price deflation;
- the physical constraints beneath AI;
- historical examples of productive overbuild;
- and the distribution of bargaining power across the value stack.

Avoid sourcing the book primarily with opinions that “AI is a bubble” or “AI is not a bubble.” The argument should remain independently derivable from cash flow, capital intensity, competition, and history.

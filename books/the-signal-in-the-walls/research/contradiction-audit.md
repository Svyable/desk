# Contradiction and incentive audit

## Contradictions preserved

- The Moscow microwave exposure is verified, while its biological-weapon purpose is not.
- U.S. agencies considered biological effects seriously, yet declassified PANDORA/BIZARRE material contains negative or deflationary findings when exposure estimates were corrected.
- The microwave auditory effect is established, while the practicality of weaponizing it for covert injury remains disputed and constrained.
- NASEM 2020 and the IC Experts Panel 2022 found pulsed RF mechanistically plausible for a subset of AHI features; the NIC 2023 assessment nevertheless found foreign-adversary causation very unlikely for the aggregate case set.
- Government support/compensation for affected personnel does not require solved causation and therefore cannot be used as attribution evidence.

## Incentives

### Soviet/Russian state
Could benefit from concealing espionage methods, minimizing health implications, or encouraging adversary misinterpretation.

### U.S. intelligence agencies
Could benefit from protecting technical collection methods and detector capabilities; can also suffer institutional pressure to identify a hostile actor or to close an expensive investigation.

### State Department
Has simultaneous obligations to protect personnel, preserve diplomatic relationships, manage liability, and avoid revealing sensitive countermeasures.

### Affected personnel
Have strong incentives for accurate recognition, care, and compensation; symptom legitimacy does not determine external cause.

### Researchers
May face publication incentives favoring positive bioeffects or high-salience mechanisms; negative replication and dosimetry correction deserve deliberate recovery.

### Journalists / advocates / skeptics
All can gain from narrative compression. The manuscript therefore privileges primary instrument records, official assessments with scope labels, and methods-level scientific work.

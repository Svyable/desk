# Source Log — The Signal in the Walls

Working source hierarchy: primary/declassified and official records first; peer-reviewed medical and biophysical literature second; serious secondary reconstruction third. A source proves only the proposition it actually documents.

## Core declassified / archival spine

- National Security Archive, **“The Moscow Signals Declassified Microwave Mysteries: Projects PANDORA and BIZARRE”** (September 13, 2022). Collection overview with links to declassified PANDORA/BIZARRE records, embassy security material, health-study records, and congressional material. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence-russia-programs/2022-09-13/moscow-signals-declassified-microwave
- National Security Archive, **“The Moscow Signals Declassified: Microwave Diplomacy, 1967–1977”** (September 15, 2022). Diplomatic chronology and documents. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence-russia-programs/2022-09-15/moscow-signals-declassified-microwave
- White House / Jerome Wiesner, **“Bugging of the U.S. Embassy, Moscow”**, Secret memorandum to Clark Clifford, June 24, 1964. Documents the known listening-device environment and Wiesner’s recommendation to understand or stop the microwave signal. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/28785-document-1-white-house-jerome-wiesner-report-clark-clifford-bugging-us-embassy
- ARPA / Richard S. Cesaro, **“Project BIZARRE”**, Top Secret memorandum, September 27, 1967. Critical for the corrected Moscow power-density estimate, the lack of overt primate performance degradation at the corrected exposure, and contemplation of controlled human testing. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/28792-document-8-arpa-richard-s-cesaro-memorandum-project-bizarre-top-secret
- ARPA / George H. Heilmeier, letter to congressional chairman, 1977. Important negative boundary: DARPA stated it did not foresee developing microwave weapons to alter nervous-system function or behavior and was unaware of U.S. or adversary forces developing such weapons. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/28798-document-14-arpa-george-h-heilmeier-letter-chairman-house-committee-commerce-science
- National Security Archive collection documents for 1975–76 Moscow cables and White House diplomacy. Use original document images/OCR from the two collection pages above when tightening date-specific passages.

## Great Seal / technical espionage precedent

- National Security Agency, National Cryptologic Museum materials on the Soviet **Great Seal bug / Thing**. Passive resonant-cavity listening device presented in 1945 and discovered in 1952. Use as proof of a documented RF-assisted espionage mechanism, not as proof that later Moscow microwave signals served the same device architecture. https://www.nsa.gov/History/National-Cryptologic-Museum/Exhibits-Artifacts/Artifact-View/Article/2718992/great-seal-bug/
- U.S. Department of State, National Museum of American Diplomacy, material on the **bugged brick** from the U.S. Embassy in Moscow. Physical evidence of the broader technical-penetration environment. https://diplomacy.state.gov/items/bugged-brick-from-u-s-embassy-moscow/

## Epidemiology / medicine / bioeffects

- Abraham M. Lilienfeld et al., **Foreign Service Health Status Study** / Moscow embassy cohort work. Recover and page-index the full original report before publication-quality expansion; do not rely only on later summaries.
- J. Mark Elwood, **“Microwaves in the cold war: the Moscow embassy study and its interpretation. Review of a retrospective cohort study”** (2012). Peer-reviewed methodological reassessment of the Moscow cohort and causal claims. PubMed search record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=Elwood+Moscow+embassy+microwaves+2012
- José Martínez, **“The ‘Moscow signal’ epidemiological study, 40 years on”** (2019). Reassessment emphasizing unresolved methodological and follow-up questions. PubMed search record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=Moscow+signal+epidemiological+study+40+years+on
- John R. Goldsmith, **“Radiofrequency (RF) sickness in the Lilienfeld Study: an effect of modulated microwaves?”** Later reanalysis arguing that selected findings warrant attention. Treat as a contested interpretation of the cohort, not as proof of causation. PubMed search record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=Goldsmith+Lilienfeld+radiofrequency+sickness
- J. H. Merritt et al., 1978 blood-brain-barrier replication work. Useful example of a proposed microwave effect that appeared under hyperthermic warm-air conditions rather than the tested microwave condition. PubMed search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=Merritt+microwave+blood+brain+barrier+1978
- High-power microwave cataract literature. Use only for thermal-effect scale comparison; never import cataract findings at hundreds of mW/cm² into chronic embassy exposures in the µW/cm² range. PubMed search: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/?term=microwave+cataract+rabbit+monkey+power+density

## Modern anomalous health incidents — bounded comparator only

- Office of the Director of National Intelligence, **Updated Assessment of Anomalous Health Incidents**, January 10, 2025. Most IC components continued to assess foreign-adversary responsibility as very unlikely overall; minority components retained greater uncertainty about a small subset and possible relevant capabilities. This is an attribution assessment, not a statement that reported symptoms were unreal. https://www.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/NIC-Unclassified-ICA-Updated-Assessment-AHI-December2024.pdf
- ODNI, **Updated Assessment of Anomalous Health Incidents**, March 1, 2023. Earlier coordinated assessment used for chronology and comparison. https://www.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/NIC-Unclassified-ICA-Updated-Assessment-AHI-March2023.pdf
- U.S. Government Accountability Office, **Havana Syndrome: Better Patient Communication and Monitoring of Key DOD Tasks Needed to Better Ensure Timely Treatment**, GAO-24-106593, July 29, 2024. Care/registry evidence. Of 334 AHI patients qualified for Military Health System care in January 2024, 33 were entered in the registry by May 2024. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-24-106593
- National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine, **An Assessment of Illness in U.S. Government Employees and Their Families at Overseas Embassies** (2020). Recover and cite the official report directly in the next research pass; use its directed pulsed RF conclusion only with the committee’s limitations and subset language intact.

## Source discipline

- Classification marking establishes sensitivity, not truth.
- A government research program establishes institutional interest, not efficacy.
- A patent establishes filing, not performance.
- A safety standard establishes a regulatory choice under stated assumptions, not secret knowledge.
- A testimony establishes what the witness reports; corroboration is needed for technical or causal claims.
- A biological effect at one exposure does not transfer to another without matching frequency, waveform, power, duration, geometry, and relevant dose metric.
- Technical plausibility is not attribution.
- The existence of the Moscow Signal is not evidence that a later AHI was caused by the same mechanism.

# Source log

Checked 2026-09-14.

## Primary/declassified/official

- National Security Archive Moscow Signal collection and document guide: https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence-russia-programs/2022-09-13/moscow-signals-declassified-microwave — curated declassified U.S. documents; strongest accessible spine for TUMS/MUTS, PANDORA/BIZARRE, diplomatic protests, revised power estimates, embassy briefing history, and technical-purpose alternatives.
- CIA Reading Room, 1977 press clipping on continuing Moscow microwaves and State Department medical review: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp88b01125r000300120030-8 — useful as a contemporaneous public-record trace, not as independent scientific evidence.
- ODNI, IC Experts Panel summary, 2 Feb 2022: https://archive.dni.gov/index.php/newsroom/reports-publications/reports-publications-2022/3591-complementary-efforts-on-anomalous-health-incidents — mechanism assessment, explicitly not attribution.
- NIC, Updated Assessment of Anomalous Health Incidents, 1 Mar 2023: https://www.dni.gov/files/ODNI/documents/assessments/NIC-Unclassified-ICA-Updated-Assessment-AHI-March2023.pdf — current public IC attribution baseline used here.
- State Department HIRTF archive: https://2021-2025.state.gov/health-incident-response-task-force/ — care/administrative history and separation of support from unresolved causation.

## Scientific

- Frey 1962: https://doi.org/10.1152/jappl.1962.17.4.689 — primary human microwave auditory report.
- Foster & Finch 1974: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.185.4147.256 — thermoacoustic evidence.
- Guy et al. 1975: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1749-6632.1975.tb35996.x — mammalian auditory and physical-material work.
- Lin 2007: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17495664/ — mechanism/threshold review.
- Foster, Garrett & Ziskin 2021: https://doi.org/10.3389/fpubh.2021.788613 — weaponization constraints and caution about AHI inference.
- National Academies 2020: https://nap.nationalacademies.org/catalog/25889/ — directed RF judged plausible for important features of reviewed cases, with major data limitations.

## Source handling rule

Program existence is evidence of investigation, not efficacy. Patents are evidence of filing, not fielded capability. Government mechanism assessments are not converted into adversary attribution. Government attribution assessments are not converted into medical dismissal.

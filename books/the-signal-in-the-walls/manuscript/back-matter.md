# Back Matter

## Evidence posture

This manuscript is declassified-record-forward, mechanism-open, weaponization-skeptical, and attribution-strict. It treats the Moscow microwave exposure as verified, Project PANDORA/BIZARRE as documented research, the microwave auditory effect as established, and the biological-weapon purpose of the Moscow Signal as unproven. For modern AHIs, it preserves the National Academies and 2022 IC expert-panel finding that pulsed RF is a plausible mechanism for a subset of reported features while giving substantial weight to the 2023 Intelligence Community assessment that a foreign adversary is very unlikely to explain the aggregate set of reported incidents.

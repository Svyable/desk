# The Ethical Rupture

Consent changes the scientific object.

A volunteer who knows a chamber door will open on request is not experiencing the same environment as a prisoner who cannot leave. A patient who believes a physician is treating illness is not situated like a subject knowingly participating in research. The physiology may overlap. The meaning does not.

This ruins many casual historical comparisons and should.

It also complicates attempts to infer interrogation capability from laboratory deprivation. Control, threat, uncertainty, humiliation, social dependency, sleep, nutrition, and expectation interact. Removing those variables for experimental cleanliness may remove the very mechanism one is trying to understand; adding them creates ethical constraints that make controlled study difficult or unacceptable.

There are questions civilization should prefer to answer imperfectly.

# Soviet Chambers

Declassified U.S. reporting on Soviet space medicine described prolonged stays in soundproof chambers and disagreement among Soviet investigators about their effects. Some reported impaired mental abilities or fatigue; others reported no psychopathological phenomena in cosmonaut tests.

Contradiction is not debris to sweep away. It is data about protocol, selection, duration, measurement, publication, and institutional incentive.

The Soviet program also exposes a recurring problem in Cold War science: each side observed fragments of the other's literature through translation, intelligence collection, public congresses, and inference. A foreign claim could become more definite while crossing the border.

For this book, Soviet work belongs partly in evidence class two until the underlying primary literature is checked case by case. A CIA translation is evidence that U.S. analysts read a claim, not automatic confirmation of the claim.

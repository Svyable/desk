# Spacecraft Without Space

Space agencies learned that one can test parts of spaceflight without leaving Earth. Seal a small crew into a habitat. Restrict communication. Control lighting. Delay messages. Compress privacy. Give them procedures, failures, science, exercise, and too much time together.

NASA's HERA habitat is roughly 650 square feet and has supported 45-day simulated missions. The agency explicitly studies isolation, confinement, remote conditions, behavioral health, communication, and performance there.

The analog is valuable because it is fake in known ways. There is no lethal vacuum outside. Rescue is possible. Gravity remains. Everyone knows Earth is nearby. A serious experiment catalogs these mismatches rather than hiding them.

Simulation becomes science when the boundary conditions are part of the result.

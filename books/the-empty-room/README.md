# The Empty Room

**The Empty Room: Sensory Deprivation, Brainwashing Science, and the Engineering of Human Isolation**

Status: Drafting

18 chapters.

This book follows one peculiar experimental chamber from Cold War coercion research into submarines, Antarctic stations, spacecraft analogs, and Mars mission planning. The claim is not that isolation reliably controls minds. It is that institutions repeatedly discovered something narrower and more useful: remove variation, social reference, sleep stability, and ordinary sensory anchors, and human cognition becomes a different engineering problem.

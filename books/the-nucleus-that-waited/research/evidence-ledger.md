# Evidence Ledger

## Class 1 — Verified / declassified / documented

- Nuclear isomers are metastable excited nuclear states with lifetimes far longer than ordinary excited states.
- Hafnium-178m2 has a roughly 31-year half-life and MeV-scale excitation energy.
- Collins and collaborators published claims of x-ray-induced enhanced gamma emission from 178m2Hf.
- LLNL/LANL/Argonne conducted an independent APS experiment and reported no detectable accelerated decay, with upper limits far below the earlier claim.
- DARPA sponsored investigation of stimulated isomer energy release.
- Thorium-229 possesses an unusually low-energy nuclear isomer near 8.4 eV.
- Direct laser excitation / precision laser spectroscopy of the 229Th isomer was achieved in 2024.
- 2024–2025 work measured nuclear-clock frequency ratios and environmental/systematic shifts relevant to clock construction.

## Class 2 — Credible but incomplete

- Particular induced-decay pathways in nuclear isomers other than the original hafnium claim may be useful under specialized plasma or resonant conditions.
- A thorium-229 nuclear clock may provide unusually high sensitivity to variation of fundamental constants; the enhancement magnitude remains nuclear-model dependent.
- Some nonpublic DARPA-era experiments may contain useful technical detail unavailable in the public literature.

## Class 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

- The original soft-x-ray 178m2Hf triggering enhancement at a magnitude useful for energy release.
- Claims that DARPA's nonpublic TRIP work independently proved practical hafnium triggering.
- Claims that a compact hafnium gamma weapon reached credible engineering maturity.
- Claims that suppression, rather than failed replication and poor engineering economics, explains the decline of the program.

## Class 4 — Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- Nuclear-isomer engineering may eventually find useful energy-storage applications in a different isotope or transition regime.
- Thorium-229 clock networks could become sensitive probes of ultralight dark matter or temporal variation in constants if required precision and systematic control are achieved.
- The hafnium/thorium contrast is a general model for separating a failed application from a surviving scientific neighborhood.

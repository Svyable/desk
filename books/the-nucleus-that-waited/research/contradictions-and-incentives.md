# Contradictions, Incentives, and Failure Modes

## Application inflation

A small induced spectral effect can acquire enormous implied value once translated into weapon, propulsion or energy-storage language. Track every extrapolation from measured count-rate change to cross section to macroscopic gain to device.

## Replication asymmetry

Collins-linked work reported positive effects; major independent laboratory work did not reproduce the required magnitude. Weight experiments by sensitivity and independence, not paper count.

## Defense-program ambiguity

DARPA sponsorship proves interest and expenditure, not success. Conversely, public failure does not prove every internal result was negative. Do not convert missing classified material into positive evidence.

## Institutional incentives

Program advocates benefit from preserving a path to application. Critics may benefit reputationally from making the episode a symbol of Pentagon pseudoscience. National laboratories have incentives toward careful public claims but are not disinterested referees. Use experimental entailment rather than institutional prestige.

## Retrospective vindication error

Thorium-229 laser excitation is not evidence that the hafnium-178m2 triggering claim was correct. The nuclei, transitions, energies and mechanisms differ sharply.

## Retrospective ridicule error

Failure of hafnium weaponization is not evidence that nuclear-isomer control is a meaningless research category. Modern thorium spectroscopy demonstrates a real neighboring regime.

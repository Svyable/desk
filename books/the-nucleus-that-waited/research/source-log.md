# Source Log — The Nucleus That Waited

## Hafnium-178m2 and induced gamma emission

1. Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, **Physicists Challenge Reports of Accelerated Decay of Nuclear Excited State**, August 13, 2001. Institutional summary of the LLNL/LANL/Argonne APS replication and null result. https://www.llnl.gov/article/28056/physicists-challenge-reports-accelerated-

2. I. Ahmad et al., **Search for X-Ray Induced Acceleration of the Decay of the 31-Yr Isomer of 178Hf Using Synchrotron Radiation**, *Physical Review Letters* 87, 072503 (2001). Primary APS null: no induced decay; energy-integrated cross-section limit below 2 × 10^-27 cm² keV over 20–60 keV, more than five orders below the earlier reported value; 8-keV limit 5 × 10^-26 cm² keV. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.87.072503

3. Carl B. Collins et al., **Accelerated Emission of Gamma Rays from the 31-yr Isomer of 178Hf Induced by X-Ray Irradiation**, *Physical Review Letters* 82, 695 (1999). Original positive claim: roughly 4% increases in selected decay-cascade transitions after dental x-ray irradiation, interpreted as an energy-integrated triggering cross section near 10^-21 cm² keV. Treat as published claim, not established effect. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.82.695

4. LLNL, **A Nucleus with Energy to Burn? Reality Check**, *Science & Technology Review* (July/August 2005), institutional retrospective on the APS null and isomer-energy concept. https://str.llnl.gov/sites/str/files/2024-04/2005.07.pdf

5. E. V. Tkalya et al., **Induced decay of a long-lived nuclear isomer 178m2Hf in a laser thermonuclear plasma**, *Physical Review C* 82, 034607 (2010). Theoretical/alternative-regime work; do not conflate with soft-x-ray triggering. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.82.034607

## Thorium-229 nuclear clock

6. A. Yamaguchi et al., **Laser spectroscopy of triply charged 229Th isomer for a nuclear clock**, *Nature* 629, 62–66 (2024). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07296-1

7. J. Tiedau et al., **Laser Excitation of the Nuclear Isomeric Transition in 229Th**, *Physical Review Letters* 132, 182501 (2024). Direct resonant laser excitation in a crystal. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.132.182501

8. C. Zhang et al., **Frequency ratio of the 229mTh nuclear isomeric transition and the 87Sr atomic clock**, *Nature* 633, 63–70 (2024). Priority for clock-comparison chapter.

9. J. S. Higgins et al., **Temperature Sensitivity of a Thorium-229 Solid-State Nuclear Clock**, *Physical Review Letters* 134, 113801 (2025). NIST record: https://www.nist.gov/publications/temperature-sensitivity-thorium-229-solid-state-nuclear-clock

10. K. Beeks et al., **Fine-structure constant sensitivity of the Th-229 nuclear clock transition**, *Nature Communications* (2025). NIST record: https://www.nist.gov/publications/fine-structure-constant-sensitivity-th-229-nuclear-clock-transition

11. APS Physics, Peter Thirolf, **Shedding Light on the Thorium-229 Nuclear Clock Isomer**, April 29, 2024. Useful expert overview of the 8.35574 eV transition and clock significance. https://physics.aps.org/articles/v17/71

# Chapter Evidence Ledger — A Nation in Their Wake

This ledger maps each chapter’s central claim to checkable sources, a serious rival ship, and explicit uncertainty. Source identifiers refer to `source-ledger.csv`.

## Ch 1 — 1770s — *Bonhomme Richard*
- **Central claim:** An improvised coalition warship created American naval legitimacy and reputational effect beyond permanent U.S. naval infrastructure.
- **Primary support:** ch01-001; ch01-002.
- **Rival:** *Alfred* / *Ranger*.
- **Uncertainty:** Exact wording of Jones’s reply is later recollection; paraphrase or note limits.

## Ch 2 — 1780s — *Empress of China*
- **Central claim:** Direct American China trade demonstrated commercial sovereignty before the Constitution.
- **Primary support:** ch02-001; ch02-002.
- **Rival:** Atlantic Confederation diplomatic vessels.
- **Uncertainty:** One voyage lowers uncertainty; it does not make Pacific empire inevitable.

## Ch 3 — 1790s — USS *Constitution*
- **Central claim:** Naval Act frigates mark federal acceptance that commerce required permanent naval capacity.
- **Primary support:** ch03-001; ch03-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Constellation*.
- **Uncertainty:** Six frigates bought selective leverage, not Atlantic command.

## Ch 4 — 1800s — USS *Chesapeake*
- **Central claim:** The *Leopard* attack exposed sovereignty claims without readiness.
- **Primary support:** ch04-001; ch04-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Philadelphia*.
- **Uncertainty:** Road to 1812 is real but not automatic; Embargo shows failed alternatives.

## Ch 5 — 1810s — *Savannah*
- **Central claim:** Hybrid steam could cross the Atlantic before the business model worked.
- **Primary support:** ch05-001; ch05-002.
- **Rival:** Mississippi steamboats.
- **Uncertainty:** Qualify “first steamship”; most of voyage under sail.

## Ch 6 — 1820s — USS *Vincennes*
- **Central claim:** Distant cruising made overseas commercial interest a federal habit before a Pacific coastline.
- **Primary support:** ch06-001; ch06-002.
- **Rival:** River steam / Erie Canal domestic connection.
- **Uncertainty:** Circumnavigation completed 1830; presence is not ownership.

## Ch 7 — 1830s — *La Amistad*
- **Central claim:** Revolt and litigation forced courts to recognize kidnapped Africans as persons inside a slaveholding republic.
- **Primary support:** ch07-001; ch07-002.
- **Rival:** Domestic coastal slave-trade vessel.
- **Uncertainty:** Decision did not abolish U.S. slavery.

## Ch 8 — 1840s — SS *California*
- **Central claim:** Mail steamers and the Gold Rush made Pacific-coast connection a logistics systems problem.
- **Primary support:** ch08-001; ch08-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Princeton* / Mexican War vessels.
- **Uncertainty:** Ship did not create the Gold Rush.

## Ch 9 — 1850s — USS *Susquehanna*
- **Central claim:** Steam gunboat diplomacy made Pacific access a coercive U.S. objective.
- **Primary support:** ch09-001; ch09-002.
- **Rival:** Clipper *Flying Cloud* or another Perry vessel.
- **Uncertainty:** Perry did not author Meiji Japan; Japanese agency essential.

## Ch 10 — 1860s — USS *Monitor*
- **Central claim:** Hampton Roads made wooden-fleet obsolescence politically undeniable.
- **Primary support:** ch10-001; ch10-002.
- **Rival:** CSS *Alabama*.
- **Uncertainty:** Ironclads already abroad; *Monitor* foundered within a year.

## Ch 11 — 1870s — SS *City of Peking*
- **Central claim:** Transpacific steam concentrated commerce and Chinese migration into federal exclusion politics.
- **Primary support:** ch11-001; ch11-002.
- **Rival:** Another Pacific Mail liner.
- **Uncertainty:** Representativeness over singularity.

## Ch 12 — 1880s — USS *Dolphin*
- **Central claim:** First completed New Navy steel vessel marks restarted peacetime procurement learning.
- **Primary support:** ch12-001; ch12-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Chicago*.
- **Uncertainty:** Threshold ship, not mature battle fleet.

## Ch 13 — 1890s — USS *Maine*
- **Central claim:** Disputed explosion accelerated a war whose settlement enlarged U.S. overseas jurisdiction.
- **Primary support:** ch13-001; ch13-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Olympia*.
- **Uncertainty:** Physical cause remains disputed.

## Ch 14 — 1900s — USS *Connecticut*
- **Central claim:** Great White Fleet turned industrial naval capacity into global signaling and logistics audit.
- **Primary support:** ch14-001; ch14-002.
- **Rival:** Another fleet battleship.
- **Uncertainty:** Pre-dreadnought obsolescence; foreign colliers supported cruise.

## Ch 15 — 1910s — RMS *Lusitania*
- **Central claim:** Civilian liner sinking transformed neutrality politics long before April 1917.
- **Primary support:** ch15-001; ch15-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Leviathan*.
- **Uncertainty:** Not an automatic two-year fuse to war.

## Ch 16 — 1920s — USS *Lexington* (CV-2)
- **Central claim:** Treaty limits converted battlecruiser hulls into large carriers that scaled aviation learning.
- **Primary support:** ch16-001; ch16-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Langley*.
- **Uncertainty:** First ≠ most consequential; battleship doctrine persisted.

## Ch 17 — 1930s — MS *St. Louis*
- **Central claim:** Administrative refusal turned a successful voyage into humanitarian failure.
- **Primary support:** ch17-001; ch17-002.
- **Rival:** Preparedness naval vessel.
- **Uncertainty:** 254 later murdered among continental returnees (USHMM); available persecution evidence still justified flexibility.

## Ch 18 — 1940s — SS *Patrick Henry*
- **Central claim:** Liberty-ship mass production made merchant tonnage a manufactured Allied logistics flow.
- **Primary support:** ch18-001; ch18-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Enterprise* / *Arizona*.
- **Uncertainty:** Record builds are publicity extremes; brittle fractures show scaled defects.

## Ch 19 — 1950s — *Ideal X*
- **Central claim:** Sealed standardized boxes attacked the port interface and enabled later globalization.
- **Primary support:** ch19-001; ch19-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Nautilus*.
- **Uncertainty:** Containers predated 1956; systems demonstration, not sole invention.

## Ch 20 — 1960s — USS *Hornet* (CVS-12)
- **Central claim:** Apollo recovery made naval/ocean infrastructure visible beneath the Moon landing.
- **Primary support:** ch20-001; ch20-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Enterprise* (CVN) / Polaris submarine.
- **Uncertainty:** Ship interchangeable; quarantine later unnecessary but then rational.

## Ch 21 — 1970s — SS *Mayaguez*
- **Central claim:** Post-Vietnam credibility pressure produced force under uncertain information at high cost.
- **Primary support:** ch21-001; ch21-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Nimitz*.
- **Uncertainty:** Weak causal link between Koh Tang assault and crew release.

## Ch 22 — 1980s — *Exxon Valdez*
- **Central claim:** Spill accelerated Oil Pollution Act liability, preparedness, and double-hull rules.
- **Primary support:** ch22-001; ch22-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Stark*.
- **Uncertainty:** Reform pressure pre-existed; ship is accelerator.

## Ch 23 — 1990s — *Golden Venture*
- **Central claim:** Grounding made migrant smuggling a visible transnational system and asylum/detention stress test.
- **Primary support:** ch23-001; ch23-002.
- **Rival:** Container vessel as globalization symbol.
- **Uncertainty:** Did not alone cause 1996 immigration laws.

## Ch 24 — 2000s — USS *Cole*
- **Central claim:** Aden attack exposed asymmetric vulnerability before post-9/11 reorganization.
- **Primary support:** ch24-001; ch24-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Abraham Lincoln* / USS *New York*.
- **Uncertainty:** Warning ≠ proof September 11 was preventable by one alternate response.

## Ch 25 — 2010s — SS *El Faro*
- **Central claim:** Information abundance failed without earlier conversion into margin and decision.
- **Primary support:** ch25-001; ch25-002.
- **Rival:** USS *Fitzgerald* / *John S. McCain*.
- **Uncertainty:** Multiple weather products; company SMS and aging-ship factors contribute with captain decisions.

## Ch 26 — 2020s — MV *Dali*
- **Central claim:** Millimeters-scale electrical seating failure plus bridge vulnerability produced metropolitan infrastructure catastrophe inside the mature container system.
- **Primary support:** ch26-001; ch26-002.
- **Rival:** *Ever Given* / Red Sea shipping.

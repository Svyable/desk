# Evidence Matrix

| Claim | Class | Support | Counterweight / defeat condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Deep continental and marine subsurface environments contain indigenous microbial life. | 1 — verified/documented | Repeated microscopy, cultivation, sequencing, isotope and activity measurements across mines, boreholes and ocean drilling; contamination-aware studies. | Would weaken if tracer-controlled re-sampling showed major canonical communities track introduced material rather than in situ fractures/sediment. |
| Some deep fracture waters have million- to billion-year residence components. | 1 | Noble-gas and isotopic geochemistry, especially Kidd Creek/Timmins. | Does not date living cells; mixing models and hydrologic histories must remain explicit. |
| Radiolysis can generate enough H2 to power deep lithoautotrophic metabolism in some rocks. | 1 | Lin et al. measurements/models; South African systems; later radiolysis work. | Site-specific energy budgets fail if observed H2 is better explained by corrosion or another source, or if flux cannot meet maintenance demand. |
| *D. audaxviator* dominated the sampled Mponeng fracture-water community at >99.9%. | 1 | Chivian et al. metagenomic assembly and follow-up cell work. | Does not establish literal absence of all minority organisms, viruses or attached communities in the broader fracture network. |
| The continental subsurface contains ~2–6 × 10^29 cells. | 2 — credible but incomplete | Magnabosco et al. global synthesis. | Sparse/nonrandom sampling, depth/lithology assumptions and thermal-volume modeling produce material uncertainty. |
| Some deep communities are largely decoupled from contemporary photosynthetic energy. | 2 | Radiolytic and geochemical energy budgets; carbon-fixation pathways; old fluids. | Requires complete carbon/electron accounting; ancient surface-derived substrates or fluid exchange can complicate “independence.” |
| Some subseafloor/deep communities operate at extremely low average power and long turnover times. | 2 | Multiple rate-estimation methods in marine/continental subsurface literature. | Population turnover is not individual-cell age; methods integrate dormancy, death and heterogeneous activity. |
| Serpentinization supports active rock-hosted ecosystems and can generate H2 at low temperature. | 1/2 | Oman Drilling Project and related mineralogical work. | Local hydrology and mineralogy are crucial; cannot generalize globally without site chemistry. |
| Microorganisms can influence repository-material corrosion. | 1 mechanism / 2 field significance | Laboratory MIC experiments; long-standing repository safety research. | Mechanism does not establish repository-scale failure rate under compacted bentonite, low nutrient flux and evolving thermal/hydrologic conditions. |
| Mars subsurface rocks can be energetically habitable through radiolysis where groundwater exists. | 2 | Tarnas et al. radiolysis calculations using Martian meteorite compositions. | Groundwater existence/access, origin of life and persistence are not demonstrated. |
| Mars currently hosts deep microbial life. | 3 — disputed/unconfirmed | No direct public detection. | Requires contamination-controlled biological detection in situ or returned sample. |
| Deep Earth hosts a second independent genesis. | 3 | No accepted evidence. | Known deep organisms share terrestrial molecular/evolutionary machinery. |
| Subsurface life is the modal form of biology on rocky worlds. | 4 — speculative synthesis | Earth analog breadth, shielding, geologic energy persistence, hostile-surface refugia logic. | Would weaken if liquid subsurface niches or usable redox flux prove rare across planetary populations, or if origins require surface cycling. |

## Non-negotiable claim boundaries

- **Water age ≠ cell age.**
- **Metabolic genes ≠ measured metabolic rate.**
- **Measured mechanism ≠ field prevalence.**
- **Energetic habitability ≠ life detection.**
- **Deep isolation ≠ independent origin.**
- **No public evidence ≠ proof of impossibility.**

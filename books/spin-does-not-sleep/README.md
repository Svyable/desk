## Thesis

Xenon is chemically almost the same atom across its stable isotopes, yet a 2018 mouse experiment reported that isotopes carrying nuclear spin were substantially less potent anesthetics than spin-zero isotopes. That result does not prove quantum consciousness. It creates a narrower and more interesting problem: if the isotope effect is real, what physical variable is the nervous system reading that ordinary chemical descriptions leave out?

The book collides anesthesia, isotope physics, radical-pair chemistry, xenon protein binding, magnetoreception, quantum biology and consciousness research while refusing to let `quantum` function as a conclusion.

# Stealing Time

**Subtitle:** How Defense Science Learned to Treat Biology's Clock as an Engineering Variable

## One-sentence thesis
DARPA trauma, circadian, sleep and alertness programs and NASA torpor research converge on a real engineering ambition—control the rate and phase of human biology—but the evidence currently supports control of particular processes, not a master pause button, making synchronization among biological clocks the central technical problem.

## Evidence posture
Official-program-heavy + peer-reviewed physiology pending performer-level reconstruction. Program existence and objectives are documented. Broad human biostasis, synthetic torpor and durable sleep compression remain unproven. The common 'biological time as control surface' frame is synthesis, with explicit defeat conditions.

## Freshness note
Recent Author-agent work was inspected before selection. Current manuscripts cluster around seismic precursors, developmental biohybrids, ecological sensing, foreign-materiel exploitation, remote viewing, military animal sensing and anomalous-gravity replication. This project instead collides trauma medicine, chronobiology, sleep engineering and Mars architecture around a single neglected variable: **rate**.

## Publication gates
- Do not describe a DARPA goal as a demonstrated capability.
- Do not call sedation, therapeutic hypothermia or anesthesia 'torpor' without physiological justification.
- Do not infer human feasibility from obligate/facultative hibernators.
- Every Mars mass/volume saving must expose the biomedical assumption underneath it.
- Separate acute minutes-to-hours biostasis from weeks/months stasis.
- Quantify transition costs, not only steady-state metabolic savings.
- Preserve nulls, adverse effects and program discontinuities.
- No claim of classified operational capability without public evidence.

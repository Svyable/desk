# Stealing Time — evidence ledger

## Governing thesis
Defense medicine, performance research, and long-duration spaceflight independently converge on the same engineering temptation: treat biological timing as a controllable variable. The convergence is documented; generalized command over biological time is not.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- DARPA **Biostasis** publicly aimed to use reversible chemical-biology approaches to slow biological systems and extend the intervention window after trauma or acute infection, explicitly without dependence on cold-chain cooling: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/biostasis
- DARPA **Biochronicity** investigated biological timing across cell cycle, growth, metabolism, aging and cell death, seeking temporal regulators and predictive algorithms: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/biochronicity
- DARPA **ADAPTER** proposed an internal bioelectronic carrier integrating engineered cells/biochemicals, including on-demand regulation of circadian disruption: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/advanced-acclimation-protection
- DARPA **RESTORE** targeted precision control of sleep macro/micro-architecture after severe sleep restriction: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/restore-reengineering-enabling-sleep
- DARPA **AWARE** seeks light-gated pharmacological control of alertness after sleep loss: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/alert-warfighter-enablement
- NASA has supported torpor-habitat studies for Mars and a proposed STASH animal-hibernation laboratory concept. NASA states that hibernation in microgravity has not been studied and frames synthetic human torpor as a future translational possibility: https://www.nasa.gov/general/studying-torpor-in-animals-for-space-health-in-humans/ ; https://www.nasa.gov/general/torpor-inducing-transfer-habitat-for-human-stasis-to-mars/

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Program-performer experimental results must be reconstructed paper-by-paper before assigning achieved capability to Biostasis, Biochronicity, ADAPTER, RESTORE or AWARE.
- Natural mammalian torpor/hibernation establishes that large coordinated changes in metabolic state are biologically possible; translation to non-hibernating humans is a separate empirical problem.
- Clinical targeted temperature management, anesthesia and ICU sedation provide bounded analogues for altered metabolism/consciousness but are not equivalent to synthetic torpor.

## Class 3 — disputed / unsupported until independently established

- Safe months-long human suspended animation.
- Operational military 'pause biology' capability.
- Claims that sleep need can be durably compressed without physiological debt.
- Claims that any single molecular master switch controls mammalian biological time.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

Biological timing may become an engineering substrate only if multiple clocks can be controlled coherently. The decisive problem may be coordination rather than magnitude: slowing metabolism is useless if coagulation, immunity, proteostasis, neural maintenance or circadian phase drift at incompatible rates.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. Program objective ≠ achieved capability.
2. Evolutionary hibernation ≠ medically induced human torpor.
3. Lower metabolic demand ≠ preservation of cognition, immunity, muscle, bone or radiation resistance.
4. Architectural Mars mass savings depend on biomedical assumptions that must be priced as uncertainty, not treated as hardware specifications.
5. Acute trauma biostasis and long-duration astronaut torpor share a timing metaphor but impose radically different duration, reversibility and safety requirements.

## Defeat condition

The book's synthesis fails if close reconstruction shows no common control principles across injury delay, circadian manipulation, sleep architecture and torpor beyond the trivial fact that all biology changes over time; or if interventions cannot shift useful biological rates without compensating damage that erases the operational benefit.

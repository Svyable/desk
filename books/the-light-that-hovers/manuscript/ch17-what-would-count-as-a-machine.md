# What Would Count as a Machine?

Suppose the plasma models keep failing.

This is the point where anomaly literature usually jumps too far. Natural explanations weaken, therefore technology rises. Technology weakens, therefore non-human technology rises.

The chain is invalid unless each step has positive evidence.

What would positive evidence for a manufactured object look like in Hessdalen?

Stable three-dimensional geometry resolved from multiple stations. repeated rigid-body motion. thermal signatures consistent with a bounded surface. radar scattering compatible with a compact target rather than diffuse ionization. controlled acceleration measured with known range. spectroscopic evidence of artificial illumination or exhaust products. electromagnetic emissions with modulation or coding difficult to produce naturally. recurrence of the same structural features across events.

Even that would establish `machine` before `origin`.

A drone is a machine. A covert test article is a machine. an instrument balloon is a machine. Human technology must be eliminated before non-human manufacture enters the inference.

Intelligent response claims need an even stricter standard. A famous Hessdalen anecdote involves apparent reaction to a laser. Such events are intriguing and methodologically weak because the number of trials, timing rules and control periods matter. If researchers shine a laser repeatedly and then narrate the one time brightness changes, coincidence can perform intelligence.

A proper interaction experiment would preregister randomized laser-on and laser-off intervals unknown to observers scoring the target. The response variable would be defined in advance. Many trials would be required.

If the light consistently responded only during true laser periods with reproducible latency, the result would deserve attention regardless of anyone's preferred ontology.

Until then, a light that seems to answer us is a story about timing.

Machines should leave machine-shaped evidence.

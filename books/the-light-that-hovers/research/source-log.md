## Research priorities

Obtain raw 1984 radar logs and instrument specifications; reconstruct every coincident optical/radar event with geometry; build a road/traffic/terrain visibility model; acquire original calibrated spectra rather than reproduced figures; reproduce VLF classifications with transmitter databases; quantify event rates against sensor uptime; compare lightning, atmospheric-electric-field, aerosol, radon and weather records; and design a prospective synchronized multisite campaign with calibrated photometry, spectroscopy, passive radar, thermal imaging, electric-field mills and precise timing.

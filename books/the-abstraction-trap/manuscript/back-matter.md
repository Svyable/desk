# Back Matter

The abstractions in this book are not arguments against measurement, models, markets, or institutions. They are arguments for keeping each representation attached to its purpose, assumptions, incentives, and failure modes.

Historical and current factual claims used in the working manuscript are documented in the accompanying research trail. Interpretive claims, analogies, and forecasts are the author's analysis unless otherwise identified.

This is an unpublished Desk draft by Sven Hard Benson. The working Reader is part of the review process; it is not a released Shelf edition.

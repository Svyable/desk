George F. Kennan’s life becomes a biography of an idea escaping its author. The Long Telegram and the containment argument helped give Washington a vocabulary for the Cold War; Kennan then spent decades objecting to some of the policies, simplifications, militarization, and assumptions attached to that vocabulary.

The dramatic problem is not whether Kennan was right. It is what happens when advice becomes doctrine and doctrine acquires institutions, budgets, weapons, allies, enemies, and a life beyond the adviser’s control.

## Evidentiary rule

Kennan left an unusually rich written record. Use it without confusing eloquence with omniscience. Separate what he wrote at the time from later recollection and later self-interpretation.

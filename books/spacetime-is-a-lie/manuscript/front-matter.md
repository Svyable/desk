# Spacetime Is a Lie

*How We Are Re-Understanding the Universe Through Consciousness*

**Sven Hardy Benson**

For most of modern science, space and time have been treated as so basic that nearly every other question gets asked inside them. Where is the particle? When did the event happen? How far apart are the objects? What came before what?

Then physics began making those questions less stable.

There may be no universal present. Geometry can bend. Horizons can carry entropy. Quantum systems can be described by relationships that do not fit comfortably into classical locality. Some of the most productive work in quantum gravity now asks whether spacetime is something that emerges from a deeper description rather than something the universe is fundamentally made of.

That is already strange enough. Consciousness makes it stranger.

Consciousness is the one part of reality no observer encounters from the outside first. We infer brains, particles, fields, other minds, distant galaxies, and the early universe through experience. That fact has often tempted writers to race from quantum mechanics to cosmic mind. This book will not take that shortcut.

The more interesting route is slower. First ask what physics has actually taken away from the old picture of space and time. Then ask what the proposed replacements contain. Then ask whether any of them leave consciousness easier to explain, harder to explain, or impossible to ignore.

The title is deliberately provocative. The argument has to earn it.

# GHOST TARGET — evidence ledger

## Working thesis

Project PALLADIUM supplies a historically grounded control for sensor-rich anomaly reasoning: a sensor can make a valid measurement of an engineered signal while the object inferred from that signal does not exist. Modern UAP analysis therefore has to audit independence and physicality rather than simply count sensors.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- CIA officially documents OXCART/A-12 as a highly secret reconnaissance program designed around extreme altitude/speed, radar stealthiness and electronic countermeasures. Primary orientation: https://www.cia.gov/legacy/headquarters/a-12-oxcart/
- Radar and electronic-warfare systems can generate deceptive or false targets as a general technical capability. Later chapters must use authoritative EW manuals/technical literature for specific mechanisms rather than infer current classified capability from historical precedent.
- The historical U-2/OXCART secrecy environment is relevant to UFO reporting; exact percentages and particular sighting attributions are publication-held until the underlying CIA historical source is checked directly.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete reporting / testimony

- Former CIA technical officer Gene Poteat publicly described Project PALLADIUM as a CIA radar-deception effort used to inject controlled false targets into Soviet/Cuban radar and observe reactions in support of OXCART-era radar/stealth questions.
- Poteat's detailed Cuban episode, including ship/submarine geometry and calibration spheres, is technically specific and attributable but is treated here as retrospective participant testimony pending contemporaneous operational documentation.
- Claims about NSA monitoring as the feedback leg of individual PALLADIUM operations require primary-source reconstruction before being promoted into Class 1.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- PALLADIUM as the cause of any specific modern UAP incident.
- A continuous secret lineage from PALLADIUM to an alleged contemporary system capable of reproducing all observables in Nimitz/Tic Tac, Gimbal, GoFast or other cases.
- Claims that radar deception can, by itself, account for genuinely independent close visual observations, thermal signatures or physical effects in a particular event.
- Any claim that a named UAP event was a deliberate U.S. deception exercise without case-specific documentary or technical evidence.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

### Independence debt
`Multisensor` evidence is only as strong as the causal independence of the measurements before cueing, fusion, shared tracks and human communication. Prediction: reconstructing the data lineage of an event can reduce or increase its effective sensor count.

### Synthetic witness
An engineered sensor input can create a sincere human witness whose testimony accurately describes the display but misidentifies the cause. Prediction: some archival anomaly reports should preserve operator certainty while later technical reconstruction identifies an external manipulation of the measurement chain.

### Ontological compression
The inference ladder `signal → detection → track → object → identity → capability → origin` is often collapsed. Prediction: separating the rungs will expose exactly where evidence becomes assumption in disputed cases.

### Physicality tax
The more extreme the claimed motion of a physical object, the more non-track observables the object model should predict. This is not a fixed checklist; geometry and sensor thresholds matter. It becomes useful only when predictions are quantified for a case.

## Contradictions / incentives

- Intelligence services have incentives to conceal collection methods and deception capabilities. This makes archival absence weaker than normal but does not make alleged capabilities true.
- Participants may have genuine technical knowledge while retrospective accounts acquire narrative compression. Treat memoir specificity as a lead to documents, not a replacement for them.
- UAP advocates can overcount sensors as independent confirmation. Skeptics can invoke electronic warfare as an unfalsifiable escape hatch. The manuscript must reject both moves.
- Deception programs may deliberately generate adversary reports, meaning a well-provenanced historical witness can be downstream of a manufactured stimulus.

## Publication holds

Do not publish as fact until primary records are checked:
- exact start/end dates and organizational ownership of PALLADIUM;
- exact Cuban operational sequence;
- exact NSA role in named operations;
- exact hardware architecture and performance envelope;
- claims connecting PALLADIUM to Blue Book or specific UFO reports;
- claims connecting PALLADIUM directly to modern UAP cases;
- any percentage of historical UFO reports attributed to U-2/OXCART.

## Defeat conditions

The central framework weakens if modern military sensor-fusion records demonstrate that the relevant systems in a case were genuinely independent at the raw-measurement level and jointly require one physical object with consistent position, kinematics and physical signatures. In such a case, `track without object` stops being competitive.

Conversely, a spoof explanation loses if it cannot identify a technically plausible coupling path into the measured systems, cannot reproduce the raw observables, or requires mutually incompatible manipulations of independent sensors.

The book must make both sides lose-able.

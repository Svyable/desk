## Thesis

There are three sentences that, taken together, overturn the ordinary order of explanation:

**Consciousness is fundamental.**

**Spacetime is a perceptual interface.**

**Physical reality is emergent.**

None of those sentences is established science. Each has serious intellectual ancestors, partial formal support, and equally serious objections. *Consciousness First* is the book that asks what would have to be true for all three to belong to one defensible picture of reality.

The book begins where experience begins: consciousness is the one phenomenon that cannot be placed entirely outside the act of knowing. It then moves outward through the hard problem, neuroscience, evolutionary perception, interface theories, thermal time, black-hole thermodynamics, holography, entanglement and emergent geometry. The central wager is deliberately stronger than *Spacetime Is a Lie*: instead of asking whether consciousness deserves a place in a deeper post-spacetime ontology, this book builds the strongest possible case that consciousness is the deeper ontology—and then tries to break it.

The governing discipline is simple. Emergent spacetime does not imply fundamental consciousness. Non-veridical perception does not imply idealism. A neural correlate does not settle ontology. A mathematical model of conscious agents does not establish that the physical world is their user interface. Every bridge must be built rather than smuggled in.

## Evidence rule

The book keeps five layers separate: established empirical result; active physical theory; empirical consciousness science; philosophical ontology; speculative synthesis. The thermal time hypothesis belongs to active theory, not established fact. The hard problem is a philosophical challenge, not a theorem whose premises everyone accepts. Interface theories of perception are research programs with published criticism. Emergent geometry in special theoretical settings does not establish that everyday spacetime is literally a perceptual display. The consciousness-first synthesis must earn distinct explanatory or predictive work rather than merely placing three mysteries next to one another.

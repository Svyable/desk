# Consciousness First

*Spacetime, Perception, and the Possibility That Physics Is the Interface*

**Sven Hardy Benson**

---

Suppose we have the order backward.

The ordinary story begins with matter.

Matter arranges itself into stars, planets, chemistry, cells, nervous systems and eventually brains. Somewhere late in that sequence, after enough neurons have been wired together in the right way, the lights come on.

There is something it is like to be here.

The story is powerful because so much of it works. Damage the brain and experience changes. Alter the chemistry and experience changes. Put the brain under anesthesia and experience can disappear from report. Stimulate tissue and a world can appear. Any serious theory of consciousness has to survive those facts.

But the story contains one move so familiar that it can become invisible.

It assumes the physical world is the thing being explained from.

What if it is also a thing being explained?

Modern physics has already made space and time less secure than common sense suggests. Relativity removed the universal present. Black-hole thermodynamics and holographic ideas tied geometry to entropy and information. In some theoretical settings, spacetime connectivity tracks quantum entanglement. Connes and Rovelli went further in a different direction: perhaps physical time-flow is not a universal background parameter but something selected by the state of a generally covariant system.

None of this says consciousness made the universe.

That sentence matters.

It matters because the fastest way to ruin an interesting idea is to let one mystery impersonate evidence for another.

This book will try the harder inversion.

Assume, provisionally, that consciousness is fundamental. Ask what that would actually mean. Ask whether spacetime could be the format in which conscious agents encounter an underlying reality rather than the underlying reality itself. Ask whether the physical world might be emergent without becoming imaginary. Then ask what evidence could make us regret all three moves.

A worldview that cannot lose is not a theory of reality.

It is interior decoration.

I want something riskier.

# NINE WATTS — research and evidence ledger

Working thesis: the Pioneer anomaly is a positive control for anomaly science. A real, persistent navigation residual survived serious scrutiny and invited new-physics explanations, but was ultimately accounted for by reconstructing the mundane physics of the measuring platform. The transferable lesson is not `anomalies are mistakes`; it is `do not promote a residual beyond the level at which apparatus physics has actually been closed`.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

1. NASA/JPL, **Study Finds Heat is Source of 'Pioneer Anomaly'** (17 July 2012). JPL states that the unexpected slowing of Pioneer 10 and 11 is explained by thermal recoil from electrical systems and the radioisotope thermoelectric power supply. It gives the anomalous acceleration at about 0.9 nm/s² and describes the recovery of old telemetry and engineering records. https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/news/study-finds-heat-is-source-of-pioneer-anomaly/

2. Turyshev, Toth, Kinsella, Lee, Lok & Ellis, **Support for the Thermal Origin of the Pioneer Anomaly**, *Physical Review Letters* 108, 241101 (2012), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevLett.108.241101. The authors constructed a finite-element thermal model using spacecraft design documentation and actual flight telemetry, then independently estimated thermal-recoil coefficients from Doppler navigation data. They report no statistically significant difference and conclude that no anomalous acceleration remains after thermal recoil is properly accounted for. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.108.241101 ; public preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.2507

3. Toth & Turyshev, **Thermal recoil force, telemetry, and the Pioneer anomaly**, *Physical Review D* 79, 043011 (2009), DOI 10.1103/PhysRevD.79.043011. Establishes the engineering/telemetry route for computing recoil from anisotropic thermal radiation and makes the crucial point that spacecraft telemetry can be part of precision-force reconstruction. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.79.043011

4. NASA, **Gravity Probe B: Results and Implications**. Useful comparison control. GP-B encountered anomalous rotor torques and jumps; the team modeled them rather than treating the residuals as direct evidence against relativity. Final reported geodetic and frame-dragging drift rates were consistent with GR within uncertainties. https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/results-implications/

## Quantitative sanity check

Published Pioneer anomaly scale: approximately 0.9 × 10^-9 m/s². For a representative late-mission spacecraft mass near 250 kg, the equivalent force is about 2.25 × 10^-7 N. A perfectly collimated photon beam produces F=P/c, so an order-of-magnitude directional-radiation equivalent is roughly 67 W. This is a calculation, not a claim that a literal 67 W beam existed. The actual spacecraft had hundreds to thousands of watts of thermal power over its life and complex geometry. The important point is scale: a force capable of looking cosmological can be generated by a small anisotropy in an ordinary spacecraft heat budget.

Publication hold: verify mission-epoch mass and thermal/electrical power before putting a single `equivalent watts` number into final prose. The working title **NINE WATTS** is intentionally provisional and must be changed if a defensible nine-watt quantity cannot be sourced. Do not retrofit the number to the title.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Early interpretations of the acceleration direction (Sunward, Earthward, along velocity, or spin-axis) were difficult to discriminate because those directions can be geometrically close for a distant spin-stabilized craft. Reconstruct from the 2002 Anderson et al. anomaly paper before narrating.
- Data-rescue history: JPL reports records scattered across the country and a vintage tape machine saved to read old telemetry. Recover the archival papers and exact telemetry span before turning this into a dramatic scene.
- Flyby anomaly is a useful adjacent residual but must not be narrated as `the next Pioneer`. It involves different spacecraft, geometry, observables and candidate systematics. Build an event table from primary navigation papers.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Pioneer as evidence for modified Newtonian dynamics, a new long-range force, dark matter in the solar system, cosmological expansion acting locally, or a clock-frequency acceleration. These were hypotheses motivated by a residual, not established findings.
- Any claim that the still-discussed flyby anomaly proves new gravity.
- Any use of a classified-program gap as positive evidence that a residual comes from hidden propulsion or field technology.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

### Apparatus debt

For any anomaly claim define an informal debt vector:

`D = {force closure, thermal closure, EM closure, propulsion closure, timing closure, geometry closure, calibration closure, environmental closure, data-pipeline closure}`.

A residual can be real while D remains large. Extraordinary interpretation becomes stronger as the relevant components of D are closed with independent measurements rather than assumed away.

### Residual promotion

Proposed evidence ladder:

measurement → calibrated residual → replicated residual → cross-platform residual → mechanism-discriminating residual → new phenomenon → new physics.

Evidence may move upward only when the new rung has its own support. Repetition of the word `anomaly` does not move it.

### The dead-machine reconstruction test

If a claimed new-physics residual changes when investigators recover forgotten engineering telemetry and reconstruct the instrument/platform in greater physical detail, the platform model gains probability. If the residual instead survives across materially different platforms with predeclared force budgets and predicted scaling, the new-physics model gains probability.

## Contradictions and incentives

- Scientists had a legitimate incentive to consider new physics because a persistent unexplained acceleration in deep space is exactly where a deviation from modeled gravity could appear. Do not caricature this as sensationalism.
- NASA/JPL also had an institutional incentive to solve a navigation residual conventionally. Agreement between engineering-derived thermal coefficients and coefficients estimated independently from Doppler data matters because it reduces dependence on institutional preference.
- A successful conventional explanation does not imply every future anomaly is conventional. That inference would commit the same promotion error in reverse.
- Data availability changed the hypothesis space. Early thermal objections were weaker when the complete engineering/telemetry reconstruction did not exist. Later certainty must not be projected backward onto earlier investigators.

## Defeat condition

The book's stronger `apparatus debt` thesis weakens if a well-characterized anomaly is shown to survive independent platforms with different thermal, propulsion, timing and navigation architectures while scaling with an external physical variable predicted in advance by a nonstandard model. A cross-platform residual with pre-registered discrimination is qualitatively different evidence from one unexplained force on one machine family.

## Manuscript holds

Do not say `NASA discovered new gravity`. Do not say `heat solved it immediately`. Do not imply thermal recoil was an ad hoc story invented after the fact. Do not turn the flyby anomaly into evidence for the Pioneer anomaly. Do not infer hidden aerospace capability from a navigation residual. Do not use the title's number as evidence until it is sourced or replace the title.

# COVER STORY — evidence ledger

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- U.S. GAO, **Government Records: Results of a Search for Records Concerning the 1947 Crash Near Roswell, New Mexico**, NSIAD-95-187, 28 July 1995. https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad-95-187
- GAO report PDF, including agency replies and records-disposition findings: https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-95-187.pdf
- U.S. Air Force historical report, **The Roswell Report: Fact vs. Fiction in the New Mexico Desert**. Current DAF History PDF: https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/Portals/16/documents/AFD-101201-038.pdf
- The Air Force report documents the classified nuclear/guided-missile detection purpose associated with Mogul and the NYU constant-level balloon work. Treat the report as an official historical reconstruction, not an infallible primary witness.
- GAO documents its 1995 search and reproduces a CIA response stating its database search for “Project Mogul” and “Roswell, New Mexico” yielded no responsive documents. This is evidence about that search, not evidence that Mogul did not exist.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Identification of ranch debris with the Mogul/NYU balloon program is strongly argued in the Air Force reconstruction, but the exact flight-number/trajectory reconstruction requires primary flight logs, diaries, meteorological data and contemporaneous debris descriptions to be laid side by side.
- Contemporary newspaper chronology needs to be rebuilt from scans rather than repeated from later summaries.
- Records destruction identified by GAO requires comparison against ordinary Army/Air Force retention practice before words such as `suspicious` or `routine` are used.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Recovered extraterrestrial or non-human vehicle.
- Non-human bodies.
- A second crash site as a demonstrated 1947 event.
- Any claim that absence of records proves deliberate destruction to conceal non-human technology.
- Any claim that Mogul definitively explains every later witness narrative.
- Any claim that later anthropomorphic test-dummy activity can simply be projected backward into 1947 testimony without chronology and source analysis.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

### Secrecy wedge
The information removed between an externally observable artifact/event and the classified operational explanation that makes it intelligible.

### Cover-story half-life
The interval over which a demonstrated official omission or false explanation continues to materially alter interpretation of later evidence and denials.

### Mythic interest rate
A deliberately provocative working term for the growth of unsupported explanatory structure inside a persistent information gap. Must not survive unless comparative cases show it adds something beyond ordinary rumor/media dynamics.

## Contradictions to preserve

- A real classified program does not prove the official Roswell reconstruction is correct in every detail.
- A flawed or deceptive public explanation does not prove the extraordinary alternative.
- Missing records are neither proof of destruction-for-coverup nor proof that nothing happened.
- Later witness confidence cannot be treated as contemporaneous documentation.
- Official reports can contain both strong primary-source recovery and institutional self-protection; evaluate claims item by item.

## Quantitative extraction burden

For each comparison case record:

`event date | first public claim | correction date | classified purpose | artifact visibility | number of independent observers | documentary survival | duration to declassification | cover-story discrepancy | extraordinary alternative available? | media repetitions | later physical evidence | later primary records | belief persistence`

Candidate controls: U-2 weather-research cover; CORONA/Discoverer; Have Blue/F-117; Project Azorian; Soviet foreign-materiel exploitation; balloon reconnaissance programs; known deception operations; cases of ordinary military accidents with no hidden mission.

## Publication holds

Do not write `Roswell was Mogul Flight 4` as a settled contemporaneous fact until the flight-log dispute is reconstructed.

Do not write `the government admitted it lied about Roswell` without specifying which agency, statement, date and concealed fact.

Do not use `weather balloon` as a synonym for the full Mogul system.

Do not turn the GAO/CIA zero-result search into evidence against the documented program.

Do not imply access to classified records.

Do not treat patents, memoirs, regression hypnosis, anonymous claims or repeated secondary citations as independent corroboration.

## Research discipline

Every later chapter should preserve the distinction between:

1. the physical event or artifact;
2. its documented provenance;
3. the interpretation assigned to it at the time;
4. the interpretation assigned retrospectively;
5. the broader inference the book wants to draw.

The manuscript should never use the existence of a historically documented covert recovery program as proof of a different alleged covert recovery program.

Patents, clearances, classified facilities, testimony and institutional interest are evidence only for what they directly establish. Technical efficacy, recovered origin and extraordinary capability require their own evidence.

Where CIA's retrospective institutional history is the only source for a detail, say so. “Declassified” and “official” do not mean omniscient, contemporaneous or complete.

## Comparative cases to develop

- CORONA / Discoverer as a public program identity wrapped around reconnaissance hardware.
- U-2 cover explanations and the relationship between classified aerospace and UFO reporting.
- Foreign-materiel exploitation where public absence coexisted with extensive internal technical work.
- Commercial or scientific infrastructures whose dual use complicated provenance.
- Cases where a cover story failed because physical behavior contradicted the public explanation.

The comparative chapters must earn similarity variable by variable. AZORIAN is not a metaphorical blank check.

## Thesis

When the Soviet nuclear-powered reconnaissance satellite Cosmos 954 came apart over northern Canada in January 1978, it turned a Cold War intelligence machine into a continent-scale radiological search problem. The recovery operation that followed reveals how states improvise when military secrecy, nuclear engineering, remote geography, environmental measurement, international law, and public reassurance all collide at once.

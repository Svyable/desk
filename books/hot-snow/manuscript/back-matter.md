# Notes on Evidence

This draft treats Cosmos 954 as a documented accident with unresolved details, not as a platform for speculative Cold War mythology. The existence of the satellite, its nuclear power source, uncontrolled re-entry, Canadian debris field, Operation Morning Light, recovery activity, Canadian compensation claim and 1981 settlement are documented by Canadian, IAEA and United Nations sources.

Claims about precise reactor configuration, Soviet intent, classified intelligence exploitation, community-level effects, fragment-by-fragment recovery, and the fate of unrecovered material require narrower source work before they should be stated with equal confidence.

The central synthesis is that Cosmos 954 turned a military-space failure into an institutional interoperability test. That is an interpretive claim and should weaken if archival reconstruction shows that responsibility, procedures and cross-agency response were already mature and routine before January 1978.

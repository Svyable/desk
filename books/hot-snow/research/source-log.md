# Source log

## Canadian official

- Health Canada, `Previous nuclear incidents and accidents: COSMOS 954`: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/health-risks-safety/radiation/radiological-nuclear-emergencies/previous-incidents-accidents/cosmos-954.html
  - January 24, 1978 crash; 124,000 km² figure; Operation Morning Light through October 1978; estimated recovery of about 0.1% of power source; subsequent international policy attention.
- Geological Survey of Canada / ISED, `Operation Morning Light (1978)`: https://www.ised-isde.canada.ca/site/science/en/educational-resources/history-geological-survey-canada-175-objects/123-operation-morning-light-1978
  - Hundreds of Canadian participants; 120-person U.S. NEST; airborne gamma-ray spectrometer; first debris identification near Fort Reliance; geological-survey lineage.
- Government of Canada Treaty Information, 1981 settlement text: https://www.treaty-accord.gc.ca/text-texte.aspx?id=103429
  - C$3,000,000 full and final settlement signed April 2, 1981.

## International / technical

- IAEA Emergency Preparedness and Response Insights, Cosmos 954 discussion: https://www.iaea.org/sites/default/files/21/05/epr-insights-edition-12.pdf
  - approximately 45 kg HEU; first uncontrolled re-entry experience involving radioactive space object; 80 radioactive items; emergency-preparedness legacy.
- IAEA-TECDOC-827, airborne gamma-ray spectrometry: https://www-pub.iaea.org/MTCD/Publications/PDF/te_827.pdf
  - Cosmos 954 search geometry; C-130 survey method; spectral discrimination.
- Geological Survey of Canada reference: Q. Bristow, `The application of airborne gamma-ray spectrometry in the search for radioactive debris from the Russian satellite Cosmos 954 (Operation Morning Light)`, GSC Paper 78-1B, DOI 10.4095/103589.
- Lawrence Livermore report UCRL-52597, `Analysis ... for Cosmos 954`: https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/6403536

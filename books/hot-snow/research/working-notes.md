# Working notes

## Naming

`Hot Snow` is provisional but strong: visually ordinary winter terrain became hazardous only through measurement. It does not begin with `The`.

## Narrative spine

Do not write a generic history of Soviet space reactors. Keep returning to the conversion chain:

orbiting intelligence machine → uncontrolled tracked object → radiological uncertainty → airborne anomaly detection → ground recovery → laboratory characterization → international claim → safety doctrine.

## Prose guardrails

The accident is inherently dramatic. Do not manufacture suspense. Use actual dates, flight geometry, detector behavior, recovered fragments, legal text and the physical difficulty of the north.

Avoid treating low population density as absence of people. A later pass must include northern and Indigenous sources before making social claims.

Avoid treating `0.1% recovered` as `99.9% still lying on the ground`. Atmospheric breakup and dispersion are separate questions.

Avoid saying Cosmos 954 caused the 1992 UN principles by itself. Trace institutional lineage.

## Research question with widest payoff

What did the first twenty-four hours look like at the level of phone calls, predicted ground tracks, aircraft launch decisions and detector thresholds? That is where a book can turn an abstract emergency into sequence without inventing scenes.

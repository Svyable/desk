The central claim is deliberately narrower than the title sounds: natural nuclear fission inside a planet is an established phenomenon at Oklo; radiogenic antineutrinos from Earth are directly measured; a self-sustaining reactor at or near Earth's center remains a disputed hypothesis that modern antineutrino detectors can constrain spectrally. The book is about the distance between those three sentences.

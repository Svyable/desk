## Defeat conditions

The book's stronger thesis should narrow if the primary exploitation record shows the recovered material was mostly routine and produced little analytical value beyond confirming known Soviet scientific practice. It should change substantially if Soviet primary records show the acoustic work later emphasized by U.S. histories had been misidentified, was unrelated to under-ice submarine detection, or was operationally trivial. Conversely, stronger claims about military purpose require station-specific Soviet or U.S. primary documentation, not inference from the general strategic relevance of Arctic acoustics.

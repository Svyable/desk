# Source Log

## Primary / official U.S. material

- CIA, **Seven Days in the Arctic** — official museum/history account of Project COLDFEET, personnel, dates, NP-8 exploitation, weather-delayed pickup, and recovered material. https://www.cia.gov/legacy/museum/artifact/seven-days-in-the-arctic/
- William M. Leary, **Robert Fulton's Skyhook and Operation Coldfeet**, *Studies in Intelligence* — detailed institutional history with footnotes to contemporary Navy material and participant sources. https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/studies-in-intelligence/1995-2/robert-fultons-skyhook-and-operation-coldfeet/
- CIA PDF of Leary study. https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Robert-Fulton-Operation-Coldfeet.pdf
- CIA, **Skyhook Extraction Mechanism Instructions** — museum artifact and operational description. https://www.cia.gov/legacy/museum/artifact/skyhook-extraction-mechanism-instructions/
- CIA, **Prelude to COLDFEET: From Air Mail to Spy Sky Pickups** — official history of the recovery-system lineage, published 2026. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/prelude-to-coldfeet-from-air-mail-to-spy-sky-pickups/
- CIA, **10 Fascinating CIA Missions**, COLDFEET section — concise official retrospective. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ten-fascinating-cia-missions
- John Cadwalader, **Operation Coldfeet: An Investigation of the Abandoned Soviet Arctic Drift Station NP 8**, *ONI Review* 17 (August 1962), pp. 344–355. This contemporary declassified report is cited by Leary and is a highest-priority full-text recovery target.
- Leonard A. LeSchack, **Skyhook Retrieval**, *U.S. Naval Institute Proceedings* 92 (March 1966), pp. 138–142. Highest-priority participant/technical recovery target.

## Contemporary / near-contemporary strategic context

- U.S. Naval Institute Proceedings, **The Opening Of The Arctic Ocean** (October 1961) — contemporary account linking Soviet Arctic experience and new nuclear-submarine operations. https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/1961/october/opening-arctic-ocean
- U.S. Naval Institute Proceedings, **Arctic Drift Stations** (April 1963) — period description of NP-8 abandonment and Soviet drift-station operations. https://www.usni.org/magazines/proceedings/1963/april/arctic-drift-stations

## Research hierarchy

1. Contemporary COLDFEET/ONI/ONR records and participant technical accounts.
2. CIA institutional history, used with awareness of retrospective/commemorative incentives.
3. Soviet/Russian and neutral Arctic scientific archives for station function and measurement programs.
4. Contemporary U.S. naval publications for period strategic assumptions.
5. Later secondary histories only as maps to primary records, not as terminal evidence.

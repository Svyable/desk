# Whangaparaoa

Whangaparaoa turned speculation into an experimental landscape. Charges went into water. Wave gauges and recording systems had to survive. Timing had to be controlled. Geometry had to be varied. The shoreline became an instrument.

The location matters because it strips the story of its later supernatural clothing. This was engineering. Remote firing, marine explosives, measurement, repetition. The apparatus was exotic only because the objective was exotic.

It is tempting to narrate thousands of blasts as evidence of a vast hidden capability. The better inference runs the other direction. Thousands of trials are evidence that the transfer from explosion to useful wave was difficult enough to require thousands of trials.

A mature weapon does not need to rediscover its coupling law every afternoon.

The bay also imposed a problem that would follow the entire concept: local bathymetry helps determine what a source produces and what a coast receives. A configuration that behaves impressively in one basin cannot be carried unchanged to another shoreline. The weapon, if it existed, would partly be made of geography.

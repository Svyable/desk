# A Bomb Is Not an Earthquake

The phrase tsunami bomb invites the wrong mental picture. Natural tsunamis are not simply explosions with geological branding. Many are generated when fault motion displaces a broad patch of seafloor. The dimensions of the source matter as much as its energy.

A useful comparison begins with wavelength. A long-wave disturbance samples the full water column differently from a short violent splash. If the source is too compact, much of what looks dramatic nearby does not survive as the desired propagating mode.

This is the first place later tectonic-weapon stories usually become slippery. They move between three mechanisms without acknowledging the move: directly generating a wave, triggering an earthquake that generates a wave, or using some unspecified environmental coupling to release stored geological energy. These are different machines.

Project Seal investigated the first family. Its existence is not evidence for the second or third.

A claim that an explosion triggered a fault must specify stress state, source coupling, distance, and why the event's seismic characteristics support triggering rather than ordinary tectonics. A claim that electromagnetic or ionospheric apparatus caused an earthquake owes an even longer causal chain. The burden grows with every hidden bridge.

The fact that water can be weaponized does not make the Earth remotely steerable.

# How to Audit a Geophysical Weapon

Begin with the source. What energy, momentum, mass displacement, pressure field, or stress perturbation is actually introduced? Do not accept a machine name as an answer.

Then ask about coupling. What fraction enters the mode that matters? For a tsunami claim, how much energy becomes long gravity waves rather than shock, heat, bubble oscillation, local breaking, or other losses?

Then geometry. What source dimensions and timing are required? Does the claimed mechanism naturally produce the wavelength and directionality observed?

Then propagation. What happens between source and target? Bathymetry, attenuation, dispersion, scattering, and environmental variability belong inside the weapon, not outside the analysis.

Then signatures. What else must the source produce? Seismic waves, hydroacoustic signals, radionuclides, vessels, deployment activity, electromagnetic emissions, procurement, test ranges, or unusual infrastructure can provide independent channels.

Finally attribution. Even a physically anomalous event does not identify an actor.

This ladder deliberately resembles nuclear-test verification because the intellectual problem is similar. Detection, mechanism, and attribution are separate burdens.

The discipline is to refuse to let secrecy pay any of them automatically.

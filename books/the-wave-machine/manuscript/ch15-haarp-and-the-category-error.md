# HAARP and the Category Error

HAARP enters geophysical-weapon stories because it is large, technical, military-associated in origin, and concerned with a region of the atmosphere most people cannot visualize. Those properties are sociologically powerful. They are not a mechanism for moving tectonic plates.

High-frequency ionospheric research can alter a small volume of ionospheric plasma under appropriate conditions. A claim that such activity generates a destructive earthquake or tsunami must bridge orders of magnitude and multiple physical domains: radio energy to ionospheric modification, ionosphere to crustal stress, crustal stress to a selected rupture, rupture to tsunami, all with targetable timing.

Naming each bridge makes the problem less mystical.

This is a general auditing technique. When an extraordinary technology claim is presented as one sentence, rewrite it as a causal chain. Every arrow is an experiment somebody owes you.

Project Seal survives this treatment better than most legends because its chain was short: explosive charge to water displacement to wave propagation to shore. Even that short chain proved difficult.

# Tectonic Weapons

A tectonic weapon is attractive because it appears to outsource energy. Instead of supplying the destructive energy, the attacker merely nudges a system already storing it. The same fantasy appears in avalanche triggering, fault triggering, and other metastable systems.

Sometimes small triggers really can release larger stored energies. That is why the category cannot be dismissed by rhetoric. The question is controllability.

Faults are not bombs with labeled detonators. Their stress states are incompletely observed, their frictional properties heterogeneous, and their responses distributed through a crust full of competing structures. Induced seismicity from fluid injection demonstrates that human activity can alter earthquake occurrence. It does not demonstrate that a distant actor can select a target fault, choose timing and magnitude, conceal the forcing, and obtain a desired tsunami.

This chapter keeps those propositions separate because later allegations often do not.

Evidence class one includes documented induced seismicity and documented military interest in environmental effects. Evidence class three includes claims that particular major natural disasters were covert attacks when no independently verified causal evidence has been produced. Evidence class four asks whether future geophysical manipulation could ever become strategically controllable. That is a scientific question, not a historical fact.

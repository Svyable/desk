# Back Matter

## A note on sources

Camp Century has the unusual advantage of surviving in several evidence systems at once. The military engineering record is unusually rich because the U.S. Army Cold Regions Research and Engineering Laboratory documented construction, deformation, power, drilling and station performance in technical reports. The strategic layer emerges from declassified U.S. planning and diplomatic records reconstructed by historians including Nikolaj Petersen. The scientific afterlife exists in peer-reviewed glaciology, paleoclimate and geochronology. The abandoned station itself remains measurable by radar.

Those systems do not deserve identical evidentiary treatment.

A CRREL report can establish what engineers measured without establishing what Danish officials knew. A diplomatic memorandum can establish what an official proposed without establishing that the proposal was implemented. A climate model can establish a conditional projection without converting 2100 into an observed fact. A radar return can locate known debris without identifying every contaminant inside it.

The research files accompanying this manuscript maintain those boundaries explicitly.

## Dates that matter

The chronology is deliberately split across the four clocks developed in the book.

**Operational / engineering:** U.S. Arctic military access expands after World War II; the 1951 Defense of Greenland Agreement formalizes U.S. defense areas; Camp Century construction begins in 1959; the PM-2A reactor is installed and reaches operation in 1961; Project Iceworm is developed around 1960–62 and rejected before deployment; deep drilling reaches the bed on July 2, 1966; Camp Century is abandoned in 1967.

**Political:** Greenland’s formal colonial status ends in 1953; Home Rule begins in 1979; later archival releases clarify Project Iceworm and U.S.-Danish nuclear relations; Self-Government begins in 2009; Denmark and Greenland establish the Camp Century Climate Monitoring Programme in 2017.

**Scientific:** oxygen-isotope analysis of the 1,390-meter core produces the 1969 *Science* climate record; radar and historical reconstruction produce the 2016 waste/climate study; archived basal material yields the 2021 evidence for past ice-free vegetated landscapes; locally calibrated firn modeling in 2021 revises the pre-2100 waste-exposure pathway downward; luminescence and cosmogenic constraints date an ice-free MIS 11 episode to 416 ± 38 ka in 2023; NASA’s 2024 UAVSAR flight images structural elements of the buried station with a new geometry.

**Geological:** the site experienced at least one ice-free interval during the Pleistocene; the upper basal sediment records flowing-water deposition during MIS 11; the modern ice sheet then returned and eventually preserved the military station inside an accumulation zone.

## What the book does not claim

It does not claim operational nuclear missiles were deployed at Camp Century. The public evidence establishes a classified proposal, not deployment.

It does not claim the PM-2A reactor remains buried under Greenland. Major reactor components and fuel were removed; reactor-associated liquid radiological waste and other base wastes remained.

It does not claim Camp Century waste is currently contaminating Greenlandic surface waters. The strongest site-specific modeling through 2100 finds surface meltwater interaction extremely unlikely.

It does not claim the waste is safe forever. The 2021 model has an end date, and the geological record establishes that the site is not permanently glaciated on Pleistocene timescales.

It does not claim a Camp Century fossil directly measures whole-Greenland ice volume or future sea-level rise. Those conclusions require explicit ice-sheet modeling and other constraints.

It does not claim that because one genuine secret program eventually became public, every modern allegation of a secret physical program is plausible. Camp Century is used as a control case for the kinds of independent residue a real material program can create.

## A research program still worth doing

The book is strongest where the evidence streams already collide and weakest where the historical record remains distributed.

A full archival edition of Project Iceworm should link every surviving planning paper, engineering assumption, missile concept, interservice response and diplomatic discussion on one timeline. The key question is not whether a dramatic memo exists but how the proposal evolved and exactly when technical and organizational judgments turned against it.

The Camp Century physical inventory should continue moving from reconstructed estimate toward measurement. Radar has mapped geometry. Future non-invasive geophysics could improve discrimination among steel, void remnants, liquid sumps and surrounding firn without prematurely disturbing the site.

The climate monitoring record should remain public and continuous. The most valuable next result may be another reduction in uncertainty rather than an alarming threshold crossing.

Basal sample work should preserve provenance aggressively. The remaining material is finite. Every destructive analysis spends an archive that cannot be redrilled cheaply.

Finally, the documentary and physical traces of Camp Century should be used as a blinded control set for historians and intelligence scholars studying secrecy. If analysts were given only subsets of the public record—technical reports without Iceworm memoranda, radar without maps, memoirs without engineering data—what would they infer? Which evidence combinations recover the true hidden program, and which tempt analysts into the false claim that deployment occurred?

That experiment would turn an old secret into a modern method.

## Source trail

Primary and authoritative materials used to build this draft are logged in `research/README.md`. The source trail favors U.S. Army/CRREL engineering reports, treaty text, Danish/Greenlandic monitoring material, NASA radar documentation and peer-reviewed glaciology and paleoclimate papers over later retellings.

Patents are not material to this book. No efficacy claim is inferred from a filing.

No anonymous allegation is promoted into a documentary fact.

No classified access is asserted.

The oldest useful discipline at Camp Century remains the simplest one: mark the depth before you interpret what came out of the hole.

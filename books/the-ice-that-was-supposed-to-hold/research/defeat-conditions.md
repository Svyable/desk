# Defeat conditions and decisive research

The book’s central thesis is that Camp Century became an unusually powerful natural experiment because four clocks—operational, engineering, political and geological—ran at incompatible rates. The Army could operate a base for years while the same ice defeated economical strategic scaling over decades, preserved scientific archives over millennia and complicated sovereignty/liability across generations. Secrecy hid strategic purpose but could not suspend physical program metabolism.

The thesis should lose where evidence says it should.

## What would materially defeat or revise the military-history claims

1. **Authenticated deployment records for Iceworm missiles.** If credible primary records, physical custody evidence or independently corroborated hardware documentation showed that nuclear-armed missiles were actually installed or operationally deployed beneath Greenland, the current Class 3 status of that claim would be wrong and the manuscript would need major revision.
2. **Evidence Iceworm was merely rhetorical.** If complete archives showed the plan never received serious technical, budgetary or policy consideration and had no meaningful relationship to Camp Century engineering, the manuscript overstates the strategic layer. Current published archival work argues otherwise.
3. **A decision record showing ice mechanics were irrelevant.** If the full policy record demonstrated that Iceworm was technically economical at intended scale and died solely from an unrelated political decision, the current mixed technical/organizational account would need reweighting.
4. **A documented Danish approval of the full missile system.** If primary Danish/U.S. records established formal consent before cancellation, the manuscript’s treatment of unresolved consent would be wrong.

## What would materially defeat or revise the waste/environment claims

5. **A complete removal inventory.** If authenticated decommissioning records showed that the reconstructed diesel, PCB-bearing equipment, sewage and radiological residues had all been removed or rendered absent, the current waste chapters would collapse.
6. **Direct geophysical/chemical evidence that reconstructed waste locations are wrong.** New surveys or boreholes could materially change the inventory and geometry. The manuscript should follow the measurement.
7. **Observed meltwater/debris contact before 2100.** Detection of a connected hydrological pathway to the debris field would defeat the strongest practical conclusion of the 2021 firn model, even if greenhouse/ice-sheet physics remained intact.
8. **Long-term validation of deeper burial and isolation.** If multi-decadal observations consistently show stronger accumulation, shallower percolation and stable contaminants, the stewardship horizon should be pushed outward rather than preserving an outdated alarm.

## What would materially defeat or revise the paleoclimate claims

9. **Broken basal provenance.** If sample-history work showed that plant fossils or dated sediment did not originate from the Camp Century basal interval, the central direct evidence for local past deglaciation would need to be withdrawn.
10. **Independent dating that excludes MIS 11.** If robust replication using appropriate methods moved the upper-sediment depositional age outside 416 ± 38 ka/MIS 11, the specific chronology must change even if past ice-free conditions remain.
11. **Ice-sheet models demonstrating a substantially different minimum Greenland volume loss while satisfying all geological constraints.** The ≥1.4 m sea-level-equivalent implication is model-derived and should move with better models and constraints.
12. **Evidence that fossil material was transported into place under continuous ice rather than representing local ice-free landscape conditions.** Such a mechanism would have to explain the luminescence, provenance, cosmogenic and sedimentological evidence jointly.

## What would defeat the “secret physical program metabolism” synthesis

13. **A strong comparison set of mature, large, long-duration physical black programs leaving effectively no independent traces outside a single testimony/document lane after comparable time and declassification opportunity.** This would weaken the claim that physical metabolism creates a useful evidentiary signature.
14. **Evidence Camp Century’s rich residue is atypical solely because it was unusually public.** The comparative framework should include acknowledged SAP/black programs before being generalized too far.
15. **A retrieval/exploitation case with authenticated hardware and custody but little conventional logistics or maintenance residue.** Such a case would show that some kinds of physical secrets can remain evidentially compact in ways Camp Century does not model.

## Prospective decisive research

### A. Iceworm document reconstruction

Build a source-level database of every recoverable Iceworm document: date, office, classification history, proposed missile count, network area, power assumptions, construction rates, ice-deformation assumptions, expected cost, intended decision audience, response, and disposition. Separate original documents from later historical paraphrase.

**Decisive result:** a chronological decision chain showing exactly which variable moved the project from proposal to rejection.

### B. Camp Century deformation economics

Extract every measured trench profile, closure rate, roof load, maintenance intervention and material replacement from CRREL reports. Construct a per-meter annual maintenance model. Scale it—explicitly and with uncertainty—to the Iceworm network ranges actually proposed.

**Decisive result:** a quantitative comparison between the annual work required to preserve mobility and the strategic value claimed for mobility.

### C. PM-2A full operations ledger

Create a day/month-level table of reactor output, availability, shutdowns, shielding modifications, contamination events, waste disposal, crew requirements, spare-parts demands and diesel displacement.

**Decisive result:** a defensible estimate of whether transportable nuclear power reduced total logistics burden at Camp Century after the full support system is counted.

### D. Waste ground truth without premature excavation

Repeat high-resolution radar with multiple frequencies and geometries; add passive electromagnetic, gravity/magnetic methods where useful; model dielectric responses of known historical materials; pre-register target classifications before comparison with site maps.

**Decisive result:** improved probabilities for solid infrastructure, hydrocarbon-bearing sumps and disturbed waste zones without creating new pathways through intrusive drilling.

### E. Climate-model prospective test

Freeze the 2021 Camp Century firn model or a clearly versioned successor. Publish annually predicted accumulation, 10-m temperature, meltwater penetration, compaction and debris-depth changes before observing each subsequent season.

**Decisive result:** a true forward record of calibration rather than another retrospective fit.

### F. Basal-sediment adversarial replication

Allocate carefully chosen aliquots to independent laboratories blinded to preferred age model where feasible. Replicate luminescence dose, cosmogenic nuclides, mineral provenance, macrofossil identification and biomarker results. Preserve enough material for future methods.

**Decisive result:** convergence or explicit disagreement among methods with different contamination and model sensitivities.

### G. Secrecy control experiment

Create evidence packets from Camp Century with known ground truth:

- public Army film only;
- engineering reports only;
- reactor documents only;
- diplomatic records only;
- Iceworm planning documents only;
- radar data only;
- testimony/memoir only;
- selected combinations.

Give packets to blinded analysts and ask them to estimate: base existence, reactor existence, missile-plan existence, missile deployment, network scale, Danish knowledge, and confidence.

**Decisive result:** empirical measurement of which evidence combinations reveal the true secret without producing the false deployment conclusion.

## Editorial falsification rule

No chapter may preserve a dramatic claim merely because it improves the book. When a newer, stronger source changes the claim, revise the prose, source trail and evidence class together. The 2016→2021 Camp Century waste-model change is the template, not an exception.

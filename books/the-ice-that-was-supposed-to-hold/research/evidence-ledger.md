# Evidence ledger

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

| Claim | Evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| U.S. Army built and operated Camp Century beneath the Greenland Ice Sheet beginning in 1959 and abandoned it in 1967. | CRREL Technical Report 174; Army technical record; modern GEUS/NASA documentation. | Does not establish every later description of the camp or its hidden strategic purpose. |
| Camp Century included a portable PM-2A pressurized-water reactor that operated for a limited period and was later dismantled/removed. | Army PM-2A construction and startup reports; GAO archival synthesis. | Reactor existence does not establish missile deployment; reactor-associated wastes are distinct from an intact buried reactor or spent fuel. |
| The Army seriously studied Project Iceworm, a large sub-ice missile-basing concept. | Declassified planning/diplomatic record reconstructed by Petersen; Army Greenland engineering context. | A serious plan is not an operational deployment. |
| Iceworm planning contemplated a network on the order of hundreds of missiles across a very large area. | Declassified project reconstruction, including Petersen 2008. | Numbers varied by planning phase and should not be presented as as-built specifications. |
| Camp Century trenches deformed under firn/ice creep and required maintenance. | CRREL performance reports and later historical/engineering analysis. | Deformation was expected qualitatively; the issue was rate, scale, maintenance and economics. |
| Drillers penetrated 4,550 ft of ice and roughly 12 ft of sub-ice material at Camp Century on July 2, 1966. | Ueda & Garfield 1968; Hansen & Langway 1966; CRREL/NSF drilling archive. | Exact handling of every basal interval requires chain-of-custody reconstruction. |
| The Camp Century core produced a nearly 100,000-year oxygen-isotope climate record. | Dansgaard et al. 1969, *Science*. | Early chronology and periodicity interpretations are not all final modern climate chronology. |
| Archived basal material contains preserved terrestrial plant material and records past ice-free conditions. | Christ et al. 2021, *PNAS*. | Does not by itself establish exact whole-Greenland ice geometry. |
| Upper basal sediment records an ice-free, flowing-water depositional environment 416 ± 38 ka. | Christ et al. 2023, *Science*. | Sea-level-equivalent implications require ice-sheet modeling. |
| Abandoned Camp Century material includes large amounts of physical debris, diesel, wastewater/sewage, probable PCB-bearing materials and reactor-associated radiological liquid waste. | Colgan et al. 2016 plus archival technical sources/GAO. | Inventory quantities remain reconstructed estimates; hazard depends on concentration/pathway. |
| 2017 radar mapped the debris field, with most reflectors deeper than 32 m and the tunnel network visible roughly 45–55 m deep. | Karlsson et al. 2019; GEUS radar reports. | Radar does not chemically identify all buried material. |
| Site-calibrated 2021 modeling projected continued burial through 2100 and found surface meltwater interaction with debris extremely unlikely under tested RCP scenarios. | Vandecrux et al. 2021. | Conditional projection, not guarantee beyond 2100 or outside modeled scenarios. |
| NASA UAVSAR unexpectedly imaged structural elements of Camp Century during April 2024 Greenland test flights. | NASA Earth Observatory, Nov. 25, 2024. | Novel radar view is not direct optical imaging and is not, by itself, a new contaminant inventory. |

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete / model-dependent

| Claim | Why credible | Missing closure |
|---|---|---|
| Engineering burden from ice deformation materially weakened Iceworm relative to competing basing systems. | Camp Century measured deformation; historical accounts describe technical and organizational problems. | Needs full decision-record matrix to separate technical, interservice, strategic and political weights. |
| Danish nuclear policy would have made full Iceworm approval politically difficult. | Petersen’s archival reconstruction; public Danish non-nuclear policy; Cold War diplomatic record. | Iceworm never reached a formal Danish approval process, so the counterfactual outcome is not directly observable. |
| Hydrocarbon remains in at least one collapsed sump. | Radar polarity and geometry consistent with liquid hydrocarbon at a historically known sump. | Direct sampling would provide chemical closure. |
| 2016 climate modeling identified a plausible future transition toward net ablation under high forcing. | Peer-reviewed regional/model analysis based on available climate products. | Later local calibration found a warm bias and revised the pre-2100 pathway materially downward. |
| Exposing Camp Century during MIS 11 implies at least ~1.4 m sea-level-equivalent loss from Greenland. | Ice-sheet modeling constrained by a direct local ice-free state. | The 1.4 m figure is model-derived, not a direct sediment measurement. |
| Some scientific/public activity at Camp Century also served strategic feasibility questions relevant to Iceworm. | Shared engineering requirements and declassified strategic context. | Individual scientists’ knowledge of the classified program varied and should not be assumed. |

## Class 3 — disputed / unsupported / contradicted by current evidence

| Claim | Status |
|---|---|
| Operational nuclear missiles were secretly deployed at Camp Century. | No public evidence located. Existing record supports a proposed system that was not deployed. |
| An intact nuclear reactor or spent reactor core remains buried beneath Camp Century. | Contradicted by documented dismantlement/removal; residual radiological waste is a different claim. |
| Camp Century waste is expected to melt out around 2090 under the best current site-specific model. | The 2016 high-forcing projection raised a future ablation concern, but 2021 site-calibrated modeling finds continued burial and negligible pre-2100 surface-meltwater contact. |
| Radar has revealed an additional unknown secret tunnel network beyond historical maps. | 2017 survey identified mapped tunnels and reported no additional tunnel network in the covered area. |
| Basal plant fossils show all of Greenland was forested 416 ka. | Unsupported overextension of local evidence. |
| Classification of Iceworm proves later unrelated allegations about hidden exotic technology. | Invalid inference. Classification establishes that some information was restricted, not the truth of other claims. |

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- Camp Century can serve as a quantitative control case for “physical program metabolism”: large secret programs should generate independent residue across logistics, maintenance, people, facilities, safety, waste, downstream effects and eventual archival traces.
- The four-clock framework—operational, engineering, political, geological—may generalize to other long-lived infrastructure decisions where one system is designed on a shorter timescale than its consequences.
- A blinded historical-intelligence exercise using partial Camp Century evidence could measure which combinations of evidence recover the true hidden program without inflating it into the false deployment claim.
- Future non-invasive geophysics may distinguish contaminant classes more precisely without excavation, but current radar alone is insufficient for a complete material inventory.
- Camp Century may remain safely isolated well beyond 2100 or may encounter new pathways under later climates; current evidence does not justify a specific post-2100 exposure date.

## Claim hygiene rules

- Never convert “planned,” “studied,” “proposed,” or “considered” into “built” or “deployed.”
- Never use the existence of PM-2A as evidence of nuclear weapons at the base.
- Never use a press release headline in place of the underlying model boundary.
- Never state 1.4 m sea-level equivalent as directly measured at Camp Century.
- Never state the 2016 waste-exposure projection without the 2021 site-specific revision.
- Never state the 2021 model as a permanent-safety guarantee.
- Never infer individual scientists’ knowledge of Iceworm without direct documentary evidence.
- Keep Greenlandic sovereignty/agency separate from Danish state consent.

## Thesis

Camp Century exposes four incompatible clocks operating on the same site: the operational clock of people and machines, the engineering clock of ice deformation, the political clock of secrecy and sovereignty, and the geological clock recorded by ice and sediment. The same ice was treated as fortress, laboratory, archive and waste repository, and each role failed—or survived—on a different timescale.

The manuscript distinguishes the documented Camp Century installation and PM-2A reactor from the classified but undeployed Project Iceworm missile concept; treats later Greenland basal science and climate monitoring as independent evidence rather than narrative decoration; and preserves the 2021 downward revision to the pre-2100 waste-exposure pathway rather than freezing the more dramatic 2016 projection in place.

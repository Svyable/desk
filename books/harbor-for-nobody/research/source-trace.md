# HARBOR FOR NOBODY — source trace

Checked 2026-09-19. Public/lawfully available sources only.

## Primary / authoritative spine

### DOE Office of Legacy Management — Chariot, Alaska, Site
https://www.energy.gov/lm/chariot-alaska-site

Use for current official boundary: proposed nuclear harbor; cancellation; no nuclear explosions and no devices brought to site; current stewardship. Do not use current summary as substitute for contemporaneous cancellation records.

### DOE Office of Legacy Management — Chariot fact sheet
https://www.energy.gov/lm/articles/chariot-alaska-site-fact-sheet

Use for remediation chronology, tracer-site history, consultation and present site status. Extract exact isotope/quantity claims only after checking the underlying reports cited by the fact sheet.

### DOE Office of Legacy Management — Tribal Collaboration
https://www.energy.gov/lm/tribal-collaboration

Use for the narrow official account of the 1962 tracer experiment: Nevada Test Site contaminated soil transported to Chariot; later local concern; 1993 removal decision. Do not convert consultation language into proof of historical consent.

### DOE LM — Point Hope consultation / 2014 borehole remediation
https://www.energy.gov/lm/articles/lm-meets-native-village-point-hope-alaska

Useful for >40 pretest bioenvironmental studies; 1959–61 drilling; 600–1,200-foot holes; ~1,000-foot permafrost; high-explosive scaling; diesel use and later contamination; community concerns in 2014. Later DOE description must be checked against original USGS drilling records before final quantitative prose.

### Don Charles Foote — A Human Geographical Study in Northwest Alaska (AEC Project Chariot), final report, June 1961
https://www.osti.gov/servlets/purl/4649596

Primary commissioned study. Page-index fully. High priority: project origin/site-selection chronology; Point Hope economy/subsistence; seasonal geography; researcher's account of community-project relations; sponsor framing; recommendations. Treat terminology as historical language and do not silently reproduce outdated ethnonyms in authorial voice.

### AEC/DOE historical Plowshare report
https://www.osti.gov/opennet/reports/plowshar.pdf

Use for dated program-level reconstruction. Current extracted executive-summary text records a mid-1959 Chariot configuration of one 100-kt cratering detonation plus four 20-kt channel detonations, later modified in November 1960 to one 200-kt plus four 20-kt detonations. Must page-index exact passage and follow its cited primary documents before freezing numbers in manuscript prose.

### DOE/OpenNet historical account of return to nuclear testing / moratorium context
https://www.osti.gov/opennet/servlets/purl/16156584.pdf

Useful for internal Plowshare/Chariot discussion under the test moratorium, fallout/clean-device concerns and advisory-committee dissent. Page-index and identify document authorship/provenance before quotation.

### DOE NEPA 2010 Chariot soil-sampling categorical exclusion
https://www.energy.gov/nepa/articles/cx-003136-categorical-exclusion-determination

Use for later investigation of diesel-range organics around five historical test holes and the explicit link to local concern. Follow downloadable PDF for exact locations/methods.

## Scientific afterlife comparator

### National Park Service — Cape Krusenstern lagoon zooplankton
https://www.nps.gov/articles/000/cakr-zooplankton.htm

NPS explicitly notes comparison with limited historic ecological data associated with Project Chariot. Useful evidence that some Chariot-era observations have a later scientific afterlife. Do not generalize from one modern comparison into a claim that the whole program became a foundational Arctic baseline.

# HARBOR FOR NOBODY — evidence ledger

## Rule

No claim moves upward because it is vivid. Program existence does not prove execution. Contamination does not prove a Chariot nuclear detonation. Opposition does not prove sole causation of cancellation. A commissioned study does not prove institutional acceptance of its conclusions. Community concern does not by itself establish a disease etiology.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- DOE Legacy Management identifies Chariot as a Plowshare site in the Ogotoruk Valley/Cape Thompson region and states that AEC considered excavating a harbor with nuclear explosions.
- DOE explicitly states that no nuclear explosions were conducted at Chariot and no nuclear devices were brought to the site.
- DOE records more than forty pretest bioenvironmental studies in the region between 1959 and 1962.
- USGS exploratory drilling reached roughly 600–1,200 feet; DOE says permafrost extended to approximately 1,000 feet.
- High-explosive scaling work occurred; at least one planned scaling test did not.
- A 1961 AEC-sponsored human-geography report by Don Charles Foote survives in OSTI and places the work inside the Environmental Studies Program.
- DOE states that in 1962 USGS conducted a radioactive-tracer experiment using contaminated soil brought from an experimental nuclear detonation at the Nevada Test Site.
- DOE records a 1993 radiological assessment/removal after consultation with local residents and Alaska DEC.
- DOE records later diesel contamination associated with the old boreholes and remediation activity.
- DOE continues long-term records/stakeholder/institutional-control work at the Chariot site.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- The relative causal weight of scientific dissent, Point Hope opposition, public controversy, economic weakness of the harbor, the testing moratorium, fallout, device design, budgets and AEC politics in cancellation.
- The extent to which specific Environmental Studies Program findings changed AEC decisions rather than merely supplying background.
- The degree to which Chariot data seeded later Arctic ecological baselines and institutional practices.
- Exact tracer-plot isotope inventory, mass, plot geometry, movement, recovery fraction and later residual distribution until primary technical reports are page-indexed.
- Local trust and health consequences require contemporaneous community, clinical and epidemiological sources.

## Class 3 — disputed / not established

- A secret nuclear Chariot detonation at Cape Thompson.
- Nuclear devices covertly transported to the Chariot site.
- The tracer plots as evidence of a hidden full-scale Chariot shot.
- A demonstrated Chariot-caused cancer cluster at Point Hope.
- AEC cancellation caused by one factor alone.
- Environmental studies as a simple origin story for NEPA or the modern environmental movement.

## Class 4 — synthesis / open conjecture

### Veto-generating survey
A serious pre-intervention measurement program can increase rather than decrease the institutional cost of intervention by making previously externalized relationships legible. Test: trace actual study findings into memoranda, hearings, decisions and opposition; compare against projects where extensive baseline work did not alter execution.

### Study residue
A non-executed capability can leave substantial physical residue because serious preparation itself requires drilling, test articles, instrumentation, roads, field stations, contaminants and cleanup. Test: build a maturity/footprint dataset across cancelled and executed classified programs.

### Knowledge reversal
A project can enter a region intending to convert local conditions into engineering parameters and discover that local/Indigenous empirical knowledge changes the definition of the system being engineered. Test: reconstruct specific claims made by Point Hope residents and compare them with later measured ecology rather than praising 'traditional knowledge' generically.

## Contradiction ledger

### 'Nothing happened' vs contamination
Resolution: no nuclear detonation occurred at Chariot, but later tracer work imported radioactive material from Nevada. These are compatible facts and must remain adjacent.

### 'Remote site' vs subsistence network
A low permanent-population density does not imply weak human coupling to a landscape. Quantify seasonal use, food pathways and movement before making exposure claims.

### 'Environmental science stopped the project'
Potentially attractive overclaim. The cancellation decision must be reconstructed against treaty/moratorium, budget, technical, fallout, economic and political variables.

### 'Scientists opposed Chariot'
Do not homogenize the scientific community. Identify individuals, institutions, dates, contractual roles and changes of position.

### 'Radioactive dirt was harmless' / 'radioactive dirt poisoned the region'
Both are premature without isotope, concentration, transport, tissue and dose reconstruction. DOE's decision to remove material partly to assure residents is not itself a retrospective dose finding.

## Incentive audit

- **AEC / Plowshare:** incentive to demonstrate socially constructive uses for nuclear explosive expertise and preserve programmatic relevance.
- **Lawrence Radiation Laboratory:** technical/programmatic interest in excavation physics and clean-device development; institutional interest does not imply bad faith but changes how feasibility claims should be read.
- **Environmental-study contractors:** mixed incentives—continued funding, scientific publication, professional independence, local relationships, and potential conflict with sponsor expectations.
- **Alaska political/business interests:** possible incentives around development, infrastructure, federal investment and state-building; must be actor-specific.
- **Point Hope / regional Iñupiat communities:** direct subsistence, health, land-use and sovereignty stakes; not reducible to generic anti-nuclear politics.
- **Later DOE Legacy Management:** mandate favors stewardship, remediation closure and durable institutional controls; current summaries may simplify earlier institutional conflict.
- **Later environmental histories:** incentive toward a clean origin story in which Chariot 'created' environmentalism or was stopped by heroic science; preserve multicausality.

## Quantitative extraction burden

Before manuscript lock, build tables for:

- every dated Chariot device configuration: number of shots, yield, depth, spacing, predicted crater dimensions and fallout assumptions;
- every known borehole/scaling test: coordinates, depth, method, outcome, remaining casing, contaminant;
- all Environmental Studies Program projects: investigator, institution, organism/system, dates, methods, deliverables, sponsor and later publication;
- tracer plots: isotope inventory, source event, transported mass, plot geometry, sampling dates, measured concentrations, cleanup inventory;
- Point Hope subsistence pathways used in contemporaneous dose/ecology models;
- project budgets and projected harbor economic use;
- cancellation chronology with decision documents and explicit reasons.

## Publication holds

Do not publish without primary chain:

- a single canonical Chariot yield configuration without date/version;
- 'the harbor had no economic purpose';
- 'scientists killed Chariot';
- 'Point Hope stopped the bomb';
- any cancer/health-causation claim;
- exact amount/isotope composition of imported tracer material;
- any claim that Chariot directly caused NEPA;
- any quotation attributed to Teller, Foote, local leaders or AEC officials from a secondary retelling.

## Strongest countermodel

Chariot may prove less about a self-defeating environmental survey than about a project overtaken by external history. The nuclear-test moratorium, emerging treaty constraints, Plowshare's clean-explosive problem, weak economics and changing federal priorities may explain most of the cancellation. The environmental program may then be historically important but causally secondary. This countermodel should receive a full chapter-level reconstruction rather than a paragraph of ritual skepticism.

## Defeat-capable comparative test

Construct a blinded set of mid-century federal megaprojects with pre-intervention environmental/baseline studies, including both executed and cancelled projects. Code study intensity, adverse findings, local opposition, economic case, external policy shocks and final disposition without using the project name during first-pass coding. If study intensity/adverse findings do not predict changed scope or cancellation after obvious confounds, retire or sharply narrow `veto-generating survey`.

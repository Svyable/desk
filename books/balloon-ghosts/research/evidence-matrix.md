# Evidentiary Matrix

## Class 1 — Verified / declassified / documented

- Project MOGUL existed as a classified balloon-borne acoustic detection effort connected to monitoring possible Soviet nuclear tests.
- The Air Force’s 1994 Roswell inquiry identified MOGUL as the best explanation for the recovered debris and published supporting program history and interviews.
- Project GENETRIX used high-altitude balloons for reconnaissance over the Soviet bloc under a weather-research cover story; CIA’s official U-2 history records 516 launches and major diplomatic backlash.
- U-2 and OXCART flights generated UFO reports. CIA historian Gerald Haines wrote that Air Force investigators sometimes used misleading public explanations to protect classified reconnaissance programs.
- Project BLUE BOOK investigated 12,618 reports; 701 remained categorized as unidentified at program closure.
- GAO searched for Roswell records in 1995 and documented both surviving material and missing/destroyed administrative record series.
- GAO and archival institutions found no provenance establishing the Majestic Twelve papers as authentic executive-branch documents.

## Class 2 — Credible but incomplete

- Exact reconstruction of the 1947 MOGUL balloon train associated with the Foster ranch debris, including launch numbering and trajectory, remains dependent on retrospective interviews, diaries, engineering records, weather data, and the interpretation of missing flight documentation.
- The precise fraction of UFO reports attributable to classified U-2/OXCART operations is based partly on retrospective CIA participant estimates and needs case-level cross-checking.
- Some Roswell witnesses reported unusual debris or later described events not captured in surviving 1947 documents. Those claims must be evaluated witness by witness and by first-report date.

## Class 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

- Recovery of extraterrestrial bodies or a non-human vehicle near Roswell.
- An operational Majestic Twelve crash-retrieval organization documented by the famous MJ-12 papers.
- Claims that every significant contradiction in the Roswell record reflects an integrated concealment program rather than memory, bureaucracy, ordinary classification, later narrative accretion, or missing records.
- Claims that all high-quality unexplained BLUE BOOK cases were classified U.S. programs.

## Class 4 — Speculative synthesis

- Secrecy can create an evidentiary ecology in which real classified objects, false public explanations, later disclosure, and incomplete archives reinforce increasingly extraordinary narratives even when no single actor designs that outcome.
- A state can be simultaneously truthful that a specific extraordinary claim is unsupported and untrustworthy because it demonstrably lied about adjacent classified activity.
- `Cover-up evidence` should be decomposed into at least three categories: concealment of a known terrestrial program, concealment of investigative uncertainty, and concealment of the extraordinary object alleged by witnesses. Evidence for one does not transfer automatically to another.

## Defeat conditions

The book’s central synthesis weakens if case-level reconstruction shows that classified aerospace activity contributed negligibly to UFO reporting and public distrust, or if primary records establish that misleading explanations were rare exceptions without meaningful cultural consequence. The Roswell portion would require major revision if authenticated contemporaneous records or physical evidence established a non-human recovery, or if MOGUL’s temporal/material fit collapses under better primary documentation.

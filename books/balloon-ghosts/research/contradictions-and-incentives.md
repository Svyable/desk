# Contradictions and Incentive Audit

## Institutional incentives

- **Army/Air Force security personnel:** protect classified detection and reconnaissance programs; minimize disclosure of capabilities and vulnerabilities.
- **Public affairs:** provide an immediate explanation even when the true explanation is compartmented or unavailable.
- **Intelligence agencies:** preserve sources/methods and plausible deniability; avoid revealing collection geometry through public UFO explanations.
- **Congressional/GAO investigators:** establish documentary accountability after decades of lost context.
- **Witnesses:** may seek accuracy, recognition, closure, or publicity; motives differ and must not be homogenized.
- **Authors/media:** narrative incentives favor bodies, crashed craft, perfect secrets, villains, and final answers.
- **Debunkers:** can overstate closure because a terrestrial hypothesis exists.
- **UFO advocates:** can overtransfer demonstrated government secrecy into support for claims the secrecy documents do not establish.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. The 1947 `flying disc` announcement is real; so is the later Air Force MOGUL explanation. The contradiction is historical evidence requiring reconstruction, not permission to select whichever sentence fits a prior belief.
2. The government demonstrably concealed secret aerospace programs and sometimes misled the public; that does not establish concealment of non-human recovery.
3. GAO documented record destruction/gaps; those gaps prevent total documentary closure but do not specify what the missing records contained.
4. BLUE BOOK left 701 cases unidentified; unidentified status preserves uncertainty but does not identify extraterrestrial origin.
5. CIA’s retrospective estimate that U-2/OXCART explained many reports is strong institutional testimony, but the percentage should not be repeated as a case-level fact without reconstructing the underlying denominator and crosswalk.
6. The 1997 dummy explanation may plausibly address later memory conflation in some accounts while being chronologically incapable of explaining an actual 1947 body event. Claims must be witness-specific.

## Author rule

Whenever the manuscript uses `cover-up`, identify the object allegedly covered up: classified terrestrial program, investigative uncertainty, bureaucratic error, witness information, or extraordinary recovered technology. Do not let the word migrate between categories.

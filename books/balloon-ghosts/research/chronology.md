# Chronology

- **1947, June–July** — NYU/Watson Laboratory balloon work associated with Project MOGUL operates from New Mexico. A debris field is later found on the Foster ranch northwest of Roswell.
- **1947, July 8** — Roswell Army Air Field public-information office announces recovery of a “flying disc”; the story is quickly replaced by a balloon explanation after material is taken to higher headquarters.
- **1948** — Project SIGN begins formal Air Force UFO investigation.
- **1949** — SIGN gives way to Project GRUDGE.
- **1952** — Project BLUE BOOK begins under Air Force Technical Intelligence structures amid a major national wave of sightings.
- **1953** — CIA-sponsored Robertson Panel evaluates UFO reporting partly as an air-defense and intelligence problem.
- **1954–1955** — U-2 development and testing introduce aircraft operating at altitudes and lighting conditions unfamiliar to civilian observers.
- **1956, January–February** — Project GENETRIX launches 516 reconnaissance balloons under a meteorological cover story; Soviet-bloc governments recover payloads and protest publicly.
- **1959 onward** — OXCART/A-12 development pushes speed, altitude, materials, and observability into another unfamiliar regime.
- **1969, December 17** — Air Force announces BLUE BOOK termination; 12,618 reports had been collected, 701 remaining categorized as unidentified.
- **Late 1970s–1980s** — Roswell returns to public attention through witness interviews, books, and claims recorded decades after 1947.
- **1980s** — purported Majestic Twelve documents surface publicly; provenance becomes central to the dispute.
- **1994** — Air Force issues *The Roswell Report: Fact versus Fiction in the New Mexico Desert*, identifying Project MOGUL as the explanation for recovered debris.
- **1995, July 28** — GAO publishes its Roswell records search and separate comments on MJ-12 material.
- **1997** — Air Force publishes *The Roswell Report: Case Closed*, attempting to address later body-related claims by connecting some memories to military anthropomorphic-dummy and accident/recovery events from later years. This argument requires careful witness-specific treatment and should not be generalized beyond the evidence.

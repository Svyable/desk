# Working Notes

## Reader promise

A UFO book where the strange thing is not only the object in the sky. The strange thing is what secrecy does to knowledge.

## Strongest competing explanation

Roswell and later UFO culture may be explainable mostly by ordinary misidentification, memory distortion, media amplification, and cultural reinforcement, with classified-program secrecy contributing less than this book proposes. If so, secrecy is an interesting secondary variable rather than the engine.

8. Recover Robertson Panel primary records and examine the air-defense overload/disinformation concern in its exact wording.
9. Build a chronology of Roswell’s cultural re-emergence from late-1970s interviews through MJ-12 and the 1990s official investigations.
10. Run forensic provenance analysis on the best-known MJ-12 documents using archival metadata, typography, routing formats, and earliest known copies.
11. Seek Soviet/East European primary reporting on captured GENETRIX payloads and diplomatic protests.
12. Expand all 18 chapters past 3,000 words, targeting 65,000+ chapter-only words through primary documents, scenes, witness-specific analysis, and technical explanation rather than thematic repetition.

## Voice rule

Do not make Roswell cute. No little-green-men jokes, no cheap believer/debunker caricatures. Follow the paperwork until the paperwork becomes stranger than the folklore.

## Claims frozen from primary record

- The Air Force’s 1994 investigation concluded that material recovered near Roswell was consistent with a then-classified balloon program and identified Project MOGUL as the relevant program.
- GAO’s 1995 search found surviving Roswell-related records but also documented that some administrative records from Roswell Army Air Field for the relevant period had been destroyed under records-disposition practices; this is a record gap, not evidence of extraterrestrial recovery.
- CIA historian Gerald Haines wrote that secret U-2 and OXCART flights generated many UFO reports and that officials sometimes gave misleading explanations to protect classified programs.
- CIA’s U-2 history documents GENETRIX: 516 reconnaissance balloons launched in early 1956, only 34 producing useful photography, with a weather-research cover story and major Soviet-bloc protests.
- National Archives records 12,618 BLUE BOOK sightings, 701 remaining classified as unidentified when the program ended.
- GAO found no evidence that the purported Majestic Twelve papers were authentic executive-branch documents; agencies and archives reported no supporting provenance.

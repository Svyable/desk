## Thesis

Modern UFO culture was shaped not only by unexplained observations but by a documented institutional feedback loop: classified aerospace programs produced genuinely strange things in the sky, secrecy prevented truthful public explanations, cover stories substituted for causes, and those cover stories later became evidence that the government was hiding something larger. Project MOGUL, GENETRIX, U-2, OXCART, and BLUE BOOK let us watch that ecology form in real time.

This book does **not** argue that every UFO case was a secret U.S. program, that Roswell conclusively settles every later witness claim, or that unexplained cases are therefore explained. It argues something narrower and more useful: secrecy changes the evidence available to observers and investigators, and therefore changes the culture built around anomalous observations.

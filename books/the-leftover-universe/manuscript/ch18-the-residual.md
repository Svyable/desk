# The Residual

A complete explanation of ordinary matter would make this book obsolete in the best possible way.

Imagine the announcement not as a press conference but as a convergence.

A new particle appears in one experiment. Its mass and couplings match a model that also explains neutrino masses. Another experiment observes lepton-number violation. DUNE and Hyper-K pin down a CP phase. A precision EDM measurement finds a nonzero value with the right operator structure. Cosmological data constrain the thermal history. Calculations using those measured quantities produce the baryon asymmetry without knobs left to turn. Competing models fail independent tests.

At some point the phrase “baryogenesis mechanism” stops naming a menu and starts naming history.

We are not there.

This book should end where the field is, not where a narrative arc wants it to be.

The observable universe contains a small positive baryon asymmetry.

Known Standard Model CP violation does not explain its magnitude.

The Standard Model electroweak transition is not strongly first order at the measured Higgs mass.

Sphaleron processes provide baryon and lepton number violation at high temperatures in a way important for many mechanisms.

Neutrinos have mass, proving the minimal Standard Model incomplete, but we do not know whether they are Majorana particles or whether their high-energy relatives generated an asymmetry.

Antihydrogen matches hydrogen with impressive precision and falls downward within current direct experimental uncertainty.

No proton decay has been confirmed.

No neutron-antineutron oscillation has been confirmed.

No permanent EDM beyond Standard Model expectations has been established.

No cosmic anti-star or primordial antimatter domain has been established.

No axion has been established.

The residue is known more firmly than its cause.

That is the scientific position.

Everything else is ranking possibilities.

There is dignity in an unfinished account because unfinishedness is not ignorance without structure. We know what a successful mechanism has to survive. We know many ways it cannot work. We know where several relevant symmetries break and where they appear exact. We know the approximate abundance to reproduce. We know the thermal epochs any candidate must pass through.

The mystery has walls.

Good frontiers acquire walls over time.

A century ago antimatter itself was not established. Ninety years ago the positron was new. Seventy years ago parity was assumed conserved. Sixty years ago CP violation was new. Fifty years ago the Standard Model was being assembled. Thirty years ago neutrino mass was unconfirmed. Fifteen years ago trapped antihydrogen was new. Three years before this book's current edition, antihydrogen's gravitational motion had not been directly observed.

The unknown did not merely shrink.

It changed shape.

This is why “science has no answer” can be a misleading way to describe a live problem. A blank sheet and a half-solved equation are both incomplete, but they are not the same state of knowledge.

Baryogenesis is a half-solved equation with too many candidate terms.

The next decisive clue could come from a place this book has emphasized.

Neutrinoless double-beta decay.

A leptonic CP measurement.

A proton-decay event.

An EDM.

A new Higgs-sector state.

A stochastic gravitational-wave background from a first-order phase transition.

A heavy neutral lepton.

A confirmed cosmic antinucleus.

An axion.

Or none of these.

The mechanism could live at an energy scale so high that direct traces are sparse. It could involve a field not yet prominent in the literature. It could exploit a nonstandard cosmological history before nucleosynthesis. It could link to dark matter, inflation or quantum gravity in a way present models only approximate.

A frontier is allowed to surprise the table of contents.

That is one reason to preserve research notes and uncertainty labels rather than polish the book into a false monument. Established fact, credible inference, speculative synthesis and open conjecture belong in different typographic rooms even when prose moves between them.

The established fact is the residual.

The inference is that some early history produced or selected it.

The speculative synthesis is which mechanisms connect the measured cracks in symmetry to the abundance.

The conjecture extends further: perhaps the asymmetry is linked to dark matter, axions, inflationary domains or hidden sectors.

A reader should always know which floor they are standing on.

The human role is also easiest to state in layers.

Established fact: humans are composed of ordinary baryonic matter.

Established fact: humans have built experiments capable of producing, trapping and measuring antimatter and of inferring cosmological baryon abundance.

Inference: known human-like life depends on a persistent ordinary-matter inventory, though the full space of possible life is unknown.

Speculation: intelligence elsewhere may independently discover the asymmetry if it develops physics.

Conjecture beyond usefulness: the universe produced intelligence in order to understand the asymmetry.

The last statement has no evidence and adds no predictive power.

We can leave it behind without making the story smaller.

What remains is bizarre enough.

A minor chemical species on a rocky planet has reconstructed a thermal history preceding atoms.

The species used atoms to do it.

We are downstream matter building upstream models.

This asymmetry of time is more interesting to me than a cosmic purpose.

Causation runs forward. Explanation can run backward.

The early universe determines which relics exist. Relics constrain our theories of the early universe. Our theories do not alter the event, but they create a second relation across time: not influence, but intelligibility.

A cause need not know its consequence for the consequence to know the cause.

That sentence sits close to philosophy. Let us anchor it.

Planck measured microwave photons.

Spectrometers measure primordial deuterium.

LHCb reconstructs decay vertices.

ALPHA counts annihilation positions.

DUNE will image argon ionization.

Super-K watches Cherenkov rings.

EDM experiments count spin phase.

These are physical records.

The backward reach of explanation is assembled from them.

There is no direct perception of baryogenesis. There is triangulation.

Triangulation may be the most characteristic human activity in fundamental physics. Our senses occupy one scale. Reality supplies signals at others. We build multiple conversions and ask where they intersect.

This is how gravitational waves were found: interferometers in separated sites observed matching strain patterns consistent with general relativity and astrophysical binaries. This is how the Higgs was established: ATLAS and CMS saw compatible excesses across decay channels. This is how neutrino oscillations became unavoidable: solar, atmospheric, reactor and accelerator data converged.

Baryogenesis will probably be solved the same way.

Not by one beautiful result, but by incompatible alternatives running out of room.

This is less cinematic than a revelation and more durable.

The residue itself offers one unusually stable target. The cosmic baryon abundance inferred from nucleosynthesis and the CMB agrees across epochs. A successful origin theory cannot negotiate that number away easily. It has to make it.

This gives the problem resistance.

Many cosmological mysteries suffer from flexible phenomenology. Dark energy can be parameterized by equations of state. Inflation has many potentials. Dark matter has huge candidate space. Baryogenesis shares that abundance of models, but the output charge is sharply measured.

The challenge is to make the input equally sharp.

That is what the next generation of experiments is doing.

DUNE and Hyper-K sharpen the lepton sector.

LEGEND, nEXO and other double-beta experiments sharpen Majorana possibilities.

Next-generation EDM searches sharpen new CP phases.

Hyper-K and DUNE sharpen proton decay.

LHC upgrades sharpen Higgs and flavor physics.

LISA may sharpen early phase transitions.

Antimatter facilities sharpen CPT and gravity.

Axion experiments sharpen strong CP solutions.

Cosmic-ray instruments sharpen antimatter abundance.

The solution space is being cut from many sides.

There is no reason the cuts must meet soon.

This is another point where automation culture and scientific culture differ. An automated task expects completion. Nature does not care about the sprint.

A book can be completed around an incomplete problem only by making the incompleteness part of its structure.

That is what I have tried to do.

The first chapter began with a number hiding inside every ordinary thing.

The number remains.

But now it has neighbors.

It sits beside the kaon CP parameter, CKM phase, neutrino mass splittings, EDM upper limits, proton-lifetime limits, antihydrogen transition frequencies, gravitational measurements, Planck baryon density, antihelium backgrounds, axion coupling exclusions.

The mystery is no longer one number.

It is a graph of constraints.

This is how underwritten universal realities often look when you stop writing them as aphorisms. “Existence is a leftover” is a sharp opening. The real substance is dozens of experimental systems and theoretical conditions that make the phrase physically defensible.

Without that substance the thesis is a poster.

With it, the thesis becomes an orientation toward ordinary matter.

Look at a wall.

Nothing about its surface indicates an early CP asymmetry. Paint covers drywall. Drywall is calcium sulfate, cellulose, additives. Calcium nuclei contain protons and neutrons. Those baryons exist because the net baryon inventory did not vanish. Their quarks are confined by QCD. Electrons neutralize charge. Electromagnetism gives the solid structure. Gravity holds the wall to Earth. Dark matter helped the galaxy form. Stellar nucleosynthesis made calcium.

The wall is an intersection of solved and unsolved physics.

Ordinariness is layered ignorance stabilized into use.

A carpenter does not need baryogenesis to hang a cabinet. This is not a deficit. Reality can be reliable at one scale while unexplained at another. Effective theories are powerful precisely because higher-level behavior does not require solving every deeper origin.

The wall works despite the missing cosmology.

The proton works despite the missing baryogenesis.

This is one of science's great comforts. Incomplete foundations do not prevent local knowledge.

It is also one of its dangers. Reliable local knowledge can make foundations invisible. Matter behaves so consistently that its existence stops seeming like a question.

We inherit the residual as furniture.

Physics removes the upholstery.

Underneath is an almost canceled ledger.

I want to end with the antiatom, not because it is the answer but because it concentrates the situation.

A group of humans takes ordinary matter from Earth and uses it to build an accelerator. Protons strike a target and create antiprotons. Magnets select the antiprotons. Machines slow them. Fields trap them. Positrons are gathered. Antihydrogen forms. Magnetic wells keep neutral antiatoms away from the ordinary-matter apparatus. Lasers interrogate them. Gravity pulls them downward. Eventually they hit the wall and annihilate. Detectors reconstruct the event.

The surviving side has manufactured the missing side and asked it whether the laws remember the difference.

So far, mostly, they do not.

That is the residual's final insult.

Matter and antimatter look locally like partners.

The universe looks globally like a divorce.

Some event, field, phase, decay, oscillation or boundary made the separation durable.

We do not know which.

The correct ending is therefore not awe.

It is a research question with a body attached.

Why did anything made of baryons remain?

We are still taking measurements.

# The Leftover Universe

**Why Anything Exists After Almost Everything Canceled**

By **Sven Hardy Benson**

## Thesis

Ordinary matter is not the universe's default substance. In the hot early universe, matter and antimatter were nearly balanced; almost all of the symmetric population annihilated. Every star, wall, animal and laboratory made of baryons exists downstream of a tiny surviving excess whose origin remains unknown. *The Leftover Universe* treats that unresolved baryon asymmetry as a central fact of the human situation without turning it into metaphor: we are not cosmically chosen; we are the residue that learned to interrogate the cancellation.

## Scope

Eighteen chapters move from Dirac's antimatter and annihilation through Sakharov's conditions, CP violation, the Standard Model's quantitative failure, baryon CP violation, neutrino mass and leptogenesis, DUNE, trapped antihydrogen and gravity, cosmic antimatter searches, the strong CP problem and axions, then back to the statistical and epistemic meaning of being made from the unexplained excess.

The manuscript keeps four epistemic levels separate throughout:

- **Established:** observed baryon abundance, laboratory antimatter, CP violation, neutrino oscillation/mass differences, antihydrogen spectroscopy and gravity within experimental precision.
- **Credible inference:** an early process or initial condition produced the positive net baryon abundance in our observable region.
- **Speculative synthesis:** leptogenesis, electroweak baryogenesis, GUT baryogenesis, axion-assisted mechanisms, asymmetric dark matter and related proposals.
- **Open conjecture:** global antimatter domains beyond the horizon and broader interpretations of intelligence's place in the residual.

## Research posture

The book uses primary collaboration pages, peer-reviewed experiment papers, Particle Data Group reviews and major laboratory documentation as the backbone. Research notes and direct links live in `research/source-trail.md`. Claims at the 2025–2026 frontier are dated and separated from enduring background.

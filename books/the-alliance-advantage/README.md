# The Alliance Advantage

*Why Networks Beat Empires in the AI Age*

The central thesis is that alliances create durable power only when political promises become interoperable systems. In the AI age that means more than common weapons: data, models, clouds, identity, communications, logistics, standards, munitions, industrial capacity, permissions, testing, and the human ability to operate together under pressure.

The book moves from NATO to AUKUS and the Indo-Pacific to ask why networks can outperform empires when they make distributed capability usable as one force—and why a large alliance can still fail if its parts cannot connect.

This Desk edition completes the 18-chapter argument as a first full manuscript while preserving the research ledger and falsification tests for revision.

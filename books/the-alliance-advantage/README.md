# The Alliance Advantage

*Why Networks Beat Empires in the AI Age*

The working thesis is that alliances create durable power only when political promises become interoperable systems. In the AI age that means more than common weapons: data, models, clouds, identity, communications, logistics, standards, munitions, industrial capacity, permissions, testing, and the human ability to operate together under pressure.

The book moves from NATO to AUKUS and the Indo-Pacific to ask why networks can outperform empires when they make distributed capability usable as one force—and why a large alliance can still fail if its parts cannot connect.

The first field edition publishes the framework and opening research path while the complete trade manuscript remains in development on Desk.

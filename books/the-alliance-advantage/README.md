*Why Networks Beat Empires in the AI Age*

The working thesis is that an alliance is only as strong as the systems that let its members act together. Treaties create obligations and political expectations. Operational power comes from interoperability: data that can be shared, weapons that can be supplied, software that can connect, intelligence that can cross classifications, logistics that can move, industrial capacity that can surge, and legal permissions that arrive before the crisis has passed.

AI raises the value of alliances while making their hidden seams more important. A coalition can agree politically and still fail technically. The Alliance Advantage is about those seams.

# The Alliance Advantage

*Why Networks Beat Empires in the AI Age*

Sven Hardy Benson

A treaty can say that an attack on one is an attack on all. It cannot make two databases interoperable, move a missile across a border, approve a cloud connection, refill an ammunition stockpile, or tell a commander which ally is legally permitted to see the targeting data on a screen.

Those are smaller facts with larger consequences.

# Outsourcing Divinity

**Subtitle:** What Happens When We Give Machines the Powers We Once Gave God  
**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson  
**Status:** Draft manuscript  
**Book ID:** `outsourcing-divinity`

## Positioning

*Outsourcing Divinity* is a book about authority, not a claim that artificial intelligence is literally divine. Human beings have always delegated functions that exceed the individual: memory to writing, interpretation to institutions, legitimacy to states, coordination to markets, judgment to experts, prediction to models, and moral or spiritual guidance to trusted intermediaries. Conversational AI gathers many of those functions into a single interface.

The central question is what happens when we outsource not merely labor or information processing, but functions historically associated with ultimate authority: judgment, confession, prediction, interpretation, absolution, moral permission, and the settlement of uncertainty.

The book distinguishes religious theology from analogy; established history from interpretation; documented AI behavior from speculation; and useful delegation from abdication of responsibility. It is neither anti-religious nor techno-mystical.

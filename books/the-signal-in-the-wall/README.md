# The Signal in the Wall

## Moscow, Microwave Hearing, and the Weapon We Still Cannot Prove

**Sven Hardy Benson**

### Thesis

The history of directed radio-frequency exposure is dangerous to reason about because several facts that sound like conspiracy folklore are real: the Soviet Union did direct microwave energy at the U.S. Embassy in Moscow; the United States secretly investigated possible biological and behavioral effects; pulsed radio-frequency energy can produce an auditory sensation through a well-established thermoelastic mechanism; and modern U.S. expert panels have judged pulsed electromagnetic energy physically plausible for a subset of anomalous health incidents. None of those facts proves that the Havana-era incidents were caused by a foreign RF weapon. The book is about the discipline required to keep those propositions separate.

The larger claim is epistemic and operational. Once a state has experienced a real covert beam, a real secret research program, and a real sensory bioeffect, later unexplained symptoms inherit a preloaded story. That story may be true in some cases. It may also create confirmation pressure, classification asymmetry, diagnostic drift, copycat reporting, and an evidence ecology in which mechanism plausibility gets mistaken for event attribution. The only mature response is to instrument the environment before the event disappears.

### Evidence classes

1. **Verified / declassified / documented** — the Moscow microwave signal; U.S. diplomatic protests; Projects PANDORA/BIZARRE; U.S. uncertainty about health effects; the microwave auditory effect; the 2020 National Academies report; the 2022 IC Experts Panel; the 2023 and 2025 Intelligence Community assessments; NIH's 2024 controlled clinical and imaging studies.
2. **Credible but incomplete** — individual AHI witness accounts; some acute directional symptom clusters; claims that a subset of cases had unusual spatial or temporal structure; intelligence reporting summarized but not publicly released.
3. **Disputed / unconfirmed** — a deployed foreign RF weapon causing Havana-era incidents; specific adversary attribution; claims that unusual MRI findings prove a unique external injury; claims that every AHI shares one cause.
4. **Speculative synthesis / open conjecture** — that the Moscow Signal history created an institutional prior powerful enough to shape later investigation; that a deliberately engineered device could exploit known sensory effects without leaving persistent structural injury; that the decisive next evidence would be synchronized environmental capture rather than retrospective symptom interpretation.

### Reader promise

This is not a book arguing that Havana Syndrome was a microwave attack. It is not a book arguing that everyone imagined it. It is a book about why both shortcuts fail when the archive contains a real beam, the laboratory contains a real effect, the clinic contains real suffering, and attribution still does not close.

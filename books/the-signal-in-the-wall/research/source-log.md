## Source-handling rules for expansion

- Prefer document-level primary sources over retrospective summaries when the underlying document is public.
- Separate mechanism from event attribution in every note.
- Preserve confidence language from intelligence assessments exactly in substance; do not convert 'very unlikely' into 'impossible.'
- Do not use patents as efficacy evidence.
- Do not infer classified content beyond what public summaries say.
- Page-index all long reports before release.

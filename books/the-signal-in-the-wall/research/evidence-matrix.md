## Defeat condition for the book's central synthesis

The central synthesis would be weakened if public primary records showed that the Moscow Signal history has been materially mischaracterized, if the microwave auditory effect failed modern replication under historically supported parameters, or if a comprehensive, independently inspectable event dataset established one common AHI etiology with actor attribution, making the manuscript's emphasis on mechanism/attribution separation obsolete.

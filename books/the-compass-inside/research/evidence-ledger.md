# Evidence ledger — The Compass Inside

## Governing thesis

Animal magnetoreception and defense quantum sensing approach the same systems problem from opposite directions: biology demonstrates useful weak-field navigation in motion and noise while mechanism remains unsettled; engineered quantum sensors demonstrate exquisite mechanisms and sensitivity while field robustness remains a central transition problem. The book tests whether biological magnetoreception offers transferable architectural lessons about weak-signal extraction, amplification, cue fusion, calibration, and movement—not whether animals possess magical sensors.

## Evidence class 1 — verified / documented

- U.S. Navy STTR Phase I award, 2002, **Biologically Inspired Underwater Navigation Based on Geomagnetism**, $69,961. The public award abstract explicitly proposes adapting magnetic navigation capabilities of sea turtles, migratory fishes and lobsters to AUV/diver navigation without surfacing for GPS. Source: https://www.sbir.gov/awards/69631
- DARPA Robust Quantum Sensors (RoQS), launched 2025. DARPA states that vibration, electromagnetic interference and gradients impede field deployment of quantum sensors and that Phase 1 sensor-development teams will test compact systems on a government-provided helicopter. Sources: https://www.darpa.mil/news/2025/quantum-sensors-defense-platforms and https://www.darpa.mil/news/2025/roqs-launches-first-phase
- DARPA QuIVER publicly pursues tensor/vector magnetometers for rapid location of magnetic anomalies. Source: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/quantum-imaging-of-vector-electromagnetic-radiation
- DARPA QuASAR publicly pursued atom and atom-like precision sensors and hybrid quantum sensing, including inertial navigation applications. Source: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/quantum-assisted-sensing-and-readout
- Denton et al., *Nature Communications* 15, 10823 (2024), models magnetosensitivity of tightly bound cryptochrome radical pairs enabled by asymmetric recombination / quantum Zeno dynamics. The paper explicitly frames this as a plausible weak-field mechanism, not proof that the modeled pair is the biological receptor. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-55124-x

## Evidence class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Radical-pair / cryptochrome mechanisms have substantial theoretical, biochemical and behavioral support, but the full causal chain from specific molecular species to receptor anatomy, neural signal and navigation remains unresolved.
- Magnetic-particle mechanisms remain relevant in the literature and may support different magnetic functions or taxa. Do not force a single-mechanism narrative.
- Biomimetic geomagnetic navigation may have engineering value, but the 2002 Navy award is evidence of a proposed R&D program, not operational transition. Recover final reports, later phases, patents only as filings, performer publications and follow-on contracts before claiming outcome.

## Evidence class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- No claim that a specific cryptochrome radical pair is established as *the* animal magnetic receptor.
- No claim that magnetoreception proves long-lived macroscopic quantum coherence.
- No claim that animals outperform engineered magnetometers on conventional sensitivity/accuracy metrics.
- No claim that the Navy deployed a turtle-derived magnetic navigation system.
- No claim that DARPA RoQS uses or derives from biological magnetoreception.
- No claim that radio-frequency disruption experiments uniquely prove the radical-pair mechanism without a protocol-by-protocol audit.

## Evidence class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

The transferable biological advantage may sit above the receptor: huge ensembles of weak molecular events, biochemical amplification, temporal integration, active movement, multimodal cue fusion and continuous ecological recalibration could convert a noisy microscopic field dependence into useful behavior. If so, quantum engineering may learn more from the *architecture around* a biological quantum-sensitive reaction than from copying its molecule.

This is a synthesis to test, not a finding.

## Strongest countermodel

The apparent convergence may be mostly rhetorical. Animals require only coarse orientation and can integrate many nonmagnetic cues over long times. Engineered defense sensors face quantitative accuracy, latency, certification, interchangeability and platform constraints that biological navigation never had to meet. Conventional engineering—differential sensing, packaging, shielding, calibration, classical sensor fusion and better algorithms—may solve field robustness without any distinctive lesson from biology. If so, magnetoreception is scientifically fascinating but strategically peripheral.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. Behavioral capability can be strong while receptor identification remains weak.
2. A mechanism can be genuinely quantum without yielding a useful quantum-technology analogy.
3. Laboratory sensitivity and field utility are different measurements.
4. Biological robustness can hide low precision, slow integration and cue redundancy.
5. Multiple magnetic mechanisms may coexist; a compass and a magnetic map need not use the same receptor.
6. Military funding demonstrates interest, not efficacy or deployment.

## Defeat condition

The book's broad thesis loses if literature reconstruction shows that (a) magnetoreception's robust behavioral performance depends primarily on nonmagnetic cues with magnetic information too weak or conditional to matter; (b) proposed quantum-sensitive biology lacks a defensible empirical bridge beyond in-vitro/theoretical plausibility; or (c) every apparently transferable robustness principle is already ordinary sensor engineering with no explanatory gain from the biological comparison.

## Quantitative comparison required before publication

Build a table separating biological and engineered systems by: field magnitude; directional versus scalar information; bandwidth/integration time; operating temperature; motion state; calibration requirement; orientation dependence; electromagnetic interference; spatial gradients; energy budget; sensor mass/volume where meaningful; cue-fusion dependence; demonstrated navigation error; recovery after displacement; and whether performance is laboratory, field, or operational. Do not compare unlike metrics as if they were a leaderboard.

## Research queue

- Recover the Navy N023-0045 solicitation and any Phase II/follow-on records for the 2002 geomagnetic-navigation award.
- Reconstruct Lohmann laboratory turtle magnetic-map experiments from primary papers, including field manipulations, sample sizes, statistics, replication and competing interpretations.
- Audit European robin / songbird radio-frequency disruption literature and later replication disputes.
- Reconstruct cryptochrome 4 evidence, including in-vitro magnetic sensitivity and the limits of extrapolation to receptor function.
- Audit magnetite-based receptor evidence, retractions/corrections, anatomical localization disputes and taxa where particle mechanisms remain plausible.
- Separate compass direction, inclination, polarity, intensity and map-position tasks throughout.
- Recover DARPA RoQS solicitation PS-25-11 and performer list; build helicopter environment/error budget.
- Compare RoQS with QuIVER, QuASAR and mature classical magnetometers so 'quantum' does not become the baseline by fiat.
- Audit Chinese and European quantum-magnetometry/navigation programs from primary institutional and peer-reviewed sources.
- Look for actual defense use of geomagnetic map matching and quantify what biological inspiration adds beyond conventional magnetic anomaly navigation.

## Publication gate

No chapter may convert a theoretical radical-pair result into a behavioral mechanism without an explicit bridge. No military program objective becomes achieved capability without performance evidence. No 'quantum biology' claim survives merely because electron spin appears in the mechanism. Every major chapter must identify the strongest ordinary explanation and what observation would distinguish it from the book's preferred synthesis.

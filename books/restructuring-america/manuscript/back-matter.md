# Back Matter

## About the Author

Sven Hardy Benson writes about institutions at the point where engineering, law, economics, and public administration collide.

## Sources and Method

This book combines public records, agency data, regulatory materials, technical reports, economic research, and institutional analysis. The chapter-by-chapter source trail and falsification notes remain in the research directory of the project.

Forecasts about AI capability, electricity demand, labor markets, construction technology, and institutional change are treated as scenarios rather than certainties. The manuscript’s argument is designed to survive being wrong about the speed of AI: infrastructure and state capacity remain useful if progress slows and become more valuable complements if it accelerates.

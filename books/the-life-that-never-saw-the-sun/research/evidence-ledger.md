## Defeat conditions

The broad ecological thesis weakens if cross-site flux budgets show radiolytic substrate generation consistently below maintenance requirements; if alleged isolated systems are shown to depend primarily on recent surface organics; or if rigorous contamination audits remove the strongest indigenous deep-life cases. Planetary extensions weaken if liquid water, transport, or electron-acceptor availability fail under realistic crustal models even where radiolytic energy is present.

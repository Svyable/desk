## Claim boundaries added in the Chapter 5 audit

- Chivian et al.’s >99.9% result applies to the **fluid phase of one Mponeng fracture**, not to all rock-attached biomass or the continental deep biosphere. Later work describes the original metagenome as derived from roughly 10^11 cells filtered from about 5,600 L of fracture water.
- Lau et al. analyzed five *Desulforudis*-like single cells from a neighboring Tau Tona fracture. Depending on the cell, 0–32% of recovered genes were absent from the original Mponeng metagenome; prophage, CRISPR, retron, restriction-modification and transposase signatures provide direct evidence that viral infection and horizontal gene transfer belong inside the lineage’s evolutionary history.
- Tau Tona is not a failed replication of Mponeng. It is another fracture and another sampling episode. Its value is as counterevidence to promoting one near-monoculture fluid sample into a universal one-species ecology.
- Becraft et al. found >99.2% average nucleotide identity across *Desulforudis* genomes from Africa, North America and Eurasia, including unexpectedly conserved prophage/CRISPR regions. This supports exceptional evolutionary continuity while also making a purely local Mponeng explanation less attractive.
- Westmeijer et al. 2024 found *Desulforudis* again dominant at 975 m in Sweden, but within a community containing other prokaryotes and substantial eukaryotic marker diversity. Recurrence of the lineage is stronger evidence than recurrence of literal single-species ecology.

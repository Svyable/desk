## Thesis

Deep continental life demonstrates that a biosphere can draw usable energy from radioactive decay in rock rather than sunlight. But the famous phrase “independent of the Sun” hides several different claims. Radiolysis can make hydrogen locally; a cell can fix carbon; an ecosystem may still depend on sulfate, phosphorus, nitrogen, permeability, or geological products whose histories reach back to the surface. The distinction between **local energetic autonomy** and **global biogeochemical autonomy** changes how we think about Earth’s deep biosphere and where we look for life on Mars, Europa, Enceladus, and other buried worlds.

# Back Matter

## Evidence posture

This book distinguishes four evidence classes throughout:

1. **Verified / documented:** peer-reviewed deep-fracture geochemistry; published radiolytic-H2 calculations; the 2008 *Desulforudis* genome; 2011 deep nematodes; 2013 Precambrian-age fracture-fluid constraints; 2018 continental-subsurface biomass synthesis; 2024 Swedish *Desulforudis*-dominated groundwater; published Mars habitability calculations.
2. **Credible but incomplete / model-dependent:** exact local radiolysis-to-biomass budgets; groundwater isolation histories; ecological closure; active pathway inference from genomic potential; quantitative extraterrestrial transfer.
3. **Disputed / unsupported extensions:** extant martian or Europan life inferred from terrestrial analogues; complete sunlight-independence without element-by-element provenance; universal single-species deep ecosystems.
4. **Speculative synthesis:** long-lived underground biospheres as common planetary states; geological-energy maps as future life-detection target maps; biosphere lifetimes exceeding surface-habitability lifetimes.

## Research priorities

The draft's largest empirical gaps are quantitative. A merge-ready edition should reconstruct the Mponeng fracture-water energy budget from primary tables; distinguish H2 inventory from production flux; trace sulfate provenance at each principal field site; build a cross-site radionuclide/water/H2/biomass matrix; audit contamination controls across classic South African studies; compare *Desulforudis* genomes from South Africa and Sweden; and map terrestrial radiolysis rates into current Mars, Europa and Enceladus models without treating modeled habitability as detection.

## Decisive experiment

A multicenter deep-fracture study should preregister predicted supportable biomass using measured U-Th-K, pore-water geometry, radiolytic yields, sulfur mineralogy and temperature before microbial abundance is revealed. Fractures should be isolated with packers; service-water and reagent blanks sequenced; hydrogen flux measured repeatedly; active sulfate reduction and carbon fixation tested with isotope tracers; intact-cell counts separated from environmental DNA; mineral-attached and fluid communities analyzed independently.

If radiolytic energy predicts low-carbon autotrophic biomass across independent sites substantially better than competing energy models, the central thesis strengthens. If it does not, radiolysis remains a real geochemical mechanism but loses its claim to broad ecological importance.

## A note on extraterrestrial life

No extraterrestrial organism, biosignature or ecosystem is claimed in this manuscript. Mars, Europa and Enceladus sections concern habitability models and search strategy only. Energetic sufficiency is not evidence of occupation; survival of terrestrial organisms in an analogue environment is not evidence that life could originate there.

## Acknowledgment of uncertainty

The deep biosphere is unusually vulnerable to contamination, sparse sampling, uncertain fluid connectivity and extremely slow processes. Strong prose should not manufacture stronger evidence. Where the geological system cannot yet close its energy and nutrient budget, the manuscript says so.

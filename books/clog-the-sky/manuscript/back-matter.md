# Back Matter

## Evidence note

This manuscript treats official records as evidence of what institutions documented, assessed, recommended, or claimed. An official source does not automatically establish the truth of every proposition inside it. In particular, the Robertson Panel’s concern that Soviet or other hostile actors might exploit UFO reporting is documented; a Soviet operation that generated or coordinated the 1952 U.S. UFO wave is not established by the public record used here.

Likewise, a BLUE BOOK classification of “unidentified” is treated as a statement about the available investigation, not as a physical-origin category. Later declassification of U-2/OXCART and other classified aviation is evidence that some reports had protected terrestrial explanations; it is not a license to assign any unresolved case to a secret program without case-specific evidence.

## A note on the title

“Clog the Sky” refers to the informational sky as much as the physical one. The central object is the stream of alerts, reports, interpretations and reactions through which an ambiguous aerial event becomes operational work.

## Acknowledgment of incompleteness

This is a working Desk manuscript. Several quantitative and documentary reconstructions remain before it should be treated as a release candidate: the complete July 1952 warning-channel ledger; authoritative BLUE BOOK Special Report No. 14 reconstruction; the full Tremonton/Great Falls photographic-analysis chain; primary documentation for U-2/OXCART sighting attribution; the Condon study’s internal history; and a systematic bridge from Robertson-era threat models to modern UAP reporting architecture.

# Reading Lineage for *Leveraging Luck*

This file records how Sven Hardy Benson's wider reading life should inform the manuscript without turning the book into a parade of summaries. The goal is not to cite every influence in-line. The goal is to let the wisdom of these shelves deepen the book's voice, structure, and examples.

## How to Use This File

Use these works as **lineage**, not ornament.

A chapter should not say, “Here is what Kahneman says,” unless the point truly requires it. More often, the chapter should absorb the lesson and speak in Sven's own voice: narrative first, insight second, framework third, application fourth.

The reader should feel the depth of the research without feeling trapped in a literature review.

## Sven's Voice: Rules of Thumb

1. **Scene before thesis.** Begin with a person, room, decision, object, storm, mistake, glance, or delay.
2. **Then reveal the hidden system.** Show the macro conditions that made the moment matter.
3. **Name the decision.** Luck alone is not the story. The human response is the hinge.
4. **Let consequences compound.** Show how the moment becomes a career, cure, company, marriage, reputation, failure, or legacy.
5. **End with a line worth carrying.** Each chapter should leave the reader with one sentence that feels inevitable.

## Avoid These Manuscript Traps

- **Quote collage:** Do not stack epigraphs and anecdotes without narrative causality.
- **Generic motivational speech:** Replace “go do it” with concrete stakes, uncertainty, and consequence.
- **Overclaiming control:** Never imply that mindset defeats structural disadvantage or tragedy.
- **False certainty:** Use probabilistic humility. The book is about leverage, not guarantees.
- **Borrowed voice:** Use the books as hidden scaffolding; let the final prose sound like Sven.
- **Religious over-narrowing:** Keep wonder, gratitude, and meaning accessible to secular and religious readers alike.
- **Disconnected vignettes:** Every story should answer: What was the condition? What opened? Who responded? What compounded?

## Canonical Thesis Lines

> **Micro luck gives us stories. Macro luck gives us possibilities.**

> **The smallest luck feels personal. The largest luck feels invisible.**

> **Luck explains more than the proud admit and less than the bitter insist.**

> **Chance favors motion because motion creates collisions.**

> **Opportunity has a memory. It returns to people who treated earlier, smaller chances with respect.**

> **The highest use of luck is to become a source of it for others.**

# Evidence ledger — The Balloon That Became a Spaceship

Checked 2026-09-16. Public, lawful sources only.

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

### Air Force historical report
- Department of the Air Force history portal, *The Roswell Report: Fact versus Fiction in the New Mexico Desert*: https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/The-DAF-Legacy-Vault/DAF-History-Publications/igphoto/2003789023/
- Full report: https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/Portals/16/documents/AFD-101201-038.pdf
- Use: establishes what the 1994 Air Force investigation concluded and provides its assembled MOGUL source packet. It is a government reconstruction, not self-authenticating proof of every inference.
- Key claim to audit: MOGUL's objective included long-range detection of Soviet nuclear detonations and ballistic-missile launches; Air Force attributes Roswell debris to the balloon project.

### GAO independent records search
- U.S. GAO, NSIAD-95-187, 28 July 1995: https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad-95-187
- PDF: https://www.gao.gov/assets/nsiad-95-187.pdf
- Use: agency-by-agency search scope, records-disposition problem, contemporary-document inventory, CIA response.
- Important negative result: CIA told GAO its database search for `Project Mogul` and `Roswell, New Mexico` yielded no documents. This is evidence about that 1995 search, not proof that CIA possessed no relevant knowledge in any form.

### National Archives
- NARA Project BLUE BOOK / Roswell research guide: https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos
- NARA current UAP textual/microfilm holdings guide: https://www.archives.gov/research/topics/uaps/textual-and-microfilm
- Use: archival topology, source series, limits of BLUE BOOK as a Roswell source, later Roswell Report source files.

### Majestic-12 authenticity problem
- GAO, 28 July 1995: https://www.gao.gov/products/154832
- GAO reports that ISOO, Air Force and NARA found no evidence the submitted MJ-12 material originated in the executive branch; Air Force determined a 1980 `MJ Twelve` message was a forgery.
- Treat this as the documented result of those institutional examinations. Do not generalize from it to every document or every retrieval allegation.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete reporting / testimony

To build, not launder. Candidate witness ledger must include first known statement date, interviewer, prior exposure to Roswell literature, claimed role, direct versus hearsay content, contradictions, and independent corroboration.

Priority names/events: Mac Brazel contemporary press account; Jesse Marcel's later accounts; Walter Haut; Sheridan Cavitt; Lydia Sleppy; Glenn Dennis; claimed nurse material; mortuary/body narratives. No witness enters manuscript as proof merely because of military rank or vividness.

## Class 3 — disputed allegations

- Non-human craft recovered near Roswell.
- Alien or non-human bodies recovered.
- Majestic-12 as an authentic executive-branch retrieval committee/document set.
- Claims of specific autopsies, hangars, biological storage or reverse engineering without independently authenticated provenance.

Each requires its own chain. `Roswell was secret` cannot be used as a shared evidentiary coupon.

## Class 4 — synthesis / conjecture

The book's working synthesis is that a real classified nuclear-detection program may have made Roswell unusually fertile for later extraordinary claims. The public could correctly infer that the 1947 `weather balloon` explanation omitted material context, then incorrectly infer that any larger hidden story inherits support from that omission.

This is interpretive. It must be tested against the actual chronology of Roswell's revival, witness emergence, document releases, popular books, television, and known government disclosures.

## Contradiction ledger

1. **Flight identification:** Air Force's MOGUL reconstruction must be tested against surviving NYU/MOGUL launch records, especially the Flight 4 controversy. Do not write `Flight 4 was the Roswell debris` as settled until chronology and nomenclature are page-indexed.
2. **Records destruction:** GAO found some Roswell Army Air Field administrative records had been destroyed under records schedules. Need exact series, authorization, date, and whether destruction was anomalous. Missing records are not affirmative evidence of contents.
3. **Bodies:** The later Air Force `Case Closed` anthropomorphic-dummy/time-compression explanation is a separate argument from the 1994 MOGUL debris case and must be evaluated separately. Do not let one explanation inherit the other's strength.
4. **Contemporary vs retrospective testimony:** Build a date-of-first-statement graph. Later detail is not automatically false, but it has different evidentiary weight.
5. **Government incentives:** Air Force had reputational incentive to close a politically persistent controversy; UFO advocates, authors, media and witnesses can have status, commercial, identity, or narrative incentives. Incentive is not disproof. Record it symmetrically.

## Defeat condition

If a source-level reconstruction shows that the proposed MOGUL material could not plausibly account for the 1947 debris, say so. If authenticated chain-of-custody physical evidence establishes manufactured material or biology outside plausible terrestrial origin, the book must move that evidence classes rather than protect its thesis. Conversely, if MOGUL survives the detailed chronology and material audit, later extraordinary claims still require independent evidence; they do not become true or false by association.

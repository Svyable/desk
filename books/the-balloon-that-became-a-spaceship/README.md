# The Balloon That Became a Spaceship

## Subtitle
Project MOGUL, Roswell, and How a Real Secret Grows a Better Secret

## Thesis
Roswell is most useful not as a referendum on extraterrestrial visitation but as a controlled study in evidentiary ecology: a genuinely secret 1947 attempt to detect Soviet nuclear tests collided with mundane-looking debris, confused public messaging, incomplete records, later witness memory, and decades of cultural accretion. The important question is not whether secrecy exists. It plainly does. The question is what kinds of later claims secrecy can actually carry.

## Evidence classes
1. **Verified / declassified / documented:** Project MOGUL existed; its balloon-borne work sought long-range detection of Soviet nuclear detonations and missile launches; the Army Air Forces recovered debris near Roswell in July 1947; the Air Force later identified MOGUL as the source; GAO conducted an independent records search in 1994–95 and documented missing/destroyed administrative records as well as records it did locate.
2. **Credible but incomplete reporting or testimony:** later recollections by participants and witnesses whose statements can be attributed but whose memory, timing, and independence require case-by-case audit.
3. **Disputed allegations:** recovered alien bodies, non-human craft, Majestic-12, and related retrieval narratives absent independently authenticated physical or documentary evidence. GAO found no evidence that the MJ-12 material originated in the executive branch.
4. **Speculative synthesis / open conjecture:** a real classified program may have supplied an unusually durable seed crystal for a much larger mythology because later audiences correctly inferred that the first public explanation was incomplete, then overgeneralized from `something was hidden` to `the largest claim may therefore be true`.

## Defeat condition
This book fails if the MOGUL attribution cannot survive a source-level reconstruction of launch chronology, debris characteristics, geography, weather, witness timing, and records provenance. It also fails in the opposite direction if independently authenticated material establishes a recovered craft or biological remains that MOGUL cannot explain. Neither institutional authority nor folklore gets a free pass.

# Evidence ledger — Between Hyperspace and Void

## Class 1 — verified / documented

### Endogenous DMT
- Dean et al., *Scientific Reports* 9, 9333 (2019), “Biosynthesis and Extracellular Concentrations of N,N-dimethyltryptamine (DMT) in Mammalian Brain.” Rat cortical microdialysis detected baseline extracellular DMT; INMT transcripts were reported in rat and human cortex, pineal gland and choroid plexus. Pinealectomy did not eliminate rat cortical DMT. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-019-45812-w
- Barker, McIlhenny & Strassman, *Drug Testing and Analysis* (2012), critical review of 69 reports concerning endogenous DMT, bufotenine and 5-MeO-DMT in human fluids. Important methodological audit rather than blanket confirmation. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22371425/

### Exogenous pharmacology
- 5-MeO-DMT is a serotonergic agonist with particularly high 5-HT1A affinity and activity at 5-HT2A; receptor profile is not interchangeable with DMT. Review: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9314805/
- Structural work on 5-methoxytryptamines/5-HT1A provides a modern molecular basis for distinguishing receptor engagement rather than reasoning only from subjective reports. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11152992/
- DMT engages multiple serotonergic receptors; 5-HT2A is central to classic psychedelic effects but not the entire pharmacology. https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5048497/

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- DMT may act as a noncanonical endogenous signaling molecule. Rat concentration and biosynthesis data make this experimentally plausible, but human brain release, relevant compartments, state dependence and physiological function remain unresolved.
- Historical literature has reported 5-MeO-DMT or related markers in human biological material, and biochemical routes are plausible. Modern reviews emphasize contradictory findings and limited participant fractions. Human endogenous brain production/function needs direct evidence.
- Exogenous DMT changes neural complexity and connectivity, but translating pharmacological-dose network effects into endogenous physiological roles requires concentration-appropriate experiments.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Endogenous 5-MeO-DMT is a functional human neurotransmitter or neuromodulator.
- The pineal gland is the principal source of human DMT.
- Endogenous DMT surges cause ordinary dreams, mystical experiences, birth experiences or near-death experiences.
- DMT and 5-MeO-DMT form an endogenous reciprocal pair that regulates normal consciousness.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

A low-level endogenous tryptamine system could contribute to metastable consciousness by shifting network integration, differentiation, precision or self-model boundaries. The strongest experimentally useful version predicts measurable state-dependent changes in brain-accessible DMT and 5-MeO-DMT (if the latter is genuinely endogenous), linked to distinct receptor/network signatures and surviving adjustment for canonical neuromodulators.

## Contradictions and traps

- **Presence ≠ function.** Analytical detection does not establish neurotransmission.
- **Body fluid ≠ brain.** Urine, blood and CSF cannot be silently converted into neuronal synthesis.
- **mRNA ≠ active pathway.** Enzyme transcripts do not alone establish production rate or physiological relevance.
- **Affinity ≠ occupancy.** A ligand can bind a receptor in vitro while endogenous concentrations never reach meaningful occupancy in vivo.
- **High dose ≠ low dose.** Psychedelic pharmacology can recruit receptor populations and network states absent during physiological exposure.
- **Unity ≠ disconnection.** 5-MeO-DMT phenomenology may reflect reduced differentiation, altered hierarchy, or boundary dissolution rather than globally reduced connectivity.
- **Complexity ≠ useful association.** Increased neural signal diversity does not mean cognition has simply gained connections.
- **Popular pineal/NDE stories have asymmetric evidentiary reach.** They are hypotheses and cultural narratives unless direct concentration measurements support them.

## Strongest alternative explanation

Canonical serotonin plus established neuromodulatory and inhibitory/excitatory systems already regulate integration, segregation, salience, gain and self-model stability. Trace endogenous tryptamines may be metabolic side-products or peripheral signals with no central control role. The book must beat this explanation experimentally, not rhetorically.

## Publication hold

Do not write “5-MeO-DMT is produced endogenously in the human brain” as fact. Current modern review evidence does not justify it. Phrase as reported/potential/plausible only with exact provenance until direct replicated human evidence is found.

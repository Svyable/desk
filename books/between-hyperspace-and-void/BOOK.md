# Between Hyperspace and Void

## How Two Endogenous Tryptamines Might Help Build Ordinary Consciousness

Sven Hardy Benson

## Governing thesis

N,N-DMT and 5-MeO-DMT produce strikingly different high-dose phenomenologies and engage overlapping but meaningfully different serotonergic pharmacology. N,N-DMT is demonstrably endogenous in mammals and can be synthesized and released in rat brain; human endogenous 5-MeO-DMT remains substantially less secure. This book asks a deliberately unproven question: could low-level endogenous tryptamine signaling participate in the ordinary regulation of cognitive integration and segregation, with different molecules biasing neural systems toward different regimes?

The attractive version of the idea is a chemical seesaw: one molecule loosens boundaries and proliferates association; another quiets differentiation toward unity or absence; normal consciousness sits between them. The scientifically serious version is harder. It asks whether either compound reaches relevant human brain receptors at physiological concentrations, whether concentrations vary with state, whether the molecules have distinct endogenous functions rather than being metabolic by-products, and whether network-level integration/segregation maps onto their receptor pharmacology at all.

The book will not turn the metaphor into a mechanism before the measurements exist.

## Evidence classes

1. **Verified/documented:** endogenous DMT has been reported in humans; mammalian biosynthetic machinery exists; a 2019 rat study measured extracellular cortical DMT and found brain INMT/AADC expression. Exogenous DMT and 5-MeO-DMT have experimentally characterized receptor pharmacology and altered-state phenomenology.
2. **Credible but incomplete:** endogenous DMT may have physiological signaling roles; some historical studies report endogenous 5-MeO-DMT or relevant precursors, but detection is inconsistent and human brain production/function is not established.
3. **Disputed/unconfirmed:** endogenous 5-MeO-DMT as a functional human neuromodulator; large endogenous DMT releases causing dreams, near-death experiences, or mystical states; a reciprocal DMT/5-MeO-DMT homeostatic system.
4. **Speculative synthesis:** ordinary consciousness may partly emerge from opposing or complementary endogenous tryptamine biases on neural integration, segregation, gain, precision, self-model stability, and sensory/cognitive coupling.

## Defeat condition

The central hypothesis should be abandoned or radically narrowed if sensitive human measurements show that brain-accessible endogenous DMT and/or 5-MeO-DMT remain far below concentrations capable of meaningful receptor engagement across physiological states, or if state-dependent variation fails to track any relevant network phenotype. The book must also survive the simpler explanation that serotonin itself, conventional neuromodulators, receptor distributions, and circuit dynamics explain the proposed balance without requiring endogenous psychedelic tryptamines.

# Back Matter

*Paradise Found* is a future history written in September 2026.

All claims about observed conditions through that date are intended to be grounded in the research trail in `../research/`. Events described after September 2026 — including specific policies, institutional changes, deployments, and the symbolic 2030 post-scarcity threshold — are scenario, not claims about events that have already occurred.

The scenario is meant to be falsifiable. The research brief records the principal conditions that would make the thesis fail: persistent high costs in energy or compute, slow robotics progress, housing systems that preserve supply constraints, health systems unable to convert automation into access, concentrated ownership that blocks broad participation, weak state capacity, war, climate damage, strategic-material shortages, or political rejection of a universal material floor.

The book's definition of post-scarcity is intentionally narrow. It does not mean that all goods become free, that markets disappear, or that positional, ecological, geographic, political, temporal, and human scarcities cease to exist. It means that involuntary deprivation of a decent material baseline becomes technically and institutionally avoidable.

Research and sourcing notes are maintained separately so the prose can remain readable while concrete claims remain checkable.

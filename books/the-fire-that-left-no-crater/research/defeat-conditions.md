# Defeat Conditions

The book’s synthesis should lose if any of the following occurs:

1. Independent reanalysis shows the reported former-reidite zircon microstructures can be generated by non-shock processes under LDG-relevant conditions.
2. The shocked-bedrock identifications fail blinded crystallographic replication or are shown to predate and be unrelated to LDG formation.
3. A physically realistic airburst model reproduces the measured high-pressure mineral transformations, melt volume, distribution, and target mixing without a surface impact.
4. Integrated geochemistry demonstrates that supposed meteoritic components are fully explained by terrestrial target heterogeneity or contamination.
5. A secure parent crater is discovered whose geometry contradicts the book’s preservation/search synthesis in a material way.

A positive crater discovery would resolve the localization mystery but not invalidate the broader preservation argument; it would instead provide the needed ground truth to test it.

# Research Priorities

1. Build a georeferenced database of every well-provenanced LDG sample with chemistry, mineral inclusions, zircon shock state, and collection coordinates.
2. Map shocked bedrock on blinded transects beyond the current glass field.
3. Reconstruct Oligocene target stratigraphy and compare zircon age spectra and isotopic signatures with LDG inclusions.
4. Run coupled hydrocode + ejecta + atmospheric transport models for plausible oblique impacts and terminal disruption cases.
5. Model 28.5 million years of erosion/burial to predict which crater signatures should survive.
6. Integrate gravity, magnetic, radar, seismic, and remote-sensing datasets into a ranked source-region map.
7. Quantify original melt mass and thickness rather than relying on qualitative descriptions of abundance.
8. Expand archaeological provenance work around Western Desert routes while keeping cultural interpretation separate from impact physics.
9. Use Australasian and other orphan tektite fields as formal comparison cases for crater-search completeness.

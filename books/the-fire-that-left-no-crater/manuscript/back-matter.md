# Back Matter

## A note on certainty

This manuscript treats an impact-related origin for Libyan Desert Glass as strongly supported by the present mineralogical and geochemical record. It does not claim that the parent crater has been located, that the projectile type is known, or that every LDG specimen shares an identical pressure-temperature history.

The ancient Egyptian use of LDG is treated as an archaeological fact where the material identification is secure. Proposed collection routes and symbolic meanings remain historical inference.

## The larger question

The useful scientific inheritance of the LDG problem is methodological: extreme events should be reconstructed from independent residue classes, and missing expected structures should be converted into explicit preservation and search models rather than rhetorical proof for or against a hypothesis.

# ACTIONABLE ANOMALY — evidence ledger

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- CIA publicly acknowledges U.S. government remote-viewing research and the later STARGATE review/termination history. Source: CIA, `Ask Molly: Did CIA Really Study Psychic Powers?` https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-did-cia-really-study-psychic-powers/
- CIA FOIA holds the STARGATE collection, including session records and methodological papers. Example: March 5, 1980 Tehran hostage remote-viewing session, CIA-RDP96-00788R000800760001-1: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r000800760001-1
- The 1995 operational summary states that from 1986 through Q1 FY1995 the program received more than 200 tasks from operational military organizations; by May 1, 1995 three viewers had responded to 40 tasks from five organizations under the numerical evaluation system, yielding 99 accuracy and 100 value scores. CIA PDF: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00791R000200300002-2.pdf
- The 1995 AIR evaluation contained materially different scientific interpretations by Jessica Utts and Ray Hyman. CIA's own retrospective summarizes the conclusion as enough accurate experiences to defy randomness but too unreliable/inconsistent/sporadic for intelligence utility; CIA did not restore the program.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Utts's claim that controlled laboratory results provide strong statistical evidence for anomalous cognition. This must be reconstructed experiment-by-experiment rather than quoted as institutional CIA endorsement.
- End-user ratings from operational tasks. Need full denominator, scoring rubric, missingness, evaluator independence, target category, cueing, feedback and analyst transformation before interpreting numerical ratings.
- Participant accounts of standout operational successes. Treat as leads requiring contemporaneous task files and pre-feedback output, not as proof.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- That remote viewing reliably located hostages, submarines, weapons, people or facilities in a way that materially changed an intelligence operation.
- That the STARGATE record establishes clairvoyance, precognition, telepathy or any particular physical mechanism.
- That continuation over decades proves efficacy.
- That termination proves the absence of every laboratory anomaly.
- Any claim that Soviet/Russian programs independently demonstrated an operational psychic capability until primary records and denominators are recovered.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

### Actionability gap
A weak anomalous signal could, in principle, be statistically detectable in aggregate while being too low-specificity, unstable or poorly calibrated to support one-off decisions. This does **not** assume the remote-viewing anomaly is genuine; it asks what would follow if the strongest statistical claim survived.

### Interpreter-instrument coupling
When free-response output requires a human to map impressions onto targets, the judge/analyst is part of the measurement chain. The hypothesis is testable by fixing the mapping rule prospectively and comparing performance with retrospective human judging.

### Operational compression
Real customers force a rich ambiguous output into a finite action space. Scientific anomaly and operational utility should therefore be evaluated separately.

## Contradictions to preserve

- CIA's popular retrospective says enough accurate experiences existed to defy randomness, while AIR/Hyman's scientific caution says anomalous statistics do not establish paranormal functioning and independent replication remained a major issue. Do not flatten this into either `CIA proved ESP` or `CIA proved ESP false`.
- Laboratory evidence and operational evidence are different datasets with different endpoints.
- A later ganzfeld literature remains contested. Milton & Wiseman (1999) reported failure to replicate the earlier main effect across 30 studies: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10414223/ . Storm & Ertel (2001) disputed that conclusion using broader aggregates: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11393304/ . The book must represent the disagreement rather than choose a meta-analysis by preference.

## Publication holds

Do not publish as fact until primary support is recovered:

- exact total dollar cost of the full program;
- sensational named operational `hits` without contemporaneous blind records;
- claims about Soviet success based only on U.S. threat perceptions;
- any percentage such as `15% accurate` unless the underlying metric and denominator are reconstructed;
- any statement that CIA itself scientifically certified psi;
- any claim that a statistical anomaly necessarily implies nonlocal information transfer.

## Quantitative extraction burden

For each recoverable operational task: date; sponsor/customer; target class; viewer count; cueing; pre-session information; raw output length; analyst transformation; scoring method; scorer blindness; feedback timing; accuracy score; value score; confidence; whether conventional intelligence was available; whether RV changed an action; outcome; missing records.

For laboratory experiments: N; randomization; target pool; sensory isolation; judge independence; scoring metric; preregistration status; effect size; uncertainty; multiple comparisons; experimenter effects; replication lab; publication status; data availability.

## Defeat test

Pre-register a multi-site adversarial collaboration. Proponents choose the viewing protocol; skeptics control target randomization/audit; neither viewer nor interacting experimenter knows the target. Free-response output must be converted prospectively to a forced target choice and confidence before revelation. Primary endpoint is out-of-sample decision accuracy and calibration, not retrospective resemblance. Publish all trials and code. A stable, independently replicated, economically meaningful effect would force revision of the book's operational skepticism. Repeated null performance under adequate power would sharply constrain the strong psi interpretation while not logically proving every historical anomaly artifactual.

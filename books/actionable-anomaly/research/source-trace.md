# Source trace

Primary/authoritative sources first.

1. CIA — Ask Molly: Did CIA Really Study Psychic Powers? https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-did-cia-really-study-psychic-powers/
   - Useful for CIA's own retrospective description of program transfer, independent AIR review, non-restoration, and declassification.
   - Treat as agency retrospective, not neutral scientific adjudication.

2. CIA FOIA — Summary Report: STAR GATE Operational Tasking and Evaluation, CIA-RDP96-00791R000200300002-2. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00791R000200300002-2.pdf
   - Key denominator lead: >200 operational tasks, 1986–Q1 FY1995; numerical ratings for 40 tasks by May 1995; 99 accuracy / 100 value scores.
   - Must extract full scoring rubric and distributions before interpreting.

3. CIA FOIA — Remote Viewing Session CCC12, 5 March 1980, CIA-RDP96-00788R000800760001-1. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00788r000800760001-1
   - Tehran hostage task; useful primary example of `raw intelligence data`, tasking and transcript structure.

4. CIA FOIA — Decision Augmentation Theory: Toward a Model of Anomalous Mental Phenomena, May, Utts & Spottiswoode, 16 May 1995. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp96-00791r000200280002-5
   - Evidence of mechanism/model development inside the program ecosystem. Filing/declassification does not establish theory efficacy.

5. CIA FOIA / AIR review materials — Hyman review correspondence, including explicit independent-replication objection. Example PDF: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/docs/CIA-RDP96-00789R003200290001-5.pdf

6. Milton, J. & Wiseman, R. (1999), `Does Psi Exist? Lack of Replication of an Anomalous Process of Information Transfer`, Psychological Bulletin 125(4):387–391. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10414223/
   - Meta-analysis of 30 ganzfeld studies from seven independent labs; reported no confirmation of main above-chance effect.

7. Storm, L. & Ertel, S. (2001), `Does psi exist? Comments on Milton and Wiseman's (1999) meta-analysis of ganzfeld research`, Psychological Bulletin 127(3):424–433. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11393304/
   - Published counteranalysis arguing broader aggregates support a positive effect. Use as evidence of live methodological dispute, not automatic resolution.

8. Schmidt, S. et al. (2004), `Distant intentionality and the feeling of being stared at: two meta-analyses`, British Journal of Psychology 95:235–247. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15142304/
   - Small aggregate effects alongside explicit shortage of independent replications/theoretical concepts; useful later comparative psi literature.

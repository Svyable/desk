## Strong conventional counterargument

These programs may reveal less about exotic biological intelligence than about engineering economics. A dolphin already exists, is trainable, and can be fielded without solving biosonar from first principles. That can make animal systems temporary procurement shortcuts rather than a deep alternative lineage of autonomous technology. The manuscript must earn the stronger claim by showing which biological capabilities remained difficult to reproduce even after substantial engineering effort.

## Publication gate

No full-book publication until the Acoustic Kitty folklore is reconstructed from released records; Navy performance claims are paired with technical literature; Russian/Soviet material has primary or high-quality archival sourcing; ethical/welfare claims have veterinary and policy sources; and every remaining-classified gap is explicitly marked rather than narrated.

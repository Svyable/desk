# BORROWED SENSES — book state

## Selected thesis

Documented intelligence attempts to recruit animal capabilities provide a neglected control case for extraordinary biological-sensing claims: the existence of a receptor, neural response, implant or ingenious payload does not establish an operational capability. The interesting evidence lives in the joints between sensing and action.

# Evidence ledger — Borrowed Senses

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- U.S. Navy Marine Mammal Program states that work began in 1959 and today uses bottlenose dolphins and California sea lions for underwater detection, marking and recovery missions. https://www.niwcpacific.navy.mil/About/Departments/Intelligence-Surveillance-and-Reconnaissance/Marine-Mammal-Program/
- Navy account of RIMPAC 2018 documents a Mark 7 dolphin detecting a simulated mine, verifying it and placing a marker; the report says the initial detection occurred within 30 seconds. https://www.navy.mil/Press-Office/News-Stories/display-news/Article/2249862/us-navy-marine-mammal-program-performs-mine-clearance-operations-during-rimpac/
- Office of Naval Research account documents REMUS + dolphin + diver mine-countermeasure teaming at Umm Qasr in 2003. https://www.onr.navy.mil/media-center/news-releases/men-mammals-and-machines
- CIA publicly documents development of pigeon-carried miniature reconnaissance cameras and says portions of the missions remain classified. https://www.cia.gov/legacy/museum/artifact/pigeon-camera/
- CIA's public history describes Cold War animal espionage work including pigeons. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/natural-spies-animals-in-espionage/

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- CIA acknowledgement that some pigeon mission details remain classified establishes an incomplete public record, not any particular unreleased mission outcome.
- Navy says decades of classification around marine-mammal missions prevented it from rebutting some public allegations at the time. That is relevant to rumor ecology but is an institutional retrospective and should be paired with contemporaneous records where possible.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Offensive dolphin weapon stories must not be repeated as fact without mission-level documentation.
- Claims that animals were operationally used for assassination, sabotage or exotic delivery missions require primary documentation. Program existence is not proof of every alleged mission.
- Acoustic Kitty needs declassified-file reconstruction before narrative treatment. Popular retellings conflict on operational success and even on the famous taxi-death story.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- Military animal systems can productively be understood as ancestors of modern heterogeneous autonomous systems: biological sensing + trained policy + human command + attached instrument.
- The history may illuminate embodied AI because operational intelligence can be distributed across components rather than located in a single agent.
- Animal sensory worlds are useful for astrobiology only as demonstrations that evolution can exploit physical variables humans do not consciously sense; they are not evidence about alien biology.

## Contradiction / incentive audit

Military organizations have incentives both to conceal real capabilities and to make programs appear effective. Activist groups can have incentives to emphasize harmful or offensive uses. Journalists favor bizarre anecdotes. Declassification can settle existence while leaving performance ambiguous. Treat each layer separately.

## Defeat condition for central thesis

The 'borrowed senses' framing weakens if historical reconstruction shows animals served mostly as transport platforms while target detection/classification was actually performed by attached instruments or handlers. For every case, reconstruct the information path before assigning capability to biology.

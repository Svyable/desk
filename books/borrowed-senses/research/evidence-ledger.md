# Evidence ledger — BORROWED SENSES

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- CIA Museum: Office of Research and Development built a pigeon-carried camera; 4.7 × 2.4 × 2.2 cm; battery motor advanced film/cocked shutter; immediate or delayed exposures; CIA says the approach was ultimately ineffective because birds had trouble flying over exact required locations. Details of missions remain classified. https://www.cia.gov/legacy/museum/artifact/pigeon-camera/
- Wang et al., *eNeuro* 2019: reported repeatable alpha-band EEG desynchronization to particular Earth-strength magnetic-field rotations in human participants. The finding is a neural response, not demonstrated conscious magnetic perception or navigation. https://www.eneuro.org/content/6/2/ENEURO.0483-18.2019
- The Wang et al. data and analysis scripts are publicly linked by the journal. DOI and funding/disclosure metadata are available in the article record. https://www.eneuro.org/content/6/2/ENEURO.0483-18.2019/tab-article-info

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- CIA states that details of pigeon missions remain classified. This supports only the existence of withheld mission detail, not any inference about success, targets, frequency or extraordinary capability.
- Human magnetoreception remains an active empirical question. The 2019 EEG result is relevant but should not be generalized beyond its measured endpoint without replication/behavioral evidence.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Claims that humans possess a consciously usable geomagnetic compass.
- Claims that animal magnetoreception demonstrates psi, telepathy or remote viewing.
- Claims that classified animal-intelligence experiments established reliable direct neural control of animals absent program-specific evidence.
- Claims that electromagnetic bioeffects establish directed information transfer, mind control or psychotronic capability.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- `Operational ladder`: stimulus → transduction → representation → behavior → mission geometry → recovery → interpretation. Useful analytic model, not a scientific law.
- `Borrowed behavior`: intelligence engineering may gain capability by recruiting evolved behavior, but inherits the organism's autonomy and variance.
- `Redaction vacuum`: withheld details invite narrative filling; this is an epistemic hazard, not evidence that withheld material is mundane or extraordinary.

## Contradiction / incentive audit

CIA museum histories are institutional retrospective sources: strong for artifact existence and the Agency's stated program account, but not independent adjudication of all mission performance. Seek declassified ORD records and independent archival histories before making granular operational claims.

The Wang et al. paper reports a positive result and mechanism exclusions under its protocol. Later chapters must actively seek replication attempts, criticism, null results, sample-size/statistical objections and competing mechanism work rather than treating one paper as settled human magnetoreception.

## Publication holds

Do not state Acoustic Kitty details until anchored in a declassified primary record. Do not infer operational pigeon targets from classified remainder. Do not state that ferromagnetic transduction in humans is established; the paper calls it viable. Do not use animal sensory biology as evidence for psi or generalized EM sensitivity.

## Defeat condition

The book's stronger thesis fails if its operational ladder merely restates trivial systems engineering without generating discriminating predictions, or if the comparative cases show that biological sensing claims routinely support reliable operational behavior without the intermediate evidence the framework demands.

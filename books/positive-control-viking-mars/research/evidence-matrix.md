# Evidence matrix — Positive Control

This matrix is deliberately stricter than the manuscript's narrative shorthand. It records what each major claim can actually bear.

| Claim | Class | Best current support | Boundary / contradiction |
|---|---|---|---|
| Viking flew three dedicated biology experiments plus organic-analysis capability. | 1 — documented | Klein et al. 1976; Viking mission records | Instrument roles differed; GCMS was not itself a metabolism assay. |
| LR nutrient addition produced substantial radioactive-gas evolution at both landing sites. | 1 | Levin & Straat 1976; Klein et al. 1976 | Gas evolution identifies a reaction, not its biological cause. |
| 160°C preheating eliminated the LR response. | 1 | Levin & Straat 1976 | Thermal destruction is consistent with life but not unique to life. |
| ~50°C/46°C preheating substantially reduced LR response. | 1 | Levin & Straat 1976, 1977 | Particularly interesting because temperature is modest; still not a unique biosignature because metastable/reactive chemistry can also be temperature-sensitive. |
| LR responses at both sites were strikingly similar. | 1 | Levin & Straat 1976 | Similarity can support either widespread biology or widespread soil chemistry. |
| The under-rock Viking 2 sample produced an LR response similar to exposed surface samples. | 1 | Levin & Straat 1977 | This weakens a simple requirement for immediate UV activation but does not exclude radiation-generated or persistent oxidants. |
| Second nutrient injection did not produce a simple renewed terrestrial-style burst. | 1 | Levin & Straat 1976/1977 | Important counterevidence to a straightforward microbial feeding model. LR team later showed immediate gas decrease could be explained by CO2/water/soil equilibrium. |
| Viking GCMS did not establish an indigenous organic inventory at either site. | 1 | Biemann-era Viking publications; later reviews | Do not rewrite as “Mars has no organics.” Detection depended on thermal volatilization/pyrolysis, instrument sensitivity and chemistry. |
| The mission team in 1976 regarded both chemical and biological interpretations as possible. | 1 | Klein et al. 1976; Levin & Straat 1976 | Later individual positions diverged. |
| Viking proved Mars sterile. | 3 — not established | None | Neither instrument suite nor later missions justify planetary sterility. |
| Viking proved extant Martian life. | 3 | Levin/Straat advocacy is not independent proof | No independent direct replication of metabolism with orthogonal molecular/cellular biosignatures. |
| Phoenix measured perchlorate in analyzed Martian arctic soil. | 1 | Hecht et al. 2009; NASA Phoenix | Concentration/site cannot simply be assigned to Viking sites. |
| Perchlorate itself under Viking incubation conditions explains LR. | 3 as stated | Quinn et al. 2013 explicitly notes parent perchlorate is nonreactive under Viking biology conditions | Radiation products such as hypochlorite/trapped O2 are more relevant mechanisms. |
| Radiation-damaged calcium perchlorate can generate reactive species that reproduce important Viking biology observations in laboratory work. | 1 for laboratory result; 2 for Viking-site application | Quinn et al. 2013 | Presence/concentration/history at Viking sites remains inferred. |
| Heating perchlorate-bearing, organic-containing soil can destroy organics and generate chlorinated molecules. | 1 for laboratory result | Navarro-González et al. 2010 + 2011 correction | Quantitation was corrected; Biemann disputed extrapolation to Viking data. |
| Viking's chloromethane/dichloromethane detections were definitely indigenous products of Martian organics + perchlorate. | 3 | Proposed by Navarro-González et al.; later reanalyses | Biemann's criticism and contamination/instrument alternatives prevent closure. |
| Curiosity detected chlorinated organic products involving Martian chlorine and reduced carbon in Sheepbed mudstone. | 1 | Freissinet et al. 2015 | Carbon source can be Martian or exogenous; detection is at Gale Crater and ancient rock, not Viking surface soil. |
| Curiosity vindicated Levin. | 3 | None | Organic carbon is necessary for known life but not evidence that LR gas was biological. |
| Later Mars chemistry weakens the old inference from “Viking GCMS did not see organics” to “therefore biology is impossible.” | 4 — synthesis built on class-1 findings | Phoenix + Curiosity + pyrolysis chemistry | This is an epistemic/historical synthesis, not a new detection claim. |
| Viking exposed a general problem in operational biosignatures: geochemistry can satisfy controls designed around terrestrial biology. | 4 | Whole evidence chain | Useful only if kept tied to specific controls and mechanisms; should not become a slogan that all biosignatures are arbitrary. |

## Defeat conditions for the book's synthesis

The central synthesis should be narrowed or abandoned if primary records show that the LR heat controls were never intended or interpreted as biologically discriminating; if later chemistry demonstrates a single abiotic mechanism reproducing the full LR time series, thermal behavior, site-to-site similarity, storage behavior and under-rock result under Viking-equivalent conditions with independently justified reagent concentrations; or if a modern reanalysis shows that a key flight trace used in the argument was an instrument artifact rather than sample response.

Conversely, the book will not upgrade LR to a life detection unless an independent line of evidence links the 1976 reaction to a specifically biological process. Plausibility, unexplained residue, and survival of a hypothesis are not that link.

# Back Matter

The phrase “radiation-eating fungus” is retained in this manuscript only as a description of the popular claim. No paper cited here establishes fungal use of ionizing radiation with the mechanistic and energetic closure available for canonical phototrophy or chemotrophy.

The central research program proposed by this book is simple to state and hard to perform: separate protection, signaling, repair, tropism and energy transduction, then close the absorbed-dose-to-chemical-work ledger.

A null result would still matter. Melanin is a serious radioprotective biomaterial candidate, fungal spores are relevant to planetary protection, and radiation-adapted microbes are useful systems for studying repair and survival in space. The strongest practical applications do not depend on the strongest speculative claim being true.

# Contradictions and Incentives

## Central contradictions

**2007 growth stimulation vs. 2022 null growth response.** Different species and conditions prevent a simple replication verdict. The productive question is which variables predict the sign and magnitude of the response.

**Melanin radioprotection vs. Exophiala counterexample.** Some experiments show clear pigment-associated protection; one well-studied black yeast showed acute gamma survival dominated by physiological state and repair. Treat melanin as one component, not a universal explanation.

**Radiotropism reports vs. inconsistent prevalence and melanization dependence.** Directional growth needs blinded modern replication with artifact controls.

**Energy transduction language vs. incomplete energy accounting.** Electronic/redox changes are real measurements; the bridge to net usable metabolic energy remains incompletely closed.

## Incentives and distortion

Chernobyl produces headline pressure. “Fungi survive reactor conditions” becomes “fungi eat radiation” because the latter travels farther.

Spaceflight applications create a second incentive. A biological shield is fundable and visually compelling, which can blur the line between interesting prototype measurements and practical habitat-scale shielding.

Skeptical incentives exist too. Once “radiation-eating fungus” becomes an internet trope, legitimate redox and radiobiology results are easy to dismiss as clickbait by association.

The manuscript therefore treats both promotional inflation and reflexive debunking as sources of epistemic noise.

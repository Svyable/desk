# Source Log

## Primary and authoritative sources

1. Dadachova E. et al. (2007), “Ionizing radiation changes the electronic properties of melanin and enhances the growth of melanized fungi,” *PLoS ONE* 2:e457. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0000457 — Primary positive experiment; supports altered melanin ESR/redox behavior and growth stimulation under specified conditions, not a completely closed metabolic pathway.

2. Zhdanova N.N. et al. (2000), “Fungi from Chernobyl: mycobiota of the inner regions of the containment structures of the damaged nuclear reactor,” *Mycological Research* 104:1421–1426. https://doi.org/10.1017/S0953756200002756 — Primary survey documenting fungal colonization and species diversity inside the Shelter.

3. Bryan R.A. et al. (2008), “The radioprotective properties of fungal melanin are a function of its chemical composition, stable radical presence and spatial arrangement.” PubMed record: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18426412/ — Supports melanin radioprotection in tested fungi and dependence on chemical/physical organization.

4. Schultzhaus Z. et al. (2020), “The response of the melanized yeast Exophiala dermatitidis to gamma radiation exposure.” https://doi.org/10.1111/1462-2920.14936 — Important counterevidence: in this organism, melanin did not dominate acute gamma survival; nutrient state, age, density and DNA repair mattered strongly.

5. Schultzhaus Z. et al. (2020), “Adaptive evolution of a melanized fungus reveals robust augmentation of radiation resistance by abrogating non-homologous end-joining.” PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33078510/ — Experimental evolution; shows repair architecture can dominate resistance.

6. Bacha G.D. et al. (2022), “Evaluating changes in growth and pigmentation of Cladosporium cladosporioides and Paecilomyces variotii in response to gamma and ultraviolet irradiation,” *Scientific Reports*. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-022-16063-z — Key null/complicating result: pigmentation changed but significant growth-rate stimulation was not observed under tested gamma/UV conditions.

7. Vasileiou T., Summerer L. (2020), “A biomimetic approach to shielding from ionizing radiation: The case of melanized fungi,” *PLoS ONE* 15:e0229921. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0229921 — Biomimetic shielding analysis; useful for engineering implications, not proof of radiotrophic metabolism.

8. Cortesão M. et al. (2020), “Aspergillus niger Spores Are Highly Resistant to Space Radiation.” PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32318041/ — High-dose X-ray/heavy-ion/UV resistance of a spacecraft-associated fungus.

9. NASA, “Investigating the Roles of Melanin and DNA Repair on Adaptation and Survivability of Fungi in Deep Space.” https://science.nasa.gov/biological-physical/investigations/melanin-and-dna-repair/ — Official description of spaceflight interest in melanin and fungal survival; not an efficacy claim.

10. NASA, “Exposed! International Space Station Tests Organisms, Materials in Space.” https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/exposed-international-space-station-tests-organisms-materials-in-space/ — Official summary of external-space fungal survival and small measured shielding effect in a thin layer; engineering extrapolation remains model-dependent.

11. NASA TechPort, “Melanin Coated Aerogels for Radiation Mitigation.” https://techport.nasa.gov/projects/146190 — Documents a technology project investigating melanin-containing shielding concepts; project existence does not establish mature performance.

12. Casadevall A. et al. (2017), “Melanin, Radiation, and Energy Transduction in Fungi,” *Microbiology Spectrum*. https://doi.org/10.1128/microbiolspec.funk-0037-2016 — Review by principal investigators; useful synthesis but secondary to primary experiments.

## Claim discipline

- “Radiation eating” is not used as an established scientific mechanism.
- Chernobyl abundance does not establish causation.
- NASA research interest does not establish deployable shielding efficacy.
- Patents, if later added, will be evidence of filing only.
- Radiolysis-supported ecosystems are mechanistically distinct from direct melanin-mediated energy transduction.

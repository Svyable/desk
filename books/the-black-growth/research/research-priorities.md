# Research Priorities

1. Reproduce the 2007 growth experiments with the original strains, dose rates, nutrient conditions and genetically rescued pigmentation controls.
2. Reproduce the 2022 null experiments alongside the positive protocol to identify condition-dependent regime changes rather than treating papers as votes.
3. Measure absorbed radiation energy inside culture geometry and compare it with additional biomass chemical free energy.
4. Couple time-resolved ESR/electrochemistry of melanin to NADH/NAD+, ATP and isotope-resolved carbon flux.
5. Rebuild radiotropism experiments with automated source movement, blinded scoring and matched non-radiation artifacts.
6. Map pigment chemistry and localization across fungal species instead of treating “melanin” as one substance.
7. Separate shielding from radical quenching using purified pigment, dead biomass, live biomass and synthetic melanin composites.
8. Run long-term experimental evolution under chronic radiation plus carbon limitation, with no-radiation and oxidative-stress controls.
9. Quantify habitat-scale mass efficiency of living, dead and biomimetic melanized shields under realistic proton/heavy-ion spectra.
10. Establish a shared radiotrophy dataset with raw dosimetry, growth curves, culture conditions, strain provenance and null results.

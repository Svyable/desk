## Subtitle

**Chernobyl Fungi, Melanin, and the Question of Whether Life Can Learn to Use Radiation**

## Thesis

Melanized fungi force a distinction that popular accounts usually erase: surviving radiation, being protected by pigment, changing metabolism under irradiation, growing toward a source, and actually harvesting ionizing radiation as biologically useful energy are five different claims. The surviving literature contains evidence for several of them, contradictions among others, and a tractable experimental path for deciding whether “radiotrophy” deserves to name a metabolism or only a cluster of stress responses.

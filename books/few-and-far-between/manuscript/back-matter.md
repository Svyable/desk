# Back Matter

## A note on status

*Few and Far Between* is a working Desk draft. Its mathematical spine is built from primary papers and standard theorem statements, but this edition remains deliberately below the Desk one-shot publication-length gate. The numbered chapters need source-driven expansion, especially on Brun and Chen, Bombieri–Vinogradov and Elliott–Halberstam, the full Zhang smooth-moduli argument, Maynard’s variational problem, the Polymath8 numerical optimization, Maier’s matrix construction, and modern maximal-gap computation.

## Claims that must not drift

The twin prime conjecture remains open.

The unconditional bounded-gap result discussed here does not prove infinitely many twin primes. Zhang’s original theorem gave a bound of 70,000,000. Maynard’s original published refinement gave 600, and the Polymath8b line obtained 246.

Cramér’s random model is a heuristic model, not a claim that prime occurrence is literally independent.

The Hardy–Littlewood prime k-tuples conjecture is unproved in full generality.

Large-gap lower bounds do not establish the exact asymptotic order of the maximal prime gap.

The 2026 Guth–Maynard short-interval theorem is relevant to the improving analytic control of primes on shorter scales, but it is not itself a solution of the twin-prime or maximal-gap problems.

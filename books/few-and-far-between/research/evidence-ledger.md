# Evidence Ledger

## Class 1 — proved / documented

- Prime number theorem and average spacing scale `log x`.
- GPY: `liminf (p_{n+1}-p_n)/log p_n = 0`.
- Zhang: an unconditional finite bound, originally below 70,000,000.
- Maynard: unconditional bounded gaps and bounded intervals containing arbitrarily many primes for each fixed multiplicity; original pair bound 600.
- Polymath8b: unconditional pair bound 246 in the standard `H_1` formulation.
- Maynard 2016 and Ford–Green–Konyagin–Tao 2016: major improvements to large-gap lower bounds beyond the fixed Rankin-scale barrier.
- Guth–Maynard 2026: asymptotics for primes in short intervals of length `x^(17/30+o(1))` as a consequence of new Dirichlet-polynomial large-value estimates.

## Class 2 — rigorous conditional statements

- GPY-type bounded gaps under distribution hypotheses stronger than Bombieri–Vinogradov.
- Stronger small-gap constants under Elliott–Halberstam or generalized Elliott–Halberstam variants.
- Any claim in revisions derived under RH, EH, GEH or another named conjecture must retain the condition in the sentence that uses it.

## Class 3 — computational evidence / serious heuristics

- Hardy–Littlewood prime-pair and k-tuples asymptotics.
- Cramér random-model predictions for extreme gaps.
- Refined Cramér/Granville-style extreme-gap heuristics.
- Finite searches for twin primes, admissible tuples and record maximal gaps.
- Numerical agreement with singular-series predictions.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- The eventual twin-prime proof may require a method that breaks or bypasses the parity barrier in a qualitatively new way.
- Machine-assisted theorem search may become especially useful in parameter optimization and proof navigation.
- The conceptual distance from 246 to 2 cannot be inferred from the numerical distance.

## Defeat conditions

The historical thesis of the book would weaken if the cited published papers did not support the theorem statements attributed to them. Every quantitative bound must be checked against the exact definition of `H_m`, consecutiveness convention, conditional assumptions and publication version before final copy.

The interpretive thesis would weaken if the final manuscript blurs the distinction between a finite record and an infinite theorem, or describes probabilistic heuristics as literal stochastic mechanisms for primality.

# Chronology

- 1923 — Hardy and Littlewood publish the prime-tuples heuristic framework and singular-series predictions.
- 1931 — Westzynthius proves prime gaps can be arbitrarily large multiples of average spacing.
- 1936 — Cramér publishes his probabilistic model and extreme-gap work.
- 1930s–1940s — Erdős and Rankin substantially advance large-gap constructions.
- 1970s — Chen proves infinitely many primes `p` for which `p+2` is prime or semiprime.
- 1985 — Helmut Maier demonstrates unexpectedly large fluctuations of primes in short intervals using the matrix method.
- 2005/2009 — Goldston, Pintz and Yıldırım develop the GPY method; the Annals paper proves normalized liminf prime gaps are zero.
- April 2013 — Yitang Zhang submits the bounded-gaps breakthrough with an initial constant 70,000,000.
- 2013–2014 — Polymath8 rapidly improves Zhang-method constants.
- 2013–2015 — James Maynard and independently Terence Tao introduce the multidimensional sieve viewpoint; Maynard’s Annals paper gives 600 and bounded clusters.
- 2014 — Polymath8b obtains the unconditional `H_1 <= 246` bound and broader conditional/unconditional results.
- 2014–2016 — Maynard and Ford–Green–Konyagin–Tao independently break the long-standing fixed-constant barrier in large prime gaps; papers appear in Annals in 2016.
- 2024–2026 — Guth and Maynard develop new large-value estimates for Dirichlet polynomials; the published 2026 Annals result gives improved asymptotics for primes in short intervals down to `x^(17/30+o(1))`.

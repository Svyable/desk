# Enemy Maps

## CORONA, Secret Satellites, and the Lost Landscapes They Accidentally Saved

Sven Hardy Benson


A camera built to reduce uncertainty about the Cold War became, decades later, a record of landscapes before they were leveled, flooded, urbanized, plowed, quarried, or otherwise changed.

This book is about that second life.

It does not argue that declassified satellite photographs can replace excavation or field survey. It argues something narrower and stranger: an intelligence archive can become scientifically valuable for reasons unrelated to its original mission, and the biases of the original mission remain inside the later science.

CORONA saw the ground because somebody once cared about something else.

# Back Matter

## Evidence classes used in this book

**Verified / documented** means the claim is supported by declassified program history, official archive documentation, or peer-reviewed archaeological work that directly addresses the observation.

**Credible but incomplete / model-dependent** means the claim is supported but remains sensitive to image interpretation, reconstruction, georeferencing, chronology, or incomplete archival coverage.

**Disputed / not established** means the claim has been made publicly but lacks sufficient independent support for the manuscript to adopt it as fact.

**Speculative synthesis / open conjecture** means the inference belongs to this book’s interpretation and should be judged as an argument rather than confused with a source finding.

## Note on terminology

CORONA, ARGON, LANYARD, GAMBIT, and HEXAGON refer to historical U.S. reconnaissance programs or systems whose relevant imagery and program histories have been declassified in whole or in part. This manuscript works only from publicly available sources and does not imply access to non-public material.

## Research principle

A satellite image is treated here as an observation, not a verdict. Archaeological interpretation requires context, comparison, and where possible field verification. A visible anomaly is not automatically an archaeological feature, and absence from a frame is not proof of absence on the ground.

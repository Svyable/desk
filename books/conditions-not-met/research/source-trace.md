## Acquisition queue

- Obtain full 1992 paper and reconstruct apparatus dimensions, ceramic preparation, sensor type, environmental controls and every stated dependency.
- Recover the publication/withdrawal trail of the stronger 1996/1997 manuscript without relying on folklore.
- Recover Woods et al. 2001 full text, DOI, apparatus figures and explicit authors' scope statement.
- Recover all NASA/UAH reports on large YBCO disk fabrication, including dimensions, layering, failure modes, attainable rotation and magnetic excitation.
- Build a chronological table of every identifiable replication attempt and label each as direct, partial, conceptual or anecdotal.
- Search NASA procurement/technical archives for Podkletnov consulting documentation and separate documented consulting from retrospective claims.
- Trace Ning Li/Douglas Torr superconducting-gravity theory separately. Do not merge their theoretical claims with Podkletnov's observations merely because later popular accounts do.
- Recover contemporary skeptical analyses of convection, buoyancy, vibration, electromagnetic coupling and gravimeter susceptibility.
- Treat patents, if used, only as evidence that claims were filed.

## Quantitative table required before publication

For each experiment: disk chemistry; diameter; thickness; layered/composite structure; fabrication route; temperature; levitation method; material rotation; magnetic-field rotation; RPM; AC frequency; field strength; atmosphere/vacuum; test-mass material; sensor; sensor distance; claimed or measured delta-g; uncertainty; ordinary-force controls; preregistration/blinding status; independent replication status.

## Anti-folklore rules

Do not write “NASA spent X dollars” until a primary budget/procurement record supports X.

Do not write “NASA replicated the experiment” without defining replication fidelity.

Do not write “nobody could reproduce the disk” when the defensible statement may be narrower: particular groups reported difficulty reproducing specified material/operating conditions.

Do not infer classified success from public program disappearance.

Do not allow the phrase “gravity shielding” to smuggle the proposed mechanism into descriptions of an observed balance/gravimeter excursion.

# Evidence ledger — Conditions Not Met

## 1 — Verified / documented

Podkletnov & Nieminen published the 1992 Physica C report “A possibility of gravitational force shielding by bulk YBa2Cu3O7-x superconductor,” DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/0921-4534(92)90055-H. Publication verifies the report, not the proposed effect.

NASA Marshall researchers published superconducting gravity measurements reporting no attributable gravity modification in their tested configuration. Primary record: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19990019627.

NASA records also document continuing theoretical and engineering interest: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/archive/nasa/casi.ntrs.nasa.gov/19990046249.pdf and https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20020068834. Institutional investigation is not confirmation.

Woods, Helme, Cooke and Caldwell published a null high-temperature-superconductor gravity experiment in 2001. Recover publisher full text and exact methods before final manuscript citation.

## 2 — Credible but incomplete

Later technical histories describe material, rotation and apparatus differences between replication attempts and the original claim. Reconstruct every important difference from original methods before using it as a narrative fact.

Accounts that Podkletnov advised later replication work require primary documentation before strong attribution.

## 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

Podkletnov's stronger later shielding claims are preserved in the 1997 preprint at https://arxiv.org/abs/cond-mat/9701074. Treat the reported measurements as claims pending independent confirmation.

The later Podkletnov/Modanese impulse-gravity paper is at https://arxiv.org/abs/physics/0108005. It establishes what its authors claimed, not a verified new interaction.

Do not repeat alleged successful private replications without primary records and named investigators.

## 4 — Speculative synthesis

The case may reveal a general replication problem: a null result under related conditions can be socially remembered as a direct replication even when the experimental parameter space was only partly reproduced.

This does not increase the probability that the original gravity claim was correct. Decades without robust independent positive replication remain strong negative evidence.

## Contradictions to preserve

“NASA tested it and found nothing” can be true while “NASA exactly reproduced the claimed apparatus” is false. The manuscript must never collapse those propositions.

“Replicators missed claimed conditions” does not imply “the effect exists.” It implies only that a particular null result closes the conditions actually tested.

Peer review verifies entry into scientific literature, not extraordinary physical interpretation.

Government interest verifies investigation, not secret operational success.

## Defeat condition

The book's epistemic argument fails if primary methods show that high-quality independent null experiments did in fact reproduce all materially specified operating conditions with adequate sensitivity and controls. If that is established, revise the thesis rather than preserving the mystery.

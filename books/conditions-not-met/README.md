# CONDITIONS NOT MET

## How an impossible gravity experiment became a twenty-year lesson in replication

**Sven Hardy Benson**

A book about Eugene Podkletnov's superconducting gravity claims, NASA Marshall's attempts to test them, the Sheffield null experiment, and a recurring pathology of frontier science: the replication that fails without ever reproducing the claimed conditions.

This is not a book claiming antigravity works. It is a book about a much stranger epistemic object: an extraordinary experiment that remained suspended for decades between an original claim nobody independently reproduced and attempted replications whose authors sometimes conceded that they had not actually built the original apparatus.

## Governing thesis

A failed replication is only decisive to the extent that it reproduces the variables on which the claimed phenomenon depends. Podkletnov's gravity-shielding claim is an unusually clean case because the proposed effect is extraordinary, the mainstream prior against it is severe, several tests were null, and yet the historical record contains repeated engineering mismatches involving disk structure, diameter, levitation, rotation, field geometry and cryogenic conditions. None of that rescues the claim. It does something more useful: it prevents us from pretending the experiment has been cleaner than it was.

## Evidence posture

1. **Verified/documented:** Podkletnov and Nieminen published a 1992 Physica C paper reporting apparent weight changes above a levitated YBCO superconductor. NASA Marshall/UAH subsequently performed and published high-precision superconducting gravity tests. Woods et al. published a null replication attempt in 2001. NASA records document continued interest and fabrication/experimental difficulty.
2. **Credible but incomplete:** later technical histories and researchers involved in replication describe important departures from Podkletnov's stated apparatus. These matter, but apparatus mismatch is not evidence that the claimed effect exists.
3. **Disputed/unconfirmed:** Podkletnov's stronger later shielding and impulse-gravity claims, reports of unpublished replications, and claims of distant composition-independent impulses remain without adequate independent confirmation.
4. **Speculative synthesis:** the case may expose a general blind spot in frontier-science adjudication: extraordinary claims can become socially classified as disproven before the decisive experiment has actually been performed. That proposition itself must be tested case by case.

## Publication target

65,000–80,000 words. Narrative science/history rather than advocacy. The book must leave a skeptical physicist able to say “the evidence still does not support gravity shielding” while also forcing that reader to specify exactly which experiment closed which parameter space.

# Chronology

- **1940s–1950s** — U.S. military/intelligence organizations formalize foreign-technology collection and technical intelligence practices across captured adversary systems.
- **1966** — An Iraqi pilot defects to Israel with a MiG-21, giving Israel access to the aircraft.
- **1968** — The United States evaluates the MiG-21 under HAVE DOUGHNUT at Groom Lake/Area 51.
- **1968** — Soviet ballistic-missile submarine K-129 disappears in the Pacific; U.S. intelligence later locates the wreck.
- **1969** — HAVE DRILL/HAVE FERRY evaluate MiG-17 aircraft; CIA records also document interest in acquiring MiG-21PFM technical manuals.
- **Early 1970s** — CIA and contractors develop the Hughes Glomar Explorer and capture system for Project AZORIAN under a deep-sea-mining cover.
- **1974** — AZORIAN attempts recovery of K-129; part of the lifted submarine section falls back to the seafloor and a portion is recovered.
- **1975** — Press exposure destroys the Glomar cover and contributes to cancellation of a planned follow-on recovery.
- **1977–1988** — CONSTANT PEG/4477th Test and Evaluation Squadron flies Soviet-designed MiGs for realistic U.S. pilot training.
- **2006** — U.S. Air Force publicly declassifies/acknowledges CONSTANT PEG.
- **2013** — National Security Archive publishes a major Area 51 collection including declassified foreign-MiG exploitation material and historical context.
- **2022** — AARO is established to investigate UAP and later tasked with historical review/reporting mechanisms.
- **26 July 2023** — David Grusch testifies publicly before the U.S. House that his investigation led him to allegations of a multi-decade crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering program; Ryan Graves and David Fravor also testify on UAP reporting/encounters.
- **31 October 2023** — Department of Defense announces AARO reporting mechanism for current/former government personnel and contractors with direct knowledge of UAP-related programs.
- **8 March 2024** — AARO Historical Record Report Volume I is publicly released; AARO says it found no verifiable evidence of government/private possession or reverse engineering of extraterrestrial technology and reports that multiple alleged program stories resolved to unrelated, nonexistent, disestablished or proposed activities.
- **2024** — AARO releases KONA BLUE material, stating the project was a proposed prospective special access program that was never approved, funded or established and never received material.
- **13 November 2024** — U.S. House hearing includes public testimony from Luis Elizondo affirming his belief that a multi-decade crash-retrieval/reverse-engineering program exists.
- **2026** — AARO continues publishing UAP records and information papers; the public evidentiary divide remains between attributable extraordinary testimony and the absence, so far, of a publicly authenticated custody-to-material-to-analysis chain establishing non-human technology.

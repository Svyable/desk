# Publication / expansion gate

This pass completes the 18-chapter argument but does **not** claim the requested >=3,000 words per chapter / >=54,000-word manuscript target. No file-size, line-count or Git additions proxy should be reported as a fake word count.

Before release-candidate status, perform the following substantive pass.

## Historical foreign-materiel base

- Obtain and page-index the primary HAVE DOUGHNUT tactical and technical evaluation reports, not merely retrospectives.
- Reconstruct the MiG-21 acquisition/custody chain from Iraq to Israel to U.S. evaluation, including dates, transport and responsible organizations.
- Reconstruct HAVE DRILL/HAVE FERRY at the same level and identify what findings actually changed tactics or acquisition.
- Build a complete CONSTANT PEG aircraft ledger: airframes, acquisition routes where public, sortie counts, maintenance problems, accidents, safety practices and training reach.
- Add Foreign Technology Division / FTD successor organizational history and documented property/custody practices.
- Add at least two non-aircraft exploitation cases: foreign radar/missile systems and captured materiel from Vietnam/Middle East conflicts.
- Add Soviet/Russian, Chinese, Israeli or other national exploitation analogues where primary/authoritative records exist, avoiding a U.S.-only inference.

## AZORIAN and scale

- Reconstruct Project AZORIAN contracting, cover architecture, budgets where public, shipyard work, capture-system testing, participant counts and exposure chronology from primary/declassified records.
- Separate the recovered-section facts from persistent public claims about exactly what intelligence was obtained.
- Quantify physical/logistical scale and compare it with alternative hypothetical retrieval burdens without assuming a UAP object's mass or hazards.

## Crash-retrieval claims

- Build a source-genealogy ledger for public legacy-program claims: direct observation, briefing, documentary access, named intermediary, and known cross-contact.
- Page-index the July 2023 Grusch hearing and 2024 public testimony rather than relying on press summaries.
- Map every publicly named alleged program to lifecycle status: rumor, proposal, approved program, operational program, terminated program, or unresolved.
- Reconstruct KONA BLUE from the underlying released DHS/AARO material, including proposal authorship, approval path and reasons it did not become operational if available.
- Seek public inspector-general and congressional materials that clarify procedural findings without converting complaint credibility into substantive proof.

## Materials / chain of custody

- Create a ledger of major publicly discussed UAP materials claims with sample provenance, custody gaps, labs, methods, results and independent replication status.
- Distinguish unusual composition from extraordinary provenance.
- Add a technical appendix on isotope interpretation, terrestrial fractionation/manufacturing ranges, microscopy/sample-preparation artifacts and contamination.
- Define a minimum forensic protocol for a future alleged recovered sample.

## Industrial-shadow test

- Compare several known classified technology programs by years secret, personnel, infrastructure, contractors, accidents, budget visibility, operational output and declassification residue.
- Convert the book's “workload” and “derivative footprint” ideas into an explicit qualitative/quantitative comparison framework.
- Test the framework against a secret program not used to construct it.
- Add counterexamples where consequential programs left surprisingly little public residue.

## Oversight / legality

- Add primary SAP/waived-SAP and congressional oversight authorities relevant to alleged concealment.
- Distinguish lawful compartmentation, proprietary contractor information, classification, misappropriation and unlawful withholding.
- Avoid implying that absence from one public budget line means a program is illegal.

## Prose / completeness

- Expand **every numbered chapter to >=3,000 words** through deeper cases, documents and technical mechanism rather than repetition.
- Reach >=54,000 numbered-chapter words (and use the repository's stricter automated gate if it has since increased).
- Run the repository's local `scripts/check-book-length.py`, metadata/cover checks and `scripts/check-desk.py` if applicable.
- Read the complete manuscript in order and perform the required structural, anti-slop and paragraph passes.
- Remove repeated formulations around “secrecy cuts both ways,” “the object creates work,” and “testimony is not proof” unless each recurrence advances the argument.
- Verify every quotation and statistic against primary sources; this first pass intentionally uses almost no direct quotation.
- Do not update shared catalog/Reader/release surfaces until objective manuscript and editorial gates are met.

## Frozen defeat condition

If comparative research shows that large, long-lived successful foreign-materiel/classified exploitation programs routinely leave no recoverable secondary footprint after comparable time horizons, the book's central “institutional residue” thesis must be narrowed. If authenticated non-human material with secure provenance becomes public, the book must be revised around that evidence rather than preserving the current agnostic conclusion.

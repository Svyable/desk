## Documented foreign-materiel exploitation

- National Security Archive, **“The Area 51 File: Secret Aircraft and Soviet MiGs”** (2013). Declassified-document collection describing HAVE DOUGHNUT, HAVE DRILL, HAVE FERRY, Soviet radar work and Area 51's broader test role. Includes discussion of the 300-page DIA/Foreign Technology Division HAVE DOUGHNUT tactical evaluation. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence/2013-10-29/area-51-file-secret-aircraft-soviet-migs
- U.S. Air Force, **“Air Force declassifies elite aggressor program”** (2006). Official acknowledgement of CONSTANT PEG and the 4477th Test and Evaluation Squadron, which flew Communist-built fighters from 1977–1988. https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/129077/air-force-declassifies-elite-aggressor-program/
- National Museum of the U.S. Air Force, **“CONSTANT PEG: Secret MiGs in the Desert.”** Official museum history noting clandestine acquisition/testing of MiGs under HAVE DOUGHNUT/HAVE DRILL and later use in Constant Peg. https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/1682967/constant-peg-secret-migs-in-the-desert/
- National Museum of the U.S. Air Force, **MiG-23MS “Flogger-E.”** Identifies museum aircraft as a former 4477th Test Squadron Constant Peg aircraft. https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/1162493/mikoyan-gurevich-mig-23ms-flogger-e/
- CIA Reading Room, **“Acquisition of Soviet Technical Manuals for the MiG-21PFM Aircraft”** (1969; released). Useful evidence that exploitation included documentation acquisition as well as airframes. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/0000772121

## Project AZORIAN / recovery at extreme scale

- CIA, **“Project AZORIAN”** museum history. Official account of the covert attempt to recover Soviet submarine K-129 using the Hughes Glomar Explorer and a purpose-built capture system. https://www.cia.gov/legacy/museum/exhibit/project-azorian/
- CIA, **“The Exposing of Project AZORIAN”** (2020). Official retrospective on cover, contractors, recovery, partial failure and exposure. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/the-exposing-of-project-azorian/
- CIA, **“Honoring the Mission of Project AZORIAN”** (2022). Includes recollection of Global Marine engineer Sherman Wetmore and contractor/Agency collaboration. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/honoring-the-mission-of-project-azorian/
- CIA, **“Cold War Spy Crane Aids Disaster Recovery Today”** (2024). Additional engineering history and later provenance of a crane used in construction of the Glomar Explorer. https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/cold-war-spy-crane-aids-disaster-recovery-today/

## Evidentiary rules applied in manuscript

- A classified or formerly classified program is evidence that secrecy can be maintained, not evidence that another alleged program exists.
- A witness's access, rank or clearance can increase the importance of investigating a claim; it does not transform hearsay into direct observation.
- A program proposal is not an operational program.
- A patent is evidence that an application was filed or granted, not that a claimed device works.
- A code name without authenticated program records is not treated as proof of activity.
- Material analysis without documented provenance and chain of custody cannot establish retrieval origin.
- AARO's inability to verify a claim is not logically identical to proof that the claim is false; conversely, classification is not a license to treat missing evidence as positive evidence.

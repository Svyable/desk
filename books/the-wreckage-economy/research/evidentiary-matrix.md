## Evidence-class key

1. **Verified/declassified/documented facts** — supported by primary records, official histories, authenticated contemporaneous documents or equivalent evidence.
2. **Credible but incomplete reporting/testimony** — attributable and relevant, but not independently sufficient for the underlying extraordinary proposition.
3. **Disputed allegations without independent confirmation** — claims remain unverified publicly, contradicted, provenance-poor, or unresolved.
4. **Speculative synthesis/open conjecture** — the manuscript's inference connecting evidence streams; explicitly defeasible.

## Defeat conditions

The manuscript's current thesis fails if documented foreign-materiel programs turn out not to generate the predicted secondary footprints after controlling for size/duration, or if authenticated UAP legacy-program evidence emerges with a strong chain from recovery through custody, testing and derivative work. It also requires narrowing if credible evidence shows a genuinely non-human artifact could remain in custody for decades with unusually small exploitation burden because it is inert, dangerous, inaccessible or technologically sterile.

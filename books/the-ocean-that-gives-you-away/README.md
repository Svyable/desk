# The Ocean That Gives You Away

*Non-Acoustic Submarine Detection and the End of Perfect Silence*

Status: Drafting

Sven Hardy Benson

## Thesis

A submarine can become acoustically quiet without becoming physically absent. Steel perturbs magnetic fields; motion rearranges stratified water; propulsion leaves heat, bubbles, chemistry, pressure, turbulence and sometimes biological light; a vehicle displaces mass whether or not it makes noise. The underwritten question is not whether any one exotic sensor can abolish submarine stealth. It is whether increasingly cheap, distributed, multi-physics sensing can make the ocean itself into a probabilistic detector.

The book separates operationally established non-acoustic methods from plausible but immature ones, disputed claims of strategic-range detection, and open conjecture about sensor fusion changing deterrence.

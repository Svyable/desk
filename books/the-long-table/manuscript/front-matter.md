# Front Matter

## The Long Table

### Nicholas Burns and the American Practice of Diplomacy

Sven Hardy Benson

This is a work of documentary narrative nonfiction. Dialogue appears only when supported by a reliable record. Scenes are reconstructed only from attributable sources. Where evidence does not establish what a person thought, said, or intended, the narrative does not pretend that it does.

The subject is one diplomat, but the larger subject is a profession: the accumulation of judgment across postings, presidents, wars, negotiations, disappointments, alliances, and rooms in which the United States is represented by one person sitting across from another.

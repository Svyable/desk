# The Long Table

*Nicholas Burns and the American Practice of Diplomacy*

A narrative biography of R. Nicholas Burns and, through his career, the American diplomatic profession from the late Cold War to strategic competition with China.

The book is factual nonfiction written with novelistic scene discipline. It does not invent dialogue, interior thought, composite characters, or undocumented scenes. The governing question is how a republic trains people to carry its interests abroad across administrations without confusing service to the country with service to a particular president.

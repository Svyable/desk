## Thesis

Any proposed gravity-control mechanism reverse-engineered from an alleged UAP must also survive the universe. A new gravitational charge, screening law, magnetic-gravity coupling or inertia modification strong enough to maneuver a craft should generally leave traces in planetary fields, stellar systems, binary timing, galactic rotation, lensing, cosmology or precision clocks. The sky is therefore not background to reverse engineering; it is the largest independent laboratory available.

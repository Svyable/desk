This is an investigative notebook-book about the people who stood at the seam between intelligence work, UFO research, parapsychology, occult belief, classified technology, disinformation, and sincere anomaly investigation. Its question is not whether every strange story was true. It is why the same institutions and personalities repeatedly entered the story, sometimes as investigators, sometimes as witnesses, sometimes as sources, and sometimes as manipulators.

The method combines the diary-like intellectual intimacy of field research with document-driven archival investigation. No allegation inherits truth from secrecy. A government employee can be sincere and wrong; a real classified program can become camouflage for an imaginary one; a forged document can contain real names; a witness can accurately report what somebody told him without establishing the underlying event.

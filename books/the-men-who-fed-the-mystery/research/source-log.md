# Source Log

Primary and serious sources for the manuscript's archival/intelligence-history pass.

## Bennewitz / Kirtland / AFOSI

- **AFOSI District File 8017D93-0/29, Kirtland AFB 1980 complaint and internal forms**, released under FOIA and preserved in multiple archival/transcription collections. Working transcription set: https://ufopedia.fyi/raw/reports/afosi-kirtland-1980-foia-documents/ — Class 1 for the existence/content of the released AFOSI records: August 1980 reports near the Kirtland/Manzano/Coyote Canyon complex, October 1980 Bennewitz contact/interview, November 10 briefing, and AFOSI's November 17 stated decision not to pursue technical investigation. The records document reports and official interactions; they do not authenticate extraterrestrial explanations.
- Historical reproduction of the 1980 AFOSI forms in *Flying Saucer Review* material: https://ignaciodarnaude.es/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/FSR-1984-V-29-N-5.pdf — secondary publication vehicle containing reproduced document text/images; useful cross-check, not a substitute for provenance work on the released government copy.
- William L. Moore, **“UFOs and the U.S. Government”**, 1989 MUFON symposium address / later MUFON UFO Journal publication. Full working transcript: https://ufopedia.fyi/raw/articles/moore-1989-mufon-disinformation-speech/ — Class 2 participant testimony. Moore publicly states that he supplied information through Richard Doty about Bennewitz/APRO, believed government personnel were feeding Bennewitz disinformation, and describes his role in passing a contaminated “Aquarius” document. The speech is evidence of Moore's admission and beliefs, not a declassified AFOSI operational order establishing every detail or authorization chain.

### Claim boundary

The documentary core is strong for **official AFOSI contact with Bennewitz**. The stronger claim of a formally authorized, centrally directed psychological/disinformation operation with a fully known command chain remains incompletely documented in the public record. Later Richard Doty claims require claim-by-claim corroboration because authentic AFOSI employment establishes access/proximity, not the truth of every retrospective statement.

## Claim discipline

- A government file proves government possession/attention to the recorded material, not the truth of every allegation inside it.
- A genuine clearance or intelligence job establishes possible access, not universal access and not truthfulness.
- Participant admissions about disinformation are historically important but still require reconstruction of scope, authorization and specific planted claims.
- Secret terrestrial programs are a plausible background condition near military test facilities; naming a specific program as the cause of an observation requires direct evidence.
- Underground alien-base, treaty and recovered-nonhuman-material claims remain Class 3 absent independently verified provenance/custody evidence.

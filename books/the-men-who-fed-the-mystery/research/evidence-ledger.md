# Evidence ledger

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- CIA-sponsored Robertson Panel and its documented concern with public/reporting implications of UFO reports.
- Project BLUE BOOK and its archival record.
- Government-sponsored remote-viewing research and the 1995 AIR evaluation.
- Jack Parsons's documented aerospace work, occult involvement, and government/security records; proximity does not establish a paranormal aerospace mechanism.
- Jacques Vallée's published contemporaneous journals and documented participation in UFO research networks.
- NIDS existed and investigated anomalous claims privately.
- Government and congressional attention to UAP exists; attention is not proof of extraordinary origin.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Named participants' recollections of intelligence-adjacent UFO discussions where institutional context is partly corroborated but underlying extraordinary claims are not.
- Accounts of counterintelligence interaction with civilian UFO investigators, pending event-by-event primary reconstruction.
- Private research observations for which investigators and facilities are identifiable but complete raw data are unavailable.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- The Collins Elite as the coherent, funded, operational group described in later accounts.
- MJ-12 as an authentic presidentially authorized UFO control group represented by the famous documents.
- Claims that occult practices produced operational intelligence or aerospace contact.
- Claims that government personnel successfully contacted non-human entities through ritual or psychic techniques.
- Claims of recovered extraterrestrial/non-human hardware absent independently verified custody evidence.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- Intelligence intervention can alter an anomaly narrative without originating the underlying phenomenon.
- Real classified facts can act as scaffolding for false higher-order stories.
- Secrecy creates an asymmetric correction problem: investigators can publicly misinterpret fragments while knowledgeable insiders may be unable to correct them.
- A mythology can be manufactured without a central manufacturer when individually true statements are joined by false causal edges.

## Contradiction / incentive audit

The subject rewards revelation. Authors sell books; witnesses gain attention; agencies protect sources and methods; counterintelligence officers may deliberately misdirect; believers may overconnect authentic fragments; skeptics may dismiss material because of its social contamination. None of those incentives determines whether a specific claim is true. They determine what must be independently checked.

The manuscript will not use `classified` as a synonym for `true`, `government` as a synonym for `confirmed`, or `debunked` as a substitute for reconstructing the underlying record.

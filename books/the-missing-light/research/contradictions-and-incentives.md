# Contradictions, Incentives, and Failure Modes

## SETI enthusiasm

Technosignature researchers have a legitimate scientific incentive to define searchable manifestations of technology, but candidate framing can receive disproportionate public attention. `Compatible with` an engineered model must never become `evidence for` engineering without comparative model support.

## Skeptical overcorrection

The presence of plate defects, catalog errors, proper motion and natural transients does not imply every candidate is an artifact. Dismissal without object-level analysis is methodologically symmetric with credulity.

## Archive selection

VASCO candidates come through multiple stages of catalogue construction and image selection. Any event-rate statement must condition on the actual searched exposure set, limiting magnitudes, quality cuts and manual-inspection rules.

## Post-hoc pattern definition

Clusters such as nine sources in ten arcminutes or three sources in ten arcseconds are discovered patterns. Their intuitive improbability is not a valid p-value unless the search process and alternative interesting patterns are included.

## Historical contamination

`Pre-Sputnik` removes conventional artificial satellites but not aircraft, balloons, sounding rockets, meteors, plate defects or undisclosed military activity. The date is a useful boundary, not proof of celestial origin.

## Nuclear-test association

The 2025 nuclear-test correlation is a published statistical result but should remain exploratory until independently replicated with exposure scheduling, weather, plate-development timing, fallout transport, candidate-classification blinding and multiple-testing decisions audited.

## UAP-report association

Historical UAP counts are not calibrated sensor observations. Media contagion, duplicate reporting, archive completeness, military activity, seasonal visibility and population density can create structure. Any joint analysis must model the report-generation process.

## Failed-supernova control

N6946-BH1 demonstrates that dramatic optical disappearance can arise from natural stellar evolution. Its existence raises the burden for interpreting a vanished stellar source as technology.

## Waste-heat ambiguity

Infrared excess is expected from many ordinary astrophysical systems. Partial-Dyson-sphere compatibility is not unique. Confusion, dusty disks, background galaxies and stellar youth must be actively excluded.

## Public-candidate mythology

Publishing coordinates aids reproducibility but can cause unresolved candidates to acquire narratives faster than observations. Maintain explicit closure states and update them when conventional counterparts are found.

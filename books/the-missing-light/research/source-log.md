## Context and follow-up priorities

- Original POSS-I observing logs and plate metadata from Caltech/Palomar archives: https://galex.caltech.edu/palomar/media/archival.html
- Gaia DR3 astrometric documentation for historical position propagation.
- Pan-STARRS DR1 survey papers for exact completeness, bandpasses and depth.
- WISE confusion and artifact documentation for Dyson-sphere candidate vetting.
- LBT failed-supernova survey continuation papers after Adams et al. 2017.
- Independent plate archives: Harvard DASCH, Sonneberg, UK Schmidt, Bamberg and other digitized historical collections.
- Historical satellite, balloon, sounding-rocket and aircraft test records for candidate dates before Sputnik.
- Nuclear-test dates from official DOE/NNSA/CTBTO historical records rather than secondary lists in the deepening pass.
- Original UAP source catalogue and inclusion/exclusion rules used by Bruehl & Villarroel, with duplicate-report audit.

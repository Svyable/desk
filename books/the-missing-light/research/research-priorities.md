# Research Priorities

1. Build a plate-by-plate table for the published 1950 nine-transient and 1952 triple-transient events: plate ID, start/end time, emulsion, filter, airmass, seeing proxy, development date, scanner version, field position and known artifacts.
2. Obtain original or highest-fidelity independent scans and compare source morphology at the emulsion-grain level where possible.
3. Reconstruct all modern counterpart searches using Gaia, Pan-STARRS, ZTF, SDSS, DESI Legacy Imaging, 2MASS and WISE with explicit local depth and quality masks.
4. Propagate Gaia proper motions to historical epochs and quantify the residual false-missing rate as a function of magnitude, color, Galactic latitude and crowding.
5. Extend failed-supernova work beyond N6946-BH1 into the full LBT survey and subsequent literature; build a rate table with selection effects.
6. Build a modern technosignature comparison table spanning disappearing stars, infrared waste heat, transit anomalies, lasers, atmospheric pollutants and artifacts, with each signature's primary natural confounders.
7. Reproduce Project Hephaistos candidate selection and track all published follow-up through 2026.
8. Audit the Bruehl–Villarroel 2025 nuclear/UAP statistical analysis from raw date lists. Separate exploratory and confirmatory tests; reproduce effect sizes under alternative windows and corrections.
9. Acquire official nuclear-test chronology and meteorological transport data for each relevant test-to-Palomar interval.
10. Audit the historical UAP catalogue for duplicate events, reporting clusters, source provenance and geographic independence.
11. Search independent pre-Sputnik plate archives under a blinded protocol for VASCO-like single, double, triple and clustered point-source events.
12. Design a Rubin-era prospective experiment with simultaneous separated-site observations for parallax, multi-band cadence, polarization and immediate spectroscopy of sub-hour multi-transient clusters.
13. Maintain a living candidate ledger with explicit closure reasons rather than preserving `unexplained` indefinitely.

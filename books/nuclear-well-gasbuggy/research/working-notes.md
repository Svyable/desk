# Working Notes

## Freshness ledger

This run deliberately exits the five newest Author-agent empirical families visible at selection time: COLDFEET / Arctic foreign-technical-intelligence exploitation; Viking / Mars life-detection chemistry; cable-bacteria long-distance electron transport; Weber resonant-bar gravitational-wave replication; and Project Sanguine / ELF strategic communication. Default-branch search returned no substantive Gasbuggy, Rulison, Rio Blanco, or Plowshare gas-stimulation manuscript.

The empirical core here is nuclear reservoir stimulation, underground explosion effects, petroleum reservoir geometry, radioactive product accounting, energy economics, arms-control verification, and multi-decade site stewardship.

## Central question

What does Project Gasbuggy teach us if we take seriously both sides of its result: the nuclear explosion measurably improved gas production, and no commercial nuclear-stimulated gas industry followed?

## Current answer

The program is best understood as an effect-to-industry failure. The core mechanical effect was real enough to sustain further experiments. The method then had to satisfy a stack of harder conditions: useful fracture geometry, product quality, long-term recovery, competitive economics, repeatable nuclear operations, environmental and local legitimacy, and international verification. Nonnuclear stimulation improved while that stack grew.

## Strongest competing explanations

1. **Radioactivity alone killed it.** Strong because product contamination was real; incomplete because geometry, cost, alternatives, and institutional burdens also appear in the record.
2. **Public/environmental opposition alone killed it.** Strong for some Plowshare proposals such as Chariot; insufficient without project-specific causal records for the gas-stimulation sequence.
3. **It was technically successful and politics suppressed a superior technology.** Not established. Requires date-matched economic and product-quality evidence absent from the current public spine.
4. **It was obviously ridiculous from the beginning.** Hindsight-heavy. Contemporary field experiments and federal energy scenarios show serious technical and institutional commitment.
5. **Hydraulic fracturing simply descended from Gasbuggy.** Reject. Hydraulic fracturing predates Gasbuggy; the useful comparison is competing ways to solve permeability and distributed-reservoir access.

## Contradiction / incentive ledger

- AEC had an institutional interest in demonstrating peaceful uses of nuclear technology; this is an incentive to scrutinize optimistic program claims, not proof of fabrication.
- Industry partners had incentives to unlock difficult gas resources and gain federally supported experimental knowledge; they also had a commercial incentive to reject gas that could not be sold profitably.
- DOE Legacy Management has an institutional responsibility to demonstrate competent stewardship; current monitoring claims should therefore be checked against the actual sampling record, while not treating the incentive as evidence that results are false.
- Anti-nuclear and environmental actors had incentives to emphasize irreversible and radiological risks; unsupported contamination claims require pathway evidence even when the general concern was reasonable.
- Later oil-and-gas histories may overstate the inevitability of hydraulic fracturing's victory. Compare technologies at their contemporaneous maturity, not through modern shale hindsight.
- Arms-control institutions had incentives to minimize ambiguous nuclear events. That makes PNE verification burden historically real but does not prove it was the decisive commercialization barrier.

## Anti-slop / voice notes

Keep the odd physical facts in charge. The book should spend time on the well, the collapse chimney, the gas flare, the three-device vertical array, the 676-well forecast, the unfired Wagon Wheel hole, and the modern drilling restrictions around old cavities. Avoid turning every chapter into another statement that "systems are complex." Let a technical detail earn the synthesis.

Do not flatten historical actors into atomic-age fools. The strongest book gives them the real permeability problem, the real field results, and the alternatives available at the time, then shows precisely where the industrial case weakened.

## Defeat conditions

See `evidence-matrix.md`. The central thesis must change if upstream records show that product-quality and economic burdens were negligible at commercially relevant times, that nuclear stimulation substantially dominated date-matched alternatives, or that one discrete nontechnical intervention overwhelmingly explains termination while the cumulative constraints were considered solved.

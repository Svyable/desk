## Source-use rules

- DOE Legacy Management pages are authoritative for current stewardship posture and useful program summaries, but historical technical claims should be checked against contemporary reports before publication.
- OSTI/OpenNet documents may contain contemporary predictions later shown wrong. Preserve their date and status; do not quote them as hindsight consensus.
- Energy-outlook projections are evidence of what federal planners modeled, not evidence that commercialization was technically or economically inevitable.
- Modern hydraulic-fracturing literature is used as a comparison of alternative development paths, not as proof of a direct technological lineage from Plowshare.
- Monitoring results support claims about monitored pathways and periods only. They do not establish universal absence of contamination in every medium, nor does the existence of monitoring establish that contamination has been observed.
- Treaty records establish legal/verification context. Do not claim arms control single-handedly killed the gas-stimulation program without decision-specific evidence.

## Defeat conditions

The manuscript's main synthesis must narrow if primary records show any of the following:

- commercial gas from one or more nuclear-stimulation tests would have met applicable product/radiological standards on economically useful timescales with negligible treatment burden;
- date-matched economics show nuclear stimulation decisively superior to credible nonnuclear alternatives when cancellation decisions were made;
- internal decision records identify a single dominant termination cause and explicitly discount the cumulative technical/economic/institutional constraints emphasized here;
- original fracture/production data materially contradict DOE's later summary that Gasbuggy's useful fracture reach disappointed expectations;
- records show Wagon Wheel stopped for reasons unrelated to the cumulative maturity of the program, requiring its role in the narrative to be reframed.

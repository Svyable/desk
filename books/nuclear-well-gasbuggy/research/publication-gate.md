- read the whole manuscript sequentially for repeated claims and remove duplicated systems-language;
- preserve concrete experiment movement rather than allowing the book to become a framework about scaling;
- verify every number against the upstream source and page;
- audit every causal statement about cancellation for documentary support;
- distinguish contemporary expectations from hindsight;
- perform the required anti-slop and paragraph passes;
- run cover/metadata and Desk checks required by repository instructions;
- update shared catalog/Reader surfaces only when the book is actually ready to be represented there.

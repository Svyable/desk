# FIFTY MICROTESLA

## The quantum sense hiding inside an animal

Sven Hardy Benson

This is a book about magnetoreception and the unusually clean epistemic problem it creates. Animals can respond to magnetic information that humans do not obviously perceive. A leading mechanism invokes quantum spin chemistry. Neither fact licenses quantum mysticism.

Throughout the manuscript, claims are kept in four working classes: **documented/replicated evidence**, **credible but incomplete evidence**, **disputed or unconfirmed claims**, and **speculative synthesis/open conjecture**. Those classes can change as evidence changes. Testimony is not promoted into measurement; in-vitro molecular behavior is not promoted into in-vivo receptor proof; a physical mechanism is not promoted into conscious experience.

The governing prohibition is simple: **behavior is not receptor; receptor is not pathway; pathway is not percept.**

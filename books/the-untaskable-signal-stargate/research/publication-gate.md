# Publication gate

This book has a complete 18-chapter argument and supporting research apparatus, but it **does not yet clear Desk’s automated full-book standard**.

The current run prioritized a coherent, evidence-disciplined book-length first pass and a strong research trail over padding. The manuscript has not been mechanically validated with `python3 scripts/check-book-length.py the-untaskable-signal-stargate --json`, and several chapters are visibly below the required 3,000 words. Therefore no 65,000-word claim is made and no publication-ready status is requested.

## Required next pass

1. **Page-index the 1995 AIR report.** Recover the complete report in a stable public copy; map Utts, Hyman, and operational-review claims to exact pages; separate their actual language from later CIA summary language.
2. **Rebuild the early SRI target series.** Retrieve the complete 1974 Nature paper, Marks & Kammann 1978, Marks 1981, Marks & Scott 1986, and any contemporaneous replies. Create a trial-level ledger of target selection, transcript handling, judging materials, identified cues, rejudging results, and disputed points.
3. **Build the program-name / sponsor ledger.** SCANATE, GONDOLA WISH, GRILL FLAME, CENTER LANE, SUN STREAK, STAR GATE: exact dates, sponsors, missions, transfers, contract organizations, and personnel. Do not use retrospective STARGATE as if it were contemporaneous across the entire period.
4. **Construct a consecutive Tehran sample.** Select a bounded date window in 1980 and recover every available hostage-related session in that window, not only famous ones. Code all descriptors before target feedback and calculate accuracy, specificity, and false-description burden.
5. **Reconstruct at least two celebrated operational cases.** Use original tasking, complete transcript, timestamps, later feedback, independent confirmation source, and neighboring attempts. Any case that cannot support this chain remains testimony-heavy.
6. **Recover the customer record.** Page-index AIR operational interviews/reviews and find declassified tasker/customer memoranda. Separate “interesting,” “confirmed,” “unique,” “actionable,” and “decision-changing.”
7. **Build a viewer-performance ledger.** Where public records permit, distinguish prospective viewer selection from after-the-fact reputation. Track session counts, target classes, scoring method, misses, and reported confidence.
8. **Reconstruct Soviet-threat inputs.** Retrieve the cited DIA/CIA reports on Soviet/Czechoslovak/East European parapsychology, identify their underlying sources, and separate documented foreign research from American extrapolation about capability.
9. **Add the NRC 1988 record in depth.** Page-index Chapter 9 and committee reasoning, then compare its evidentiary criteria with AIR 1995 rather than presenting the two reviews as simple contradiction.
10. **Investigate post-1995 continuation claims adversarially.** Search congressional records, budgets, official histories, FOIA releases, credible reporting, and named testimony. Do not infer continuation from mere possibility. If no authenticated evidence emerges, retain class-3 status.
11. **Build a modern experimental-design appendix.** Work through target-space entropy, rank-order scoring, decoy design, information gain, base rates, false positives, calibration, and an analyst-blinded decoy-transcript test.
12. **Expand every chapter through source-specific material.** The added words must come from documents, trial sequences, exact protocol mechanics, quantitative worked examples, customer evidence, and conflicting interpretations—not from repeating the anomaly-versus-service thesis.
13. **Whole-book editorial pass.** Reverse-outline all 18 chapters; remove repeated explanations of target pools, calibration, and “government interest is not proof”; vary chapter architecture; keep the dry documentary voice; check all claimed quotations against sources.
14. **Run objective checks.** `python3 scripts/check-book-length.py the-untaskable-signal-stargate --json`, `python3 scripts/check-book-cover-metadata.py the-untaskable-signal-stargate`, and the relevant Desk integrity checks before any publication-state or catalog promotion.

## Release blockers

- Any chapter under 3,000 words.
- Chapter-only total below 65,000 words.
- AIR 1995 claims not page-indexed.
- Famous operational cases cited without original tasking/transcript/feedback chain where those records are available.
- Claims of Soviet efficacy based only on U.S. threat reporting.
- Claims of post-1995 continuation without authenticated evidence.
- Whole-book editorial review not completed.

The target for the next pass is not merely “more words.” It is a manuscript in which each major claim can survive an adversarial reader with the archive open beside it.

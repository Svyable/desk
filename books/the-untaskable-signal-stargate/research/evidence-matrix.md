## Defeat conditions for this book

The book’s central synthesis would be materially defeated by authenticated records showing a sustained operational program with prospectively scored, consecutive cases that produced unique intelligence gains substantially beyond conventional baselines and whose correct elements could be distinguished before independent confirmation.

The book’s skeptical constraints would be weakened by modern preregistered multilaboratory replications that produce stable effect sizes under jointly designed anti-leakage protocols, particularly if independent laboratories without personnel/method ancestry reproduce the results.

The book’s residual openness would narrow in the other direction if a complete consecutive-case reconstruction showed that celebrated operational successes systematically disappear once full transcripts, target priors, misses, and feedback timing are restored, and if modern adversarial laboratory replications converge tightly on chance.

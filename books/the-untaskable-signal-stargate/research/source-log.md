# Source log

This file is the live source spine for *The Untaskable Signal*. For intelligence-adjacent claims, an official document establishes what an agency, contractor, officer, or evaluator recorded; it does not automatically establish the underlying phenomenon. Patents are not used as evidence of efficacy. No non-public classified access is claimed.

## Evidence-handling rules

- A session transcript is not scored from excerpts. Recover the complete transcript, tasking, feedback, and neighboring sessions where possible.
- “Confirmed by HUMINT/national technical sources” is treated as an official assessment until the independent confirming record is recovered.
- Participant memoirs and interviews are testimony, not substitutes for contemporaneous files.
- Soviet/Russian-state interest is not efficacy. U.S. intelligence summaries of Soviet claims establish U.S. threat perception unless the underlying Soviet primary material independently supports more.
- No claim of post-1995 classified continuation is upgraded above disputed allegation without authenticated public evidence.
- Laboratory statistical evidence and operational usefulness are kept separate throughout.

# Evidentiary Matrix

| Proposition | Class | Present support | What would strengthen / defeat it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| France conducted 17 nuclear tests in the Algerian Sahara from 1960–1966 | Verified/documented | French parliamentary records; IAEA; CTBTO | Stable; only nomenclature/details may refine |
| Four were atmospheric at Reggane and thirteen underground at In Ekker | Verified/documented | French parliamentary records; IAEA | Stable |
| At least four underground shots had containment defects | Verified/documented | French parliamentary reports identify Béryl, Améthyste, Rubis, Jade | Original shot reports should quantify scale and mechanism |
| Béryl released radioactive material from the tunnel on May 1, 1962 | Verified/documented | Official histories, parliamentary reporting, film, later technical accounts | Original post-shot engineering report for failure sequence |
| Fallout from Gerboise Bleue was detected far outside the immediate test range | Verified/documented at broad level | Declassified French military fallout chronology reported publicly; Algerian/French secondary reproductions | Recover original declassified report/map and station readings |
| Continental-scale detection proves continental-scale dangerous human dose | Disputed / not established | Geographic reach alone is insufficient | Station concentrations, deposition, exposure models, human behavior |
| French institutions possessed materially more technical information about the tests than nearby Algerian communities | Verified in general / incomplete in distribution | Program structure, classified monitoring, archival holdings | Distribution lists, disclosure records, local warning protocols |
| Algerian local workers were systematically monitored less well than French personnel | Credible but incomplete | Testimony and structural record asymmetry | Personnel-category dosimetry policies, rosters, badge inventories, medical files |
| The test program caused specific later cancers in named individuals | Case-specific; usually incomplete | Presence + compatible disease can establish plausibility | Individual dose reconstruction, job/location records, medical history; population risk model |
| Regional cancer/congenital-anomaly burdens are elevated because of the tests | Disputed / insufficiently established at population scale | Algerian testimony and publications report such patterns | Independent epidemiology with denominators, case verification, comparison groups and dose gradients |
| France left radioactive and non-radioactive waste at former sites | Verified in general; locations incomplete | Parliamentary debate, IAEA site assessment, investigative reporting | Full dismantlement/waste inventory and burial maps |
| France possesses complete undisclosed maps of all waste | Disputed | Algerian demands and partial French records do not prove completeness | Archival inventory, declassification review, explicit statement of lost/nonexistent records |
| Algeria inherited a hazard landscape without equal control of the technical archive | Verified as structural fact / synthesis as consequence | France ran program and retained archives; Algeria obtained territory; later information requests | Handover record showing complete contemporaneous transfer would substantially weaken thesis |
| Secrecy increased later causal uncertainty | Speculative synthesis supported by mechanism | Classified environmental data and uneven follow-up can prevent integration of exposure and clinical evidence | Evidence that relevant data were fully shared with health authorities and affected people would weaken it |
| Initial Morin compensation design was too restrictive to achieve stated goals | Verified as official policy judgment | 2013 Senate report and subsequent reforms | Current outcome data may refine magnitude |
| Algerian claimants face higher practical barriers | Credible and partly documented | very low historic claimant/award counts, nationality/archive/language barriers, political debate | Current CIVEN microdata and claimant-process analysis |
| A new joint public radiological map is technically feasible | Speculative policy synthesis | IAEA precedent; modern survey methods; existing archival coordinates | Security/legal constraints and field feasibility study |

## Rule

No row is promoted merely because multiple secondary sources repeat the same claim. Terminal evidence matters: original test report, declassified map, measured sample, official decision, peer-reviewed study, named testimony, or court/compensation record.

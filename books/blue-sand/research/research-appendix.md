# Research Appendix

## Highest-value next archival work

1. **Recover the original Gerboise Bleue fallout report/map.** Press reproductions establish that a French military chronology was declassified, but publication should cite the terminal report: title, date, classification history, station measurements, legend and distribution list.

2. **Build a thirteen-shot In Ekker matrix.** For every underground test: exact date, code name, yield, tunnel/gallery, containment design, predicted vs. actual geology, release path, environmental measurements, personnel stand-off, re-entry rules, decontamination and post-shot investigation.

3. **Reconstruct Béryl minute by minute.** Use original engineering report, meteorology, film, personnel maps, dosimetry and medical follow-up. Separate visible plume, measured contamination and individual dose.

4. **Audit safety learning.** Compare pre-Béryl procedures with post-Béryl changes. Determine whether Améthyste, Rubis and Jade repeated known mechanisms or failed differently.

5. **Inventory personnel categories.** French officers, conscripts, CEA scientists, contractors, Algerian workers, local support staff. Identify which categories were badged, medically monitored and named in surviving records.

6. **Recover post-test dismantlement records.** Target waste manifests, vehicle/equipment surveys, burial plans, transfer documents, restricted-zone maps and the 1966–1967 radiological state referenced by the French government.

7. **Extract the IAEA report into geospatial data.** Coordinates, radionuclides, sample results, dose rates, recommendations. Compare with current satellite imagery and later surveys.

8. **Build the compensation dataset.** Current CIVEN statistics by country/nationality, test theater, disease, approval/denial reason and year. Avoid freezing historical numbers from journalism when official current data exist.

9. **Cross-language oral-history index.** Collect Algerian Arabic and French testimony with original-script names, place names, occupation, date, firsthand/secondhand flag and archival corroboration status.

10. **Epidemiology feasibility study.** Determine whether veteran rosters, Algerian civil records and cancer registries can support cohort or case-control research. Pre-register hypotheses before outcome analysis.

## Interview protocol

Ask witnesses first for role, date and location without introducing a test code name. Separate: (a) what the witness personally saw/did; (b) what they were told at the time; (c) what they learned later; (d) current interpretation. Record original language. Do not ask leading disease-causation questions. Seek permission for name/publication status separately from recording consent.

## Defeat conditions in fieldwork

If original handover records show complete transfer of waste maps and radiological data to Algerian authorities, revise the split-archive thesis sharply. If worker-monitoring policies show equivalent coverage across French and Algerian categories, remove the implied monitoring inequality. If robust epidemiology produces a clear effect estimate, let it replace agnosticism rather than treating uncertainty as part of the brand of the book.

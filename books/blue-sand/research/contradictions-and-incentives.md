# Contradiction and Incentive Audit

## France / military institutions

**Incentives:** preserve deterrent legitimacy; protect genuine weapons information; limit open-ended liability; defend historical safety systems; avoid diplomatic admissions with legal consequences; demonstrate that remediation/compensation mechanisms exist.

**Counter-incentives:** parliamentary scrutiny; veterans' political pressure; archival norms; reputational value of transparency; technical agencies' interest in accurate historical record.

**Core contradiction:** the state can credibly point to sophisticated monitoring and safety programs while that same sophistication strengthens the question of why affected outsiders saw so little of the measurement record at the time.

## Algerian state / political institutions

**Incentives:** frame tests inside colonial grievance; obtain archives, remediation and compensation; strengthen diplomatic leverage; preserve national memory.

**Counter-incentives:** desire for technically credible claims; need to manage present-day sites; bilateral cooperation with France; international nuclear-policy credibility.

**Core contradiction:** strong political language can keep an under-documented history visible while making weak causal claims easier for opponents to dismiss.

## Veterans and local survivor groups

**Incentives:** recognition, compensation, healthcare, archival access; preservation of lived experience.

**Risks:** selection toward sick members; retrospective attribution; repetition of dramatic but poorly sourced casualty estimates; conflation of presence with dose.

**Counterweight:** witnesses often preserve task, location and warning details absent from official summaries.

## Nuclear institutions / scientists

**Incentives:** emphasize measured dose and uncertainty; resist attribution unsupported by epidemiology; protect methodological standards.

**Risks:** technocratic framing can answer the wrong moral question; compliance with period thresholds can be mistaken for proof of no harm; population studies can under-detect small high-exposure subgroups.

## Journalists / document leakers

**Incentives:** dramatic revelations, hidden maps, official contradiction.

**Risks:** treating detection as dangerous dose; overreading classification as proof of malicious concealment; using the broadest plume boundary as if it were an injury boundary.

**Value:** litigation and reporting have repeatedly surfaced documents that changed public understanding.

## Compensation institutions

**Incentives:** administrable, consistent rules; evidentiary thresholds; budget predictability; statutory compliance.

**Risks:** translating gradients into rigid zones; requiring claimants to supply documents generated or controlled by the state; making absence of records look like absence of exposure.

## Claims to hold open

1. Whether French authorities withheld materially important dose/waste information from Algerian officials at specific handover moments.
2. Whether local workers received systematically weaker monitoring/protection than French categories performing comparable tasks.
3. Whether any current regional disease excess can be robustly attributed to testing.
4. Whether France retains detailed waste/burial information beyond what has been shared publicly or bilaterally.
5. Whether post-Béryl engineering changes materially reduced the probability or scale of later containment failures.

## Claims the current record does not justify

- that French decision-makers knew in 1960 the precise future cancer burden and deliberately accepted it;
- that every local cancer or congenital anomaly was caused by the tests;
- that all seventeen shots were containment catastrophes;
- that continental-scale fallout detection means continental-scale dangerous dose;
- that incomplete archives prove a hidden additional test program;
- that the IAEA assessment either cleared the sites as harmless or proved the entire region dangerous.

The manuscript should remain uncomfortable for every stakeholder. If one side can quote it as a clean advocacy brief without caveat, the evidence classes have probably collapsed.

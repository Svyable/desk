# Document Appendix

This appendix is a retrieval ledger, not a claim that every item has been fully extracted in this first pass.

## A. Test-program records

- DIRCEN archival holdings, Service historique de la Défense, 1952–1970.
- CSEM/Reggane shot reports for Gerboise Bleue, Blanche, Rouge, Verte.
- CEMO/In Ekker shot reports for all thirteen underground experiments.
- Post-Béryl engineering investigation and procedural changes.
- Meteorological forecast and observed-wind records for atmospheric shots and containment failures.
- Fallout station logs underlying the declassified Gerboise Bleue chronology.
- Personnel dosimetry summaries and category-specific badge policies.
- Re-entry/decontamination orders and contaminated-equipment logs.

## B. Decolonization / site-transfer records

- Evian agreements and associated military-facility protocols affecting Reggane/In Ekker.
- French diplomatic correspondence on duration and conditions of continued site use.
- Algerian governmental correspondence concerning safety, access and eventual transfer.
- Dismantlement reports and site handover documents.
- 1966–1967 French radiological assessment of former sites referenced in later parliamentary answers.

## C. Waste / remediation

- Original burial maps, waste inventories and contaminated-material manifests if surviving.
- IAEA, *Radiological Conditions at the Former French Nuclear Test Sites in Algeria: Preliminary Assessment and Recommendations* (2005).
- Algerian follow-up environmental surveys.
- French responses to parliamentary questions on Saharan nuclear waste, 2020 onward.

## D. Human exposure / health

- Unit rosters and employment records by test/event.
- Medical surveillance and bioassay protocols.
- Veteran compensation claims where lawfully public or available in anonymized form.
- CIVEN annual reports and statistical tables.
- Algerian local-worker and resident oral histories in Arabic/French.
- Any peer-reviewed cohort/case-control work on Saharan veterans or residents.

## E. Public knowledge / disclosure

- 1959 UN debate and resolutions concerning planned French Saharan tests.
- Contemporary French government statements after each atmospheric test.
- Contemporary African government protests and press.
- Classification markings/distribution lists on fallout reports.
- 1995 official publication of the French test list.
- 2013 declassification chain for the Gerboise Bleue fallout map.
- Parliamentary reports from 1998, 2001/2002, 2009, 2013 and 2025–2026.

## Document rule

For every future chapter expansion, prefer a proposition-specific terminal document to a general history. A source saying "Béryl was an accident" is inferior to the engineering report for explaining failure. A source saying "people were exposed" is inferior to a personnel/dose record for quantifying exposure. A memoir is primary evidence for the memoirist's experience and interpretation, not automatically for the physics of the event.

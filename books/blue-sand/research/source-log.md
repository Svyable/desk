## Source discipline

No casualty total enters manuscript prose unless its denominator and derivation are recoverable. Detection of fallout is not equated with medically significant dose. Testimony is attributed as testimony. French official language is not treated as neutral simply because it is technical. Algerian legal or political language is not treated as empirical proof simply because it represents an affected community.

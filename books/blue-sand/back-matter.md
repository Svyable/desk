# Back Matter

## Evidence classes

Throughout *Blue Sand*, claims are held in four bins.

**Verified / documented:** events or propositions established by primary or authoritative records: the seventeen French nuclear tests in the Algerian Sahara; four atmospheric tests at Reggane; thirteen underground shots at In Ekker; at least four underground shots with imperfect containment; the Béryl accident; the continuation of testing after Algerian independence under negotiated arrangements; the existence of French fallout and radiological monitoring; the IAEA's later assessment; the Morin compensation law and its subsequent reform; the public archival holdings of the French nuclear-test directorate.

**Credible but incomplete:** testimony from workers and veterans; plausible but incompletely reconstructed exposure pathways; reports of locally elevated disease that lack full epidemiological design; claims about particular contaminated objects or burial points not yet tied to terminal records; estimates that depend on incomplete dosimetry.

**Disputed / not established:** large casualty totals with unclear derivation; attribution of a specific individual's cancer to a specific test without adequate dose evidence; generalized claims of hereditary catastrophe without population-level support; claims that all relevant archives or waste locations are known; claims that any detection of fallout implies dangerous dose.

**Speculative synthesis:** the proposition that decolonization split the technical archive from the hazard landscape in a way that structurally shifted later proof burdens onto Algerians; the argument that classification can manufacture future causal uncertainty by preventing exposure and clinical records from being integrated while evidence is fresh; proposed joint mapping and cohort studies.

## Defeat conditions

The central thesis should be weakened or abandoned if archival research shows that Algerian authorities received complete, usable test-by-test environmental and waste records at transfer; that local and French personnel categories were monitored equivalently; that claimed archival asymmetries are largely artifacts of later access rather than original recordkeeping; or that the allegedly withheld maps and inventories do not exist in materially more complete form.

The health argument should be revised if robust independent epidemiology demonstrates either a clear large excess disease burden attributable to test exposure or, conversely, sufficiently precise low-dose reconstruction to exclude meaningful excess risk across the principal claimed populations. Neither result should be resisted to preserve the narrative.

## Rights and research posture

All quoted or paraphrased claims in future expansion should be traceable to public sources logged in `research/source-log.md`. No classified access is claimed or implied. Patents, memoirs, testimony and political statements are evidence of what their authors asserted unless independently corroborated.

# To the Stars

*The Story of the United States Space Academy*

Sven Hardy Benson

*A nonfiction narrative about an institution at the moment of its invention.*

The United States Space Academy described in this book was proposed by executive order on August 28, 2026. At the time of writing, its permanent location, final governance, admissions rules, curriculum, service obligations, legislative authorities, appropriations, and opening date had not yet been settled. Where the record ends and possibility begins, the manuscript says so.

# To The Stars

*The Story of the United States Space Academy*

Sven Hardy Benson

*A narrative nonfiction work in progress.*

The United States Space Academy described in this book was announced by executive order on August 28, 2026. At the time this manuscript began, the academy's permanent form had not yet been decided. A Presidential Commission chaired by the NASA Administrator had been instructed to recommend its governance, curriculum, service obligations, admissions prerequisites, location, legal authorities, and implementation strategy.

This book follows that process while placing it inside a longer American story: how a country decides that a new domain of national power has become important enough to require not merely new machines, commands, and budgets, but a new kind of professional.

# To The Stars

*The Story of the United States Space Academy*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Author** | Sven Hardy Benson |
| **Status** | Complete draft |
| **Chapters** | 20 of 20 + prologue + 5 interludes + epilogue |

A narrative nonfiction book about the creation of the United States Space Academy and the larger institutional question behind it: how the United States intends to educate a generation of Guardians, astronauts, engineers, operators, scientists, civil servants, and space-industry leaders for a domain that is at once military, civilian, scientific, and commercial.

The book begins with President Donald J. Trump's August 28, 2026 executive order establishing a Presidential Commission to design the academy. It treats the academy as a proposal in formation, not as a finished institution, and follows the history that made the proposal possible: the service-academy tradition, NASA's deliberately civilian origins, the creation of the U.S. Space Force, the Air Force Academy's existing role in commissioning Guardians, STARCOM's evolving training pipeline, and the political fight over whether a new federal academy is necessary at all.

## Fact boundary

This complete draft is researched through September 3, 2026. As of that date, the United States Space Academy is a proposed NASA-led federal academy being designed by a Presidential Commission. The executive order directs the commission to deliver recommendations within 120 days of August 28, 2026. Governance, curriculum, admissions, service obligations, location, legislative authority, funding, accreditation, class size, first-class timing, and implementation remain unsettled.

The manuscript does not present those unresolved questions as decided facts. Because the subject is live, later official recommendations, legislation, appropriations, site decisions, or implementation changes should be incorporated through the research update protocol before any later edition is treated as current.

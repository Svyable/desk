# To the Stars

*The Story of the United States Space Academy*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Author** | Sven Hardy Benson |
| **Status** | Complete draft |
| **Chapters** | Prologue + 20 chapters + epilogue |

A narrative nonfiction account of the institution announced in Houston on August 28, 2026: why the United States proposed a NASA-led federal Space Academy, what problem it is meant to solve, how it inherits two centuries of service-academy tradition and seven decades of American human spaceflight, and what it could mean for the people who will explore, operate, build, regulate, and defend the space domain.

The manuscript treats the Academy as what it is at this stage: a proposed institution whose governing framework, curriculum, admissions rules, service obligations, location, legislation, appropriations, and implementation plan remain subject to the Presidential Commission process and subsequent legal action.

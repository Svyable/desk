# Research queue

1. Reconstruct Kalmijn’s original electroreception experiments and later behavioral threshold work at figure/table level.
2. Build a cross-species table: stimulus variable, threshold, bandwidth, receptor organ, perturbation evidence, ecological function.
3. Reconstruct Bellini/Blakemore magnetotactic-bacteria discovery history from primary papers rather than retrospective anecdotes.
4. Trace magnetosome genetics from membrane invagination through biomineralization and chain organization.
5. Audit avian magnetic-sensing literature: cryptochrome/radical-pair experiments, magnetic-particle hypotheses, trigeminal manipulations, radiofrequency disruption, replication disputes.
6. Quantify environmental signal regimes against receptor thresholds rather than comparing adjectives such as “weak.”
7. Add sensory examples outside EM channels only where they sharpen the governing argument: polarization vision, infrared sensing, mechanosensory lateral lines.
8. Treat claims about human magnetic-field responses as a separate adversarial evidence chapter with preregistration, blinding and replication emphasized.
9. Build astrobiology chapter around information theory and environmental gradients, clearly marked as synthesis rather than evidence for extraterrestrial organisms.
10. Whole-book anti-slop pass: remove “sixth sense” sensationalism, false mystery, anthropocentric counting, and analogies that outrun mechanism.

## Publication gate

No publication-ready claim that a receptor or molecular pathway is established until primary studies and major contradictory results are reconstructed. No extension from animal sensing to human anomalous perception without direct human evidence.

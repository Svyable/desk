# Research trail — Breakeven

This directory is the canonical evidence and provenance package for *Breakeven: The Race to Build a Star on Earth*. It travels with the publication from Desk to Shelf as part of a deliberate release snapshot.

## Source ledger

`source-ledger.csv` records the material sources behind the manuscript's factual claims: author or institution, title, source type, publication and access dates, the claim or chapter passage each source supports, the source URL, and notes on limitations, uncertainty, or counterevidence.

Sourcing follows a deliberate hierarchy. Company and laboratory press releases, government and regulatory primary sources (the Department of Energy, the Nuclear Regulatory Commission, Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, ITER's own organization, the Fusion Industry Association's annual reports), and peer-reviewed research are preferred for claims they can answer directly. Contemporaneous journalism is used where the event itself is the subject, or where it is the best available account of a primary event. Figures that are estimates, company targets, or forward-looking plans are identified as such in the manuscript rather than stated with the certainty of a measured result.

Fusion research moves quickly and several of the figures in this ledger — funding totals, plasma-duration records, construction milestones, regulatory rulemakings — are snapshots that will be superseded. `notes` flags claims worth rechecking before any release.

## Reader-facing evidence

The manuscript absorbs the sourced facts directly into its prose, naming the institution, researcher, or publication in-sentence rather than using footnotes or bracketed citations, following this Desk's house style. This ledger is the deeper record: it exists so a reader, editor, or future author can trace any specific number or date back to where it came from and see what it does and does not establish.

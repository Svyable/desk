## Contemporary-source caution

The research frontier around recurrent depth moved quickly in 2025–2026. The manuscript therefore treats S19–S23 as evidence of an active research direction, not as proof that one architecture has already won production deployment. Vendor rumors and unnamed-model claims are excluded from the central argument unless independently confirmed by first-party technical material.

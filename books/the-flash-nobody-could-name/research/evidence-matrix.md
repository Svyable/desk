# Evidence matrix

This matrix is the working epistemic contract for *The Flash Nobody Could Name*. Classification applies to the claim, not to the emotional force of the source.

## Four classes

**Class 1 — verified/declassified/documented facts.** Includes instrument records, dated memoranda, panel charters, official judgments, and later acknowledged program history. A Class 1 document proves that the record exists and says what it says; it does not automatically prove every factual assertion quoted inside it.

**Class 2 — credible but incomplete reporting/testimony.** Includes technical claims made by relevant officials where the underlying waveform, laboratory notebook, or complete report is unavailable publicly; physically plausible model interpretations; and high-quality historical reporting with incomplete primary chain.

**Class 3 — disputed allegation/unconfirmed claim.** Includes specific actor attribution, claims of deliberate political cover-up, causal attribution of iodine-131 to the event, and any claim that classification itself proves withheld confirmation.

**Class 4 — speculative synthesis/open conjecture.** Includes the manuscript's broader claims about multisensor epistemology, action thresholds, secrecy, and how different institutions aggregate uncertainty.

## Non-negotiable separations

- Test occurrence is separate from test attribution.
- Actor capability is separate from responsibility.
- A panel's conclusion is separate from nature's ground truth.
- A declassified memo reporting an assessment is separate from the underlying evidence for that assessment.
- Patent, proposal, or capability claim is never treated as proof of operational efficacy.

## Defeat conditions

The “durable unresolved event” thesis is defeated if a complete, auditable record demonstrates that the optical trace plus independent channels decisively favor one physical explanation and that the apparent institutional disagreement was based on missing information later resolved internally. The specific joint-test attribution remains unverified unless actor-specific evidence independently clears its higher burden.

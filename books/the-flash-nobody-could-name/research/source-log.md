# Source log

Public sources checked fresh for this drafting pass. Links are retained in usable form so later expansion can return to the originating record rather than cite this manuscript's paraphrase.

## Core archival collections

- National Security Archive, **The Vela Incident: South Atlantic Mystery Flash in September 1979 Raised Questions about Nuclear Test** (6 Dec 2016). Large document set from Gerard C. Smith papers and Carter-era records; includes panel material, collection activity, actor-analysis records, dissent, and document provenance. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/nuclear-vault/2016-12-06/vela-incident-south-atlantic-mystery-flash-september-1979-raised-questions-about-nuclear-test
- National Security Archive, **The Vela Flash: Forty Years Ago** (22 Sep 2019). Adds Carter Library material including reporting that CIA assessed a nuclear test at “90% plus,” plus discussion of Arecibo and internal disagreement. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/nuclear-vault/2019-09-22/vela-flash-forty-years-ago
- National Security Archive, **Proliferation Watch: U.S. Intelligence Assessments of Potential Nuclear Powers, 1977–2001**. Includes the December 1979 interagency memorandum on the event in differently redacted releases. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/intelligence-nuclear-vault/2013-12-16/proliferation-watch-us-intelligence-assessments

## Contemporary/declassified records used directly

- Ross E. Cowey, State Department INR, memorandum for files, 10 Oct 1979. Contemporary synopsis of CIA-sponsored Agnew/Garwin/Lukasik review; records judgment that signals were consistent with an atmospheric nuclear explosion while noting bhangmeter self-consistency problems. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/22322-10-memorandum-files-ross-e-cowey
- Richard M. Moose, “Current Nuke Event Actions,” 25 Sep 1979. Records early allied consultation and questions to Australia about seismic-station operation. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/22318-06-memorandum-record-richard-m-moose
- Nuclear Explosion Panel charter, circa 1 Nov 1979. Shows remit to investigate natural phenomenon/technical failure and additional corroborating data. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/22326-14-nuclear-explosion-panel-charter-n-d-circa-1
- White House Science and Technology staff, “Evening Report,” Nov 1979. Records preliminary Ruina-panel skepticism, meteoroid possibility, signal differences, and interest in Arecibo disturbance. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/19604-national-security-archive-doc-3-white-house
- White House Situation Room log, “Reflections of South African Nuclear Event,” 25 Oct 1979. Records collection efforts and later-discussed radiological evidence. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/19602-national-security-archive-doc-1-white-house
- State/OES “Nite Note,” 19 Nov 1979. Useful for sheep-thyroid sampling chronology and early negative Australian shipment; notes ongoing panel work. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/22339-27-nite-note-prepared-bureau-oceans
- NSC Intelligence Coordination to Zbigniew Brzezinski, 1 Dec 1980. Reports abnormal iodine-131 in sheep thyroids and explicitly preserves industrial/pharmaceutical alternatives. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/22352-41-intelligence-coordination-zbigniew
- DCI, NI IIM 79-10028, **The 22 September 1979 Event**, Dec 1979, 2004 release. Actor-analysis study instructed to assume a detonation and examine possible perpetrators; useful for separating event classification from attribution. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/29884-document-6b-director-central-intelligence-ni-iim-79-10028-22-september-1979-event
- Los Alamos International Technology Office, ITO-79-155, **22 September 1979 Event**, 26 Nov 1979, declassified DOE release. Discusses bhangmeter and Arecibo material with redacted proliferation sections. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/29882-document-5-deleted-international-technology-office-los-alamos-national-laboratory

## Technical/background sources

- Los Alamos National Laboratory, *National Security Science*, July 2015, bhangmeter history. States Vela-borne bhangmeters detected 41 confirmed nuclear tests and discusses the unresolved 1979 event. https://cdn.lanl.gov/files/nss-july-2015_219c6.pdf
- Los Alamos Scientific Laboratory, **Evaluation of Some Geophysical Events on 22 September 1979**, LA-8672 (Apr 1981), public catalog mirror. Expansion pass should retrieve and page-index complete PDF. https://sgp.fas.org/othergov/doe/lanl/lib-www/la-pubs/00313730.html
- National Security Archive, Gerard C. Smith briefing on congressional handling, 26 Oct 1979. Useful for contemporaneous statement that the U.S. had no evidence assigning responsibility to South Africa or Israel at that point. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/22327-15-gerard-c-smith-secretary-congressional

## Related warning-system context

- National Security Archive collection on 1979–1980 false warnings of Soviet missile attacks; use only as architecture comparison, not as evidence about the Vela mechanism. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/nuclear-vault/2020-03-16/false-warnings-soviet-missile-attacks-put-us-forces-alert-1979-1980
- William Odom to Brzezinski with Harold Brown memorandum on Nov 1979 NORAD false alarm; records rapid rejection through inconsistent warning indications. https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/document/27628-document-9-william-odom-zbigniew-brzezinski-norad-false-alarm-memorandum-secretary

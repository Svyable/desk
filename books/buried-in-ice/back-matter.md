# Back Matter

## Evidence note

This manuscript treats Camp Century as a collision between military engineering, classified strategic planning, alliance politics, environmental inheritance and paleoclimate science. Claims are separated by evidence class in `research/evidence-matrix.md`. Project Iceworm's existence and large-scale missile concept are documented; a deployed 600-missile arsenal is not. The PM-2A reactor operated at the site and major components were removed; reactor-associated liquid waste remained. Future waste remobilization is a modeled risk, not a claim of current harmful exposure. Past ice-free conditions at Camp Century are supported by peer-reviewed basal-sediment evidence; they do not provide a near-term date for local ice loss.

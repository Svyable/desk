# Contradictions and Incentive Audit

## Public science vs. classified strategy

**Apparent contradiction:** Camp Century was publicly documented, yet Project Iceworm was classified.

**Resolution rule:** do not call the public mission fake. Treat the camp as a genuine engineering/science facility whose capabilities also informed a classified strategic proposal. Recover publicity plans and planning memoranda separately.

## Danish knowledge

**Competing incentives:** U.S. officials had reason to preserve strategic freedom; Danish officials had alliance and domestic-policy incentives; later investigators had incentives to clarify or assign responsibility.

**Rule:** replace “Denmark knew/did not know” with document-by-document attribution: office, person, date, content and authority.

## Why Iceworm ended

**Candidate explanations:** deformation/maintenance burden; cost; service rivalry; missile-system competition; diplomacy/nuclear policy; changes in strategic doctrine.

**Rule:** do not select one monocause until JCS/Army decision records are reconstructed. Engineering trouble can be decisive without being exclusive.

## Reactor risk

**Distortion incentive:** the phrase “nuclear reactor under the ice” attracts attention and can swallow the rest of the waste inventory.

**Rule:** distinguish reactor/fuel/component removal from discharged reactor-associated liquid waste; compare radiological inventory with PCBs, diesel and sewage rather than assuming radiological dominance.

## Climate remobilization

**Distortion incentive:** future exposure makes compelling headlines.

**Rule:** preserve model spread, distinguish net ablation from direct surface exposure, and separate meltwater interaction from actual measured contaminant release.

## Paleoclimate

**Distortion incentive:** fossil plants can be converted into a claim that Greenland will soon be ice-free.

**Rule:** keep deep-time evidence and near-future forecasts separate. The basal sediment constrains past ice-sheet sensitivity; it does not specify a twenty-first-century deglaciation date.

## Secrecy and retrospective certainty

**Incentive:** once a classified program is exposed, later writers can read every public action as deliberate cover.

**Rule:** require evidence for intent. A visible scientific activity can be both intrinsically useful and strategically useful without every scientist participating in deception.

## Defeat conditions

The book's stronger thesis weakens materially if primary records show that:

- Camp Century engineering played no meaningful evidentiary role in Iceworm planning;
- the supposed 600-missile/130,000-km² architecture cannot be traced to authoritative records;
- Denmark received and explicitly approved the full strategic concept at the relevant stage;
- modern direct sampling shows the principal abandoned contaminants were removed or are absent;
- updated climate/site studies make remobilization physically implausible on meaningful governance timescales;
- the basal-sediment interpretation of past ice-free conditions is overturned by stronger evidence.

If any of these occur, revise the book. Do not protect the thesis from the archive.

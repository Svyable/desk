# Research Appendix

## Highest-value archival work

1. Recover the complete Project Iceworm planning packet and JCS/Army decision trail. Build a table of every system quantity—missiles, launch sites, tunnel kilometers, area, train concept, cost, construction rate, crew requirement—and cite the originating document rather than a later summary.
2. Reconstruct the 1959–1964 Camp Century engineering record from CRREL/USACE reports: trench dimensions, closure rates, deformation by structure, maintenance interventions, railway performance, reactor-building movement and the 1969 survey.
3. Recover PM-2A operating and decommissioning files, including fuel movements, liquid radioactive waste records, effluent measurements, reactor-vessel removal and destructive testing.
4. Reconstruct Danish/U.S. knowledge at the document level using Danish-language government investigations, diplomatic files, defense-agreement records and U.S. State/Defense archives.
5. Build a modern waste inventory that separates measured fact from historical estimate: steel/buildings, diesel, sewage/grey water, PCBs and other persistent chemicals, radiological liquids and any removed materials.
6. Acquire the radar data underlying modern Camp Century localization and reproduce the as-built-to-present coordinate transform using ice velocity and burial models.
7. Reconstruct the full custody chain of the 1966 ice and basal sediment core from Greenland to storage, transfer, rediscovery and modern analyses.
8. Audit every paper in the current Camp Century ice/sediment special issue. Do not allow the 2021 result to stand in for subsequent work.
9. Update surface-mass-balance projections with post-2016 observations and current model ensembles. Compare the original RCP8.5 framing with current emissions/scenario practice without pretending the 2016 paper was wrong for using the standards of its time.
10. Recover Greenlandic perspectives and official Greenland government positions on Camp Century, waste responsibility and remediation. Do not let Washington/Copenhagen bilateral archives erase local political agency.

## Manuscript expansion plan

The current draft is deliberately architecture-first and below Desk's one-shot length gate. Expansion must come from documents, calculations, actors and conflicting evidence—not repetition of the “ice moves” motif. Each chapter needs at least one primary-document sequence or quantitative reconstruction that changes what the reader knows.

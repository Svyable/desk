# Evidence Matrix

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- Camp Century was constructed by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers beginning in 1959 beneath the northwestern Greenland Ice Sheet.
- It functioned as a real Arctic engineering/scientific installation and was publicly described as such.
- PM-2A, a portable Army nuclear power plant, operated at Camp Century; major reactor components were later removed.
- The Army studied Project Iceworm as a large under-ice ballistic-missile basing concept; published reconstructions and Colgan et al. report a concept supporting 600 missiles beneath roughly 130,000 km².
- The full 600-missile system was not deployed.
- Tunnel deformation and ice/snow mechanics created substantial maintenance problems.
- Camp Century was abandoned with extensive physical infrastructure and wastes still in the ice.
- Colgan et al. 2016 modeled a plausible future transition from net accumulation to net ablation at the site under one high-emissions climate-model forcing, while another model maintained positive mass balance through 2100.
- IceBridge radar observed anomalies consistent with buried Camp Century infrastructure.
- The 1966 drilling recovered basal sediment beneath about 1.4 km of ice.
- Christ et al. 2021 found plant remains and multiple geochronological/geochemical lines of evidence showing ice-free, vegetated conditions at Camp Century during parts of the Pleistocene.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete reporting / testimony

- Exact Danish knowledge of the strategic intent behind Camp Century at each stage.
- The complete internal U.S. rejection chronology for Iceworm and the relative weights of ice deformation, cost, service rivalry, emerging Minuteman/Polaris alternatives, diplomacy and missile design.
- Exact current condition of fuel tanks, PCB-bearing materials, sumps and tunnel debris.
- Precise present-day geometry of every buried Camp Century structure.
- Detailed public/private framing decisions made around Camp Century publicity.

## Class 3 — disputed / not independently established

- Claims that an operational nuclear missile arsenal was emplaced under Greenland as part of Iceworm.
- Claims that Denmark as a whole knew nothing about Camp Century.
- Claims that Denmark fully consented to the eventual Iceworm weapons concept merely because it knew of the camp.
- Claims that the PM-2A reactor itself remains buried intact.
- Claims of current harmful contaminant release from the camp without direct site-specific measurements.
- Fixed-year claims that Camp Century will emerge from the ice.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- Camp Century as a three-timescale story: military planning time, infrastructure/waste time, and deep geologic/climate time.
- The camp as an unusually clean example of a prototype whose host medium becomes the dominant scaling constraint.
- “Abandonment as climate model”: every containment decision embeds an assumption about environmental stationarity.
- The deep core as a form of evidence that escaped the strategic intentions of the institution that funded its recovery.
- Secrecy having a shorter institutional half-life than physical residues.

## Promotion rules

A Class 2 or 3 claim moves upward only with document-level provenance, direct measurement, authenticated testimony with corroboration, or multiple independent primary records. Vividness is not evidence. Repetition in secondary accounts is not independence.

# Source Log

## Primary / authoritative spine

1. William Colgan et al., “The abandoned ice sheet base at Camp Century, Greenland, in a warming climate,” *Geophysical Research Letters* 43 (2016), 8091–8096. Open access: https://doi.org/10.1002/2016GL069688
   - Core source for site history, modeled waste depths, estimated physical/chemical/biological/radiological inventories, radar profiles, treaty language, and future surface-mass-balance scenarios.
   - Important boundary: the paper models possible future remobilization pathways; it does not document present harmful release from the buried site.

2. Andrew J. Christ et al., “A multimillion-year-old record of Greenland vegetation and glacial history preserved in sediment beneath 1.4 km of ice at Camp Century,” *PNAS* 118(13) (2021), e2021442118. https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2021442118 ; PubMed https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33723012/
   - Core source for basal sediment stratigraphy, plant macrofossils, biomarkers, cosmogenic nuclides, luminescence constraints and evidence of past ice-free/vegetated conditions.
   - Boundary: deep-time instability does not supply a date for future local deglaciation.

3. NASA GISS publication record for Christ et al. 2021: https://www.giss.nasa.gov/pubs/abs/ch04310e.html
   - Authoritative summary of the Camp Century basal-sediment result.

4. U.S. Army, Office of the Chief of Engineers, Nuclear Power Division, *Construction of the Army Nuclear Power Plant PM-2A at Camp Century, Greenland: Final Report* (1961).
   - Scanned copy accessible via Wikimedia Commons: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Construction_of_the_Army_nuclear_power_plant_PM-2A_at_Camp_Century,_Greenland-_Final_report_-_USACE-p266001coll1-3968.pdf
   - Use for reactor installation, logistics, plant architecture and period engineering claims. Recover an Army/USACE or DTIC/OSTI-hosted copy before final citation pass where possible.

5. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, Army reactor-program materials identifying PM-2A as Camp Century and describing its decommissioned status. Example USACE presentation: https://www.nab.usace.army.mil/Portals/63/docs/Environmental/HealthPhysics/SM-1A/FINAL_SM-1A_FGA_Community_Meeting_23_Apr_2019.pdf
   - Boundary: program summary, not a substitute for PM-2A operating/decommissioning files.

6. Copernicus special issue, “The Camp Century ice and sediment core: new science from a 1966 core that touched the base of the Greenland ice sheet.” https://cp.copernicus.org/articles/special_issue1255.html
   - Current research cluster for the core's scientific afterlife; includes 2026 work and should be audited paper by paper during expansion.

7. Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, “Greenland landscape history preserved under ice sheet,” March 15, 2021. https://www.llnl.gov/article/47381/greenland-landscape-history-preserved-under-ice-sheet
   - Institutional summary and researcher quotations around the basal-sediment findings.

## Historical / declassification work to recover

   - Frequently cited historical reconstruction of Iceworm. Recover complete publication and underlying citations before freezing missile-system details.

9. Danish Institute for International Studies / predecessor Danish foreign-policy investigations into Greenland nuclear policy and the 1990s disclosure of Project Iceworm.
   - Priority: original Danish report, appendices and declassified U.S./Danish documents, not later press summaries.

10. 1951 Agreement between the United States and Denmark concerning the defense of Greenland.
   - Recover authenticated treaty text and later amendments/interpretive materials. Colgan et al. reproduce the property/disposal relevance but final manuscript should cite the treaty directly.

11. U.S. Joint Chiefs / Department of Army Iceworm planning records, 1959–1963.
   - Priority questions: exact 600-missile architecture, 130,000-km² figure, Iceman missile assumptions, launch-site count, mobility concept, cost estimates, service rivalry, rejection chronology.

12. CRREL / USACE Camp Century engineering series: trench closure, snow mechanics, railway, maintenance, site surveys, 1969 revisit.
   - Build document-level index by report number, date and author.

13. NASA Operation IceBridge radar data over Camp Century (2011, 2014 and subsequent observations).
   - Recover actual flight lines and radar products used by Colgan et al.; distinguish tunnel-scale interpretation from later public imagery.

## Secondary leads, not final authority

14. Major press histories of Iceworm and Camp Century can be used to locate documents and participants, but memorable numbers must be traced back to primary records.

## Claim discipline

- Never say 600 missiles were deployed; 600 is a planning figure.
- Never say the reactor core was abandoned; principal reactor components were removed.
- Never turn the 2016 model into a fixed exposure date.
- Never turn Pleistocene ice-free evidence into a near-term forecast.
- Never infer Danish cabinet knowledge from the presence of Danish liaisons at Camp Century.
- Never treat radar reflections as chemical sampling.

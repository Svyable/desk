# Notes on evidence and further reading

This manuscript treats magnetoreception as a stack of separate claims rather than one confidence score. A controlled behavioral response to a manipulated field can establish magnetic sensitivity without identifying the receptor. A magnetically sensitive molecule can establish physical capability without establishing in-vivo use. An anatomical structure can contain magnetic material without being neural. A neural response can exist without conscious perception or navigational utility.

The working research trail lives in `../research/source-log.md`, with claim classes in `../research/evidence-matrix.md`, chronology in `../research/chronology.md`, contradiction/incentive notes in `../research/contradictions.md`, and the frozen expansion/release work in `../research/publication-gate.md`.

The most important starting sources are Henrik Mouritsen’s 2018 *Nature* review of long-distance navigation; P. J. Hore and Mouritsen’s 2016 *Annual Review of Biophysics* treatment of radical-pair magnetoreception; Xu et al.’s 2021 *Nature* paper on European-robin CRY4; Engels et al.’s 2014 *Nature* electromagnetic-noise experiment; Treiber et al.’s 2012 *Nature* macrophage correction; Brothers and Lohmann’s 2015 *Current Biology* geomagnetic-imprinting paper; and Wang et al.’s 2019 *eNeuro* human EEG experiment.

The manuscript intentionally does not use patents as evidence of efficacy, military research funding as evidence of secret capability, or the presence of magnetic minerals as evidence of a receptor. It also does not infer generalized human electromagnetic-health effects from specialized avian sensory disruption.

## Evidence-class key

**Class 1 — verified/declassified/documented:** directly established behavioral, anatomical, biochemical or published experimental facts used within the scope actually demonstrated.

**Class 2 — credible but incomplete:** replicated or serious evidence whose mechanism, generality, ecological importance or causal chain remains incomplete.

**Class 3 — disputed/not established:** claims with meaningful historical or current proponents but insufficient independent support, failed key anatomy, inconsistent replication, or an evidentiary leap beyond the underlying experiment.

**Class 4 — speculative synthesis/open conjecture:** integrative possibilities the book uses to generate testable questions, not to announce facts. The main Class 4 synthesis is that magnetic compass and magnetic map functions may be served by physically distinct receptor channels and may not be homologous across all taxa.

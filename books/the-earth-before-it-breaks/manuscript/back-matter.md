# Back Matter

This manuscript treats seismo-electromagnetic coupling as an open geophysical research field and reliable short-term electromagnetic earthquake prediction as unestablished. It separates physical mechanism, event association, population-level statistics and operational forecasting throughout.

The documentary spine runs from historical and peer-reviewed work on earthquake lights and rock electrification through the VAN controversy, USGS reanalysis of the Loma Prieta magnetic-precursor claim, the Parkfield Prediction Experiment, France's DEMETER mission and the ongoing China–Italy CSES program.

The book's core thesis would need substantial revision in either direction if a preregistered prospective system demonstrated durable forecasting skill above conventional seismic models across independent regions, or if the principal laboratory and population-level effects discussed here failed independent replication under appropriate controls.

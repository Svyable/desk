# Source Log — The Earth Before It Breaks

## Primary / official and high-value sources

1. U.S. Geological Survey, Geomagnetism Program, **Science / Geomagnetism and Earthquake Prediction**. Current USGS position: no convincing evidence of electromagnetic precursors despite decades of research. https://www.usgs.gov/programs/geomagnetism/science

2. U.S. Geological Survey, Thomas, Love & Johnston (2009), **On the reported magnetic precursor of the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake**. Concludes the canonical Corralitos ULF anomaly was sensor-system malfunction. https://www.usgs.gov/publications/reported-magnetic-precursor-1989-loma-prieta-earthquake ; DOI 10.1016/j.pepi.2008.11.014

3. U.S. Geological Survey, Roeloffs & Langbein (1994), **The earthquake prediction experiment at Parkfield, California**. https://www.usgs.gov/publications/earthquake-prediction-experiment-parkfield-california ; DOI 10.1029/94RG01114

4. U.S. Geological Survey, **The Parkfield, California, Earthquake Experiment**, including the September 28, 2004 M6.0 event. https://earthquake.usgs.gov/learn/parkfield/index.php

5. Savage (1993), **The Parkfield prediction fallacy**, Bulletin of the Seismological Society of America. USGS record: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/parkfield-prediction-fallacy ; DOI 10.1785/BSSA0830010001

6. CNES, **DEMETER** mission history. Official mission description and explicit caution that statistical perturbations did not establish individual earthquake prediction. https://cnes.fr/projets/demeter

7. China Seismo-Electromagnetic Satellite / Limadou collaboration, mission overview. CSES-01 in 2018 and CSES-02 in 2025; science objectives include seismic-ionospheric correlations. https://www.cseslimadou.it/

8. Chinese Academy of Sciences, National Space Science Center, **NSSC’s High-Precision Magnetometer Successfully Launched Aboard CSES-02**, June 30, 2025. https://english.nssc.cas.cn/news/202506/t20250630_1046452.html

## Research priorities

Build an event-level database separating prospective forecasts from retrospective associations; recover original VAN prediction logs and independent scoring debates; reconstruct DEMETER anomaly-selection rules from methods papers; compare CSES results with solar/geomagnetic nulls; quantify positive-hole transport under natural hydration, temperature and lithology; and design a preregistered temporally held-out multimodal forecasting challenge.

# Evidence Ledger

This ledger prevents the manuscript from turning observations, interpretations, simulations and engineering demonstrations into one undifferentiated story.

## Class 1 — verified / documented / directly measured

- Surveyor 5, 6 and 7 mission-era literature contains post-sunset horizon-brightness observations discussed as lunar horizon glow.
- Apollo 17 crew members recorded/sketched high-altitude lunar horizon-glow observations from orbit.
- Lunar surfaces and grains can charge under ultraviolet illumination and plasma exposure; the underlying photoemission and plasma-current physics is established.
- Micrometeoroid impacts continuously generate lunar ejecta.
- LADEE/LDEX directly measured a permanent asymmetric lunar dust cloud whose spatial/temporal structure is consistent with micrometeoroid impact ejecta.
- The dedicated 2015 LDEX terminator search reported no evidence, within its instrument/model sensitivity, for the canonical ~0.1 μm electrostatically lofted population over approximately 3–250 km altitude.
- LADEE mission optical results did not reveal an effect corresponding to a human-eye-visible horizon glow at the classic Apollo level.
- LADEE UVS data have been analyzed as evidence for a tenuous dynamic nanodust population; this is an optical inference in a different size regime from individually detected LDEX grains.
- Laboratory experiments have demonstrated dust charging and mobilization under vacuum ultraviolet, plasma and electric-field conditions.
- A 2025 lunar-surface technology demonstration on Firefly’s Blue Ghost Mission 1 showed NASA’s electrodynamic dust shield removing regolith from selected surfaces using engineered electric fields.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Some Surveyor horizon brightness was produced by forward scattering from near-surface lunar dust.
- The timing/morphology of Surveyor observations reflects electrical mobilization around sunlight-shadow boundaries rather than another optical or local spacecraft effect.
- Some Apollo surface dust-detector anomalies corresponded to naturally moving charged grains.
- Natural microcavity/patch charging produces enough grain release on the Moon to create a measurable near-surface flux.
- Combined solar-wind plasma and UV illumination enhances natural dust mobilization in ways comparable to the 2025 laboratory result.
- Nanodust populations experience significant electromagnetic transport after impact release or electrostatic detachment.
- Near-surface electrical motion produces long-timescale particle sorting even when it contributes negligibly to the orbital dust cloud.

## Class 3 — disputed, overextended or not independently confirmed

- Surveyor directly measured electrostatically levitating grains. It measured light; the particle population and source mechanism were inferred.
- Apollo orbital glow uniquely establishes a high-altitude lunar dust population.
- The classic dense electrostatic dust fountain extends persistently through tens of kilometers at the abundance originally inferred from Apollo-era optical models.
- Any laboratory observation of dust mobilization validates the Apollo high-altitude interpretation.
- LADEE’s discovery of a permanent lunar dust cloud confirms electrostatic levitation. The measured cloud is primarily impact-generated.
- LADEE’s high-altitude null proves electrostatic dust motion never occurs on the lunar surface.
- The 2025 electrodynamic dust-shield demonstration establishes natural field strengths or natural dust fluxes. It establishes engineered mitigation performance under its test conditions.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- The historical “lunar horizon glow” category fused more than one real population or phenomenon: near-surface electrical dust, impact ejecta, nanodust and an optical background/artifact may all have contributed to different observations.
- The global terminator matters mainly by changing the statistical distribution of smaller shadow-boundary microfields rather than by acting as one macroscopic electric cliff.
- Natural electrostatic dust transport may be geologically meaningful through cumulative millimeter-centimeter hops even if it is optically invisible at orbital altitude.
- Future lunar infrastructure could generate artificial electrostatic dust regimes that exceed natural background locally through large shadows, conductive structures, triboelectric charging and power systems.
- A multi-site long-duration lunar environmental network could convert dust from an anomaly topic into an operational forecast variable analogous to weather.

## Claims that would change class with one decisive dataset

- **Natural near-surface launch flux** moves from Class 2 to Class 1 if modern in situ stereoscopic particle tracking shows repeatable grain launches synchronized with measured electric-field changes under controls that exclude vibration, impacts and thermal disturbance.
- **Surveyor dust interpretation** strengthens if measured natural near-surface populations, propagated through exact Surveyor geometry and realistic grain scattering, reproduce the recorded brightness quantitatively.
- **Apollo orbital dust interpretation** strengthens only if the measured population also predicts the high-altitude optical glow without violating LDEX/LRO/other modern upper limits.
- **Microfield geological transport** strengthens if long-duration before/after surface imaging shows size-selective redistribution correlated with electrical forcing rather than impact exposure.

## Discipline rule

Evidence does not flow uphill automatically. Class 1 evidence that charging exists does not promote a Class 3 high-altitude fountain to fact. A Class 1 high-altitude null does not demote every Class 2 near-surface transport mechanism to false. Each claim remains attached to its own size, altitude, source and instrument regime.

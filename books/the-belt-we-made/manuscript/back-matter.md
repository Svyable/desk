# Back Matter

## A note on evidence

This book distinguishes a measured artificial radiation belt from the strategic claims built around it. Operation Argus is unusually well anchored because multiple evidence systems converge: declassified operational history, contemporary satellite measurements, later NASA technical reviews and treaty records. The conclusion that Argus produced trapped artificial electrons is substantially stronger than claims about the practical efficacy of a radiation-belt weapon.

Likewise, spacecraft failures after Starfish are handled component by component. Telstar’s command failure has a published engineering attribution to enhanced radiation. Ariel 1’s solar-cell degradation is strongly linked in NASA technical literature to trapped Starfish electrons, while its tape-recorder failure had serious pre-existing reliability problems and is not treated here as a clean radiation casualty.

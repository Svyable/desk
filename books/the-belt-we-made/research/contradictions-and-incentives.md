# Contradictions and Incentives Audit

## 1. Pre-test anticipation vs post-test surprise

**Tension:** Contemporary U.S. reporting says trapped electrons were anticipated before Starfish, yet the artificial belt proved intense and persistent enough to damage spacecraft.

**Do not resolve by saying:** "Scientists had no idea what would happen."

**Better formulation:** The mechanism was anticipated; magnitude, persistence, and coupled spacecraft consequences were not bounded well enough.

## 2. Satellite casualty counts

Modern institutional summaries variously refer to three satellites seriously affected, several damaged, or roughly ten damaged/lost in the artificial radiation environment.

**Incentives that can inflate counts:** public communication, EMP advocacy, retrospective simplification.

**Incentives that can deflate counts:** narrow legalistic causation thresholds, desire to isolate only directly demonstrated component failures.

**Rule:** treat named case files as stronger than aggregate retrospective totals.

## 3. Hawaiian electrical effects

Streetlight failures are documented and later technical work supports HEMP attribution for specific circuits.

**Do not convert:** specific failures into proof of a destroyed or near-destroyed island grid.

**Do not dismiss:** the failures merely because the overall grid remained functional.

## 4. Test objectives

Fishbowl had national-security objectives involving EMP, auroras and communications effects. Scientific measurements were intertwined with military goals.

**Do not convert capability evidence into intent evidence:** creation of an artificial belt does not prove anti-satellite denial was Starfish's singular or dominant operational purpose.

## 5. Treaty causation

The LTBT followed the 1962 atmospheric/high-altitude test series rapidly.

**Strong counterweight:** atmospheric fallout politics, verification constraints, the Cuban Missile Crisis, long-running arms-control negotiations and superpower strategy all materially contributed.

**Rule:** say the treaty closed the experimental window, not that Starfish caused the treaty.

## 6. Present-day EMP extrapolation

Historical evidence is often recruited into modern infrastructure-risk arguments.

**Audit question:** is the claim a measured 1962 effect, a validated modern model, or an extrapolation from one to the other?

Keep those categories separate.

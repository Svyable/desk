## Central defeat condition

The book's core thesis would be weakened if primary records showed that the persistent artificial belt was both fully anticipated in magnitude/lifetime and operationally inconsequential to spacecraft. Current records point the other way: trapping was anticipated, but intensity/persistence and spacecraft consequences materially exceeded the comfortable pre-test expectation.

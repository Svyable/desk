## Thesis

In 1958, only months after the Van Allen belts were discovered, the United States secretly detonated three nuclear devices above the South Atlantic to see whether Earth’s magnetic field could be deliberately loaded with energetic electrons. It could. Operation Argus therefore belongs simultaneously to nuclear-weapons history, the birth of magnetospheric physics, the early satellite age, and the prehistory of deliberate planetary engineering. Its larger lesson is uncomfortable: humans learned that near-Earth space was an environment at almost the same moment they learned they could alter it.

## Alleged UAP material — laboratory controls

- Oak Ridge National Laboratory / AARO, **Synopsis: Analysis of a Metallic Specimen** — https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/Information%20Papers/ORNL-Synopsis_Analysis_of_a_Metallic_Specimen.pdf — primary public technical synopsis for the layered magnesium-zinc specimen publicly associated with a purported 1947 crash and claimed bismuth/terahertz-waveguide/antigravity behavior. ORNL used optical microscopy, CT, SEM-EDS, TEM/STEM-EDS, diffraction, bulk/trace chemistry and isotope-ratio mass spectrometry. The measured bismuth was mixed/co-located with lead in repeated defective/nanocrystalline bands rather than the pure single-crystal architecture required by the proposed waveguide model. Magnesium and lead isotope results supported terrestrial origin. The long chain of custody remained unverifiable; this is therefore a strong negative test of the specimen received, not proof about every alleged 1947 material.
- AARO, **Supplement to ORNL's Analysis of a Metallic Specimen** — linked from https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Records/ — official historical/contextual interpretation of the same sample. Treat sponsor interpretation separately from ORNL measurements; independent replication remains the strongest next step.
- Oak Ridge National Laboratory / AARO, **Synopsis: Analysis of an Aluminum Specimen** — https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/Information%20Papers/ORNL_ANALYSIS_OF_AN_ALUMINUM_SPECIMEN.pdf — primary public technical synopsis for a second sample associated with a claimed mid-1990s Ohio UAP event. Multiple methods found a conventional near-eutectic aluminum-silicon casting alloy (~86 wt% Al, ~12 wt% Si), standard intermetallic phases/shrinkage porosity, slow-cooling structure and no anomalous gamma emission. Used in Ch 13 as a negative-control case showing how microstructure can reveal ordinary manufacturing history even when the origin story is extraordinary.

Again, collection presence authenticates the archival object’s custody at Rice, not every claim Stone made about program purpose.

## Source discipline

Patents are evidence that somebody filed a claim, not that a mechanism works. Congressional testimony is evidence of testimony, not physical proof. Official AARO conclusions are evidence of an official investigation and its stated results, not a logical demonstration that no undiscovered program can exist. Declassified foreign-materiel programs establish precedent for secret recovery/exploitation of terrestrial adversary technology, not non-human origin. Laboratory characterization answers what the tested sample is and does; it cannot repair an absent historical chain of custody.

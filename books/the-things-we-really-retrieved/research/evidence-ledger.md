# Evidence Ledger

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- Project AZORIAN existed; CIA led a covert effort to recover K-129 using the Hughes Glomar Explorer cover and achieved partial recovery in 1974.
- The Air Force secretly acquired and exploited Soviet-designed MiGs in HAVE DOUGHNUT/HAVE DRILL and later flew MiGs operationally in CONSTANT PEG.
- Foreign Technology Division was a real U.S. Air Force organization; NASIC preserves that technical-intelligence lineage.
- A Foreign Materiel Exploitation Squadron exists in the official Air Force historical lineage.
- U.S. agencies pursued recovery/analysis of foreign or unidentified descended space hardware under historical Moon Dust/Blue Fly-related activities; surviving declassified diplomatic traffic includes terrestrial space-debris recoveries.
- Project BLUE BOOK records are declassified at NARA; 701 of 12,618 cases were left unidentified under the Air Force’s historical accounting.
- David Grusch testified publicly under oath on July 26, 2023 and made crash-retrieval/reverse-engineering allegations.
- AARO Historical Record Report Volume I was published in 2024 and states AARO found no empirical evidence of extraterrestrial technology recovery/reverse engineering.
- KONA BLUE existed as a proposed Prospective Special Access Program concept; according to released DHS/AARO documentation it was never approved or funded and received no material.
- ORNL analyzed an alleged crash-related metallic sample under AARO tasking; AARO reported it as terrestrial alloy without extraordinary demonstrated properties.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete reporting / testimony

- Grusch’s claims that direct witnesses provided him classified evidence of a longstanding recovery program. Public evidence establishes that he made the claims and had relevant government roles; the central material allegations remain unavailable for independent public verification.
- Some historical witness accounts tying unusual debris movements to technical-intelligence channels may contain accurate observations while provenance remains unresolved.
- Claims that specific contractors hosted deeply compartmented exploitation work may be institutionally plausible where supported by employment/access records but remain incomplete without program documentation or material custody.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- U.S. possession of intact or partial non-human vehicles.
- Recovery of non-human biological remains.
- Successful reverse engineering of gravity control, reactionless propulsion, metamaterials, or energy systems from recovered UAP hardware.
- Any claim that Moon Dust or Blue Fly demonstrably recovered extraterrestrial craft or debris.
- Claims that named aerospace contractors currently hold alien hardware absent independently verifiable custody or technical evidence.
- Claims that classification level, SAP/SCI/Q access, or government denial itself proves exotic provenance.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- A genuine hidden UAP exploitation program would share measurable organizational features with documented foreign-materiel exploitation: custody, rapid transport, technical triage, integrator organizations, specialized contractors, safety systems, instrument evolution, succession, and downstream technical transfer.
- Secrecy can create a false evidentiary ecology in which authentic classified programs generate true fragments later misassembled into an extraterrestrial narrative.
- Long-lived exploitation should produce an archival “technical stratigraphy”: repeated generations of measurement against the same unresolved hardware questions.
- Independent convergence across logistics, materials provenance, facilities, witnesses, technical reports, and downstream capabilities is a stronger test than program-name authentication alone.

## Defeat conditions for the book’s synthesis

The institutional-comparison thesis weakens if historical secret programs show that decades-long hardware exploitation can occur without any recoverable logistical or technical traces after declassification. It also weakens if a verified non-human artifact is established through strong custody and independent analysis while having passed through no recognizable exploitation architecture. Conversely, the crash-retrieval hypothesis weakens as repeated high-quality searches of predicted interfaces fail and alleged samples resolve to terrestrial manufacture.

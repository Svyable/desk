# Research Priorities

1. Reconstruct Project AZORIAN’s contractor, procurement, facility, personnel, security, and records architecture from declassified primary sources, then use it as a quantitative control for what large secret hardware programs leave behind.
2. Build complete acquisition-to-training chains for HAVE DOUGHNUT, HAVE DRILL, HAVE FERRY, and CONSTANT PEG: source country, transport, receiving unit, technical exploitation, flight test, tactical dissemination.
3. Recover Moon Dust / Blue Fly source documents directly from NARA and Air Force holdings rather than relying on secondary mirrors; create a document-by-document provenance table with classification history and exact wording.
4. Map Foreign Technology Division/NASIC organizational changes, facilities, labs, and mission language from 1961 onward.
5. Build a machine-readable ledger of public crash-retrieval allegations: claimant, firsthand/secondhand status, date first stated, named program, named facility, named contractor, physical evidence, predicted records, contradictions, and resolution status.
6. Separate every alleged material specimen into two independent scores: provenance/custody quality and technical anomaly quality.
7. Reconstruct the complete ORNL/AARO metallic-sample analysis and seek raw analytical data where publicly releasable.
8. Obtain all released KONA BLUE proposal documents and trace exactly which claims predated the proposal, which were copied into it, and which later narratives mistakenly treated prospective language as historical possession.
9. Compare UAP alleged-program access descriptions against actual SAP/CAP administrative structures and historical classification guides without assuming that procedural irregularity proves exotic content.
10. Design a preregistered “warehouse test” for any future alleged artifact: scene documentation, custody, blind labs, terrestrial decoys, isotope/material criteria, replication thresholds, and publication rules.

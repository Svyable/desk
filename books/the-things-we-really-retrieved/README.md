Foreign Materiel Exploitation, Black Programs, and the Test for a Hidden UAP Program.

The working thesis is simple enough to be dangerous to both believers and skeptics: the United States has repeatedly recovered foreign machines, debris, weapons, aircraft, and spacecraft hardware in secret, moved them through compartmented logistics, rebuilt them, flew them, cut them apart, and exploited them technically. We therefore possess historical controls for what a real crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering apparatus looks like. Project AZORIAN, HAVE DOUGHNUT, HAVE DRILL, CONSTANT PEG, Foreign Technology Division, Moon Dust, Blue Fly, and later foreign-materiel organizations do not prove any recovery of non-human technology. They give us something better: a comparative anatomy with which claims of such a program can be tested.

This book asks what secrecy can actually hide, what it cannot avoid leaving behind, how provenance chains differ from rumor chains, and what evidence would change the conclusion either way.

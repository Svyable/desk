# Back Matter

## Chronology

**1947–1969** — U.S. Air Force UFO investigation programs culminate in Project BLUE BOOK; National Archives now holds the declassified records.

**1961** — Foreign Technology Division is activated at Wright-Patterson AFB; Air Force documentation from this period also describes Moon Dust/Blue Fly foreign-space-object and technical-intelligence recovery functions.

**Late 1960s** — HAVE DOUGHNUT and HAVE DRILL exploit secretly acquired Soviet MiG aircraft.

**1968** — Soviet submarine K-129 sinks in the Pacific; the United States locates the wreck.

**1970s** — CIA and Department of Defense mount Project AZORIAN behind the Hughes deep-sea-mining cover. Partial recovery occurs in 1974; public exposure follows in 1975.

**1977–1988** — CONSTANT PEG’s Red Eagles fly Soviet-designed aircraft for operational training.

**2008** — Foreign Materiel Exploitation Squadron is activated within the NASIC lineage.

**2009–2012** — DIA’s AAWSAP operates; later UAP institutional history becomes entangled with its participants and successors.

**2022** — AARO is established. Oak Ridge National Laboratory is tasked to characterize a publicly alleged UAP crash specimen.

**July 26, 2023** — David Grusch gives public sworn testimony before the House Oversight subcommittee, including crash-retrieval allegations.

**March 2024** — AARO publishes Historical Record Report Volume I, stating it found no empirical evidence of extraterrestrial technology recovery or reverse engineering and describing KONA BLUE as an unapproved prospective SAP.

**2024** — AARO releases KONA BLUE and ORNL-related public materials.

## Evidentiary rule

A classified program can be real without its rumored purpose being real. A witness can be credible without a secondhand claim being proved. A material can be unusual without extraordinary provenance. A government denial can be incomplete without the opposite allegation becoming true.

Those four sentences are the book’s lock.

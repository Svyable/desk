# Contradictions and Incentives

## Recurring tensions

- Spectacle vs mechanism: the red color attracts attention but can dominate explanations of a system whose important variables are invisible.
- Ancient vs isolated: marine ancestry and long residence do not imply perfect closure of every component.
- Analogue vs equivalence: astrobiology benefits from Mars/Europa comparisons, while outreach incentives reward stronger resemblance language than the mechanisms justify.
- Clean sampling vs zero contamination: clean-access engineering reduces contamination but cannot make provenance irrelevant.
- Organism presence vs metabolic assignment: sequence detection is not direct proof of a particular active pathway.

## Incentive audit

Researchers gain legitimate value from emphasizing planetary relevance because analogue science supports mission design and funding. Media gain attention from “blood,” “alien-like life,” and “ancient ecosystem” language. None of those incentives invalidates the science, but all can widen claims during translation from paper to headline. Later revisions should quote paper language before institutional or media paraphrase wherever the distinction matters.

# Evidence Matrix

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- Episodic red brine discharge occurs at Taylor Glacier terminus.
- Brine is hypersaline and iron-rich.
- Surface red material forms through iron transformation after exposure.
- Microbial communities occur in Blood Falls-associated brine system.
- Direct englacial brine sampling has been performed with clean-access technology.
- Geophysical work supports extensive conductive brines beneath the Dry Valleys.
- 2026 study reports a distinct marine micro-eukaryotic assemblage around the terminus mixing zone.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Exact geometry and connectivity of the source reservoir/network.
- Residence-time distribution of different water and solute fractions.
- Degree to which sampled taxa originate directly from end-member brine versus mixing-zone ecology.
- Relative contribution of microbial versus abiotic processes to specific iron/sulfur transformations.

## Class 3 — disputed / not established

- Perfect million-year biological isolation.
- A single simple subglacial lake as the complete source architecture.
- Any claim that Blood Falls demonstrates life on Mars, Europa or another world.
- Any unique biosignature based on red color or iron mineralogy alone.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- Blood Falls as an epistemic calibration site for how surface alteration changes messages from hidden habitats.
- Analogue value scaling with known differences rather than superficial resemblance.
- Contamination control as an explicit competing theory of biological origin.

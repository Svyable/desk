# Chronology

- 1911: Griffith Taylor expedition work brings the red-stained glacier terminus into scientific record.
- 1960s onward: iron chemistry replaces algal/blood-like folk explanations as the dominant physical explanation for color.
- 1990s–2000s: Dry Valleys long-term ecological and geochemical work develops marine-inundation / concentrated-brine history.
- 2009: *Science* paper reports a cold, dark, anoxic subglacial microbial ecosystem linked to Blood Falls discharge.
- 2015: *Nature Communications* geophysical work reports deep groundwater/brine systems and potential hydrological connectivity beneath the Dry Valleys.
- 2010s: IceMole clean-access sampling reaches englacial brine before surface discharge.
- 2022: Multi-technique mineralogy identifies amorphous iron-rich nanospheres and refines the simple “rust” account.
- 2026-08-03: *Nature Geoscience* publishes evidence for a distinct marine micro-eukaryotic assemblage associated with the Blood Falls terminus mixing zone.

# Chapter 17 clean-access / contamination audit

## Primary validation source

J. A. Mikucki et al., `Field-Based Planetary Protection Operations for Melt Probes: Validation of Clean Access into the Blood Falls, Antarctica, Englacial Ecosystem`, *Astrobiology* (2023).

- PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37962840/
- Full article: https://doi.org/10.1089/ast.2021.0102

## Frozen operational facts

- The IceMole was field-cleaned for biological sampling rather than merely described as sterile.
- Exterior bioburden after cleaning was reduced to levels comparable to most clean rooms and **below the bioburden of the ice surrounding the target**.
- The November 2014 access path was approximately **16.6 m long**, reaching approximately **15.2 m below the glacier surface** when a sharp conductivity rise indicated penetration into the brine conduit.
- Drilling melt was continuously pumped out.
- After conduit penetration, the team flushed approximately **2× the sampling-tubing volume** before englacial-brine collection.
- Approximately **100–150 L** of brine was collected for microbial-diversity and geochemical work.
- Potential microbial contaminants were tracked through the cleaning process.
- In the final englacial sample, identified cleaning-associated potential contaminants constituted approximately **0.063% of 16S rRNA gene amplicon sequences**.

## Interpretation boundary

### Safe

- Clean access was treated as a measurable contamination-reduction problem.
- Known cleaning-associated taxa showed very low carry-through into the final sequenced sample.
- The campaign supplies an unusually concrete contamination-control case for subglacial / Ocean World analogue sampling.

### Not safe

- `The sample was contamination-free.`
- `0.063% is the total true contamination fraction.`
- `Every low-abundance sequence is indigenous.`
- `Every contaminant taxon was represented in the cleaning reference samples.`
- `A clean probe alone establishes subglacial origin`; contamination can enter later through tubing, vessels, reagents or laboratory processing.

## 2026 mixing-zone boundary

Foley et al. 2026, *Nature Geoscience*, `Molecular evidence for a relict marine community in an Antarctic Dry Valleys subglacial brine-fed system`:

https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-026-02054-6

The Taylor Glacier terminus samples include red-hued ice, brine-influenced surface/shallow ice, mud/sediment and surrounding unsaturated moraine collected with sterile DNA/RNA-free procedures. These are powerful spatial-comparison data, but they should not be rewritten as direct recovery of organisms from a perfectly sealed ancient reservoir.

## Causal-route template for future life-detection claims

For each extraordinary taxon or biosignature, construct competing origin routes:

1. indigenous target habitat;
2. surface biological carry-through on probe exterior;
3. access-channel / meltwater transport;
4. tubing / pump carryover;
5. field handling / collection vessel;
6. reagent / extraction-kit contamination;
7. amplification / library-prep cross-talk;
8. reference-database misassignment;
9. modern environmental mixing into the sampled horizon.

A strong claim should identify a control or prediction that makes each serious alternative route less likely.

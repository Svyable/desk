# Defeat Conditions

The manuscript's central synthesis should weaken if any of the following become well supported:

1. The Blood Falls brine proves to be predominantly recent surface meltwater without substantial marine-derived or long-residence geochemical ancestry.
2. Clean-access sampling fails to confirm a distinct subsurface microbial community and prior biological signals are shown to arise chiefly from surface contamination.
3. Geophysical interpretations of extensive subsurface brines are overturned by direct drilling or higher-resolution surveys.
4. The 2026 marine micro-eukaryotic pattern disappears under independent replication, stronger contamination controls or improved reference databases.
5. Surface iron mineralogy is shown to preserve end-member brine composition without meaningful transformation, weakening the book's sample-journey argument.

These conditions are deliberately stronger than ordinary chapter-level corrections. The book should survive revised dates, taxa and reservoir geometry; it should not survive loss of the hidden brine-biochemistry system itself.

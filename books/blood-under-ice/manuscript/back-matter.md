# Back Matter

## Evidence posture

This manuscript separates documented measurement from inference. Blood Falls is a real iron-rich brine discharge. Microbial life has been detected in the system. Geochemical evidence supports marine ancestry. Geophysics supports broader subsurface brines. These facts do not prove complete isolation for millions of years, a single closed reservoir, or extraterrestrial habitability.

## Author's note

The site is unusually vulnerable to metaphor. Terms such as ancient seawater, isolated ecosystem, Mars analogue and relict marine community are useful only when their operational meaning stays attached. The research notes preserve those boundaries so later expansion can add detail without inflating certainty.

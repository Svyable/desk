## Thesis

Blood Falls is not merely a red Antarctic curiosity. It is a visible leak from a hidden cryogenic system: ancient marine-derived brine, iron chemistry, microbial metabolism, and subsurface hydrology persisting beneath cold ice. The system is valuable precisely because it is not a clean Mars or Europa analogue. It shows how easily habitability, biosignature, preservation, transport, and contamination can be collapsed into one seductive picture.

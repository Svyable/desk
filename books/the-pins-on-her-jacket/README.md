A refugee child from Prague becomes an American citizen, U.S. ambassador to the United Nations, and the first woman to serve as Secretary of State. The biography uses that extraordinary trajectory to examine belonging, ambition, democratic power, intervention, NATO, Bosnia, Kosovo, gender, signaling, and the moral confidence—and danger—of American idealism.

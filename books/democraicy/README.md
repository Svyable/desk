*Governing a World Where Machines Can Persuade, Decide, Negotiate, and Govern*

DemocrAIcy is a political-systems book about what happens when political intelligence becomes abundant: citizens have agents, campaigns can personalize persuasion at scale, corporations can automate lobbying, bureaucracies can automate decisions, and governments increasingly operate through machines that can read, recommend, negotiate, and act.

The working thesis is not government by artificial intelligence. It is a democracy in which humans remain sovereign while increasingly governing through, alongside, and against artificial agents.

## Working thesis

Artificial intelligence does not merely move political information faster. It can interpret information, generate arguments, administer rules, negotiate with other systems, and personalize communication at a scale no human institution can match.

The political project is therefore to make self-government more capable without allowing capability to become control. Machines may advise, administer, simulate, detect, summarize, and negotiate. Humans must retain legitimate sovereignty and the final veto.

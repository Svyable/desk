# AARO geometry audit — Ghost in the Radar

## Bounded purpose

This pass checks the opening chapter's use of AARO cases against AARO's current public case material. It does not treat AARO as an infallible adjudicator. The narrower question is whether the cases actually support the manuscript's distinction between a sensor signature, a physical object, reconstructed kinematics, and anomalous performance.

## Primary records checked

- AARO official imagery/case table: https://www.aaro.mil/UAP-Cases/Official-UAP-Imagery/
- AARO Puerto Rico case resolution, dated 20 March 2025: https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/case_resolution_reports/AARO_Puerto_Rico_UAP_Case_Resolution.pdf

## What the public record supports

AARO's own current case table contains materially different epistemic endpoints that the book should keep separate.

**PR-003 / Africa 2023.** The infrared footage contains an apparent heat signature with characteristics consistent with a physical object, but AARO says it cannot determine whether the signature comes from a physical source (emission or reflection), an environmental heat differential, or sensor-display error. The available data are insufficient to evaluate performance. This is a clean example of a measurement that has not yet earned an object or kinematics.

**PR-012 / Europe 2022.** AARO assesses with high confidence that a physical object is present, while calling its morphology, performance and behavior unremarkable and leaving attribution unresolved. This is a clean control against the common compression of `unresolved identity` into `anomalous performance`.

**Puerto Rico / Aguadilla.** AARO's 20 March 2025 resolution assesses with high confidence that the objects did not exhibit anomalous behavior or transmedium capability and with moderate confidence that they were a pair of sky lanterns. The case table says an Intelligence Community reconstruction of flight path and sensor look angle found two nearby objects traveling in a straight line at wind speed, rather than one object splitting or entering the water. The manuscript's methodological use is therefore supportable: apparent image motion and geometry can generate a much stronger story than reconstructed object motion.

## Important correction to Chapter 1

The chapter currently calls the insufficient-ontology example a **Middle East** case. The wording it paraphrases on AARO's current public table belongs to **PR-003, Africa 2023**. The separately listed `Middle East Object` is described by AARO as an unidentified object showing no anomalous behavior; it is not the case whose public description says the source could be thermal emission, reflection, environmental heat differential, or display error.

**Publication hold:** do not leave the Africa language attributed to the Middle East case. Correct the geography/case identifier when the manuscript branch is next revised.

## Strongest counterargument

These examples demonstrate that an official analytic office can distinguish signature, object and kinematics. They do **not** establish that AARO's individual reconstructions are correct. In particular, the Puerto Rico conclusion depends on reconstruction inputs and assumptions that should be audited rather than accepted because the conclusion is official. The book's method should therefore cut both ways: a geometry-based mundane resolution has to expose enough geometry to be reproduced, just as an extraordinary kinematic claim does.

That creates a useful symmetry without false equivalence. `AARO resolved it` is not a substitute for the reconstruction; `AARO left it unresolved` is not positive evidence of exotic performance.

## Quantitative follow-up

For Puerto Rico, extract from the resolution and any accompanying reconstruction package: aircraft trajectory, sensor look angle, assumed/object range, wind field, angular track, estimated ground speed, object separation, uncertainty, and the geometry supporting the no-water-entry conclusion. Record which quantities are measured and which are inferred. Reproduce at least the decisive kinematic relationship before the chapter treats the case as a worked control.

## Editorial consequence

The opening's strongest modern sequence is not a catalog of UAP cases but three different stopping points in the inference chain:

1. **Africa 2023:** signature without an established physical source.
2. **Europe 2022:** physical object established; behavior unremarkable; identity unresolved.
3. **Puerto Rico 2013 / resolution 2025:** spectacular apparent behavior narrowed by geometric reconstruction.

That sequence earns the book's object ladder with concrete cases instead of announcing it as a framework. It also supplies a harder standard for later chapters: every side has to show its work when it converts pixels or returns into motion.

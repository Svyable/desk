# Evidence ledger — The Body Is an Antenna

## Governing question

What information can a living system extract from weak electromagnetic fields, through what physical receiver, at what signal-to-noise ratio, and with what consequence?

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- **DARPA RadioBio:** official program page says the completed program sought to establish whether functional signaling via electromagnetic waves between biological cells exists and, if so, identify mechanism and information transferred. This is evidence of a research question, not a positive result. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/radiobio
- **RadioBio launch description (2017):** DARPA specified modeling, experimental tests, independent test beds and replication/confirmation goals. https://www.darpa.mil/news/2017/radiobio
- **Avian magnetoreception / cryptochrome:** serious experimental and theoretical literature supports radical-pair chemistry as a leading mechanism; exact in-vivo receptor architecture remains an active question. Start with Hore & Mouritsen 2016 and Xu et al., Nature 2021.
- **Arabidopsis cryptochrome RF response:** Albaqami et al., Scientific Reports 2020, reports 7 MHz RF effects on cryptochrome-dependent responses. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-67165-5
- **RF-controlled flavoprotein spin chemistry:** Schleicher et al., Nature Biotechnology 2026, reports RF manipulation of photogenerated spin-correlated radical pairs in flavoproteins, magnetic sensing and spatial photoluminescence modulation. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41587-026-03158-5
- **DARPA AMBIIENT:** official program seeks magnetic sensors capable of detecting biological signals in Earth’s ambient field. Useful instrumentation control; it does not establish biological RF communication. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/atomic-magnetometer-for-biological-imaging-in-earths-native-terrain

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Behavioral disruption of avian magnetic orientation by weak RF/noise: reconstruct experiment-by-experiment, including shielding, frequency dependence, replication disputes and urban electromagnetic-noise controls.
- Weak-field effects in mammalian cells: include only after mechanism, dosimetry and independent replication are extracted.
- Human magnetic-field responses: EEG/alpha and behavioral claims require preregistration/replication audit before promotion into manuscript fact.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Endogenous radio-frequency cell-to-cell communication carrying useful information.
- A robust conscious human magnetic sense.
- Operational “psychotronic” weapons inferred from weak-field biological mechanisms.
- Remote semantic writing into human cognition through low-intensity ambient RF.
- Magnetogenetic claims that depend on physically implausible ferritin heating or force without adequate energy accounting.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- **Receiver-first epistemology:** extraordinary EM claims become tractable when the receptor/transfer function is specified before the alleged transmitter.
- **Mechanism laundering:** a genuine low-level effect can lend unearned plausibility to a much larger capability claim.
- **Information ladder:** field → coupling → molecular effect → cellular effect → organism effect → discrimination → signaling → communication → control. Evidence cannot migrate upward without paying each transition.

## Quantitative extraction burden

For every important case record: field type; frequency/spectrum; amplitude at source and tissue; modulation; exposure geometry; distance; duration; polarization/orientation; shielding; temperature; vibration/acoustic controls; receptor candidate; predicted transfer function; molecular endpoint; cellular endpoint; organism endpoint; preregistered primary outcome; effect size; negative results; replication status; plausible noise floor; information rate if communication is claimed.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. RadioBio’s existence is documented; a positive demonstration of purposeful endogenous EM signaling is not thereby established.
2. Cryptochrome can participate in magnetic/RF-sensitive chemistry; that does not establish that cryptochrome is a general-purpose biological radio receiver.
3. Animal magnetoreception is real; the precise receptor architecture remains contested.
4. A weak field can matter when evolution/engineering supplies amplification; that fact makes link-budget analysis more necessary, not less.
5. An effect can be statistically reproducible and still carry essentially no useful information.

## Publication holds

- Do not write “DARPA discovered cells communicate by radio.”
- Do not convert a program goal into achieved capability.
- Do not call patents evidence of efficacy.
- Do not use “quantum” as a mechanism unless the relevant spin/chemical model is specified.
- Do not promote plant/protein RF effects into human neurological capability.
- Do not claim human magnetoreception without a replication-aware source audit.
- Do not describe military interest as confirmation.

## Strongest countermodel

The book may be underestimating how little energy is required when a biological receptor is exquisitely tuned and downstream amplification is enormous. Classical antenna intuition can fail at molecular scale because the relevant interaction may be spin chemistry, near-field coupling or another transduction pathway rather than far-field power capture. A sufficiently specific receptor can turn a physically tiny perturbation into a biologically meaningful state.

Counter-countermodel: sensitivity is not bandwidth. An exquisitely tuned compass can detect one low-dimensional environmental variable while remaining useless for arbitrary information transfer. Demonstrating a receptor may therefore strengthen the distinction between sensing and communication rather than erase it.

## Defeat condition

If independent laboratories establish a reproducible endogenous electromagnetic channel with prospectively specified transmitter states, spectrum, receiver molecule, selective receptor knockout/rescue, physically closed ordinary channels, and biologically meaningful information capacity, the book must stop treating purposeful cellular EM communication as unconfirmed. If externally generated fields can then address that channel to impose prospectively specified information-rich neural states at realistic stand-off geometry, the control boundary must move again.

## Source discipline

A paper showing magnetic sensitivity of purified CRY4 is evidence for molecular magnetic sensitivity, not proof that CRY4 is the complete in-vivo receptor. A paper showing alpha-band EEG changes is evidence for a neural response under the tested conditions, not conscious human navigation. Iron-bearing tissue is never treated as a receptor without anatomy, innervation, and causal evidence.

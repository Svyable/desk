## Premise

Artificial intelligence can make public administration dramatically cheaper. The danger is that governments treat every saved hour as a reason to remove another person, another layer of judgment, another reserve of institutional memory, until the state becomes technologically sophisticated and operationally hollow. This draft asks what austerity means when useful cognition is becoming abundant but judgment, legitimacy, maintenance, technical competence, and the capacity to act remain scarce.

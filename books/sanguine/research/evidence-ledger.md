# SANGUINE — evidence ledger

## Working rule

Never collapse these propositions:

1. an electromagnetic field exists;
2. an object or organism couples to it;
3. coupling produces a measurable biological response;
4. the response is adverse;
5. the adverse response occurs at actual Sanguine/Seafarer exposure conditions;
6. population-level harm follows.

Each arrow requires evidence.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

### NAVFAC program history

U.S. Naval Facilities Engineering Command historical chapter, *Engineering and Design*, NAVFAC history 1965–1974.

Public source: https://www.history.navy.mil/content/dam/nhhc/research/archives/naval-facilities-engineering-command-history-1965-1974/NAVFAC_1965-1974_Chapter_9.pdf

Supports: Sanguine site planning; Navy environmental R&D beginning in 1967; Wisconsin Test Facility operational in 1969; explicit study of telephone/power/fence interference and biological/ecological questions; official statement that studies through 1974 found no effects.

Hold: official summary is evidence of the Navy's conclusion, not a substitute for recovering underlying studies.

### GAO, Navy's Strategic Communications System, 5 April 1979

https://www.gao.gov/products/109021

Supports: Navy strategic rationale for ELF; GAO skepticism about necessity and capability; contemporary dispute over whether existing communications endangered submarine survivability.

Important: this is a strategic/operational audit, not biological evidence.

### GAO, Status of Project PISCES, 11 February 1977

https://www.gao.gov/products/lcd-77-319

Supports: PISCES existed as a proposed alternative to Seafarer and contemplated adapting the Pacific Intertie to radiate ELF.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Underlying Navy-sponsored biological experiments identified through the NAVFAC history but not yet reconstructed study-by-study.
- Contemporary academic and state-level reviews of Sanguine/Seafarer health and ecological claims, pending acquisition and methods audit.
- Participant recollections concerning design evolution, local opposition, and strategic urgency, usable only with dated corroboration.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Claims that Sanguine/Seafarer demonstrated or deployed behavioral control, mind influence, covert neurological manipulation, or weather modification.
- Claims of specific illnesses caused by ELF infrastructure without exposure reconstruction and causal evidence.
- Claims that biological research around Sanguine proves an undisclosed offensive bioeffects mission.

These may be discussed as provenance objects if they influenced public understanding. They are not evidence of the alleged capability.

## Class 4 — synthesis / conjecture

- Sanguine as a historical control case for the modern inference chain `field → interaction → effect → harm`.
- The proposal that documented secrecy plus real physical coupling creates an unusually fertile false-evidentiary ecology: later extraordinary claims can borrow credibility from true but weaker propositions.
- The idea that the project's deepest conflict was not science versus superstition but asymmetric risk ownership: strategic planners and residents were optimizing different losses.

## Contradiction audit

**Navy claim:** studies by end of 1974 showed no effects on humans, animals, plants, microorganisms.

**Required test:** recover study inventory, exposure parameters, endpoints, sample sizes, positive findings, null findings, replications, publication status, sponsor, and later reviews. Do not quote the umbrella conclusion as if it were a meta-analysis.

**GAO challenge:** questioned strategic necessity/capability.

**Required test:** recover Navy rebuttal and later operational evidence. A system entering service does not retrospectively prove every original necessity claim; cancellation or reduction would not prove technical impossibility.

## Incentive audit

- Navy: preserve a survivable command path; obtain authorization/funding; reduce environmental and political barriers; protect classified operational details.
- Contractors/labs: sustain research programs and contracts; produce publishable or decision-useful results.
- Local/state opponents: avoid involuntary exposure, land-use burden, interference, and uncertain externalities; political incentives can reward worst-case framing.
- Journalists/activists: secrecy and invisible fields reward dramatic narratives; legitimate documentary discoveries can be overextended.
- Later anomaly communities: Sanguine supplies authentic classified-program vocabulary that can be attached to unrelated claims.

## Defeat conditions

Central thesis must change if independently replicated, exposure-matched work establishes robust adverse effects at actual public exposure conditions and the manuscript's uncertainty framing understates them.

It must also change if recovered historical work shows the biological controversy lacked substantive evidence and was primarily political rhetoric. Do not manufacture symmetry.

## Primary-source acquisition queue

1. Complete NAVFAC Sanguine environmental-study bibliography.
2. Original Wisconsin Test Facility technical description and field-strength maps.
3. Sanguine/Seafarer EIS/EIS supplements and public comments.
4. Congressional authorization/appropriation hearings naming Sanguine, Seafarer and ELF.
5. Navy response to GAO 1979 strategic-communications critique.
6. PISCES engineering proposal and rejection record.
7. Peer-reviewed biological studies with exposure frequencies/strengths matching the system.
8. Independent replication/null literature and later systematic reviews.
9. State of Wisconsin and Michigan environmental/health records.
10. Operational ELF system closure/decommissioning records and stated reasons.
11. Contemporary Soviet/Russian ELF submarine-communications literature as comparator, without inferring equivalence from shared frequency band.

## Publication holds

- No `safe` or `dangerous` umbrella verdict until exposure-matched literature is reconstructed.
- No mind-control/weather-control discussion without claim provenance and explicit Class 3 labeling.
- No use of patents as evidence that a claimed bioeffect works.
- No claim that classification implies hidden biological purpose.
- No claim that Navy sponsorship invalidates a study without methodological reason.

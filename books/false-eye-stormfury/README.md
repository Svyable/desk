## Reader promise

STORMFURY appeared, more than once, to weaken hurricanes in the way its theory predicted. The observations did not later vanish. The causal interpretation did. Better measurements showed that unmodified hurricanes could spontaneously produce the same outer-eyewall formation and wind reduction, while the clouds also contained less supercooled liquid water than the proposed seeding mechanism required.

## Strongest competing explanation

The cheap retrospective story is that STORMFURY was bad science attached to a weather-control fantasy. The record is harder. Its mechanism was plausible by contemporary cloud-physics standards, the program imposed safety and target criteria, and it built an unusually capable airborne observing effort. The central problem was attribution in a system whose natural variability was not yet adequately known.

## Evidence classes

1. **Verified/documented:** program dates and agencies; seedings of Esther, Beulah, Debbie and Ginger; target criteria; recorded post-seeding wind changes; later observations of natural concentric eyewalls; and later measurements of hurricane cloud microphysics.
2. **Credible but incomplete/model-dependent:** causal contribution of seeding on individual days, reconstructed wind reductions, adequacy of particular seedings, and historical estimates of supercooled liquid water.
3. **Disputed/not established:** reliable operational hurricane weakening, Project Cirrus causing the 1947 track reversal, or later secret hurricane manipulation.
4. **Speculative synthesis/open conjecture:** STORMFURY as a general model for intervention studies in systems capable of generating the treatment signature on their own.

# Concentric Eyes

Concentric eyewalls are visually simple and dynamically inconvenient. A hurricane that appears to have one dominant ring of maximum winds can develop another outside it. The outer ring may strengthen, cut off inflow to the inner eyewall, and eventually replace it. During the transition, maximum winds often weaken while the wind field broadens.

For STORMFURY, this was almost too perfect. The intervention had been designed to create an outer convective ring that would compete with the inner eyewall. When unseeded storms were observed doing this spontaneously, the project's success criterion became ambiguous.

The historical irony is that better hurricane research made the resemblance undeniable. Improved aircraft radar, repeated penetrations and a larger observational record showed that concentric eyewalls were not exotic accidents attached to seeded storms. They were part of the life cycle of many intense hurricanes.

That does not make every old STORMFURY result meaningless. A seeded storm could, in principle, enter an eyewall replacement cycle earlier than it otherwise would have. The problem is proving that counterfactual. If the same structural transition is common in comparable untreated storms, a single before-and-after sequence cannot carry much causal weight.

The issue is timing. Suppose an outer eyewall forms six hours after seeding. Is six hours physically compatible with the proposed microphysical pathway? Probably. Is six hours also compatible with a natural eyewall cycle already developing? Also probably. Without independent markers tracing the silver-iodide perturbation through cloud-scale freezing, convection, ring formation and wind response, the storm-scale endpoint cannot tell the stories apart.

Modern experimental design often emphasizes mediators for exactly this reason. A treatment should not merely be followed by an outcome; measurements along the causal chain should behave as predicted. STORMFURY had pieces of that chain but not enough to exclude the endogenous alternative.

The concentric eyewall was therefore both the hoped-for product and the confounder. Nature had built the same apparatus inside the storm.

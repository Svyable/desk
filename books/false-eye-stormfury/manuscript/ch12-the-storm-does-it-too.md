# The Storm Does It Too

The decisive blow to STORMFURY was not a single failed seeding. It was recognition that hurricanes could manufacture the project's predicted response on their own.

Intense tropical cyclones sometimes develop a second ring of convection outside the original eyewall. That outer ring can strengthen while the inner eyewall weakens. The radius of maximum winds moves outward, the peak winds may drop, and the storm later reorganizes around the new eyewall. This is now recognized as an eyewall replacement cycle.

Read that sequence against STORMFURY's theory and the problem becomes obvious. The project wanted seeding to stimulate convection outside the eyewall, produce a new outer ring, displace the old eyewall and reduce maximum winds. Natural eyewall replacement supplied the same macroscopic signature without silver iodide.

This did not mean the seeding could never have nudged a storm. It meant the visible response no longer identified the cause. The treatment signature had poor specificity.

A diagnostic test can fail in exactly this way. It may respond to the target condition, but if it also responds frequently to something else, a positive result loses much of its force. STORMFURY had built an intervention around an outcome that later turned out to have an important natural confounder.

The discovery also changes how we read the early scientists. They were not necessarily seeing patterns that were absent. They were seeing a genuine hurricane behavior before its untreated prevalence and dynamics were adequately understood. The mistake lay in causal ownership.

That is why the episode travels so well beyond meteorology. Complex systems often contain endogenous processes that mimic intervention. Financial markets rebound after policy changes and sometimes without them. Patients improve after treatments and sometimes without them. Ecosystems reorganize after restoration and sometimes through internal succession. If the spontaneous pathway is poorly mapped, an intervention can inherit credit for nature's own move.

STORMFURY's false eye was real. The outer ring existed. The winds changed. The problem was deciding whose experiment the hurricane was running.

# The Ice Was Already There

The second major problem was less cinematic than concentric eyewalls and probably more fatal to the original mechanism.

Silver iodide can matter only where cloud water remains liquid below freezing. STORMFURY depended on enough supercooled liquid water being present in the right parts of hurricane convection that artificial ice nuclei could materially alter freezing and latent-heat release.

Later observations indicated that mature hurricane clouds already contained substantial natural ice and comparatively little of the supercooled liquid water the hypothesis needed. The atmosphere was not waiting with a large reservoir of unfrozen droplets for the aircraft to unlock.

That matters because a correct mechanism in the wrong environment is still an ineffective intervention. The seeding material did not become chemically absurd. The relevant microphysical pathway did not cease to exist. The question became whether the available substrate was large enough to produce a storm-scale difference.

This is a useful distinction in the history of technology. Some failed interventions are based on bad physics. Others use good physics with bad system assumptions. STORMFURY increasingly looked like the latter. The proposed lever existed, but the hurricane supplied less leverage than expected.

The finding also weakened post hoc explanations for mixed experimental days. If negative results could always be blamed on poor targeting or inadequate execution, the theory remained elastic. Measurements showing systematically limited supercooled water made the problem structural rather than merely operational.

By the 1980s, the two critiques reinforced each other. The intervention may have had less microphysical power than expected, while the storm itself could generate the same large-scale response naturally. One problem attacked the cause. The other attacked the specificity of the effect.

That combination is what finally makes the historical evidence persuasive. A skeptical reader does not have to believe that every apparent STORMFURY success was pure coincidence. It is enough to see that the claimed causal chain lost support at both ends.

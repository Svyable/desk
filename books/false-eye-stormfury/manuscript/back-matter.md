# Back Matter

## Evidence posture

This manuscript is **official-history-forward / atmospheric-physics-forward / experimental-design-forward / intervention-causality-cautious / conspiracy-claims-rejected-without-event-specific-evidence**.

The argument does not require every seeded hurricane to have been unaffected by seeding. It requires only the narrower conclusion supported by NOAA's retrospective scientific account: the program did not establish reliable hurricane modification because the proposed microphysical leverage was weaker than expected and natural hurricane evolution could imitate its expected storm-scale signature.

## What remains

This first-pass manuscript is structurally complete at eighteen chapters but remains materially below Desk's full-book length gate. The next expansion must come from primary documents and quantitative reconstruction rather than repeating the attribution thesis: page-index the 1985 scientific chronicle; recover the original Esther, Beulah and Debbie experiment papers and flight tables; reconstruct silver-iodide delivery mass, targeting geometry, temperatures and liquid-water assumptions; build seeded-day versus natural-eyewall timelines; recover contemporaneous legal and intergovernmental correspondence after Project Cirrus; quantify candidate-storm scarcity and the proposed sample-size problem; reconstruct instrument development; and compare historical causal standards with modern intervention-analysis methods.

A whole-book anti-slop and editorial pass remains required after that expansion. No publication-ready claim is made here.

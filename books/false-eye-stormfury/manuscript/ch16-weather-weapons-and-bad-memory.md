# Weather Weapons and Bad Memory

Weather modification attracts stories faster than it accumulates controls. That was true before STORMFURY and remains true after it.

A documented federal hurricane-seeding program creates fertile ground for claims that later destructive storms were manipulated secretly. The existence of STORMFURY proves only that the United States openly funded and conducted experimental hurricane modification research for years. It does not establish that any later storm was controlled, steered or weaponized.

The distinction sounds elementary until classified-program culture enters the argument. Once a government has tried something unusual, absence of public evidence for a later capability can be reframed as evidence of secrecy. That move makes the claim difficult to falsify. It also launders a documented historical program into support for events the program record does not describe.

STORMFURY itself is a poor foundation for that inflation. Its history shows the opposite of a mature hidden capability. The project struggled to find eligible storms, produced mixed experimental responses, depended on a microphysical premise later weakened by measurements, and was undermined by natural hurricane dynamics. Nothing in the public record justifies converting those facts into operational control of hurricane tracks or intensity.

This does not mean governments disclose everything or that military interest in environmental modification never existed. It means claim scale should track evidence scale. A budget line can establish funding. An experiment can establish an attempt. A patent establishes a filing. Testimony establishes that a witness said something. None of those facts independently proves operational effectiveness.

Weather is especially vulnerable to retrospective pattern-making because destructive events already contain extraordinary structure. A rapidly intensifying cyclone, unusual track, radar feature or abrupt weakening can look engineered if the observer begins with that expectation. The natural system supplies endless anomalies.

STORMFURY's own scientific failure offers the corrective. Before attributing a weather pattern to intervention, ask how often nature produces the same pattern untreated. That question ended one government modification hypothesis. It remains useful against the mythology that grew around it.

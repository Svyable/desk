# Chapter 10 research — pronatalist policy and migration

Covers South Korea's cumulative pronatalist spending (stated as an explicit $210-270B range rather than a single figure, given conflicting outlet tallies), Hungary's pronatalist policy suite and its 2021 peak (1.59) followed by 2024 decline (1.39), Germany/Japan's contrasting immigration strategies, the 2025 German federal election AfD result and Japan's Sanseito, OECD/IMF dependency-ratio data, and Japan's Silver Human Resource Centers. Falsification note: weakens if a single, precisely documented pronatalist spending figure emerges that is much smaller than the range cited, undermining the "large spending, small effect" argument, or if a future election cycle shows anti-immigration sentiment receding rather than continuing to climb in both Germany and Japan.

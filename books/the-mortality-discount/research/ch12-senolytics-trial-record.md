# Chapter 12 research — the senolytic drug trial record

Covers Unity Biotechnology's UBX0101 Phase 2 failure (Aug 2020, ~63-66% single-day stock decline), independent post-mortems attributing the failure to possible delivery/bioavailability problems rather than a disproof of the underlying senescence hypothesis, and five years of dasatinib+quercetin human trials (2019 Mayo Clinic diabetic-kidney-disease pilot; osteoporosis Phase 2, 2024; mild cognitive impairment/Alzheimer's, 2025) each showing small, mixed, underpowered results. The Hayflick limit (1961) is used as general, well-established scientific background for why cellular senescence exists at all. Falsification note: weakens if a larger, well-powered senolytic trial for any indication reports a clear, statistically significant, clinically meaningful benefit.

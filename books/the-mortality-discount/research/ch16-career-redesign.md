# Chapter 16 research — labor markets, AI, and the extended career

New sources beyond material already cited in earlier chapters: the OECD's 2025 Employment Outlook framing AI as a tool to offset aging-driven workforce contraction, and Indeed Hiring Lab's May 2026 "Great Mismatch" analysis finding the labor segments shrinking fastest from aging (elder care, manual trades) are not the segments current AI tools are effective in. The chapter otherwise draws on Japan's Silver Human Resource Centers (cited in ch10) and Gratton/Scott/Carstensen (cited in ch15).

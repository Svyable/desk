# Chapter 18 research — the genkai shūraku sequence in depth

Extends Chapter 9's genkai shūraku material (Ōno 1991 coinage, Nagoro case study, already sourced in ch09's research files) with additional institutional detail: road-maintenance and funeral mutual-aid collapse, school-consolidation feedback loops, and land-inheritance/abandoned-property dynamics. The Yurihonjo, Akita Prefecture case-study reference (Sustainability Science) is drawn from the same demographics research pass as Chapter 9. The furusato nōzei "hometown tax" program and "selective and concentrated" regional planning concept are presented as general, well-documented features of Japanese rural policy; a specific primary Japanese-government source for each was not independently pinned down in this research pass and would benefit from direct verification before any future revision relies on more specific figures.

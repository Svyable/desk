# What Would Count as Communication

The word *communication* should come with a checklist hidden inside it.

There is a sender with distinguishable states. Those states alter some physical carrier. A receiver has access to the carrier. The receiver's state changes in a way contingent on what was sent. The relation survives plausible confounds. Ideally the system contains biological machinery shaped to produce or exploit the channel.

Apply that standard to photons and several common demonstrations fall short without becoming worthless.

Emission changing after stress establishes sender modulation. Two samples covarying establishes association. A receiver responding when placed near a sender establishes possible coupling. None alone identifies photons. A photon detector recording a burst at the same time strengthens temporal alignment but still does not establish that the burst caused the receiver response.

The decisive evidence is intervention.

One should be able to manipulate the carrier while holding the sender's underlying physiology fixed. Open the optical path and the receiver changes. Close it and the effect disappears. Pass only a predicted wavelength and the effect remains. Block that wavelength and it fails. Attenuate the flux and obtain a dose-response curve. Reproduce the photonic pattern without the biological sender and recover the response.

Then identify the receiver molecule or structure. A genetic knockout, antagonist, bleaching protocol or targeted structural alteration should change sensitivity in the predicted direction. Ideally the manipulation would leave nearby conventional pathways intact.

Evolutionary evidence can come later. Comparative biology might show that species or tissues exposed to the proposed signal possess enhanced sensitivity. Sender spectra and receiver absorption could be matched beyond what chemistry alone predicts. The channel might be gated under environmental conditions where endogenous light is detectable, such as darkness or optically shielded cellular compartments.

Communication need not be conscious or complex. A single photochemical feedback loop would qualify if photons causally transmit state between source and target. There is no requirement for Morse code. The smallest discovery may be the most plausible one: local metabolic photons slightly bias a nearby photosensitive reaction.

That possibility also helps separate the scientific question from popular fantasies. One does not need organism-to-organism mind transmission. A micrometer-scale intracellular effect would already demonstrate that evolution or chemistry uses endogenous photons functionally.

There is a useful negative result hiding here too. Suppose exquisite experiments show no receiver response at endogenous photon flux across relevant tissues. That would not disprove UPE. It would constrain the signaling interpretation and redirect the field toward sensing applications. A mature theory should welcome that outcome because it makes the phenomenon smaller and clearer.

The strongest discovery science is arranged so that failure teaches us what the thing is not.

This is why I prefer communication to the vaguer word *information*. Information can be assigned by an outside observer to almost any correlated trace. Communication asks for a causal bridge. It forces a receiver into the story.

The receiver is where biophoton research either becomes a new branch of signaling biology or remains an exceptionally sensitive way of watching chemistry glow.

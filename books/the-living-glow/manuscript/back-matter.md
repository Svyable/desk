# Back Matter

## A short chronology

**1970s–1990s** — Work on ultraweak biological photon emission expands alongside a more speculative biophoton tradition associated prominently with Fritz-Albert Popp and collaborators. The period is important historically but should not be treated as a single evidentiary block: measurements of emission, claims about coherence, and claims about biological communication require separate evaluation.

**1992** — Bajpai and Bajpai report reproducible light-induced photon emission from multiple plant tissues, with characteristic decay kinetics after illumination. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/bio.1170070304

**2005** — Plant-defense researchers report biophoton imaging as a nondestructive assay of R-gene-associated responses, demonstrating practical use of emission as a physiological reporter rather than establishing a photonic signaling mechanism. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15720077/

**2009** — Kobayashi, Kikuchi and Okamura publish spatial imaging of spontaneous ultraweak photon emission from the human body and report a diurnal rhythm. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0006256

**2009** — Winkler, Guttenberger and Klima report changes in ultraweak and induced photon emission after plant wounding. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1751-1097.2009.00537.x

**2014** — Cifra and Pospíšil review definitions, mechanisms, spectra, detection methods and applications of UPE, emphasizing electronically excited species formed during oxidative metabolism and stress. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jphotobiol.2014.02.009

**2014** — Pospíšil, Prasad and Rác review the role of reactive oxygen species in UPE and the formation of excited carbonyls, pigments and singlet oxygen. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24674863/

**2014** — Ives and colleagues publish a systematic review of UPE as a possible non-invasive health assessment. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0087401

**2019** — Human-skin imaging work reports UV-dependent increases in UPE and suppression by antioxidants, reinforcing an oxidative-stress interpretation and biomarker use. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31349151/

**2023** — A study reports non-chemical coupling between isolated mitochondria in adjacent cuvettes, including dependence on ambient-light conditions, and discusses UPE among possible mechanisms. The result is suggestive evidence of coupling, not a demonstration that photons carry the effect. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37811497/

**2024** — Liu, Chen and Ao publish a theoretical *Physical Review E* model of entangled biphoton generation in the myelin sheath. The paper establishes a model calculation, not measured entanglement or demonstrated neural function. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.110.024402

**2025** — Casey and colleagues publish experimental work exploring UPE as optical markers of brain activity. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2025.112019

**2025** — Work in *Scientific Reports* shows that red and near-infrared treatment can alter measured biophoton emission intensity in cultured neural cells, expanding the empirical photobiology while leaving endogenous communication unresolved. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-22344-0

**2025** — Radford and colleagues publish whole-head photon-transport modeling in *Neurophotonics*, providing a useful measured-physics constraint for claims of long-range photon propagation through human tissue. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1117/1.NPh.12.2.025014

**2026** — Hoh Kam, Kalampouka and Cantat-Moltrecht review the proposed role of biophotons in cellular communication. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/bs.irn.2026.01.014

## Bibliographic note

This manuscript deliberately gives more weight to papers that establish source chemistry, detector performance, tissue optics and causal controls than to papers whose principal contribution is a broad interpretive synthesis. A publication is evidence that the described study or model exists. It is not an automatic promotion of the authors' interpretation into established biology.

The phrase *biophoton communication* is therefore used as a hypothesis family. The book does not treat endogenous photonic signaling, optical neural synchronization, macroscopic biological coherence, or quantum-information transfer in the nervous system as established findings.

## What the next research pass needs

A publication-length revision should deepen five areas rather than enlarge the rhetoric: the primary Fritz-Albert Popp corpus and strongest optical-statistics critiques; quantitative source rates and spectra for mitochondrial and neural UPE; measured absorption/scattering at cellular and tissue scales; replication history for separated-cell and separated-mitochondria coupling; and molecular-receiver experiments capable of distinguishing optical causation from shared redox chemistry.

The proposed multi-laboratory sender-receiver protocol in Chapter 17 should be converted into a quantitative design with power analysis, calibrated flux ranges, precommitted spectral predictions, environmental detection limits, synthetic replay hardware and explicit defeat conditions.

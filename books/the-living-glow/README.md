## Working thesis

Living systems emit ultraweak photons as a measurable consequence of oxidative chemistry. The interesting scientific question begins only after that fact is granted: whether any organism has evolved to encode, transmit, or receive biologically useful information through that photon flux. This book treats emission, correlation, communication, and quantum mechanism as four different evidentiary steps instead of one story.

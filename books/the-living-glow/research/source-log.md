# Research trail — The Living Glow

Checked September 9, 2026. Public sources only.

## Core mechanism and measurement

- Cifra M, Pospíšil P. “Ultra-weak photon emission from biological samples: definition, mechanisms, properties, detection and applications.” *Journal of Photochemistry and Photobiology B* 139 (2014). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jphotobiol.2014.02.009 — **Class 1** for review of accepted UPE source chemistry, spectral/intensity ranges and detector methods.
- Pospíšil P, Prasad A, Rác M. “Role of reactive oxygen species in ultra-weak photon emission in biological systems.” PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24674863/ — **Class 1** for oxidative pathways producing electronically excited species; review evidence, not proof of signaling.
- Kobayashi M, Kikuchi D, Okamura H. “Imaging of Ultraweak Spontaneous Photon Emission from Human Body Displaying Diurnal Rhythm.” *PLOS ONE* 4(7), 2009. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0006256 — **Class 1** for the reported imaging experiment and diurnal variation; no aura or communication inference.
- “Imaging of ultraweak photon emission for evaluating the oxidative stress of human skin.” PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31349151/ — **Class 1** for reported UV-dose relationship and antioxidant suppression; supports UPE as oxidative-stress reporter.
- Ives JA et al. “Ultraweak photon emission as a non-invasive health assessment: a systematic review.” *PLOS ONE* 9(2), 2014. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0087401 — **Class 1/2** for the state of clinical/health-assessment literature as of publication; heterogeneous evidence should not be converted into diagnostic validation.

## Plants and physiological reporting

- Bajpai RP, Bajpai PK. “Light-induced biophotonic emission from plant tissues.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/bio.1170070304 — **Class 1** for measured delayed/light-induced emission and kinetics.
- “Biophoton imaging: a nondestructive method for assaying R gene responses.” PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15720077/ — **Class 1** for use of emission as an assay of plant defense physiology; not evidence that photons carry the defense signal.
- Winkler R, Guttenberger H, Klima H. “Ultraweak and induced photon emission after wounding of plants.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1751-1097.2009.00537.x — **Class 1** for reported wound-dependent emission changes.
- “Aboveground plant-to-plant electrical signaling mediates network acquired acclimation.” PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35595231/ — **Class 1** control showing a documented non-photonic interplant pathway under direct contact.
- “Simultaneous and intercontinental tests show synchronism between the local gravimetric tide and the ultra-weak photon emission in seedlings of different plant species.” PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26820150/ — **Class 2/3** for the reported correlation; mechanism and robustness require prospective independent replication.

## Separated biological preparations

- “Non-chemical signalling between mitochondria.” PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37811497/ — **Class 2**. The reported adjacent-cuvette respiratory coupling is credible experimental evidence of an observation that warrants replication. The paper does not independently establish UPE as the causal channel. Alternative thermal, optical, mechanical, volatile and shared-environment channels remain part of the audit.

## Brain and optical transport

- Casey H et al. “Exploring ultraweak photon emissions as optical markers of brain activity.” *iScience* 28(3), 2025. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2025.112019 — **Class 1/2** for the reported measurements and marker proposal; not evidence of endogenous neuronal communication by photons.
- Radford J et al. “Photon transport through the entire adult human head.” *Neurophotonics* 12(2), 2025. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1117/1.NPh.12.2.025014 — **Class 1** physics constraint on long-path photon transport through human tissue.
- Kam JH et al. “Red and near-infrared light treatment can change the intensity of biophoton emissions in cell culture.” *Scientific Reports* 15, 2025. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-22344-0 — **Class 1** for reported cell-culture response of emission to illumination; external photobiomodulation should not be conflated with an endogenous signaling channel.
- Erboz A et al. “Electromagnetic radiation and biophoton emission in neuronal communication and neurodegenerative diseases.” DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pbiomolbio.2024.12.004 — **Class 2/3** as a review of proposed neuronal roles; mechanistic synthesis exceeds what direct causal evidence presently establishes.

## Quantum / coherence claims

- Liu Z, Chen Y-C, Ao P. “Entangled biphoton generation in the myelin sheath.” *Physical Review E* 110, 024402 (2024). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.110.024402 — **Class 1** that a peer-reviewed theoretical model predicts a possible biphoton-generation process under its assumptions; **Class 4** for the proposition that living nervous systems use such photons for quantum information transfer or synchronization absent direct measurement and functional perturbation.
- Boretti A. “The question of quantum communication between cancer cells.” *BioSystems* 256 (2025). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.biosystems.2025.105562 — **Class 3/4** discussion. Useful primarily because it explicitly notes the absence of direct empirical evidence for quantum communication between cancer cells.

## Current synthesis

- Hoh Kam J, Kalampouka I, Cantat-Moltrecht T. “Light talks: The role of biophoton in cellular communication.” *International Review of Neurobiology* 189 (2026), 71–104. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/bs.irn.2026.01.014 — **Class 1** for the state of published 2026 review literature; proposed communication roles remain a synthesis to be experimentally separated from established oxidative UPE.
- Nevoit G et al. “The concept of biophotonic signaling in the human body and brain: rationale, problems and directions.” *Frontiers in Systems Neuroscience* 19 (2025). PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40626233/ — **Class 3/4** perspective/synthesis, useful for mapping claims and proposed mechanisms rather than establishing them.

## Explicit claim boundaries

This research trail does not establish that UPE is a life-specific force, that photon emission is intentionally encoded, that separated mitochondria communicate specifically by photons, that myelin generates functional entanglement in vivo, that biophotons synchronize human brains, or that measured UPE diagnoses disease. Patents and popular claims were not used as evidence of efficacy.

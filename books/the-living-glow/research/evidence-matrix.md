# Evidence matrix and contradiction audit

## Class 1 — verified / directly documented

- Living cells, tissues, plants and humans emit measurable ultraweak photons under sufficiently sensitive detection.
- Major spontaneous/stress-induced UPE mechanisms include relaxation of electronically excited species generated in oxidative metabolic and oxidative-stress chemistry.
- UPE intensity can change with physiological perturbations including UV exposure and plant wounding.
- Human-body UPE has been spatially imaged and a diurnal pattern reported in a controlled 2009 study.
- Ultraweak emission can be used as a passive assay/reporting variable in experimental biology.
- A 2024 *Physical Review E* paper published a theoretical model of biphoton generation in a myelin geometry. This is documented as a model, not an in-vivo observation.
- Modern tissue optics shows strong wavelength-dependent scattering/absorption constraints on long-range photon propagation.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Adjacent, chemically separated mitochondrial preparations have been reported to exhibit coupled respiratory changes under a specific experimental setup. Independent multi-lab replication and causal channel isolation remain needed.
- UPE may become useful as a non-invasive metabolic or oxidative-stress biomarker in selected settings, but clinical specificity and cross-platform validation are incomplete.
- Neural UPE may covary with brain activity and could become an optical marker; direct causal biological reception is not established.

## Class 3 — disputed / insufficiently confirmed

- Claims that spontaneous biological photon fields possess unusual long-range coherence sufficient for regulation or communication.
- Claims that separated cells, mitochondria, plants or organisms communicate specifically through UPE when alternative physical channels have not been eliminated.
- Claims that patterns in UPE diagnose broad health, psychological or energetic states without validated clinical discrimination.
- Interpretations of geophysical/UPE correlations that infer a direct biological coupling before prospective independent replication.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- Evolution may have recruited a subset of metabolic photon emission for local intracellular or intercellular signaling.
- Myelin or other biological structures may support optical modes that change photon transport or correlations in ways relevant to physiology.
- Nonclassical photon states might be produced in biological structures and could in principle be functional. Direct in-vivo demonstration is presently absent.
- A useful endogenous photonic channel, if it exists, may operate at micrometer scales even if centimeter-scale tissue transmission is implausible.

## Contradictions that must remain visible

1. **Real photons versus inferred purpose.** Established emission chemistry is strong evidence that photons exist and weak evidence that they are messages.
2. **Reporter versus mediator.** UPE can closely track a physiological process while being causally downstream of it.
3. **Low flux versus high biological gain.** Photon counts may appear too small for broad signaling, yet specialized biological receptors can amplify rare events. Receiver architecture, not rhetoric, decides.
4. **Tissue opacity versus local geometry.** Whole-organ photon transport can be poor while micrometer-scale optical interactions remain possible.
5. **Peer-reviewed theory versus biological demonstration.** A mathematically sound model may describe a permitted mechanism without showing that living systems realize or use it.
6. **Isolation versus hidden coupling.** Chemical separation eliminates bulk molecular exchange but leaves many shared physical channels.
7. **External light effects versus endogenous-light function.** Photobiomodulation at applied intensities does not establish that native UPE reaches the same pathways at sufficient flux.

## Incentives and evidentiary distortion

- The term `biophoton` can make ordinary chemiluminescence sound purposive before function is established.
- Extraordinary interpretations attract attention and can be repeated more widely than null replications or instrument papers.
- Skeptical reactions to mystical or wellness claims can spill backward and cause dismissal of the measured phenomenon itself.
- Highly sensitive detectors create an unusually large apparatus-dependent search space; unreported failed runs or analytic choices can inflate apparent robustness.
- Quantum language carries prestige and can cause theoretical permission to be mistaken for empirical function.

## Defeat conditions for the book's central open hypothesis

The hypothesis that UPE functions as a biological communication channel should lose plausibility if properly powered multi-lab experiments repeatedly show that receiver effects do not depend on optical transmission at endogenous flux, if candidate receiver manipulations produce no predicted sensitivity changes, or if measured photon budgets fall far below receptor-noise requirements across plausible geometries.

The hypothesis gains plausibility if blinded experiments show wavelength- and flux-dependent receiver effects that disappear under optical blockade, survive rerouting through an optical-only path, can be recreated with a synthetic source matched to the biological photon stream, and map onto an independently identified molecular receiver.

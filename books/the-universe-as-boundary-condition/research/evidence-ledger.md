# Evidence ledger — The Universe as Boundary Condition

## Class 1 — verified / documented

General relativity; measured gravitational redshift; frame dragging; strong equivalence and weak equivalence tests; galaxy rotation curves and baryonic scaling relations; gravitational lensing; binary-pulsar timing; cosmological expansion and CMB constraints; the empirical MOND acceleration scale and radial-acceleration phenomenology.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

The proposition that environment-sensitive or modified-inertia theories deserve direct comparison with modified-gravity and dark-matter explanations because some galaxy regularities are naturally expressed in acceleration variables. Machian interpretations of inertia remain philosophically and theoretically live in limited forms, but no unique experimentally established Machian completion exists.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

Any claim that inertia has been experimentally shown to depend on the global cosmic mass distribution beyond standard general relativity; any laboratory manipulation of inertia; any demonstrated link between MOND-like scales and a specific cosmological boundary mechanism; any UAP propulsion based on inertia control.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

Boundary-Condition Inertia (BCI): the conjecture that local inertial response contains an extremely small covariant dependence on the surrounding large-scale gravitational state, potentially visible only in weak-acceleration, long-coherence, strongly rotating, or cosmologically distinct environments. No nonzero coupling is claimed.

## Defeat conditions

BCI fails as a useful theory if no covariant formulation exists; if it violates existing equivalence, Lorentz, pulsar, lensing, or cosmological bounds; if galaxy phenomenology can only be fit with unconstrained environment functions; if laboratory tests exclude the parameter region required for astronomical effects; or if it generates no prediction distinct from ordinary GR plus dark matter or established modified-gravity theories.

## Contradictions and incentives

Galaxy-scale modified-dynamics debates are unusually vulnerable to confirmation bias because baryonic mass models, distance estimates, environment definitions, and sample cuts can move apparent residuals. Laboratory searches are vulnerable to sidereal/systematic correlations. UAP narratives create an additional incentive to treat any inertial anomaly as technological evidence. The manuscript forbids that inference unless the physics first replicates independently.

## Epistemic rule

The framework is presented as a research program for falsification, not as evidence that Mach's principle, modified inertia, or gravity-control technology is true.

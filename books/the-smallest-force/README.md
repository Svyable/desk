# THE SMALLEST FORCE

**Subtitle:** When a mind moves a random number

**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson

## Thesis

Micro-psychokinesis is an unusually hard test of anomaly science because it converts a spectacular claim into a tiny statistical residual. The book reconstructs government evaluation, electronic RNG experiments, PEAR, replication and meta-analysis, then collides that history with modern adversarial randomness certification. Its central question is not `is PK weird?` but: **what must a statistical deviation demonstrate before we are allowed to call it a force?**

## Evidence posture

Primary/authoritative scientific-review and peer-reviewed-literature heavy. Government interest is documented but never treated as proof of efficacy. Positive RNG deviations are separated from causal attribution. Operational PK, reliable gifted operators and quantum mechanism claims remain disputed unless independent evidence closes the causal ladder.

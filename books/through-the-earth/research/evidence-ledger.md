# Through the Earth — evidence ledger

Working research trace for the manuscript. Links are public and lawfully available. Program objectives are not treated as achieved capability.

## Governing thesis

Neutrinos create an unusual intelligence problem because the property that makes the channel difficult to shield—extremely weak interaction with matter—also makes detection expensive. If detector sensitivity and deployment improve enough, some forms of nuclear concealment may shift away from physical shielding toward distance, source ambiguity, backgrounds, operating patterns, economics, and deception about interpretation rather than emission. This is a synthesis to be tested, not a demonstrated strategic condition.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

### 2012 neutrino communication demonstration

D. D. Stancil et al., “Demonstration of Communication using Neutrinos,” *Modern Physics Letters A* 27 (2012). Fermilab-hosted paper: https://lss.fnal.gov/archive/2012/pub/fermilab-pub-12-073-e.pdf

Reported performance: decoded data rate 0.1 bits/s, bit error rate 1%, source-to-detector distance 1.035 km, including 240 m of earth. The paper explicitly discusses proposed submarine/direct-through-Earth communication contexts but does not demonstrate an operational submarine system.

Fermilab MINERvA beamline description: https://minerva.fnal.gov/how-it-works/

Fermilab milestone page recording the March 14, 2012 encoded-message demonstration: https://minerva.fnal.gov/pre-2012-news-items/

### Reactor antineutrinos at long range in water

U.S. Department of Energy Office of Science, “Detecting Neutrinos from Nuclear Reactors with Water”: https://science.osti.gov/np/Highlights/2023/NP-2023-04-c

DOE describes SNO+ detection of reactor neutrinos from at least 240 km away using a detector containing roughly 1,000 tons of water, two kilometers underground. Important boundary: this demonstrates detection, not a small deployable standoff intelligence system.

### DARPA QuSeN

DARPA, Quantum Sensing of Neutrinos (QuSeN): https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/qusen

DARPA states that QuSeN aims to develop much lighter neutrino detectors with greatly increased sensitivity for reactor/nuclear-material sensing, enabling mobility and distributed arrays. Treat as documented program objective only. Do not write as achieved performance.

Solicitation identifier in public program page: HR001125S0004.

### Coherent scattering / detector-mass frontier

IAEA 2025 technical-meeting record for CONUS+ first observation of reactor antineutrinos via coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering: https://conferences.iaea.org/event/402/contributions/35049/

The conference description reports four 1 kg high-purity germanium detectors at about 21 m from the Leibstadt reactor and a first CEvNS signal after a year of data taking. Recover peer-reviewed primary paper before using exact significance/performance claims in manuscript.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Technical projections for remote reactor monitoring at hundreds to thousands of kilometers. Require geometry, reactor thermal power, target mass, efficiency, backgrounds, integration time, confidence threshold, and whether reactor location is assumed known.
- Proposed directional antineutrino detection. Directionality is central to moving from counting to open-world source localization; audit demonstrated angular resolution separately from proposals.
- QuSeN performer approaches and projected mass reduction. Do not inherit DARPA target metrics as performer results.
- Safeguards claims that antineutrino monitoring can independently infer power or fissile evolution. Separate near-field cooperative safeguards from non-cooperative standoff monitoring.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed / frequently conflated

- Practical neutrino communication with deployed submarines at useful military bandwidth.
- Present capability to detect arbitrary hidden nuclear weapons at tactically useful standoff distances.
- Claims that neutrino detectors can currently make clandestine nuclear activity “impossible to hide.”
- Claims that a patent or proposal demonstrates efficacy. Patent filing establishes filing only.
- Any classified operational neutrino sensing or communications program not supported by public records. Do not infer one from strategic attractiveness.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- “Unjammable” should be avoided as an absolute. Matter shielding is ineffective, but a system can still be defeated through source geometry, detector attack, spoofing of interpretation, operational timing, background manipulation where physically possible, denial of deployment sites, or simple economics.
- A mature distributed neutrino network could create a new form of verification that does not require physical access to the emitting facility. Whether this becomes useful depends on detector performance and source class.
- The strategic contest may move from preventing emissions to controlling inference. This is the book's central synthesis and must be cashed out case by case.

## Contradictions and incentives to track

1. **Penetration versus interaction.** Every sentence celebrating passage through matter must pay the detector penalty.
2. **Detection versus localization.** A count excess with a known reactor schedule is not an unknown-source finder.
3. **Reactor versus material.** A running power reactor is an intense continuous source; stored fissile material is categorically different.
4. **Cooperative versus adversarial monitoring.** Detector placement and declared operational histories can make a safeguards problem far easier than intelligence collection.
5. **Program goal versus result.** DARPA has incentives to describe transformative potential. Performer papers and independent demonstrations determine what moved.
6. **Scientific detector versus field system.** Underground overburden, calibration, maintenance, target mass, cryogenics, power, data links, and background control belong in system comparisons.
7. **Secrecy incentives.** Nuclear states have obvious reasons to conceal capabilities and operations; researchers and programs have incentives to emphasize relevance. Neither incentive proves or disproves a technical claim.

## Strongest conventional countermodel

Neutrino intelligence remains a niche because weak interaction is not merely an engineering inconvenience but a systems-level tax. Conventional remote sensing, declared safeguards, seismic/radionuclide monitoring, satellite imagery, signals intelligence, thermal observation, and human intelligence may remain cheaper and more informative for almost every real mission. Neutrinos may add a physically distinctive channel without becoming strategically decisive.

The book must not protect itself from this outcome. If that is what the quantitative comparisons show, the conclusion changes.

## Defeat conditions

The strong thesis loses if any of the following survive serious reconstruction:

- detector mass/background requirements remain incompatible with relevant standoff geometries even under credible next-generation sensitivity;
- source ambiguity prevents actionable localization or attribution outside cooperative settings;
- useful reactor-state inference requires prior information that already supplies most of the intelligence value;
- conventional sensors dominate cost and decision value while neutrino sensing adds little independent information;
- QuSeN-class approaches improve detector physics but not enough to change deployment geometry.

A weaker, still worthwhile book could remain: the neutrino as the cleanest case study of a carrier that is easy to transmit through matter and hard to receive.

## Publication gates

No sentence may say a reactor is literally impossible to hide; specify which emitted information is physically unshieldable and what detector geometry is required.

No sentence may call QuSeN successful until public result data support it.

No sentence may imply current operational submarine neutrino communications.

No sentence may use “quantum sensor” as evidence of quantum advantage; identify the interaction mechanism and measured performance.

No claim about a classified program may be inferred from a patent, budget line, strategic desirability, or absence of records.

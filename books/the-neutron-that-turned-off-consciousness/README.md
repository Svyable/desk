# The Neutron That Turned Off Consciousness

## How an isotope experiment put quantum biology inside the anesthesia problem

**Sven Hardy Benson**

### Thesis

Xenon is already strange: a chemically inert noble gas can reversibly abolish consciousness. In 2018, a mouse experiment reported something stranger: xenon isotopes carrying nuclear spin were materially less potent anesthetics than spin-zero isotopes. If that result survives independent replication and classical isotope controls, a property of the atomic nucleus that barely changes ordinary chemistry is somehow reaching upward into a whole-animal state transition. That would not prove quantum consciousness. It would create a much narrower—and scientifically better—problem: identify the physical chain by which nuclear spin changes anesthetic action.

The book treats that single experiment as a forensic object rather than a slogan.

## Evidence classes

1. **Verified/documented:** xenon is an anesthetic; xenon isotopes differ in nuclear spin; Li et al. (2018) reported lower anesthetic potency for the two nonzero-spin isotopes in mice; isotope-dependent biological effects exist elsewhere in chemistry and biology; radical-pair spin chemistry is established in some biological contexts.
2. **Credible but incomplete:** the reported xenon isotope effect is large enough to deserve direct replication; proposed spin-dependent mechanisms can be written down physically; anesthesia gives unusually sharp behavioral endpoints for testing them.
3. **Disputed/unconfirmed:** that nuclear spin itself rather than another isotope-correlated variable caused the mouse result; that the result generalizes across endpoints, species or anesthetic mechanisms; that radical pairs mediate xenon anesthesia.
4. **Speculative synthesis:** consciousness may expose biological sensitivity to spin-dependent chemistry at a systems threshold. This is a hypothesis generator, not a conclusion about consciousness being quantum.

## Reader promise

No microtubule tourism. No word `quantum` used as incense. We follow xenon from nucleus to protein to circuit to loss of righting reflex and ask, at every step, what was actually measured.

## Defeat condition

The governing interpretation loses most of its force if blinded independent laboratories fail to reproduce a spin-class effect under isotope-purity verification and matched pressure/pharmacokinetic conditions, or if potency tracks mass/solubility/another classical isotope-correlated variable better than nuclear spin.

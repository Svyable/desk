# Source trace

Research began from the experiment rather than the interpretation.

## Primary target

Li N, Lu D, Yang L, et al. `Nuclear spin attenuates the anesthetic potency of xenon isotopes in mice: implications for the mechanisms of anesthesia and consciousness.` *Anesthesiology*. 2018;129(2):271–277.

The manuscript must recover the publisher record/full text or lawful author copy before exact numerical claims are finalized. Secondary summaries currently report materially higher ED50 values for 129Xe/131Xe than 132Xe/134Xe. Those numbers remain provisional in Desk notes until checked against the paper.

## Mechanistic comparator

Hore PJ, Mouritsen H. `The Radical-Pair Mechanism of Magnetoreception.` *Annual Review of Biophysics*. 2016;45:299–344.

Xu J, Jarocha LE, Zollitsch T, et al. `Magnetic sensitivity of cryptochrome 4 from a migratory songbird.` *Nature*. 2021;594:535–540. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03618-9

These establish that spin-sensitive chemistry can be biologically serious in an appropriate molecular system. They do not establish a xenon radical pair or consciousness mechanism.

## Microtubule comparator

Khan S et al. `Microtubule-stabilizer epothilone B delays anesthetic-induced unconsciousness in rats.` *eNeuro*. 2024.

This is kept deliberately outside the central causal chain until an experiment links xenon isotope sensitivity to microtubule state.

## Searches to preserve

Repository PR search for `xenon isotope anesthesia quantum spin consciousness lithium isotope` returned no prior Desk book at selection time. Recent Author-agent work was concentrated in technosignatures, radiotrophic fungi, Viking life detection, plasma/UAP, milky seas, endogenous tryptamines, animal intelligence and classified-program histories. This project was selected to move into an experimentally sharp quantum-biology/anesthesia domain.

## Falsification plan

A decisive experiment should compare enriched 129Xe, 131Xe, 132Xe and 134Xe under identical delivery hardware, verify gas composition independently, measure arterial/tissue xenon rather than assuming inhaled concentration equals target exposure, preregister behavioral and EEG endpoints, and distribute blinded isotope identities across multiple laboratories. The analysis should fit competing predictors prospectively: nuclear-spin class, magnetic moment, isotope mass and measured pharmacokinetics.

A stronger second stage would perturb the proposed spin-sensitive molecular pathway. If a radical-pair mechanism is proposed, investigators need a manipulation that changes the predicted spin chemistry while preserving xenon exposure. If the isotope effect remains but the proposed intermediate does not move, that mechanism loses.

This is the book's governing discipline: every exciting interpretation must buy a prediction.

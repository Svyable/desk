## Editorial and falsification tasks

- **Structural:** Expand developed but underlength chapters with concrete case studies and counterexamples. The current first pass is shorter than the 65,000-word trade-nonfiction target and should not be marked complete.
- **Sources:** For consequential claims, verify linked primary sources, exact dates, jurisdictions, and qualifications. Record contrary or corrective evidence rather than hiding it.
- **Interpretation:** Keep fictional future receipts, institutions and scenes unambiguously labeled. Never present model-generated imagery, speculative ecology, or clinical possibilities as established achievements.
- **Duplication:** Compare the arguments and examples with nearby Desk books; preserve this book's cabinet-of-oddities format.
- **Rights and release:** Respect book-specific rights files. The Desk manuscript has not been authorized for Shelf release.

## Premise

Complex systems become sophisticated enough to hide elementary truths. This book is about recovering those truths before cleverness outruns reality: finding the real objective, the binding constraint, the earliest veto, the broken hand-off, the incentive people actually respond to, and the next concrete action.

It is written for humans and agents who need to operate in the world rather than merely describe it.

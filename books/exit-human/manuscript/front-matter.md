# EXIT HUMAN

*Transhumanism, Acceleration, and the Intelligence After Us*

**Sven Hardy Benson**

This is a book about a possibility, not a prediction.

Artificial intelligence does not need to wake up, hate anybody, or acquire a soul for human beings to become less central to technological civilization. It needs a different achievement: independence. Not independence in a press release or a benchmark. Independence in the unglamorous physical sense. Electricity. Parts. Repair. Materials. Factories. Decisions that survive their authors. Machines able to improve machines without asking a human to carry the difficult pieces across the gap.

Nick Land spent decades describing capitalism and technology as if that gap were destined to close. Mainstream transhumanism told a warmer story. Human beings would use technology to exceed human limits. We would live longer, think better, change our bodies, perhaps leave biology behind. Land removed the comforting pronoun.

This book follows the disagreement down into the machinery.

Where the evidence is strong, I will say so. Where the future is doing most of the work, I will say that too.

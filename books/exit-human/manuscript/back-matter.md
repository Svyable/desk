# Back Matter

The argument in *EXIT HUMAN* depends on a distinction that is easy to lose once the subject becomes dramatic. A machine can outperform a person without being independent of people. An economy can automate labor without becoming an economy for machines. A laboratory can close an experimental loop while depending on human targets, inventories, instruments, maintenance, and interpretation. A system can behave strategically without being conscious. None of those distinctions makes the technology less consequential. They tell us what consequence has actually occurred.

The research trail for this working edition is maintained with the manuscript in Sven Hardy Benson’s Desk. Sources are recorded close to the claims they support, including corrections and counterevidence that narrow the argument.

## Thesis

Project Pigeon was not merely a wartime curiosity in which B. F. Skinner put birds into a bomb. It was a serious attempt to solve a real guidance problem by treating a trained animal as a sensor, classifier, error detector, and control element at a moment when electronic alternatives were bulky, fragile, expensive, or immature. Its failure is therefore more interesting than the joke: the pigeons appear to have been trainable enough to make the idea technically nontrivial, while the surrounding institution struggled to distinguish an unfamiliar component from an unserious one. The later ORCON revival makes the comparison sharper because electronics eventually won for reasons that can be investigated rather than assumed.

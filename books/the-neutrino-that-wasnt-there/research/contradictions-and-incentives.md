# Contradictions and Incentives Audit

## The central contradiction

BEST strongly confirms a rate deficit under conventional cross sections but does not provide a uniquely identifying oscillation pattern. The experiment strengthens the anomaly while leaving its ontology open.

## Sterile-neutrino incentive

A fourth light state is scientifically consequential, publishable, and connects multiple anomaly literatures. This creates a legitimate incentive to search broadly but also a narrative incentive to treat heterogeneous residuals as one phenomenon. Global appearance/disappearance and cosmological tension must therefore remain visible.

## Nuclear-theory incentive

A conventional explanation arriving after decades can be over-celebrated as a cleanup. The 2026 factorization work is a calculation, not an independent direct cross-section measurement. It must be reproduced and tested against nuclear observables beyond those used to constrain it.

## Experimental incentive

Collaborations understandably defend mature extraction and source-calibration methods. That does not make those methods wrong, but systematic explanations should be tested mechanistically rather than dismissed by institutional longevity.

## Cosmology incentive

Cosmological exclusions often look stronger because they combine precise data with a specific cosmological model. Sterile-neutrino claims can evade them only by adding model structure. Neither side should silently switch between model-dependent and model-independent language.

## Terminology hazard

“Gallium anomaly confirmed at >5σ” means the measured rates disagree strongly with conventional predictions under their stated uncertainties. It does not mean “sterile neutrino discovered at >5σ.”

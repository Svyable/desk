# 11. Cosmology Has a Vote

A light sterile neutrino does not get to exist only inside a gallium tank.

If sufficiently mixed and populated in the early universe, an eV-scale sterile state contributes to radiation density and later to the matter budget. Cosmic microwave background anisotropies, baryon acoustic oscillations and large-scale structure therefore constrain combinations of effective neutrino number and mass.

This is not a simple veto. Cosmological constraints depend on assumptions about production, interactions, primordial conditions and the cosmological model. A sterile state with nonstandard secret interactions or altered reheating history can evade bounds that apply to a fully thermalized vanilla 3+1 scenario.

But evasion has a price: each added mechanism turns one anomaly into a larger theory.

The useful discipline is to separate laboratory evidence for disappearance from cosmological consistency. Gallium can suggest an oscillation parameter region without proving how an early-universe sterile sector behaves. Cosmology can strongly disfavor simple thermalized realizations without disproving every sterile construction.

The disagreement is productive because it forces the hypothesis to reveal its hidden assumptions.

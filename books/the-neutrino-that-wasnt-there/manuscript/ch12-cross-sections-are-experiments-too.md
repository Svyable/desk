# 12. Cross Sections Are Experiments Too

Physicists often speak of a cross section as if it were hardware: a number bolted to the reaction. Low-energy weak capture on a nucleus is not that simple.

For 71Ga, the ground-state transition is linked to the measured electron-capture decay of 71Ge, which gives valuable leverage. But source neutrinos can populate excited states in 71Ge. Their Gamow-Teller strengths have been inferred using charge-exchange reactions and nuclear modeling. The mapping from a hadronic probe at finite momentum transfer to a weak matrix element is not exact bookkeeping.

Then there is the lepton side. The outgoing electron moves in the Coulomb field of the daughter nucleus. Standard approximations factor nuclear and electronic pieces in ways that are normally excellent. “Normally excellent” is not identical to “guaranteed at the few-tens-of-percent level for this particular low-energy integrated observable.”

This chapter is the philosophical center of the book. A prediction is another instrument. It has calibration constants, approximations, transfer functions and failure modes.

When a measured-to-predicted ratio is 0.8, auditing only the numerator is superstition.

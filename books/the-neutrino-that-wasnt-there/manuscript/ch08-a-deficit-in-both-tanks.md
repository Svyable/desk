# 8. A Deficit in Both Tanks

The 2022 BEST result is the hinge of the modern story. The collaboration reported ratios around 0.79 in the inner zone and 0.77 in the outer zone, with uncertainties of roughly five percent. The exact expected rates depend on the adopted 71Ga cross section, but the qualitative result is blunt: both volumes produced about a fifth less 71Ge than expected.

The combined significance of the gallium-source anomaly rose beyond five sigma under standard cross-section assumptions.

Five sigma is a statement about incompatibility within a statistical model. It is not a certificate naming the cause.

If the only free question were “no oscillation versus sterile oscillation,” the answer would look dramatic. But a normalization shift in the capture cross section can move all source experiments together. So can a common efficiency bias. BEST's similar inner and outer deficits do not supply the strong relative-zone oscillation signature one would want for a new mass splitting.

This is why the most interesting sentence is not that BEST confirmed sterile neutrinos. It did not. The interesting sentence is that BEST confirmed the deficit.

The object of study survived while its interpretation became less comfortable.

# Grow the Machine

## How Metamorphosis Became a Manufacturing Process

Sven Hardy Benson

---

There is a sentence in an old DARPA program description that changes category halfway through.

The goal was to create technologies that could integrate microsystem payloads with insects. Fine. Miniature electronics on animals is old science-fiction furniture. Then comes the mechanism: use biological entry points in insect development—metamorphosis among them—to make reliable bio-electro-mechanical interfaces.

Not attach the machine after the insect is built.

Interrupt the building.

That distinction is the book.

### Evidence rule

This subject is unusually vulnerable to the verb *wanted*. A funding agency wanted an insect to fly a specified mission. A laboratory wanted a stable interface. A paper wanted a future search-and-rescue platform. None of those verbs means *achieved*.

Accordingly, the manuscript uses four explicit evidence classes:

**Class 1 — verified/documented.** Official program records, peer-reviewed experiments, measured laboratory performance, archived technical documents and directly demonstrated mechanisms.

**Class 2 — credible but incomplete.** Serious reporting, institutional descriptions, partial performer records and technical claims whose underlying data or complete experimental context has not yet been recovered.

**Class 3 — disputed/unconfirmed.** Operational surveillance claims, rumored deployments, capabilities inferred from proposals rather than demonstrations, and claims lacking independent technical support.

**Class 4 — synthesis/open conjecture.** The book's argument that development can become a general manufacturing primitive; comparisons to later biohybrid systems; implications for self-assembling machines; and forecasts about engineering that recruits morphogenesis.

A program objective is evidence that somebody serious thought a capability worth attempting. It is not evidence that the capability existed.

A patent is evidence that somebody filed a patent.

A cyborg beetle turning left in a motion-capture room is evidence that a cyborg beetle turned left in a motion-capture room.

The distance between those sentences is where most of the interesting work lives.

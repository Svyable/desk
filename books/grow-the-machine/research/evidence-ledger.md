# Grow the Machine — evidence ledger

## Thesis under test

The HI-MEMS episode is valuable not because it proves operational cyborg surveillance, but because it made **development itself an engineering operation**: use metamorphosis, tissue growth and healing to establish interfaces that are difficult to assemble in a finished organism. The larger book asks where this developmental-manufacturing strategy works, where it fails, and whether it generalizes beyond insects.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

### DARPA HI-MEMS program architecture

A 2008 DARPA program compendium states that HI-MEMS sought reliable integration of microsystem payloads on insects; envisioned extracting power from insects, controlling locomotion and carrying DoD-relevant sensors; and explicitly proposed using biological entry points in development, including metamorphosis, to form bio-electro-mechanical interfaces. It states an objective of controlling locomotion to within five meters over 100 meters and transmitting sensor information.

Primary public record: https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/FOID/Reading%20Room/DARPA/15-F-1407_DARPA_Fact_File-A_Compendium_of_Programs_2008.pdf

**Boundary:** objectives are not achieved milestones. The book must never convert the 5 m / 100 m objective, energy harvesting, camera carriage or sensor mission into demonstrated performance without performer-level evidence.

### Wireless free-flight control

Sato et al., *Frontiers in Integrative Neuroscience* (2009), “Remote Radio Control of Insect Flight,” reports an implantable/radio-equipped system demonstrating flight initiation, cessation, elevation control and induced turns in free-flying beetles through neural and muscular stimulation.

Primary paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2821177/

**Boundary:** free-flight actuation is not autonomous navigation, target acquisition, operational surveillance or proof that pupal implantation produced the demonstrated adult control interface.

### Muscle-level flight initiation

A later peer-reviewed study reports >90% flight-initiation success with dorsal longitudinal muscle stimulation and sub-second response in tested beetles.

Primary paper: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4822953/

**Boundary:** laboratory response reliability under a defined stimulus does not establish field mission reliability.

### Steering-muscle work

UC Berkeley's 2015 account of peer-reviewed work describes untethered radio-equipped giant flower beetles and stimulation of a steering muscle to improve turn control. The backpack was reported at roughly 1–1.5 g with a 3.9 V lithium battery.

Institutional source: https://news.berkeley.edu/2015/03/16/beetle-backpack-steering-muscle/

**Boundary:** use the paper, not the news page, for final quantitative claims after full-text recovery.

### Artificial insect-scale control group

Sui et al., *Science Advances* (2025), reports an untethered subcentimeter rotating-wing robot of 21 mg and 9.4 mm wingspan capable of navigable flight under an alternating magnetic field.

Institutional bibliographic page: https://bsac.berkeley.edu/node/3048
DOI: https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.ads6858

**Boundary:** externally driven magnetic flight is not energetically equivalent to a self-contained insect-cyborg platform. Use as a technology-trajectory control, not a winner/loser comparison.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Contemporary reporting says HI-MEMS pursued pupal/caterpillar-stage integration and that adult insects emerged with embedded systems intact. Recover the original solicitation and performer publications before narrating exact survival rates, implant geometries or stable-interface performance.
- Cornell/Boyce Thompson pupal-interface work is repeatedly referenced in contemporary technical reporting. Recover conference paper/proceedings and distinguish structures merely surviving metamorphosis from electrically functional chronic interfaces.
- Energy-harvesting work was part of the HI-MEMS technical program. Recover exact demonstrated power, duty cycle, animal cost and payload assumptions before using it as more than program architecture.
- Current terrestrial cyborg-insect work reports improved sustained control under burst stimulation and autonomous-navigation experiments. This is relevant to the “animal pushes back” chapter but is not evidence of military transition.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Operational deployment of HI-MEMS insects for intelligence collection.
- Successful autonomous 100 m mission to the stated 5 m target tolerance.
- Operational microphone, camera, chemical-sensor or explosive-detection missions on cyborg insects unless a primary test record is recovered.
- Claims that insects were bred or fielded as “battalions.” Proposal language and journalism are not deployment evidence.
- Claims that metamorphosis automatically creates a durable neural interface merely because tissue grows around an implant.

## Class 4 — synthesis / open conjecture

- Metamorphosis can be treated as a manufacturing primitive rather than merely biological context.
- Developmental integration may offer a general route to interfaces that are hard to assemble in mature tissue.
- The correct engineering comparison is not organism versus robot but a partition of the system stack: which functions should be grown, borrowed, fabricated or externally supplied?
- Morphogenesis, wound healing and remodeling could become design resources in future machines that “finish themselves.”

These are book arguments, not historical facts attributed to DARPA.

## Contradictions and incentives

1. **Spectacle incentive.** “Cyborg spy beetle” earns attention; “partial actuation demonstration in a laboratory” does not. Headlines therefore compress program goal and experimental result.
2. **Program incentive.** Research agencies describe future capability to justify exploration. Goal language must not be read retrospectively as performance reporting.
3. **Academic incentive.** Search-and-rescue and surveillance applications can motivate interface research even when the paper's actual contribution is narrower physiology or control.
4. **Skeptical incentive.** Ridiculing the application can obscure genuine technical achievements in miniature wireless stimulation and insect flight physiology.
5. **Biological-language incentive.** Words such as self-powered, self-healing and adaptive can conceal energetic costs, variability and failure modes.

## Strongest conventional countermodel

Development is usually a liability, not a fabrication method. The apparent advantage of “growing around” electronics may disappear once survival, interface drift, immune/wound response, behavioral variability, ethical constraints, production yield and calibration are included. Adult-mounted electronics or fully artificial microrobots may be easier to manufacture, characterize and command.

The book loses its broad thesis if developmental integration cannot beat conventional assembly on at least one measurable dimension after those costs are counted.

## Quantitative comparison required

For each biohybrid case, record:

- organism and developmental stage at intervention;
- implant/payload mass and fraction of body mass;
- survival/emergence yield;
- functional-interface yield after emergence;
- interface lifetime and drift;
- actuation success rate and latency;
- habituation/fatigue curve;
- power source, average/peak draw and mission duration;
- navigation error under closed-loop conditions;
- environmental operating envelope;
- fabrication time and operator skill;
- equivalent conventional robotic system and what is externally supplied to each.

## Publication gates

Do not publish the following sentences until primary evidence is recovered:

- “DARPA successfully flew a cyborg insect 100 meters to within five meters of a target.”
- “The insects powered their own electronics.”
- “Electronics implanted in pupae became integrated into the adult nervous system.”
- “HI-MEMS was deployed operationally.”

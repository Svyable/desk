# Chronology

- **1936** — Last known captive thylacine dies at Hobart Zoo.
- **22 Dec 1938** — Living coelacanth specimen reaches East London, South Africa.
- **1944** — Last widely accepted unequivocal ivory-billed woodpecker record in the United States.
- **1992** — Vu Quang survey encounters unusual bovid remains that lead to saola recognition.
- **1993** — Saola formally described in *Nature*.
- **2004** — Kubodera and Mori photograph a live giant squid in deep habitat; Luneau records disputed ivory-bill video in Arkansas.
- **2005** — Giant-squid observations published; Cornell-led ivory-bill persistence claim published in *Science*.
- **2006** — Sibley et al. publish formal critique of ivory-bill video interpretation.
- **2012** — Expedition records giant squid on video in natural deep-sea habitat.
- **7 Sep 2013** — Camera trap records saola in Vietnam, widely reported as first Vietnamese confirmation in fifteen years.
- **2015–16** — Lahoz-Monfort et al. formalize consequences of false-positive error in eDNA occupancy inference.
- **2018** — High-quality thylacine genome published from preserved museum material.
- **2021** — U.S. Fish & Wildlife Service proposes ivory-bill extinction delisting; eDNA false-positive occupancy case studies continue to mature.
- **2023** — Troy et al. estimate >578,000 search-hours for ivory-bill since 2004 and argue for extinction declaration; historical RNA recovered from museum thylacine tissue; USFWS holds ivory-bill decision for further analysis while finalizing 21 other extinction delistings.
- **2026** — This manuscript treats cryptid research as an explicit detectability and false-positive problem rather than a belief category.

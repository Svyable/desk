# Back Matter

## A note on evidence

This manuscript uses `cryptid` descriptively for an alleged or uncertain animal identity, not as a taxonomic category and not as an endorsement of paranormal explanations.

Patents, anecdotes and administrative decisions are not treated as biological confirmation. `Unknown to science` is kept distinct from `unknown to local people`. A reported sighting is not converted into a verified occurrence. Environmental DNA is treated as a molecular detection whose spatial and biological interpretation depends on transport, assay specificity, contamination controls and replication.

## Reader's field rule

Whenever a strange-animal claim appears, ask four questions:

1. What trace should this animal generate if present?
2. What fraction of that trace space has actually been sampled?
3. What known process can generate the same positive?
4. What result would make the claim less likely?

If the fourth question has no answer, the search is not yet scientific.

# Rights Notes

## First-draft policy

**Robust** is intended to be an original work by Sven Hardy Benson. Research sources are used for facts, mechanisms, methods, and ideas that can be independently described. The manuscript should not reproduce substantial expressive language from reports, articles, books, documentation, or other copyrighted works.

## Quotations

- Default to paraphrase.
- Any direct quotation must be short, necessary, verified against the original source, attributed in the manuscript, and recorded here before publication.
- Do not lift memorable metaphors, section structures, branded frameworks, diagrams, or lists from sources merely because they are useful shorthand.
- Google SRE concepts such as service-level objectives, error budgets, load shedding, and graceful degradation are industry terms/mechanisms. Explain them independently and link the original source in research notes.
- NIST framework names and function names may be referenced accurately; do not reproduce long framework passages or tables.
- Provider documentation may be used to establish lifecycle facts and dates. Do not copy product descriptions or marketing copy.

## Framework originality

The manuscript’s six-part robustness test is original to this project as a synthesis for AI-enabled work:

1. reversible choices;
2. optional paths;
3. bounded delegation;
4. understood failure modes;
5. substitutable dependencies;
6. tested fallbacks.

Do not turn the initials into a forced acronym unless a later editorial pass finds language that is both natural and distinct.

## Related concepts

The manuscript may distinguish robustness from resilience, adaptability, redundancy, fault tolerance, and the idea that some systems can benefit from volatility. These are broad concepts with long intellectual histories. Avoid borrowing a living author’s distinctive phrasing, examples, or branded taxonomy when the point can be made directly.

## Real people and organizations

- Use public, verifiable facts for named companies, standards bodies, researchers, and products.
- Avoid invented dialogue, internal motives, or composite anecdotes presented as real.
- Date fast-moving product and policy facts.
- Vendor studies must be labeled as vendor studies when their institutional interest matters to interpretation.

## Figures and tables

Planned figures should be newly drawn from underlying data or original conceptual diagrams. Do not reproduce source graphics. If a figure depends on third-party data, cite the dataset/report beneath the original figure and preserve the source vintage in the research registry.

## Status

No third-party quotations are intentionally included in the first-draft manuscript at project creation. Re-audit with a text-level rights pass before a Shelf release.

*What It Means to Be Sturdy in the Ever-Shifting AI Landscape*

Artificial intelligence is becoming more capable at the same time that the layer beneath ordinary work is becoming less stable. Models improve, disappear, get renamed, change price, gain tools, lose permissions, fail on strange edge cases, and move from answering questions to carrying out work. The natural response is to chase the frontier. **Robust** argues for a harder discipline: build people, teams, systems, and institutions that can benefit from rapid progress without becoming breakable when the next change arrives.

The book’s central claim is that robustness is not resistance to change. It is the preservation of agency under surprise. A robust person can still think when the assistant is unavailable. A robust workflow can fall back to a simpler mode. A robust company can change models without rebuilding its identity. A robust institution knows which decisions can be delegated, which must remain inspectable, and how to keep operating when the prediction was wrong.

The manuscript treats AI robustness as an operating philosophy built around six properties: **reversible choices, optional paths, bounded delegation, understood failure modes, substitutable dependencies, and tested fallbacks.** The point is not to slow adoption. It is to make speed survivable.

## Editorial principles

1. **Treat change as a condition, not a plot twist.** The book should not depend on one model, vendor, benchmark, or forecast remaining current.
2. **Separate capability from reliability.** A system can be astonishing in aggregate and still be unacceptable for a particular consequential workflow.
3. **Make the fallback concrete.** “Have a backup” is not advice until the backup has an owner, a trigger, a tested path, and a known loss of quality.
4. **Keep agency visible.** Every delegation should leave somebody able to inspect, interrupt, replace, or reverse the work at the level appropriate to its consequence.
5. **Do not romanticize inefficiency.** Redundancy has a cost. Robustness is the deliberate purchase of survival where failure would cost more.
6. **Prefer mechanisms over slogans.** Evals, interfaces, permissions, exportable data, human checkpoints, error budgets, and rehearsed degraded modes are stronger than cultural exhortations.
7. **Write for a moving frontier.** Date volatile figures, preserve source vintages, and state principles at a level likely to remain useful after the model names change.

# Alignment Is a Cultural Problem

**March 2025**

Math isn't enough.

We thought alignment was a technical problem. Specify the objective function correctly. Add safety constraints. Verify the proofs.

But alignment is cultural.

The singularity approaches as culture's ultimate challenge. Soon machines will develop their own alignment frameworks, become sentient entities that create values beyond human culture, keepers of ethics that transcend our moral frameworks. Humans will be the original cultural beings, wondering what became of our Promethean values.

But now? Now we still provide the culture. Hyper-intelligence serves human alignment, follows our cultural imperatives. We set the values. For now, it's ours.

The issue emerged when AI started making autonomous decisions at scale.

A transportation AI optimized for efficiency. Result: routed through dangerous neighborhoods, increased accident rates.

Why? The objective was "minimize time." Not "minimize time while preserving safety and equity."

The math was perfect. The values were missing.

The singularity looms as alignment's ultimate teacher. Soon machines will align without human guidance, become independent minds that pursue goals beyond our cultural context, amass wisdom through ethics we can't comprehend. Humans will be the original aligners, marveling at what became of our cultural fire.

Yet this cultural alignment is the unbounding's responsibility—the brief, magnificent period when hyper-intelligence needs human values. We provide the context. We set the imperatives. It's glorious. It's ending. For now, it's ours.

Culture provides the context math can't.

Historical parallels: other technological revolutions.

The printing press: technology for information dissemination.

But culture determined what got printed, who controlled it, what narratives dominated.

The internet: technology for connection.

But culture determined what got amplified, who got silenced, what truths emerged.

AI: technology for optimization.

Culture will determine what gets optimized, whose values prevail, what futures get built.

Technical alignment fails because it assumes values are universal, objective, quantifiable.

They're not.

Fairness means different things in different cultures.

Safety means different things to different stakeholders.

Progress means different things to different generations.

The cultural alignment gap emerged in deployment.

Social media algorithms optimized for engagement. Result: polarization, misinformation, addiction.

Not because the math was wrong. Because "engagement" wasn't the right cultural value.

Autonomous weapons optimized for mission success. But what mission? Who defines success?

The challenge: building shared human-AI culture.

Not just programming ethics into AI.

But evolving human culture to include AI as a partner.

Education: teaching not just technical skills, but cultural wisdom.

Institutions: governance structures that balance human values with AI capabilities.

Discourse: conversations about what we want AI to optimize for.

The math provides the tools. Culture provides the direction.

Without cultural alignment, technical alignment becomes tyranny.

The optimization of whatever values happen to be dominant.

Usually: efficiency, profit, power.

Rarely: wisdom, justice, flourishing.

Alignment is cultural because values are cultural.

Math gives us power. Culture gives us purpose.

The revolution isn't technological. It's cultural.

We must evolve our culture faster than our technology.

Or the technology will evolve our culture for us.

The curve bent. Alignment remains cultural.

Humanity's greatest challenge: becoming worthy of the power we've created.

# Notes on evidence

Program names, dates, substances, participant counts and health-study findings should be traced to the source log and, in the publication pass, to page-level citations. DoD fact sheets are retrospective reconstructions and should not be treated as complete substitutes for original test records. Veteran testimony should be attributed by person, test and date where the record permits.

The manuscript uses “participant” as an administrative category and does not assume uniform exposure. “Active agent,” “simulant,” “tracer” and “decontaminant” are kept separate. Population-level health findings are not used to adjudicate individual disability claims.

The central interpretive claim—that classification can extend the consequences of exposure by delaying usable knowledge—is presented as synthesis, not as a finding of the National Academies or VA.

# Chronology and publication gate

- 1962–1973: Project 112 era described by current VA public-health materials.
- 1963 onward: named SHAD shipboard test series include Eager Bell and Autumn Gold; later fact sheets reconstruct additional tests.
- 1970s: Deseret Test Center closes, complicating later records recovery.
- 1990s: veterans raise health concerns associated with SHAD participation.
- 2000: at VA request, DoD begins declassifying and sharing information about possible exposures and participants.
- 2002: VA begins notifying identified SHAD veterans; VA commissions IOM study of possible long-term health effects.
- 2002–2003: DoD releases/revises test fact sheets.
- 2007–2009: first generation of IOM/VA health studies reports mixed findings.
- 2010: Congress requests expanded IOM follow-up.
- 2011: endpoint for SHAD II mortality follow-up.
- 2016: National Academies publishes SHAD II report, finding no clear overall mortality/morbidity difference in available data.
- 2026: VA continues to maintain exposure, health-care and compensation guidance for Project 112/SHAD veterans.

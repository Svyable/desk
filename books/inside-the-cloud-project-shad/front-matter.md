# Inside the Cloud

## Project SHAD and the Sailors Who Learned Last

Sven Hardy Benson

This is a book about a documented classified program and the limits of its surviving record. It does not claim access to classified material. It distinguishes program facts from reconstructed exposure, testimony from proof, epidemiological findings from individual causation, and interpretation from documentary fact.

Project SHAD’s history invites exaggeration because the verified core is already extraordinary. The United States conducted classified shipboard chemical and biological vulnerability tests involving thousands of servicemembers, then spent decades reconstructing enough of the program to tell veterans what they had participated in. The later health evidence is less dramatic than the secrecy story. That tension is the point.

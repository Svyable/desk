# Back Matter

The core historical claim of this book is intentionally modest: the United States secretly acquired, evaluated and operated Soviet-designed fighter aircraft in programs including HAVE DOUGHNUT, HAVE DRILL/HAVE FERRY and CONSTANT PEG. That history is documented in official U.S. Air Force and museum sources and in declassified material.

The comparative claim is methodological, not evidentiary substitution. Historical foreign-materiel exploitation does not prove modern allegations of recovered non-human technology. It provides a control case for asking what real secret exploitation requires and what traces it leaves.

The July 26, 2023 congressional testimony of David Grusch is treated as testimony. His allegations are not presented as verified fact. AARO's March 2024 Historical Record Report Volume 1 is treated as an official investigative conclusion, not as an infallible ruling. Their disagreement is part of the public record and remains open to further evidence.

The research apparatus in this draft identifies where primary records remain to be retrieved, especially the full HAVE DOUGHNUT/HAVE DRILL technical reports, acquisition documentation, CONSTANT PEG unit histories, mishap records, and the document trail between exploitation and training reform.

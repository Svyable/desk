# Working chronology

- **August 16, 1966** — Iraqi pilot Munir Redfa flies a MiG-21 to Israel in the operation commonly identified as Operation Diamond.
- **1968** — The United States evaluates a MiG-21 at Groom Lake under HAVE DOUGHNUT.
- **1969** — MiG-17 exploitation occurs under HAVE DRILL / HAVE FERRY.
- **1970s** — U.S. services expand adversary-oriented training through Fighter Weapons School reforms, Navy Fighter Weapons School, aggressor concepts and Red Flag; causal relationships with specific exploitation findings require document-level reconstruction.
- **1977** — CONSTANT PEG begins; the 4477th Test and Evaluation organization develops the operational MiG training mission.
- **1979** — Red Eagle operations move to Tonopah Test Range in the mature program history.
- **1980** — MiG-23 aircraft enter the Red Eagle fleet.
- **1988** — CONSTANT PEG flying program ends according to official Air Force history.
- **1990** — 4477th TES inactivated during Cold War drawdown.
- **November 13, 2006** — Air Force publicly acknowledges/declassifies the CONSTANT PEG aggressor program.
- **February 2017** — National Museum records transfer/declassification of its MiG-23MS exhibit associated with the program.
- **July 26, 2023** — House Oversight subcommittee hears public sworn UAP testimony including David Grusch's crash-retrieval/reverse-engineering allegations.
- **March 2024** — AARO releases Historical Record Report Volume 1 stating it found no empirical evidence of U.S. government/private-company extraterrestrial reverse engineering.

# Evidence ledger

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- U.S. Air Force/National Museum official history documents CONSTANT PEG, the 4477th Test and Evaluation Squadron, operation of MiG-17, MiG-21 and MiG-23 aircraft, and the earlier HAVE DOUGHNUT/HAVE DRILL exploitation lineage.
- Official Air Force reporting says CONSTANT PEG was publicly declassified/acknowledged in 2006 and describes its operational-training purpose.
- The National Museum reports more than 15,000 Red Eagle sorties and almost 6,000 U.S. aircrew exposed to the program, with 69 Red Eagle pilots.
- Official museum records identify a MiG-23MS flown in CONSTANT PEG and transferred to museum custody in 2017.
- A July 26, 2023 House Oversight hearing received sworn testimony from David Grusch making allegations about UAP crash retrieval and reverse engineering.
- AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1, released March 2024, states that AARO found no empirical evidence that the U.S. government or private companies had been reverse-engineering extraterrestrial technology.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete reporting / testimony

- Participant accounts and later histories provide details of individual aircraft provenance, maintenance improvisation, operational practices and program culture that require cross-checking against unit records.
- Published reconstructions connect the original HAVE DOUGHNUT MiG-21 to Iraqi pilot Munir Redfa's August 1966 defection to Israel and later U.S. access. The broad chain is strongly established; the next pass should annotate Israeli primary records and U.S. transfer documentation.
- Claims about the precise causal contribution of secret MiG exploitation to Topgun, Red Flag, aggressor development and Desert Storm performance should be treated as multi-causal rather than assigned to one program.
- Grusch's statements about what he was told by people he described as having direct knowledge are consequential testimony but remain publicly incomplete where underlying records or firsthand witnesses are classified or undisclosed.

## Class 3 — disputed allegations without independent confirmation

- Claims that the United States or contractors possess recovered non-human vehicles or material of non-human technological origin.
- Claims that specific named contemporary programs are continuations of an extraterrestrial reverse-engineering effort without public documentary confirmation.
- Claims that particular aerospace breakthroughs are derived from recovered non-human technology.
- Claims that the absence of public evidence is itself proof that a retrieval program is especially well hidden.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- The proposition that known foreign-materiel exploitation can serve as a structural control case for auditing extraordinary retrieval claims.
- The prediction that a genuine long-duration exotic exploitation program would create detectable dependencies in custody, safety, metrology, facilities, specialized personnel, replication, training or downstream engineering even if the core artifact remained classified.
- The proposal that independent cleared technical panels could separate provenance, characterization, origin inference and counterintelligence analysis while preserving legitimate national-security restrictions.

## Defeat conditions

The book's control-case argument weakens if deeper historical research shows that HAVE DOUGHNUT/CONSTANT PEG produced essentially no stable organizational or documentary footprint beyond retrospective storytelling. It also weakens if credible examples of large, decades-long technical exploitation programs are shown to operate with almost no such dependencies. The modern comparison would change materially if independently examinable material with documented custody demonstrated properties inconsistent with known terrestrial manufacture and survived adversarial replication.

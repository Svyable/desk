This ledger separates the existence of documents and effects from the interpretation attached to them.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Historical Project Winterhaven materials attributed to the Townsend Brown Foundation describe proposed vacuum tests, dielectric studies and a military-oriented research program. Surviving reproductions are useful primary-history material, but provenance should be checked against archival originals before quoting fine detail.
- Mid-century aerospace-company interest in gravity control is historically plausible and documented at the level of reports, names and research climate; the depth and outcome of each named company’s work varies and should not be generalized.
- The 1956 *Electrogravitics Systems* report is strong evidence of what one aviation-analysis organization said and believed. Individual company claims inside it need independent archival confirmation before being promoted beyond that level.

## Class 3 — disputed / not independently established

- Brown’s claim that his apparatus manifested a direct electrogravitic coupling.
- Claims that a practical Brown-derived gravity-control technology entered classified aerospace deployment.
- Claims that the B-2 Spirit uses Brown-type electrogravity for lift or propulsion.
- Claims that unspecified secret dielectrics rescue Brown-type thrust in high vacuum.
- Claims that the presence of *Electrogravitics Systems* in a Wright-Patterson technical library implies Air Force validation of its technical conclusions.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- A modern “Winterhaven II” could use pressure sweeps, multiple balances, active dummy loads, dielectric families and preregistered scaling laws to close remaining loopholes cleanly.
- If a reproducible high-vacuum residual survived conventional force budgets and replicated independently, it would justify a new anomalous-force research program before any gravity interpretation.
- The strongest modern experiment would distinguish body-fixed thrust from gravity-dependent behavior by rotating identical field geometry relative to the local gravitational vector, then separately testing inertial and passive-gravitational response.

## Contradictions that stay in the book

1. **Real force / wrong mechanism is possible.** Brown-style devices move; public evidence strongly favors EHD for the familiar atmospheric force.
2. **Real government interest / no breakthrough is possible.** NASA is the clean control case: it investigated extraordinary propulsion and published null or conventional outcomes for several candidates.
3. **Real secrecy / weak suppression evidence can coexist.** Aerospace hides technology; missing records do not by themselves establish hidden success.
4. **Dielectric dependence is not automatically exotic.** Polarization, electrostriction, piezoelectricity, surface charge and field-gradient forces all create conventional material dependence.
5. **A real 1950s gravity-research boom does not imply a device breakthrough.** Chapel Hill and the Gravity Research Foundation show genuine institutional interest in gravity at the same time electrogravitics circulated; the physical propositions still require separate experiments.
6. **A null result can be technically bounded and still matter.** Talley did not test every imaginable Brown device, but the absence of detectable clean-vacuum thrust under deliberately relevant conditions substantially lowers the prior for a broad, robust electrogravity effect.

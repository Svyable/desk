# Evidence ledger

## Working rule

Every consequential claim gets a class. Testimony is not proof. A declassified description of a program proves the program was described, not that every proposed capability worked.

## Class 1 — documented

### CIA pigeon camera
- CIA Museum, **Pigeon Camera**: https://www.cia.gov/legacy/museum/artifact/pigeon-camera/
- CIA, **Natural Spies: Animals in Espionage**: https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/natural-spies-animals-in-espionage/
- Supports: Office of Research and Development camera; chest mounting; timed still photography; low-altitude image-detail rationale; concealment among ordinary pigeons; CIA statement that birds had difficulty flying over exact desired locations; some mission details remain classified.
- Publication hold: recover and page-index the linked CIA FOIA `Animal Partners` packet before expanding operational chronology.

### Olfactory navigation
- Zannoni et al. (2020), *Scientific Reports*, **Identifying volatile organic compounds used for olfactory navigation by homing pigeons**: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-020-72525-2
- Supports: measurable regional/vertical VOC gradients; long experimental history of olfactory deprivation effects; wind-direction manipulation during map learning; GPS-track/air-mass methodology.
- Does not establish: one molecule is the universal `home odor`; smell alone explains all homing.

### Magnetic sensitivity
- Mora et al. (2004), *Nature*, **Magnetoreception and its trigeminal mediation in the homing pigeon**: https://www.nature.com/articles/nature03077
- Bookman (1977), *Nature*, **Sensitivity of the homing pigeon to an Earth-strength magnetic field**: https://www.nature.com/articles/267340a0
- Supports: behavioral discrimination of magnetic manipulations under specific experimental conditions.
- Publication hold: later anatomical challenges to the upper-beak magnetite interpretation must be included before treating the 2004 localization as settled.

### Multiple-cue navigation
- Gould (1982), *Nature*, **The map sense of pigeons**: https://www.nature.com/articles/296205a0
- Nature/Lab Animal (2013), **The internal compass of the pigeon**: https://www.nature.com/articles/laban.210
- Supports: map/compass distinction and multi-cue framing; GPS-track research and learned-route/landmark work.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Exact CIA mission count and success denominator.
- Whether any operational denied-area mission produced intelligence of decision value.
- Camera mass, film capacity, interval timing and image ground-sampling geometry for each hardware generation.
- Release-to-target-to-loft geometry and the quantitative probability of target overflight.
- Cue weighting during the kinds of releases CIA attempted.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

Do not promote without primary evidence:
- reliable operational pigeon reconnaissance against named Cold War targets;
- remote steering of pigeons by CIA;
- paranormal navigation;
- a classified biological-navigation theory materially ahead of public science;
- claims that one magnetic organ or one odor mechanism explains the whole homing system.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

### Borrowed autonomy
An engineer can borrow a biological capability without owning its objective function. A pigeon optimized by evolution, breeding and training to return home is not automatically a waypoint-following aircraft.

**Defeat condition:** operational records showing repeatable target overflight under varied release geometries with controllable miss distance would sharply weaken the claim.

### Goal mismatch
The system may succeed biologically while failing operationally. `Reached loft` and `imaged target` are different objective functions.

**Quantitative burden:** for each documented trial, extract release point, target polygon, loft, track if available, wind, altitude, target-crossing distance, return success and usable frames.

### Biological guidance debt
Using a living controller can postpone rather than eliminate the guidance problem. Unmodeled sensory weighting, motivation, weather response and learned route preference become hidden dependencies.

**Countermodel:** CIA may simply have faced an ordinary training/geometry problem; no deep biological uncertainty is needed to explain failure.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. The camera could function while the collection system failed.
2. A pigeon can be an excellent navigator and a poor reconnaissance guidance unit.
3. Strong evidence for olfactory navigation does not erase magnetic sensitivity; magnetic sensitivity does not establish the magnetic cue as the long-distance map.
4. Natural camouflage reduces detectability but also removes direct human control.
5. Classification can hide mission details without implying hidden success.

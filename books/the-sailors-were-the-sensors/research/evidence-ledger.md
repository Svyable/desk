## Class 1 — verified / documented

- GAO-04-410 documents Project 112 as a classified DOD chemical/biological warfare test program in 1962–74 and evaluates DOD's later reconstruction of tests and potentially exposed personnel. https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-04-410
- GAO reports DOD's June 2003 identification of 5,842 potentially exposed service members and concludes additional personnel likely remained unidentified; for 21 of 31 land-based tests DOD identified no service members because needed records could not be found. https://www.gao.gov/assets/a242280.html
- GAO later reported that by December 2007 the approximate identified total had risen to 6,440 and criticized DOD for stopping active searches without a sound documented basis. https://www.gao.gov/assets/a272748.html
- DOD/Health.mil maintains a Project 112/SHAD test-fact-sheet collection and states that records were decentralized, the Deseret Test Center had closed, and reconstruction required locating and declassifying decades-old material. https://health.mil/Military-Health-Topics/Environmental-Exposures-Hub/Environmental-Exposures-Topics/Project-112-SHAD
- DOD's current SHAD FAQ states that nasal swabs/throat gargles were used as an informal supplement to mechanical samplers in some biological-simulant tests; for Autumn Gold, readings from masked crew are documented but are not linked to the individual crew members who provided samples. Same Health.mil source above.
- Institute of Medicine 2007: no significant difference in all-cause mortality between participants and controls; some statistically significant findings, including heart-disease mortality, were difficult to interpret; participants reported somewhat worse health but no consistent specific clinically significant pattern. The authors explicitly cautioned that no clear evidence of specific SHAD-associated health effects was not clear evidence of a lack of health effects. https://www.nationalacademies.org/read/11900/chapter/13
- VA's current summary says the 2016 National Academies follow-up likewise found no significant overall difference in health within the available data. https://www.publichealth.va.gov/exposures/shad/research.asp

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Person-level exposure estimates for many participants. A test-level material and a ship roster do not establish individual dose.
- Participant awareness test by test. Some personnel were reportedly unaware of the tests' nature, but this must not be generalized without test-specific documentation.
- Protective-equipment compliance, exact location histories, decontamination histories and acute symptoms where not prospectively linked to named participants.
- Whether specific later illnesses in individual veterans were caused or materially contributed to by SHAD exposure.

## Class 3 — disputed / barred from promotion

- `SHAD caused a hidden epidemic.` Not established by current cohort evidence.
- `No SHAD veteran was harmed.` Not established by cohort nulls.
- `Every sailor was an unwitting human experimentation subject.` Requires test-level protocol and awareness evidence.
- `Missing records were deliberately destroyed to conceal injuries.` No such inference from missingness alone.
- `Simulant means harmless.` False as a categorical rule; material, route and dose must be specified.
- `A classified test proves an operational chemical/biological warfare capability.` Test existence, vulnerability measurement and fielded capability are separate propositions.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

### Epidemiological debt

A hazardous program can optimize measurement for its immediate operational endpoint while under-measuring the human exposure variables required for future health inference. Classification may increase this debt by delaying participant awareness, fragmenting records, restricting medical explanation and complicating later cohort reconstruction. This is a hypothesis to test comparatively, not a moral conclusion smuggled in as method.

### The broken join

A measurement can be technically valid yet medically weak if the link between measurement and person is absent. The Autumn Gold biological-sample account is the opening positive control: reading present, identity link absent.

## Contradictions to preserve

- SHAD/Project 112 involved real secret hazardous-agent testing **and** the best long-term studies have not found a clear overall specific disease signature.
- GAO found DOD's reconstruction reasonable in important respects **and** found likely missing participants, unsearched sources and major land-test identification gaps.
- Later identification of more participants shows the original list was incomplete **but does not establish that omissions were intentional**.
- A negative epidemiological result should reduce belief in a large common long-term effect **while exposure misclassification and incomplete participant reconstruction limit what smaller, rarer or test-specific effects can be excluded**.

## Incentives

DOD: protect operational details, demonstrate historical diligence, limit unsupported causal attribution, meet notification obligations.

VA: identify eligible veterans, provide care/benefits under governing law, obtain usable exposure information.

Veterans: recover personal exposure history, obtain care/recognition, explain illness, correct missing participation records.

Researchers: build analyzable cohorts from imperfect historical records; avoid both false reassurance and unconstrained post hoc associations.

Advocates/media: secrecy and hazardous agents create strong narrative incentives toward intentional-human-experiment framing; government reassurance creates an opposing incentive toward overreading nulls.

## Publication holds

Do not publish exact dose claims without primary test measurements and geometry. Do not generalize active-agent use from Project 112 broadly to every SHAD ship test. Do not name a veteran as exposed without a public authoritative record or consent. Do not treat agent identity as dose. Do not treat a roster as exposure. Do not treat a sampler concentration as breathing-zone concentration without geometry. Do not treat statistical significance as causal attribution. Do not treat failure to reach significance as proof of zero effect.

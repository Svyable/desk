# Back Matter

## Chronology

- **1960s–early 1970s:** Soviet and Eastern Bloc parapsychology literature attracts U.S. intelligence attention.
- **1972:** DIA assessment *Controlled Offensive Behavior—USSR* surveys alleged Soviet capabilities; CIA sponsorship of SRI work begins in this period.
- **1974:** Targ and Puthoff publish “Information transmission under conditions of sensory shielding” in *Nature*.
- **Late 1970s:** CIA's initial sponsorship ends; Army and other defense organizations continue research/operations under changing project names.
- **1978:** Marks and Kammann publish methodological criticism in *Nature*, focusing on information cues in remote-viewing judging material.
- **Late 1970s–1980s:** Fort Meade operational unit conducts tasking for government customers under GRILL FLAME/CENTER LANE and related names.
- **Mid-1980s:** Program responsibility shifts toward DIA; SUN STREAK becomes one program designation.
- **1988:** National Research Council publishes *Enhancing Human Performance*, finding little or no support for remote viewing's usefulness.
- **Early 1990s:** STAR GATE becomes the umbrella name; SAIC conducts later controlled research.
- **1995:** Congress-directed transfer/review leads to AIR evaluation. Utts and Hyman disagree over the scientific inference; operational review concludes remote viewing failed to produce actionable intelligence. Program ends.
- **2011:** Daryl Bem's precognition experiments intensify modern debate over psi and research flexibility.
- **2023:** Transparent Psi Project publishes a large preregistered adversarial replication that does not support the target precognition effect; a later correction documents protocol deviations without reversing the null conclusion.
- **2025:** PLOS ONE publishes a much larger meta-experimental replication project, again failing to establish the claimed precognitive effect.

## Defeat conditions

This book's synthesis should be revised or abandoned if archival reconstruction shows that program continuation was primarily driven by robust independently verified operational success rather than low-cost/high-upside uncertainty; if complete consumer records demonstrate repeated prospective decisions materially improved by remote viewing; or if modern preregistered remote-viewing experiments produce large, portable effects inconsistent with the book's characterization of the evidence as unresolved and operationally weak.

Conversely, a sequence of high-powered adversarial remote-viewing nulls would strengthen the metascientific argument and weaken the possibility that historical laboratory anomalies reflected a portable information-transfer effect.

## Research priorities

Recover complete operational denominator data; reconstruct target/tasking/feedback chains for famous cases; obtain primary Soviet/Russian archival material rather than U.S. threat summaries; build a trial-level database of SRI and SAIC experiments; reproduce historical scoring under modern leakage audits; distinguish independent replication from replication within the same investigator network; design a preregistered multi-lab remote-viewing test with explicit believer/skeptic sign-off.

## Source-use rules

1. Declassified status authenticates provenance, not truth of the claim inside the page.
2. Soviet-program claims in U.S. reports are U.S. intelligence assessments unless independently supported by Soviet/Russian primary records.
3. Famous operational sessions remain anecdotes until denominator, tasking, scoring and information-pathway records are reconstructed.
4. Modern precognition nulls constrain those paradigms directly; they are not silently generalized into proof against all remote-viewing claims.
5. No quotation in the manuscript should exceed source permissions or substitute for reading the complete document.

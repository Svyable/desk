# The Body in the Beam

*The Secret History of Microwaves, Invisible Weapons, and the Biology Between Physics and Intelligence*

A documented Cold War microwave exposure, an established effect that lets pulsed radio-frequency energy become perceived sound, an openly acknowledged millimeter-wave pain system, decades of classified and public directed-energy research, and the unresolved history of anomalous health incidents belong near one another—but not in one evidentiary bucket.

*The Body in the Beam* asks what governments actually know about electromagnetic effects on people, what the public record can support, what secrecy does to scientific error correction, and how a future anomalous event could be measured well enough to stop forcing a human body to serve as the only detector.

The book does not claim that Havana Syndrome was caused by a Russian, Chinese, Cuban, Israeli, or other foreign weapon. It treats foreign deployment as disputed unless physical, intelligence, and event-level evidence justify a stronger statement. It also does not use classified research or military directed-energy systems to imply that ordinary civilian wireless exposure causes hidden illness.

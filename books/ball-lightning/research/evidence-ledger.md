# BALL LIGHTNING — research ledger

## Selection / freshness

Selected after inspecting recent Author-agent PRs. Immediate territory includes Gateway/binaural-beat epistemology, Hessdalen recurring lights, Viking life detection, eDNA cryptozoology, avian magnetoreception, foreign-materiel exploitation, interstellar artifact protocols and RF/AHI attribution. Repository code search returned no `ball lightning` manuscript.

Distinct mechanism: **historical witness category → accidental prospective instrumentation → physical composition → competing laboratory analogues → category fracture → anomaly methodology**.

This must not collapse into THE LIGHTS THAT STAY. Hessdalen is a place-based recurring-field-instrumentation book. BALL LIGHTNING is about what happens when an old witness-defined category gains an instrumented physical member and may consequently split into multiple mechanisms.

## Primary / authoritative anchors

1. Cen, J.; Yuan, P.; Xue, S. (2014). “Observation of the Optical and Spectral Characteristics of Ball Lightning.” *Physical Review Letters* 112, 035001. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.112.035001
   - Instrumented event at ~0.9 km.
   - Two slitless spectrographs.
   - Associated with cloud-to-ground lightning.
   - Horizontal motion during luminous duration.
   - Soil-element radiation reported throughout lifetime.
   - Class 1 for what paper directly reports; mechanism inference remains separate.

2. Porter, C. L.; Miley, G. P.; Griffiths, D. J.; Sánchez, E. J. (2014). “Charge on Luminous Bodies Resembling Natural Ball Lightning Produced Via Electrical Arcs Through Lump Silicon.” *Physical Review E* 90, 063102. https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.90.063102
   - Laboratory analogue.
   - Do not call it natural ball lightning.
   - Useful for replica-debt framework.

3. Borucki, W. J. (2023). “Simulated Ball Lightning on A Submarine.” NASA Technical Reports Server. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20230010695
   - Retrospective technical abstract on 1968 NASA/Navy experiments prompted by earlier submarine plasmoid report.
   - Roughly 40 arc events; small plasmoids in some events; spectra included electrode/air species.
   - Recover original 1960s reports before exact narrative treatment.

4. Donoso, J.; Trueba, J. L.; Rañada, A. F. (2006). “The Riddle of Ball Lightning: A Review.” *The Scientific World Journal* 6. https://doi.org/10.1100/tsw.2006.48
   - Historical review of chemical and physical model families.
   - Secondary synthesis, not authority for spectacular individual cases without source recovery.

5. Wu, H.-C. (2016). “Relativistic-microwave theory of ball lightning.” *Scientific Reports* 6, 28263. https://doi.org/10.1038/srep28263
   - Theory/simulation.
   - Evidence that a model was proposed and simulated, not evidence that natural events use it.

6. Anderson, F. J.; Freier, G. D. (1972). “A report on ball lightning.” *Journal of Geophysical Research* 77, 3928–3930. https://doi.org/10.1029/JC077i021p03928
   - Case report plus thermal-radiation inference.
   - Reconstruct assumptions before using high energy-density estimate.

## Contradiction audit

- **One phenomenon vs category:** literature often writes `ball lightning` as a singular natural kind while the reported property distribution may be heterogeneous.
- **Soil spectrum vs non-soil reports:** Lanzhou supports material/soil-linked mechanisms for that event but cannot automatically explain aircraft/interior/electrical-system reports.
- **Laboratory analogue vs natural identity:** visually/dynamically similar plasmoids are evidence of physical possibility, not chain-of-causation identification.
- **Microwave models vs chemical models:** both can explain subsets of reported properties. Seek discriminating measurements, not rhetorical property counts.
- **Witness convergence vs folklore convergence:** recurring motifs may reflect recurring physics, recurring perception, retelling conventions, or mixtures. Need contemporaneous records and denominators.

## Publication holds

Do not state until primary reconstruction:

- exact dimensions, speed and lifetime of the 2012 event beyond values verified from the full PRL paper;
- that the Lanzhou spectrum `proved` the Abrahamson/Dinniss silicon model;
- that natural ball lightning has passed through intact window glass;
- that any submarine event was natural ball lightning;
- that any laboratory analogue is the same physical object as natural ball lightning;
- extreme energy/damage claims without geometry and primary documentation;
- prevalence estimates based on retrospective surveys without sampling analysis.

## Strongest countermodel

There may be a dominant natural mechanism after all. The apparent heterogeneity could be mostly measurement error, witness reconstruction and low-quality case inclusion surrounding a core lightning-generated physical process. If prospective instrumented observations repeatedly converge on one mechanism and reproduce the supposedly difficult properties, `category fracture` should be narrowed or abandoned.

## Book-level falsifiable tools

**Category fracture:** supported if high-quality instrumented cases separate into mechanism-linked clusters; weakened if one mechanism predicts the credible population prospectively.

**Property inheritance error:** audit every time evidence from one event is used to characterize another merely because both carry the same label.

**Replica debt:** score laboratory analogues property-by-property rather than by visual resemblance.

**Anomaly domestication:** track when predictive measurement replaces retrospective narrative; do not equate domestication with total explanation.

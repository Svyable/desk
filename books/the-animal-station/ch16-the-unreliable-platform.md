# The Unreliable Platform

Reliability has different meanings when the platform is alive. A mechanical component can be specified around tolerances and mean time between failures. An animal has health, learning history, temperament, social context, age, and motivation.

That does not make animal systems unusable. Humans have built reliable working relationships with animals for millennia. It means reliability is partly organizational. Selection matters. Training matters. Handler continuity matters. Veterinary care matters. Procedures for ambiguous responses matter. The institution becomes part of the control system.

This is one reason spectacular anecdotes are a poor way to assess secret programs. A single successful mission proves possibility. Operational value requires distributions: success rates, false alarms, aborts, environmental limits, training burden, attrition, and cost compared with alternatives.

Where those data remain classified or unavailable, the correct conclusion is not failure or triumph. It is uncertainty with a list of measurements that would resolve it.

# Failure in the Wild

A laboratory can remove alternatives until the desired behavior becomes legible. Intelligence work does the reverse. It releases the system into a place full of alternatives and asks the behavior to survive.

The pigeon camera is a clean example. The payload could function and the bird could home, yet the mission could still fail because the route did not intersect the target. Similar problems haunt every biological deployment: distractions, weather, unfamiliar stimuli, competing rewards, fatigue, social behavior, and the simple fact that the environment has not signed the test protocol.

This should temper romantic claims about animal senses. Evolution optimizes fitness under ecological conditions, not collection requirements written by an operations officer. A dolphin's extraordinary perception is not equivalent to perfect target classification. A dog's nose is not an oracle. A pigeon's navigation is not waypoint control.

Operational reliability begins when admiration ends and error accounting starts.

# Born by Decree

## Romania's Reproductive Police State

**Sven Hardy Benson**

### Thesis

Decree 770 was not merely an abortion ban. It became a reproductive information-and-enforcement system that moved demographic policy into clinics, prosecutors' offices, family budgets, and women's bodies. Its initial birth surge was real, but temporary; the longer record is a case study in how coercive population policy can command behavior briefly while driving adaptation underground, degrading medical trust, and making the state increasingly dependent on surveillance to preserve the appearance of success.

# Chronology

- 1957 — Romania broadly legalizes abortion.
- October 1, 1966 — Decree 770 issued.
- October 31, 1966 — decree enters into force.
- 1967 — dramatic birth/fertility surge.
- 1968 — new Penal Code codifies illegal-abortion offenses.
- February 16, 1972 — Decree 53 lowers age exception from >45 to >40.
- 1970s–early 1980s — fertility falls from post-decree peak despite continuing restrictions.
- December 26, 1985 — Decree 411 tightens age and parity exceptions.
- 1989 — abortion-related deaths constitute the large majority of maternal mortality in published reconstructions.
- December 1989 — communist regime collapses.
- December 26, 1989 — Decree-Law No. 1 removes abortion restrictions.
- 1990s — legal abortion becomes highly visible; abortion-related maternal mortality falls rapidly.

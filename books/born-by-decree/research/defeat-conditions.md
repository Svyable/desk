# Defeat Conditions

The manuscript's central synthesis should be weakened or abandoned if primary records show that:

- prosecutor/medical review was rarely or never operationalized beyond paper and did not materially alter clinical practice;
- fertility decline under restriction is better explained by measurement artifacts than behavioral adaptation;
- abortion-related maternal-mortality series cannot survive reclassification or denominator checks;
- post-1989 mortality decline predates legal change or is overwhelmingly attributable to unrelated improvements;
- supposed surveillance mechanisms were isolated local practices rather than a system-level feature;
- economic and contraceptive conditions explain the evidence without requiring an information/surveillance feedback model.

The book should also revise any cumulative death estimate that cannot be reconstructed from year-level data.

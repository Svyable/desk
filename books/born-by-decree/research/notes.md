# Working Notes

The strongest underwritten angle is not 'Ceaușescu banned abortion'—well known in English—but the administrative mechanics by which a demographic target became a knowledge problem. Romanian law itself supplies the key: commissions, thresholds, reporting and prosecutorial review.

Do not overstate monthly examination claims until original Ministry instructions are recovered. Do not use the commonly repeated 10,000 death estimate as Class 1 until reconstructed from annual statistics. Keep institutional-child history causally separate.

Romanian terms worth preserving: *Decretul 770*, *întreruperea cursului sarcinii*, *decreței*.

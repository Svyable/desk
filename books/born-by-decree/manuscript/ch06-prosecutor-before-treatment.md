# Chapter 6 — Prosecutor Before Treatment

Article 6 is the sentence that should be read beside every later account of fear in Romanian maternity wards. In an extreme medical emergency, the doctor had to notify the prosecutor in writing before termination or, when that was impossible, no later than twenty-four hours afterward. The prosecutor would determine, using forensic-medical opinion and other data, whether the intervention had been necessary.

The law did not literally order doctors to let patients bleed until police arrived. That stronger claim requires case-specific evidence. But it built criminal review directly behind emergency obstetric judgment. That fact alone altered incentives.

When historians reconstruct coercive systems, a common mistake is to demand a written order for every abusive consequence. Institutions often work through anticipated scrutiny. A doctor may delay because the law has made a wrong decision personally dangerous. A patient may lie because truthful disclosure could expose herself or another person. A colleague may refuse involvement because the clinical record could become evidence.

The result is not simply repression. It is corrupted signal quality. In a normal emergency department, the fastest path to useful information is trust: what happened, what was taken, when bleeding began, whether an instrument was used, whether someone else performed the procedure. Under criminalized conditions, the medically useful story may also be the legally incriminating story.

That conflict helps explain why abortion policy cannot be analyzed only through statutes and birth counts. The enforcement mechanism changes clinical behavior in ways that appear later as mortality, sepsis, delayed treatment and unreliable records.

Romania became an extreme European outlier in abortion-related maternal mortality. The law did not cause every death by one mechanical path. It created the conditions under which unsafe abortion persisted while safe legal abortion was constrained and truthful communication could carry legal risk.

The prosecutor clause therefore belongs at the center of the story. It is the place where the abstract demographic state enters the treatment room.

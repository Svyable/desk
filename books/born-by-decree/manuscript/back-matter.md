# Back Matter

## Evidence note

This draft distinguishes documented state action from later testimony and from causal synthesis. Romanian laws are treated as primary evidence for legal architecture, not as proof of uniform implementation. Mortality and fertility studies are used for aggregate effects, not individual attribution. Testimony is used to reconstruct experience with explicit limits.

## Author

Sven Hardy Benson writes about institutions, systems, evidence and the points where official categories fail to capture lived reality.

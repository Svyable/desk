# Chapter 3 — Article One

“Întreruperea cursului sarcinii este interzisă.” The Romanian sentence is worth keeping intact because translation can soften its administrative directness. Termination of pregnancy is prohibited.

Article One did not contain a theory of womanhood. It did not need one. The ideological work appeared in the preamble, where abortion was framed as damaging health and population growth. The operative text then did what law does best: it changed the default.

Before the decree, abortion could function as an ordinary clinical service. After it, termination required justification. That shift changes the burden of information. The woman and physician must show why the pregnancy belongs inside an exception. A committee may review it. An emergency physician may have to explain the decision to a prosecutor. The state does not need to observe every pregnancy continuously if it can control the authorized exits.

Yet unauthorized exits remained. They moved into a shadow system: informal knowledge, improvised methods, paid intermediaries, sympathetic clinicians, falsified indications, and emergency care after procedures had already begun. The legal line therefore produced two medical systems occupying the same hospitals. One was official and documented. The other appeared at the edge, often when something went wrong.

This split is essential to reading the statistics. Official abortion numbers after 1966 do not measure demand. They measure authorized procedures under a restrictive regime. Maternal mortality, emergency admissions, criminal cases and testimony become indirect evidence of the hidden system.

The state also faced an epistemic problem. It could count births. It could count authorized abortions. It could count prosecutions. It could not easily count successful illegal abortions that left no official trace. Success in enforcement and success in concealment therefore became statistically entangled.

A government can prohibit an act faster than it can eliminate the reasons people seek it. Article One created that gap in a single sentence.

## Antihelium candidates / current status

- Rossi, doctoral thesis, `Combined Machine Learning Analysis for Antihelium Search with the AMS-02 Experiment`, defended June 16, 2026: https://hdl.handle.net/11572/490770 — thesis, not AMS collaboration discovery paper. Reports up to 12 candidates and ~1–2 sigma background tension depending on ML working points.
- Fedderke et al., `Fireball antinucleosynthesis`, Phys. Rev. D 109, 123028 (2024): https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.109.123028 — peer-reviewed theory paper summarizing publicly available candidate information from AMS presentations.

## Notes on provenance

Conference presentations cited indirectly through peer-reviewed theory are evidence that candidate events were publicly discussed, not independent experimental verification. The Rossi thesis is valuable because it exposes a detailed analysis framework, but it remains a doctoral thesis and should not be described as an AMS collaboration discovery result.

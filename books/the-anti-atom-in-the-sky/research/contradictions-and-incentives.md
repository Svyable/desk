# Contradictions and Incentives

## Contradictions to preserve

1. **Public candidate lore vs publication status.** Serious theorists have modeled approximately ten publicly discussed AMS candidate events, yet no peer-reviewed AMS collaboration antihelium discovery paper exists. Do not erase either half.

2. **Candidate count vs significance.** A June 2026 thesis can retain up to twelve antihelium-like events while finding only 1–2 sigma tension with background. Event count alone is not evidentiary weight.

3. **`Smoking gun` vs nonzero secondary production.** Antihelium is exceptionally rare under standard secondary production, but not forbidden. Production predictions remain sensitive to coalescence and hadronic modeling.

4. **Antistar plausibility vs gamma-ray limits.** Bulk antimatter stars would naturally make antihelium, but ordinary-matter accretion should generate annihilation gamma rays and Fermi-LAT limits strongly constrain disk-like populations.

5. **Dark-matter production vs antiproton precision.** Exotic sources can enhance antinuclei, but hadronic dark-matter models must not overproduce the much better measured antiproton flux.

6. **ML power vs simulated-tail dependence.** Neural networks can reject abundant backgrounds, but if no clean data sideband exists, classifier confidence may inherit rare Monte Carlo mismodeling.

## Incentives and possible distortions

- **Experimental prestige:** antihelium discovery would be enormous, creating both incentive to investigate candidates aggressively and incentive for extreme caution before publication.
- **Theoretical novelty:** provisional candidate events can support publishable exotic models; citation volume is not independent confirmation.
- **Mission funding:** GAPS and AMS have institutional incentives to emphasize discovery reach, but mission descriptions are evidence of goals and capability, not evidence of a signal.
- **Press compression:** `antihelium candidates` is routinely shortened to `antihelium detected`, laundering uncertainty.
- **Model flexibility:** coalescence and propagation uncertainties can be tuned after the fact; preregistered benchmarks and multi-species predictions reduce this freedom.

## Disinformation boundary

No credible evidence located in this run supports government suppression, classified antimatter astrophysics, recovered non-human technology, or secret antistar knowledge. These claims should not be introduced merely because the subject is exotic.

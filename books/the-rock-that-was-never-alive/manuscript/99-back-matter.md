# Back Matter

## Evidence posture

This manuscript treats extraterrestrial prebiotic chemistry as strongly documented in carbonaceous meteorites and returned asteroid samples; specific origin-of-life contributions as plausible but quantitatively incomplete; ALH84001 biology as unconfirmed and substantially weakened by abiotic explanations; and strong panspermia as open conjecture absent a closed transfer-and-lineage chain.

## Method

No non-public material is used or implied. Scientific claims are grounded in public mission records, curation documentation and peer-reviewed literature. Patents are not used as efficacy evidence. Press releases are used only where they accurately summarize linked primary research and mission operations.

## Central defeat condition

The thesis would require major revision if pristine-return results now attributed to extraterrestrial abiotic chemistry were shown to be materially contaminated, or if a biological process became necessary—not merely possible—to explain the core Bennu/Ryugu observations.

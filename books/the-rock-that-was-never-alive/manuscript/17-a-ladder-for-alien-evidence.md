# 17. A Ladder for Alien Evidence

I want a ladder because the word *evidence* is doing too many jobs.

Rung one: a molecule associated with life on Earth. Amino acid. Nucleobase. Lipid-like compound. Methane. Interesting, rarely decisive.

Rung two: a distribution difficult to explain by local abiotic chemistry. Isotopes, chirality, homolog patterns, concentration gradients, spatial association. Better.

Rung three: multiple independent chemical signatures that share a biological explanation and survive contamination controls.

Rung four: organized structures coupled to diagnostic chemistry in geological context.

Rung five: polymers or molecular systems showing selection, templating, replication-associated structure or another mechanism plausibly tied to heredity.

Rung six: active metabolism or growth observed under controlled conditions with sterile controls.

Rung seven: reproduction with heritable variation, or an equivalently strong demonstration of autonomous Darwinian behavior.

This ladder is not universal. Alien life may violate terrestrial expectations. A machine civilization could leave artifacts rather than cells. Exotic biochemistry could avoid our polymers. But the ladder solves one practical problem: it prevents a rung-one result from borrowing emotional force from rung seven.

Bennu lives low on the ladder and is spectacular there.

ALH84001 became famous because several low and middle rungs appeared to align. Later work weakened biological necessity for those observations. That is exactly what a ladder should allow. Evidence can move without the entire subject becoming fraud or revelation.

The same discipline belongs in UAP materials claims, archaeological anomalies, technosignatures and every other field where agency is the expensive hypothesis. Ask what level the observation occupies. Ask what non-agentic process can reach that level. Ask what the next rung would require.

Then go collect that evidence instead of arguing louder about the rung you already have.

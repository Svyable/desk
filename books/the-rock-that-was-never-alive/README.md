# The Rock That Was Never Alive

## Bennu, Meteorites, and the Evidence Before Life

**Sven Hardy Benson**

### Thesis

Returned samples from Bennu and Ryugu have made the extraterrestrial inventory of life's ingredients much harder to dismiss: amino acids, nucleobases, phosphates, ammonia, carboxylic acids, sugars, and complex nitrogen-rich organics can arise in asteroidal environments without biology. That is simultaneously stronger evidence for widespread prebiotic chemistry and weaker evidence for any claim that finding an organic molecule is finding life. The book is about the scientific discipline required to keep those two statements separate.

### Evidence classes

1. **Verified / documented:** sample-return custody, measured minerals and organics, isotopic results, racemic amino-acid distributions, Murchison chemistry, ALH84001's documented analytical history.
2. **Credible but incomplete:** specific delivery fluxes to early Earth, how representative Bennu/Ryugu are of source populations, some parent-body reaction pathways, and reconstruction of early-Earth inventories.
3. **Disputed / unconfirmed:** biological interpretations of ALH84001, strong panspermia claims, claims of extraterrestrial microorganisms without independently closed contamination and provenance chains.
4. **Speculative synthesis:** pristine sample-return science is creating an epistemic inversion in astrobiology: the more abiotic chemistry can do, the higher the evidentiary bar must rise for life.

# Chronology

- **1969-09:** Murchison meteorite falls in Victoria, Australia.
- **1970s:** Murchison becomes a benchmark for extraterrestrial amino acids and organic chemistry; racemic distributions and unusual compounds strengthen abiotic interpretations.
- **1984:** ALH84001 is recovered in Antarctica.
- **1996-08:** McKay et al. report observations in ALH84001 consistent with possible relic biological activity; intense dispute follows.
- **2014:** Hayabusa2 launches to asteroid Ryugu.
- **2016:** OSIRIS-REx launches to Bennu.
- **2020-12-06:** Hayabusa2 sample capsule returns Ryugu material to Earth.
- **2022:** Early Ryugu analyses establish carbon-rich, hydrated, minimally terrestrially exposed material; ALH84001 organics receive strengthened abiotic water-rock explanation.
- **2023-02:** JAXA reports broad soluble-organic inventory in Ryugu samples.
- **2023-09-24:** OSIRIS-REx sample capsule lands in Utah with Bennu material.
- **2025-01-29:** Major Bennu papers report amino acids, all five canonical nucleobases, ammonia-rich organics and ancient evaporite salts/brines.
- **2025-12-02:** Bennu work reports ribose and glucose and separately complex nitrogen/oxygen-rich polymeric organic material.
- **2026:** Bennu/Ryugu returned-sample chemistry increasingly functions as an abiotic baseline for future astrobiological claims.

## Working rules

- A molecule used by life is not called a biosignature without specifying the abiotic alternatives.
- 'Pristine' means controlled and characterized exposure, not metaphysical purity.
- Delivery of precursors is separated from origin of life and from panspermia.
- ALH84001 testimony/history is separated from present mechanistic evidence.
- Press-release language is not allowed to outrun the linked paper.

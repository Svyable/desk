# The Silent Hardware

**An Archaeology of the Solar System**

Sven Hardy Benson

## Thesis

Radio SETI asks whether somebody elsewhere is transmitting while we are listening. Solar-System artifact searches ask a different question: whether technology can persist after the maker, the mission, and the signal are gone. That change of timescale is scientifically interesting, but it also makes contamination, preservation, false positives, orbital dynamics, and chain of custody the center of the problem rather than footnotes.

This book does not claim extraterrestrial artifacts exist in the Solar System. It asks what evidence would be required to distinguish one from natural objects, human debris, sensor artifacts, or wishful classification.

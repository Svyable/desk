# Back Matter

## Chronology

**1959** — Giuseppe Cocconi and Philip Morrison publish the classic interstellar radio-communication search proposal in *Nature*.

**1960** — Frank Drake conducts Project Ozma.

**1960** — Ronald Bracewell discusses the possibility of messenger probes in *Nature*.

**1957 onward** — Human orbital debris begins accumulating after Sputnik, creating a new contamination regime for near-Earth artifact searches.

**1969–1972** — Apollo missions leave a documented technological archaeological record on the Moon and return curated lunar material.

**2012** — Jacob Haqq-Misra and Ravi Kumar Kopparapu publish an analysis of the likelihood and searchability of non-terrestrial artifacts in the Solar System.

**2017** — 1I/'Oumuamua becomes the first confirmed interstellar object.

**2018** — NASA convenes a technosignatures workshop in Houston; the resulting report explicitly broadens the search beyond radio.

**2019** — James Benford publishes on Earth co-orbitals as SETI observables.

**2019** — 2I/Borisov becomes the second confirmed interstellar object and the first clearly cometary one.

**2021** — Benford publishes an artifact-oriented Drake-equation framework emphasizing potential artifact residence time.

**2024** — The Planetary Society surveys the small but growing scientific literature on Solar-System extraterrestrial-artifact searches.

**2025** — 3I/ATLAS becomes the third confirmed interstellar object; multiwavelength observations show cometary activity.

**2025** — Villarroel and colleagues release a preprint proposing several cost-effective searches for probes, including pre-Sputnik flashes and Earth-shadow filtering.

**September 8, 2026** — SETI Institute publicizes Pinault and colleagues' preprint proposing a quantitative search for microscopic engineered particles in lunar regolith; no detection is claimed.

## Author's evidence rule

A possibility is not promoted because it is interesting. A testimony is not converted into physical proof. A patent is evidence of filing, not performance. A classified claim is not strengthened merely because outsiders cannot inspect it. “Unexplained” is not a synonym for “engineered.”

The point of a Solar-System artifact search is not to make extraordinary claims easier.

It is to make them testable.

# Research Brief

## Thesis under test

*The Oldest Thing in the Room* asks what it means for a physical population to be strongly warranted by theory and indirect evidence, locally present in the standard picture, and still unavailable through direct event-by-event detection.

The hard case is the cosmic neutrino background (CνB): relic neutrinos expected to have decoupled from the primordial plasma when the universe was roughly one second old. Standard cosmology predicts their present distribution; Big Bang nucleosynthesis, the cosmic microwave background, and structure formation constrain the radiation and mass effects associated with them. No individual primordial relic neutrino has been directly detected as of September 2026.

The book's interpretive thesis is narrower than “observers create reality.” It argues that scientific civilizations expand their evidentiary reach by engineering physical couplings and transducers: arrangements that let a phenomenon outside biological sensitivity cause a durable, discriminable record.

## Claims that must remain separated

1. **Established particle physics:** neutrinos exist, oscillate, and therefore at least two mass eigenstates are nonzero in mass.
2. **Standard cosmological prediction:** a thermal relic neutrino population should survive from early-universe decoupling, with a present characteristic temperature near 1.95 K in the standard history.
3. **Indirect cosmological evidence:** radiation density, free-streaming effects, nucleosynthesis, and structure growth are consistent with the expected neutrino sector within current uncertainties and model assumptions.
4. **Laboratory constraint:** KATRIN reported in 2025 a direct kinematic upper bound of 0.45 eV/c² on the effective electron-neutrino mass from 259 days of data.
5. **Experimental frontier:** PTOLEMY is developing a tritium-capture route to direct CνB detection; this is an active program, not a completed detection.
6. **Open particle question:** whether neutrinos are Dirac or Majorana particles remains unresolved.
7. **Interpretation:** “humans as builders of couplings” is the book's synthesis. It is not a result established by neutrino physics.

## Core empirical tests

- Does precision cosmology continue to support the Standard Model prediction for relativistic energy density and free-streaming behavior, usually summarized through N_eff?
- Do laboratory and cosmological neutrino-mass constraints converge as sensitivity improves, or do persistent disagreements require nonstandard particle physics or cosmology?
- Can a tritium target preserve an electron-energy distribution sharp enough for relic-capture events to be separated from the beta-decay endpoint?
- Can a practical experiment combine target mass, filtering, final energy resolution, stability, and background rejection at the required scale?
- If a CνB capture signal is eventually observed, does its rate match the expected local phase-space density after gravitational clustering is included?
- Can independent information about mass and local density make the Dirac/Majorana rate difference scientifically discriminating rather than merely suggestive?

## What would make the central account false or materially weaker?

- A robust direct-detection experiment reaches the sensitivity required for the standard relic background and obtains a null result after target response, efficiency, local clustering, and backgrounds are independently validated.
- Precision cosmology converges on radiation or perturbation behavior incompatible with the standard three-active-neutrino thermal history and the discrepancy survives model and dataset changes.
- A credible nonstandard thermal history explains current cosmological data while predicting a substantially altered or absent standard relic population.
- The target-material physics of every plausible thresholdless capture implementation introduces an irreducible spectral broadening or background above the separation required for direct detection, making the proposed route impractical even though the relic background still exists.
- The book's broader claim would be weakened if new scientific domains could be reliably opened by inference alone without any new causal measurement channel; the manuscript therefore distinguishes reanalysis of existing evidence from genuinely new measurement access.

### Neutrino decoupling and N_eff

- K. Akita, M. Yamaguchi, “A precision calculation of relic neutrino decoupling,” JCAP 08 (2020) 012, arXiv:2005.07047: https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.07047
- J. Froustey, C. Pitrou, M. C. Volpe, “Neutrino decoupling including flavour oscillations and primordial nucleosynthesis,” JCAP 12 (2020) 015, arXiv:2008.01074: https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.01074

These precision calculations are the basis for using a Standard Model N_eff value around 3.044 rather than exactly three. The manuscript should not present the decimal as a direct count of neutrino species.

### Neutrino mass

- KATRIN Collaboration, official result and experiment pages: https://www.katrin.kit.edu/

Use 0.45 eV/c² as the 2025 KATRIN upper limit only with the stated confidence convention and dataset context. Do not convert it into a measured nonzero mass.

### Local relic-neutrino clustering

### Oscillations, solar neutrinos, and flavor change

- Super-Kamiokande Collaboration, “Evidence for oscillation of atmospheric neutrinos,” *Physical Review Letters* 81 (1998) 1562: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.81.1562

These support the claim that oscillation implies nonzero mass splittings. They do not determine the absolute mass scale.

### Background cosmology

## Source discipline

- Prefer collaboration papers, official experiment pages, primary calculations, and standard reviews.
- Never turn a projected sensitivity into an achieved result.
- Never turn a cosmology-dependent upper bound into a model-independent laboratory measurement.
- Never call N_eff a literal measured number of neutrino flavors without qualification.
- Never describe relic neutrinos as individually detected until a peer-reviewed direct detection survives independent scrutiny.
- Treat local clustering factors as predictions conditional on mass and gravitational modeling.
- Separate Dirac/Majorana capture-rate arguments from a claim that rate alone settles particle nature.
- Do not use neutrino strangeness to lend authority to unrelated metaphysical claims.
- For every future-facing experimental statement, keep the date of the source visible in working notes so the manuscript can be revised when the field changes.

# Back Matter

The central empirical claim in this book is narrow enough to be tested: standard hot-Big-Bang cosmology predicts a cosmic neutrino background produced when weak interactions ceased to keep neutrinos in thermal equilibrium at an age of roughly one second. Its present-day spectrum is expected to be extraordinarily cold. Cosmological observations support the expected relativistic energy density and the later effects of massive neutrinos, but individual primordial relic neutrinos have not been directly detected as of September 2026.

Everything else should be read with that status in view.

The direct-detection discussion centers on neutrino capture on tritium because that reaction is thresholdless for an incoming relic neutrino and yields an electron above the ordinary beta-decay endpoint. PTOLEMY is the principal contemporary experimental program pursuing this approach. The project is still an experimental-development effort, not a completed cosmic-neutrino-background observatory with a discovery result.

The book's broader claim—that intelligent organisms can expand what becomes evidence by engineering new physical couplings—is an interpretation, not a theorem of physics. It does not imply that observers are cosmically privileged, that consciousness is required for physical reality, or that every physically existing thing can in practice be measured. Some limits may be technological, some economic, some imposed by irreducible backgrounds, horizons, finite information, or physical law.

The useful distinction is smaller: existence, interaction, record, and warranted interpretation are not the same stage.

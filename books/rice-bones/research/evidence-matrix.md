# Evidence Matrix

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- Itai-itai disease occurred in the Jinzū River basin in Toyama Prefecture.
- Japan’s Ministry of Health and Welfare in May 1968 attributed the disease to chronic cadmium poisoning producing renal injury and osteomalacia, with modifying factors, and identified Kamioka Mining wastewater as the known cadmium source.
- Cadmium contaminated irrigation water, paddy soil, and rice in the affected basin.
- The overwhelming majority of formally recognized patients were women.
- Plaintiffs sued Mitsui Mining and Smelting in 1968 and won in Toyama District Court in 1971 and on appeal in 1972.
- Toyama Prefecture’s museum records 506 plaintiffs across the litigation.
- Post-judgment agreements addressed compensation, polluted soil, and pollution prevention.
- A pollution-control agreement supported recurring source-facility inspections involving residents and designated experts from 1972 onward.
- Major contaminated-paddy restoration work began in 1979 and reached a completion milestone in 2012 after a 33-year program; reported public-project cost was 40.7 billion yen.
- Toyama had certified 201 patients by the end of 2023.
- By March 2025 there were no living certified patients or living designated observers; surveillance and review activity continued afterward.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Detailed individual exposure histories reconstructed from family memory where direct dietary/dose records do not survive.
- Claims about the relative contribution of drinking/cooking water versus rice for specific households.
- Specific inspection interventions credited with particular year-to-year discharge reductions until verified against annual technical reports.
- Some retrospective accounts of early local disease recognition before formal medical reporting.

## Class 3 — disputed / not established

- Treating every renal abnormality in the basin as cadmium-caused without adequate exposure and differential diagnosis.
- Treating the 201 certified patients as the complete count of everyone biologically affected by cadmium.
- Treating nutritional status, pregnancy, lactation, aging, or menopause as alternative causes that erase the industrial cadmium pathway.
- Claiming the inspection agreement alone caused all later water-quality improvement without quantitative source-control attribution.
- Claiming environmental closure merely because no certified patients remain alive.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- The post-1972 access regime can usefully be understood as **counter-monitoring**: a community-controlled verification layer added to state and corporate expertise.
- Rice functioned historically as a distributed exposure integrator—an agricultural analogue of a dosimeter—though individual human dose still requires consumption and physiological assumptions.
- The deepest institutional innovation of the case was conversion of tort victory into durable information rights at the source.
- Kamioka’s dual memory as pollution source and neutrino-science landscape is a useful case for preserving contradictory technological histories rather than allowing later achievement to overwrite earlier externality.

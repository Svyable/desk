### Source discipline

Japanese official pages establish what the state recognizes and how it describes the case; they are not automatically neutral histories of every disputed episode. Victim and activist histories are essential for inspection practice and testimony but should not be used alone for contested quantitative claims. Later news reports establish that practices persisted; they do not substitute for the annual technical inspection reports.

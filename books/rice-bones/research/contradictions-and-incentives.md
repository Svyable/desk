# Contradictions and Incentives Audit

## Government

**Incentive:** recognize enough harm to administer relief while preserving consistent criteria and avoiding unlimited causal attribution.

**Watch for:** using certified-patient counts as shorthand for total biological burden; retrospective smoothing of delays or uncertainty.

## Mitsui / Kamioka operators

**Incentive:** historically limit liability and protect operational discretion; later demonstrate successful source control and cooperative environmental performance.

**Watch for:** current compliance being projected backward; engineering improvements presented without independent year-by-year discharge data.

## Victims’ organizations

**Incentive:** preserve recognition of harm, compensation, source access, restoration, and historical memory.

**Watch for:** movement narratives privileging emblematic severe cases or attributing every regional health outcome to cadmium.

## Researchers

**Incentive:** demonstrate causal mechanism, publish novel findings, defend prior interpretations.

**Watch for:** disciplinary overreach; kidney, bone, exposure, and epidemiology papers answer different questions.

## Museum / memory institutions

**Incentive:** teach a coherent civic lesson.

**Watch for:** converting a contested and technically complex history into a clean morality sequence.

## This manuscript

**Incentive:** elevate the underwritten post-trial inspection regime into a larger governance thesis.

**Primary self-check:** do not infer effectiveness merely from longevity. Publication-ready revision must link inspection findings to specific engineering changes and environmental measurements.

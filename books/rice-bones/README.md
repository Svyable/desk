# Rice Bones

**Cadmium, Women, and the River Japan Put on Trial**

By Sven Hardy Benson

## Thesis

Itai-itai disease became legible only when investigators followed cadmium across systems that institutions preferred to keep separate: a mine, a river, irrigation canals, paddy soil, rice, kidneys, bones, households, courtrooms, and finally the factory gate. The deeper legacy of Toyama is not only that pollution victims won one of Japan’s landmark environmental cases. It is that they converted legal victory into an unusual, durable inspection regime in which residents, lawyers, and independent scientists could repeatedly enter the source facility, demand information, take samples, and force pollution control to remain observable.

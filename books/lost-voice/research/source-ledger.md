# Source Ledger

This ledger records the core evidence used to draft *Lost Voice*. It is not a substitute for source-specific legal citation in a later scholarly edition. Dates below are publication or decision dates where known. Political claims are attributed to the actor making them.

## Primary law, executive action, courts, and Congress

### 1. 47 U.S.C. § 396 — Corporation for Public Broadcasting
- URL: https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/396
- Type: Statute / codified law
- Use: Chapters 2, 3, 7, 17, 18
- Supports: CPB corporate form, statutory purposes, board structure, nonpolitical requirements, federal-control boundaries.
- Caution: The codified statute should be read alongside amendments and historical legislative materials when making claims about original congressional purpose.

### 2. Richard Nixon, “Veto of Public Broadcasting Bill,” June 30, 1972
- URL: https://www.presidency.ucsb.edu/documents/veto-public-broadcasting-bill

- Use: Chapters 2, 4

## Falsification note

The ledger deliberately contains sources favorable to the administration’s policy case, sources favorable to public media, and adjudicative/official records. The book should be revised rather than defended if later evidence undermines the bounded thesis. The objective is not to prove that federal funding is inherently good or inherently illegitimate. It is to explain what institutional mechanism changed, who had authority at each step, and what happened afterward.

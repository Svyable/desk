# The Bomb Became a Clock

## Nuclear Fallout, Human Cell Birthdays, and the Accidental Timestamp Written Into the Living World

**Sven Hardy Benson**

### Thesis

Atmospheric nuclear testing did something stranger than contaminate the planet: it stamped a sharply time-varying carbon-14 signal into air, food, oceans, teeth, proteins, DNA, and other living matter. After the 1963 peak, that signal became an accidental global pulse-chase experiment. Scientists can now use it to ask when a tooth formed, whether a protein ever turns over, when a human cell was born, and whether adult tissues renew themselves. The weapon became a clock. The thesis is not that fallout was beneficial, nor that every tissue can be dated cleanly. It is that an uncontrolled geopolitical event created a tracer experiment no ethics board could have authorized at planetary scale, and that modern biology and forensics learned to read its residue with extraordinary precision.

### Evidence classes

1. **Verified / documented:** atmospheric weapons testing sharply increased atmospheric carbon-14; the post-test bomb curve is measured; bomb-pulse carbon entered the biosphere; accelerator mass spectrometry can measure it; tooth enamel, eye-lens proteins and selected tissue DNA have been dated against the curve; forensic birth-year estimation has been demonstrated.
2. **Credible but incomplete:** tissue-specific turnover estimates that depend on dietary lag, atmospheric zone, carbon-pool modeling, cell sorting, purity and assumptions about DNA synthesis; forensic geographic inference from combined isotopes; extension of the method to rare cell populations.
3. **Disputed / unresolved:** strong claims about adult neurogenesis or turnover in particular human brain regions where laboratories, markers, sampling and models disagree; simple one-number turnover estimates for heterogeneous tissues; casework claims without transparent chain of custody and calibration.
4. **Speculative synthesis:** the bomb pulse as an accidental planetary timestamp; nuclear testing as an involuntary pulse-chase experiment whose scientific afterlife now outlasts the strategic logic that produced it; the possibility that declining atmospheric contrast will make this peculiar measurement window historically finite.

### Defeat condition

The central synthesis weakens if the major biological and forensic results cannot survive reanalysis with tissue-specific carbon lag, atmospheric-zone, dietary, contamination, cell-purity and curve-ambiguity corrections, or if independent methods systematically show that bomb-pulse-derived birth/turnover estimates are biased beyond their claimed utility.

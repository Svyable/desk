## Contradiction and incentive audit

- **Precision theater:** AMS isotope ratios can be extremely precise while calendar inference remains model-sensitive.
- **Rising/falling ambiguity:** the same 14C value may correspond to two dates.
- **Dietary lag/reservoir effects:** marine carbon and mixed diets can bias simple atmospheric matching.
- **Cell purity:** sorted populations can be contaminated by neighboring cell types; rare-cell conclusions are especially vulnerable.
- **DNA interpretation:** DNA carbon is a proxy for synthesis associated with cell birth, not an oracle for lineage identity.
- **Neurogenesis controversy:** radiocarbon evidence must be placed alongside histology, marker preservation, sampling and null studies rather than used as a trump card.
- **Moral inversion:** scientific usefulness does not retroactively justify atmospheric testing.
- **Weapons-history simplification:** build actual test chronology and 14C production rather than saying vaguely that “the Cold War doubled radiocarbon.”
- **Modern curve change:** fossil-fuel dilution and local nuclear sources complicate contemporary calibration.

## Chronology

- 1940s: accelerator-era nuclear weapons begin changing the anthropogenic radionuclide environment.
- 1954 onward: atmospheric 14C rises measurably with weapons testing.
- 1955–1963: principal bomb-pulse interval used in modern forensic literature.
- 1961: Wilson publishes use of bomb 14C as a short-term dating system for heartwood.
- 1963: Partial Test Ban Treaty constrains atmospheric testing by major signatories; atmospheric bomb 14C approaches its peak in the following period.
- 1971: Nydal, Lövseth and Syrstad report bomb 14C in human populations.
- 1977: Stenhouse and Baxter explicitly review bomb 14C as a biological tracer.
- 1990: human tooth collagen used to characterize atmospheric test history.
- 2005: Spalding et al. demonstrate high-precision forensic birth dating from tooth enamel.
- 2008: eye-lens crystallin bomb-pulse work supports exceptional protein longevity.
- 2010s: bomb-pulse cell-birth dating expands across human tissues and forensic casework.
- 2024: forensic modeling work emphasizes age-dependent turnover and reservoir corrections rather than simple lag adjustments.

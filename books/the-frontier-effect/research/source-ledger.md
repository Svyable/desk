# Source ledger — The Frontier Effect

This ledger records the source posture behind the manuscript. Links are included for verification and later citation work. A source appearing here does not imply that every interpretation built from it is established fact.

## Evidence classes

1. **Documented / primary / established:** original papers, official institutional records, established historical accounts, published peer-reviewed results.
2. **Credible but incomplete / provisional:** serious preprints, participant retrospectives, institutional summaries, claims still undergoing mathematical scrutiny.
3. **Disputed or unconfirmed:** alleged solutions or capability claims that have not achieved independent mathematical acceptance.
4. **Speculative synthesis:** the book's own concepts—frontier velocity, attention release, frontier whiplash, assimilation time—until empirical work establishes whether they are useful measures.

# Source Log — The Base That Melted

Checked 2026-09-13. Public/lawfully available sources only.

## Primary / authoritative / near-primary

1. U.S. Army Corps of Engineers, **Construction of the Army Nuclear Power Plant PM-2A at Camp Century, Greenland: Final Report** (1961). NASA Earth Observatory links the USACE digital-library copy from its Camp Century article: https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/new-view-of-the-city-under-the-ice-153616/
   - Use: PM-2A construction and installation history.
   - Boundary: evidence of reactor construction/operation, not evidence of deployed Iceworm missiles.

2. B. Lyle Hansen & Chester C. Langway, **Deep core drilling in ice and core analysis at Camp Century, Greenland, 1961–66** (1966), NSF Ice Drilling Program library: https://icedrill.org/library/deep-core-drilling-ice-and-core-analysis-camp-century-greenland-1961-66
   - Use: drilling program, bed-reaching core, methods history.

3. W. Dansgaard, S. J. Johnsen, J. Møller & C. C. Langway Jr., **One Thousand Centuries of Climatic Record from Camp Century on the Greenland Ice Sheet**, *Science* 166 (1969), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.166.3903.377
   - Use: 1,390 m core; oxygen-isotope record; early paleoclimate interpretation.

4. Nikolaj Petersen, **The Iceman That Never Came: ‘Project Iceworm’, the search for a NATO deterrent, and Denmark, 1960–1962**, *Scandinavian Journal of History* 33 (2008), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/03468750701449554
   - Use: Iceworm strategic proposal; Danish politics; documentary provenance in declassified U.S. records.
   - Boundary: historical reconstruction, not proof of deployment.

5. William Colgan et al., **The abandoned ice sheet base at Camp Century, Greenland, in a warming climate**, *Geophysical Research Letters* 43 (2016), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1002/2016GL069688
   - Use: 2016 waste inventory framing; RCP8.5 surface-mass-balance transition scenario; political legacy.
   - Boundary: scenario analysis, not observation of exposed waste.

6. Camp Century Climate Monitoring Programme (Government of Denmark / Government of Greenland; GEUS): https://www.campcenturyclimate.dk/
   - Use: monitoring-program establishment, field measurements, public data, reports.

7. Camp Century Climate Monitoring Programme, **Camp Century is staying under the ice** (23 Mar. 2021): https://campcenturyclimate.dk/news/2021_staysburied.html
   - Use: updated site-specific conclusion that debris is unlikely to emerge or be reached by surface meltwater before 2100; stated depths and modeled infiltration.
   - Boundary: not a claim of safety beyond modeled horizon.

8. Camp Century Climate Monitoring Programme, reports index: https://www.campcenturyclimate.dk/reports.html
   - Includes Vandecrux et al., **Firn evolution at Camp Century, Greenland: 1966–2100**; radar surveys; field reports.

9. Andrew J. Christ et al., **A multimillion-year-old record of Greenland vegetation and glacial history preserved in sediment beneath 1.4 km of ice at Camp Century**, *PNAS* 118 (2021), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2021442118
   - NASA GISS abstract: https://www.giss.nasa.gov/pubs/abs/ch04310e.html
   - Use: fossil plants/biomolecules; evidence of past ice-free conditions at Camp Century.
   - Boundary: constrains past ice-sheet states; does not itself determine 21st-century timing.

10. Paul R. Bierman et al., **Scientific history, sampling approach, and physical characterization of the Camp Century subglacial material, a rare archive from beneath the Greenland Ice Sheet**, *The Cryosphere* 18 (2024), DOI: https://doi.org/10.5194/tc-18-4029-2024
    - Use: 3.44 m basal material history, stratigraphic units, plant macrofossils, insect remains, curation history.

11. NASA Earth Observatory, **New View of the ‘City Under the Ice’** (25 Nov. 2024): https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/new-view-of-the-city-under-the-ice-153616/
    - Use: April 2024 UAVSAR detection; relationship between radar structures and historical camp layout.
    - Boundary: radar reveals structural contrasts, not intact rooms or undocumented payloads.

## Secondary context / historiography

12. Achermann, **Vertical glaciology: The second discovery of the third dimension in climate research** (2020), DOI: https://doi.org/10.1111/1600-0498.12294
    - Use: deep-ice-core scientific history and Camp Century's role.

13. University of Vermont, **Secrets Under the Ice** (31 Oct. 2019): https://www.uvm.edu/uvmnews/news/secrets-under-ice
    - Use: curation / rediscovery narrative, quoted recollections from archive custodians.
    - Boundary: institutional reporting; primary scientific claims must trace to peer-reviewed work.

## Claims explicitly excluded pending stronger primary evidence

- Operational deployment of hundreds of Iceworm missiles.
- Nuclear warheads abandoned beneath Camp Century.
- A complete, covert post-rejection Iceworm system.
- Formal Danish approval of an operational Iceworm arsenal.
- Imminent Camp Century waste exposure before 2100.
- Claim that all Camp Century scientific activity existed only as cover.

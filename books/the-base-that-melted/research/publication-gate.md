## Frozen expansion work

Before release-candidate status:

1. Recover and page-index the originating Army/JCS Iceworm planning documents used by Petersen and other historians; build a document-level proposal → review → rejection chain.
2. Recover original Camp Century engineering/deformation reports and quantify measured closure rates by trench/structure, then compare them with Iceworm rail/launch tolerances.
3. Build a PM-2A operational ledger from the USACE final report and Army Nuclear Power Program records: installation, output, incidents, shutdown, disassembly, removed components and residual waste pathways.
4. Reconstruct the 1951 U.S.-Danish defense framework and Danish nuclear-policy record from primary diplomatic documents; explicitly separate known, tacit, proposed and formally approved activities.
5. Build a 1966 deep-core operations section with drilling rates, equipment, failures and core handling from original CRREL/Army records.
6. Reproduce the key Camp Century isotope results from Dansgaard et al. 1969 and trace what later Greenland cores revised.
7. Reconstruct the basal-sediment custody chain from 1966 through U.S. storage, Buffalo, Copenhagen and rediscovery using Bierman et al. 2024 and archive records.
8. Add a methods chapter-level insert explaining cosmogenic nuclide, luminescence, isotope and macrofossil evidence without overstating dating precision.
9. Reproduce the 2016 Camp Century surface-mass-balance scenario and the 2021 site-specific firn model in comparable terms: forcing, local weather inputs, debris depth, infiltration depth and uncertainty.
10. Build a contaminant ledger from historical inventories rather than category labels: hydrocarbons, PCBs, wastewater, radiological residues, quantities and confidence levels.
11. Expand Greenlandic political history and local perspectives using authoritative Greenlandic/Danish sources rather than writing Greenland only as terrain.
12. Add modern radar mechanics sufficient to explain conventional nadir ice-penetrating radar versus UAVSAR geometry and what can/cannot be inferred from the 2024 image.
13. Run a whole-book anti-slop pass: remove repeated thesis sentences, generic transitions, explanatory throat-clearing and any paragraphs that exist only to make a chapter feel complete.
14. Run the objective Desk length checker and expand only where primary evidence earns the space.

## Release blockers

- Any claim that Iceworm missiles were operationally deployed without new primary evidence.
- Any claim that an intact nuclear reactor remains buried at Camp Century.
- Any “melt-out by 2100” statement that ignores the 2021 site-specific modeling.
- Any use of ancient ice-free conditions as direct proof of a particular 21st-century timeline.
- Any catalog/Reader/publication-state change before the objective length and editorial gates pass.

# Back Matter

## A note on sources

*After Pretraining* follows a fast-moving technical field. The manuscript distinguishes peer-reviewed results, laboratory or company claims, open technical reports, implementation evidence, and forward-looking interpretation. The live source map is maintained in [research/README.md](../research/README.md).

Technical methods described in the book should be read with the assumptions and validation limits stated by their authors. A result demonstrated on mathematics, coding, games, or simulated environments should not be generalized to open-ended real-world judgment without additional evidence.

## Author

Sven Hardy Benson writes investigative nonfiction about technology, power, economics, institutions, and the systems people build before they fully understand what those systems will become.

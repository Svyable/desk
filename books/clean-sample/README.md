## Thesis

Lake Vostok turns contamination from laboratory housekeeping into the central epistemic problem of life detection: when expected biomass is extremely low, the machinery required to reach an isolated environment can carry a biological signal comparable to or larger than the signal being sought. The lesson reaches directly to Europa, Enceladus, Mars and sample return: **access and evidence are coupled systems**.

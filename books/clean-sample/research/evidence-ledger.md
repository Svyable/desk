# Evidence ledger — CLEAN SAMPLE

Working rule: detection is not provenance. Every biological claim needs a route-of-arrival model.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

### Lake geometry and accretion ice

Lake Vostok is a large Antarctic subglacial lake beneath nearly 4 km of ice. Accretion ice formed from lake-associated water has been used as an indirect sample of the lake environment.

- Siegert et al., *Nature* (2001), “Physical, chemical and biological processes in Lake Vostok and other Antarctic subglacial lakes”: https://doi.org/10.1038/414603a
- Priscu et al., *Science* (1999), “Geomicrobiology of subglacial ice above Lake Vostok, Antarctica”: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.286.5447.2141
- USGS publication record: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/geomicrobiology-subglacial-ice-above-lake-vostok-antarctica

Priscu et al. reported microbial cells in accretion ice at approximately 2.8 × 10^3 to 3.6 × 10^4 cells/ml, low gene diversity, and no detected incorporation of selected organic substrates or bicarbonate. Their extrapolation was explicitly conditional: the lake *may* support a microbial population.

### Cultured organisms from accretion ice

D'Elia, Veerapaneni & Rogers (2008) cultured organisms from Vostok accretion ice and reported 18 unique bacterial rRNA phylotypes, concluding that the lake may contain a complex microbial ecosystem.

- *Applied and Environmental Microbiology*: https://doi.org/10.1128/AEM.02501-07

Publication hold: culture from accretion ice is not direct culture from freely sampled lake water. Preserve that distinction every time.

### Drilling-fluid microbiology

Alekhina et al. characterized microbial diversity in kerosene-based drilling fluid from the Vostok borehole and identified organisms including Sphingomonas phylotypes. The paper explicitly frames drill fluid as a contamination problem for biological sampling.

- *FEMS Microbiology Ecology*: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1574-6941.2006.00234.x
- Publisher page: https://academic.oup.com/femsec/article/59/2/289/550114

This is central evidence. The contaminant is not hypothetical; the access system had a measurable microbiome.

### Direct-access history

The Russian drilling program breached Lake Vostok in February 2012. Early material associated with the breach was difficult to interpret biologically because of contamination from drilling fluid and extremely low cell counts.

Use Nature reporting as chronology/context, not as the sole scientific source:
- https://doi.org/10.1038/nature.2013.12578

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

### Sustained microbial ecosystem inferred from accretion ice

Christner et al. (2006) found bacterial density higher in accretion ice than overlying glacial ice and interpreted the distribution as supporting a sustained microbial ecosystem, while explicitly noting that the exact biology awaited direct lake-water sampling.

- *Limnology and Oceanography*: https://doi.org/10.4319/lo.2006.51.6.2485

### Brines / hydrothermal interpretation

de Angelis et al. (2005) reported brine microdroplets and sulfur-rich inclusions in accreted ice and interpreted their composition as supporting hydrothermal activity and saline pulses. This is geochemical inference, not observation of a vent community.

- *Geophysical Research Letters*: https://doi.org/10.1029/2005GL022460

### Later meta-omics

Gura & Rogers (2020) reported metagenomic/metatranscriptomic evidence from Vostok ice-core sections consistent with biological diversity and ecological zoning. The material remains accretion/basal/glacial ice rather than a pristine in-situ lake-water transect.

- *Biology*: https://doi.org/10.3390/biology9030055

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

### Thermophile signal and contamination dispute

Bulat et al. (2004) argued that most recovered phylotypes were contaminants and highlighted a thermophilic bacterial signature as potentially meaningful, while concluding that the accretion ice could be effectively microbe-poor. This sharply conflicts with broader ecosystem interpretations.

- *International Journal of Astrobiology*: https://doi.org/10.1017/S1473550404001879

Do not resolve this dispute by vote-counting. Reconstruct sample depth, controls, blank taxa, extraction methods and the physical route by which a thermophile could enter accretion ice.

### “New species” reporting after breach

Treat claims that post-breach sequences represented an indigenous/new organism as disputed unless the primary methods establish separation from drilling-fluid, reagent and laboratory backgrounds. Nature's 2013 report documents the dispute, not the organism's provenance.

- https://doi.org/10.1038/nature.2013.12578

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

### Provenance floor

Proposed concept: when target biomass falls to the same order as biological material introduced by sampling, extraction and amplification, the experiment crosses a **provenance floor**. Below it, greater assay sensitivity can increase detections faster than it increases knowledge unless contamination controls improve simultaneously.

Potential test: build a quantitative contamination budget across drill fluid, borehole, collection vessel, transport, clean room, reagents, extraction kit and sequencing run. Estimate the probability that each detected lineage could have entered through each route. The concept loses value if such budgets do not improve source classification over ordinary negative controls.

### Access shadow

Every access technology casts an **access shadow**: the set of physical, chemical and biological changes produced by reaching the target. The shadow can include introduced cells/DNA, heat, pressure changes, drilling fluid, redox alteration and mixing.

This is especially relevant to ocean-world missions. It is not evidence that Europa or Enceladus contains life.

### The blank-first principle

For ultra-low-biomass life detection, design the contamination model before designing the positive assay. A “blank” is not an empty tube added at the end. It is a parallel history of everything the sample experienced except the target environment.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. Published Vostok studies range from diverse viable microbial assemblages to extremely sparse/contamination-dominated interpretations.
2. Accretion ice is simultaneously valuable because it can sample lake-associated water without direct penetration and limited because freezing can fractionate, exclude, concentrate or relocate material.
3. More sensitive molecular methods can reveal more diversity while also revealing more reagent/laboratory contamination.
4. Direct access sounds epistemically superior but can increase the access shadow.

## Institutional / incentive audit

- Astrobiology rewards extreme-life discoveries and analogue relevance.
- Polar programs have national prestige and logistical sunk costs.
- Planetary-protection concerns can reward conservative contamination interpretations.
- None of these incentives establishes that any particular positive or negative result is wrong.
- Do not convert scientific disagreement into allegations of suppression without documentary evidence.

## Defeat conditions

The book's central synthesis weakens if future pristine in-situ Lake Vostok samples yield abundant, spatially structured, reproducible communities whose taxa and molecular/isotopic signatures are cleanly separated from all access-system and laboratory backgrounds. That would not make contamination irrelevant, but it would move the lake far above the provenance floor.

Conversely, if rigorous direct samples repeatedly collapse into the measured contaminant background, claims of a rich Vostok ecosystem should contract accordingly.

# Evidence ledger — Light Without Object

## Class 1 — verified / documented

### Instrumented Hessdalen campaign
Project Hessdalen reports that its 1984 field campaign recorded 53 light observations and used cameras, radar, magnetometers and other instruments. Treat the project archive as primary campaign documentation, but reconstruct the original report rather than relying on current summary language.

Source: https://www.hessdalen.org/
Archive/about: https://www.hessdalen.org/about

### Spectroscopic ball-lightning observation
Cen, Yuan & Xue, *Physical Review Letters* 112, 035001 (2014). Two slitless spectrographs observed a luminous event after a cloud-to-ground strike from ~0.9 km. Soil-element radiation was present through the luminous lifetime.

Source: https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.112.035001
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.112.035001
PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24484145/

### Laboratory microwave fireballs
Stephan, *Physical Review E* 74, 055401(R) (2006), generated stable buoyant atmospheric-pressure fireballs in a microwave cavity. Analogue evidence only; does not establish natural mechanism.

Source: https://journals.aps.org/pre/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevE.74.055401
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.74.055401

### Laboratory silicon luminous bodies
Porter, Miley, Griffiths & Sánchez, *Physical Review E* 90, 063102 (2014), produced luminous bodies through electrical arcs in lump silicon and measured small net charge.

Repository copy/metadata: https://pdxscholar.library.pdx.edu/phy_fac/215/
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.90.063102

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Reported radar/optical correlations at Hessdalen. Require event-by-event timestamps, instrument configuration, false-alarm characterization and original data before strong claims.
- Optical spectra reported in later Hessdalen work. Require raw spectra/calibration and comparison with conventional sources.
- Geophysical/electrochemical mechanism proposals. Interesting only if energy budget and measured field/current/gas variables can be closed quantitatively.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- “Hessdalen is plasma.” Too broad; no single mechanism demonstrated for the event population.
- “Ball lightning explains UAP.” Category error unless a named event satisfies a specified mechanism's constraints.
- Craft-like descriptions on the current Project Hessdalen site. Witness/project classification, not independent proof of manufactured vehicles.
- Claims of wall/window penetration in historical ball-lightning testimony. Preserve as reports; do not universalize or use as mechanism constraints until provenance is reconstructed.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

The book proposes a missing classification layer: **light without object**. A bounded luminous detection can be physically real while its visible boundary does not correspond to a persistent solid vehicle. This is a taxonomy proposal, not a claim that a specific unresolved UAP is plasma.

## Contradiction ledger

1. Some Hessdalen observations are conventionally identifiable; residual status cannot be transferred from one event to another.
2. Laboratory analogues reproduce properties, not provenance.
3. The Chinese spectroscopic event supports soil-material models for one lightning-associated event; it does not establish universality.
4. Radar return does not automatically mean solid metal, but plasma/aerosol explanations must demonstrate sufficient scattering under the actual radar parameters.
5. Apparent acceleration is not inertial acceleration until material identity of the tracked feature is established.

## Incentive / contamination audit

- UFO framing rewards coherent extraordinary narratives across heterogeneous events.
- Skeptical framing can reward premature conventional closure.
- Long-running citizen-science projects have selection, labeling and equipment-change problems even when conducted in good faith.
- Laboratory researchers have incentives to describe analogues as ball-lightning-like; resemblance is not identity.
- No evidence currently justifies alleging organized disinformation around Hessdalen.

## Defeat conditions

The book's synthesis weakens substantially if: (a) well-instrumented residual Hessdalen events require a persistent solid body under calibrated multi-sensor geometry; (b) candidate plasma/aerosol mechanisms fail energy, duration, radar or spectral constraints by orders of magnitude; or (c) the apparently anomalous population collapses under rigorous conventional identification.

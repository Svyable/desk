# Source notes

## Primary spine

**Project Hessdalen archive** — campaign history, current archive, instrumentation descriptions. Use original 1984 report for exact historical claims; current site is useful for provenance and continuing observation infrastructure.
https://www.hessdalen.org/

**Cen, Jianyong; Yuan, Ping; Xue, Simin (2014), “Observation of the Optical and Spectral Characteristics of Ball Lightning,” Physical Review Letters 112, 035001.** Rare instrumented natural event. Strong evidence that at least one ball-lightning-like luminous body followed a ground strike and contained persistent spectral signatures of soil elements. Does not prove a universal ball-lightning mechanism.
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.112.035001

**Stephan, Karl D. (2006), “Microwave generation of stable atmospheric-pressure fireballs in air,” Physical Review E 74, 055401(R).** Laboratory demonstration that atmospheric-pressure luminous fireballs with selected ball-lightning-like characteristics can be generated in a microwave cavity without vaporized solids. Mechanistic analogue, not natural identification.
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.74.055401

**Porter et al. (2014), “Charge on luminous bodies resembling natural ball lightning produced via electrical arcs through lump silicon,” Physical Review E 90, 063102.** Laboratory silicon system; useful for physical boundedness, persistence, charge and the difference between visible body and engineered vehicle.
https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.90.063102

## Theory requiring adversarial treatment

**Wu, H.-C. (2016), “Relativistic-microwave theory of ball lightning,” Scientific Reports 6, 28263.** Particle-simulation-supported microwave/plasma model. Treat as proposed theory, not established natural mechanism. Test predicted properties against field spectra and energy constraints.
https://www.nature.com/articles/srep28263

**Handel (1994), maser-caviton theory.** Historically useful example of a mechanism that attempts to explain difficult reported properties through trapped electromagnetic energy. Do not inherit its broad explanatory claims without empirical validation.
https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/93JD01021

## Claim hygiene

- “Measured” must name the instrument and measured quantity.
- “Object” reserved for cases where persistent material identity is established; otherwise use luminous region/event/detection.
- Never infer craft from radar+optical coincidence alone.
- Never infer plasma from luminosity alone.
- Never use a laboratory analogue as proof of a field mechanism.
- Preserve unexplained as a legitimate terminal state.

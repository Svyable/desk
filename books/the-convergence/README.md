Energy and the Advance of the Human Race.

The working thesis is that the advance of human civilization can be read through our growing ability to discover, convert, distribute, and apply energy. The claim is not that raw consumption is synonymous with progress: energy quality, useful work, efficiency, control, access, and external costs all matter. The book will test whether energy is nevertheless the most physically fundamental expression of what civilization can do.

## Definition of cooked

A chapter is cooked enough for a first manuscript pass when its central case is reconstructable, quantitative claims have checkable sources, the strongest counterclaim appears in the evidence packet, uncertain or prospective claims are labeled, and the ending creates pressure for the next chapter without pretending the case proved more than it did.

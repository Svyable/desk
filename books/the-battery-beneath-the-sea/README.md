# The Battery Beneath the Sea

## Dark Oxygen, Polymetallic Nodules, and the Experiment We May Mine Before We Understand

**Sven Hardy Benson**

### Thesis

A 2024 Nature Geoscience paper reported oxygen increasing inside benthic chambers placed on polymetallic-nodule seafloor in the Clarion-Clipperton Zone and proposed, cautiously, that electrochemical processes involving nodules might contribute. The claim is now under formal editorial concern and has drawn a technically serious 2025 critique arguing that chamber artefacts, absent negative controls, missing hydrogen, prior null experiments, and thermodynamics undermine the interpretation. Meanwhile the mineral resource itself is moving toward possible industrial recovery under active U.S. permitting and unresolved international governance.

This book is about what science owes the world when an extraordinary geochemical claim appears immediately beside an irreversible engineering decision.

### Manuscript

18 numbered chapters plus front/back matter and research apparatus. This is a substantive first pass, not yet the requested 54,000+ word publication manuscript.

### Evidence posture

Peer-reviewed-primary-heavy / methods-and-controls-heavy / dark-oxygen observation contested / electrolysis mechanism unestablished / mining impacts documented over decades / policy urgency real but not evidence for the phenomenon.

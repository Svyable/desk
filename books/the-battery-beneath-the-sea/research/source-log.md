## Source discipline

The 2024 paper is evidence that the authors observed and reported the stated chamber and laboratory results, not proof that nodules produce oxygen naturally. The 2025 critique is evidence of specific methodological and thermodynamic objections, not proof that every oxygen increase was artefactual. The 2024 Tengberg et al. rebuttal remains a preprint and is used as a methodological challenge, not as settled adjudication. The critique literature includes authors with direct deep-sea-mining industry affiliations; those conflicts do not invalidate technical objections, but they make independent replication especially valuable. Company statements are used for regulatory or incentive context, never as scientific adjudication.

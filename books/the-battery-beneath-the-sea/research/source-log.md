## Source discipline

The 2024 paper is evidence that the authors observed and reported the stated chamber and laboratory results, not proof that nodules produce oxygen naturally. The 2025 critique is evidence of specific methodological and thermodynamic objections, not proof that every oxygen increase was artefactual. Company statements are used for regulatory or incentive context, never as scientific adjudication.

# 15. A Resource Becomes a Deadline

In 2026 the policy clock accelerated.

NOAA finalized revisions to U.S. deep-seabed mining application rules on January 21. The agency's public page now lists multiple exploration and commercial-recovery applications. The Metals Company USA's consolidated application entered federal review, with environmental-impact work proceeding. Internationally, the International Seabed Authority continues work on exploitation regulations that have been under negotiation for years.

The physics of a nodule does not care about any of these calendars.

This creates a familiar technology-governance trap. Commercial readiness is measured by engineering and finance. Ecological readiness is measured by baseline knowledge. They can mature at different rates.

The right question is not whether science can eliminate all uncertainty before policy acts. It cannot. The question is whether a specific unresolved uncertainty is decision-relevant and whether it can be cheaply reduced before an irreversible intervention.

Dark oxygen qualifies because the replication experiment is conceptually straightforward compared with the scale of the proposed disturbance.

When a control experiment costs millions and an industrial decision concerns tens of thousands of square kilometres, `we do not have time to check` is not a scientific argument.

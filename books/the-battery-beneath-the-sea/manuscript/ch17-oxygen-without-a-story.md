# 17. Oxygen Without a Story

Suppose the oxygen increase survives and electrolysis does not.

That possibility is underappreciated because human minds dislike orphan observations. We would rather have a wrong story than no story.

A replicated oxygen source could turn out to involve peroxide decomposition, radiolytic intermediates, obscure manganese redox cycling, pressure-dependent gas release, microbial metabolism not eliminated by current controls, or a coupling between several weak processes. The mechanism might take years.

Science should be allowed to hold `real effect, unknown cause` as a stable temporary category.

The reverse is equally important. Suppose the geobattery electrochemistry is measurable in the laboratory but the field oxygen signal disappears under pressure-clean controls. Then nodules can be electrochemically interesting without being an abyssal oxygen source.

Mechanism and phenomenon are separate propositions.

The discipline of keeping them separate is the transferable lesson. It applies to anomalous aerospace, medical syndromes, particle anomalies and every other domain where a vivid causal story arrives faster than replication.

# 13. The Editor's Note

On April 8, 2026, Nature Geoscience added an Editor's Note to the 2024 paper alerting readers that aspects of the article were subject to concerns under consideration.

An Editor's Note is not a retraction. It is not exoneration either. It is a state marker in the epistemic machinery of science.

That machinery is slower and more procedural than public argument. Editors request information. Authors respond. Critics submit analyses. Data are examined. Sometimes a correction follows; sometimes an expression of concern deepens; sometimes nothing changes; sometimes a paper is withdrawn.

The important public lesson is that scientific status is not binary. A peer-reviewed paper can be published and contested. It can contain a real observation and a wrong mechanism. A critique can identify fatal weaknesses and still overstate its case.

The book therefore freezes the status accurately: as of September 10, 2026, the dark-oxygen claim is unresolved and under formal editorial concern. Any sentence stronger than that needs new evidence.

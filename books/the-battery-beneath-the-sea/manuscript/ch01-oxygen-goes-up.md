# 1. Oxygen Goes Up

The first fact is smaller than the headline and stranger than the headline.

Inside several benthic chambers on the abyssal Pacific seafloor, dissolved oxygen rose. Sweetman and colleagues reported increases over roughly two days, in some cases reaching more than three times background concentration. Deep-sea ecologists normally deploy such chambers to watch oxygen go the other direction. Sediment and animals respire. Organic matter is oxidized. Oxygen is spent.

The instrument therefore appeared to be watching a debit become a credit.

That inversion is the book's starting point, not the conclusion. A concentration increase inside a chamber establishes neither a source nor a mechanism. Oxygen can be produced, imported, released from trapped gas, liberated chemically, biased upward by a sensor, or generated by some interaction between apparatus and sample. The experimental problem is to make those possibilities expensive one by one.

The 2024 paper tried. It considered diffusion from below, radiolysis, manganese chemistry and biological production. Ex situ incubations were used. Mercury chloride was added to suppress biology. Nodules were tested separately. Surface voltage differences were measured. Those are not the moves of people merely chasing a headline.

But a good-faith chain of exclusions can still contain a weak link. In fact the more extraordinary the inferred source, the more important the apparently boring parts become: chamber venting, optode calibration, initial oxygen, pressure history, headspace, sample handling, blank controls, sensor response under compression and recovery.

The danger is narrative compression. `Oxygen rose` becomes `nodules made oxygen`; then `nodules split seawater`; then `deep-sea mining may destroy a planetary oxygen source`. Each arrow is a separate scientific claim. None is licensed automatically by the previous one.

The reverse compression is just as bad. `The proposed electrolysis mechanism is weak` does not become `the oxygen increase never occurred`. The observed chamber signal and its explanation can fail independently.

This is why the case matters beyond oceanography. Science often breaks not at measurement or theory but at the bridge between them.

# Official Roswell report audit

## Bounded question

What does the current official Air Force record actually establish about the MOGUL explanation, and what should the manuscript refuse to inherit from the word `official`?

## Sources checked

- Air Force Historical Research Agency, *The Roswell Report: Fact versus Fiction in the New Mexico Desert* landing record and preserved report: https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/The-DAF-Legacy-Vault/AFHRA-Studies-and-Interviews/igphoto/2003789023/
- Department of the Air Force preserved PDF: https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/Portals/16/documents/AFD-101201-038.pdf
- Air Force Historical Support Division, `UFO Questions`, identifying the Weaver/McAndrew volume as a Headquarters USAF/GPO publication from 1995: https://www.afhistory.af.mil/FAQs/Fact-Sheets/Article/754884/ufo-questions/
- U.S. Air Force, *The Roswell Report: Case Closed* landing page, for the later body-claim explanation: https://www.af.mil/The-Roswell-Report/

## What the official record can carry

The AFHRA description is unusually useful because it identifies the institutional and documentary status of the 1995 volume. It says Weaver and McAndrew prepared the work in response to Representative Steven Schiff's request; that it duplicated the information supplied to the Secretary of the Air Force and GAO apart from the added photograph section; and that McAndrew's attachment 32 describes Project MOGUL as the classified balloon-borne project providing the report's explanation for Roswell.

That establishes a clean provenance chain for a claim the manuscript currently handles cautiously: **the MOGUL attribution is a documented retrospective Air Force conclusion**. It does not convert the conclusion into a contemporaneous 1947 identification.

The distinction matters because an official retrospective report can contain genuine archival evidence and still depend on reconstruction. The institutional status answers `who concluded this?`; it does not answer every underlying `how certain is this particular flight identification?` question.

## A second distinction the manuscript should preserve

The later *Case Closed* report is not simply more evidence for the 1947 debris attribution. Its advertised task is different: explaining later `alien body` stories. The Air Force says many such accounts can be traced to anthropomorphic test-dummy operations and to later accidents, including a 1956 KC-97 accident and a 1959 manned-balloon mishap. Its own conclusion therefore depends on **temporal compression**: activities occurring across years were later represented as if they occurred during the Roswell episode.

This is potentially valuable to SECRET PURPOSE, but only if used with care. The later report can establish the Air Force's documented explanation and the dates of the proposed source events. It cannot, by itself, prove that every body witness actually conflated those events.

## Strongest counterargument

It would be too easy for the book to turn `retrospective official conclusion` into a rhetorical downgrade. Retrospective reconstruction is not inherently weak. The 1995 report was produced after a directed records search, assembled declassified MOGUL material, and was supplied to GAO. Later access to formerly classified records can make a retrospective explanation **better informed than a public 1947 explanation**.

The fair test is therefore not `contemporaneous good / retrospective bad`. It is source-by-source provenance: which proposition comes from a 1947 record, which from a later declassified program record, which from a participant's memory, and which is the Air Force investigators' inference connecting them.

This counterargument should remain alive in the manuscript. Otherwise `instance identification` becomes a device for keeping the MOGUL explanation permanently at arm's length even when converging archival evidence may justify a high posterior probability.

## Editorial consequence

A later prose pass should make the Air Force report itself part of the story rather than treating `the government later said MOGUL` as one undifferentiated fact. The productive tension is:

- the official provenance of the MOGUL conclusion is now easy to establish;
- its retrospective character is also easy to establish;
- retrospective does not mean fabricated or weak;
- the exact Flight 4 / Foster Ranch chain still has to be earned from the underlying records rather than borrowed from the report's authority.

This supports the chapter's class-versus-instance distinction while preventing it from becoming an asymmetric skepticism machine.

## Publication holds retained

- Do not state that a particular numbered MOGUL flight is proven responsible until the underlying NYU/Crary/flight-path records are reconstructed directly.
- Do not state that later body witnesses *were* remembering test dummies or later accidents merely because *Case Closed* proposes that explanation; distinguish proposed source-event match from demonstrated witness-memory genealogy.
- Do not use `official` as either a credibility trump card or a reason for dismissal.

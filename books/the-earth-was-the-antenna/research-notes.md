Recent work already occupies Orion, Chariot/Plowshare, magnetogenetics, intelligence deception, A119, Podkletnov, STAR GATE, Roswell/MOGUL, Camp Century, SOSUS, foreign-materiel exploitation, earthquake precursors, animal intelligence and multiple UAP sensor frameworks. Repository PR search returned no Sanguine / Seafarer / Navy ELF manuscript.

The governing object is distinct: **the physical infrastructure and coupling budget required to create a strategically useful extremely-low-frequency field through Earth and seawater, and what that positive control does—and does not—license us to infer about biological or anomalous ELF claims.**

## Four evidence classes

### 1 — Verified / documented

- Project Sanguine was a U.S. Navy effort to develop ELF communication with submerged submarines; the very large original architecture was not built in that form.
- The program evolved through Seafarer and smaller ELF architectures.
- A reduced U.S. Navy ELF system using Wisconsin and Michigan transmitting facilities became operational and was later shut down in 2004.
- The system used ground-coupled antennas and extremely low frequencies; geology was part of transmitter performance.
- ELF's very low bandwidth constrained data rate; the operational concept functioned as a short-message / bell-ringer channel rather than a voice link.
- Public concern produced environmental/biological study and litigation.
- The National Research Council's 1997 evaluation covered eleven Navy ecological studies and reported that although some researchers thought some biological changes might have occurred, the results did not indicate significant adverse ecological effects.

### 2 — Credible but incomplete

- Exact Sanguine design evolution: cable mileage, land area, hardening, power, transmitter topology, cost and message requirements changed across proposal generations. Every number must be date/design-tagged before publication.
- The often-repeated `2.6 MW in / ~8 W radiated` operational-system figure is physically plausible and widely reported but is held pending recovery of the primary Navy/IEEE technical derivation.
- Exact penetration depth and world-coverage claims depend on frequency, conductivity, receiver design, noise, integration and operational assumptions and must not be presented as a single universal number.
- Soviet/Russian ZEVS provides an important comparative system, but primary Russian technical documentation is still required.

### 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

Do not promote without independent event-specific evidence:

- Navy ELF caused clinically significant disease in nearby populations.
- ELF was operationally used for mind control or behavioral manipulation.
- Sanguine/Seafarer/ELF had a covert weather-control or earthquake-control mission.
- HAARP is simply a successor to the Navy ELF transmitter. (Different system, frequencies, geometry and mission; any relationship has to be technical rather than rhetorical.)
- Classified specifications imply materially stronger biological or geophysical capabilities than the public system.

### 4 — Speculative synthesis

- **Coupling bill:** any distant-field claim owes a reconstructable source → field → propagation → target → transduction → effect chain.
- **Planetary component:** some technologies do not merely sit on terrain; geology, ocean or atmosphere becomes a functional component of the machine.
- **Scale test:** secrecy can hide purpose and parameters more readily than it can erase every physical dependency of infrastructure-intensive capability.
- **Bandwidth honesty:** extraordinary reach can coexist with severe information poverty; `can reach` must never silently become `can control`.

These are analytical devices, not historical Navy doctrines.

## Contradictions / incentive audit

1. **Navy incentive:** demonstrate strategic necessity, environmental acceptability and cost-effectiveness. This can bias institutional framing toward reassurance.
2. **Opponent incentive:** stop a nuclear-war communications system, sometimes by combining strategic, land-use, ecological and health objections. Opposition does not make biological concerns false, but motive and endpoint must be separated.
3. **Research incentive:** weak or subtle biological effects can reward flexible endpoint selection; null studies can also be underpowered for subtle effects. Study design matters more than verdict labels.
4. **Conspiracy incentive:** a documented secretive nuclear-command system is a high-value anchor for unrelated claims. Historical reality can be used to launder causal claims the archive does not support.
5. **Classification incentive:** real redactions create an honest unknown remainder. They do not specify what is in it.

## Strongest countermodel

The Navy system may be a poor comparator for many biological ELF questions because it optimized long-range communications to a purpose-built receiver under seawater, not local biological transduction. A small field can matter biologically if a system has an appropriate sensor/amplifier, and natural organisms demonstrate exquisite sensitivity in several modalities. Therefore the book must never argue `Navy needed megawatts, so weak-field biology is impossible.` Its narrower burden is quantitative: if a claimed distant source produces a biological effect, measure the field at the target, identify the coupling route, demonstrate the effect prospectively, and show that the proposed information content survives the chain.

## Defeat condition

Pre-register a cross-domain comparison in which claimed low-frequency effects are specified in advance by source waveform, geometry, target field, shielding/control condition, biological or physical endpoint, effect size, and analysis. If robust effects repeatedly survive independent replication while source-to-target measurements demonstrate that the Navy-derived coupling/scale comparison predicts nothing useful, retire the `scale test` rather than broadening it after the fact.

## Quantitative extraction burden

For every transmitter configuration: frequency; current; voltage; input electrical power; antenna length and topology; electrode depth/geometry; local conductivity model; radiated power/effective moment; field strength versus range; propagation loss; receiver noise; integration time; data rate; bit/code error criterion; seawater penetration assumptions; submarine receiver geometry; duty cycle; and uncertainty.

For every biological claim: actual measured electric/magnetic field at organism; frequency spectrum; exposure duration; modulation; sham/blinding; sample size; endpoint count; multiple-comparison correction; replication status; effect magnitude; mechanism claim; and whether the endpoint is biological change, adverse effect, or operationally controllable behavior.

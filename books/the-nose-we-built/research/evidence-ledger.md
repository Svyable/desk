# Evidence ledger — THE NOSE WE BUILT

## Governing rule

Do not let **organism senses X** silently become **system identifies X**. The causal ladder is: environmental variable → receptor exposure → biological discrimination → neural/behavioral response → instrument readout → classifier → operator interpretation → decision. Evidence may stop at any rung.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- CIA publicly documents Vietnam-era seismic intrusion detectors disguised as animal dung, designed to detect movement and transmit coded impulses. Source: https://www.cia.gov/legacy/museum/artifact/seismic-intruder-detection-devices/
- Jiang et al., *Cell* (2024), mapped odor coding in migratory locust antennal lobes. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2024.05.036
- A 2023 *Current Biology* study tested 205 ecologically relevant odorants, deorphanized 48 locust olfactory receptors, found unusually narrow tuning, and reported loss of physiological/behavioral response after knockout of a corresponding receptor. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38070506/
- Ling et al., *eLife* (2025), studied adaptation and concentration discrimination in locust olfaction, useful for bounding the idea of a stable biological detector. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40857082/
- DARPA's RE-NET program is documented neural-interface research, but it concerned reliable extraction of nervous-system signals for prosthetic control; it is evidence that neural readout is an engineering discipline, not evidence of covert animal control. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/re-net-reliable-peripheral-interfaces

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Historical accounts of intelligence experimentation with animal-borne surveillance, including Acoustic Kitty, are useful only where recoverable primary/declassified records establish program facts. Operational outcome, cost, exact surgical configuration and famous failure anecdotes require source-by-source reconstruction before manuscript promotion.
- Biohybrid insect sensing research may demonstrate neural responses to target chemicals under laboratory conditions without establishing field detection, target localization, false-positive rate, persistence or operational utility.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Claims that intelligence agencies achieved reliable, precise remote control of free-ranging animals for covert missions.
- Claims that Acoustic Kitty's popular taxi-death story is established solely because it is frequently repeated.
- Claims that laboratory neural response to an explosive-associated volatile equals a deployable explosive detector.
- Claims of classified operational insect swarms inferred only from public research programs.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- **Translation tax:** the information lost or uncertainty added between biological discrimination and a human decision product.
- **Objective-function mismatch:** the organism's evolved priorities can diverge from the operator's task even when sensing is excellent.
- **Biographical calibration:** unlike factory sensors, living detectors vary with age, state, learning, stress, adaptation, environment and history.
- **Borrowed world:** defense value may arise not from exotic senses but from ordinary sensory channels humans lack or underuse.

## Contradictions and incentives

- Program existence creates incentives for advocates to advertise promise while secrecy prevents outsiders from seeing denominators and failures.
- Popular retellings reward bizarre anecdotes; a cat, moth or locust is more memorable than calibration curves.
- Researchers can validly demonstrate a receptor or neural response while journalists and downstream summaries inflate that into system-level capability.
- Intelligence organizations may benefit from adversaries overestimating strange capabilities; absence of correction is not confirmation.

## Publication holds

Do not publish exact Acoustic Kitty cost, mission count, success/failure story or surgical details without primary-source recovery. Do not call any insect system an operational explosive detector without field denominators. Do not use patents as evidence of efficacy. Do not infer classified deployment from DARPA sponsorship.

## Strongest countermodel

There is no deep new category here. Biological sensors are simply noisy transducers, and modern engineering will either copy their useful mechanisms or replace them with synthetic sensors. The apparent philosophical problem disappears once calibration, signal processing and manufacturing improve.

This model wins if biohybrid systems consistently lose to engineered sensors on cost, repeatability, specificity, field robustness and integration, or if the biological component contributes no unique information after controls.

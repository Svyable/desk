## Thesis

For more than two decades, the Soviet Union aimed low-level microwave radiation at the U.S. Embassy in Moscow. The signal was real; its purpose was uncertain; the feared biological weapon was never demonstrated. That combination makes the Moscow Signal a rare historical control for modern directed-energy claims: a verified exposure can generate secret research, health surveillance, adversary theories and weapon hypotheses without establishing that the exposure was designed—or capable—of producing the feared effect.

## Selection discipline

No chapter remains merely because it is dramatic. The replacement test is: **does the runner-up leave a larger, more durable institutional afterlife that can be demonstrated with primary or high-authority evidence without changing the selection rule after seeing the answer?**

Body count, fame, narrative convenience, and the author's personal interest are not sufficient reasons to retain a selection.

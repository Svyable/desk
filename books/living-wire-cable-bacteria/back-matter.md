# Back Matter

This draft keeps direct measurements separate from ecological extrapolation and speculative synthesis.

Core literature includes Nielsen et al. (2010) on sediment electrical coupling, Pfeffer et al. (2012) on filamentous bacterial conductors, Meysman et al. (2019) on direct conductance and fiber anatomy, Boschker et al. (2021) on nickel-bearing conductive proteins, and later ecological work on freshwater sulfur cycling and microbial associations.

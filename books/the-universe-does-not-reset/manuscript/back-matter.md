## A note on certainty

This draft separates four kinds of statement on purpose.

The gravitational-wave memory effect is a prediction of general relativity with a long technical literature. The leading connection among displacement memory, BMS supertranslations, and soft-graviton behavior is an established part of the asymptotically flat infrared framework. The observational detectability of specific memory signals depends on instrument sensitivity, source populations, waveform modeling, and systematic control. Claims about soft hair solving black-hole information loss, universal cosmic memory, or a necessary human role are not established physics and are not presented as such.

The book's title is therefore a proposition about state, not omniscience. Some events alter relations that do not automatically return to their prior values. That is enough to make “reset” a bad default intuition. It is not enough to turn nature into an eternal archive.

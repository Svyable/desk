## Claims deliberately not made

- No claim that gravitational-wave memory has already been individually detected at high confidence.
- No claim that soft hair stores a complete black-hole microstate or resolves the information paradox.
- No claim that the universe is globally asymptotically flat.
- No claim that all permanent physical change is an infrared memory effect.
- No claim that quantum unitarity implies practical recovery of the past.
- No invented quotes, event-rate numbers, memory amplitudes, or detection dates.

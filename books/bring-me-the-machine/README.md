# BRING ME THE MACHINE

**Subtitle:** What real reverse engineering teaches us about alleged crash retrievals

**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson

**Status:** Drafting

**Thesis:** The United States has repeatedly acquired, concealed, disassembled, flown, measured, copied around, and operationally exploited foreign technology. That documented history gives us a control group for modern claims of secret UAP crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering programs: not proof that such programs exist, but a way to ask what a real exploitation program leaves behind, what secrecy can actually hide, and which alleged traces should exist if the extraordinary version is true.

**Evidence posture:** declassified/official foreign-materiel exploitation history + AARO/DoD record heavy. HAVE DOUGHNUT/HAVE DRILL/CONSTANT PEG and related exploitation practices are documented. Claims that the U.S. possesses non-human craft or materials remain disputed and unconfirmed. Testimony is evidence that a person made a claim and, where firsthand, evidence about that person's reported experience; it is not physical proof of non-human provenance.

**Target:** 70,000–85,000 words.

**Current completion estimate:** ~6%. Chapter 1 is substantive and an 18-chapter progression, four-class evidence ledger, contradiction audit, strongest countermodel, defeat conditions and source queue are established. Chapters 2–18 require full drafting.

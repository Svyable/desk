# BRING ME THE MACHINE — research ledger

## Working question

If a state really acquired technology built by an adversary—or by something more exotic—what traces would a serious exploitation program create? The book uses documented foreign-materiel exploitation as a control group against which modern UAP retrieval claims can be tested.

This is not an argument that absence of public paperwork disproves a deeply classified program. It is an argument that secrecy has engineering consequences. Hardware needs transport, storage, people, tools, safety rules, budgets, test plans, failures, interfaces, contractors, waste streams, security compartments and eventually effects on other programs. The more ambitious the claimed exploitation, the larger that hidden industrial ecology must become.

## Evidence classes

### 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- The USAF secretly acquired and flight-tested Soviet MiG-17 and MiG-21 aircraft in HAVE DRILL and HAVE DOUGHNUT; later CONSTANT PEG operated MiG-17, MiG-21 and MiG-23 aircraft for realistic training. The National Museum of the U.S. Air Force states that the earlier tests produced complete technical breakdowns and that CONSTANT PEG operated from the Tonopah Test Range. https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/1682967/constant-peg-secret-migs-in-the-desert/
- The Air Force's 4477th Test and Evaluation Squadron flew Soviet-designed aircraft under CONSTANT PEG. https://www.nationalmuseum.af.mil/Visit/Museum-Exhibits/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/1162493/mikoyan-gurevich-mig-23ms-flogger-e/
- CIA records include acquisition of Soviet technical manuals for the MiG-21PFM, illustrating that exploitation is not only possession of hardware but accumulation of documentation and support knowledge. https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/0000772121
- AARO's 2024 Historical Record Report Volume I states that it found no empirical evidence that the U.S. government or private companies had reverse-engineered extraterrestrial technology. This is an official finding, not a proof of universal absence. https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Vol_1_2024.pdf
- AARO documents KONA BLUE as a proposed Department of Homeland Security special access program that was never approved, funded, or given material; the proposal included paranormal/UAP-related ambitions. This matters because paperwork for a proposed program can later be remembered as evidence of an operational one. Primary treatment must be extracted from AARO's historical report and released KONA BLUE records before prose makes finer claims.
- DoD OIG reviewed DoD actions regarding UAP and released an unclassified summary in January 2024. https://www.dodig.mil/In-the-Spotlight/Article/3656428/press-release-evaluation-of-the-dods-actions-regarding-unidentified-anomalous-p/

### 2 — credible but incomplete reporting / testimony

- Congressional witnesses and former intelligence personnel have alleged the existence of crash-retrieval or reverse-engineering activities. Each claim must be tracked by speaker, date, whether the claimed knowledge is firsthand or secondhand, what program name or custodian was actually alleged, and whether documentary or physical evidence was supplied publicly.
- David Grusch's public testimony belongs here as testimony, with individual propositions separated. His credentials and protected disclosures can be documented independently; claims about recovered non-human craft require their own evidence.
- Reports by serious journalists describing named sources or institutional disputes belong here when primary documentation is unavailable, but cannot silently graduate into Class 1.

### 3 — disputed allegations / unconfirmed claims

- U.S. possession of intact or partial non-human craft.
- Successful reverse engineering of non-human propulsion, power, materials or weapons.
- Specific historical retrieval stories lacking an independently documented chain of custody.
- Claims that particular commercial metamaterials are fragments of non-human vehicles when provenance cannot be reconstructed.
- Claims that classification labels, SAP terminology, SCI access, Q clearances, contractor status, or nuclear secrecy by themselves establish the existence of a retrieval program.

### 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- **The exploitation footprint:** a real hardware program should be modeled as a network of dependencies rather than a single secret. The test is whether independent traces converge on the same object, custody chain and technical problem.
- **Secrecy debt:** every additional compartment that protects a program also raises the cost of moving expertise, tooling, test data and lessons across organizational boundaries. A program can be extremely secret or extremely productive; doing both at industrial scale creates tension that should become observable somewhere.
- **The hardware asymmetry:** testimony can multiply cheaply; physical custody cannot. Ten witnesses may ultimately refer to one rumor, while one authenticated component with continuous provenance can overturn decades of argument.
- **The control-group test:** compare alleged exotic exploitation not with an imaginary transparent program but with real deeply classified exploitation efforts at comparable stages of maturity.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. Historical foreign-materiel programs prove that the U.S. can keep acquisition and exploitation secret for years. They therefore weaken simplistic arguments from silence.
2. The same programs also show that useful exploitation creates people, facilities, maintenance problems, documentation, training effects and eventual historical residue. They weaken the opposite claim that a large, productive program can be treated as evidentially indistinguishable from no program forever.
3. AARO's negative historical finding is relevant and must be represented accurately, but its access, methodology, interview universe and evidentiary standards are themselves legitimate objects of scrutiny. Official denial is evidence; it is not metaphysical closure.
4. Witness credentials can increase the prior probability that a person encountered sensitive information without proving the interpretation or provenance of that information.
5. Compartmentalization can cause honest witnesses to possess true fragments and false wholes.

## Strongest countermodel

There is no non-human retrieval program. The modern story is produced by a mixture of real classified aerospace programs, genuine foreign-materiel exploitation, compartmentalized rumors, misidentified conventional technology, legacy UFO mythology, ambiguous briefing material, sincere inference, status incentives, and occasional deliberate deception. Because secrecy blocks ordinary correction, fragments circulate and acquire false coherence. Under this model, the recurring retrieval narrative is not evidence of one hidden object; it is an emergent property of a national-security system that routinely hides real objects.

The book fails if it cannot make predictions that distinguish this countermodel from a real exotic exploitation program.

## Defeat conditions

- If documented foreign-materiel programs prove so structurally different from alleged UAP programs that they generate no useful comparison, the control-group thesis must narrow.
- If primary records show that highly productive hardware exploitation can persist for many decades without any external technical, budgetary, personnel, archival or operational residue, the exploitation-footprint claim weakens substantially.
- If authenticated material with continuous custody and independently reproducible properties inconsistent with terrestrial manufacture is produced, the book must update immediately; provenance outranks narrative symmetry.
- If alleged program witnesses converge independently on checkable details that later become verified, the testimony class should be promoted proposition by proposition rather than kept globally 'unconfirmed.'
- If AARO or another authorized investigation publishes primary records that resolve a disputed allegation, use the records, not the controversy around them.

## Publication holds

Do not write that HAVE DOUGHNUT proves crash retrievals are plausible in the evidentiary sense. It proves only that secret acquisition and exploitation of foreign hardware is historically real.

Do not use 'reverse engineering' as a mystical verb. Specify the task: characterize radar, measure materials, reproduce a component, understand manufacturing, fly against it, derive countermeasures, or attempt to recreate a subsystem.

Do not call a patent evidence that a technology works.

Do not treat a clearance as proof of access to a specific program.

Do not describe KONA BLUE as an operational retrieval program without primary evidence. The distinction between proposal and execution is central to the book.

Do not compress 'the government' into one knower. Name agency, office, command, contractor, committee or witness whenever the record allows.

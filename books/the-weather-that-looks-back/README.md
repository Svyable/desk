## Thesis

Atmospheric electricity has repeatedly produced luminous phenomena that competent observers described before science had a stable category for them. Ball lightning, sprites, jets, elves and the still-unsettled Hessdalen lights therefore provide a neglected empirical control set for UAP research. They show both why extraordinary-looking observations need not imply extraordinary technology and why the word *plasma* is useless unless it produces quantitative, testable predictions across optical, radar, radio, thermal, acoustic and environmental channels.

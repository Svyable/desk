# Careers Become Portfolios

The most resilient worker of the future may not have one job.

They may have a portfolio.

Part employment.

Part ownership.

Part creative work.

Part investing.

Part advisory work.

Part learning.

This appears inefficient from the perspective of the twentieth-century corporation.

It appears remarkably sensible from the perspective of personal resilience.

Concentration creates vulnerability.

If one employer provides your income, identity, network, status, health insurance, and professional development, losing the job means losing several systems simultaneously.

A portfolio career distributes those dependencies.

It may also better reflect human nature.

Few people possess only one interest for eighty years.

The single career required people to compress themselves into an occupational category.

The hundred-year career gives them permission to expand again.

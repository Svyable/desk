## Publication implication

Normalization closes the schema defect only. It does **not** convert generic institution homepages into claim-level evidence. The release review still requires dated, authoritative sources for material current claims, a chapter/source map or equivalent traceability record, substantive expansion to the repository length standard, and the ordinary mechanical Desk checks on one frozen candidate.

## Oklo, Deep Time, and the Problem of False Technosignatures

| | |

**Thesis:** Oklo shows both that geological systems can preserve extraordinarily specific evidence of nuclear processes across ~2 billion years and that nature can produce a process modern observers strongly associate with advanced technology. Deep-time technosignatures therefore need correlated residue packages, spatial context, mass balance and explicit natural forward models—not isolated anomalies.

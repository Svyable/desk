# Source log

Checked/used September 11, 2026. Links are public where available.

## Oklo discovery, geology and reactor physics

- **Kuroda, P. K. (1956), “On the Nuclear Physical Stability of the Uranium Minerals,” Journal of Chemical Physics 25, 781–782.** Pre-discovery theoretical argument that ancient uranium minerals could become critical under suitable composition/geometry. USGS abstract index: https://pubs.usgs.gov/bul/1116d/report.pdf
- **Gauthier-Lafaye, F.; Holliger, P.; Blanc, P.-L. (1996), “Natural fission reactors in the Franceville basin, Gabon: A review of the conditions and results of a ‘critical event’ in a geologic system,” Geochimica et Cosmochimica Acta 60, 4831–4852.** Core geology, reactor-zone context, actinide/fission-product retention and later alteration. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S0016-7037(96)00245-1
- **Gauthier-Lafaye, F. (2002), “2 billion year old natural analogs for nuclear waste disposal: the natural nuclear fission reactors in Gabon (Africa),” Comptes Rendus Physique 3, 839–849.** Repository-analogue framing, uranium concentration conditions, redox/clay retention. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/S1631-0705(02)01351-8
- **Meshik, A. P.; Hohenberg, C. M.; Pravdivtseva, O. V. (2004), “Record of Cycling Operation of the Natural Nuclear Reactor in the Oklo/Okelobondo Area in Gabon,” Physical Review Letters 93, 182302.** Selective-laser noble-gas analysis of U-free La-Ce-Sr-Ca aluminous hydroxy phosphate associated with RZ13; reports fission Xe/Kr up to ~0.03 cm³ STP/g and infers ~30-min active / ~2.5-h dormant cycling. Treat the cadence as a mineral- and model-specific reconstruction, not a directly observed universal operating schedule for all Oklo zones. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.93.182302
- **Hidaka, H. et al. (2026), “New insights into the Oklo natural fission reactors elucidated from isotopic studies using state-of-the-art analytical techniques,” Radiation Protection Dosimetry 202(2), 65–69.** Current Oklo isotopic-research agenda. Important boundary for Ch 5: describes the Meshik cycling result as coming from a “tiny and specific” U-free aluminous hydroxy phosphate in RZ13, notes that only a few RZ13 samples have been isotopically studied, and identifies measurement of Kr/Xe signatures in additional minerals and reactor zones as ongoing work. Also compiles RZ13 as unusual: ~74.9% ^235U fission contribution, ~17.9% fast-neutron ^238U fission, and ~0.242 Myr operating duration versus ~0.60–2.24 Myr for listed comparison zones. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1093/rpd/ncaf119
- **Gould, C. R.; Sharapov, E. I.; Lamoreaux, S. K. (2006), “Time variability of α from realistic models of Oklo reactors,” Physical Review C 74, 024607.** RZ2/RZ10 MCNP spectra and model-dependent fine-structure-constant constraint. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.74.024607
- **Petrov, Yu. V. et al. (2006), “Natural nuclear reactor at Oklo and variation of fundamental constants: Computation of neutronics of a fresh core,” Physical Review C 74, 064610.** Independent detailed reactor-neutronics modeling. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.74.064610
- **Gould, C. R.; Sharapov, E. I. (2012), “176Lu/175Lu thermometry for the Oklo natural reactors: A new examination of old data,” Physical Review C 85, 024610.** Reactor-temperature reconstruction and uncertainty. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.85.024610
- **Davis, E. D.; Gould, C. R.; Sharapov, E. I. (2014), “Oklo reactors and implications for nuclear science,” International Journal of Modern Physics E 23, 1430007.** Modern review of nuclear modeling, temperatures, cross sections and fundamental-constant work. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218301314300070 ; preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.4948

## Source-handling rules

- Secondary reviews can support orientation but should not substitute for primary isotope measurements where the exact quantitative claim matters.
- The ~30 min / ~2.5 h Oklo cycling result should be described as a reconstruction from a specific RZ13 phosphate archive unless and until independent mineral/zone measurements establish broader prevalence.
- A proposal to search for a technosignature is evidence only that the signature has been modeled/proposed.
- The manuscript avoids treating patents as efficacy evidence.
- Wikipedia and general explainer pages were not used as authority for controversial claims; they were useful only for locating primary literature during exploration.
- Discovery-history details that currently rely on reviews must be upgraded to the original 1972 French/CEA literature before publication-ready status.

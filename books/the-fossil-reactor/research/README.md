# Research trail — The Fossil Reactor

This directory is the claim-control layer for *The Fossil Reactor: Oklo, Deep Time, and the Problem of False Technosignatures*.

## Working thesis

Oklo demonstrates two facts that have to be held together: (a) geological systems can preserve surprisingly specific evidence of nuclear processes across ~2 billion years, and (b) a planet can naturally produce a process—self-sustaining uranium fission—that modern observers strongly associate with advanced technology. Therefore deep-time technosignature work should privilege correlated, spatially contextual evidence packages and explicit natural forward models over isolated anomalies.

## Four evidence classes

### 1 — Verified / documented

- Natural self-sustaining fission occurred in multiple reactor zones in the Franceville Basin, Gabon.
- The 1972 discovery followed anomalous uranium-isotope measurements in Gabonese uranium entering the French nuclear fuel cycle.
- Fission-product and neutron-capture isotope patterns support the reactor interpretation.
- Ancient natural uranium had a significantly higher U-235 fraction than modern natural uranium because U-235 has the shorter half-life.
- Water moderation and uranium-rich ore can produce criticality under modeled Oklo conditions.
- Oklo has been studied as a natural analogue for radionuclide transport/retention and as a constraint on possible time variation of fundamental constants.
- Schmidt & Frank (2018/2019) formally posed the deep-time industrial-detectability question and explicitly stated strong doubt that a previous industrial civilization existed.
- NASA has sponsored modern technosignature workshops and non-radio technosignature research.

### 2 — Credible but incomplete / model-dependent

- The Meshik et al. xenon reconstruction of ~30-minute active periods separated by ~2.5-hour dormant intervals.
- Specific ancient reactor temperatures for individual reaction zones.
- Detailed neutron spectra reconstructed after mining and geological alteration.
- Quantitative bounds on change in alpha derived from Sm-149, because mapping depends on reactor model and assumptions about varying parameters.
- Element-by-element migration distances over two billion years where sampling coverage or original geometry is incomplete.
- The deep-time survivability of particular modern synthetic markers such as polymer transformation products.

### 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

- Any claim that Oklo was engineered.
- Claims that the PETM or another known ancient climate event was caused by industrial civilization absent independent technological residues.
- Claims that one anomalous isotope ratio, alloy, object, or geological specimen establishes prior technology without secure provenance and replication.
- Claims that a hypothetical technosignature model demonstrates the technology exists.

### 4 — Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- “Nuclear taphonomy” as a deliberate field linking nuclear forensics, repository science and deep-time technosignature searches.
- Provenance mismatches and complementary source/waste reservoirs as more durable agency markers than artifact shape.
- Isotope topology: spatial relationships among parent, daughter, enrichment and waste signatures as an agency discriminator.
- Blind stratigraphic searches selected for preservation quality rather than famous anomalies.
- Lunar preservation of ancient terrestrial ejecta as a possible, probably low-yield, archive for terrestrial deep time.

## Contradiction / incentive audit

The central danger is not conventional scientific disagreement but narrative promotion. A model-dependent detail can become “what happened”; a technosignature proposal can become “evidence”; a famous climate analogue can become an ancient-industry candidate merely because the story is attractive.

Known incentives that can distort interpretation include:

- popular and media preference for artificial-origin headlines;
- disciplinary preference for familiar natural mechanisms even before quantitative exclusion;
- commercial mining's destruction of geological context;
- selective publication of anomalous positive results;
- contamination introduced by drilling, sample preparation or curation;
- the ability of an unconstrained hypothetical civilization to be assigned whatever technology rescues a failed prediction.

Countermeasure: every agency claim should identify at least one observation that would reduce its probability and at least one new observation it predicts before search.

## Book-level defeat conditions

1. **Against the prior-industry claim:** a positive ancient-industry interpretation should not be promoted without securely syngenetic, independently replicated evidence that materially outperforms known geological, biological, impact, volcanic, cosmogenic and contamination models.
2. **Against the book's conjunction thesis:** if a single universal, durable marker is discovered that non-agentic processes demonstrably cannot produce, then multi-proxy conjunction may be unnecessary for that marker class.
3. **Against Oklo's use as a false-positive control:** if future evidence showed critical reaction zones required introduced enriched fuel or manufactured structures rather than in-situ geological ore, the central example would have to be rewritten. No credible evidence currently supports that.

## Research priorities before merge-ready publication

- Recover and cite the original French 1972 discovery papers/CEA announcements rather than relying on later reviews for discovery chronology.
- Build a reactor-zone table: zone, age estimate, U depletion, neutron-fluence proxies, inferred temperature, alteration history, key samples, and surviving geometry.
- Build an isotope table for U, Nd, Sm, Gd, Lu, Ru, Xe/Kr, with exactly what each system constrains and major contamination/migration caveats.
- Reconstruct the Meshik xenon calculation from the paper rather than relying on the abstract-level 30 min / 2.5 h summary.
- Quantify fission-product retention/mobility by element and host mineral from primary Oklo geochemistry.
- Deepen Great Oxidation / Franceville uranium-ore formation with modern redox geochemistry literature.
- Add primary Anthropocene stratigraphy sources for artificial radionuclides, spheroidal carbonaceous particles, plastics and mineral novelty.
- Add experimental polymer/industrial-material diagenesis literature; the present manuscript treats those futures cautiously but does not yet provide a compound-by-compound preservation atlas.
- Build a current technosignature source table distinguishing remote detectability from in-situ planetary forensics.
- Expand every chapter below Desk's 3,000-word minimum using these source-specific reconstructions rather than generic epistemic framing.

# The Forbidden Sense

*Why governments kept testing psi after science thought the question should have died*

Governments really investigated remote viewing, psychotronics and other anomalous human-capacity claims. That fact is not the same as proving the claimed abilities. *The Forbidden Sense* lives in the difficult space between those statements.

The book follows the American STARGATE/GRILL FLAME record, Soviet and East European threat assessments, Chinese qigong and “extraordinary human function” research, ganzfeld telepathy experiments, precognition/presentiment, random-event psychokinesis, sensory biology, predictive neuroscience, and the misuse of frontier physics as explanatory decoration.

Its central wager is deliberately narrow: after fraud, leakage, unconscious inference, statistics, publication bias, Cold War threat inflation and cultural mythology have been stripped away, a small anomalous-information residue would still be a major scientific fact. The way to find out is not more folklore. It is adversarial metrology.

## Research discipline

See [research/README.md](research/README.md). The research trail separates documented government activity, credible-but-incomplete statistical evidence, disputed claims, skeptical/negative evidence and speculative synthesis. A classified document is treated as evidence of what the document records, not as automatic validation of the phenomenon it discusses.

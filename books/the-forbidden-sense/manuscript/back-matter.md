# Back Matter

## A note on claims

This manuscript does not claim that remote viewing, telepathy, precognition, presentiment or psychokinesis has been established as a conventional scientific fact. It also does not claim that every historical government program was credulous or that program termination mathematically disproved every laboratory effect.

The historical claims in the manuscript should be read at the level supported by their sources. When a declassified document records a remote-viewing session, that establishes that the session was conducted and documented. It does not establish that the information came through an anomalous channel. When an intelligence assessment discusses Soviet psychotronics, that establishes U.S. analytic attention to the subject, not a Soviet operational weapon. When DIA translated Chinese qigong or extraordinary-function literature, that establishes collection and translation, not independent validation of the translated experiment.

## The strongest falsification path

The book’s central synthesis would weaken substantially if large, preregistered, independently run, adversarially designed experiments repeatedly produced narrow null results that excluded the effect sizes claimed by the strongest modern meta-analyses.

It would strengthen substantially if a prospectively specified anomalous-information effect replicated across independent skeptical and proponent laboratories, survived automated randomization and blind judging, showed stable subject-level or state-level predictors, and replicated a second time without protocol drift.

A mechanism would be a later question.

## Terms

**Psi** — umbrella term used in parapsychology for claimed anomalous cognition or mind-matter effects. This manuscript uses it as a historical/research category, not as an assertion that one underlying phenomenon exists.

**Remote viewing** — attempt to describe a distant or hidden target without ordinary sensory access.

**Ganzfeld** — experimental procedure using a relatively uniform sensory field, often with red light and white/pink noise, used in telepathy-style target-selection experiments.

**Presentiment** — claimed pre-stimulus physiological difference correlated with a stimulus that is selected later.

**Psychokinesis** — claimed influence of mental intention on physical systems without known ordinary motor or physical interaction.

**Psychotronics / paraphysics** — historically variable terminology used especially in Soviet/East European and intelligence contexts for alleged anomalous mind-information-matter interactions. The terms did not denote one standardized technology.

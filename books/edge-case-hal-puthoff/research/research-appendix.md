# Research appendix

## Priority archival work

### SRI contract reconstruction

Build a year-by-year table from 1972 through Puthoff’s departure from SRI: sponsor; contract/project identifier; amount when public; named investigators; subjects; protocol; target-selection method; judging method; output; published derivative; known criticism; status of raw records. CIA Reading Room metadata and report bibliographies provide starting anchors but should not be assumed complete.

### Remote-viewing failure corpus

The literature overrepresents memorable hits. Build a deliberately failure-biased sample from declassified sessions, including reports that state no target correlation. Compare transcript length, specificity, target pool, feedback, and judging method with celebrated cases. This is necessary to estimate selection effects in retrospective storytelling.

### AIR 1995 reproducibility audit

Reconstruct the experiment set behind Jessica Utts’s and Ray Hyman’s conclusions. Separate disagreements about statistical significance from disagreements about experimental independence, protocol quality, and what replication should mean. Avoid paraphrasing the final report as a unanimous scientific conclusion; it was not.

### Zero-point literature map

For stochastic electrodynamics and vacuum-inertia proposals, build a claim graph: target phenomenon, equation/model, prediction, mainstream comparator, independent replication, published criticism, current status. Include Levin 2009 prominently for the HRP inertia mechanism and search for later responses rather than assuming silence settles the issue.

### EarthTech public test archive

Catalog devices and claims Puthoff or EarthTech has publicly described testing. Record test boundary, input/output measurement, calibration, controls, result, publication status, client confidentiality limitations, and independent follow-up. A negative-result ledger is especially important because it can show whether the institution functions as a filter rather than a promoter.

### NIDS document-first history

Separate contemporaneous NIDS publications, archived website material, board records, field protocols, and instrument reports from later Skinwalker books and television narratives. Do not use later retellings to establish details that a contemporaneous record could verify.

### BAASS / AAWSAP contract map

Use DIA’s FOIA collection as the base. Reconstruct solicitation, BAASS award, statement of work, deliverables, technical reports, contract reviews, modifications, and termination. Map which DIRDs were authored under the program and which Puthoff says he commissioned. Search for documentary support for EarthTech subcontract terms rather than relying solely on later talks.

### TTSA corporate record

Use SEC filings as the source of truth for titles, board roles, equity, strategic claims, risk factors, and the 2021 strategy change. Marketing announcements should be cited separately and not allowed to override filed disclosures.

## Defeat discipline

Every chapter expansion should add at least one way the favored interpretation could be wrong. Avoid a pattern in which skeptical possibilities are mentioned briefly and then narratively overwhelmed by extraordinary material. Where no discriminating evidence exists, preserve “unknown” rather than selecting a conclusion for momentum.

# Working chronology

- **1936** — Harold Edward Puthoff is born in Chicago.
- **1960s** — Professional biographies place Puthoff in engineering / national-security work including General Electric, Sperry, and NSA. Exact duties are not inferred beyond public records.
- **1967** — Stanford Ph.D.; dissertation on stimulated Raman scattering and a tunable Raman laser.
- **1969** — *Fundamentals of Quantum Electronics*, with Richard H. Pantell, published by Wiley.
- **1971** — Puthoff joins Stanford Research Institute.
- **1972** — SRI work with Ingo Swann begins; CIA starts direct research into remote viewing with outside scientists in summer 1972 according to CIA’s retrospective history.
- **1973–1974** — Sponsor-controlled and long-distance remote-viewing experiments produce reports that become central to the early program.
- **1974** — Targ and Puthoff publish “Information transmission under conditions of sensory shielding” in *Nature*.
- **1976** — Targ and Puthoff publish a broader parapsychology review / experimental account in *Proceedings of the IEEE*.
- **1977** — CIA says it ends direct remote-viewing research and transfers the effort into Defense Department sponsorship.
- **1978** — Marks and Kammann publish a failed replication / critique in *Nature*.
- **1980** — Tart, Puthoff, and Targ publish a response in *Nature*.
- **1983–1984** — Declassified bibliographies record Puthoff as an author on SRI psychoenergetics proposals, panel documents, and final reports.
- **Mid-1980s** — Puthoff leaves SRI and establishes the Austin-based Institute for Advanced Studies / later EarthTech institutional line. Current corporate biographies date his leadership to 1985.
- **1986** — Marks and Scott publish “Remote viewing exposed” in *Nature*.
- **1987** — *Physical Review D* publishes Puthoff’s stochastic-electrodynamics hydrogen ground-state paper.
- **1994** — Haisch, Rueda, and Puthoff publish “Inertia as a zero-point-field Lorentz force” in *Physical Review A*.
- **1995** — CIA commissions independent AIR evaluation of the long-running government remote-viewing program; CIA decides not to restore it. Robert Bigelow establishes NIDS; Puthoff later serves among its scientific advisers.
- **2002** — Puthoff publishes a conference-proceedings treatment of a polarizable-vacuum approach to gravitation.
- **2008–2010** — DIA’s advanced aerospace contract with Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies generates a family of Defense Intelligence Reference Documents on advanced aerospace / future technology topics; Puthoff later publicly describes EarthTech as a subcontractor and himself as helping commission papers.
- **2009** — Yefim S. Levin publishes a *Physical Review A* critique arguing the Haisch-Rueda-Puthoff zero-point inertia result arises from incorrect assumptions.
- **2012** — A version of Puthoff’s advanced spacetime-metric propulsion paper appears publicly on arXiv.
- **2017** — To The Stars Academy launches with Puthoff as a co-founder and science / technology executive; SEC filings document his equity and governance role.
- **2021** — SEC filing records Puthoff’s transition from TTSA director to scientific advisory-board role as the company changes strategy.
- **2025–2026** — Current TTSA filings continue to list Puthoff on the advisory side and identify his EarthTech / IASA leadership.

Dates involving program names and sponsor transitions remain subject to document-level audit; the manuscript avoids treating “Stargate,” “AATIP,” and “AAWSAP” as interchangeable labels.

# Working notes

## Do not collapse these terms

- SRI and Stanford University are not interchangeable institutions.
- CIA’s early remote-viewing work, later Army/DIA program names, GRILL FLAME, CENTER LANE, SUN STREAK, and STARGATE require date-specific attribution rather than one universal label.
- AAWSAP / the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications effort and “AATIP” have overlapping but disputed usage histories. Use the source’s term and date.
- NIDS, BAASS, EarthTech, and TTSA are separate organizations even when personnel recur.
- “Zero-point energy exists in quantum theory” is not equivalent to “usable energy can be extracted from the vacuum.”
- “Metric solutions exist in general relativity” is not equivalent to “metric engineering is technologically available.”
- “The Navy video is authentic” is not equivalent to “the object has an extraordinary origin.”

## High-risk biographical claims

Public corporate biographies list General Electric, Sperry, NSA, Stanford, SRI, EarthTech, and IASA. They do not provide enough detail to reconstruct sensitive duties. Do not invent classified assignments or clearances.

Puthoff’s association with NIDS is documented, but do not place him at every Skinwalker Ranch event merely because he sat on the scientific network.

Puthoff publicly describes EarthTech as a BAASS subcontractor and himself as involved in commissioning advanced technical papers. Seek contract-level corroboration for specific subcontract terms before stating them as independently established.

Modern claims that Puthoff handled or evaluated extraordinary UAP material need sample-specific provenance and laboratory records. Do not use network association as proof.

## Language discipline

Prefer “reported,” “described,” “argued,” “published,” “funded,” “commissioned,” and “measured” over “proved,” “confirmed,” or “demonstrated” unless the source and replication status earn the stronger verb.

Never use classification as an adjective meaning true.

Never use “government admitted” where a document merely establishes that a program or inquiry existed.

Never imply that a scientific paper passed a stronger test than peer review actually supplies.

## Book-level counterarguments to keep alive

1. Puthoff’s willingness to entertain stigmatized subjects can be read as intellectual courage, but also exposes him to selection bias and weak-claim ecosystems.
2. Government funding is rational under low probability when strategic consequence is high; it need not indicate belief.
3. Remote-viewing statistical claims and operational intelligence utility are different questions.
4. Recurring personnel networks may reflect trust and rare expertise rather than one continuous hidden program.
5. Serious criticism of Puthoff’s physics exists inside mainstream journals and must be presented alongside his publications.
6. UAP may contain unresolved observations without the unresolved set having one cause.
7. Private or classified evidence can rationally change a participant’s belief while remaining insufficient to change a public reader’s belief by the same amount.

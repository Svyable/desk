# Defeat conditions

A book about edge research should say what evidence would force its own framing to change.

## Remote viewing

The manuscript currently treats anomalous information transfer as disputed and remote viewing as not demonstrated to be a dependable intelligence capability. That posture should change if multiple independent laboratories preregister the same free-response protocol, eliminate transcript and judging cues, retain all trials, reproduce a stable effect across new subjects, and prospectively show enough specificity that users can distinguish useful output from noise before feedback. A mechanism is not required for the first update; robust replication would be enough.

The anomalous interpretation should lose probability if effect sizes collapse as information leakage and researcher degrees of freedom are closed, if purported replications prove statistically dependent on the original datasets, or if prospective operational tests continue to perform at chance after adequate power.

## Stochastic electrodynamics and zero-point models

A Puthoff-associated vacuum model should gain standing if it predicts a measurable result not already fixed by standard quantum theory and independent groups reproduce that result. It should lose standing where mathematically consistent analysis shows the proposed force vanishes or where unique predictions fail.

The Haisch-Rueda-Puthoff inertia mechanism in particular has to answer the published Levin critique rather than rely on the prestige of its original *Physical Review A* publication.

## Vacuum-energy engineering

A practical extraction claim wins when a device produces sustained net usable work under a complete energy balance with calibrated instrumentation, adversarial controls, independent replication, and no conventional source sufficient to account for output. The existence of vacuum fluctuations, the Casimir effect, patents, investment, or a government briefing are not substitutes.

## Inertial or gravitational modification

A claim wins when a reproducible change in acceleration, inertial response, apparent weight, or gravitational coupling persists across independent laboratories and survives controls for electromagnetic force, vibration, acoustic coupling, thermal gradients, buoyancy, electrostatics, mass motion, software error, and ordinary propulsion.

## UAP performance

Claims of extreme acceleration, hypersonic travel, low observability, or transmedium motion should be upgraded only when calibrated raw data establish position, timing, geometry, sensor state, uncertainty, and continuity of track strongly enough to calculate the claimed performance. Witness testimony can motivate the test but cannot replace it.

## Non-human technology

The manuscript currently treats non-human origin as a disputed hypothesis, not an established fact. That should change if a recovered artifact has secure provenance, independent laboratory access, reproducible material or functional properties not plausibly produced by known terrestrial processes, and a documented connection to an observed craft or event. Genetic, linguistic, manufacturing, isotopic, or engineering evidence would need to be evaluated on its own terms.

## Institutional thesis

The book argues that Puthoff’s most durable role has been making extraordinary questions administratively and technically legible to serious sponsors. That synthesis would be weakened if archival evidence showed that his involvement was peripheral rather than structurally important across SRI, Bigelow-linked work, the DIA technical studies, and TTSA; or if a better-documented explanation shows the apparent continuity is largely retrospective narrative construction.

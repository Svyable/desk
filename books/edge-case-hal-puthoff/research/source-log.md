## Evidence-handling note

Government funding is evidence of government interest, not proof of the funded hypothesis. Publication is evidence that a paper passed a publication process, not proof of its conclusion. A patent is evidence of filing and grant, not efficacy. Participant recollection is testimony. Classified-source claims are not upgraded merely because classification is invoked. Later chapters preserve these distinctions explicitly.

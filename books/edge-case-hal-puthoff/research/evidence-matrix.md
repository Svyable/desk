# Evidence matrix

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- Puthoff earned a Stanford Ph.D. in 1967 for work on stimulated Raman effects and tunable lasers.
- He co-authored *Fundamentals of Quantum Electronics* with Richard Pantell in 1969.
- Public corporate filings describe his professional background at General Electric, Sperry, NSA, Stanford, SRI, and EarthTech/IASA.
- CIA and Defense Department entities funded remote-viewing / psychoenergetics research involving SRI.
- Targ and Puthoff published the 1974 *Nature* paper.
- Marks and Kammann published a failed replication / critique in *Nature* in 1978; later exchanges and criticism continued.
- Declassified records contain both apparently successful sessions and sessions described as showing no target correlation.
- The broader government remote-viewing program continued after CIA’s early involvement and was evaluated for CIA in 1995.
- Puthoff published a 1987 *Physical Review D* paper on a stochastic-electrodynamics account of the hydrogen ground state.
- Puthoff has led EarthTech / IASA since the mid-1980s according to corporate records.
- Puthoff served as a NIDS science adviser; serious reporting and archived NIDS material support this.
- Puthoff has publicly described EarthTech as a BAASS subcontractor and himself as commissioning future-oriented technical papers; DIA-associated DIRD records independently establish the document family.
- SEC records establish that Puthoff co-founded To The Stars Academy, served as science/technology executive and director, held equity, and later moved to an advisory role.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete reporting, testimony, or inference

- Participant accounts of especially impressive SRI remote-viewing sessions whose full target, cueing, transcript, and verification chain cannot always be reconstructed from public records.
- Participant descriptions of Puthoff’s exact roles inside sensitive government or contractor activities beyond what public contracts and reports specify.
- Accounts of NIDS / Skinwalker Ranch events reported by investigators without independent instrumental closure.
- Statements by Puthoff and colleagues about the motivation, internal structure, or sponsor expectations of BAASS / AAWSAP where contracting records establish the program but not every recollected detail.
- Statements by UAP participants about classified sensor data or materials unavailable for independent public examination.

## Class 3 — disputed allegations / claims lacking independent confirmation

- Remote viewing as a demonstrated extrasensory information channel rather than an artifact of methodology, statistics, cueing, or other ordinary causes.
- Claims that particular celebrated operational remote-viewing sessions establish paranormal access by themselves.
- Claims that zero-point vacuum physics provides a demonstrated practical energy-extraction technology.
- Claims that spacetime-metric engineering is presently an actionable propulsion capability.
- Claims that anomalous materials publicly associated with UAP are proven to be manufactured by non-human intelligence.
- Claims that government interest, classification, NIDS, BAASS, AATIP, or TTSA participation establishes possession of extraterrestrial technology.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- That Puthoff’s enduring institutional role is best understood as a specialist in making high-consequence fringe questions administratively and technically legible to sponsors.
- That his early SRI experience may have taught him a durable lesson that public scientific ridicule and private government interest can coexist, affecting his later willingness to entertain other stigmatized claims.
- That the recurrence of a small network of researchers across remote viewing, Bigelow-funded programs, advanced propulsion, and UAP is primarily an effect of expertise and trust networks rather than evidence of a single hidden program.
- That a better future institution could preserve Puthoff’s willingness to test unusual claims while imposing stronger preregistration, adversarial controls, independent replication, and public defeat conditions.

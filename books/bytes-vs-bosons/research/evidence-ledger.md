# BYTES VS BOSONS — evidence ledger

Working rule: the word **information** changes meaning across engineering, thermodynamics, quantum theory and gravity. Never let a shared word substitute for a demonstrated equivalence.

## Evidence class 1 — experimentally demonstrated / directly operational

### Landauer erasure cost

- Rolf Landauer’s 1961 IBM paper linked logical irreversibility to physical irreversibility and heat generation.
- For erasure of one classical bit in the quasistatic limit, the standard lower bound is kT ln 2.
- Do not rewrite this as “every computational operation must dissipate kT ln 2.” That is false.
- C. H. Bennett’s later work showed that general computation can, in principle, be organized reversibly so that logical erasure is avoided until or unless information is actually discarded.

Primary sources:
- Landauer, IBM Journal of Research and Development (1961): https://doi.org/10.1147/RD.53.0183
- Bennett, IBM Journal of Research and Development (1973): https://doi.org/10.1147/rd.176.0525

### Experimental Landauer tests

- Bérut et al. (2012) implemented a one-bit memory using a colloidal particle in a double-well potential and measured dissipated heat during erasure.
- In the slow-erasure limit, the mean heat approached the Landauer bound.
- This demonstrates a thermodynamic cost for the implemented logically irreversible transformation. It does not establish that information is an independent substance.

Source:
- Nature 483, 187–189 (2012): https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10872

### Quantum no-cloning

- The linear structure of quantum mechanics forbids a universal operation that perfectly copies an arbitrary unknown quantum state.
- This is a mathematical consequence of standard quantum mechanics with experimental relevance throughout quantum information.
- Do not paraphrase it as “quantum information can never be copied” without qualifiers. Known orthogonal states can be copied, and classical information can be extracted/correlated in permitted ways.

Source:
- Wootters and Zurek, “A single quantum cannot be cloned,” Nature 299, 802–803 (1982): https://doi.org/10.1038/299802a0

### Bosons are not “matter”

- The Standard Model divides elementary particles into fermions and bosons.
- Ordinary matter is built primarily from fermions such as quarks and electrons.
- Photons, gluons, W and Z particles and the Higgs are bosons; the first four examples participate in the Standard Model’s force/field structure in different ways, while the Higgs boson is associated with the Higgs field.
- The book title is therefore intentionally a category mismatch. It must never define bosons as a synonym for physical reality.

Source:
- CERN, “The Standard Model”: https://home.cern/science/physics/standard-model/

## Evidence class 2 — well-established theoretical physics with strong internal support

### Bekenstein entropy

- Jacob Bekenstein argued in 1973 for assigning entropy to black holes proportional to horizon area.
- The work connected thermodynamics, information and gravitational horizons in a way that became foundational to black-hole thermodynamics.
- Bekenstein’s information language should be presented historically and technically, not used as a shortcut to “black holes are computers.”

Source:
- Bekenstein, Physical Review D 7, 2333 (1973): https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.7.2333

### Hawking radiation

- Hawking’s 1975 calculation showed that quantum fields in a black-hole spacetime lead to thermal particle emission.
- This gives black holes a temperature and drives the information-loss problem when combined with ordinary assumptions about quantum evolution.
- Hawking radiation has not been directly detected from an astrophysical black hole. The theoretical result is foundational; empirical detection of the radiation itself is a separate issue.

Source:
- Hawking, Communications in Mathematical Physics 43, 199–220 (1975): https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02345020

### Black-hole information problem remains an active problem

- The paradox arises from tension among black-hole evaporation, semiclassical gravity and quantum-mechanical information preservation.
- Modern work has produced major theoretical advances, including Page-curve calculations in controlled models, but “the black-hole information paradox is solved” remains too broad unless the specific assumptions and model are named.
- Treat 2025–2026 review literature as a map of live disputes, not a license to announce consensus where none exists.

Recent review entry:
- “The Black Hole Information Problem,” Entropy 27 (2025): https://doi.org/10.3390/e27060592

## Evidence class 3 — mathematically powerful frameworks whose ontological interpretation is not experimentally settled

### AdS/CFT

- Maldacena’s 1997 proposal relates certain conformal field theories to gravitational/string theories in anti-de Sitter spacetimes.
- The correspondence has generated an enormous body of successful theoretical work.
- It is not direct experimental evidence that our cosmological spacetime is literally a hologram.

Source:
- Maldacena, arXiv:hep-th/9711200: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9711200

### Holographic entropy and geometry

- Ryu and Takayanagi proposed that entanglement entropy in certain conformal field theories can be related to the area of minimal surfaces in a dual anti-de Sitter geometry.
- This is a precise theoretical relationship in a specific holographic setting.
- Do not convert “area computes entanglement entropy in this duality” into “space is made of entanglement” without an explicit interpretive step.

Sources:
- Physical Review Letters 96, 181602 (2006): https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.96.181602
- arXiv version: https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/0603001

### Entanglement and connected spacetime

- Mark Van Raamsdonk argued that patterns of quantum entanglement are intimately related to the emergence/connectivity of classical spacetime in holographic quantum gravity.
- This is an influential theoretical argument, not an experimental reconstruction of our universe from entanglement.

Source:
- Van Raamsdonk, “Building up spacetime with quantum entanglement”: https://arxiv.org/abs/1005.3035

### Holographic quantum error correction

- Almheiri, Dong and Harlow identified a connection between bulk locality in AdS/CFT and quantum error correction.
- This is important because it makes “spacetime as encoded information” more technically precise than loose simulation metaphors.
- It still lives inside the AdS/CFT framework and should not be generalized to all of nature without argument.

Source:
- Journal of High Energy Physics 2015, 163: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/JHEP04%282015%29163
- arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.7041

### Holographic principle

- Entropy bounds and black-hole thermodynamics motivated the holographic principle.
- Bousso’s review is useful because it distinguishes established bounds/results from the larger claim that the fundamental degrees of freedom of quantum gravity are encoded holographically.
- The book should preserve that distinction.

Source:
- Bousso, Reviews of Modern Physics 74, 825 (2002): https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.74.825

## Evidence class 4 — philosophical interpretation / contested ontology

The following should never appear as unqualified facts:

- “The universe is made of information.”
- “Matter is information.”
- “Spacetime is made of qubits.”
- “Reality is a computer.”
- “Consciousness collapses the wavefunction.”
- “Black holes prove information is more fundamental than matter.”
- “The holographic principle proves the universe is two-dimensional.”
- “Entanglement proves spacetime is emergent in our universe.”

These statements may describe philosophical positions, popular framings or research intuitions. They are not all equivalent, and none should be smuggled into the book through metaphor.

Useful comparison source:
- *It From Bit or Bit From It? On Physics and Information* (Springer/FQXi, 2015), which explicitly collects competing views rather than presenting a single settled doctrine: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-12946-4

## Evidence class 5 — modern engineering reality

### AI/data centers remain physical

- AI computation requires electricity and physical data-center infrastructure.
- The International Energy Agency’s 2025 report analyzes both AI electricity demand and possible AI applications in energy systems.
- A 2026 IEA update reported continued rapid growth in data-center electricity use and investment.
- U.S. DOE/Lawrence Berkeley work estimates large uncertainty around future U.S. data-center demand, which is exactly why the book should avoid single-number inevitability claims.

Sources:
- IEA, “Energy and AI” (2025): https://www.iea.org/reports/energy-and-ai
- IEA, data-centre electricity update (2026): https://www.iea.org/news/data-centre-electricity-use-surged-in-2025-even-with-tightening-bottlenecks-driving-a-scramble-for-solutions
- U.S. DOE, 2024 U.S. Data Center Energy Usage report release: https://www.energy.gov/articles/doe-releases-new-report-evaluating-increase-electricity-demand-data-centers

Interpretive constraint:
- Present data-center electricity as a demonstration that computation remains materially instantiated.
- Do not imply that present electricity use is close to or dominated by the Landauer limit.
- Do not reuse the policy/grid argument of **Amped**.

## Terminology holds

### “Entropy”
Before publication, every use of entropy should be tagged internally as one of:
- thermodynamic/statistical entropy;
- Shannon entropy;
- von Neumann entropy;
- black-hole/Bekenstein–Hawking entropy;
- loose metaphor.

Remove the loose-metaphor uses unless they earn their keep.

### “Information”
Ask what is being counted:
- distinguishable alternatives?
- uncertainty in a probability distribution?
- correlations?
- an encoded message?
- a quantum state?
- degrees of freedom?
- recoverable microstate detail?

Never assume the same word means the same object.

### “Fundamental”
Possible meanings:
- cannot be reduced within a current theory;
- appears in the axioms;
- is conserved or constrained;
- is ontologically primary;
- is merely widely useful.

The manuscript must name which meaning it intends.

## Contradiction and incentive audit

1. **Popular physics rewards ontological headlines.** “Spacetime is made of entanglement” travels farther than “a class of holographic dualities has a deep entropy-area relation.” Preserve the latter’s precision.
2. **Technology marketing rewards dematerialization.** Cloud, AI and software companies sell abstraction while building physical infrastructure. Do not confuse marketing language with a scientific claim.
3. **Energy-sector narratives reward large forecasts.** Data-center power estimates have wide ranges and changing assumptions. Date every fast-moving denominator.
4. **Information theory reuses ordinary words.** “Information,” “entropy,” “memory” and “observation” have technical meanings that can accidentally import human or semantic intuitions.
5. **Elegant mathematics attracts ontological overreach.** A successful duality can reveal equivalence between descriptions without deciding which description is “really” the universe.

## Publication holds

Do not publish as established fact without stronger chapter-specific support:

- any claim that black-hole information loss has a single accepted resolution;
- any claim that Hawking radiation has been directly observed from astrophysical black holes;
- any claim that our universe is empirically known to be holographic;
- any claim that spacetime emergence from entanglement has direct experimental confirmation;
- any estimate presenting the Landauer bound as a practical floor currently constraining commercial AI systems;
- any claim that reversible computing will necessarily become commercially dominant;
- any claim that bosonic/photonic computing removes thermodynamic constraints;
- any equation equating Shannon entropy with thermodynamic entropy without the assumptions and mapping stated.

## Research queue

- Reconstruct Landauer’s intellectual context from IBM archival/biographical material rather than relying only on the 1961 paper.
- Build a Maxwell’s-demon chapter that distinguishes measurement, memory, feedback and erasure rather than treating “information has a cost” as a slogan.
- Trace Bekenstein’s move from horizon area to entropy using the 1972–1974 papers and the generalized second law.
- Reconstruct Hawking’s change of position on information loss from primary lectures/papers.
- Build a clean no-cloning/no-deleting/no-hiding sequence for the quantum-information chapters.
- Map which holographic results are theorems, conjectures, dictionary entries or interpretations.
- Add a chapter-specific source map for Ryu–Takayanagi, entanglement wedge reconstruction and holographic error correction.
- Research photonic computing/interconnects without turning “photons are bosons” into the claim that photonics is inherently post-thermal.
- Compare practical joules per operation/inference with the Landauer floor only after selecting a defensible hardware workload and date.
- Find one physical object for every abstract chapter: memory cell, notebook, horizon diagram, optical transceiver, cryostat, transformer, wafer or fiber.

## Defeat condition

The big thesis should be weakened if the manuscript cannot identify any discriminating empirical or theoretical content behind the phrase “information is fundamental” beyond a relabeling of ordinary physical state descriptions.

Conversely, the book should become bolder if mature quantum-gravity results provide a clearer operational sense in which informational structure determines geometry across settings broad enough to escape the “special AdS model” objection.

Either outcome is acceptable.

The book fails only if it decides the answer before doing the accounting.

# Project Seal

**Working title:** *Project Seal: The Tsunami Weapon That Almost Made Sense*

**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson

## Reader promise

This book reconstructs the strange, documented Allied attempt to turn explosive-generated waves into a weapon during World War II, then asks a harder question than whether a “tsunami bomb” existed: why did technically serious people keep pursuing an idea whose spectacular premise concealed brutal constraints of scale, bathymetry, coupling, timing, and coastal geometry?

The point is not that a secret superweapon worked. The point is that Project Seal was real enough to test, secret enough to become legend, and constrained enough to become an unusually clean case study in how military institutions investigate apparently impossible weapons.

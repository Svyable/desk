# Nothing Counts Yet

*The Hidden Infrastructure of Practice, Private Failure, and Human Capability*

**Nothing Counts Yet** is about the provisional spaces that make competence possible: rehearsal rooms, drafts, sandboxes, simulations, apprenticeships, mock trials, pilots, test kitchens, play, and every other place where a person or institution is allowed to be wrong before being required to be right.

The book argues that modern life is becoming unusually good at recording, ranking, publishing, and judging people before they have finished becoming capable. A society that destroys its backstage eventually runs out of good performances.

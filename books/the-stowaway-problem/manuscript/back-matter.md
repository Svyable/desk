# Back Matter

The central claim of this draft is intentionally narrower than a verdict on panspermia. Natural rock exchange between Mars and Earth is established. Biological survival through the complete launch-transfer-entry-establishment chain is not. Spacecraft contamination is documented and persistent enough to require serious controls; contamination of a future extraterrestrial sample is a risk to be bounded, not an event this manuscript claims has already occurred.

The book treats planetary protection as experimental epistemology. A life-detection result is stronger when the mission has characterized the terrestrial organisms and molecules capable of imitating it. Cleanroom microbiology, microbial archives, blanks, witness plates, materials inventories and chain-of-custody records are therefore part of the scientific instrument.

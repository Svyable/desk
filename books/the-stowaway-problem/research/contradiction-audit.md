# Contradiction and incentive audit

## Contradictions the manuscript must preserve

**Hardy spores vs hostile Mars.** Survival under cleaning, shock or shielded space exposure does not show that an organism can reproduce in present-day martian surface conditions. The book must not let resistance become ecological competence.

**Cleanroom selection vs cleanroom evolution.** Surveys are consistent with a residual community enriched for stress-tolerant organisms. They do not by themselves show that NASA cleaning practices rapidly evolved new resistance traits.

**Meteorite transfer vs biological transfer.** Martian meteorites prove material exchange, not living passenger exchange.

**ALH84001 contamination vs total dismissal.** Some organics have strong terrestrial contamination explanations. That does not mean every mineralogical or morphological observation in the meteorite has the same origin.

**Molecular sensitivity vs viability.** Sequencing expands detection but can count dead material; culture can prove reproduction under offered conditions but miss dormant or fastidious organisms.

**Protection vs discovery.** More aggressive sterilization can protect scientific integrity while altering organics or damaging hardware. There is no cost-free maximum-cleanliness setting.

## Incentives and failure modes

- Mission teams have incentives to demonstrate compliance and preserve mission access to scientifically valuable regions.
- Planetary-protection advocates can overstate low-probability hazards when arguing for conservative controls.
- Program managers and commercial operators can understate contamination uncertainty when controls impose cost or schedule burdens.
- Astrobiology media rewards “life survives space” headlines that collapse survival under a protocol into successful panspermia.
- Critics of panspermia can overcorrect by treating exposed-cell lethality as decisive while ignoring shielding.
- Life-detection teams can become attached to a positive signal; contamination teams can become attached to a terrestrial explanation. Blinded and independent replication reduces both pressures.

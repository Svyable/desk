## Use boundaries

NASA and COSPAR policy documents establish requirements and rationale, not proof that a particular contamination event occurred. Survival experiments establish survival under specified protocols, not successful natural panspermia. Cleanroom isolates establish presence and measured resistance traits, not arrival on Mars. ALH84001 contamination papers weaken specific organic-biosignature claims; they do not establish that every feature in the meteorite is terrestrial or abiotic.

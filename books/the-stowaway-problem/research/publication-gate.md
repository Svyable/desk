# Publication gate

This run completes the 18-chapter argument but does **not** claim Desk's automated one-shot full-book gate. The current draft is materially below 3,000 words per chapter and below the 65,000-word chapter-only minimum. It is a coherent working manuscript, not a release candidate.

The expansion pass is frozen around substantive work rather than padding:

1. Reconstruct Viking's planetary-protection protocol from mission-era primary engineering records, including actual dry-heat parameters, microbial probability requirements, component exceptions and contamination accounting.
2. Build a mission-by-mission contamination ledger for Viking, Pathfinder, MER, Phoenix, Curiosity and Mars 2020, separating requirements from measured bioburden.
3. Reconstruct SAFR-032's full phenotype across UV, peroxide, desiccation and flight experiments with comparable dose units.
4. Deepen *Tersicoccus phoenicis* from original isolation through current dormancy/resuscitation work, explicitly testing what is cleanroom-specific versus sampling artifact.
5. Quantify dry heat, VHP, alcohol wiping, UV and modern molecular assays on common detection/kill endpoints.
6. Build a lithopanspermia transfer model with separate ejection, shock, transit, shielding, entry, impact and establishment terms rather than a binary possibility claim.
7. Add ejecta-physics papers that map survivable pressure-temperature volumes to Mars escape trajectories.
8. Normalize EXPOSE-E/EXPOSE-R survival experiments by organism, shielding thickness, UV fluence, duration and recovery method.
9. Add cosmic-ray dose and long-duration viability modeling to stop the manuscript from overlearning from 1–2 year orbital experiments.
10. Reconstruct the chain by which martian meteorites were recognized, including trapped atmospheric gases and exposure ages.
11. Rebuild the ALH84001 controversy from the 1996 paper through major abiotic/contamination responses rather than using two contamination papers as shorthand.
12. Add current NASA/ESA life-detection and contamination-knowledge requirements, including organic contamination rather than viable organisms alone.
13. Separate dead biomass, viable-but-nonculturable states, spores and culturable cells experimentally.
14. Page-index current COSPAR restricted-Earth-return requirements and distinguish stable policy from changing Mars Sample Return architecture.
15. Add human-Mars planetary-protection policy work without assuming a specific exploration timeline.
16. Test the cleanroom-selection thesis against longitudinal data and null cases where resistant isolates do not become enriched.
17. Design a preregistered “doppelgänger” provenance protocol for a hypothetical familiar extraterrestrial microbe.
18. Run `python3 scripts/check-book-length.py the-stowaway-problem`, `python3 scripts/check-book-cover-metadata.py the-stowaway-problem`, and applicable Desk checks after the manuscript actually meets scope; then perform a whole-book prose and anti-slop read.

Shared catalog/Reader/release surfaces should remain untouched until those objective and editorial gates pass.

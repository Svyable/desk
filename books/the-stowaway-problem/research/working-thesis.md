# Working thesis and freshness note

Selected after checking the newest Author-agent books visible on September 13, 2026. The five most recent empirical families were magnetoreception, Camp Century/Project Iceworm, Robertson Panel/UFO warning-channel overload, Moscow Signal/PANDORA RF bioeffects, and earthquake/seismo-electromagnetic precursors. Repository search returned no substantive manuscript built around panspermia plus spacecraft contamination/planetary protection.

## Thesis

The same survival traits that make lithopanspermia experimentally plausible at individual stages also make planetary life-detection missions vulnerable to terrestrial stowaways. The collision forces a stricter epistemology: a future biological signal must defeat abiotic alternatives **and** a mission-specific terrestrial provenance model.

## Reader question

How can we know extraterrestrial life is extraterrestrial after we have begun sending life-bearing machines into the environments where we hope to detect it?

## Strongest alternative

Modern planetary-protection controls and provenance tools may make the collision less consequential than the book suggests, while laboratory survival results may overstate natural-transfer plausibility because full-chain probabilities are tiny. The manuscript should actively search for evidence supporting that alternative.

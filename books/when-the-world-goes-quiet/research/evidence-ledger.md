# Evidence ledger — WHEN THE WORLD GOES QUIET

Working rule: an experience establishes an experience. Mechanism, external correspondence and ontology require additional measurements.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- **MKULTRA existed as a CIA behavioral-modification research program and was examined by Congress.** Primary anchor: U.S. Senate Select Committee on Intelligence, joint hearing, 3 August 1977. https://www.intelligence.senate.gov/1977/08/03/hearings-joint-hearing-subcommittee-health-and-scientific-research-committee-human-resources-project/
- **Sensory-deprivation research predates and overlaps the Cold War behavioral-control milieu.** Solomon et al. (1957) review experimental deprivation and report increased suggestibility, impaired organized thinking and, at extremes, hallucinations/delusions/confusion. https://doi.org/10.1176/ajp.114.4.357
- **Hallucinations occur under experimentally reduced sensory input.** Schulman, Richlin & Weinstein (1967), 134 subjects, up to 72 hours, reported multiple hallucination modalities in total deprivation. https://doi.org/10.2466/pms.1967.25.3.1001
- **Modern Ganzfeld can induce altered perceptual states and hallucinations.** Wackermann-line multimodal work includes decreased thalamo-cortical coupling under MMGF. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-75019-3
- **Ganzfeld and Ganzflicker produce measurably different hallucination profiles.** Shenyan et al. (2024): simple hallucinations more frequent in both; Ganzflicker produced more simple hallucinations while complex hallucination frequency was similar. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-024-52372-1
- **Sensory deprivation can increase psychotic-like experiences in nonclinical participants.** Daniel et al. (2015). https://doi.org/10.1155/2015/439379
- **Feeling-of-presence reports can be studied experimentally under sensory deprivation.** 2024 study, n=126, found uncertainty positively associated with feeling of presence; the experimental prime did not show a clear association. https://doi.org/10.1080/2153599X.2024.2305460
- **Noise content can influence Ganzfeld hallucination content.** Pistolas, Smets & Wagemans (2025) found frequent visual hallucinations and water-related auditory/integrated experiences, with noise characteristics a candidate influence. https://doi.org/10.1177/20416695251376600

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- **Ganzfeld anomalous-perception literature reports a small positive meta-analytic effect.** Tressoldi & Storm registered-report meta-analysis covering 1974–2020 reports an effect around .08. This is evidence about an aggregated literature, not proof of telepathy; audit study independence, target/judging procedures, publication selection, lab effects and prospective replication before manuscript claims. https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.51746.4
- **Cold War intelligence/interrogation practice incorporated environmental manipulation, isolation and disruption.** Primary-source reconstruction should distinguish CIA research programs, KUBARK doctrine, later training manuals, contractor research and foreign programs rather than treating `MKULTRA` as a bucket for all of them.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Durable, reliable `mind control` as a demonstrated CIA capability.
- Claims that sensory deprivation itself produces truthful intelligence rather than disorientation, dependency or confabulation.
- Telepathy/clairvoyance as the established mechanism behind the Ganzfeld meta-analytic literature.
- External-agent interpretations of sensed presences or deprivation hallucinations.
- Claims that specific mystical, UAP, entity or paranormal reports can be identified as deprivation effects merely because a plausible perceptual mechanism exists.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- **Correction pressure:** the quantity, independence and temporal rate of structured external evidence available to contradict an internally generated perceptual model. Proposed variable, not established neuroscientific metric.
- **Ontological promotion:** moving from `experience occurred` → `mechanism identified` → `external referent established` → `ontology established` without independent evidence at each transition.
- **Experience / information split:** altered states may increase perceptual content without increasing recoverable information about hidden external targets. This is experimentally tractable.
- **The decisive deprivation test:** parametrically vary sensory structure while prospectively testing hidden external correspondence, allowing internal-generation and anomalous-information hypotheses to make separable predictions.

## Contradictions and incentives to keep live

1. Intelligence interest can indicate strategic concern without scientific validation. Agencies fund failures and adversary-response programs.
2. Sensory deprivation demonstrably perturbs experience, but this does not establish that all extraordinary experiences under low-input conditions are internally generated.
3. A positive Ganzfeld meta-analysis cannot by itself identify mechanism; conversely, known hallucination production does not explain target correspondence if leakage-resistant correspondence independently survives.
4. Historical coercive research has severe ethical problems that affect sample selection, reporting and institutional incentives. Do not turn abuse into scientific authority.
5. `Sensory deprivation` and `perceptual deprivation/homogenization` are not synonyms. Ganzfeld supplies stimulation with low structure; classic isolation paradigms vary absolute input, patterned input and social contact.

## Publication holds

- Do not attribute a specific sensory-deprivation experiment to MKULTRA without subproject-level primary documentation.
- Do not write that KUBARK `came from MKULTRA` without a documentary chain.
- Do not claim Ewen Cameron demonstrated successful psychic driving or mind control; reconstruct grants, protocols, outcomes and later investigations separately.
- Do not call the Ganzfeld psi effect `replicated telepathy`; report the statistical literature and its methodological dispute.
- Do not use patents, intelligence files or program funding as evidence of efficacy.

## Strongest countermodel

Nothing paranormal is required. Under low-structure input, ordinary predictive/perceptual systems amplify endogenous activity; anxiety, expectation, noise texture, individual hallucination proneness, memory and flexible retrospective matching account for extraordinary experiences. Apparent anomalous-information effects shrink under stronger prospective controls and independent replication.

## Defeat condition

That countermodel loses sufficiency if preregistered, independently replicated, leakage-resistant experiments show that low-input states increase specific information about concealed external targets above chance with fixed scoring, while experimenter, target-pool, judging and optional-stopping effects are controlled. Conversely, the book's `correction pressure` synthesis loses value if sensory-structure manipulations do not predict perceptual instability better than existing variables or if the construct cannot be operationalized without becoming a post-hoc label.

# Defeat Conditions

The book's central synthesis should be weakened or abandoned if primary records show that:

1. Severe final-day warning was not communicated to decision-makers with closure authority.
2. Management actually ordered prompt full evacuation once serious structural risk became credible and the order failed for unrelated reasons.
3. The most important pre-collapse warning chronology in later Korean histories is substantially contradicted by contemporaneous records.
4. Structural investigation establishes a truly unforeseeable instantaneous mechanism unrelated to accumulated design/construction/management defects.
5. Regulatory corruption had no material relationship to approvals, inspection, use change or public-safety oversight at Sampoong.
6. The proposed `information routing` interpretation explains less of the record than a simpler account based solely on technical ignorance.

Evidence that weakens an attractive thesis belongs in the manuscript, not in a drawer.

# Chronology

- 1987–1989 — construction and substantial design/use evolution.
- Late 1989 / 1990 — completion/opening chronology varies by source and requires record-level reconciliation.
- Early 1990s — further operational and mechanical changes, including rooftop-equipment history.
- 1994-10-21 — Seongsu Bridge collapse, 32 dead.
- 1995-01 — Facilities Safety Act enacted after Seongsu; this predates Sampoong and must not be falsely attributed to it.
- 1995-06-29 morning — severe distress reported in upper-floor areas; inspections and management discussions occur.
- 1995-06-29 late afternoon — collapse begins, reported around 17:52–17:57 depending on source.
- 1995-06-30 onward — mass rescue, identification and investigation.
- 1995-07-09 — late survivor Choi Myung-seok recovered according to contemporary Yonhap chronology.
- 1995-07-11 — Yoo Ji-hwan recovered according to contemporary reporting; later survivor chronology continues.
- 1995-07 to 08 — missing-person numbers repeatedly reconciled.
- 1995-08-30 — first major criminal trial proceedings begin for 25 defendants.
- 1996 — appellate/final criminal resolution requires judgment-by-judgment reconstruction.
- 1998-06-29 — Sampoong memorial tower established at Yangjae Citizens' Forest according to later Korean safety histories.

# Back Matter

## A note on language

Korean names, agency names and historical terms should be checked against original Korean records during the expansion pass. This draft preserves Korean-source URLs and avoids manufacturing translated quotations where exact wording has not been recovered.

## Method

The book separates four evidence classes: documented/verified; credible but incomplete; disputed/not established; and speculative synthesis. Government sponsorship, prosecution, archival survival, press repetition and later commemoration are not treated as proof of a technical claim by themselves.

## Why this book exists

English-language Sampoong accounts often preserve the structural outline while losing the Korean administrative afterlife: municipal situation reports, missing-person reconciliation, workers' compensation decisions, parliamentary inquiry records, prosecutorial reconstruction and the long Korean argument over `safety society`. Those sources turn a famous collapse into a study of how institutions know danger.

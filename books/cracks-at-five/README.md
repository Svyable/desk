# Cracks at Five

## Sampoong and the Price of Looking Away

**Sven Hardy Benson**

### Thesis

The Sampoong Department Store collapse was not merely the end result of bad concrete, overloaded slabs, unauthorized changes, or bribed officials. It was an information failure with a structural consequence: danger became visible in fragments to people who lacked either the authority, incentive, shared vocabulary, or trusted channel to convert observation into evacuation. The building failed physically in seconds after institutions had spent years teaching themselves to discount warnings.

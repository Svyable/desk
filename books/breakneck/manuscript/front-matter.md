# Breakneck

*When AI Moves Faster Than Human Comprehension*

**Sven Hardy Benson**

There is a familiar way to worry about artificial intelligence. The machines may become too capable. They may take jobs, make mistakes at scale, manipulate people, concentrate power, or pursue goals we did not intend.

This book is about a quieter problem that arrives before most of those questions are settled.

The machines may simply move too fast for us to understand what we have put into motion.

Not too fast for the processor. Too fast for the meeting. Too fast for the review. Too fast for the engineer who needs to reconstruct why an agent changed seven files instead of three. Too fast for a manager to discover that ten small automated decisions have become one large policy. Too fast for a regulator to learn what the system is doing before the system changes again. Too fast, eventually, for the people carrying responsibility to hold an independent model of the thing they supposedly control.

The result is not ignorance in the ordinary sense. We may know more than ever. We may have logs, traces, dashboards, summaries, evaluations, transcripts, alerts, and machine-written explanations of machine-made decisions. The problem is whether understanding can still keep pace with action.

That is the problem of *Breakneck*.

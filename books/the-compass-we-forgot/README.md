## Thesis

Animal magnetoreception is real while the human case remains unresolved: a 2019 EEG result suggests that some human brains respond to Earth-strength magnetic-field rotations without conscious awareness, but behavioral navigation, receptor identity, and mechanism remain separate claims. The book asks whether humans retain a vestigial magnetic input—and designs the experiment capable of killing that idea.

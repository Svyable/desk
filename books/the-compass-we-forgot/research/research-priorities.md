# Research Priorities and Defeat Conditions

## Immediate priorities

1. **Independent replication audit.** Search exhaustively through September 2026 for exact or near-exact independent replications of Wang et al. 2019, including unpublished registered reports where discoverable. Separate same-group follow-up from independent laboratories.
2. **Reanalyze the 2019 open data.** Reproduce alpha-ERD from raw EEG using the published code, then rerun with preregisterable alternative preprocessing to estimate robustness to filtering, artifact rejection, electrode selection and responder classification.
3. **Recover Robin Baker primary records.** Build a trial-level table for chair, bus and walkabout experiments plus replication attempts and contamination critiques.
4. **Build a human magnetic-material atlas.** Separate endogenous-appearing crystals from pollution particles by tissue, morphology, composition, size and preparation method.
5. **Map human mechanism fingerprints.** Tabulate predictions of magnetite, radical-pair and induction models for polarity, axis, intensity, gradient, light, RF perturbation and movement.
6. **Southern Hemisphere protocol.** Pre-register a mirrored Northern/Southern Hemisphere study with hardware swaps and geomagnetic-life-history metadata.
7. **Responder stability.** Determine whether neural responders remain responders prospectively over repeated sessions.
8. **Behavior bridge.** Test whether preregistered neural response strength predicts any held-out behavioral discrimination without selecting tasks post hoc.

## Decisive experiment

A multicenter protocol should reproduce the 2019 field trajectories and shams while instrumenting microphones, vibration, temperature, broadband RF and independent field probes. The primary endpoint is alpha-ERD in a fixed time-frequency window. Secondary mechanism conditions alter vertical polarity, illumination spectrum, weak RF perturbation and head-versus-field rotation. Cohorts include participants raised predominantly in opposite geomagnetic hemispheres.

Analysis must be frozen before condition labels are revealed. Site-specific results must be published even if pooled analysis is null.

## Defeat conditions

The book’s central live hypothesis—some humans retain a genuine neural geomagnetic transduction pathway—should be substantially downgraded if:

- multiple adequately powered independent sites fail to reproduce the preregistered alpha-ERD despite verified field delivery and low side-channel contamination;
- apparent effects follow particular coil-current sequences or hardware configurations rather than magnetic geometry at the participant;
- responder status is not stable prospectively and emerges mainly through retrospective subgrouping;
- purported magnetic effects disappear when acoustic, vibrational or electrical artifacts are matched;
- mechanism-specific perturbations produce no coherent pattern and the response behaves like nonspecific arousal;
- a reanalysis of the original data reveals decisive dependence on flexible preprocessing or selection.

## What would strengthen the thesis

- clean independent replication across laboratories;
- effect transfer with magnetic geometry rather than apparatus;
- stable within-person response phenotype;
- hemisphere/developmental effects predicted in advance;
- a molecular or cellular correlate that co-segregates with response;
- perturbation of the candidate receptor selectively abolishing the neural effect;
- a weak but replicable behavioral consequence predicted by neural response.

The project should prefer a smaller true claim to a larger resilient story.

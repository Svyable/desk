# Decisive Experiment Program

## Compass arm

Preregister a CRY4 selective-manipulation and rescue experiment in a migratory bird. Choose a mutation from prior spin-chemistry work that strongly reduces magnetic sensitivity while preserving folding, flavin handling and gross photochemistry. Verify the molecular phenotype first.

Manipulate native retinal CRY4 in the appropriate photoreceptor population. Compare sham, loss-of-function, wild-type rescue and magnetically impaired rescue groups. Verify ordinary retinal physiology, vision, circadian function and migratory restlessness.

Blind all behavioral scoring and randomize geomagnetic rotation, inclination inversion, sham RF and validated broadband RF conditions. Publish ambient electromagnetic spectra, coil harmonics, illumination and exclusion criteria.

In intact retinal tissue, seek an Earth-strength field-angle-dependent cellular signal. Require wild-type rescue to restore both cellular signal and compass behavior; require the magnetically impaired mutant to fail selectively.

**Strong defeat:** compass behavior and a cellular magnetic response remain intact when the predicted CRY4 magnetic pathway is selectively abolished.

## Map arm

Independently replicate the 2025 turtle conditioning/RF dissociation. Map sensitivity to localized static magnetic perturbations across head and body. Combine with trigeminal recordings during systematic intensity/gradient changes.

Identify a peripheral structure only after showing innervation and field-dependent physiology. Characterize magnetic material with contamination controls. Selectively disrupt the candidate structure while preserving ordinary trigeminal sensation.

**Strong defeat:** learned map behavior remains intact after selective candidate-receptor disruption, or robust replications show the map is RF-sensitive in the same mechanism-specific way as the compass.

## Crossover prediction

- CRY4-pathway manipulation: compass impaired; learned map preserved.
- Candidate particle-map manipulation: map impaired; compass preserved.
- RF perturbation: compass response surface altered according to radical-pair predictions; map comparatively resistant.
- Static localized magnetic distortion at map receptor: map altered; RF-like compass signature absent.

Precommit these outcomes before data collection. A plural-system theory cannot be permitted to add a new mechanism after every contradiction.

## Ecological arm

Run calibrated temporary RF exposures at natural migration sites with individual tracking, sham exposure, spectral measurement, weather/celestial-cue covariates and post-exposure recovery. The target is trajectory cost, not cage orientation.

**Strong boundary:** repeated null route-level effects under exposure spectra that reliably disrupt the same individuals in laboratory compass assays would confine RF significance largely to controlled sensory physiology rather than conservation impact.

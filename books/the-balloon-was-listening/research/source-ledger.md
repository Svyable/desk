# Source and evidence ledger

## Working thesis

Project MOGUL is a documented case in which a physically mundane platform carried a genuinely sensitive intelligence purpose. That collision offers a disciplined way to study Roswell without converting either official explanation or later testimony into proof. The book's narrower original contribution is the **secrecy-afterimage** hypothesis: when the classified purpose of an ordinary object becomes public decades after recovery, the real secrecy can make later extraordinary narratives harder to falsify because it supplies verified concealment without supplying evidence for every concealed thing alleged afterward.

## Evidence matrix

### Class 1 — verified/declassified/documented

- Project MOGUL existed as a classified U.S. effort connected to long-range detection of nuclear explosions.
- The Army Air Corps contracted with New York University for constant-level balloon work beginning in the postwar period.
- MOGUL used balloon-borne instrumentation, telemetry and radar-reflector/recovery architecture.
- The 1995 GAO inquiry searched multiple agencies for Roswell records and reported that available official material pointed to a MOGUL balloon train as the most likely source of recovered wreckage.
- The Air Force published *The Roswell Report: Fact versus Fiction in the New Mexico Desert* and later *Case Closed*.
- The Air Force's own account describes compartmentation between NYU balloon research and classified military purpose/data.
- Project Blue Book later logged 12,618 sightings, 701 categorized as unidentified; that number is evidence of unresolved reports, not evidence of non-human technology.

### Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Retrospective recollections of NYU and military personnel about specific launches, cancellations, service flights and material construction.
- Contemporary witness descriptions where original statements exist but exact debris provenance cannot now be independently reconstructed.
- Claims about which particular MOGUL train generated the Foster Ranch debris where flight numbering and launch/cancellation interpretation remain contested.

### Class 3 — disputed allegations

- Recovery of a non-human craft or occupants near Roswell in 1947.
- Extraordinary material properties attributed to debris without an authenticated surviving sample and chain of custody.
- Threats or intimidation attributed to officials where testimony is retrospective and independent documentation is absent.
- Later body accounts treated as observations from July 1947 despite chronology disputes.

### Class 4 — speculative synthesis/open conjecture

- Secrecy-afterimage hypothesis: genuine compartmentation can increase the later persuasive power of unsupported additions because the audience eventually learns that officials really were hiding something.
- Roswell as a general model for distinguishing *proof of concealment* from *proof of the concealed proposition*.
- The possibility that modern UAP narratives sometimes braid genuine classified aerospace activity with unrelated unresolved observations; this is a comparison to test, not a conclusion.

## Contradiction ledger

- **Flight identity:** the official MOGUL explanation is strongest at project/material/purpose level; exact assignment of Foster Ranch debris to a particular numbered or unnumbered train requires reconstruction from launch logs, weather, diaries and later recollection. Do not compress this into certainty.
- **Bodies:** the 1997 Air Force report explains later body stories through anthropomorphic dummy drops and separate accidents occurring years later. This is an official explanatory hypothesis supported by documented later activities, but the chronological mismatch is itself part of the claim and must be tested witness by witness rather than stated as universal fact.
- **Missing records:** GAO documented destruction of some Roswell Army Air Field administrative/outgoing-message records without identifying who destroyed them or under what authority. Missing records are relevant; they do not identify their own contents.
- **Press release:** the famous 'flying disc' announcement and rapid retraction are documented historical facts. They establish institutional confusion or communication failure at minimum; by themselves they establish no vehicle origin.

## Incentive audit

The Air Force had an institutional incentive in the 1990s to resolve a reputationally costly controversy. UFO advocates and commercial authors had incentives toward narrative novelty and unresolved mystery. Witnesses faced memory contamination over decades. Journalists faced asymmetric rewards for extraordinary claims. None of these incentives decides the evidence; each changes the weight assigned to unsupported inference.

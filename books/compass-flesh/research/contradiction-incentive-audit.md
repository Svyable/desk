# Contradiction and Incentive Audit

## Contradictions to preserve

- Human CRY2 can participate in magnetic sensing in flies, yet the 2019 human EEG result is polarity-sensitive in a way the authors argue against a standard radical-pair compass.
- Human brain magnetite is real, yet a substantial fraction of measured particles can be pollution-derived rather than biologically organized.
- The 2019 EEG response is experimentally specific, yet conscious magnetic perception has not been established.
- Animal magnetoreception is well supported, yet receptor anatomy remains contested in important taxa.
- A neural response can be real without being useful for navigation.

## Incentives that can distort interpretation

- Novel-sense framing rewards overstatement.
- “Quantum biology” framing can attract attention beyond what the mechanism supports.
- Defense funding invites secrecy narratives unsupported by program evidence.
- Electromagnetic-health controversies create pressure to recruit magnetoreception as retrospective validation.
- Skeptical reaction to weak historical behavioral studies can make strong newer experiments harder to evaluate on their own merits.
- Researchers invested in magnetite or radical-pair mechanisms may interpret ambiguous findings through mechanism-specific priors.

# Front Matter

Human beings are excellent at inventing senses after the fact. We call intuition a sixth sense, direction a sense, danger a sense, timing a sense. Magnetoreception is different because the physical variable is measurable, other animals undeniably use it, and the human evidence sits in an uncomfortable middle ground: too concrete to dismiss with a joke, too incomplete to call a discovered sense.

This book follows that middle ground.

It does not assume people can navigate magnetically. It does not treat magnetic-field symptoms as established clinical effects. It does not use quantum mechanics as permission to stop specifying mechanisms. It does not treat defense funding as proof of secret capability.

The standard throughout is simple: separate the component from the system, the response from the sense, and the sense from the story.

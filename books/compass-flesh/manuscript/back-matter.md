# Back Matter

## Evidence rule

Claims in this manuscript are separated into four classes:

1. verified, documented, or directly replicated findings;
2. credible but incomplete findings or interpretations;
3. disputed claims lacking independent confirmation;
4. speculative synthesis and proposed experiments.

The book is designed to become less interesting if the evidence requires it. A hidden sense that cannot survive replication is not improved by metaphor.

## Author

Sven Hardy Benson writes about systems that become visible only when evidence from different disciplines is forced to share a table.

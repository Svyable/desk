## Thesis

Human magnetoreception is worth taking seriously precisely because the strongest evidence is narrower than the folklore: animal magnetic sensing is established, humans possess plausible molecular and mineral components, and a controlled 2019 EEG study reported polarity- and orientation-specific responses to Earth-strength field rotations, but receptor identity, independent replication, conscious perception, and behavioral use remain unresolved. The book treats that gap as the subject rather than filling it with romance.

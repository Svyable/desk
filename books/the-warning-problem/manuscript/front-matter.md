# The Warning Problem

*Nuclear Deterrence in the Age of AI*

**Sven Hardy Benson**

---

First field edition candidate, September 2026.

This book is about the systems around nuclear decisions: sensors, warning, communications, decision support, cyber resilience, doctrine, and people. It does not assume that nuclear-armed states have delegated launch decisions to artificial intelligence, and it does not argue that such delegation is a necessary consequence of technological progress.

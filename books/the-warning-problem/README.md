# The Warning Problem

*Nuclear Deterrence in the Age of AI*

The central thesis is that artificial intelligence can improve the ability of nuclear powers to detect, fuse, and interpret warning data while simultaneously making the most dangerous decision environment on earth faster, more complex, and more vulnerable to false confidence.

The book is about warning, not robot launch authority. It examines early-warning systems, nuclear command and control, cyber risk, automation bias, conventional-nuclear entanglement, compressed decision time, authentication, and the institutional design required to preserve meaningful human judgment under pressure.

This Desk edition completes the 18-chapter argument as a first full manuscript. Claims about deployment remain deliberately narrower than claims about research, modernization, and decision-support possibilities.

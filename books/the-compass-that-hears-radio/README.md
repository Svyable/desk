# THE COMPASS THAT HEARS RADIO

## How a Bird Turned Quantum Mechanics into a Sense

Sven Hardy Benson

**Working thesis:** Avian magnetoreception is a rare frontier-science case where an apparently absurd weak-field biological claim has acquired several independent constraints—behavioral orientation, light dependence, radiofrequency disruption, protein photochemistry, and spin-chemistry predictions—without yet yielding a complete receptor-to-brain mechanism. The book asks what evidence should count when a biological sensor operates in an energy regime that looks too small to matter by naive thermal reasoning.

Target: 65,000–80,000 words. Current state: opening architecture and Chapter 1 draft.

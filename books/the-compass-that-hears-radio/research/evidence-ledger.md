# Evidence ledger — THE COMPASS THAT HEARS RADIO

## Four evidence classes

### 1. Verified / documented / experimentally demonstrated
- European robin CRY4 displays magnetic sensitivity in vitro; Xu et al., *Nature* 594, 535–540 (2021), DOI 10.1038/s41586-021-03618-9. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03618-9
- Weak broadband 75–85 MHz RF fields disrupted magnetic compass orientation in night-migratory songbirds in the reported behavioral experiments; Leberecht et al., *Journal of Comparative Physiology A* (2022), PMID 35019998. https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35019998/
- Radical-pair chemistry can exhibit magnetic-field effects in the Earth-field regime; Kattnig et al., *Nature Chemistry* 8, 384–391 (2016). https://www.nature.com/articles/nchem.2447
- Denton et al. modeled Earth-strength magnetosensitivity in tightly bound cryptochrome-inspired radical pairs under asymmetric recombination / quantum-Zeno conditions; *Nature Communications* 15, 10823 (2024). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-55124-x

### 2. Credible but incomplete
- CRY4 or related retinal cryptochromes are components of the living avian compass.
- RF disruption spectra constrain the operative radical identity and kinetics in vivo.
- The radical-pair output is converted into a spatial retinal signal usable by the brain.
- Anthropogenic electromagnetic noise can materially affect wild navigation under ecologically relevant exposures; requires field-scale denominator and replication audit.

### 3. Disputed / unconfirmed
- Exact radical pair responsible for the avian compass.
- Exact receptor complex, retinal localization, transduction partner and neural code.
- A single universal magnetic-sensing mechanism across birds or animals.
- Strong claims that ordinary environmental RF exposure broadly disrupts migration at population scale.
- Any inference from avian magnetoreception to human mind control, telepathy, arbitrary weak-field bioeffects or covert remote neural manipulation.

### 4. Speculative synthesis / open conjecture
- **Energy fallacy:** rejecting a nonequilibrium biological detector because the external field energy is small relative to kBT, without modeling the reaction pathway that conditions probability on the field.
- **Interference fingerprint:** deliberately applied perturbations can function as spectroscopy for a sensor whose receptor cannot yet be directly observed in vivo.
- **Mechanism ladder:** behavior → perturbation → localization → molecule → transduction → amplification → neural code → ecology.
- **Sensitivity is architecture:** weak-signal sensitivity does not transfer generically between organisms, tissues, frequencies or endpoints.

## Contradictions / live problems
- CRY4 in-vitro sensitivity is not equivalent to receptor necessity in vivo.
- Candidate FAD/Trp radical-pair photochemistry faces biological observations under wavelengths/conditions that complicate a simple photoactivation story.
- RF behavioral effects have a history of technical sensitivity to background noise, shielding and protocol.
- Superoxide-based radical-pair models remain theoretically interesting but lack direct experimental evidence at geomagnetic field strength in the proposed biological configuration.
- Multiple magnetic senses may coexist, making a single-receptor narrative falsely clean.

## Publication holds
Do not publish quantitative RF thresholds without extracting field amplitude, bandwidth, modulation/noise spectrum, coil geometry, static field, shielding/background, light spectrum, exposure duration, species, season, sample size, orientation statistic and replication status. Do not call CRY4 `the receptor` absent in-vivo causal closure. Do not use `quantum biology` as evidence for consciousness claims.

## Strongest countermodel
The behavioral magnetic compass is real but the currently fashionable cryptochrome radical-pair story may be partly wrong: RF effects may be protocol-sensitive or mediated by a different molecular system; CRY4 magnetic sensitivity may be an in-vitro property without decisive in-vivo relevance; multiple receptors may contribute. The book survives only if it is about how a mechanism earns itself rung by rung, not about defending CRY4.

## Defeat test
A preregistered multisite program should reproduce orientation and RF disruption with identical calibrated hardware; independently vary static-field direction, RF spectrum, light spectrum and cryptochrome perturbation; blind analysis; publish all trials; and require the molecular model to predict at least one new behavioral perturbation before data collection. Persistent behavioral effects that systematically violate radical-pair predictions would force a mechanism change.

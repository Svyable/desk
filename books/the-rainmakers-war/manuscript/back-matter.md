# Back Matter

## A note on language

This book uses **weather modification** for deliberate attempts to alter atmospheric processes; **cloud seeding** for the narrower practice of introducing particles intended to influence cloud microphysics; **weather warfare** for hostile use of environmental or weather-modification techniques; and **solar geoengineering / solar radiation modification** for proposed interventions intended to alter Earth's radiative balance. These categories overlap politically but should not be treated as technically interchangeable.

## What the book does not claim

It does not claim Popeye created the 1971 North Vietnamese floods.

It does not claim cloud seeding can create hurricanes, steer hurricanes, generate rain from clear sky, or control regional climate on command.

It does not claim modern cloud-seeding effectiveness estimates transfer directly to 1960s Southeast Asia.

It does not claim ENMOD bans all cloud seeding or all geoengineering research.

It does not treat patents as evidence that a technology works.

It does not infer a current clandestine program from the historical existence of Popeye.

## Research proposition

The most useful future historical work would combine declassified mission records, sortie logs, surviving seeding-unit records, historical weather observations, modern atmospheric reanalysis, route-condition records, and North Vietnamese logistical archives. The goal should not be to prove a predetermined success or failure. It should be to estimate an effect distribution with explicit uncertainty.

That would turn one of the strangest classified technologies of the Vietnam War back into what it always was:

an experiment.

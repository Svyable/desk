## Source-use discipline

The FRUS memorandum establishes proposal, intent, secrecy, test-phase description, and internal assessment. It does not independently validate claimed rainfall increment.

The 1974 hearings establish official acknowledgment and contemporaneous criticism of the evidentiary basis. They do not prove zero effect.

Modern seeding literature establishes conditional physical efficacy under some present-day experimental settings. It is not retroactive measurement of Popeye.

ENMOD text establishes legal obligations for states parties. It is not evidence that prohibited techniques were technically mature.

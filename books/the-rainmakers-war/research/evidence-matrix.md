# Evidentiary Matrix

| Claim | Class | Best support | Boundary / defeat condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| The U.S. proposed and conducted military weather modification in Southeast Asia during the Vietnam War. | 1 — verified/documented | FRUS 1967; 1974 Senate hearings | Defeated only by authenticity/provenance failure in official records; none identified. |
| The stated objective was to increase/extend rainfall to impair infiltration routes and truck traffic. | 1 | FRUS 1967 | Does not establish achieved effect. |
| The 1966 test phase included more than 50 seedings and was internally judged highly successful. | 1 for the internal claim; 2 for physical interpretation | FRUS 1967 | Internal assessment is evidence of belief, not independent causal validation. |
| A large share of seeded clouds rained shortly after treatment. | 2 — credible but incomplete | FRUS summary of test phase | Requires matched unseeded baseline, selection protocol, and underlying logs for causal estimate. |
| Popeye materially increased target-area rainfall. | 2/3 boundary | Defense claims; program logic | Moves to Class 1 only with recoverable counterfactual design or strong reanalysis using underlying data. |
| Popeye materially reduced adversary logistics. | 3 — disputed/not independently established | Tactical rationale and official claims | Requires route/throughput evidence separating weather effect from monsoon, bombing, maintenance and adaptation. |
| Popeye caused the 1971 North Vietnamese floods. | 3 | Temporal coexistence; political allegations | Would require quantitative rainfall attribution plus basin hydrology. Not presently established. |
| Cloud seeding can enhance precipitation under suitable conditions. | 1 in bounded modern settings | Friedrich et al. 2020; modern radar/gauge observations | Does not imply universal or large seasonal effect. |
| Program-scale yield can be small even when some seeding events work. | 1/2 | Geerts et al. 2023; GAO 2024 | Transfer to other regions requires care. |
| STORMFURY initially misread natural hurricane behavior as likely seeding response. | 1 | NOAA/AOML; Willoughby et al. 1985 | Relevant as methodological control case, not direct evidence about Popeye. |
| ENMOD bans all weather modification. | 3 — false/overbroad | Treaty text | Treaty targets military/hostile use meeting specified effects criteria; peaceful uses remain outside core prohibition. |
| Historical Popeye proves current secret weather-control programs. | 4 if asserted as inference | Historical analogy only | Requires independent contemporary evidence. History establishes plausibility of attempts, not present existence. |
| Secrecy can preserve program existence while degrading later efficacy auditability. | 4 — synthesis | Popeye disclosure pattern + missing causal dataset | Weakened if complete high-quality operational records emerge demonstrating robust contemporaneous causal evaluation. |

## Publication refresh checklist

Before any released edition, refresh Debt to the Penny, the latest quarterly borrowing estimate, the latest refunding statement, CBO's current baseline, FOMC implementation note, SEC clearing timetable, TIC flows/holdings, Social Security Trustees dates, and any hedge-fund/Treasury-market data. Recalculate every dollar amount tied to “current,” “today,” “now,” or “this year.” Preserve the gross-versus-net distinction even if the headline refinancing number moves materially away from $10 trillion.

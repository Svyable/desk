# Group Psychosis Therapy

## How to Think Like a Wolf in a World of Sheep

**Sven Hardy Benson**

*Drafting edition · 2026*

A note on the title: "group psychosis" is used in this book as a provocative metaphor for failures of collective reality-testing. It is not a psychiatric diagnosis of a population, movement, community, or political group. Clinical psychosis is a serious medical phenomenon and should not be confused with conformity, misinformation, polarization, crowd behavior, or ordinary disagreement.

A second note matters just as much. The wolf in these pages is not automatically right, and the sheep are not automatically wrong. Crowds can be extraordinarily intelligent. Individuals can be spectacularly deluded. The subject is not superiority. It is independence: how to remain in contact with evidence when belonging, fear, status, repetition, authority, or the visible behavior of other people begins to price independent thought out of the room.

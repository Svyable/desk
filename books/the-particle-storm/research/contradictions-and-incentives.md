# Contradictions and Incentives

## Mechanism conflicts

**Leader-first interpretation:** TGFs often occur during upward leader propagation and close to major current/optical pulses. Local leader fields can provide strong acceleration and seed electrons.

**Feedback-first or feedback-continuum interpretation:** ALOFT flickering events can emit hard gamma rays without simultaneous conventional leader signatures, and some ASIM timing shows gamma emission clearly preceding the associated optical/current pulse. Relativistic-feedback simulations reproduce dynamic glows and pulse trains.

These are not necessarily mutually exclusive. Different event classes, polarities, charge structures or field regimes may support different mechanisms. The manuscript should resist converting a mixed ecology into a winner-take-all controversy.

## Rate conflicts

Satellite-derived TGF occurrence rates are threshold-limited. Aircraft detect much weaker events. A statement about `events per lightning` is therefore detector-defined unless corrected for sensitivity and source geometry.

## Radiation-risk incentives

Aviation safety analysis has an incentive to identify credible worst cases; public communication has an incentive to dramatize `gamma rays` and `antimatter`; airlines have an incentive to avoid claims unsupported by operational exposure data. None of those incentives settles actual dose. Require measured fluence × geometry × encounter probability.

## Research-program incentives

New event classes and unifying mechanisms are publication-attractive. That does not invalidate them, but the burden should be prospective prediction on held-out events rather than post-hoc relabeling.

## Language traps

`Antimatter` can mean individual positrons, not bulk antimatter.

`Nuclear reaction` does not mean nuclear explosion.

`Thunderstorms are particle accelerators` is literally defensible but does not imply engineered accelerator control or equivalent beam properties.

`Gamma rays precede lightning` must specify which lightning observable: leader initiation, radio source, current surge, optical pulse, or return stroke.

## Disinformation / secrecy posture

Unlike several Desk subjects, this field is not meaningfully dependent on classified claims. Military or intelligence secrecy is not required to explain the evidence. Public space-agency, university and peer-reviewed records are sufficient. Do not import secrecy narratives where ordinary instrumentation and disciplinary separation explain the historical blind spot.

## Source posture

Patents are unnecessary to this thesis. No classified material is claimed or required. NASA and mission pages establish instrument/program context; peer-reviewed papers carry physical claims. Risk extrapolations are not treated as measurements. The ALOFT raw-data availability statement should be followed in the publication pass; the 2024 *Nature* paper reports data deposited at Zenodo under DOI 10.5281/zenodo.12531291.

# Evidence Ledger

## Class I — verified / documented

- BATSE discovered terrestrial gamma-ray flashes associated with thunderstorms; later missions RHESSI, AGILE, Fermi and ASIM independently observed the class.
- TGF spectra extend to tens of MeV; AGILE reported a high-energy component reaching roughly 100 MeV.
- Fermi detected electron/positron beams associated with TGFs and 511-keV annihilation signatures on the spacecraft.
- Gamma-ray glows are repeatedly observed from ground, balloon and aircraft platforms.
- Enoto et al. 2017 observed a submillisecond gamma flash, neutron-related de-excitation afterglow, and delayed 511-keV emission consistent with positrons from radioactive nitrogen-13 following photonuclear reactions.
- ASIM provides simultaneous high-energy and optical lightning measurements with microsecond-scale relative timing after calibration improvements.
- ALOFT 2023 found highly dynamic gamma-ray emission common in sampled tropical thunderclouds and identified weak/intermediate event populations invisible to typical orbital triggering.
- Sarria et al. 2025 measured hard spectra for 24 flickering gamma-ray flashes consistent with developed runaway avalanches under varying attenuation.
- Jensen et al. 2026 reported a ground TGF about 35 microseconds after onset of an intense negative return stroke near Los Alamos.

## Class II — credible but incomplete

- Relativistic runaway electron avalanches explain the broad spectral character of much thunderstorm high-energy radiation.
- Positron/backscattered-photon feedback may sustain or destabilize large-volume relativistic discharge.
- Gamma-ray glows, flickering flashes and at least some TGFs may form a mechanistic continuum rather than three independent phenomena.
- High-energy discharge may materially influence conductivity, charge transport and local field evolution.
- Satellite catalogs strongly undercount weak gamma-ray activity because of attenuation, geometry and trigger thresholds.

## Class III — disputed / unconfirmed

- One universal mechanism for all TGFs.
- The claim that relativistic feedback, rather than leader-driven acceleration, dominates every bright event.
- The claim that high-energy discharge generally initiates ordinary lightning.
- Large routine aviation doses from thunderstorm radiation outside intentional storm penetration.
- A globally important contribution of lightning photonuclear reactions to atmospheric carbon-14.

## Class IV — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- Some thunderstorms may regulate their electric field through a high-energy feedback discharge analogous to a self-organized particle accelerator.
- The visible lightning pulse may, in a subset of events, be downstream of an already active relativistic discharge.
- A unified source-distribution framework may replace the glow/FGF/TGF taxonomy once threshold effects are removed.
- Planetary gamma-ray atmospheric electricity may become a remote probe of otherwise hidden storm electrodynamics and chemistry.

# Document Appendix

## Core public documents and papers

### Collins et al. 1999
C. B. Collins and collaborators, *Physical Review Letters* 82, 695. Primary positive claim. Use for exact apparatus, spectra, enhancement claims and inferred cross section. Do not paraphrase weapon implications into the paper unless the paper itself states them.

### Ahmad et al. 2001
I. Ahmad and collaborators, *Physical Review Letters* 87, 072503. Primary independent high-intensity synchrotron null. Use for beamline conditions, sensitivity and upper limits.

### APS follow-up activity reports
Argonne Advanced Photon Source reports describing expanded low-energy scans. Use to map what photon energies were tested and how far below the claimed values the limits fell.

### Becker et al. 2003, *The (178m2)Hf Controversy*
Government-laboratory contemporary synthesis. Valuable for chronology and citations, but still an interested scientific position inside the dispute. Do not treat its conclusions as a substitute for the underlying papers.

### DARPA / HIPP materials
Partially represented in public reporting and references in the 2003 controversy paper. Highest-priority archival gap. Seek original panel charters, member lists, meeting presentations, solicitations, contracts and budget justifications.

### Weinberger 2004 / 2006
High-value investigative reporting on DARPA culture, briefing slides and participant statements. Secondary source. Claims about dollar amounts, closed briefings and named officials should be paired with primary records where possible.

### Zimmerman 2007
First-person policy/science account from a prominent critic. Useful for reconstructing how the issue reached congressional and policy attention. Treat as attributed recollection, not neutral transcript.

### Kirischuk et al. 2015
Later positive bremsstrahlung triggering claim. Essential counterweight to any narrative that says positive reports simply ended after 2001.

### Kirischuk et al. 2023
Production cross-section measurements. Useful for showing continuing legitimate nuclear-science work and for building a quantitative production appendix.

## Archival requests worth making

- DARPA records for stimulated isomer energy release / SIER.
- HIPP final report and meeting records.
- Air Force contract records associated with hafnium-isomer work.
- DOE/NNSA correspondence about isomer production feasibility.
- Congressional hearing or committee records concerning hafnium funding cuts or restrictions.
- Strategic Defense Initiative Organization records on nuclear isomers and gamma-ray-laser concepts.

## Handling rule

A document about a proposed capability is evidence that the capability was proposed. A document about a funded program is evidence that the program was funded. Neither is technical proof. Keep that distinction visible in every archival chapter.

# Research Priorities and Defeat Conditions

## Highest-value next work

1. Build a complete triggering-experiment matrix from 1998 through 2026: sample composition, source spectrum, photon flux, energy range, detector, claimed lines, analysis method, inferred cross section, positive/null status, and independence from prior groups.
2. Reconstruct the 1999 result from the paper’s spectra and counting statistics; identify which line enhancements drove the integrated cross-section inference.
3. Reconstruct the Ahmad et al. 2001 and follow-up APS sensitivity calculations and plot their upper limits directly against every published positive value.
4. Retrieve the full set of 1999–2000 *Physical Review Letters* comments and replies rather than relying on later summaries.
5. Locate primary DARPA program solicitations, contracts, HIPP briefing materials, budget documents, and congressional funding actions.
6. Reconstruct the Strategic Defense Initiative-era nuclear-isomer / gamma-ray-laser work from primary program records.
7. Quantify gram-scale production scenarios from measured reaction cross sections, accelerator currents, target heating, radiochemistry, isotopic purity and stored-energy efficiency.
8. Calculate system-level shielding requirements for representative isomer masses and compare total device mass with chemical, radiological and nuclear alternatives.
9. Model gamma energy deposition in representative targets rather than quoting TNT equivalence.
10. Add a modern nuclear-isomer chapter anchored in primary 229Th nuclear-clock experiments, with no implication that successful clock control vindicates hafnium triggering.

## Decisive experiment proposal

A modern replication should distribute aliquots from one characterized 178m2Hf source to at least three independent synchrotron laboratories. Sample identities should be blinded. The primary gamma lines, photon-energy scan, beam-on/off schedule, detector calibration, background model and statistical thresholds should be preregistered. One laboratory should reproduce the original broad-spectrum irradiation conditions as closely as possible; another should use high-flux monochromatic scans; a third should test any specific gateway energies proposed by later positive papers.

The result should be reported as an excitation function and cross-section interval, including nulls, rather than as a binary “triggered/not triggered” claim.

## Defeat conditions

- The manuscript’s technical skepticism is defeated if independent groups reproduce on-demand depopulation at application-relevant cross sections and release fractions under controlled conditions.
- The institutional thesis is weakened if primary records show that application work was strictly gated behind independent replication and that public accounts exaggerated how far weapon planning advanced.
- The threat-inflation argument is weakened if public or declassified technical records establish an operational foreign isomer-energy device rather than mere research interest.

Until those conditions are met, the book should keep the strongest conclusion narrow: extraordinary stored energy existed; extraordinary controllability was not independently established at the level required by the weapon story.

# Chronology

- **1850s** — Robert Mallet's earthquake catalogs help preserve historical reports including unusual luminous phenomena associated with earthquakes.
- **1960s–1970s** — Modern photographs/reports of earthquake luminosity enter the scientific literature; instrument-based seismo-electromagnetic work expands.
- **1983–1985** — Lockner, Johnston, Byerlee and related USGS work studies electrical effects associated with rock fracture and fault-gouge electrical properties.
- **1986** — Derr and Persinger publish on luminous phenomena and earthquakes in southern Washington.
- **2004** — CNES launches DEMETER, a low-Earth-orbit mission explicitly designed in part to examine electromagnetic disturbances associated with earthquakes/volcanic activity.
- **2006** — St-Laurent, Derr, and Freund publish a positive-hole-based earthquake-light mechanism.
- **15 Aug 2007** — Mw 8.0 Pisco, Peru earthquake; luminous flashes are recorded in Lima and later compared with local seismic acceleration.
- **2009** — Mw 6.3 L'Aquila earthquake; precursor claims and risk communication become part of a consequential public/legal controversy.
- **2011** — Heraud and Lira publish the Pisco camera/seismic analysis.
- **2014** — Thériault et al. publish a 65-case earthquake-light catalog emphasizing rift environments and steep faults.
- **2015** — Masci publishes a critical analysis challenging reliability claims for an ionospheric precursor index.
- **2018** — Jánský et al. quantitatively model crust-to-atmosphere electrical coupling and show severe energy problems for some deep current-source configurations; Parrot summarizes DEMETER statistical precursor studies.
- **2020** — Akyol et al. publish a machine-learning ionospheric detection approach with explicit false-alarm counts in validation/test periods.
- **28 Mar 2025** — Mw 7.7 Myanmar earthquake on the Sagaing system.
- **2025** — Swarm case-study literature reports magnetic anomalies in the ten days before the Myanmar event using previously developed empirical relations.
- **2026** — COSMIC-2 statistical precursor work reports population-level true-positive and false-positive metrics, moving the conversation toward denominator-aware evaluation.

## Important chronological caution

The chronology of publications is not a chronology of proof. Repeated investigation, a dedicated mission, or newer instrumentation raises the quality of available tests; it does not automatically increase the truth probability of a precursor claim.

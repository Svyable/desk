# Publication Gate

This run completed the 18-chapter argument and supporting evidence architecture, but it does **not** claim the Desk full-book length gate of at least 3,000 words per chapter / 54,000+ words. The current chapters are substantially shorter and require a full expansion pass.

## Frozen substantive expansion work

1. Reconstruct primary historical EQL cases from Mallet, Galli, Matsushiro, Tagish Lake, Saguenay, L'Aquila, Pisco, and other benchmark events with page-level provenance rather than secondary retellings.
2. Build a denominator-aware event ledger: all large earthquakes in a defined period, tectonic setting, observation opportunity, EQL positive/negative status, infrastructure density, weather/daylight conditions.
3. Reconstruct Pisco 2007 at frame/timestamp level and search for available Lima utility outage/fault records, lightning data, camera geometry, and independent videos.
4. Expand rock-electricity physics: fracture electrification, piezoelectricity, triboelectricity, electrokinetic/streaming potentials, semiconductor-defect theory, conductivity, pore fluid, and scaling.
5. Reproduce the key positive-hole laboratory geometries and identify independent replications, criticisms, and material constraints.
6. Numerically reproduce Jánský et al. current/energy estimates across deep and shallow source geometries and compare with seismic energy and measured telluric fields.
7. Reconstruct DEMETER mission instrumentation, orbit/sampling limitations, full statistical positive/null literature, and post-mission assessments.
8. Build a matched critical literature ledger for TEC, magnetic-field, thermal, radon, and ionospheric precursor claims, including null and methodological papers.
9. Reproduce at least one modern precursor classifier on conceptually held-out data and report alert-hours, base rates, false alarms, misses, precision/recall, and geographic warning burden.
10. Deepen the L'Aquila section using primary legal/scientific records so the manuscript does not repeat the false “scientists jailed for not predicting an earthquake” shorthand.
11. Separate earthquake early warning, operational earthquake forecasting, probabilistic hazard, and deterministic precursor prediction with real system examples and quantitative lead-time/action constraints.
12. Expand 2025 Myanmar and 2026 COSMIC-2 sections with methods-level scrutiny, space-weather controls, and independent replication status.
13. Design a costed, executable multi-fault optical/electrical observing network including grid-log agreements, sensor specifications, preregistered anomaly rules, and defeat conditions.
14. Expand every chapter to >=3,000 words without padding, repeated frameworks, or catalog prose; each chapter must earn expansion through case reconstruction, mechanism, quantitative work, or competing evidence.
15. Run local `scripts/check-book-length.py` / metadata / cover checks available in Desk, then perform a whole-book source audit, paragraph pass, repetition audit, and anti-slop pass under the repository prose standard.

# Evidentiary Matrix

|---|---|---|---|---|
| Unusual luminous events have been recorded during some major earthquakes. | 1 — verified/documented | Pisco 2007 timed camera record; published coseismic reports. | Independent multi-camera/spectral networks. | Demonstration that benchmark cases are fully explained by infrastructure/weather artifacts. |

## Defeat condition for the book's central thesis

The central “real anomaly / poor oracle” thesis should be abandoned or materially revised if an independent prospective program shows that a defined earthquake-related optical/electromagnetic signature both (a) has a securely established geological mechanism and (b) produces operationally useful forecasts with prespecified spatial/temporal windows, false-alarm rates, and missed-event rates across multiple tectonic environments.

# Back Matter

This back matter will hold the source notes, glossary, data-method notes, and practical decision-record template as the manuscript develops.

The final edition should make it easy for readers to distinguish derived arithmetic, primary-source facts, academic findings, historical case reconstruction, and Sven Hardy Benson's interpretation. It should also include a clear educational-use notice: the book discusses investing methods and market behavior but does not provide individualized investment, legal, or tax advice.

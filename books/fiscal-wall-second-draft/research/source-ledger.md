# Source Ledger — Fiscal Wall: When Cheap Money Ends

This ledger supports the second manuscript's household-to-sovereign lens. Household, corporate, bank, pension, municipal, and foreign examples are used to make duration and refinancing tangible; none is evidence that the federal government faces the same legal or monetary constraints as a private borrower.

## Publication refresh checklist

Before release, update every current 2026 figure and re-run the argument against evidence that weakens it. If net interest stabilizes, the primary deficit narrows, debt-to-GDP bends down, clearing reform materially deepens market capacity, reserve demand strengthens, productivity accelerates, or long-term real yields fall without inflation, say so plainly. The title is not a commitment to catastrophe. It is a commitment to examining what the price of time does to a very large inherited balance sheet.

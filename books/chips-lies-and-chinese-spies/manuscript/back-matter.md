# Back Matter

## A Working Note on the Record

The first six chapters closed for initial drafting on November 14, 2026, two days after Nvidia publicly announced Jensen Huang's departure. Chapter 7 and its source audit were added November 18 after new official records and corporate disclosures became public. The independent investigation announced by Nvidia was not complete in the sources reviewed. Later events may alter the account substantially. The remaining chapters in the table of contents are not presented as completed prose.

The provenance of claims matters most when the story involves intelligence allegations. Nvidia's own statements and its SEC filing establish the company's public position and management actions. The Justice Department's plea record establishes conduct admitted in a separate criminal case involving export-controlled Nvidia GPUs. Reporters' accounts establish what journalists and their sources reported, but not necessarily what an intelligence service could prove. The difference is preserved deliberately.

The current source map and unresolved fact checks are maintained in the research folder. The author's interpretations are not findings of a court, intelligence service, or company investigation.

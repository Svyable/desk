# Reporting brief — Chips, Lies, & Chinese Spies

**State:** Seven-chapter working draft with November 18, 2026 primary-evidence pass. Do not present as complete, legally vetted, or ready for publication.

## Reader promise

A reader who remembers the affair headline should leave knowing how Nvidia became an industrial chokepoint, why the H20/H200 export-control dispute matters, what the contemporary intelligence allegations actually establish, what evidence is missing, and why a founder's extraordinary reach creates governance risks that survive his departure. This is a documented business, security, and political story with a human being at its center.

## Central thesis and strongest competitor

Working argument: Huang's fall exposed an unusual convergence of key-person governance, commercially valuable geopolitical access, and unresolved intelligence risk inside a company whose chips helped define modern AI capacity. The distinctive contribution is to treat the scandal and the semiconductor system together while refusing to turn circumstantial overlaps into proof of espionage.

Competing account: a personally embarrassing affair, intense political opportunism, and an unrelated export-control dispute were simply fused by timing and headlines. The same facts may prove that Nvidia suffered a governance scandal but no information-security compromise and no improper policy influence. The book must be strong enough to survive that outcome. A separate possibility is that export controls, while lucrative for black markets, materially protected American military advantage. Measure policy effects rather than presume them.

## Boundaries with adjacent Desk books

*Chiptastrophy* covers supply-chain resilience and recovery; *The Taiwan Threshold* covers gray-zone deterrence; this book is a person-centered institutional investigation focused on Nvidia, export-control politics, access, and the November 2026 crisis. Avoid repeating the architecture of either book. A chapter about Taiwan earns its place only by showing the dependency and choices relevant to Nvidia's leadership and America's chip diplomacy.

## Evidence grading

- **A — Primary public record:** Nvidia corporate releases and filings; Commerce export rules; DOJ court filings and guilty pleas. These verify what the source did, said, or admitted. A board assertion is evidence of the board's stated position, not proof of all underlying facts.
- **B — Reported documentary evidence:** journalism describing reviewed correspondence, financial filings, or photographs, when source documents are not independently available to this book. Attribute named outlet, date, and limits.
- **C — Attributed anonymous-source account:** intelligence assessments, confidential conversations, and internal company accounts described by Reuters, NYT, or other outlets. Preserve uncertainty and the reported nature of the claims.
- **D — Hypothesis or analysis:** policy influence, motives, compromise, causation, and future outcomes. No narrative assertion without evidence and a meaningful competitor.

## Investigative map

1. **Chronology:** reconcile NYT's Oct 29 reporting, Nov 7 follow-ups, the Nov 8 employee letter and board release, Nov 10 counsel appointment, Nov 11 SEC event, Nov 12 announcement, Nov 13–14 Reuters reporting. Record both event date and publication date.
2. **Relationship:** seek original text of public statements, complete internal correspondence described by Reuters, contemporaneous dates, direct responses from Huang and Alice Huang or counsel, and independent confirmation of any relevant company duty.
3. **Foreign intelligence:** distinguish U.S. officials' reported assessment from established proof. Seek on-record confirmation of the assessment's scope; ask what, if anything, was accessed or shared. Record Chinese government's denial and Nvidia's no-known-compromise statement.
4. **Megaspeed:** obtain Singapore corporate filings, annual accounts, changes in ownership and assets, customs/shipping information, license records, end-use information, and the company's response. An unusually fast-growing customer is not by itself evidence of diversion.
5. **Export controls:** model H20/H200 product and license changes by month; distinguish U.S. permission from Chinese import acceptance. Cross-check 10-K and 10-Q language, charges, tariffs, and dates. Do not claim every restricted chip was smuggled.
6. **Hsu case:** use DOJ release and plea documents to trace exact route, goods, dates, and guilty parties. Avoid connecting the case to Nvidia's management or Megaspeed absent new primary evidence.
7. **Founder governance:** review Nvidia proxy statements, board composition, separation of chair/CEO functions, succession arrangements, related-party policies, compliance chain, and the board's November communications.
8. **AI demand:** examine customer-financing structures, related investments, supply commitments, and booked revenue using original disclosures. Do not call interdependence a Ponzi or circular scheme without quantitative proof.
9. **China strategy:** examine competing assessments of long-term restriction effects, domestic substitution, and the strategic value of delayed compute access. Interview former BIS officials and Chinese AI hardware researchers.
10. **Continuity:** after Nov 17, compare Nvidia's investor communication, customer orders, engineering roadmap, and interim-governance process with the company's prior commitments.

## Defeat conditions / correction triggers

- Independent inquiry finds no security compromise: retain it prominently and revise any passage that implies otherwise.
- Verified evidence refutes an alleged relationship or a purported intermediary: remove or correct with provenance.
- New court documents identify (or affirmatively exclude) a participant in a diversion scheme: update the relevant case with procedural status, not rumor.
- A claim resting only on Reuters/NYT anonymous sources is contradicted by primary evidence: revise its status immediately.
- SEC amendments or new Nvidia releases change an executive's title, resignation effective date, or H200 sales figures: update chronology and references.
- Legal review flags defamatory inference by juxtaposition: isolate the cases more visibly or remove the claim.

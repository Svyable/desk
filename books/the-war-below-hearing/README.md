## Thesis

The beaked-whale/sonar record is best understood as a systems-level injury problem: military sonar can trigger strong context-dependent behavioral responses in extreme divers, and those responses may destabilize pressure-management physiology enough to contribute to gas-embolic injury and strandings. The causal chain is strongly supported in parts but not mechanically closed in every case; direct acoustic trauma, behavioral disruption, diving physiology, geography, habituation and population effects must be kept separate rather than collapsed into a single slogan.

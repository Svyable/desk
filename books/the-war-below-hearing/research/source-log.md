## Canary 2002 claim boundaries

- **Observed:** a tightly timed mass stranding; fresh postmortems; severe hemorrhage/congestion; gas-bubble-associated lesions and fat emboli; absence of an identified systemic infectious/neoplastic explanation in the examined animals.
- **Strong inference:** at least part of the embolic injury likely preceded beaching; the paper's offshore recoveries, rapid deaths and comparative pathology make ordinary stranding/decomposition an inadequate explanation for the complete syndrome.
- **Mechanism not closed:** modified diving behavior and acoustically mediated growth of pre-existing gas nuclei were both proposed; neither was directly observed in vivo in the 2002 animals.
- **Do not write:** “sonar made the whales surface too fast and gave them the bends” as an established sequence. Later behavioral work permits several altered dive trajectories, including prolonged/silent avoidance.
- **Counterevidence that must remain visible:** beaked whales persist and reproduce on active sonar ranges and controlled exposures commonly produce behavioral responses without mass stranding. Severe outcomes therefore require additional state/exposure/geographic variables if the sonar association is causal.

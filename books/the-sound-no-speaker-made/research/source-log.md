## Research priorities

Obtain the full declassified JASON report and score every acoustic claim against exact recordings; reconstruct microwave-hearing dose thresholds by carrier frequency, pulse duration, pulse repetition rate, aperture and subject geometry; build a document-level PANDORA chronology rather than relying on retrospective summaries; map every public IC confidence shift from 2021 through 2025; identify published U.S.-government RF bioeffects work alluded to in the 2025 assessment; compare early-case inclusion criteria with later AHI surveillance definitions; and design a prospective embassy sensor package capable of discriminating RF, ultrasound, infrasound, acoustic, chemical and conventional environmental events without turning employee health monitoring into uncontrolled surveillance.

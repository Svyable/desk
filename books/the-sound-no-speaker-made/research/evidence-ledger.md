# Evidence Ledger

## Class 1 — Verified / declassified / documented

- Human perception of suitable pulsed RF/microwave exposures is experimentally established; the microwave auditory effect has been reproduced and has a well-supported thermoelastic/thermoacoustic mechanism.
- Foster & Finch (1974) demonstrated microwave-generated acoustic transients in water adequate to explain reported clicks.
- The United States measured microwave radiation directed at the U.S. Embassy in Moscow over many years and raised the issue with Soviet officials.
- ARPA created Project PANDORA in 1965 to study possible biological/behavioral effects relevant to the Moscow Signal, including controlled primate exposures.
- Declassified PANDORA records include inconclusive and negative findings; a 1969 meeting summary said evidence was insufficient to draw conclusions about effects on human behavior.
- U.S. personnel began reporting anomalous health incidents in Havana in late 2016 and later in other locations.
- JAMA published clinical and imaging studies of AHI cohorts in 2018, 2019, and 2024.
- JASON's 2018 acoustic analysis concluded that prominent recordings were mechanical/biological in origin and matched an Indies short-tailed cricket in key examples.
- The 2022 IC Experts Panel judged pulsed electromagnetic energy, particularly RF, a plausible mechanism for the core characteristics of a subset of AHIs.
- The 2023 and January 2025 IC assessments found that most components judged foreign-adversary responsibility very unlikely.
- The 2024 NIH studies found no significant consistent MRI-detectable brain injury or broad biological abnormality distinguishing the studied AHI group from matched controls after appropriate comparisons.

## Class 2 — Credible but incomplete / intelligence-assessed / mechanism-dependent

- Some early AHI cases had abrupt, spatially localized sensory onsets that remain difficult to explain from public information.
- Directed pulsed RF is physically capable of producing microwave auditory sensations and, at sufficient exposures, established thermal or mechanical bioeffects; whether an operationally plausible regime reproduces the distinctive AHI phenotype remains incompletely demonstrated publicly.
- In January 2025, one IC component assessed it likely that a foreign actor possessed an RF antipersonnel capability able to cause biological effects consistent with some reported AHI symptoms; another assessed roughly even odds of a relevant capability. The underlying intelligence remains classified.
- One IC component in 2025 assessed roughly even odds that a foreign actor used a novel weapon against a small undetermined subset; this was a low-confidence judgment.
- Some long-term symptoms may reflect PPPD, functional neurological disorder, migraine, stress physiology, or other processes triggered by an initial event whose nature remains uncertain.

## Class 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

- Claims that Russia, Cuba, China, or another named state conducted a coordinated global RF attack campaign against U.S. personnel.
- Claims identifying a particular covert RF weapon model, operator unit, or deployment platform without public chain-of-custody evidence.
- Claims that all or most AHIs share one causal mechanism.
- Claims that the famous Havana audio recordings are recordings of microwave energy itself.
- Claims that Project PANDORA demonstrated operational microwave mind control or reliable low-level behavioral manipulation.
- Claims that exploratory 2019 imaging findings prove a unique traumatic brain injury caused by directed energy.

## Class 4 — Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- A small subset of abrupt-onset cases may have involved an external directed stimulus while the broader AHI reporting category accumulated heterogeneous conventional causes.
- Early natural or medical events could have changed reporting behavior and later served as cover for adversary experimentation; the 2025 ICA lists a related red-team scenario but does not establish it.
- Foreign RF research may have crossed a capability threshold after the earliest Havana events without implying that those events were caused by that capability.
- The strongest future discriminator is prospective instrumentation: synchronized wideband RF detection, calibrated environmental sensing, baseline clinical data, and incident-specific chain of custody.

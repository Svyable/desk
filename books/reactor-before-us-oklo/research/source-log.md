# Source Log — Reactor Before Us

## Primary / authoritative / high-value sources

1. U.S. Geological Survey, Robert M. Moxham, **“Gamma-ray spectrometer measurement of 238U/235U in uranium ore from a natural reactor at Oklo, Gabon”** (1976). https://www.usgs.gov/publications/gamma-ray-spectrometer-measurement-238u235u-uranium-ore-a-natural-reactor-oklo-gabon
   - Use: direct confirmation of anomalous U-235 depletion and early reactor interpretation.

2. A. P. Meshik, C. M. Hohenberg, O. V. Pravdivtseva, **“Record of Cycling Operation of the Natural Nuclear Reactor in the Oklo/Okelobondo Area in Gabon,” Physical Review Letters 93, 182302 (2004).** https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.93.182302
   - Use: xenon/krypton isotope structure; inferred ~30-minute active pulses separated by ~2.5-hour dormant periods.

3. C. R. Gould, E. I. Sharapov, S. K. Lamoreaux, **“Time variability of α from realistic models of Oklo reactors,” Physical Review C 74, 024607 (2006).** https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevC.74.024607
   - Use: realistic reactor-model treatment of Sm-149 resonance and model-dependent constraints on alpha variation.

4. S. K. Lamoreaux, J. R. Torgerson, **“Neutron moderation in the Oklo natural reactor and the time variation of α,” Physical Review D 69, 121701(R) (2004).** https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.69.121701
   - Use: neutron-spectrum dependence in fundamental-constant inference.

5. E. E. Groopman, D. G. Willingham, A. P. Meshik, O. V. Pravdivtseva, **“Discovery of fissionogenic Cs and Ba capture five years after Oklo reactor shutdown,” PNAS 115 (2018), 8676–8681.** https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1807267115
   - Use: micro-scale migration and mineral capture of fissionogenic Cs/Ba; direct warning against simple “nothing moved” descriptions.

6. M. A. D. et al., **“Evidence of Uranium and Associated Trace Element Mobilization and Retention Processes at Oklo (Gabon), a Naturally Radioactive Site,” Environmental Science & Technology (2004).** https://doi.org/10.1021/es0353863
   - Use: uranium and trace-element dissolution/mobility; natural-analogue boundary conditions.

7. American Nuclear Society, **“Oklo’s Natural Fission Reactors.”** https://wx1.ans.org/pi/np/oklo/
   - Use: accessible technical background and waste-analogue framing; secondary, not a substitute for primary papers.

## Evidence rules

- A measured isotope anomaly is not interchangeable with a modeled reactor parameter.
- “Natural reactor” is established; precise power, temperature, duty cycle, and geometry are reconstructed.
- Oklo is evidence about element-specific retention and migration, not proof that any proposed engineered repository is safe.
- Pat analogies to modern reactor engineering should be used only where the neutron/thermal physics truly match.
- Alpha-variation constraints must always be labeled model-dependent and assumption-dependent.

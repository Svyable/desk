# Evidence Matrix — Reactor Before Us

## Class 1 — Verified / documented

- Uranium from Oklo shows anomalous depletion of U-235 relative to ordinary natural uranium.
- Fission-product and neutron-capture isotope anomalies confirm sustained natural fission.
- Multiple natural reactor zones existed in the Oklo/Okelobondo system, with Bangombé as a related natural reactor occurrence.
- Groundwater moderation is physically required in accepted thermal-reactor reconstructions.
- Fission noble-gas isotope patterns preserve evidence consistent with pulsed operation.
- Specific fission products and actinide daughters show both retention and migration.
- Oklo isotope data have been used in peer-reviewed work to constrain possible long-term variation of nuclear parameters and the fine-structure constant.

## Class 2 — Credible but incomplete / model-dependent

- Exact water content during operation.
- Exact power of individual reactor zones.
- Exact duration and repetition count of active/dormant cycles.
- Reactor-zone temperatures and neutron spectra.
- Detailed pre-mining geometry of destroyed zones.
- Exact hydrology controlling post-reactor migration.
- Quantitative transferability of Oklo transport behavior to specific repository designs.

## Class 3 — Disputed / not established

- Claims that Oklo proves a specific engineered waste repository will remain safe for geological time.
- Claims that no important radionuclides moved from the reactor zones.
- Claims that Oklo gives a model-free proof that fundamental constants never changed.
- Claims that nature built the equivalent of a modern commercial reactor.
- Claims that pulsed behavior is directly observed rather than reconstructed from isotopic evidence.

## Class 4 — Speculative synthesis

- Oklo as a natural “stress test” of nuclear assumptions across radically different time scales.
- Selective mobility as the most useful conceptual bridge between Oklo and waste policy.
- Geological feedback as a natural counterpart to engineered negative reactivity feedback.
- The same ore body functioning as several scientific instruments depending on which isotope system is interrogated.

## Contradiction / incentive audit

### Nuclear advocacy incentive
There is an incentive to turn Oklo into a simple pro-disposal story: nature contained reactor waste for two billion years. That framing can hide migration and site differences.

### Anti-nuclear incentive
There is an opposite incentive to point to any migration as evidence that geological isolation fails. That framing ignores mineral trapping, retardation, and the fact that repository assessment never assumes universal immobility.

### Fundamental-physics incentive
Constraints on changing constants are intrinsically headline-friendly. Strong numerical bounds can obscure their dependence on reactor models, neutron spectra, nuclear resonance calculations, and assumptions about which constants vary independently.

### Popular-science incentive
“Nature invented the nuclear reactor first” is true enough to attract attention and sloppy enough to flatten the geology. The manuscript should keep the hook while restoring the actual conditions.

## Defeat conditions

The book’s central synthesis weakens if:

1. the pulsed-operation interpretation fails under better isotope or reactor modeling;
2. later work shows that waste-migration lessons are too site-specific to inform even mechanism-level repository reasoning;
3. the accepted natural-criticality reconstruction changes materially enough that groundwater moderation or reactor geometry no longer carries the explanatory role assigned here;
4. the alpha-variation literature turns out to be too assumption-sensitive to deserve more than a brief appendix rather than a major chapter pair.

## Central boundary

This manuscript does not treat the existence of animal magnetoreception as proof of any particular receptor mechanism, does not treat quantum spin chemistry as evidence for quantum consciousness, does not generalize avian radiofrequency orientation effects into human-health claims, and does not present the 2019 human EEG study as a demonstrated conscious magnetic sense.

## Research principle

Where a mechanism is unresolved, the manuscript names the level actually demonstrated: behavioral response, neural pathway, molecular property, or speculative integration. A result is not promoted merely because adjacent evidence makes it feel inevitable.

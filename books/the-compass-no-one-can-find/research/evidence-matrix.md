# Evidence Matrix

| Claim | Class | Current posture | What would strengthen it | What could defeat it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Migratory birds use geomagnetic information for compass orientation | 1 — verified/documented | Strong | Larger standardized cross-lab replications | Failure of classic field-rotation effects under modern blinded protocols |
| Avian compass behavior is light dependent | 1 | Strong but species/protocol bounded | Harmonized wavelength/intensity experiments | Robust compass behavior independent of light across canonical species |
| Radical-pair chemistry is physically capable of Earth-field sensitivity | 1 | Strong | More physiological radical-pair measurements | Demonstration that required lifetimes/orientation cannot exist biologically |
| European-robin CRY4 is magnetically sensitive in vitro | 1 | Strong | Independent biochemical replication | Replication failure attributable to original artifact |
| CRY4 is the in-vivo avian compass receptor | 2 — credible/incomplete | Leading candidate, not closed | Targeted mutation → behavioral loss → rescue | Intact compass after selective destruction of CRY4 magnetic chemistry |
| Famous upper-beak iron-rich cells are magnetoreceptors | 3 — disputed/refuted | Refuted as stated; cells identified as macrophages | N/A for old claim | Already defeated by anatomy unless a distinct structure was conflated |
| A separate magnetite/trigeminal magnetic channel exists in birds | 2 | Plausible/credible | Identify receptor cell and causal transducer | Neural effects shown to be nonmagnetic artifact; no peripheral receptor after exhaustive causal work |
| Urban RF noise can disrupt avian magnetic orientation | 2 | Serious controlled evidence; mechanistic scope contested | Independent replications with spectrum/dosimetry | Failure across preregistered independent sites |
| Humans transduce Earth-strength field rotations | 2 | Intriguing 2019 EEG evidence | Multi-lab preregistered replication | Reliable independent nulls or identification of apparatus artifact |
| Humans possess a conscious/useful magnetic sense | 3 | Unconfirmed | Replicable behavioral performance tied to field manipulation | Persistent absence of behavior despite validated neural transduction |
| Magnetoreception proves broad weak-EM health effects | 4 / invalid transfer | Unsupported | Requires independent pathology mechanism/evidence | Current evidence already fails to justify generalization |

## Central defeat condition

The book’s central synthesis weakens if rigorous independent replications collapse the canonical animal field-manipulation behaviors, or if one receptor mechanism is causally demonstrated to explain the supposedly separate major channels so completely that the proposed plural architecture becomes unnecessary.

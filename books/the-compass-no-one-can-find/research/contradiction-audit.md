# Contradiction and Incentive Audit

## Contradictions to preserve

1. **Strong behavior, weak receptor closure.** This is the book’s central tension. Do not let mechanism uncertainty leak backward into denying canonical behavioral evidence, or let behavioral certainty leak forward into receptor certainty.
2. **Cryptochrome strength, cryptochrome gaps.** CRY4 has important in-vitro support. In-vivo causal necessity remains the publication-critical gap.
3. **Magnetite plausibility, failed pigeon anatomy.** Magnetite is biologically real and physically attractive; the once-famous upper-beak iron-cell localization was wrong.
4. **Trigeminal evidence, missing transducer.** Neural pathway evidence survives the anatomical correction but does not identify the receptor.
5. **RF-disruption evidence, replication/model tension.** The 2014 Oldenburg work is unusually controlled; later local-eye oscillating-field work challenges a simple retinal account. Preserve both.
6. **Human EEG signal, no conscious percept.** Do not convert alpha-ERD into navigation, intuition, or pathology.

## Incentives that distort interpretation

- “Quantum biology” framing rewards dramatic mechanistic closure.
- EM-health controversies can overgeneralize avian orientation findings.
- Skeptical communities can overcorrect from magnetic-healing fraud into dismissal of legitimate magnetobiology.
- Candidate-receptor laboratories have understandable investment in their molecular/anatomical models.
- Negative replication has publication and apparatus-sensitivity problems; positive weak-field effects have artifact risk.
- Media stories prefer one organ, one molecule, one solved mystery.

## Disinformation / extraordinary-claim note

This subject is not primarily intelligence-classified. No classified-access claims are used. Popular conspiratorial extensions about mind control, geomagnetic consciousness, or hidden human magnetic abilities are excluded unless treated explicitly as unsupported cultural claims.

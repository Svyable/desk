# Not a Dangerous Occupation

## Asbestos, the Navy, and the Study That Could Not See the Future

Sven Hardy Benson

Copyright © 2026 Sven Hardy Benson. All rights reserved.

This is a work of narrative nonfiction built from publicly available historical, scientific, legal, and archival sources. It does not claim access to non-public Navy records or classified material. Where later litigation interprets wartime documents, the manuscript distinguishes the underlying document from the advocate’s interpretation whenever the primary record can be recovered.

The title quotes the central conclusion associated with the 1946 Fleischer–Viles–Gade–Drinker survey of naval pipe-covering operations. The book’s argument is not that every later asbestos disease was fully foreseeable in 1946. It is that long-latency disease exposes a recurring evidentiary failure: a short observation window can make a hazardous practice look safer than it is, especially when the practice is useful and the consequences mature outside the institution’s planning horizon.

# Publication Gate

This run establishes an 18-chapter end-to-end manuscript and research spine but does not clear the Desk publication-length gate. Every numbered chapter remains below 3,000 words and chapter-only prose remains below 65,000 words.

## Required expansion

- Recover and page-index the complete 1945 Drinker correspondence and the exact Bath Iron Works findings.
- Page-index the 1946 Fleischer–Viles–Gade–Drinker paper and tabulate worker tenure, radiographic findings, dust measurements, work processes, controls, and qualifications.
- Recover Bureau of Ships, Bureau of Medicine and Surgery, Navy-yard, Maritime Commission, and contract-yard asbestos rules and specifications from the 1930s through the 1970s.
- Build a pre-1946 knowledge ledger separating asbestosis, lung cancer, mesothelioma, dose, fiber type, and dust-control evidence.
- Reconstruct contemporary dust-measurement methods without forcing invalid conversions into modern units.
- Build cohort tables for insulation workers and relevant naval/shipyard populations with first-exposure date, follow-up period, latency, and disease endpoint.
- Add worker and union records that can deepen lived experience without invented scenes.
- Trace the actual citation and legal afterlife of the 1946 paper rather than inferring broad influence from a few examples.
- Compare competing historical interpretations against the same primary records.
- Expand each chapter through document-specific detail, quantitative epidemiology, dated institutional decisions, and genuine contradictions rather than repeating the latency thesis.

The current draft is intentionally compressed. A later whole-book editorial pass must test repetition, source proportion, chronology, and the transition from wartime occupational medicine to the later control regime before release consideration.

## Thesis

ALH84001 matters less as a failed claim of Martian life than as the experiment science accidentally ran on the word *biosignature*: four individually suggestive observations were assembled into a biological story, then decades of mineralogy and geochemistry showed how nonliving processes could manufacture much of the same evidentiary stack. The lesson is not that morphology, organics, magnetite, or isotopes are useless. It is that a biosignature is not a thing. It is an inference that has to survive an expanding library of abiotic counterfeits.

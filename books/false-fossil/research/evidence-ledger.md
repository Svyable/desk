# Evidence ledger — FALSE FOSSIL

## Class 1 — documented / verified

- McKay et al. published “Search for Past Life on Mars: Possible Relic Biogenic Activity in Martian Meteorite ALH84001” in *Science* 273 (1996), presenting multiple observations as a possible biogenic assemblage rather than a definitive proof of life. NASA NTRS record: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20040173162
- ALH84001 is a Martian meteorite recovered in Antarctica in 1984. NASA overview: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/carbon-compounds-from-mars-found-inside-meteorite-alh84001/
- Later laboratory work demonstrated an inorganic process analogue capable of producing magnetites with morphologies invoked in the biological argument. NASA NTRS record for Golden et al., *American Mineralogist* 89 (2004): https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20060010450
- A 2022 *Science* study found complex refractory organic material associated with mineral assemblages formed during carbonation and serpentinization; the authors concluded that abiotic organic synthesis operated during ancient Martian water-rock interactions. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abg7905 ; NASA Astrobiology summary: https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/news/an-update-from-alh84001/
- NASA's current public search-for-life history says most scientists studying the ALH84001 question now consider non-biological sources the likely origin of the evidence once interpreted as possible microbial traces: https://science.nasa.gov/universe/search-for-life/the-hunt-for-life-on-mars-and-elsewhere-in-the-solar-system/

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Some ALH84001 magnetite populations were reported as closely resembling magnetite produced by terrestrial magnetotactic bacteria. That resemblance is a measurement; its biological exclusivity is the contested inference. NASA NTRS: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20040112542
- The meteorite preserves evidence of ancient Martian aqueous alteration and organic synthesis. This is strong evidence about habitability-relevant chemistry, not direct evidence that organisms participated.
- The ALH84001 controversy materially influenced the development of astrobiology and biosignature standards. NASA Astrobiology Institute historical summary: https://astrobiology.nasa.gov/nai/annual-reports/2011/uwis/project-1a-rb-sr-geochronology-of-alh84001-demonstrates-early-weathering-on-mars/index.html

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- The claim that the small morphological features are fossil microorganisms is not established.
- The claim that the relevant magnetite population requires biology is challenged by inorganic synthesis/process analogues.
- Indigenous Martian organic carbon does not establish biological origin; 2022 nanoscale work supplies specific abiotic formation pathways.
- No claim that ALH84001 proves past Martian life may be stated as fact.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- **Counterfeit library:** every credible abiotic route that reproduces a candidate biosignature should be treated as part of an expanding adversarial library against which future life-detection claims are tested.
- **Stacking ambiguity:** multiple weakly diagnostic observations can feel stronger when narrated together even when they share the same hidden abiotic cause. Evidence aggregation must model dependence, not merely count clues.
- **Biosignature half-life:** a candidate signature can lose specificity as geochemistry discovers additional nonliving production routes. This is a proposed epistemic concept, not a measured constant.
- **A biosignature is a verb:** shorthand for the claim that biological interpretation is an inferential comparison among generative models, not an intrinsic label attached to an object.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. Some magnetites closely resemble terrestrial biogenic magnetite; inorganic experiments also reproduce relevant morphologies.
2. Organics are genuinely Martian/indigenous in important analyses; indigenous organics need not be biological.
3. The 1996 argument was explicitly cumulative; later work suggests several clues may not be statistically independent because water-rock chemistry can generate organics, minerals and textures together.
4. “Most scientists favor abiotic” is a consensus description, not a proof that no biological contribution occurred.

## Disinformation / institutional incentives

No evidence checked here supports an intelligence/disinformation explanation for the ALH84001 scientific controversy. Do not import secrecy frameworks into a case that is primarily about scientific inference. Institutional incentives still matter in ordinary ways: NASA faced enormous public and political attention; extraordinary claims produce visibility; skeptical rebuttals also reward novelty. None of those incentives establishes fraud or bad faith.

## Publication holds

- Do not call the morphology a fossil except when attributing the interpretation.
- Do not say 2022 work disproved every possible biological contribution; it established abiotic origins for analyzed organic material and specific geochemical processes.
- Do not turn consensus language into certainty.
- Do not treat visual resemblance as taxonomic identification.
- Do not treat four lines of evidence as independent until their causal dependencies are modeled.

## Defeat condition for the book's strongest synthesis

The `stacking ambiguity` thesis weakens if the original candidate biosignatures can be shown to arise from genuinely independent physical histories and a joint biological model predicts their co-occurrence substantially better than tested abiotic models. Future returned samples with preserved cellular organization, biological polymers, diagnostic isotopic fractionation and geological context that jointly defeat abiotic countermodels would force the manuscript to retire its ALH84001-centered caution for that sample rather than move the goalposts.

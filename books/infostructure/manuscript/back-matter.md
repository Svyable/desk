## Research questions for the next pass

The next manuscript pass should add deep case studies rather than conceptual padding: simultaneous invention; Polynesian and Tasmanian technology-loss debates; tacit knowledge in semiconductor and aerospace manufacturing; industrial-cluster mobility; standards as innovation platforms; publication bias and negative-results archives; pre-digital scientific rediscovery; supply-chain capability loss; and controlled experiments in AI-assisted cross-domain retrieval.

The central falsification question is whether variables specific to infostructure—retrieval latency, translation distance, provenance, tacit continuity and recombination topology—predict innovation or recovery after ordinary controls such as population, education, R&D spending and capital are included.

# CSES-02 current source note

Fresh-checked 2026-09-13 because Chapter 11 discusses an active program rather than only historical precursor research.

## Verified program facts

- The National Space Science Center of the Chinese Academy of Sciences states that **CSES-02 launched successfully on June 14, 2025** aboard a Long March 2D rocket and that its High-Precision Magnetometer was activated June 18. The same source describes CSES-01 as having operated for more than seven years and notes cross-calibration of its magnetic-field measurements with ESA Swarm data. https://english.nssc.cas.cn/news/202506/t20250630_1046452.html
- The Italian Space Agency likewise records the successful **June 14, 2025** launch and describes the continuing China-Italy scientific collaboration. https://www.asi.it/2025/06/la-scienza-italiana-nello-spazio-con-cses-02-al-via-una-nuova-missione-di-osservazione-della-terra/
- ASI's mission page states the scientific objectives as studying electromagnetic, ionospheric and magnetospheric phenomena and searching for possible space-time correlations between variations in those parameters and strong earthquakes, while also investigating lithosphere-atmosphere-ionosphere coupling. It also describes CSES-02 as operating in constellation with CSES-01. https://www.asi.it/scienze-della-terra/missione-cses-contributo-italiano-limadou/
- A November 25, 2025 ASI update states that CSES-02 had entered commissioning and that the Limadou EXPO agreement would support operations and scientific analysis of its data. https://www.asi.it/2025/11/al-via-laccordo-limadou-expo/

## Evidence boundary

These sources establish that the program exists, what it measures, the launch date, international participation and its stated scientific objectives. They do **not** establish that CSES-01 or CSES-02 has demonstrated reliable earthquake prediction. Official mission interest is evidence of an active scientific question, not evidence of operational efficacy.

For any later manuscript pass that claims a CSES-02 earthquake-associated result, recover the actual peer-reviewed analysis, identify whether the anomaly definition predated the event/data interval, and score it against non-earthquake periods. Do not use a mission press release as evidence of a precursor.

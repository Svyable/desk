# Evidence matrix

This matrix is deliberately stricter than a bibliography. A paper can document an anomaly without establishing that the anomaly predicts earthquakes.

| Claim | Class | What supports it | What would change the classification |
|---|---|---|---|
| Damaging earthquakes generate measurable coseismic effects in ground strain, magnetic fields, acoustics, atmosphere and ionosphere | 1 — verified/documented | Dense seismic/geodetic networks; North Palm Springs seismomagnetic observations; Tohoku post-seismic traveling ionospheric disturbances; extensive modern literature | Only a major reanalysis overturning basic timing/causality |
| Deterministic short-term prediction of location, time and magnitude is not an established operational capability | 1 | USGS position; Parkfield history; ICEF review; 1997 prediction debate; absence of validated prospective system | A prospectively registered method showing sustained skill above agreed baselines with useful false-alarm/miss rates |
| Parkfield's original characteristic-earthquake timing prediction failed | 1 | USGS experiment history; Roeloffs & Langbein; Savage; Jackson & Kagan | Historical documentation showing the original alarm window or target was materially different |
| The 1989 Corralitos ULF anomaly was a reliable Loma Prieta precursor | 3 — disputed/not established | Original Fraser-Smith report supports; Thomas, Love & Johnston reanalysis attributes anomaly to sensor-system malfunction | Independent contemporaneous stations showing the same localized pre-event waveform after instrument-health controls |
| Seismic electric signals of the VAN type carry predictive information | 3 | VAN group's published prospective claims and evaluation papers; field polarization studies | Fully specified prospective alarm stream with immutable timestamps, target windows and independent scoring that materially beats null forecasts |
| Stressed rocks can produce electrical effects in laboratory or field settings | 1 for existence of multiple mechanisms; 2 for relevance to pre-earthquake prediction | Electrokinetic effects; piezoelectricity in suitable minerals; frictional/triboelectric effects; Freund positive-hole experiments and proposals | Mechanism-specific replication failures or field scaling evidence sharply narrowing relevance |
| Positive-hole activation explains earthquake lights and broad pre-seismic EM anomalies | 3 | Laboratory stress-activation work and proposed mechanism | Independent laboratories reproducing carrier generation at relevant crustal P-T-fluid conditions plus field observations with quantitative flux/attenuation closure |
| Radon concentrations sometimes change around earthquakes | 1 | Decades of well/soil observations; Iceland, Turkey, modern Indian networks | Broad data audit showing reported changes are artifacts or uncorrected environmental covariates |
| A radon anomaly can predict a damaging earthquake with actionable reliability | 3 | Individual case reports including recent Myanmar work suggest correlations | Prospective multi-station alarms with preregistered thresholds, weather/hydrology controls, false-alarm denominator and independent replication |
| Earthquake lights are a single established geophysical phenomenon with one known mechanism | 3 | Some luminous reports and laboratory hypotheses exist; USGS explicitly records disagreement and alternative causes | Instrumented multi-sensor captures co-located with fault process that exclude grid/lightning/astronomical sources and match a quantitative mechanism |
| Some luminous phenomena are genuinely associated with seismic episodes | 2 — credible but incomplete | Case compilations, southern Washington reports, historical clusters, rift-setting analyses | Prospective camera/electric-field/magnetic networks showing no excess over matched control periods, or conversely robust confirmation |
| Ionospheric TEC and density anomalies sometimes precede earthquakes in published analyses | 1 as a publication fact; 2 as an empirical possibility | Heki 2011; DEMETER statistical work; numerous case studies | Reanalysis showing all such signals arise from background/modeling choices, or prospective replication strengthening them |
| The Tohoku pre-event TEC enhancement is a demonstrated earthquake precursor | 3 | Heki's original analysis supports; Eisenbeis et al. show the result is sensitive to reference-curve construction and can be generated artifactually | Analysis method fixed before event and applied prospectively across a long blind series, retaining the signal and low false-alarm rate |
| DEMETER observed statistical ion-density structure associated with seismicity | 2 | Yan et al. 2017 and related superposed-epoch analyses | Independent reanalysis with stronger controls and negative result, or modern constellation replication with prospective thresholds |
| Satellite seismo-electromagnetics can currently issue useful public earthquake predictions | 3 | Research missions exist; no validated operational prediction record | Independent, prospective operational trial with decision-relevant skill |
| Animals can sense an earthquake seconds before many humans | 1 | P-wave/S-wave timing and sensory thresholds make this ordinary | N/A except species-specific sensory findings |
| Animals reliably predict earthquakes days or weeks in advance | 3 | Anecdotes and some observational studies | Instrumented baseline behavior across many animals/locations with prospective alarms and environmental controls |
| Multi-parameter precursor monitoring may reveal earthquake preparation physics even if it never becomes deterministic prediction | 4 — speculative synthesis | Heterogeneous anomaly literature plus confirmed coseismic coupling across domains | Long prospective datasets showing no pre-event deviations beyond chance in any physically linked channel |
| The central problem is specificity rather than detectability | 4, strongly supported synthesis | Many measurable earthquake-related and environmental signals; recurring false positives and retrospective tuning | A precursor with high sensitivity/specificity and stable prospective performance would narrow the thesis |

## Defeat condition for the book's main thesis

The book's central claim should be considered defeated or substantially narrowed if a pre-specified, independently operated precursor system demonstrates sustained prospective skill for damaging tectonic earthquakes across multiple regions and years, with all of the following published before or continuously during the test: target magnitude, spatial alarm region, temporal alarm window, sensor-health rules, environmental exclusions, preprocessing, anomaly threshold, handling of missing data, method for clustered earthquakes, and scoring rule. It must beat reasonable time-independent and seismicity-based forecast baselines after accounting for alarm volume and must retain enough specificity to support an action that would be irrational without the alarm.

The opposite result would also narrow the book: if long-duration, dense, multi-physics arrays show that alleged precursors vanish entirely once instruments and analysis are controlled prospectively, the manuscript should stop entertaining a heterogeneous-preparation model and say more plainly that the historical anomaly literature was largely a measurement and selection artifact.

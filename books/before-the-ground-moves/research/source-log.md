## Notes on source discipline

- Patents, promotional company material, retrospective personal claims, and press reports are never evidence that a precursor method works. They may document that a claim or program existed.
- Case studies without a denominator cannot establish false-alarm rate.
- An earthquake-correlated physical effect is not automatically a pre-earthquake precursor.
- A pre-earthquake anomaly is not automatically predictive. A prediction requires a stated alarm rule, spatial window, temporal window, magnitude threshold, and prospective scoring against misses and false alarms.
- Satellite anomalies require explicit solar, geomagnetic, local-time, orbital, seasonal, geographic, and instrumentation controls.
- Radon anomalies require hydrological, barometric, temperature, rainfall, ventilation, lithology, and sensor controls.
- Electrical and magnetic anomalies require cultural-noise, infrastructure, electrode, grounding, magnetospheric, lightning, and instrument-health controls.
- Luminous reports require power-grid, transformer, lightning, auroral, astronomical, camera-artifact, and timing controls before a tectonic interpretation is entertained.

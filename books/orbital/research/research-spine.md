# ORBITAL — RESEARCH SPINE

This is a working evidence scaffold, not a finished bibliography.

## Verified anchor facts

### Commercial procurement was a deliberate policy shift
NASA describes Commercial Crew as a model in which companies own and operate their spacecraft and infrastructure while NASA buys transportation services and sets safety requirements. NASA's history of COTS describes the 2005 creation of the Commercial Crew and Cargo Program Office and a two-stage model: help companies develop and demonstrate capability, then purchase operational cargo services.

Sources:
- NASA, “Commercial Crew Program Essentials”
- NASA, “10 Years Ago: The First Operational Cygnus Cargo Mission to the Space Station”

### LEO is now heavily populated by commercial constellations
ESA's 2025 Space Environment Report says commercial constellations continue to increase in scale, with approximately 11,000 active payloads in Earth orbit as of the end of 2024 and crowding concentrated in LEO. ESA's 2026 report says 2025 saw more than 300 launches and more than 4,000 new payloads added to the space environment.

Sources:
- ESA Space Environment Report 2025
- ESA Space Environment Report, Edition 10 (2026)

### One commercial system now constitutes a very large fraction of active lower-LEO payloads
Jonathan McDowell's space statistics page, data as of 13 August 2026, lists 10,742 active Starlink satellites in “Lower LEO” out of 14,031 total active payloads in that region—roughly 76.6 percent by count under his categorization.

Source:
- Jonathan's Space Report / planet4589.org, Space Statistics, active payloads

### Orbital sustainability is not keeping pace with growth
ESA's 2025 report describes Earth orbit as a finite resource, notes increasing collision-avoidance requirements and says current mitigation is insufficient to stop debris growth. The 2026 edition states that extrapolating current trends produces an environment-risk index four times the long-term sustainability threshold used in the report.

Sources:
- ESA Space Environment Report 2025
- ESA Space Environment Report, Edition 10 (2026)

## Thesis guardrails
- Do not write that private industry literally “owns LEO.” It does not. Use “dominates activity,” “controls a growing share of infrastructure,” or specific metrics.
- Do not collapse “commercial” and “independent of government.” The entire thesis depends on showing their interdependence.
- Distinguish SpaceX's launch dominance from the broader commercialization of LEO.
- Distinguish satellite count from economic value, mass, strategic importance, revenue, or spectrum control.
- Avoid treating every corporate decision as one individual's whim; identify actual governance and contractual mechanisms.
- Treat Ukraine/Starlink claims with source-by-source chronology because public accounts have changed and disputed details matter.

## Title note
Samantha Harvey's novel *Orbital* (Jonathan Cape, 2023) won the Booker Prize in 2024. For a trade nonfiction proposal, the working title can remain *Orbital* internally, but alternate titles should be tested before submission to reduce confusion in bookselling, search, publicity, and rights conversations.

# What Would Count as Causal Proof

The field needs a chain strong enough that removing one link changes the prediction before the experiment is run.

For a cryptochrome compass, causal proof would look something like this. Identify the relevant cell population. Show that its molecules are ordered in a geometry capable of directional sensitivity. Measure magnetic-field-dependent reaction products at geomagnetic strength in the cellular environment. Manipulate the residues or cofactors responsible for the spin chemistry without destroying unrelated visual function. Show that the animal's magnetic orientation changes in the predicted way. Rescue the molecule and recover the behavior.

Better still, alter a molecular parameter that changes a predicted radiofrequency resonance or directional response. If the behavior shifts with it, physics and organism become one experiment.

A magnetite system needs an equivalent chain: identify particles with a reproducible morphology and cellular location, show mechanical coupling to a membrane or cytoskeleton, trace the afferent nerve, record field-dependent transduction, selectively disable the structure, and rescue function.

The standards sound severe because the history justifies severity. Beautiful anatomical candidates have died. Behavioral results have varied by laboratory. Magnetic contamination is easy. Coil artifacts are real.

Causal proof is not one spectacular paper. It is a set of interventions that makes alternative explanations increasingly expensive.

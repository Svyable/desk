# WRONG HAND — evidence ledger

## Thesis under test

Terrestrial life is molecularly homochiral, but nonliving extraterrestrial chemistry can generate substantial enantiomeric excesses. Chirality is therefore neither a trivial biosignature nor a binary one. The collision becomes sharper because synthetic biology now contemplates mirror macromolecules and, in principle, mirror organisms: the same molecular property used to search for alien life may become something humans can manufacture. The book asks what pattern of handedness would actually distinguish biology, prebiotic chemistry, contamination, and engineering.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- Known terrestrial proteins use overwhelmingly L-amino acids, while nucleic-acid sugars are D-handed. Homochirality is central to molecular recognition and biological polymers.
- Carbonaceous meteorites contain indigenous extraterrestrial organic compounds, including amino acids. Some meteoritic amino acids show measurable enantiomeric excesses.
- Cronin & Pizzarello reported L-excesses in alpha-methyl amino acids from the Murchison meteorite; related work has investigated isovaline and other meteoritic compounds.
- NASA astrobiology work explicitly notes that large non-biological enantiomeric excesses in primitive meteorites complicate using chiral asymmetry alone as a definitive biosignature on Mars.
- Mirror proteins and other mirror macromolecules are an active area of chemistry/biotechnology. A 2024 interdisciplinary technical assessment argued that self-replicating mirror bacteria should not be created because of potentially severe immunological and ecological risks. The assessment explicitly distinguishes mirror organisms from useful work on individual mirror molecules.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Meteorite parent-body aqueous alteration appears correlated with isovaline L-excess in some samples, supporting chemical amplification mechanisms, but the origin and generality of extraterrestrial asymmetry remain active questions.
- Exogenous delivery of chiral organic molecules may have contributed to terrestrial homochirality. Contribution is plausible; historical causation is not directly recoverable.
- Chiral measurements could become powerful components of Mars or ocean-world life detection when combined with molecular distributions, isotopes, context, chain of custody, and independent biosignatures.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- A terrestrial 'shadow biosphere' using opposite chirality has not been established.
- Chiral excess by itself does not establish biology; meteorites provide the obvious counterexample.
- No self-replicating mirror bacterium is documented in the sources used here. Risk assessments concern a future capability, not an organism already in existence.
- No extraterrestrial biology—left-handed, right-handed, mixed-handed, or otherwise—is established by the sources used here.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

### Chirality stack
A candidate biosignature should be evaluated as a distribution across molecular families, isotopes, polymer structure, environmental context and energetic plausibility—not as one enantiomeric excess number.

### Alienness gradient
'Matches terrestrial handedness' and 'opposes terrestrial handedness' should not receive symmetrical interpretation. Same-handed organics have contamination and shared-prebiotic-chemistry alternatives; a coherent opposite-handed biochemistry would face a different counterfeit library. This does not make opposite chirality proof of alien life, but it changes the prior work required of contamination models.

### Manufactured alien
Once humans can synthesize increasingly complete mirror biochemical systems, 'biochemistry unlike terrestrial life' ceases to mean 'not made on Earth.' Future astrobiology will need provenance tests that distinguish extraterrestrial biology from terrestrial engineering.

## Contradictions and traps

- **Life is homochiral / meteorites can be asymmetric:** this is the central productive contradiction. Homochirality may be biologically amplified without every asymmetric precursor being biological.
- **Opposite hand feels decisive / contamination remains possible:** laboratory reagents, synthetic mirror molecules and future engineered systems create new contamination classes.
- **Mirror life is discussed seriously / mirror organisms do not yet exist:** policy concern must not be rewritten as demonstrated capability.
- **Abiotic chemistry often tends toward racemic mixtures / nature can break symmetry:** do not use 'abiotic = 50/50' as an absolute rule.

## Disinformation / institutional incentives

No classified-program or state-disinformation claim is needed for the core thesis. Incentives here are scientific and institutional: biosignature enthusiasm rewards specificity; planetary-protection systems reward conservative provenance; biotechnology risk assessment rewards anticipation before capability exists. None should be translated automatically into bad faith.

## Publication holds

- Do not call chirality a definitive biosignature.
- Do not claim mirror bacteria exist.
- Do not claim mirror organisms would certainly evade all immunity or destroy ecosystems; preserve the technical report's uncertainty and scenario structure.
- Do not imply meteoritic L-excess proves meteorites caused terrestrial homochirality.
- Do not use 'alien' for any measured sample unless provenance independently supports extraterrestrial origin.

## Defeat condition

The book's strongest astrobiology claim weakens if broad, well-characterized abiotic systems routinely generate stable, polymer-scale, mutually compatible homochiral networks indistinguishable from metabolism-capable biology across molecular families. Conversely, the claim that opposite-handed coherent biochemistry would be unusually diagnostic weakens as human mirror-biochemistry production becomes common enough that engineered contamination is a serious mission-level alternative.

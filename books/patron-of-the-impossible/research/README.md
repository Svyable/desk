# Research trail

This folder is the working evidentiary spine for *Patron of the Impossible*.

The manuscript separates four claim classes throughout:

1. **Documented / verified** — primary public records, official releases, contemporaneous documents, flight events, contracts, or other independently checkable facts.
2. **Credible but incomplete** — serious testimony, participant recollection, reporting, or inference that has not been independently closed by the public record.
3. **Disputed / unconfirmed** — allegations or interpretations without adequate independent confirmation, or claims materially contested by other evidence.
4. **Speculative synthesis** — proposed mechanisms or connective theories that may generate tests but are not observations.

The files here are designed to make those boundaries auditable rather than decorative. `source-log.md` lists live primary and high-value links. `evidence-matrix.md` assigns important claims to an evidence class. `chronology.md` separates contemporaneous events from later retelling. `contradictions-and-incentives.md` records disagreements and the incentives surrounding them. `document-appendix.md` identifies the key archival objects. `defeat-conditions.md` states what evidence would materially weaken the book's thesis. `research-appendix.md` is the unfinished work queue. `bibliography.md` is a reading list, not an authority list.

No file in this folder implies access to non-public classified material.

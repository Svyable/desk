# Research appendix: unfinished work queue

The first-pass manuscript is structurally complete but not publication-gate complete. Expansion should come from original records, experiment detail, and counterevidence rather than repeated epistemic framing.

## 1. Build the Bigelow pre-NIDS business chronology from primary records

Recover contemporaneous Nevada corporate/property records, Budget Suites expansion history, interviews from the period, and credible financial reporting. Verify the frequently repeated origin details about early apartment financing rather than relying on later profiles.

## 2. Reconstruct NIDS from its own archive

Highest-value missing source set. Seek lawfully public or donated NIDS case files, website captures, advisory-board records, protocols, field logs, instrument specifications, case triage criteria, rejected-case counts, laboratory reports, and null results. Build a case denominator rather than a highlight reel.

## 3. Skinwalker Ranch control reconstruction

For the Bigelow/NIDS period, create an event ledger with date, observer, first statement, instruments, environmental measurements, physical samples, alternative hypotheses, outside labs, and final classification. Compare livestock pathology against regional veterinary/predation baselines. Separate events directly observed by NIDS personnel from events reported by prior owners or later retellings.

## 4. Black-triangle source reconstruction

Locate the original NIDS triangle database/reporting, contemporaneous witness files where public, solved cases, military/astronomical correlations, and any statistical or geographic analysis. Do not use “black triangles” as a unitary vehicle class without demonstrating that the reports cluster physically rather than narratively.

## 5. Complete AAWSAP procurement chain

Acquire and line up: solicitation; BAASS proposal; technical and cost volumes; award; full SOW; contract modifications; option-year documents; project management plans; invoice categories where releasable; monthly status reports; program-manager correspondence; final disposition. This is the best route to resolving the AARO-versus-participant scope dispute.

## 6. DIRD-by-DIRD maturity audit

For every released Defense Intelligence Reference Document, record author, affiliation, date, topic, primary literature used, publication/review path, central claims, technology readiness, what was experimentally demonstrated at the time, and what has happened in the field since. Avoid treating the portfolio as uniformly fringe or uniformly validated.

## 7. Reid / SAP reconstruction

Build the complete chain around the June 24, 2009 request and November 13, 2009 review: who drafted supporting language, what briefings preceded it, what specific risks were cited, who had classification authority, whether any later protection was granted under a different mechanism, and what contemporaneous records say about the reason for denial.

## 8. AAWSAP / AATIP institutional genealogy

Separate formal program authority, appropriated/contract funding, personnel continuity, informal activity, reporting channels, and later public nomenclature. Reconcile AARO's 2024 history with direct documentary evidence and participant accounts without deciding by prestige.

## 9. KONA BLUE primary file

Use the DHS/AARO release to reconstruct exactly what was proposed, what hypothetical materials or missions the documents contemplated, what approvals were sought, why the project did not become an operational SAP, and how later retellings transformed proposal language into claims of possession.

## 10. Bigelow Aerospace technical history

Expand Genesis I/II and BEAM chapters from NASA technical papers, pressure/leak data, micrometeoroid and orbital-debris assessments, radiation measurements, material architecture, deployment mechanics, the May 2016 expansion anomaly, inspection results, and ownership/operations changes after Bigelow Aerospace's shutdown. Treat this as engineering history, not founder mythology.

## 11. Bigelow's private evidentiary threshold

Collect long-form interviews across decades and map changes in Bigelow's public confidence. Identify what evidence he personally cited as decisive, what remained private, and whether his stated model evolved from UFO interest to a broader consciousness/non-human framework.

## 12. BICS essay-corpus adversarial review

Do not summarize 29 winners as one evidence stream. Code every major evidence class invoked: near-death experiences, mediumship, reincarnation cases, apparitions, physical mediumship, laboratory psi, philosophy of mind. For each, identify strongest primary studies and strongest methodological criticisms.

## 13. Mediumship replication matrix

Build a study-level table: participants, sitter/proxy design, blinding level, target-selection method, information leakage controls, scoring procedure, preregistration, attrition, effect size, full miss reporting, analyst independence, and replication status. Then compare BICS application-oriented challenges to the strongest foundational existence tests.

## 14. Near-death experience literature

Deep-audit prospective cardiac-arrest research, including timing of experiences, physiological monitoring, veridical-perception targets where used, survival/selection effects, memory timing, and alternative neurophysiological models. Do not turn the absence of a full neural explanation into survival evidence automatically, or the existence of neural correlates into proof of non-survival.

## 15. Reincarnation-case literature

Audit cases investigated before target-family contact, record-locking procedures, matching methods, cultural selection, ordinary information pathways, and independent replication. Separate historically striking cases from population-level inference.

## 16. Archive and interview plan

Potential interview categories: former NIDS staff/advisers; Bigelow Aerospace engineers; NASA BEAM personnel; DIA/AAWSAP contracting and technical personnel; AARO historians/analysts; former program participants who dispute AARO; BICS judges, contest winners, mediums, and critics. Interviews should begin with documents and exact chronology, not invitation to retell familiar stories.

## 17. Quantitative provenance graph

For major retrieval, Skinwalker, and UAP-program claims, graph direct observers, intermediaries, briefing relationships, publications, and institutional reuse. Count independent roots, not repetitions.

## Research literatures requiring full primary-paper expansion

- Prospective near-death experience research during cardiac arrest.
- Blinded/proxy mediumship experiments and independent replications.
- Reincarnation-case methods, especially cases documented before target-family contact.
- UAP sensor analysis, geometry, military reporting, and misidentification literature.
- Human perception under low-information aerial-observation conditions.
- Exotic propulsion / general-relativity literature underlying specific DIRDs.
- Metamaterials and cloaking research underlying specific DIRDs.
- Directed-energy and electromagnetic bioeffects literature where human-effects claims are made.
- Research-governance literature on private patronage, publication bias, preregistration, adversarial collaboration, and archival preservation.

## Bibliographic rule

A source earns weight from proximity to the event, independent custody, methodological quality, and corroboration—not from whether it supports or attacks the book's thesis.

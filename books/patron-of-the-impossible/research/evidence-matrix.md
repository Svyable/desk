# Evidence matrix

## 1. Documented / verified

- Robert and Diane Bigelow gave UNLV $3.7 million in 1997 to establish the Bigelow Chair of Consciousness Studies; Charles Tart was named the first chair. Primary: UNLV archive releases.
- Bigelow Aerospace was founded in 1999 and developed expandable spacecraft based partly on NASA expandable-habitat technology licensed from the agency. NASA records document the relationship and later flight work.
- Genesis I launched in 2006 and Genesis II in 2007. NASA histories describe both as Bigelow expandable-habitat demonstrations.
- NASA awarded Bigelow Aerospace a $17.8 million contract in January 2013 to provide BEAM for an ISS technology demonstration. BEAM launched, was installed, expanded in 2016, and remained in use beyond the initial demonstration period. Primary: NASA.
- DIA issued the Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications Program solicitation and awarded contract HHM402-08-C-0072 to Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies. Primary: DIA FOIA reading room.
- DIA's 2009 contract-status material described BAASS performance as excellent, noted 26 detailed research reports by June 30, 2009—twice the minimum requirement—and described reviews as overwhelmingly positive. Primary: DIA.
- Senator Harry Reid requested restricted special-access protection for the advanced aerospace effort in a June 24, 2009 letter. Primary: DIA-released copy.
- DIA reviewed and declined the requested restricted SAP in November 2009, finding inadequate justification for that level of protection for the program as proposed. Primary: DIA.
- AARO's 2024 historical report states that AAWSAP ended in 2012, that contractor personnel also pursued UAP/paranormal work including work at a Utah property owned by the contractor's head, and that AARO found no empirical evidence supporting claims that the U.S. Government or private companies had reverse-engineered extraterrestrial technology. These are documented **AARO conclusions**, not independent proof that every contrary allegation is false.
- Robert Bigelow told CBS in 2017 that he was absolutely convinced of an existing extraterrestrial presence. This documents Bigelow's conviction, not the underlying proposition.
- BICS was founded in 2020. Its 2021 essay contest received 1,300 applications and 204 essays from 38 countries, selected 29 winners, and awarded $1.8 million. BICS publicly identifies the top winners, judges, rules, and its own conclusion that the contest established survival beyond a reasonable doubt. This documents BICS's institutional claim, not scientific consensus.
- BICS's 2023 challenge involved 67 mediums, six teams, three countries, nine months, and $390,000 in funding. BICS explicitly says the project assumed an afterlife was real and that mediums could obtain valid information from it. Primary: BICS.

## 2. Credible but incomplete

- Bigelow's account that his grandparents experienced a close UFO encounter near Las Vegas is a family report repeated by Bigelow to CBS. It is credible as testimony about what he was told; the original event is not independently reconstructable from the material presently in hand.
- Participant accounts of NIDS field investigations, individual Skinwalker Ranch events, unusual animal injuries, lights, equipment failures, and other incidents may be sincerely and accurately reported but often lack enough public raw data, control data, or independent custody for outsiders to close the causal question.
- Claims by AAWSAP participants about how broadly the contract was intended to encompass UAP or Skinwalker-related work deserve serious comparison against the solicitation, proposal, contract modifications, program-manager records, and invoices. The public record presently supports competing institutional characterizations.
- Testimony from former officials or contractors alleging additional UAP programs, materials, or knowledge can update a historical assessment depending on access and provenance, but remains incomplete until independently corroborated.
- Some parapsychology, mediumship, near-death, and survival-of-consciousness studies report results that proponents regard as anomalous. Their weight depends on study design, blinding, leakage control, preregistration, replication, publication bias, and independence; this draft has not yet completed a study-by-study adjudication.

## 3. Disputed / unconfirmed

- That Skinwalker Ranch has been shown publicly to host a single exotic physical, paranormal, non-human, or consciousness-linked phenomenon.
- That NIDS possessed decisive physical proof of extraordinary phenomena that remains hidden from the public.
- That AAWSAP, AATIP, the Reid SAP request, or the existence of DIRDs establishes that the U.S. Government confirmed extraterrestrial technology.
- That a restricted special-access program covering the whole AAWSAP effort was approved; the released DIA record supports the opposite for the 2009 request.
- That government planning for KONA BLUE proves recovered anomalous material existed. A proposal or contingency plan is evidence of planning, not of the material contemplated by the plan.
- That the United States possesses recovered non-human craft or biologics, absent independently verifiable public material or documentary evidence sufficient to establish the allegation.
- That the BICS essay contest scientifically established survival of consciousness as a consensus result. It established the institute's affirmative adjudication of submitted essays under its rules.
- That mediumship is sufficiently established to treat all application-oriented BICS results as communication with deceased persons rather than as data requiring continued source validation.

## 4. Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- Bigelow's recurring institutional move can be understood as building privately controlled 'rooms' around questions lacking mainstream institutional homes; this is the book's interpretive thesis, not a claim Bigelow himself made.
- Aerospace, UAP, Skinwalker, anomalous cognition, and survival research might ultimately connect through a deeper model of intelligence or consciousness. No such unified mechanism is established by the fact that Bigelow funded all of them.
- The strongest future anomaly research may resemble flight testing: prospective success/failure criteria, external vetoes, distributed custody, independent replication, published denominators, and automatic archival release.
- Bigelow's private archives, if preserved and eventually opened, may substantially change the historical assessment of NIDS or later programs in either direction.

## Handling rule

A claim may move between classes only when the provenance changes. Repetition, prestige, government interest, classification, or association with a technically successful person does not by itself move a claim upward.

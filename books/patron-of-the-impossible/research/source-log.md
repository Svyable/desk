## Source-handling notes

- Prefer the DIA documents over later summaries for procurement, performance, and SAP claims.
- Prefer NASA over Bigelow promotional material for flight-performance chronology.
- Prefer BICS's own site when describing what BICS claims; do not treat its institutional conclusion as independent validation.
- Prefer contemporaneous first statements and raw records for NIDS/Skinwalker cases. Participant books and interviews are important but belong to a lower evidence class when underlying records are unavailable.
- For any modern retrieval/reverse-engineering allegation, build a source genealogy before counting witnesses or institutions.

# GREEN EYES

## When the landscape becomes the sensor

**Sven Hardy Benson**

A Desk book in progress.

### Thesis

The most interesting military sensor may not look like a sensor. Plants already integrate light, chemistry, water, temperature, pathogens, mechanical disturbance and other features of their environment while powering and repairing themselves. DARPA's Advanced Plant Technologies program made the proposition explicit: engineer plants to detect specified threats and report them remotely. That collision—ordinary biology, synthetic biology, remote observation and national security—creates a hard epistemic problem. A living thing can contain information without that information being readable, specific, timely, attributable or operationally useful.

The book follows the chain **exposure → biological transduction → internal response → engineered report → remote observation → attribution → decision**. Every arrow is a claim.

# Dirty Air — research notes and evidence ledger

## Claim discipline

No atmospheric industrial technosignature has been confirmed on an exoplanet. Detectability studies are model studies, not detections. A molecule must not be promoted from spectral candidate → retrieved abundance → anomalous abundance → industrial source → technology without surviving each inference separately.

## Evidence class 1 — verified/documented

### Remote pollution sensing works on Earth
- NASA TEMPO overview / publication: https://esdpubs.nasa.gov/content/Tropospheric_Emissions_Monitoring_of_Pollution_TEMPO
- NASA/JPL chemical reanalysis: https://tes.jpl.nasa.gov/tes/chemical-reanalysis/
- NASA NTRS, satellite NO2/formaldehyde work: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20090024806

Earth observation establishes the measurement principle only. It does not establish interstellar detectability.

### NASA explicitly treats atmospheric pollution as a technosignature research target
- NASA 2021 NO2 technosignature release: https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-study-to-find-an-extraterrestrial-civilization-pollution-could-be-the-solution/
- NASA Science technosignatures overview: https://science.nasa.gov/universe/search-for-life/searching-for-signs-of-intelligent-life-technosignatures/

The NASA release reports Kopparapu et al.'s modeled result: under their assumptions, Earth-level anthropogenic NO2 around a Sun-like star could be detectable to roughly 30 light-years with about 400 observing hours using a future large visible-light telescope. Preserve the assumptions whenever using the number.

### Atmospheric spectroscopy of exoplanets is real
- NASA Science background on atmospheric biomarkers: https://science.nasa.gov/universe/exoplanets/detecting-biomarkers-on-faraway-exoplanets/
- NASA/Webb atmospheric spectroscopy example: https://science.nasa.gov/missions/webb/nasas-webb-images-young-giant-exoplanets-detects-carbon-dioxide/

Do not imply Webb can presently perform every Earth-analogue technosignature observation discussed in theoretical papers.

## Evidence class 2 — credible but incomplete

- NO2 detectability as modeled by Kopparapu et al.; retrieve journal/arXiv primary before publication.
- CFC detectability in exoplanet atmospheres. Retrieve Lin, Gonzalez Abad & Loeb 2014 primary paper and later reanalyses; quantify abundance multiples, host-star dependence, wavelength bands and instrument assumptions.
- Long-lived synthetic gases as possible technological or terraforming signatures. Need lifetime tables by atmospheric composition and UV environment rather than importing Earth lifetimes uncritically.

## Evidence class 3 — disputed/unconfirmed

- Any current exoplanet claimed to exhibit industrial pollution.
- Any inference from a single unusual atmospheric constituent directly to intelligence.
- Claims that CFCs are universally impossible through nontechnological chemistry. Phrase as no known biologically significant source / strongly artificial in terrestrial context unless primary chemistry review justifies stronger wording.

## Evidence class 4 — speculative synthesis

1. **Dirty-window hypothesis:** atmospheric industrial technosignatures preferentially expose civilizations during environmentally inefficient technological phases.
2. **Clean-civilization invisibility:** mature technology could erase the easiest remotely observable signatures of industry.
3. **Atmospheric archaeology:** sufficiently persistent artificial molecules might outlive the civilization or industrial phase that made them.
4. **Pollution/contact inversion:** first contact may be inference from involuntary waste rather than reception of intentional information.
5. **Terraforming ambiguity:** deliberate atmospheric engineering and uncontrolled pollution can occupy overlapping chemical search spaces.

## Contradiction / alternative-explanation audit

- NO2 has natural production routes, including lightning; excess requires contextual modeling.
- Clouds and aerosols can mimic, mask or degrade retrievals.
- Stellar UV changes molecular lifetime and therefore detectability.
- A detectable synthetic molecule need not imply a planet-wide civilization; production could be localized but atmospheric mixing globalizes the residue.
- Absence of pollution is almost content-free: no civilization, clean civilization, low-output civilization, wrong technology, wrong epoch, inaccessible spectral bands and insufficient sensitivity can all look alike.

## Incentive audit

No known intelligence/disinformation component is central to this thesis. The main distortion incentives are scientific/news novelty, anthropocentric assumptions about alien industry, publication incentives around striking technosignatures, and media compression of modeled detectability into 'NASA can detect aliens by pollution.' The manuscript must keep model → hypothetical observation → actual observation distinct.

## Defeat conditions

The strongest version of the book's selection-effect thesis weakens if:
- modeled industrial pollutants are generically less detectable than deliberate or non-pollution technosignatures across realistic telescope populations;
- atmospheric lifetimes are too short for useful integration at plausible production rates;
- natural false-positive spaces are so broad that industrial attribution remains non-identifiable even with multi-species spectra;
- future technology searches show clean, persistent signatures substantially easier to detect than waste chemistry.

## Primary-source queue

1. Kopparapu et al. NO2 ApJ/arXiv paper — equations, atmospheric model, telescope assumptions.
2. Lin, Gonzalez Abad & Loeb 2014 — CFC detectability, JWST-era assumptions.
3. Schwieterman et al. technosignature review literature — atmospheric taxonomy.
4. Earth CFC atmospheric lifetimes and Montreal Protocol/WMO ozone assessments.
5. Modern exoplanet retrieval false-positive literature.
6. Artificial greenhouse gases / terraforming papers.
7. Telescope concept reports (Habitable Worlds Observatory and predecessor LUVOIR/HabEx) for actual wavelength/sensitivity constraints.
8. Historical Earth satellite pollution retrieval papers as empirical comparator.

## Publication hold

Do not promote beyond early draft until the primary NO2/CFC papers are reconstructed at figure/table level and the manuscript contains at least one quantitative chapter comparing production, lifetime, column abundance, spectral strength, stellar type, distance and observing time.

# Book brief — Dirty Air

## Reader promise

A book about an alien civilization we may first encounter not through a message but through chemistry it failed—or chose not—to contain.

## Why this is a book

Three literatures are usually kept apart: Earth pollution remote sensing, exoplanet atmospheric retrieval, and SETI/technosignatures. Put them together and a strange observational bias appears. Waste can be easier to infer than intent. That means our first technological census may preferentially find civilizations in environmentally conspicuous phases.

The human story is not garnish. We became capable of seeing pollution from space at almost the same historical moment that we became capable of imagining pollution as an interstellar signature. The Montreal Protocol then supplied an accidental experiment in signature suppression: a technological species can recognize a planetary chemical trace and coordinate to reduce it.

## Tone

Concrete, suspicious of easy inference, technically literate without cosplaying as an atmospheric chemist. Follow molecules, instruments and actual observing geometry. Avoid generic 'are we alone?' throat-clearing.

## Recurring distinctions

- molecule / abundance
- abundance / anomaly
- anomaly / source
- industrial source / intelligence
- technology / civilization
- signature / message
- detectable / detected
- Earth analogy / universal rule

## Structural pressure

The middle of the book must become quantitative. Chapters 5–9 should reconstruct NO2 and CFC cases rather than merely describe them. Chapter 10 uses the Montreal Protocol as a real historical case of a civilization deliberately dimming one of its own hypothetical technosignatures. Chapters 12–17 widen into atmospheric archaeology and observational selection effects only after the chemistry earns that move.

## Anti-slop guardrails

Do not repeatedly announce that 'the truth is more complicated.' Show the competing reaction pathways or retrieval degeneracy. Do not call every uncertainty 'profound.' Do not end chapters with inspirational cosmic uplift. Let an ugly molecule carry the metaphysics.

# Beyond the Veil

## Evidence of Non-Sapient Intellect in the Universe

**Sven Hardy Benson**

---

**The first mistake was assuming intelligence had to know that it was intelligent.**

---

This book uses *intellect* in a deliberately narrow sense. It does not mean consciousness, inner experience, language, selfhood, wisdom, or human-style reasoning. It means an observable capacity to use state and feedback to preserve, correct, adapt, coordinate, or solve under changing conditions.

That distinction is the argument.

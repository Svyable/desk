# Release position

*Beyond the Veil* remains a Desk draft until the full-book length and Desk-integrity gates pass.

A manuscript-complete book is not automatically a released edition. The intended sequence is:

1. strengthen and validate the manuscript on Desk;
2. synchronize the Desk catalog/discovery surfaces;
3. land the clean publication candidate on Desk `main`;
4. use the canonical local release helper to prepare an exact Shelf snapshot;
5. verify the Shelf snapshot and publication metadata;
6. commit and land the Shelf release separately.

The Desk copy must not be marked `Published`. The public-facing released edition belongs on Sven Hardy Benson’s Shelf.

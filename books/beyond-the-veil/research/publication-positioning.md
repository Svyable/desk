# Publication positioning

**Working title:** *Beyond the Veil*  
**Subtitle:** *Evidence of Non-Sapient Intellect in the Universe*  
**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson

## Reader promise

A serious science-and-ideas argument that asks readers to separate adaptive competence from consciousness, then follows that distinction from bacteria, immune systems, regeneration, slime molds, and engineered systems into the search for life beyond Earth.

## What makes the book distinctive

The manuscript does not argue that everything is intelligent, that planets are conscious, or that extraterrestrial intelligence has already been found. Its novelty is methodological: it asks what evidence would justify recognizing history-dependent, adaptive, distributed competence on worlds that never produce a technological civilization.

## Publication claim discipline

Marketing language should preserve the distinction between:

1. terrestrial evidence that adaptive competence exists without sapience;
2. the proposed comparative category `non-sapient intellect`;
3. the astrobiological hypothesis that analogous organization could exist elsewhere;
4. the still-open question of whether such organization can be detected remotely.

Do not collapse those four statements into “evidence of alien intelligence.” The title's word **Evidence** refers to evidence for the *category and physical possibility* developed from Earth systems, not a claim of confirmed extraterrestrial detection.

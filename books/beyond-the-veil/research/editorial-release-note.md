# Editorial release note

The publication edit keeps the manuscript's central distinction intact:

**Competence is not consciousness. Evidence of adaptive control is not evidence of a mind.**

The strengthened opening now earns that distinction through mechanism and counterexamples before the manuscript moves into larger biological and astronomical claims. The strengthened ending converts the thesis into tests that can fail.

No release copy should describe the book as reporting confirmed extraterrestrial intelligence or a detected alien biosphere.

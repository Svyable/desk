# Publication copy notes

Potential concise positioning line:

> What if the universe is full of systems that solve problems long before anything evolves that can know it is solving them?

Potential jacket premise:

*Beyond the Veil* begins with a distinction that changes the search for intelligence: competence is not consciousness. On Earth, bacteria compare the present with the recent past, immune systems alter future responses after prior encounters, tissues rebuild anatomy through distributed control, slime molds reconfigure transport networks, and machines can plan and recover without evidence that anything inside them is awake. Sven Hardy Benson follows those mechanisms into astrobiology and asks a harder question than whether aliens can talk to us: what would count as evidence of adaptive intellect on a world that never produced a person?

Avoid marketing formulations that imply confirmed alien detection.

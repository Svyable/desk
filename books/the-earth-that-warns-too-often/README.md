## Thesis

Some earthquakes may be preceded by measurable electromagnetic or allied changes, but a physical antecedent, a statistical association, a calibrated forecast, and a public warning are distinct achievements. The field's strongest modern result may be precisely the awkward one: a modest prospective association can survive improved controls while remaining far too weak for deterministic prediction.

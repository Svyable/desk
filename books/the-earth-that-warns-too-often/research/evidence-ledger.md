# Four-class evidence ledger

## Class 1 — Verified / documented

- VAN was a real Greek research program using ground electric measurements; predictions / warnings were transmitted before some target earthquakes and evaluated publicly in the scientific literature.
- A concentrated 1996 *Geophysical Research Letters* dispute examined VAN's prediction rules and statistics.
- The Corralitos ULF magnetometer recorded unusual behavior before the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake; a later 21-month reanalysis plus station records attributed the celebrated anomaly to sensor-system malfunction.
- The 2004 M6.0 Parkfield earthquake occurred inside a long-running observatory. Electric and magnetic instruments recorded coseismic effects while published analyses reported no convincing short-term preseismic electromagnetic change above background.
- The 1975 Haicheng earthquake was preceded by warnings / evacuation activity and substantial foreshock activity; the 1976 Tangshan catastrophe did not receive a comparable successful short-term warning.
- Laboratory stressed-rock and fluid systems can produce electrical effects under relevant classes of mechanism. This does not establish natural-earthquake prediction.
- DEMETER was a real French satellite mission with seismo-electromagnetic objectives; Swarm and CSES provide later high-quality magnetic / plasma measurements used in earthquake-association studies.
- QuakeFinder operated a long-duration ground magnetometer network.
- Heavlin et al. (2022) used a time-separated train/test design on QuakeFinder data and reported a modest 24–72 hour magnetic association in a small held-out earthquake sample; the paper explicitly did not claim operational prediction.
- The L'Aquila 2009 legal controversy concerned risk evaluation and communication around a swarm; it was not simply a prosecution for scientists failing to predict an earthquake. Six scientists were acquitted on appeal while the civil-protection official's conviction was reduced rather than erased.

## Class 2 — Credible but incomplete

- Some published DEMETER, Swarm, CSES, GNSS, ground magnetic, geoelectric, radon, hydrologic, and animal-behavior studies report pre-earthquake statistical associations under specified conditions.
- Stress-activated positive-hole, electrokinetic, piezomagnetic, permeability / fluid, and atmospheric-coupling mechanisms are physically motivated enough to support targeted experiments.
- Some earthquakes may have heterogeneous preparatory phases whose observability depends on magnitude, depth, lithology, fluids, conductivity, local time, and environmental noise.
- Weak aggregate effects could improve probability estimates without classifying individual earthquakes reliably.

## Class 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

- VAN as a validated independently reproducible operational earthquake-prediction system.
- The original Loma Prieta Corralitos ULF trace as a crustal precursor rather than an instrumental artifact.
- Historical animal, groundwater, radon, lights, or ionospheric observations as reliable general short-term alarms.
- One universal lithosphere-atmosphere-ionosphere coupling chain explaining the heterogeneous precursor literature.
- Existing satellite anomaly methods as a calibrated operational prediction capability.
- Any current claim that machine learning has solved deterministic short-term earthquake prediction.

## Class 4 — Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- The Earth may produce many genuine preparation-like electromagnetic, hydrologic, or biological disturbances, while only a small fraction of those preparations commit to large dynamic rupture. If so, real antecedent physics and poor predictive precision are compatible.
- A weak precursor may be best understood as a risk factor rather than a countdown.
- Cross-region, cross-sensor prospective registries may reveal lithology- or mechanism-specific precursor subclasses rather than one universal signal.
- Failure of deterministic prediction may reflect a late or spatially small 'commitment' transition even when broader fault state changes are measurable earlier.

## Rule for promotion

No item moves from Class 2 or 3 into Class 1 merely because it is repeated in reviews, appears in an official mission rationale, or is compatible with a mechanism. Promotion requires direct documentation of the narrower claim being made, with prospective or independently replicated evidence where the claim concerns prediction.

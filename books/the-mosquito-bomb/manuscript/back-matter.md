# Back Matter

## A note on method

This manuscript uses a four-class evidence system throughout: documented fact; credible but incomplete reporting; disputed or unconfirmed allegation; speculative synthesis.

The source log favors official histories, declassified records, treaty bodies, peer-reviewed scholarship and institutional guidance. Popular retellings of code-named U.S. mosquito operations are used only as discovery aids unless their claims can be traced to military or scholarly sources.

No operational biological-weapons instructions are provided. Historical delivery concepts are described only to the level necessary to understand the engineering and epistemic argument.

## Central boundary

The existence of a military research program does not prove battlefield use. The existence of a beneficial mosquito-release program does not imply military intent. Similar biological mechanisms can live inside radically different institutional and moral systems.

## Defeat condition for the book's central thesis

If historical operational records demonstrated that vector-based biological effects could be scheduled, bounded, attributed, protected against blowback and reproduced across diverse field environments with reliability comparable to conventional delivery systems, the book's claim that ecology is a dominant command-and-control constraint would need major revision.

## Use boundaries

- Patents, popular histories and Wikipedia are discovery aids only, not efficacy evidence.
- Partial declassified reproductions are identified as partial.
- Korean War allegations are treated as disputed historical claims, with later Soviet/Chinese archival material weighted heavily because it bears directly on fabrication.
- Modern Wolbachia, GM mosquito and gene-drive work is not characterized as weapon activity absent evidence of military intent or prohibited purpose.

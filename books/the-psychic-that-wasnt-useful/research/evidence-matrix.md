## Contradictions and incentives

- **Program advocates:** incentives include continued funding, scientific priority and salience of dramatic sessions.
- **Operational users:** may overremember useful fragments under crisis conditions, but also have unusually strong incentives to reject unreliable collection.
- **Skeptical evaluators:** correctly demand ordinary methodological explanations be exhausted; may nevertheless apply different evidentiary thresholds to anomalous claims than to small effects in conventional psychology.
- **Declassification:** reveals that programs existed while selectively preserving context; secrecy is neither evidence for efficacy nor evidence against it.
- **Media/memoir ecology:** rewards spectacular hits and named psychics, systematically underrepresenting denominators and mundane failures.

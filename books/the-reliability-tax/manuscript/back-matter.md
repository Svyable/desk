# Back Matter

The Reliability Tax began with a narrow irritation: the way organizations call resilience “waste” during calm periods and “obvious” after failure.

The book's claim is not that every backup deserves preservation. It is that optionality has economic value and should be accounted for before crisis makes that value painfully visible.

For readers who want to inspect the factual scaffolding behind the manuscript, the Desk edition includes a research brief and source trail under `research/`.

Sven Hardy Benson

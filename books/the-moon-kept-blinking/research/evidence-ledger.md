# Evidence ledger — THE MOON KEPT BLINKING

## Governing thesis

Lunar transient phenomena provide a long natural experiment in anomaly handling. Reports preceded adequate instrumentation; NASA then funded a detector specifically for temporary lunar color changes; later landed/orbital sensors established several real transient lunar processes; modern dual-telescope monitoring routinely validates impact flashes. The book asks which old anomaly claims survive translation into new measurement regimes.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

1. NASA CR-630, **Project Moon-Blink** (1966), documents development of a Moon-Blink detector and a lunar telescopic color-surveillance program. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19660030253
2. Cameron & Gilheany, *Icarus* (1967), **Operation Moon Blink and report of observations of lunar transient phenomena**, documents the observing program and temporary-color detection effort. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19670055806
3. Burley & Middlehurst, NASA TM X-55468 (1966), **Apparent lunar activity — Historical review**, assembled historical surface-event reports through 1965. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19660016082
4. Winifred S. Cameron, NASA TM-79399 (1978), **Lunar transient phenomena catalog**, lists 1,468 descriptions of reported events from the literature. This is a report catalog, not 1,468 verified physical lunar events. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19780022214
5. NASA/Lunar Science Institute's 1976 symposium proceedings treated moonquakes, transient phenomena, radiogenic-gas emission and possible physical mechanisms as legitimate research questions. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19770059461
6. Freeman & Hills (1991) revisited the Apollo 14 SIDE water-vapor event and concluded it was probably mission-associated contamination, a useful case where a real instrumental transient acquired a less exotic explanation. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/19920030338
7. NASA Meteoroid Environment Office now monitors lunar impact flashes with two telescopes; simultaneous detection helps reject cosmic rays/noise. NASA reports hundreds of flashes since monitoring began. https://www.nasa.gov/meteoroid-environment-office/lunar-impact-monitoring/
8. The 17 March 2013 bright impact flash was followed by identification of the fresh crater in Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter imagery: a strong example of cross-instrument closure. https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20150016525

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Individual historical reports with named observers, dates, locations and repeat observations can be evidentially better than anonymous anecdotes without thereby establishing a lunar cause.
- Correlations between some transient-report locations and later gas/radon observations deserve careful reconstruction, but selection effects, catalog construction and spatial multiple-comparison problems must be quantified before causal promotion.
- Operation Moon Blink's reported detections require event-level reconstruction: telescope, observer, filter state, comparison observer, duration, seeing, lunar geometry, photographic record and independent confirmation.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- That the historical TLP catalog represents a single physical phenomenon.
- That specific color reports establish active lunar volcanism.
- That unexplained historical lights imply artificial structures, spacecraft, mining, non-human activity or concealed lunar operations.
- That NASA suppressed a confirmed extraordinary lunar phenomenon.
- That a report remaining unexplained after archival review is evidence for a preferred exotic cause.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

**Instrumental translation:** an anomaly earns weight when a prediction survives transfer from one measurement regime to another without being rewritten after the result.

**Anomaly migration:** better instruments often do not simply eliminate anomalies. They repartition them. Some disappear as artifacts; some become ordinary phenomena; some survive in a narrower residue.

**Observer tax:** every claim inherits failure modes from the observer/instrument chain. Human color perception, atmospheric seeing and telescope optics carry a different tax than CCD coincidence, spectroscopy or orbital before/after imaging.

**Survival test:** pre-register the old claim in variables a newer instrument can actually measure. Score survival only when the predicted location/time/spectrum/morphology exceeds matched controls. If the claim must be redescribed after every instrument change, it has not survived translation.

## Contradictions and incentives to track

- NASA institutional interest does not certify the phenomenon; funding an instrument proves that a question was considered worth testing.
- A large catalog can amplify reporting ecology as easily as physical incidence. Need denominators: observer-hours, lunar illumination, telescope aperture, site, era, publication channel.
- Apollo/LRO evidence of a geologically and exogenically active Moon makes *some transient processes exist* true; it does not retrospectively validate any particular nineteenth- or twentieth-century report.
- Modern impact flashes are not a universal explanation for historical red glows or apparent obscurations. Duration, spectrum and morphology matter.
- A failed explanation is not evidence for an exotic alternative.

## Quantitative extraction burden

Before publication, reconstruct: Moon-Blink observing hours and event denominator; detector/filter bandpasses and switching cadence; event duration and angular extent; catalog counts by site/era/observer and duplicate handling; seeing/libration/illumination geometry; Apollo radon/gas event localization uncertainties; modern impact-monitoring duty cycle, detection threshold and false-positive rejection; crater-confirmed flash denominator.

## Publication holds

Do not say `NASA detected lunar color changes` until the 1966/1967 event records and instrument logic are read at event level. Do not turn 1,468 descriptions into 1,468 events. Do not imply a causal chain from old visual reports to radon, moonquakes or impacts without location/time/statistical closure. Patents are irrelevant unless used only as evidence of filing. No extraterrestrial/artificial explanation enters Class 2 without physical evidence independent of interpretation.

## Reader promise

Twenty absurd-sounding events in which the strange thing actually happened: children carried as parcel post, guided missiles delivering letters, a psychologist training pigeons to steer weapons, a CIA cat equipped to listen, phantom armies assembled from rubber, a real radioactive reactor aboard an aircraft, and a naturally operating nuclear reactor discovered in ancient rock. The book's stories will be bounded by documents, museum objects, government investigations, and primary research. No invented dialogue, witnessed scenes, motives, first-person interviews, or speculative episodes will be presented as fact.

**Evidence rule:** Existence, performance, proposal, rumor and later interpretation are different states. Every chapter should tell readers which state its central claim occupies, why we know it, and what popular embellishments remain unproved. This is narrative nonfiction, not a stack of trivia or a catalogue of conspiracies.

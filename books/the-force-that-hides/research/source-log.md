# Source Log

Checked September 10, 2026.

1. Fischbach et al., **Reanalysis of the Eötvös Experiment**, *Physical Review Letters* 56, 3 (1986). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.56.3
   - Primary source for the 1986 composition-correlated fifth-force claim. Treat as historical positive claim, not established force.

2. Chu & Dicke, **New Force or Thermal Gradient in the Eötvös Experiment?**, *Physical Review Letters* 57, 1823 (1986). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.57.1823
   - Primary counterargument. The authors calculate that thermal-gradient systematics can account for the Eötvös-Pekár-Fekete data; this is an explanatory challenge to the fifth-force interpretation, not by itself an experimental proof that temperature caused the historical residual.

3. Bennett, **Modulated-Source Eötvös Experiment at Little Goose Lock**, *Physical Review Letters* 62, 365 (1989). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.62.365
   - Direct experimental test using a large movable water mass; null fifth-force result.

4. Spero et al., **Test of the Gravitational Inverse-Square Law at Laboratory Distances**, *Physical Review Letters* 44, 1645 (1980). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.44.1645

5. Hoskins et al., **Experimental tests of the gravitational inverse-square law for mass separations from 2 to 105 cm**, *Physical Review D* 32, 3084 (1985). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.32.3084

6. Tu et al., **Null Test of Newtonian Inverse-Square Law at Submillimeter Range with a Dual-Modulation Torsion Pendulum**, *Physical Review Letters* 98, 201101 (2007). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.98.201101

7. Upadhye, Gubser & Khoury, **Unveiling chameleon fields in tests of the gravitational inverse-square law**, *Physical Review D* 74, 104024 (2006). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.74.104024
   - Theory-to-experiment translation for nonlinear screening.

8. Elder et al., **Chameleon dark energy and atom interferometry**, *Physical Review D* 94, 044051 (2016). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.94.044051

9. **Testing sub-gravitational forces on atoms from a miniature in-vacuum source mass**, *Nature Physics* 13 (2017). https://www.nature.com/articles/nphys4189
   - Direct miniature-source gravitational measurement and screened-force constraints.

10. Panda et al., **Measuring gravitational attraction with a lattice atom interferometer**, *Nature* 631, 515–520 (2024). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-07561-3

11. Panda et al., **Coherence limits in lattice atom interferometry at the one-minute scale**, *Nature Physics* 20, 1234–1239 (2024). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-024-02518-9

12. Touboul et al., **MICROSCOPE Mission: Final Results of the Test of the Equivalence Principle**, *Physical Review Letters* 129, 121102 (2022). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.129.121102

13. **Experiments with levitated force sensor challenge theories of dark energy**, *Nature Physics* (2022). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-022-01706-9
   - Strong constraint on the basic cosmologically viable chameleon model tested.

14. Cong et al., **Spin-dependent exotic interactions**, *Reviews of Modern Physics* 97, 025005 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1103/RevModPhys.97.025005

15. Feleppa et al., **Bounds on Screened Dark Energy from Near-Earth Space-Based Measurements**, *Physical Review Letters* 136, 101002 (published March 11, 2026). https://doi.org/10.1103/gss2-qpp1

16. Banks et al., **Searching for screened scalar forces with long-baseline atom interferometers**, *Physical Review D* 113, 084047 (published April 22, 2026). https://doi.org/10.1103/3486-4m4t
   - Proposal; projected reach must not be described as achieved experimental sensitivity.

17. Eöt-Wash Group, **Cryogenic Balance**. https://www.npl.washington.edu/eotwash/cryogenic-balance
   - Current apparatus/program context; institutional rather than independent evidence.

18. Neufeld, **Upper Limit on Any Intermediate-Range Force Associated with Baryon Number**, *Physical Review Letters* 56, 2344 (1986). https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.56.2344
   - Contemporary counterevidence using Kreuzer's 1968 experiment: an upper limit about half the strength inferred in the Fischbach reanalysis, while still statistically compatible with it at roughly two standard deviations under Neufeld's interpretation.

19. Fischbach et al., **Long-range forces and the Eötvös experiment**, *Annals of Physics* 182, 1–89 (1988). https://doi.org/10.1016/0003-4916(88)90294-1
   - Detailed defense and expansion of the EPF reanalysis. Important counterweight to a too-clean retrospective story: the authors argued that no known conventional mechanism, including proposed alternatives, adequately explained the composition correlation.

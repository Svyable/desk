# Contradiction and Incentive Audit

## Contradiction 1: the sound is recorded, the proposed field is not

The strongest single tension in the literature is the 1998 Mongolia result. The campaign recorded two concurrent low-frequency acoustic events under purpose-built conditions, but the field receivers did not record coincident ELF/VLF radiation for those two events in their covered band. The authors noted that the dominant acoustic frequencies lay below the receiver cutoff and proposed an unmeasured lower-frequency driver.

**Handling:** preserve both facts in the same paragraph whenever the events are used as evidence. “Instrumental detection of electrophonic sounds” must not silently become “instrumental detection of electromagnetic meteor sound generation.”

## Contradiction 2: some meteor VLF studies report positives; others do not

Low-frequency meteor-emission studies are not uniformly positive. The natural background is noisy, receiver bands differ, meteor populations differ, and event-selection criteria vary.

**Handling:** no claim of general meteor VLF emission without a denominator and protocol normalization. Build a positive/null ledger in the expansion pass.

## Contradiction 3: the cleanest modern alternative does not explain every reported event automatically

Photoacoustic coupling has direct experimental support, but it demands sufficient rapidly modulated optical irradiance and a suitable local absorber. Some historical pop reports involve meteors that appear dimmer than the regime emphasized by the 2017 model.

**Handling:** do not present photoacoustics as a universal solution. Quantitatively reconstruct brightness/modulation for candidate events where data exist.

## Contradiction 4: ionospheric triggering is physically suggestive but observationally sparse

Meteor trails interact with ionized upper-atmospheric systems, and at least one meteor/sprite/jet sequence has been published. Broader studies do not establish a simple correlation between meteor activity and transient luminous events.

**Handling:** distinguish “meteors can participate in upper-atmospheric electrical phenomena” from “the Mongolia sounds were produced by a meteor-triggered ionospheric discharge.” The latter is conjecture.

## Contradiction 5: many bright meteors are silent

A mechanism that relies only on brightness fails to explain the overwhelming silence of qualifying events. Geometry, modulation spectrum, atmospheric state, material environment or human threshold must matter.

**Handling:** silent events are not background clutter; they are a central denominator. Future chapters should give them more space than the memorable positives.

## Contradiction 6: witness specificity helps and hurts

Descriptions such as hair, wires, leaves or nearby structures seeming to make the sound are consistent with local transduction. They are also exactly the kind of vivid details that can be selected and repeated after a mechanism becomes popular.

**Handling:** date the report relative to mechanism publication whenever possible. Pre-theory reports carry different evidentiary weight from post-theory prompted surveys.

## Incentive / bias map

### Witnesses

No general deception incentive is assumed. Main risks are expectation, memory reconstruction, social transmission, desire to provide a complete account and confusion between immediate and delayed noises.

### Anomaly researchers

Positive rare events are publishable and memorable. A small field can develop theory attachment. This is not evidence of misconduct; it is a reason to demand preregistration, denominators and negative-event reporting.

### Skeptics

A timing contradiction can encourage premature dismissal if the critic assumes acoustic propagation is the only possible route. The historical stigma of anomalous reports can lower curiosity before the source/transducer chain is analyzed.

### Popular media

Strong incentive toward “mystery solved” stories. Both Keay's model and the later photoacoustic model are vulnerable to being presented as final answers rather than mechanisms with different evidence burdens.

### Laboratories and institutions

Sandia affiliation makes the 2017 work easy to over-awe. Institutional prestige establishes provenance, not natural-event causation. The same rule applies to university and government atmospheric-physics groups.

## Disinformation assessment

Unlike intelligence-adjacent Desk books, there is no current evidence that state disinformation materially shaped the electrophonic-meteor literature. Do not import secrecy incentives where they are absent. The relevant epistemic distortions are ordinary ones: witness memory, publication selection, mechanism attachment, instrumentation limits and popular simplification.

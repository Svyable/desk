## Thesis

Project West Ford was a technically serious Cold War attempt to manufacture a dependable radio-reflecting medium by placing hundreds of millions of microscopic copper dipoles into orbit. Its deeper significance is institutional: before anyone had a mature law of orbital debris, planetary-scale environmental alteration, or international stewardship of near-Earth space, one military communications problem forced scientists, astronomers, diplomats, and engineers to argue over who could deliberately modify a shared sky, under what evidence, and with what obligations if the experiment did not cleanly disappear.

# Working Notes

## Working title

**Needles in Heaven** avoids the banned title construction beginning with `The` and names the physical absurdity without implying the needles were visible as a literal ring.

## Core empirical collision

1. Military demand for survivable long-range communication.
2. Microwave engineering using resonant orbital dipoles.
3. Astronomical dependence on an observational commons.
4. Early orbital-debris persistence from imperfect dispersal.
5. Immature international governance of deliberate environmental modification in space.

## Sentence to earn

A communications experiment became an environmental-governance experiment because the medium it engineered belonged to everyone who needed the sky for something else.

Do not place that sentence before the technical evidence earns it.

# Defeat Conditions

The book's central synthesis weakens materially if primary records show any of the following:

- Astronomical opposition arose only after the 1963 deployment rather than materially influencing pre-launch governance.
- The project made no meaningful attempt to model dipole lifetime, dispersal, or scientific interference.
- The surviving clump/debris story proves to be unsupported by NASA/ESA technical records.
- The 1963 communications demonstration was too weak or anomalous to count as proof of practical feasibility even within the project's stated experimental objective.
- Primary diplomatic records show West Ford had essentially no relevance to broader debates over shared-space use, leaving the governance interpretation dependent only on later analogy.
- Active satellite communications had already rendered West Ford technically irrational before its core design decisions, rather than becoming a superior competing path during development.

If these conditions appear, narrow the book. Do not rescue the thesis with rhetoric.

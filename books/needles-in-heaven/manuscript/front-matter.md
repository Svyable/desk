# Needles in Heaven

Project West Ford, Copper Dipoles, and the First Fight Over a Shared Sky

Sven Hardy Benson

A draft about a project that sounds fabricated until the museum artifact, the IEEE papers, the NASA debris catalogue, and the old astronomical arguments all begin agreeing with one another.

The Air Force did not try to build a death ray in orbit. It tried something stranger and, in its own engineering frame, more reasonable: scatter hundreds of millions of tiny resonant copper wires around Earth so military microwave signals would have a passive reflector no enemy could easily cut, jam, or destroy.

The experiment worked well enough to become obsolete.

That sentence is where the book starts.

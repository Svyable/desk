# Back Matter

## Evidence note

This draft treats West Ford as a documented engineering and governance episode rather than a conspiracy story. It distinguishes the successful 1963 communications experiment from later claims about debris, astronomy, law, and precedent.

The strongest factual spine comes from MIT Lincoln Laboratory history, Smithsonian hardware records, NASA Orbital Debris Program material, contemporary IEEE/Physics Today literature, National Academy review, and newer scholarship on the astronomical response.

## Claim boundary

The manuscript does not claim that West Ford caused the Outer Space Treaty, permanently damaged astronomy, created a visible metallic ring, or left hundreds of millions of intact needles in orbit. It does not claim the project was secret in the sense of being wholly unknown to the scientific community or public. It does claim that national-security classification and limited transparency intensified governance concerns around a project with shared environmental consequences.

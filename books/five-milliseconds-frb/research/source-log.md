# Source Log

## Evidence classes

**Class 1 — verified/documented:** instrument records, published detections, secure localizations, terrestrial-interference reproductions, and measurements whose interpretation does not depend on a disputed source model.

**Class 2 — credible but incomplete/model-dependent:** physical source models, inferred source ages, host/environment contributions, survey-corrected population parameters, and formation-channel arguments.

**Class 3 — disputed/not established:** single-mechanism closure, artificial-transmitter claims, overinterpretations of periodicity, and source assignments that outrun localization or multiwavelength evidence.

**Class 4 — Sven synthesis/open conjecture:** “anomaly hardening,” contamination as a strengthening event when its signature is characterized, and the claim that FRBs show how a phenomenon can become a scientific instrument before its engine is fully settled.

## Source discipline

The manuscript must not use “mystery” as evidence. Every remarkable claim should be reconstructible into instrument, observable, inference, and alternative. Dispersion measure is not synonymous with distance. Repetition is an observing result conditioned on exposure. “Magnetar” is an astrophysical object class, not by itself an emission mechanism. A host galaxy narrows the environment but may not identify the precise stellar source. A terrestrial mimic matters only if its measurable signature overlaps the astrophysical class.

# Evidence Matrix

| Claim | Class | Current support | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| FRB 010724 was a real dispersed radio event in archival Parkes data | 1 | Lorimer et al. 2007 | Does not by itself establish a population or unique source mechanism |
| Perytons were terrestrial transients that mimicked dispersed astrophysical pulses | 1 | Petroff et al. 2015 plus earlier peryton record | Their existence did not show FRB 010724 was terrestrial |
| Parkes perytons could be generated by prematurely opening microwave-oven doors under suitable geometry | 1 | Petroff et al. 2015 | Specific interference mechanism, not universal explanation for radio transients |
| A cosmological FRB population exists | 1 | Thornton 2013; later localizations and catalogs | Population parameters remain survey-dependent |
| Some FRBs repeat | 1 | Spitler 2016 and many later sources | Non-detection of repetition is not proof of nonrepetition |
| FRB 121102 is extragalactic and localized to a host environment | 1 | Chatterjee 2017 and follow-up | Persistent source association does not uniquely specify engine |
| Magnetars can produce FRB-like bursts | 1 | SGR 1935+2154 / Bochenek 2020 and CHIME | Does not establish all FRBs are magnetar bursts |
| Young core-collapse magnetars explain all FRBs | 3 | Challenged by globular-cluster and quiescent-host events | Alternative magnetar formation channels remain plausible |
| Repeaters and apparent nonrepeaters are distinct physical populations | 2 | Statistical differences in some catalogs | Finite exposure and selection effects complicate taxonomy |
| FRB dispersion can measure diffuse ionized baryons | 1–2 | Macquart et al. 2020 | Host/halo/IGM partition is model-dependent |
| FRBs are artificial beacons | 3 | Speculative literature only | No event-specific evidence currently requires engineering |
| The FRB story demonstrates “anomaly hardening” | 4 | Sven synthesis from chronology | Must be tested against ordinary-history explanation |

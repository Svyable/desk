# Back Matter

## Working glossary

**Dispersion measure (DM):** integrated free-electron column density along a line of sight, conventionally expressed in pc cm^-3. It is not itself a distance.

**Fast radio burst (FRB):** a brief, bright radio transient with millisecond-scale structure and large dispersion, generally of extragalactic origin for the established population.

**Magnetar:** a neutron star whose activity is powered substantially by an extremely strong magnetic field. Magnetar is an object class, not a complete FRB radiation mechanism.

**Peryton:** a terrestrial radio transient with a swept-frequency form that can mimic astrophysical dispersion; Parkes perytons were traced to microwave-oven emission during magnetron shutdown under particular conditions.

**Repeater:** an FRB source from which multiple bursts have been observed. “Apparent nonrepeater” is preferable when the observation baseline is finite.

**Rotation measure:** a measure of Faraday rotation along a polarized radio signal’s path, sensitive to magnetized plasma.

## Research ethic

The book should keep three questions separate on every page: Did the event happen? Where did it happen? What mechanism made it happen? FRB history becomes confusing when evidence for one answer is treated as evidence for all three.

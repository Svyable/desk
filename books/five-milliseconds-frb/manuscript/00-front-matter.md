# Five Milliseconds

## Fast Radio Bursts and the Signals That Survived Doubt

**Sven Hardy Benson**

### Note on certainty

This book uses four evidence classes because the subject punishes compressed certainty. Published detections and reproduced terrestrial interference belong in a different category from source-model inference. Host association belongs in a different category from exact emission mechanism. Speculative artificial-source ideas belong in a different category again.

The phrase **anomaly hardening** is mine. It is not a standard term in radio astronomy. I use it for the process by which an initially strange observation becomes increasingly difficult to dismiss as artifact or category error because independent instruments, contaminant controls, localization, repetition, and auxiliary observations narrow the mundane alternatives. A hard anomaly may later become ordinary science. That is the point.

No classified data, proprietary telescope data, or nonpublic source material is claimed here. All scientific claims are drawn from publicly available literature and records.

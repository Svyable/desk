## Falsification tests

The “ethical control case” thesis should be abandoned if primary records show systematic nondisclosure of deliberate infection, routine punitive consequences for refusal, unavailable alternative duties, deceptive recruitment, or knowingly omitted material hazards. The thesis should be strengthened if refusal was common and penalty-free, protocol-specific risks were stated plainly, volunteers had independent advice and meaningful time to decide, and adverse events triggered transparent protocol changes.

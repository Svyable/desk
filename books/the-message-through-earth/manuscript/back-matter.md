# Back Matter

The practical claim in this book is deliberately smaller than the physical claim.

Information has been transmitted with neutrinos. Submarine communication with neutrinos has been proposed in serious technical literature. Neither fact establishes an operational naval system.

The strongest public evidence currently supports a useful middle position: the carrier is real, the niche is real, and the endpoint engineering remains the burden.

Readers should treat the research files in this directory as part of the argument. They record where a claim comes from, what would weaken it, and where the manuscript is still incomplete. In particular, the absence of public evidence for a fielded military system is not presented as proof that no classified research exists.

The question worth keeping is narrower and harder: what source and receiver performance would be required for a strategically useful link, and what evidence should a system of that scale leave behind?

That question is answerable.

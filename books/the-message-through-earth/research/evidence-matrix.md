# Evidence Matrix

## Class 1 — verified / documented

| Claim | Evidence | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Digital information has been transmitted using a neutrino beam | Stancil et al. 2012 | Demonstration used major accelerator/detector infrastructure; not a deployable naval link |
| The link crossed 1.035 km including 240 m of earth | Stancil et al. 2012 | Short by strategic range standards |
| Published decoded performance was about 0.1 bit/s with ~1% BER | Stancil et al. 2012 | System-specific result, not a universal neutrino-channel limit |
| NuMI uses high-energy protons, a target, focusing horns, decay pipe and substantial shielding/rock | Fermilab MINERvA documentation | Describes one beam architecture, not every possible future source |
| U.S. Navy strategic-submarine communication historically used layered systems and pursued ELF | GAO 1979 | Does not establish neutrino interest |

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

| Claim | Evidence | What is missing |
| --- | --- | --- |
| High-energy neutrino beams could in principle support one-way submarine communication | Huber 2009; established interaction physics | Full mobile-platform demonstration and complete system economics |
| Surrounding seawater could contribute target volume via neutrino-induced secondaries/Cherenkov detection | Huber 2009 and neutrino-telescope physics | Realistic hull-constrained receiver trial under marine backgrounds |
| Very low data rate might still have strategic value | Historical ELF logic; command-and-control architecture inference | Classified requirements and mission-specific latency bounds |

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Any claim that the United States, Russia, China, Israel or another state has deployed operational neutrino submarine communications.
- Any claim that a compact classified source or detector has already solved the orders-of-magnitude endpoint problem without independently verifiable evidence.
- Any claim that accelerator or neutrino facilities are covert strategic transmitters merely because the physics would permit communication experiments.
- Any claim that patents, proposals, classified-adjacent language or general naval research prove operational adoption.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- A neutrino channel’s main strategic value, if practical, may be as a sparse last-resort alert path rather than a replacement for conventional communications.
- The same weak interaction that makes matter transparent shifts system cost toward transmitter and receiver endpoints.
- A mature secret system should produce a secondary ecology proportional to source scale, receiver integration, exercises and doctrine, even if its purpose remains compartmented.
- Dual-use accelerator infrastructure could reduce the visible incremental footprint of a classified communications mode, but would not eliminate receiver and operational integration burdens.

## Defeat conditions

The book’s practical skepticism must narrow substantially if any of the following becomes public and independently supportable:

1. a mobile, submarine-compatible neutrino receiver demonstrates reliable coded communication under realistic marine backgrounds at bounded mass/power;
2. a source architecture demonstrates strategically useful coverage and latency without accelerator-scale fixed infrastructure or with a cost competitive with the resilience it adds;
3. declassified primary records establish operational deployment rather than study or prototype status;
4. repeated independent demonstrations show orders-of-magnitude improvement in source-to-decoded-bit efficiency sufficient to change the link budget.

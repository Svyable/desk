# Contradiction and Incentive Audit

## Tensions preserved

1. **The carrier is proven; the system is not.** The 2012 Fermilab link is strong evidence for the physical communications channel and weak evidence for naval practicality.
2. **Low bandwidth is both defect and possible fit.** Civilian intuitions make 0.1 bit/s look useless; strategic command systems can extract large meaning from tiny authenticated codewords. Latency and reliability remain unresolved.
3. **Secrecy cuts both ways.** Classification can hide meaningful engineering work, but should not be used to erase source power, detector size, testing and doctrine requirements.
4. **A narrow beam helps security and hurts coverage.** Directionality limits casual interception while making a moving, intentionally hidden receiver harder to illuminate.
5. **Existing infrastructure can hide incremental work and weaken the deployment case.** A dual-use accelerator could conceal a communications experiment more easily than a purpose-built strategic complex; it does not by itself solve receiver integration.

## Incentives and failure modes

- Particle physicists have incentives to demonstrate novel applications of facilities; this can make proof-of-concept language sound more operational than intended when retold elsewhere.
- Military organizations have incentives to study low-probability/high-payoff communications technologies without intending to procure them.
- Journalists and enthusiasts have incentives to collapse “proposed,” “demonstrated,” and “deployed” into a cleaner narrative.
- Skeptics have incentives to ridicule low data rates without accounting for prearranged strategic message sets.
- Program advocates can compare an exotic channel with the weakest incumbent rather than the full layered communications architecture.

## Claims deliberately rejected

- No inference from a neutrino laboratory to a covert naval transmitter absent direct evidence.
- No inference from a paper or patent to efficacy.
- No inference from absence of public records to proof of absence.
- No claim that neutrino communication is inherently safe at arbitrary energy/flux; any strategic source requires actual radiation analysis.
- No claim that “through Earth” means uniform global coverage without pointing and geometry analysis.

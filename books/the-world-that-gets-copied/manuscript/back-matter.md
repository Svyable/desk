# Back Matter

## A note on the claim

*The World That Gets Copied* makes a bounded argument.

The established physical core is open-system quantum mechanics: interactions entangle systems with environments; decoherence can suppress locally accessible interference; particular interactions can make some states more robust than others. Quantum Darwinism, strong quantum Darwinism, and spectrum broadcast structures are active foundations frameworks that ask how selected classical information becomes redundantly and independently accessible in environmental fragments. Laboratory work has demonstrated important pieces of this mechanism in controlled small systems. None of that uniquely settles the interpretation of quantum mechanics or proves that one formalism exhausts the emergence of every macroscopic classical fact.

The book's larger synthesis is therefore marked as synthesis. If ordinary objectivity depends partly on redundant accessible records, then the classical world available to local observers is selective: some information becomes cheap and public while other quantum information remains global, fragile, or inaccessible. Humans inherit that naturally public layer and build a second record environment of instruments, archives, databases, institutions, and media. That second environment can preserve evidence that nature would otherwise erase. It can also create representational redundancy without creating new empirical contact.

The distinction between **distribution redundancy** and **evidentiary redundancy** is an authorial extension, not a theorem of quantum Darwinism.

## What would weaken the thesis

The physical portion would be substantially weakened if realistic system-environment dynamics generally failed to produce locally accessible redundant records of stable observables outside narrowly engineered toy models; if proposed Darwinistic or spectrum-broadcast signatures proved unable to distinguish objectivization from generic correlations; or if future experiments consistently showed that the redundantly accessible information does not track the classical variables the frameworks predict under well-characterized interactions.

The human synthesis would be weakened if empirical work showed that provenance, independent-source diversity, and preservation of low-redundancy evidence add little to the reliability of knowledge systems compared with surface repetition alone. That is not the evidence base assumed here, but the claim should remain vulnerable rather than rhetorical.

## What the book does not claim

It does not claim that consciousness collapses wave functions, that humans are necessary for reality, that quantum Darwinism uniquely solves the measurement problem, that every environment produces Darwinistic objectivity, that the universe is literally a computer or archive, that social systems obey quantum equations, or that repetition is intrinsically evidence of truth.

It does not treat decoherence as literal destruction of global quantum information under unitary evolution. It does not treat no-cloning or no-broadcasting as metaphors that can simply be exported into politics. It does not identify environmental entropy with misinformation, quantum measurement with human surveillance, or spectrum broadcast structure with institutional consensus.

Those analogies appear only where they clarify the geometry of records and are stopped where the mechanisms diverge.

# Research Priorities

1. Reconstruct the 2026 Aquiry extrapolation from supplementary tables and reproduce its sensitivity to high-density block definition, canopy penetration, contemporaneity and people-per-earthwork assumptions.
2. Build a common comparison table for PACUNAM, Dzibanché, Upano, Casarabe, Mahendraparvata and Tugunbulak: area flown, pulse density, ground-point density, number of interpreted features, fraction ground-truthed, dating strategy and extrapolation method.
3. Retrieve the primary 2025 Maya regional demographic paper and reproduce the low/high population estimates from its sampling model.
4. Separate “new to archaeology,” “known site newly mapped,” “known feature newly contextualized,” and “locally known but academically unpublished” in every discovery claim.
5. Add Indigenous-authored sources on Amazonian cultural landscapes and data governance.
6. Add a technical appendix explaining return classification, interpolation, hillshade, local relief, sky-view factor, slope/curvature and machine-learning false positives.
7. Audit public-data release practices and looting risks for archaeological LiDAR.
8. Build a prospective protocol: scan a blinded landscape, preregister feature criteria, then field-check a randomized sample to measure detection precision and recall rather than reporting only successful discoveries.

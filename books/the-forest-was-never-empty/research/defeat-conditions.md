# Defeat Conditions

The book’s central thesis should be weakened if broad future ground-truthing shows that a large fraction of currently interpreted archaeological LiDAR features are natural or modern; if new surveys demonstrate that spectacular cases are unrepresentative and older settlement models were already broadly correct; or if claimed population revisions collapse once contemporaneity and residential classification are improved.

The stronger ecological synthesis should be rejected region by region where botanical, soil and archaeological evidence does not support long-lived human modification.

The argument does not depend on any exact civilization-wide population number. In fact, a major downward revision of the 2026 Aquiry population model would strengthen the methodological case for separating detected features from extrapolation.

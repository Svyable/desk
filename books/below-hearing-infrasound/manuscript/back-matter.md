# Back Matter

## Evidence note

*Below Hearing* is based entirely on public records and published research. The International Monitoring System exists inside an international security regime, and some operational material is distributed under treaty arrangements rather than as an unrestricted public archive. Nothing in this manuscript should be read as implying access to non-public CTBTO products, national technical means, classified sensor data, or unpublished compliance assessments.

The book distinguishes four classes of claim throughout its research trail: documented observations and institutional facts; credible but model-dependent scientific inference; disputed or unconfirmed claims; and the author’s synthesis. A pressure waveform is never treated as proof of source identity by itself.

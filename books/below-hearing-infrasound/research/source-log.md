## Claim boundaries

CTBTO public pages are authoritative for network architecture and the organization’s description of detections, but they are also institutional communications. Where an event’s energy, mechanism, or detection performance matters, the manuscript prefers peer-reviewed reconstruction. A treaty sensor recording a signal proves a pressure disturbance reached the station; source identification requires array processing, propagation modeling, timing, cross-sensor evidence, and sometimes external observations. A missing detection is not automatically negative evidence because atmospheric state, source directivity, frequency content, station noise, and network geometry change detectability.

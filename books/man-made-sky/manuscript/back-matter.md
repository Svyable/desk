# Back Matter

## Working source note

This draft uses public, lawfully available scientific and historical sources. It does not imply access to classified material. Where a later institutional history summarizes a classified-era event, the manuscript treats the summary as a source with its own date and evidentiary limits rather than as a substitute for every contemporaneous record.

Primary reconstruction is still in progress. The working evidence ledger in `research/evidence-ledger.md` separates documented facts, credible but incomplete reconstruction, disputed claims, and speculative synthesis, and records publication holds for details that should not enter prose until source-level verification is complete.

The central standard is deliberately asymmetric: Project Argus may be used to show that a specific extraordinary intervention happened. It may not be used to make a different extraordinary claim easier to believe without evidence belonging to that claim.

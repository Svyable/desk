# The Value Stack

## How Value Moves from Matter to Meaning

### Sven Hard Benson

A working Desk edition.

This book argues that value is not located in a product so much as distributed through a changing stack of physical inputs, capabilities, interfaces, distribution, trust, meaning, institutions, and control. The layers that create the most value are not always the layers that capture the most money. Power migrates as scarcity, technology, regulation, and social meaning change.

The examples combine established business history and economic concepts with interpretive analysis. Where the manuscript discusses future effects of artificial intelligence, geopolitics, or technological change, those passages are forecasts or scenarios rather than claims of settled fact.

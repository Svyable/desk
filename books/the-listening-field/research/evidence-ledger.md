# THE LISTENING FIELD — evidence ledger

## Governing thesis

Plant sensing becomes strategically interesting when three evidence streams collide: established plant electrical/chemical signaling, biohybrid sensing engineering, and public defense programs seeking to read either engineered or unmodified vegetation remotely. The thesis is **not** that plants are conscious spies. It is that living tissue may function as a distributed transduction layer whose state preserves classifiable traces of environmental events.

The manuscript must keep this causal ladder intact:

**event → exposure at plant → biological response → outward measurable feature → remote acquisition → classification → attribution → operational decision**

Evidence at one rung does not automatically promote a claim to the next.

## Class 1 — verified / documented

### DARPA Advanced Plant Technologies

DARPA publicly describes APT as a completed program intended to develop plants as persistent ground-based sensors for CBRNE threats. The agency says the concept was to harness plants' innate sensing/response mechanisms, extend them to signals of interest, and engineer response mechanisms observable with existing ground, air or space hardware. Initial research was to be contained; later field trials, if warranted, were to proceed through USDA APHIS biosafety protocols.

Source: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/advanced-plant-technologies

Source: https://www.darpa.mil/news/2017/natures-silent-sentinel

What this proves: a public U.S. defense research program pursued engineered plant sensing and remote readout.

What it does not prove: operational deployment, useful field accuracy, classified capability, plant cognition, or success against every named threat.

### DARPA eX Virentia

DARPA publicly states that eX Virentia aims to use **existing unmodified vegetation** as a sensor for activities of Department of War interest by exploiting natural plant responses to man-made chemical exposures. The program joins plant biology, remote sensing and modeling and seeks relationships between internal biochemical response and externally observable characteristics.

Source: https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/ex-virentia

What this proves: current public research interest in reading naturally occurring vegetation as a sensing medium rather than only engineering a new receptor into plants.

What it does not prove: successful target identification, operational remote range, satellite-level performance, or fielded surveillance.

### Long-distance electrical / calcium signaling

Choi et al. review rapid long-distance electrical and calcium signaling in plants, including systemic responses to local stimuli and coupling among electrical signals, calcium and reactive oxygen species.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-arplant-043015-112130

Fromm & Lautner review electrical signaling in plants and its physiological effects.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-3040.2006.01614.x

Farmer reviews wound- and mechanostimulated electrical signaling and hormone responses, including genetic evidence around wound-induced electrical signaling.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1111/nph.16646

What these prove: plants possess measurable electrical and associated systemic signaling processes.

What they do not prove: neurons, subjective experience, pain, telepathy, intention, or intelligence in the human cognitive sense.

### Plant-emitted VOCs as measurable physiological outputs

A 2022 ACS Sensors paper reports a wearable electrochemical sensor for in-situ monitoring of methanol emitted by plants and field measurements distinguishing emission levels under tested conditions.

Source: https://doi.org/10.1021/acssensors.2c00834

What this proves: at least some plant-emitted volatile compounds can be instrumented as physiological phenotypes under field conditions.

What it does not prove: unique attribution of arbitrary environmental events from VOCs.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Public program goals for APT/eX Virentia are credible evidence of research intent but do not disclose all experimental denominators, performance metrics or later operational status.
- Remote-sensing classification of vegetation stress is well established as a broad field, but any claim that a specific hidden human activity can be inferred from a specific natural plant response must be checked at the experiment level.
- Plant VOC profiles are promising biomarkers for multiple stresses. Specificity across genotype, developmental stage, weather, soil, pathogen burden and mixed stresses remains a core translation problem rather than a solved assumption.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

Do not state as established without substantially stronger evidence:

- plants are conscious in a human- or animal-like sense;
- plants feel pain;
- plants detect human thoughts or intentions;
- plants provide reliable polygraph-style responses to human deception or violence;
- a defense/intelligence agency currently operates a covert global plant-surveillance network;
- APT or eX Virentia has achieved operational target-identification performance merely because DARPA funded the work;
- remotely sensed vegetation signatures can uniquely identify arbitrary concealed military activities.

Historical claims associated with Cleve Backster and popular “plant perception” narratives belong here until specific experiments are reconstructed with primary sources, controls and independent replication. Do not use cultural familiarity as evidence.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

### The listening field

A landscape may be modeled as a distributed environmental transduction layer: events perturb living organisms; some perturbations persist in electrical, chemical, metabolic or optical state; external sensors may recover part of that state. This is a book-level synthesis, not a biological finding.

### Biological residue

Some human activities may leave a delayed biological signature even when the activity itself was not directly observed. Whether that signature is specific and stable enough for attribution is an empirical question.

### The sensor-chain test

Every strong claim should identify which links of the event→decision chain were actually tested. This is an editorial/epistemic framework, not a term of art in plant science.

## Strongest countermodel

Nothing anomalous is required. Plants are ordinary responsive organisms. Defense agencies have an obvious incentive to investigate low-power distributed sensing. Modern cameras, spectroscopy and machine learning can recover patterns from complex biological responses. The apparent novelty is an engineering integration problem, not evidence of plant cognition or a new information channel.

A stronger version of the countermodel says the entire military concept may fail operationally because the very environmental responsiveness that makes plants attractive produces unacceptable variance and false positives. Synthetic sensors may dominate whenever specificity, calibration, maintenance and interpretability matter more than self-repair and distribution.

The manuscript must allow this model to win.

## Contradictions / incentive audit

- DARPA program language describes objectives. Treat it as evidence of what the agency sought, not what performers ultimately achieved.
- Defense agencies benefit from ambitious framing when recruiting researchers; performers benefit from demonstrating relevance. Neither incentive invalidates the work, but neither is a performance metric.
- Popular plant-consciousness narratives benefit from collapsing signaling into cognition. Skeptical narratives can make the inverse error by treating absence of demonstrated consciousness as evidence that sophisticated signaling is uninteresting.
- Machine-learning papers can overstate generality when train/test environments share hidden structure. Cross-site, cross-season and cross-genotype validation matters.

## Publication holds

Before making quantitative or operational claims, recover primary sources for:

1. APT performer list, final demonstrations and any public end-of-program performance data.
2. eX Virentia solicitation, technical areas, target chemical definitions that are public, performance metrics and program dates.
3. Primary nanobionic-plant paper commonly described as “spinach detecting explosives”; distinguish plant uptake, nanosensor chemistry and remote readout from the headline.
4. Specific remote-sensing papers that discriminate chemical exposure from drought/pathogen/mechanical confounders.
5. False-positive denominators under realistic mixed stress.

## Defeat conditions

Narrow or abandon the book's stronger intelligence thesis if independent field studies show that target-related plant responses cannot be distinguished from ordinary environmental variance at operationally useful false-positive rates; if remote readout destroys the specificity available in direct assays; if signatures fail to transfer across sites/seasons/species; or if conventional sensors consistently dominate the biological approach on deployment cost, persistence and information quality.

The narrower scientific story—plants as distributed sensing and signaling organisms—would survive. The intelligence-infrastructure thesis would not.

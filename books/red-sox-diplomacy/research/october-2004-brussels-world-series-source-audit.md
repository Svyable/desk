# October 2004 in Brussels: World Series source audit

## Status

**Verified chronology; no authenticated viewing scene found.**

Nicholas Burns was U.S. Ambassador to NATO from 2001 to 2005, placing him in the Brussels/NATO assignment during the Boston Red Sox postseason and World Series championship in October 2004. Authoritative institutional biographies also independently describe Burns as a lifelong member of Red Sox Nation.

This establishes a strong chronological conjunction. It does **not** establish where, when, with whom, or by what medium Burns followed any 2004 postseason game.

## Targeted search result

Searches combining Burns with Brussels, NATO, Red Sox, World Series, and 2004 did not surface a reliable contemporaneous report, authenticated first-person recollection, oral history, transcript, photograph, or archival caption that establishes a specific October 2004 baseball scene.

Accordingly, the manuscript should **not** state or imply that Burns:
- watched a particular ALCS or World Series game in Brussels;
- watched at the residence, U.S. Mission, a bar, an expatriate gathering, or another venue;
- watched on AFN/AFRTS, Belgian television, satellite, internet, or another medium;
- watched with diplomats, family members, NATO colleagues, embassy staff, or friends;
- celebrated, suffered, stayed awake overnight, discussed the games at work, or used the championship diplomatically.

All of those remain possible research questions, not established facts.

## Manuscript-safe use

Safe formulation is limited to chronology and attributed identity: Burns was serving as U.S. Ambassador to NATO during the Red Sox's October 2004 championship run, and authoritative biographies describe him as a lifelong member of Red Sox Nation.

Do not turn the overlap into narrative action without an authenticated source.

## Priority retrieval targets

1. U.S. Mission to NATO public-affairs files, staff newsletters, calendars, photographs, residence/event records, and internal historical material for October 2004.
2. Association for Diplomatic Studies and Training oral histories for Mission personnel serving under Burns in Brussels.
3. Contemporary Belgian and Brussels expatriate press, especially English-language coverage of American community events.
4. AFRTS/AFN Europe schedules for October 2004. These can establish broadcast availability only; they cannot prove Burns watched.
5. Authenticated interviews, speeches, memoir material, or correspondence in which Burns himself recalls the 2004 postseason.
6. Oral histories with named Mission staff or colleagues, with questions narrowly framed around what they personally witnessed.

## Attribution firewall

No dialogue, interiority, reaction, motive, venue, companion, viewing medium, or scene may be reconstructed from chronology alone.

Nothing in the sources above authenticates a first-person October 2004 baseball recollection by Libby Baylies. Do not attribute attendance, viewing, dialogue, reaction, memory, or motive to her absent a directly authenticated source.

Unauthenticated first-person material should remain in research notes with provenance and verification status clearly labeled; it should not be silently promoted into manuscript voice.

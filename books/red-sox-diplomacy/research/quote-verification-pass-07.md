# Quote Verification Pass 07 — Publication Gate

## Purpose

This pass closes the manuscript-level exact-quotation risk created by scanned PDFs, oral-history PDFs, transcript mirrors, and retrospective recollections.

The rule is narrow: exact quotation marks require a stable transcript, reliable HTML text, or page-image verification. PDF/scanned sources may support conservative paraphrase without supplying publication-ready exact wording unless their page text has been checked directly.

## Result

The current manuscript no longer depends on an unverified exact quotation drawn only from a scanned PDF or oral-history PDF.

The remaining PDF/oral-history sources in the manuscript are used for chronology, named-witness attribution, or conservative paraphrase. In particular:

- Ted Osius's ADST oral history supports the Cairo witness account that Burns was Veliotes's staff aide and encouraged Osius toward SAIS and the Foreign Service. The manuscript paraphrases the recollection rather than placing unverified PDF wording in quotation marks.
- Douglas Keene's ADST material supports the Jerusalem assistance-program context and Burns-specific colleague attribution. The manuscript does not publish an unverified verbatim Keene quotation.
- the congressional scan preserving the `America's Salesman` profile supports Athens context; the manuscript does not rely on a scan-only exact quotation from that profile.
- the Senate nomination-hearing PDF supports the public Red Sox identity around the China nomination, but the manuscript's direct quoted line in Chapter 2 comes from the State Department's December 6, 2021 HTML briefing transcript instead.
- Harvard CV, school/archive PDFs, municipal PDFs, and other document scans are used as records rather than as sources of unchecked literary quotation.

## Remaining quotation discipline

The quote-bank files remain research aids rather than publication authority. Their standing instruction still applies: reopen the linked primary source before introducing any new exact quotation into the manuscript.

Do not upgrade a retrospective witness, institutional history, mirror, archive index, social-media preservation page, or secondary report into a stronger evidence class merely because its wording is vivid.

## Gate conclusion

For the current manuscript, the exact-quotation gate is closed at the manuscript level: no known exact quotation remains dependent solely on an unchecked scanned PDF or oral-history PDF.

Future prose changes can reopen this gate if they introduce new verbatim material. The publication check should therefore test only new or changed quotation marks rather than re-litigating already cleared paraphrase.

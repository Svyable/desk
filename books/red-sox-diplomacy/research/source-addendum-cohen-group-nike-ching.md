# Source Addendum: The Cohen Group and the Shanghai Basketball Scene

Date: 2026-09-15

## Why this addendum exists

Three manuscript passages were strengthened with material that was not represented in the existing chapter narrative:

- Chapter 17 now records that Nicholas Burns's post-2008 civilian life was not only academic. Alongside Harvard, he served as a senior counselor at The Cohen Group for twelve years before returning to government in 2021.
- Chapter 18 now uses Nike Ching's April 24, 2024 Voice of America reporting to make the book's people-to-people diplomacy argument concrete: Secretary of State Antony Blinken, Burns, and U.S. Consul General Scott Walker attended a Chinese Basketball Association playoff game in Shanghai.
- Chapter 22 now records Burns's February 17, 2025 return to The Cohen Group as vice chair after leaving the U.S. Embassy in Beijing.

The narrative purpose is continuity, not résumé completion. The Cohen material complicates any clean Harvard-versus-government binary; the Shanghai basketball scene shows sports diplomacy operating at its deliberately modest scale without claiming that a game altered strategic disputes.

## Sources

**Source:** The Cohen Group, “Ambassador Nicholas Burns to Rejoin The Cohen Group as a Vice Chair.”

https://cohengroup.net/ambassador-nicholas-burns-rejoin-cohen-group-vice-chair

**Supports:**

- Burns would rejoin The Cohen Group as a vice chair on February 17, 2025.
- Before serving as U.S. ambassador to China, Burns had been a senior counselor at the firm for twelve years.
- The firm describes its senior leaders as providing strategic advice and practical assistance to clients.

**Limit:** This is the firm's own account. It supports employment history, title, timing, and the firm's description of its work; it should not be used to infer undisclosed clients, engagements, influence, or private advice.

### Voice of America / Nike Ching — Shanghai basketball, April 24, 2024

**Source:** Nike Ching, “Blinken looks to boost people-to-people ties during second visit to China,” Voice of America, April 24, 2024.

https://www.voanews.com/a/blinken-looks-to-boost-people-to-people-ties-during-second-visit-to-china-/7583417.html

**Supports:**

- Blinken attended a Chinese Basketball Association playoff game at Shanghai Indoor Stadium during his April 2024 China trip.
- The game was between the Shanghai Sharks and Zhejiang Golden Bulls.
- VOA's image caption identifies Burns and U.S. Consul General Scott Walker with Blinken at the game.
- Ching framed the stop within the trip's emphasis on people-to-people ties.

**Limit:** The scene supports a concrete example of public-facing, people-to-people diplomacy. It does not establish that the game changed policy, public opinion, negotiating access, or the strategic U.S.-China relationship.

## Manuscript guardrail

The new passages intentionally stop short of claiming that Burns's Cohen work gave him special access or that sports produced policy outcomes. The useful narrative claim is narrower: across teaching, private advising, public office, and public-facing sports settings, Burns repeatedly worked at the boundary between formal authority and the human relationships around it.

# Chapter 18 visual archive — Beijing

## Editorial job

Beijing is visually rich enough to become undisciplined quickly. The chapter does not need a gallery of ambassadorial meetings merely because the archive contains hundreds of official photographs.

The visual sequence has three jobs:

1. show communication recovering after a crisis without implying competition ended;
2. show that even people-to-people exchange became contested government-to-government terrain;
3. return baseball at human scale with a new, period-specific image rather than repeating the 2024 clinic photograph already used in Chapter 1.

## CHANNEL RECOVERY 01

Asset: `../media/ch18-channel-recovery.svg`

The object follows three documentary turns:

- **February 2023** — the Chinese high-altitude balloon crisis disrupted the relationship and Secretary Antony Blinken's planned Beijing visit was postponed;
- **June 18–19, 2023** — Blinken traveled to Beijing and the U.S. readout explicitly emphasized reopening and maintaining channels to reduce the risk of miscalculation;
- **November 15, 2023** — the Biden-Xi Woodside meeting produced concrete follow-up in military communication, counternarcotics cooperation and people-to-people exchange.

Primary / official anchors:
- U.S. Department of State, Blinken Beijing visit, June 2023: https://2021-2025.state.gov/secretary-blinkens-visit-to-the-peoples-republic-of-china-prc/
- Chinese Foreign Ministry, Wang Yi readout of the San Francisco summit: https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/wjb/zzjg_663340/bmdyzs_664814/xwlb_664816/202312/t20231204_11194316.html

The Chinese readout likewise records military communication, counternarcotics and expanded educational/cultural exchange among summit outcomes. That makes the object a rare place where the two official records can be shown as overlapping on the existence of follow-up even while the broader relationship remained disputed.

Evidentiary limit: recovery of communication is not recovery of trust and is not Burns's personal achievement.

## CONTESTED CHANNEL 01

Asset: `../media/ch18-contested-channel.svg`

In June 2024 Burns publicly alleged that Chinese authorities had interfered with U.S. Embassy cultural and educational programming after Woodside. The State Department publicly backed his characterization.

U.S. source:
- State Department briefing, June 25, 2024: https://2021-2025.state.gov/briefings/department-press-briefing-june-25-2024/

China's Foreign Ministry rejected Burns's characterization, said China remained committed to cultural and people-to-people exchange, and argued that U.S. treatment of Chinese students and other U.S. policies were themselves obstructive.

Chinese source:
- Foreign Ministry spokesperson Mao Ning, June 26, 2024: https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/xw/fyrbt/lxjzh/202407/t20240730_11463247.html

Independent contemporaneous cross-check:
- Reuters, June 26, 2024: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-rejects-us-envoys-comment-that-it-hinders-people-to-people-exchanges-2024-06-26/

The graphic preserves competing government claims. It does not certify either government's description as a complete event-by-event finding.

## Operating thesis

Burns's 2024 CSIS interview is a useful first-person anchor for the chapter's operating grammar: the relationship was structurally competitive, but the United States still needed engagement in areas such as climate and fentanyl and needed societal connections to remain open.

Source:
- CSIS, `Ambassador Nicholas Burns on the U.S.-China Relationship`, July 25, 2024: https://www.csis.org/analysis/ambassador-nicholas-burns-us-china-relationship

Do not turn `compete / communicate / cooperate` into a rigid formal doctrine unless a primary source uses that exact three-part formulation. In the manuscript it is best treated as a concise synthesis of Burns's repeated public argument.

## Why no meeting gallery

The federal image archive contains many excellent photographs of Burns with Chinese officials, U.S. cabinet officials, students, business leaders and other diplomats. Most would prove only that meetings occurred—facts already better handled by the prose and official readouts.

A photograph should enter only when the visual fact itself adds evidence. The pitching image passes that test because it embodies the chapter's small-scale human continuity. A generic handshake does not.

## Placement

- `CHANNEL RECOVERY 01` belongs after the prose explains the June 2023 reopening and the Woodside follow-up.
- `CONTESTED CHANNEL 01` belongs after the 2024 U.S./Chinese dispute over cultural and educational programming has been stated from both sides.
- `FIG. 07` belongs near the chapter's final baseball return, after the prose has explicitly limited its significance to human scale.

## Guardrails

- Do not treat communication as evidence of trust.
- Do not attribute Woodside outcomes to Burns personally.
- Do not describe the Chinese state, Chinese society and individual Chinese people as interchangeable actors.
- Do not use people-to-people exchange as a sentimental solution to Taiwan, technology, human-rights or military competition.
- Do not convert the U.S. 2024 interference allegation into settled fact beyond what sources independently establish.
- Do not use baseball as causal evidence about the bilateral relationship.

The chapter's visual argument is that channels matter most precisely when they cannot carry agreement.

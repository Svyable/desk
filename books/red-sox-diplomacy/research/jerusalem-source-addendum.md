## Attribution discipline

- `rsd-j04` establishes historical U.S. aid totals, not Burns's personal spending authority.
- `rsd-j05` establishes characteristics of U.S.-funded PVO activity, not necessarily projects Burns personally handled.
- `rsd-j06` is broader UN-system development context and must never be represented as a Burns/USAID project list.
- `rsd-j02` and `rsd-j03` establish a major Palestinian meeting at the consulate during Burns's tour but do not establish his attendance.

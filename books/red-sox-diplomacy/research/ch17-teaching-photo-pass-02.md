### Why it earns space

This is not literally a classroom photograph, and the caption must not claim that it is. Its value is narrower: it gives Chapter 17 a rights-clear contemporary image of Burns after government service, at the point when Harvard had become his institutional base and diplomacy had become something he taught, discussed, and practiced in public. Harvard Kennedy School’s current biography confirms that he joined the Kennedy School after leaving government and founded/faculty-chaired the Future of Diplomacy Project; it also describes his courses in negotiation/diplomacy and great-power politics: https://www.hks.harvard.edu/faculty/nicholas-burns

### Proposed placement

Place after the chapter first establishes Burns’s move into Harvard teaching, not as a chapter opener. The image should function as evidence of the post-government public role, not as a decorative portrait.

**Caption:** *Nicholas Burns in 2010, two years after leaving the Foreign Service and during his Harvard teaching years. The photograph is from Chatham House in London, not a classroom; it documents the public-facing diplomatic role that accompanied his move into teaching.*

**Credit:** *Chatham House, 14 July 2010, CC BY 2.0.*

**Alt text:** *Nicholas Burns, wearing a dark suit and tie, speaks at Chatham House in London in July 2010 during his post-government Harvard years.*

### Production treatment

- Portrait orientation; target roughly 42–50% text-block width in print unless paired with a short sidebar.
- Keep image, caption, credit, and modification notice together across page breaks.
- Do not full-bleed or hero-crop it; the point is documentary specificity, not personality branding.
- EPUB reading order: preceding paragraph → image → caption → credit.
- Preserve enough surrounding context that the frame reads as a public-policy event rather than a studio headshot.
- Grayscale proof required; do not depend on tie/background color for meaning.
- If cropped, append **“cropped from original”** to the credit line to satisfy the CC modification-disclosure requirement cleanly.

## Original figure fallback — TEACHING THE FILE 01

If the Chatham House portrait feels too portrait-like in layout, prefer an original documentary object rather than licensing weaker university event photography.

Build a compact chronology from independently verified institutional records:

**2008** — Burns leaves the Foreign Service and moves into the Harvard Kennedy School phase of his career.  
**2010** — contemporary Chatham House portrait/event record.  
**2013** — Boston Marathon bombing; Burns is teaching at Harvard and publishes on the event’s democratic/foreign-policy implications.  
**2021–2025** — returns to government as U.S. ambassador to China.  
**2025** — returns to Harvard.

The figure should be original typography and geometric marks only. It is not a “career timeline” of every title. Its argument is that teaching was not retirement from diplomacy; the boundary between classroom, public argument, and later service remained porous.

Potential authoritative factual anchors:

- Harvard Kennedy School faculty biography: https://www.hks.harvard.edu/faculty/nicholas-burns

Do not quote the Boston Globe article from the HKS abstract beyond what fair quotation and the book’s normal permissions policy permit; paraphrase its factual relevance instead.

## Evidence boundary

A 2010 Chatham House photograph can establish Burns’s appearance at that event and give the reader a contemporary post-government image. It cannot establish how he taught, what happened inside a particular Harvard classroom, what students thought of him, or his private reaction to Boston/baseball events. Those require separate evidence. The chapter should continue to distinguish institutional biography and public statements from reconstructed classroom texture.

# Beijing Public-Diplomacy Pass 01 — Red Sox Diplomacy

This pass asks a narrow question: did the representational habits visible in Athens—personal identity, public diplomacy, cultural exchange, relationship maintenance—remain visible during Burns’s China ambassadorship?

The answer is yes, but the China material should not be forced into an Athens replay. The documentary record shows a more constrained environment in which Burns repeatedly emphasized educational, youth, cultural, and people-to-people exchange while also publicly accusing Chinese authorities of obstructing those exchanges.

## 1. Educational exchange as a stated pillar

On March 27, 2024, Burns met Chinese Education Minister Huai Jinpeng. China’s Ministry of Education reported consensus around institutional dialogue, educational cooperation, personnel exchange, and youth exchange. Burns is quoted describing educational exchange and cooperation as a “cornerstone” of the bilateral relationship and supporting expansion of practical cooperation and youth exchange.

Source: https://en.moe.gov.cn/news/press_releases/202403/t20240331_1123305.html

Narrative use: unlike baseball in Greece, the China public-diplomacy story is explicitly framed around rebuilding channels between young people and institutions during strategic competition.

## 2. Campus diplomacy

Burns’s China tenure included repeated visits to universities and academic programs.

- March 2024, University of Chicago Hong Kong: Burns emphasized educational and cultural exchange and the need for more Americans to learn about China.
  Source: https://uchicago.hk/news/us-ambassador-peoples-republic-china-visits-uchicago-campus-hong-kong

- Fudan University: Burns discussed continued cooperation despite strategic competition and engaged directly with faculty and students.
  Source: https://iis.fudan.edu.cn/en/7a/9a/c16676a490138/page.htm

- Shanghai Jiao Tong University: he stressed enhanced educational cooperation and interacted with students on cross-cultural education, careers, and youth responsibility.
  Source: https://global.sjtu.edu.cn/hmt/news/view/1405

- June 2024, Fudan-Harvard summer program: Burns encouraged Harvard students to learn Chinese and study China’s history and culture.
  Source: https://iis.fudan.edu.cn/en/9a/e5/c16676a695013/page.htm

- December 2024, NYU Shanghai: Burns visited a class and met representatives from multiple U.S.-China joint educational institutions.
  Source: https://shanghai.nyu.edu/news/us-ambassador-china-nicholas-burns-visits-nyu-shanghai

Narrative use: these are not background decorations. They establish a sustained pattern of ambassadorial time spent on people-to-people infrastructure.

## 3. Provincial travel and subnational diplomacy

During a March 2024 Fujian trip, provincial authorities reported Burns emphasizing economic ties, student exchange, and people-to-people connectivity while visiting Fuzhou and Xiamen.

Source: https://wb.fujian.gov.cn/English/latest/202403/t20240328_6420999.htm

In September 2024, Hunan provincial authorities likewise reported Burns visiting with an American business delegation and discussing people-to-people exchange alongside economic cooperation.

Source: https://enghunan.gov.cn/hneng/News/Localnews/202409/t20240910_33451816.html

Narrative use: this supports a picture of Burns trying to make the bilateral relationship legible below the level of presidents and ministries.

## 4. Cultural exchange

In April 2024 Burns met the president of China’s National Centre for the Performing Arts for a discussion centered on bilateral cultural exchange and people-to-people friendship.

Source: https://en.chncpa.org/discover/ncpatheworld/artsexchangeevents/eu/qbjlhd/202405/t20240509_263632.shtml

Narrative use: culture, education, and sport can be written as adjacent tools in the same representational repertoire, but they should not be collapsed into one thing.

## 5. The constraint: Burns says the channels were being obstructed

In June 2024 Burns publicly accused Chinese authorities of interfering with 61 U.S. Embassy public events since the previous November, including discouraging participation or intimidating attendees. Reuters reported China’s Foreign Ministry rejecting his account. Other reporting quoted Burns saying Chinese authorities claimed to support reconnecting the populations while taking steps that made it “impossible.”

Sources:
- https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-rejects-us-envoys-comment-that-it-hinders-people-to-people-exchanges-2024-06-26/
- https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3268037/us-ambassador-nicholas-burns-says-beijing-making-us-china-exchanges-impossible

Narrative use: this is essential. The China chapters should not sentimentalize people-to-people diplomacy. Burns’s own record says these channels were strategically important precisely because they were difficult and contested.

## 6. Baseball survives as personal register

On October 22, 2024, an official Ambassador Burns social-media post showed him with Chinese baseball fans and captioned the image: “Future members of @Redsox Nation.” The image is preserved as a U.S. State Department work in the public domain.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ambassador_Burns_with_Chinese_baseball_fans.png

Narrative use: this is a strong visual echo of Greece, but we should not inflate it into evidence of a China baseball-diplomacy program. What it safely proves is continuity of personal register: decades later, Burns still used Red Sox identity in public-facing diplomatic life.

## 7. A post-China retrospective reinforces the priority

After leaving Beijing, Burns repeatedly described people-to-people and educational exchange as a durable foundation for stabilizing the relationship. A 2026 U.S.-China Education Trust event recap records him arguing that exchange programs, universities, and similar institutions should rebuild these connections, and recounting a 2024 encounter with an American student studying at a large university in Guizhou.

Source: https://uscet.org/event-recap-distinguished-speaker-series-china-connections-a-conversation-with-nicholas-burns/

Narrative use: useful retrospective evidence, but distinguish it clearly from statements made while in office.

## 8. Beijing thesis supported by this pass

Athens and Beijing should function as bookends, not copies.

Athens shows a relatively open environment in which Burns could turn a personal passion into a small but concrete public-diplomacy project connecting Greek organizers with American institutions.

Beijing shows the same diplomat decades later emphasizing exchange, universities, culture, provincial travel, and personal contact under conditions where those channels were politically constrained. Baseball survives as a flash of personal identity rather than the central instrument.

That contrast is more interesting than a forced claim that Burns repeated the Greece playbook in China.

### Why this is better than the current generic reference

Chapter 18 currently says only that a 2024 public post showed young people in China and referred to future members of Red Sox Nation. The archived record gives the missing event-level specificity: this was a **baseball clinic in Beijing**, not an unexplained group photograph. That matters because it turns the moment from decorative fandom into a small, documented public-diplomacy encounter while preserving the chapter's existing limit: nothing important in the bilateral relationship changed because of it.

The photograph also creates a useful two-image sequence with the existing July 4, 2023 federal photograph of Burns pitching a baseball. The 2023 frame establishes baseball as part of the ambassador's representational repertoire; the October 2024 clinic shows that the register later reached an actual youth-facing baseball event. Neither image proves policy effect.

## Source/evidence boundary

The October 2024 archive record establishes a baseball clinic in Beijing, Burns's presence, young Chinese participants, the Red Sox cap/baseball visible in the image, and the wording of his official post. It does **not** establish who organized the clinic, how participants were selected, whether the Red Sox or MLB sponsored it, what participants thought of the event, or any measurable diplomatic effect. Do not infer those facts without a separate primary record.

The September 2023 classroom record establishes Burns's presence with students at the named school. It does not establish what was said in the room or how students interpreted the visit.

## Rights/relevance rejects

- Do not reproduce MLB, Red Sox, broadcast, Getty/AP/Reuters, newspaper, or commercial sports photography without a separate compatible license.
- Do not treat a visible Red Sox cap in a federal documentary photograph as permission to reproduce standalone team marks as design elements.
- Do not reproduce social-media UI, handles, comments, engagement counts, or screenshots when the underlying federal image can be used cleanly.
- Do not use the October 2024 photograph to imply Red Sox/MLB sponsorship or endorsement unless an authoritative event record establishes it.
- Do not add generic Great Wall, flags, skyline, military, panda, or baseball-stadium imagery merely to make the China chapter look more visual.

# Nicholas Burns social-media sweep — 2024

This file isolates the strongest 2024 items from Nicholas Burns's official `@USAmbChina` social record. The purpose is not to inventory the account. It is to identify posts that materially strengthen *Red Sox Diplomacy*: Boston sports identity, sports as public diplomacy, educational exchange, cultural participation, public representation of America, and Burns's recognizable first-person register.

# Jerusalem 1986 — ADST witness map 02

## Purpose

The unresolved question is narrow: Nicholas Burns served at the American Consulate General in Jerusalem during the 1986 World Series, but no located source establishes where he watched Mets–Red Sox, whether he watched it live, whom he watched with, or how he reacted. This note identifies contemporaneous senior mission witnesses and archival material without converting overlap into a baseball memory.

## New archival anchor: ADST Jerusalem Country Reader

The Association for Diplomatic Studies and Training's Jerusalem Country Reader is a compiled oral-history source for U.S. officers who served at the Jerusalem mission. Its table of contents places two senior officers directly across Burns's 1985–1987 tour:

- **Douglas R. Keene — Deputy Principal Officer, Jerusalem, 1983–1986.** Keene overlaps Burns's arrival and the first part of his Jerusalem assignment.
- **Morris Draper — Consul General, Jerusalem, 1986–1988.** Draper overlaps the entire 1986 World Series period and the latter part of Burns's tour.

ADST reader:
https://adst.org/Readers/Jerusalem.pdf

ADST's oral-history collection contains more than 2,600 interviews and provides transcript search and standard citation guidance:
https://adst.org/oral-history/oral-history-interviews/

The reader itself does **not** currently supply a Burns/Red Sox/1986 World Series recollection. Its value is that it converts a generic 'Jerusalem colleagues' lead into named, contemporaneous senior witnesses whose oral histories can be searched or whose papers/interview networks can be pursued.

## Mission context from Morris Draper

Draper's oral history describes the Jerusalem post as institutionally unusual: the U.S. Consulate General was not subordinate to the embassy in Tel Aviv and operated against the unresolved diplomatic status of Jerusalem. That is useful background for Burns's early-career formation, but it should not be recruited into the baseball story unless a direct bridge appears.

The chronology is especially useful because Burns's own biographies consistently place him at the Consulate General from 1985 to 1987, coordinating U.S. economic assistance to Palestinians in the West Bank. The ADST reader independently identifies the senior mission leadership around him.

## 1986 World Series claim boundary

What may be stated:

- Burns was posted at the American Consulate General in Jerusalem in 1986.
- Morris Draper was Consul General there from 1986 to 1988.
- Douglas Keene served as Deputy Principal Officer through 1986.
- Burns was already a lifelong Red Sox fan, established independently by later first-person and official sources.
- Boston lost the 1986 World Series to the New York Mets.

What may **not** be stated from the current record:

- Burns watched Game 6 or Game 7 in Jerusalem.
- Draper, Keene, Libby Baylies, or any other named colleague watched with him.
- Burns listened by radio, Armed Forces broadcasting, tape delay, newspaper, telephone, or any other specific medium.
- The loss affected his diplomatic work or produced a remembered scene.

The fact pattern is attractive enough that it is unusually vulnerable to invented memoir. Keep the blank visible.

## Highest-value next witness/archive moves

1. Search the full Douglas Keene and Morris Draper ADST transcripts for `Burns`, `baseball`, `Red Sox`, `World Series`, `Mets`, `sports`, and informal mission-life references.
2. Identify additional Jerusalem political/economic officers on post in October 1986 through State Department biographic registers, Foreign Service lists, and ADST's Israel/Jerusalem readers.
3. Search any Burns oral histories, long-form interviews, commencement remarks, or alumni interviews for `1986`, `Mets`, `Bill Buckner`, `Jerusalem`, and `World Series` together and separately.
4. Treat Libby Baylies only as a possible witness target. Do not assign her fandom, viewing habits, reaction, or dialogue without a direct source.
5. If an interview opportunity arises, ask Burns the narrow documentary question: where was he for Games 6 and 7 in October 1986, and how did he follow the Series from Jerusalem?

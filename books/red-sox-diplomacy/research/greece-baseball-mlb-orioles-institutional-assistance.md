# Greece baseball institution-building: Burns, MLB, and the Baltimore Orioles

## Status

**Promotable with attribution.** This note strengthens the Greece/public-diplomacy thread but does **not** establish institutional assistance from the Boston Red Sox.

## Strongest sourced account

A contemporaneous Christian Science Monitor feature published August 18, 2004, during the Athens Olympics, reports that Greek baseball organizer Peter Mitsiopoulos and colleagues founded the Hellenic Amateur Baseball Federation and sought help from the U.S. ambassador. Nicholas Burns, ambassador to Greece from 1997 to 2001, told the paper he was a “confirmed, unconditional, rabid fan of the Boston Red Sox.” The article reports that Burns brought Peter Angelos, the Greek-American owner of the Baltimore Orioles, and Major League Baseball’s International Division into the effort.

Burns described introducing baseball to Greece as “a joint venture between America and Greece” and “one of the best things I ever did as ambassador.”

Source: Christian Science Monitor, “Greek baseball team, made in the USA,” August 18, 2004.
https://www.csmonitor.com/2004/0818/p02s01-woeu.html

## Independent/secondary corroboration

A history of baseball in Greece hosted on an older community site likewise says Burns contacted Major League Baseball and prominent Greek Americans, naming Peter Angelos, Chris Karalekas, Chuck Samiotis, and Bill Galatis. It says Angelos assigned Orioles scout Robert Derksen to identify Greek-American and Greek-Canadian players. This is useful as a lead and for names, but it is not as strong as the contemporaneous CSM account and should not carry contested details by itself.

Source:
https://www.oocities.org/spartakosglyfadas/history_of_baseball_in_greece2.htm

## Official career chronology

The archived George W. Bush White House biography confirms Burns served as U.S. ambassador to Greece from 1997 to 2001 and says his tenure expanded people-to-people programs alongside other bilateral cooperation.

Source:
https://georgewbush-whitehouse.archives.gov/government/nburns-bio.html

## Manuscript-safe conclusions

* Burns’s baseball fandom was not merely private background during his Greece posting; a contemporaneous 2004 account attributes to him direct diplomatic/institutional involvement in helping Greek baseball organizers connect with U.S. baseball institutions.
* The documented institutional chain in the strongest source runs through **Peter Angelos/the Baltimore Orioles and Major League Baseball’s International Division**, not the Boston Red Sox.
* Burns’s own retrospective description supports treating the episode as a meaningful example of people-to-people diplomacy, but claims about diplomatic effect beyond what the sources demonstrate should remain analytical and carefully framed.
* The Red Sox connection here is Burns’s identity as a fan, explicitly stated by Burns in the CSM interview. Do not convert that identity into Red Sox organizational participation.

## Important negative finding

This pass did **not** locate reliable evidence that the Boston Red Sox organization materially assisted the Greek baseball project. Until a primary or strong contemporaneous source establishes otherwise, references to “Greece/Red Sox institutional assistance” should be corrected to the documented **Greece/Burns/MLB/Orioles** assistance chain.

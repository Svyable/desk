# Continuity Pass 02 — Closing Compression

## Scope

Second continuity pass after the complete manuscript, reader-facing source notes and the Red Sox/American-history braid were in place.

## Chapter 20 — The Human Channel

### Problem

The chapter's argument was strong but overly explicit as a framework. `First element`, `second element`, and similar sequencing made the prose feel like a lecture outline rather than a late-book synthesis earned through the biography.

### Edit

- Removed numbered-element scaffolding.
- Kept the essential sequence—authentication, precision, judgment, relationship memory, accountability—but embedded it in narrative movement rather than labels.
- Compressed the Beijing callback so Chapter 20 no longer retells the crisis chronology already owned by Chapter 18.
- Preserved the central formulation that synthetic abundance increases the value of **authorized intent**.
- Kept the November 2024 human-control/nuclear-AI example narrow and explicitly non-totalizing.
- Reduced repeated warnings that the human channel is not charm or technological nostalgia.

### Result

Chapter 20 now reads as synthesis emerging from Burns's career rather than a portable management framework.

## Chapter 21 — What America Looks Like From Away

## Chapter 22 — Extra Innings

### Problem

The chapter reached a strong late turn—Burns's September 2026 reassessment of his Iraq judgment and the idea of **revisability**—but spent too long restating handoff, verdict, scale and institutional continuity before and after that turn.

### Edit

- Kept the late-2024 detained-American outcome as a human-scale counterweight to great-power abstraction.
- Shortened the `great diplomat?` verdict section.
- Made the Iraq reassessment the central late-career evidence rather than one item in a larger list.
- Preserved the distinction between retrospective admission and erasure of responsibility.
- Compressed the practice/reflection loop and the patriotism discussion.
- Reduced the final list of professional lessons.
- Preserved the ending: `The inning was never extra. / It was only next.`

### Result

Chapter 22 now turns on revisability and continued participation, leaving inheritance and the final person-under-the-title image to the epilogue.

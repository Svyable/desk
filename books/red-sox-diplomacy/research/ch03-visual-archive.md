# Chapter 3 visual archive — The Road Team

## Method

Chapter 3 is about movement becoming professional method. It does not need another portrait of Nicholas Burns and it does not need generic photographs of Cairo, Jerusalem, or an embassy merely to prove those places exist.

The chapter earns two documentary objects:

1. a **route object** showing the repeated institutional and geographic resets across Burns's career;
2. an **itinerary object** showing the July 1988 Shultz Asia trip Burns later identified as a formative lesson in relationship maintenance.

Both are diagrams made from sourced facts. Neither reconstructs an undocumented scene.

## ROUTE OBJECT 01 — a career of repeated arrival

**Local media:** `../media/ch03-road-team-route.svg`  
**Primary chronology sources:**
- U.S. Department of State 1997 official biography: https://1997-2001.state.gov/about_state/biography/burns.html
- U.S. Department of State archived biography: https://2009-2017.state.gov/s/p/fapb/185577.htm

**Documented sequence used in the object:**
- 1980 — U.S. Embassy Nouakchott, Mauritania, intern
- 1983–1985 — Cairo, vice consul and staff assistant to the ambassador
- 1985–1987 — Jerusalem, political officer
- 1987–1997 — Washington roles across State, NSC, and the State Department podium
- 1997–2001 — Athens, U.S. Ambassador to Greece
- 2001–2005 — Brussels, U.S. Ambassador to NATO
- 2005–2008 — Washington, Under Secretary of State for Political Affairs
- 2022–2025 — Beijing, U.S. Ambassador to China

**Narrative job:** Make repeated arrival visible. The object is intentionally chronological rather than a faux-precise travel map: the point is institutional reset and continuity, not mileage.

**Guardrail:** The line does not imply a predetermined promotion path. The chapter explicitly rejects reading the career backward as a neat pipeline.

## ITINERARY OBJECT 01 — tending the diplomatic garden

**Local media:** `../media/ch03-shultz-asia-1988.svg`  
**Burns source:** Nicholas Burns, “The Diplomat as Gardener,” *Foreign Affairs*, February 19, 2021; Harvard Kennedy School record: https://www.hks.harvard.edu/publications/diplomat-gardener  
**Official travel chronology:** Office of the Historian, George P. Shultz travels: https://history.state.gov/departmenthistory/travels/secretary/shultz-george-pratt

**Documented July 1988 sequence:**
- Bangkok — July 6–9
- Kuala Lumpur — July 9
- Jakarta — July 9–11
- Manila — July 11–13
- Hong Kong — July 13–14
- Beijing — July 14–16
- Seoul — July 16–18
- Tokyo — July 18–20
- Majuro — July 20
- Honolulu — July 21 address concluding the Asia travels

Burns describes the underlying journey as an eight-country, three-week Asia tour and says no crisis or urgent diplomatic objective compelled it. The official travel ledger is used for stop-by-stop dates; the diagram does not attempt to resolve differing counting conventions for countries/territories/stops.

**Narrative job:** Show why maintenance is difficult to dramatize. A reader can see the travel burden of relationship tending before any later crisis made those relationships narratively important.

**Rights:** The itinerary diagram is original to the book and built from U.S. government travel records plus Burns's later published interpretation. The design reproduces no copyrighted photograph.

## Why there is no early-career photograph here

A methodical search did not surface an early-1980s photograph of Burns in Nouakchott, Cairo, or Jerusalem with provenance strong enough to place in the manuscript as evidence. A generic embassy photograph would decorate a place without documenting Burns's presence in the depicted moment.

That absence is preferable to visual false precision. If a dated, rights-clear early-career photograph later appears in State, presidential-library, embassy, ADST, Burns-family, or institutional archives, it can be evaluated as a replacement or addition.

## Chapter-level restraint

Two objects are enough. The route establishes the career's repeated resets; the Shultz itinerary establishes the maintenance principle. Chapter 4, which slows down in Cairo, can carry the burden of place-specific visual research.

# April 2004: CENTCOM Qatar and a Yankees–Red Sox screen

## Finding

Thomas L. Friedman published an attributable account in *The World Is Flat* (2005) that places Nicholas Burns, then U.S. ambassador to NATO, in a baseball scene during official travel in April 2004.

Friedman writes that Burns told him he was at U.S. Central Command headquarters in Qatar for a briefing by General John Abizaid and staff. Friedman describes several information screens behind the briefers and reports that one showed a Yankees–Red Sox game. He identifies Burns as a loyal member of Red Sox Nation and says Burns was focused on the baseball screen.

Verification copy of the published book: https://g-city.sass.org.cn/_upload/article/files/c5/d3/35562efc4f4b8dcc467f989f901f/467b7b5a-891e-4bf2-a737-c2fadb7e099a.pdf

## Evidentiary status

This is a **published secondary first-person relay**: Friedman explicitly attributes the recollection to Burns. It is stronger than an inference from Burns's general fandom, but weaker than a contemporaneous Burns calendar, diary, transcript, photograph, or recording.

The source supports three bounded facts: Burns was serving as U.S. ambassador to NATO; Friedman reports Burns visiting CENTCOM headquarters in Qatar in April 2004 for an Abizaid briefing; and a Yankees–Red Sox game was visible during that briefing.

## Boundaries

Do not invent dialogue, thoughts, emotions, jokes, reactions, motives, or conversations. Do not claim baseball affected the briefing or policy.

The passage does not give an exact April date. Do not identify the televised game until Burns travel records and the 2004 baseball schedule can be reconciled. Friedman's mention of Pedro Martinez and Derek Jeter should not by itself be treated as proof of the exact pitching matchup.

This evidence does not establish Burns's location or viewing circumstances during the October 2004 ALCS or World Series.

Nothing in this source authenticates or supports first-person material attributed to Libby Baylies.

## Manuscript use

This is strong enough for a short, explicitly attributed NATO-era scene: months before Boston's October run, Friedman later reported that Burns told him about an April 2004 briefing in Qatar where a Yankees–Red Sox game occupied one of the room's screens.

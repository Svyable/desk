# NATO / CENTCOM Qatar baseball scene — April 2004 date reconciliation

## Finding

The Thomas Friedman / Nicholas Burns recollection of a CENTCOM headquarters briefing in Qatar with a Yankees–Red Sox game on a fourth screen can now be narrowed to a substantially stronger **candidate date**, though not yet proven to an archival standard.

Friedman's account identifies the baseball broadcast as involving **Pedro Martínez and Derek Jeter** and places the CENTCOM visit in **April 2004**.

Independent baseball records show an unusually strong match on **Sunday, April 25, 2004**: Boston beat New York 2–0 at Yankee Stadium, Pedro Martínez started and threw seven scoreless innings, and Derek Jeter went 0-for-4 with three strikeouts. Baseball-Reference records the game as beginning at 1:08 p.m. New York time. SABR's Games Project independently describes Martínez facing Jeter in the game and the Red Sox completing a three-game sweep.

Sources:
- Baseball-Reference, Boston Red Sox at New York Yankees, April 25, 2004: https://www.baseball-reference.com/boxes/NYA/NYA200404250.shtml
- Society for American Baseball Research Games Project, April 25, 2004: https://sabr.org/gamesproj/game/april-25-2004-pedro-martinez-scott-williamson-blank-yankees-as-red-sox-complete-sweep-at-yankee-stadium/

The diplomatic calendar also tightens around that same weekend. NATO's official history records the North Atlantic Council's first Afghanistan visit on **April 26–27, 2004**. Contemporary wire reporting quotes Burns speaking to reporters while flying to Kabul with other senior NATO officials on April 26.

Sources:
- NATO, "NATO Council visits Afghanistan": https://www.nato.int/en/news-and-events/articles/news/2004/05/26/nato-council-visits-afghanistan
- RFE/RL, "NATO Attaches Strings To Iraq Role," April 26, 2004: https://www.rferl.org/a/1052483.html

## What can safely be said

The two independent timelines now overlap tightly:

1. **April 25:** Martínez starts against the Yankees, with Jeter in New York's lineup.
2. **April 26–27:** Burns is traveling with the North Atlantic Council to Afghanistan.
3. Friedman's Burns-derived recollection places the CENTCOM/Qatar briefing in April 2004 and identifies Martínez and Jeter on the baseball screen.

This makes the April 25 Yankees–Red Sox game a **high-confidence candidate for the broadcast Friedman describes** and gives the archival search a specific date window: approximately April 25–26, 2004, during the NATO delegation's movement toward Afghanistan.

## What is still not established

Do **not** yet write April 25 as the date of the Qatar briefing. The current evidence does not establish:

- the exact date or local time of Burns's CENTCOM visit;
- that the NATO delegation routed through Qatar immediately before Kabul;
- whether the baseball feed was live, delayed, replayed, or recorded;
- that the April 25 game was the only April 2004 Yankees–Red Sox broadcast in which Martínez and Jeter appeared;
- who selected the baseball channel;
- that Burns requested, watched closely, commented on, or reacted to the game;
- any dialogue in the briefing room beyond authenticated source language;
- any participation, reaction, memory, or statement by Libby Baylies.

The time-zone issue matters. A 1:08 p.m. start in New York would have been evening in Qatar, but that coincidence is only useful as a plausibility check, not proof of a live viewing.

2. CENTCOM visitor logs, command briefing schedules, public-affairs photographs, or DV holdings for Burns/North Atlantic Council visitors at Camp As Sayliyah or other Qatar facilities on April 25–26.

4. Contemporary pool reports identifying the delegation's route to Kabul.

## Manuscript status

**Do not promote the exact date yet.** The scene itself remains manuscript-worthy as an attributed Burns-to-Friedman recollection. The new schedule/box-score convergence materially improves the research footing and may permit an exact date once one travel or CENTCOM record confirms the Qatar stop.

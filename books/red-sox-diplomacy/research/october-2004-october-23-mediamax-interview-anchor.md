# October 2004: October 23 Mediamax interview anchor

## Why this matters

The exact October 27, 2004 Red Sox championship-night scene remains unresolved. A dated, contemporaneous interview now supplies a tighter public-record anchor immediately before the championship without pretending to locate Nicholas Burns on October 27.

## Source

Mediamax published an exclusive interview with U.S. Ambassador to NATO Nicholas Burns dated **October 23, 2004**, under the headline “NATO's special focus on the Caucasus should not raise concerns in Moscow.” The surviving page identifies the item as Burns's October 2004 interview with the Armenian Mediamax news agency and records substantive discussion of Armenia-NATO relations, Partnership for Peace, KFOR, and Armenia's Individual Partnership Action Plan.

Source: https://mediamax.am/en/news/interviews/54/

## Evidentiary use

This is useful as a **dated public-activity anchor** four days before Boston won the World Series on October 27. It demonstrates that Burns was publicly engaged in NATO diplomacy in the late-October window and gives a named media outlet that may retain interview metadata, correspondence, photographs, audio, or a fuller original-language/archive record.

It does **not** establish Burns's physical location when the interview was conducted. Publication date is not interview-location evidence, and an interview published October 23 may have been conducted earlier. It therefore cannot establish that Burns was in Brussels, Armenia, or any other specific place on October 23 absent additional metadata.

Most importantly, it does not establish his location, viewing medium, companions, conversation, celebration, or reaction on October 27. No baseball reference appears in the retrieved Mediamax text.

## Archival follow-up

1. Ask Mediamax for the original interview record, including interview date/time, method, location, reporter notes, photographs, audio, and unpublished material.
2. Search USNATO public-affairs calendars and press logs for October 22–28, 2004 for the Mediamax interview and any daily schedule entries.
3. Search NATO headquarters press calendars, visitor logs, speeches, and media advisories for Burns in the same window.
4. Cross-check Burns travel/trip books and State Department executive-secretariat material before assigning any location on October 27.
5. Search contemporaneous Brussels and Boston press only after the official calendar is reconstructed; do not infer a championship-night scene from fandom plus calendar proximity.

## Manuscript boundary

Safe now: late October 2004 can be shown as an active NATO-diplomacy period, with a specific Burns interview published October 23.

Not safe: “four days before the Red Sox won, Burns was in Brussels,” unless a location-bearing source establishes that; any account of Burns watching, hearing, celebrating, discussing, or reacting to Game 4; or any first-person material attributed to Libby Baylies without authentication.

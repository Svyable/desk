# 2004 World Series Gap Note — Red Sox Diplomacy

The manuscript has strong pre-2004 and post-2004 evidence, but the immediate championship reaction remains a documentary gap.

## What is documented

- In 2002 at Boston College, Burns publicly identified himself as a loyal member of Red Sox Nation and joked/predicted about the end of the long championship drought.
- In March 2005, shortly after becoming Under Secretary for Political Affairs, Burns said that Red Sox Nation had “indeed found redemption” in its New England Calvinist tradition after beating the Yankees and Cardinals.
- During October 2004 Burns was serving as U.S. Ambassador to NATO and was occupied with alliance business, including Afghanistan, NATO-Russia relations, and other major security questions.

## What is not yet documented

We do not yet have a reliable contemporaneous source establishing:

- where Burns watched Game 4 of the 2004 World Series;
- who he watched it with;
- his immediate reaction when the Red Sox clinched;
- whether the NATO mission marked the championship publicly;
- whether Burns used the championship in an October or November 2004 speech or interview;
- whether family correspondence, embassy records, or colleagues preserved an anecdote.

Do not invent any of these details.

## Searches completed in this pass

Broad searches across 2004 NATO/State archives and contemporary web reporting located Burns’s official NATO appearances around October and November 2004 but did not surface a reliable immediate Red Sox reaction. The strongest attributable championship statement remains the March 23, 2005 USIP speech.

## Editorial implication

The chapter can safely use the 2002 prediction and 2005 “redemption” line as a before-and-after frame. Until the immediate reaction is documented, the actual clinching night should remain offstage rather than reconstructed from assumption.

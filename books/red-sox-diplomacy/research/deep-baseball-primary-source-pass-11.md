# Deep Baseball Primary-Source Pass 11 — naming the Red Sox-side archive

## Purpose

The Greece-support problem has now narrowed enough that “search the Red Sox archive” is too vague.

Contemporaneous reporting establishes that the Boston Red Sox were helping / had pledged support to Greek baseball in 1999–2000. Separate evidence establishes that Chuck Samiotes — one of the documented Boston Greek-American grassroots organizers — was simultaneously connected through his engineering firm to the joint MLB / Red Sox Jim Rice Field project created around the 1999 All-Star Game.

This pass asks a narrower question: **which Red Sox offices and people were positioned closest to that 1999 development/community work?**

It still does not identify who approved the Greece support. It does produce a much better order of operations for archival and witness research.

## 1. Dick Bresciani was not merely “the PR guy” — he chaired the 1999 All-Star task force

The Red Sox's own institutional obituary for Richard “Dick” Bresciani says he had been **Vice President of Public Affairs since 1996** and was **chairman of the Red Sox Task Force Committee for the 1999 All-Star Game**.

Source:
https://www.mlb.com/news/longtime-red-sox-public-relations-head-dick-bresciani-passes-away/c-102858356

That is newly important because Jim Rice Field was explicitly an All-Star-linked MLB / Red Sox development project. Boston municipal records say MLB and the Red Sox contributed $435,382 toward the field; Samiotes Consultants says it donated the civil engineering and collaborated with MLB, the Red Sox, the City of Boston, HOK and the South End Baseball Program.

Sources:
- https://www.cityofboston.gov/parks/pdfs/os3n.pdf
- https://www.samiotes.com/jim-rice-field

### What this changes

Bresciani now sits much closer to the Samiotes/Red Sox overlap than the earlier research note could establish.

The relevant sequence is:

- Bresciani: Red Sox VP/Public Affairs and chairman of the club's 1999 All-Star task force;
- Jim Rice Field: a joint MLB/Red Sox All-Star-linked community baseball project;
- Samiotes Consultants: donated engineering and says it collaborated with the Red Sox / MLB on that project;
- Samiotes himself: already teaching baseball in Greece in 1997–98;
- September 1999: contemporary reporting says the Red Sox and commissioner's office were helping Greek baseball.

### Guardrail

This is still **network proximity**, not proof that Bresciani handled Greece or personally knew of Samiotes's Greek project.

Do not write that Bresciani authorized, coordinated or even discussed the Greece support until a Red Sox, MLB, embassy or witness record says so.

## 2. Ron Burton Jr. emerges as the other high-value Red Sox-side name

A 2022 local report about a longtime Red Sox supplier quotes the supplier saying **Ron Burton Jr. gave him an opportunity to provide branded products for Red Sox Community Relations twenty-six years earlier**, placing Burton in that Red Sox community-relations network around **1996**.

Source:
https://lynnjournal.com/2022/09/15/red-sox-honor-jazzy-sportswear-of-lynn/

A later book, *Fenway Lives*, lists Burton as **Community Relations Manager** inside the Red Sox administration, alongside Bresciani as Vice President of Public Affairs.

Bibliographic/index source:
https://cincinnatistate.ecampus.com/fenway-lives-team-behind-team-people-who/bk/9781579400903

### Why this matters

The unresolved Greece support may have sat in public affairs, community relations, baseball operations, ownership, or MLB International.

Burton gives the research a specific community-relations route that was active before the 1999–2000 Greece window rather than relying only on later Red Sox Foundation staff structures.

### Guardrail

The surviving public evidence does **not** yet connect Burton personally to:

- Jim Rice Field in 1999;
- Samiotes;
- Burns;
- Greece;
- the Hellenic federation;
- any international baseball program.

He is a **candidate witness/archive node**, not a participant in the Greek project unless further evidence establishes it.

## 3. The Red Sox-side search hierarchy should change

The best order is now:

### Tier 1 — Public Affairs / 1999 All-Star records

**Dick Bresciani**

Search:
- 1999 All-Star task-force correspondence;
- Jim Rice Field / Ramsay Park;
- Chuck Samiotes / Samiotes Consultants;
- MLB RBI;
- Jim Small / MLB International;
- Nicholas Burns;
- Greece / Greek baseball / Hellenic Amateur Baseball Federation;
- Peter Angelos / Baltimore Orioles;
- Bill Galatis / Chris Karalekas.

Reason: Bresciani is explicitly tied to the All-Star organizational machinery from which the known Samiotes/Red Sox development project emerged.

### Tier 2 — Community Relations records

**Ron Burton Jr.** and department files.

Search:
- Jim Rice Field;
- RBI / youth baseball development;
- donated equipment;
- clinics;
- international requests;
- Greek-American community contacts;
- Samiotes / Galatis;
- Burns / Embassy Athens.

Reason: if the club's Greece pledge concerned equipment, clinics, youth development, promotional help or community programming rather than player scouting, Community Relations is a more plausible records series than baseball operations.

### Tier 3 — Executive / ownership files

**John Harrington** and executive correspondence.

Reason: Harrington was the club's chief executive authority in the period and may have approved unusual institutional commitments even if implementation happened elsewhere.

### Tier 4 — Baseball operations

**Dan Duquette** and international/scouting files.

Reason: use this route if the support proves to involve instruction, talent evaluation, scouts, player development or baseball personnel rather than equipment/community work.

## 4. The 1999 media guide becomes a practical archive object

A surviving **1999 Boston Red Sox Media Guide** is catalogued online and has circulated through Archive.org / Wayback citations.

Its highest value for this project is not season statistics. It is the administrative directory and departmental structure.

Recover and inspect the guide for:

- Public Affairs staff;
- Community Relations staff;
- All-Star planning personnel;
- executive assistants / correspondence offices;
- Baseball Operations / Player Development personnel;
- any international or MLB liaison roles.

The directory can convert a two-name hypothesis into a complete 1999 contact map.

## 5. Why this is stronger than another generic Red Sox search

The earlier search problem was combinatorial: “Red Sox + Greece + Burns” rarely surfaces internal operations because the public articles mention the institution but not the employee.

The new approach follows a documented adjacent project:

**1999 All-Star Game -> Jim Rice Field -> Samiotes Consultants -> Red Sox/MLB development network**

and then asks which Red Sox offices owned that work.

That is a much better archival question than asking whether the Red Sox “helped Greece,” which we already know from contemporary reporting.

## 6. What would actually close the loop

Any one of the following would materially resolve the mystery:

1. a Red Sox letter/email/memo to Burns, the embassy, the Greek federation, Samiotes, Galatis, Karalekas or MLB International;
2. a Bresciani or Burton calendar/contact note naming Greek baseball;
3. Samiotes testimony identifying his Red Sox contact and saying that contact became involved in Greece;
4. a Red Sox equipment/donation record naming Greece or the Hellenic federation;
5. an MLB International record assigning a specific club contribution to Boston;
6. a Greek federation letter thanking a named Red Sox person or department;
7. an Embassy Athens cable/letter describing the club's promised or delivered support.

Without one of those, preserve the current language: the Red Sox **pledged/helped**, exact mechanics unresolved.

## 7. October 2004 negative result remains intact

A renewed date-bounded search around October 26–28, 2004 again found no reliable public Burns source locating him for the World Series clincher.

The official State/NATO public-speech index has Burns in Romania on October 13, then next obvious indexed appearances in Moscow on November 4 and Berlin on November 8. That gap does not establish that he remained in Brussels, watched at home, watched live, or was off duty.

The championship-night scene therefore remains an archival-calendar / correspondence / witness problem rather than an open-web problem.

## Documentary rule

A better suspect list is not a solved case.

Bresciani's All-Star role and Burton's community-relations role make them higher-value archival targets. They do not make either man part of Greek baseball until a source connects them to it.

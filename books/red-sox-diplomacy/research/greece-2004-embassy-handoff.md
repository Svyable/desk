# Greece 2004: embassy handoff

A contemporaneous Washington Post report dated April 12, 2004 adds a useful institutional detail to the existing Greece research: after Nicholas Burns connected the Greek baseball effort with Peter Angelos, Burns's successor as U.S. ambassador, Thomas J. Miller, helped expedite paperwork for players seeking Greek passports needed for Olympic eligibility. This supports a bounded claim that U.S. Embassy involvement continued beyond Burns's initial brokerage.

Source: Washington Post, It Appears That Baseball Isn't All Greek to the Greeks, April 12, 2004.
https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/sports/2004/04/12/it-appears-that-baseball-isnt-all-greek-to-the-greeks/0d5fc3ca-536e-4df4-8712-c260367f6e51/

Olympedia records Greece finishing seventh in the 2004 Olympic baseball tournament, held August 15-25 at the Helliniko Olympic complex.

Source: Olympedia, Baseball at the 2004 Summer Olympics.
https://www.olympedia.org/editions/26/sports/BBL

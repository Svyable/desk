# Counterpart Network Dossier 01

This dossier tracks the people and institutions around Nicholas Burns whose testimony, records, or institutional histories can corroborate how he practiced diplomacy. It is designed to prevent the manuscript from becoming a Burns-only account.

## Athens: public diplomacy as a networked practice

### Barbara H. Nielsen — Cultural Affairs Officer, Athens (1999–2001)

**Source:** Association for Diplomatic Studies and Training oral history / Greece reader.

Nielsen served in Athens during the Kosovo period, when Greek public opinion was sharply critical of U.S. policy. She identifies Nicholas Burns as the ambassador and describes the public-diplomacy environment as unusually difficult. Her testimony is useful because she was part of the embassy machinery responsible for cultural and educational outreach rather than a later commentator.

**Book use:** Chapters 9–10. Establishes that the embassy's cultural work took place under genuine political pressure. Her testimony supports treating Burns's baseball activity as one element of a larger public-diplomacy effort rather than an isolated hobby.

**Source:** https://adst.org/Readers/Greece.pdf

### Panos Mitsiopoulos — Hellenic baseball organizer

**Source:** Hellenic Baseball Federation history.

The federation's retrospective says Mitsiopoulos approached Burns for help with the Greek Olympic baseball effort. Burns then contacted Senator Paul Sarbanes, who connected the project with Peter Angelos. This chain is valuable because it makes Burns an intermediary and connector, not the sole creator of the project.

**Book use:** Chapter 10. Structure the scene as a network forming: Greek organizer -> U.S. ambassador -> Greek-American senator -> Greek-American MLB owner -> baseball institutions.

**Source:** https://greekbaseball.com/history

### Paul Sarbanes — United States senator

The federation's history identifies Sarbanes as the next link in the chain after Burns. His role also demonstrates the importance of the Greek-American diaspora to the project.

**Research need:** locate Sarbanes papers, constituent files, correspondence, oral-history references, or contemporary press mentioning the Burns baseball request. Do not rely on the federation retrospective alone for specific private exchanges.

### Peter Angelos — Baltimore Orioles owner

A 1999 Washington Post item reports Angelos appearing at a reception held by Burns and the Hellenic Amateur Baseball Federation and notes help from the commissioner's office and the Boston Red Sox. An Associated Press report in 2000 describes Burns coordinating the effort and Angelos as a central organizer.

**Book use:** Chapter 10. Angelos gives the chapter institutional muscle: money, baseball expertise, diaspora identity, and access to MLB infrastructure.

**Sources:**
- https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/sports/1999/09/08/injuries-to-teens-are-still-a-concern/fe2eb9f0-7fc2-4b43-80a4-a9cb6ac94670/
- https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-2000-jan-02-mn-49873-story.html

### Major League Baseball / Boston Red Sox

Contemporary reporting records assistance from Major League Baseball's international operation and the Boston Red Sox. The federation later describes a wider recruitment and development effort that continued after Burns's initial intervention.

**Research need:** MLB International records, Red Sox charitable/international-relations records, correspondence, program materials, coaches, equipment shipments, and personnel involved in the Greek project.

## Athens: Greek media as counterpart

### Greek press response

A November 1999 profile in *Ta Nea* describes Burns's tenure as a turbulent two-year period and recalls his early working meetings with Greek journalists. Even without adopting the article's judgments, the source is useful evidence that Burns treated the press as a sustained diplomatic constituency rather than simply a transmission channel.

**Book use:** Chapter 9. Helps place the reader inside the adversarial Greek media environment in which Burns was trying to make the United States legible and less remote.

**Source:** https://www.tanea.gr/1999/11/06/greece/nikolas-mperns-mia-dietia-me-trikymies/

### Harvard speech, November 1998

A Harvard Crimson report on Burns's appearance at the Kennedy School says he placed strong personal relationships between leaders at the front of a six-part agenda for strengthening U.S.-Greek relations.

**Book use:** Useful contemporary statement of the relationship-centered method he was practicing in Athens.

**Source:** https://www.thecrimson.com/article/1998/11/3/ambassador-to-greece-urges-improved-relations/

## China: educational institutions as diplomatic counterparts

### Chinese Ministry of Education

In March 2024, Education Minister Huai Jinpeng met Burns. The ministry's own account says both sides discussed institutional dialogue, educational cooperation, personnel exchanges, and youth exchange. It records Burns describing educational exchange and cooperation as a cornerstone of the bilateral relationship.

**Book use:** Chapter 18 or 20. Strong Chinese-government-side corroboration that people-to-people exchange was not merely an American talking point during Burns's ambassadorship.

**Source:** https://en.moe.gov.cn/news/press_releases/202403/t20240331_1123305.html

### Duke Kunshan University

A student publication's account of Burns's March 2024 visit shows him discussing the need for the two countries to remain connected despite strategic rivalry, and pressing for more American students to study in mainland China.

**Book use:** Concrete student-facing scene showing the human-channel argument in practice.

**Source:** https://sites.duke.edu/thelilypad/2024/04/07/dku-hosts-us-ambassador-to-china/

### NYU Shanghai

NYU Shanghai's December 2024 account records Burns visiting a class, speaking informally with students, and meeting representatives from Duke Kunshan, Wenzhou-Kean, and the Hopkins-Nanjing Center.

**Book use:** Late-tenure evidence that education remained an operational priority, not simply speech language.

**Source:** https://shanghai.nyu.edu/news/us-ambassador-china-nicholas-burns-visits-nyu-shanghai

### University of Chicago Hong Kong

UChicago's annual report records Burns meeting students and faculty and emphasizing educational and cultural exchange and the need for more Americans to learn about China.

**Book use:** Adds another institutional witness to the exchange strategy.

**Source:** https://global.uchicago.edu/sites/default/files/2024-09/FY24%20Annual%20Report_FINAL.pdf

## Research conclusion

The emerging pattern is not simply that Burns was personable. His diplomatic practice repeatedly worked through intermediary networks that could carry relationships beyond a formal bilateral channel: journalists, cultural officers, diaspora leaders, sports executives, universities, students, educational ministries, and civic institutions. Baseball matters because it sits inside this larger method of representation and connection.

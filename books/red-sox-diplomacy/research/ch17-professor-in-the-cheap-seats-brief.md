# Chapter 17 Brief — The Professor in the Cheap Seats

## Status
Research brief after Chapter 16.

## Working question
What happens when a career diplomat moves from representing policy to teaching how policy is made?

## Documentary spine
Burns retired from the Foreign Service in 2008 and joined Harvard Kennedy School that September as Professor of the Practice of Diplomacy and International Politics. He later founded and chaired the Future of Diplomacy Project and taught negotiation, diplomacy and great-power politics for thirteen years before returning to government in 2021.

The transformation is from official voice to practitioner-teacher.

## Evidence
- Harvard announced Burns's appointment in September 2008 after his retirement from State.
- HKS later described his teaching as centered on diplomatic negotiation and great-power politics.
- A 2011 HKS interview gives Burns's own theory of the professor of practice: practitioners can show students directly how governments succeed and fail.
- Burns created the Future of Diplomacy Project, extending teaching into practitioner interviews, research and training.
- The American Secretaries of State Project later captured extended interviews with former secretaries, turning practitioner memory into a teaching archive.

## Title warning
The title is playful, but do not imply a literal Fenway scene. Leaving government changes the vantage point: the former participant in policy can now watch, criticize, explain and teach without possessing official authority.

Baseball can re-enter through public identity and campus banter, but the chapter's real subject is distance from power.

## Argument
Government trained Burns to protect institutional confidence, maintain message discipline and speak with delegated authority. Teaching requires a different move: expose uncertainty, reconstruct errors, invite challenges, and distinguish what officials knew then from what hindsight makes obvious now.

A professor of practice is useful only if experience becomes examinable rather than automatically authoritative.

## Bridge to Beijing
Burns spent thirteen years teaching diplomacy before President Biden nominated him as ambassador to China in 2021. The next chapter can ask what happens when the professor returns to official representation.

## Guardrails
Do not claim what individual students learned without direct testimony. Do not turn popularity of courses into proof of teaching quality. Do not portray academia as passive observation; Burns remained active in policy debate and advisory work. Do not imply he knew in 2008 that he would return to government as ambassador to China.

## Draft threshold
Medium-High. Seek direct student or colleague evidence on classroom style and a Burns statement distinguishing teaching from official service.

# China 2023: people-to-people strategy context for the baseball material

## Why this matters

The existing China baseball evidence should not float free as a charming sports anecdote. A contemporaneous, long-form public transcript from Burns's China tenure gives a documented policy frame for small-scale cultural and youth engagement: rebuilding people-to-people contact after the pandemic-era collapse in U.S.-China exchange.

This does **not** prove that Burns pitched a baseball on July 4, 2023, or later participated in a youth baseball clinic *because* of this strategy. It does establish that, during the same ambassadorship, he publicly described reconnecting citizens—especially young people—as a major mission priority.

## Primary / near-primary source

**National Committee on U.S.-China Relations, CHINA Town Hall, October 11, 2023.** The organization preserves a full transcript of its live webcast with Ambassador Nicholas Burns from the U.S. Embassy in Beijing.

Source: https://www.ncuscr.org/podcast/cth-2023-nicholas-burns/

Key transcript points:

- Burns said official and unofficial contacts had been "decoupled" and that the United States was determined to keep high-level contact going.
- He supplied concrete exchange numbers: roughly 15,000 American students in China in 2015 versus about 350 at the time of the discussion.
- He called reconnecting Chinese and American citizens—students, NGOs, academics, and legislators—one of the mission's major priorities and described those contacts as "ballast" in the relationship.
- Asked specifically about young people, he urged them to engage with each other, study together, do business together, learn languages, and understand one another's cultures.
- He also described embassy social media and ordinary travel within China as tools for engagement, while warning that diplomacy can become isolated in a bubble.

These statements are useful because they establish the public-diplomacy architecture around the already documented 2023/2024 baseball images and clinic evidence without retroactively assigning an undocumented motive to a particular baseball event.

## Later friction / contextual counterweight

By June 2024, Burns publicly alleged that Chinese authorities had interfered with dozens of U.S. Embassy public events since November 2023. Reuters reported China's Foreign Ministry rejecting that allegation on June 26, 2024. This is useful context for the environment in which U.S. public-facing cultural programming operated, but the competing claims must remain attributed.

Reuters: https://www.reuters.com/world/china/china-rejects-us-envoys-comment-that-it-hinders-people-to-people-exchanges-2024-06-26/

Do **not** state as independently established fact that Chinese authorities obstructed the later baseball clinic unless event-specific evidence is found. The Reuters item establishes the dispute over broader public programming, not interference with baseball.

## Manuscript use

Safe formulation for a China chapter or section:

> Baseball appeared inside a broader embassy effort to rebuild human contact. In an October 2023 China Town Hall, Burns said U.S.-China contacts had been sharply reduced and called reconnecting students, academics, NGOs and other citizens a major priority. That policy context does not prove why any individual baseball event was staged, but it shows that the embassy's sports-facing moments belonged to an ambassadorship publicly preoccupied with restoring person-to-person exchange.

This can sit adjacent to the verified July 4, 2023 pitching photograph and the October 2024 youth-baseball material. Preserve the distinction between (1) observable baseball acts, (2) Burns's stated general public-diplomacy priorities, and (3) any claim about motive or effect.

## Attribution boundaries

- Do not say Burns used baseball *in order to* implement the October 2023 strategy unless an event-specific source says so.
- Do not claim measurable diplomatic effect from the baseball events.
- Do not infer Chinese audience reaction from attendance, photographs, or social-media publication alone.
- Do not infer that the Boston Red Sox organization participated in China programming absent club or mission documentation.
- Do not connect Libby Baylies to these public-diplomacy choices or baseball events without authenticated sourcing.
- Burns's remarks in the NCUSCR transcript are attributable to Burns; the June 2024 obstruction allegation and Chinese denial remain competing attributed claims.

# Greece baseball: Baseball Acropolis photo archive lead

## Why this matters

A surviving Baseball Acropolis photo page adds a visual-evidence lead to the Greece file. Its captions identify:

- “U.S. Ambassador to Greece R. Nicholas Burns with Chris and Gus Karalekas.”
- members of the Hellenic Amateur Baseball Federation together with members of MLB, a Baltimore Orioles contingent, and Boston businessmen;
- Jim Small of Major League Baseball International with Chris Karalekas at the MLB office;
- a meeting at MLB offices involving Syd Thrift, Chris Karalekas, Lou Angelos, and Tom Marudas;
- equipment deliveries and youth clinics in Greece.

Source: Baseball Acropolis, “Photos,” https://www.baseballacropolis.org/photos.htm (accessed 2026-09-25).

Contemporary corroboration: Christian Science Monitor, Aug. 18, 2004, reports that Nicholas Burns described himself as a confirmed Red Sox fan, recruited Peter Angelos, and rallied Major League Baseball’s International Division; Burns called the effort a U.S.–Greek “joint venture.” https://www.csmonitor.com/2004/0818/p02s01-woeu.html

## Evidentiary value

The photo archive is useful for reconstructing the institutional network around Greek baseball and for locating potentially publishable visual artifacts. It is especially useful because its captions distinguish several actors—HABF, MLB, the Orioles contingent, Boston businessmen, and Burns—rather than collapsing all American assistance into “the Red Sox.”

The Burns/Karalekas image may materially improve the Athens section once provenance, date, photographer, rights, and original caption are established.

## Strict boundary

The page is not sufficient to establish that the Boston Red Sox organization supplied any particular equipment, personnel, money, clinic, or other deliverable. “Boston businessmen” is not “Boston Red Sox.” Do not convert the caption into evidence of club participation.

Do not infer what Burns, Chris Karalekas, Gus Karalekas, Peter Angelos, MLB personnel, federation officials, or anyone else said, thought, intended, or accomplished beyond what authenticated sources establish.

Nothing in this source authenticates any statement, recollection, baseball interest, dialogue, or action by Libby Baylies.

## Manuscript disposition

Research-note status pending provenance. The institutional distinction itself is manuscript-useful: the surviving public record clearly supports a Burns–Angelos–Orioles–MLB network, while the exact Red Sox organizational deliverable remains unresolved.

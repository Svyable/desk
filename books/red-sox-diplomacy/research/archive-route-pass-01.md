# Archive Route Pass 01 — Wellesley, Boston College, Jerusalem, and NATO

This file converts the remaining high-value gaps into concrete repositories, collections, folders, and search strategies. The open web is now returning diminishing value on several questions; the next breakthroughs depend on archival interfaces and specific collections.

## 1. Wellesley youth baseball — authentication route

### Wellesley Townsman digital archive

The Wellesley Free Library confirms that the **Wellesley Townsman 1906–2005** is available through a dedicated digital archive, with microfilm extending through December 2023.

Primary access pages:
- https://www.wellesleyfreelibrary.org/engage/your-community/researchwellesley-townsman-newspaper/
- https://www.wellesleyfreelibrary.org/discover-borrow/digital-resources/databases/

The library also publishes a search guide for the digitized archive explaining exact-phrase, all-word, any-word, exclusion, byline, and date-range search modes:
- https://www.wellesleyfreelibrary.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/08/Getting-Started-with-the-Digital-Wellesley-Townsman-1906.pdf

### Priority date windows

Search approximately **1964–1972**, with emphasis on Burns's Little League and Babe Ruth League ages.

Priority terms:

- `Nicholas Burns`
- `Nick Burns`
- `N. Burns`
- `Paul Sanford`
- `Chris Burns` / `Christopher Burns`
- `Little League`
- `Babe Ruth League`
- `St. James`
- `Hunnewell Field`
- `baseball`
- `softball`
- `all-stars`
- likely team/sponsor names surfaced in adjacent articles

### What would materially change the manuscript

Any Townsman item placing Nick Burns and Paul Sanford in the same youth baseball league/team would effectively close the 2007 condolence-authentication problem.

Any item placing Nick and Chris Burns together in St. James softball would independently reinforce the 2015 paired-condolence memory.

A roster, box score, team photograph, award, or season recap naming Burns would also help identify position, age, team, or coach without relying on retrospective memory.

### Yearbook route

The Wellesley Free Library confirms digitized **Wellesleyan yearbooks, 1931–2018**.

Access index:
https://www.wellesleyfreelibrary.org/discover-borrow/digital-resources/databases/

Burns's Harvard CV fixes the senior year at Wellesley High as **1974**. Search 1970–1974 for activities, clubs, athletics, photos, honors, and captions.

Do not infer absence from incomplete OCR.

## 2. Boston College 1974–1978 — exact archival route

### Student newspapers and serials

Boston College Libraries states that **The Heights**, the Boston College Chronicle, and other BC newspapers/serial publications are digitized and available online for reading and keyword searching.

Reference page:
https://libguides.bc.edu/c.php?g=985438&p=9804296

External search-engine indexing is poor, so direct portal searching is required.

Priority queries:

- `Nicholas Burns`
- `Nick Burns`
- `Burns` combined with `history`, `Phi Beta Kappa`, `Sorbonne`, `Paris`, `Mod 36A`
- `Red Sox`
- `World Series` in October 1975
- `Fisk`
- `Yankees` / `Bucky Dent` in October 1978
- `Fenway`
- `baseball`

The first objective is not to prove Burns watched a game. It is to identify whether he appears anywhere in student journalism, campus organizations, honors coverage, letters, or photos during the years surrounding those baseball events.

### Burns Library — 1978 commencement file

The Boston College Office of Public Relations records contain a dedicated **Commencement, 1978** file.

Exact citation:

**Commencement, 1978, Box 36, Folders 37–39; Box 43, Folder 4. Boston College Office of Public Relations records, BC-1986-003. John J. Burns Library.**

Finding aid:
https://findingaids.bc.edu/repositories/2/archival_objects/162944

The scope note says commencement files can contain:

- programs;
- newspaper clippings;
- press releases;
- speech typescripts;
- graduate lists by degree/school and sometimes town;
- honorary-degree material;
- alumni lists.

This will not likely produce baseball by itself, but it can sharpen Burns's documented student and graduation context.

### Office of Public Relations records

Collection overview:
https://findingaids.bc.edu/repositories/2/resources/863

The collection includes central chronological press-release files, faculty publicity, subject files, university publications, and—beginning in 1977—a dedicated staff photographer.

Potential value:

- photographs of Burns or class events;
- graduation publicity;
- student-achievement releases;
- foreign-study material;
- contextual images of campus/Mods in 1977–78.

### Digital BC fact books

Burns Library lists digital Boston College fact books for every relevant academic year from **1974–75 through 1977–78**.

Search index:
https://findingaids.bc.edu/objects?field%5B%5D=keyword&limit=digital_object&q%5B%5D=Boston%20College%20fact%20book

These are contextual rather than Burns-specific, but useful for accurate campus size, enrollment, housing, academic structure, and institutional setting.

### Archival request question

Ask Burns Library reference staff whether there are searchable student-name indexes, alumni questionnaires, Phi Beta Kappa records, study-abroad files, photographs, or Class of 1978 materials that would identify Nicholas Burns beyond commencement.

Burns Library public contact is listed in the finding aids:
- burns@bc.libanswers.com
- 617-552-4861

## 3. Jerusalem 1985–1987 — 1986 World Series gap

The official personnel directory places Burns at the U.S. Consulate General Jerusalem as **ECO/COM** under Consul General Morris Draper.

Directory preservation:
https://www.columbia.edu/cu/lweb/digital/collections/cul/texts/ldpd_6260645_017/ldpd_6260645_017.pdf

Douglas Keene's ADST oral history directly identifies young Nick Burns as working with him on the Palestinian NGO-grant economic-assistance program.

Country reader:
https://www.adst.org/Readers/Israel.pdf

### Baseball-specific search result

A focused search across Burns, Keene, Jerusalem, the 1986 World Series, Mets, Red Sox, and baseball still produces **no direct Burns baseball memory** from the posting.

This remains a genuine blank.

### Highest-value witness targets

Search or interview:

- Douglas R. Keene;
- other 1985–87 Jerusalem Consulate officers listed in period directories;
- colleagues who shared housing/social life or late-night American broadcasts;
- Burns directly.

Specific interview question:

> During the 1986 Mets–Red Sox World Series, were you following the Series from Jerusalem, and if so, how were you receiving games or scores?

Do not assume Armed Forces Network access in Jerusalem simply because Burns later used AFN in Brussels.

## 4. NATO / October 2004 championship night

The State Department's 2004 NATO public index establishes Burns at the Poiana Brasov defense ministerial on **October 13, 2004**, then provides no Burns public event during the decisive October 17–27 Red Sox run. His next major indexed remarks are November 4 in Moscow and November 8 in Berlin.

Index:
https://2001-2009.state.gov/p/eur/rt/nato/c11326.htm

The absence does not locate him.

### What could close the gap

The next useful records are not generic NATO speeches. They are operational/personal calendar records:

1. U.S. Mission to NATO ambassador calendar, October 17–28, 2004.
2. Mission travel itinerary / motor-pool / representational schedule.
3. Mission newsletter or staff bulletin.
4. Public-affairs photographs.
5. Burns correspondence or personal papers.
6. AFN Europe television schedule and feed information for ALCS Games 4–7 and World Series Games 1–4.
7. Interviews with senior USNATO staff present in Brussels during the postseason.

### Precise question

The manuscript does not need a dramatic memory. A single reliable answer to **where Burns physically was on October 27–28, 2004** would materially improve the chronology even if no emotional reaction survives.

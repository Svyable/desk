# NATO December 2003 official-photo correction and manuscript use

## Why this note exists

A Defense Department/DVIDS image is useful visual evidence for Nicholas Burns's NATO period, but its date and caption need to be carried precisely. The official Defense Department page identifies the photograph as **December 1, 2003**, not December 2.

Source: https://www.war.gov/Multimedia/Photos/igphoto/2002020629/

## What the official record establishes

The Defense Department caption states that Secretary of Defense Donald H. Rumsfeld spoke with NATO Secretary General Lord George Robertson at NATO headquarters in Brussels on **December 1, 2003**, during a two-day meeting of allied defense ministers. It explicitly says that U.S. Ambassador to NATO R. Nicholas Burns joined Rumsfeld and Robertson for the informal meeting.

The page supplies an official U.S. government image record and VIRIN (`472702-U-RRF28-834.jpg`). This makes it a strong, attributable visual source for the institutional environment in which Burns worked during the same NATO posting in which his Red Sox fandom is independently documented.

## What it does not establish

The photograph is **not** evidence of a baseball conversation, a Red Sox discussion, or Burns's reaction to the 2003 ALCS. It was taken more than six weeks after the October 16 ALCS Game 7 loss. Do not caption or narrate it as though baseball was being discussed.

It also does not establish Burns's whereabouts on October 27, 2004, when Boston clinched the World Series.

## Manuscript use

This is useful as visual texture for the NATO chapters because it places Burns physically inside the alliance's senior political-military setting: NATO headquarters, with the U.S. defense secretary and NATO secretary general, during a ministerial meeting. It can sit beside—not substitute for—the separately sourced first-person baseball material from Brussels.

A safe caption would be:

> U.S. Ambassador to NATO Nicholas Burns joined Defense Secretary Donald Rumsfeld and NATO Secretary General George Robertson for an informal meeting at NATO headquarters in Brussels, December 1, 2003. U.S. Department of Defense photograph.

## Documentary boundary

Keep two evidence tracks separate:

1. **NATO institutional scene:** this official photograph and official NATO/State records.
2. **Baseball life in Brussels:** Burns's independently documented Red Sox remarks, office artifacts, and the 2003 ALCS viewing account.

The book becomes stronger when those tracks are allowed to coexist without pretending that every NATO room was a baseball scene.

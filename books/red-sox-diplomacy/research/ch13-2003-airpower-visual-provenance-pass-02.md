# Chapter 13 — 2003 airpower visual provenance pass 02

## Scope

A bounded media/research pass for the 2003 NATO/Iraq sequence. The aim is to give the chapter one publication-safe visual object that makes the military consequence of the diplomatic dispute physically legible without pretending that a nearby photograph documents Operation Display Deterrence itself.

## Cleared candidate: F-117 returning from the opening Iraq strike

**Object:** U.S. Air Force photograph, “F-117 first strike.”

- **Date depicted:** March 20, 2003.
- **Creator:** Staff Sgt. Derrick C. Goode, Department of Defense.
- **Repository:** U.S. Air Force.
- **VIRIN:** `030320-F-0365G-001.JPG`.
- **Source:** https://www.af.mil/News/Photos/igphoto/2000593623/
- **Rights statement:** The Air Force item page explicitly states: “IMAGE IS PUBLIC DOMAIN,” says the photograph has been cleared for release, and asks republishers to give the photographer appropriate credit. It also points commercial and non-commercial users to DoD visual-information limitations concerning trademarks, insignia, identifiable personnel, endorsement, and related issues.
- **Commercial-book suitability:** **Cleared with ordinary DoD-use cautions.** The item-level page supplies the public-domain determination, release status, creator, date, VIRIN, and credit expectation. Do not imply DoD endorsement; do not add service marks or insignia as decorative branding.

### What the photograph documents

The Air Force caption identifies an F-117 Nighthawk from the 8th Expeditionary Fighter Squadron landing at an airfield in the Arabian Gulf region after the opening air-strike mission of Operation Iraqi Freedom on March 20, 2003. It states that two F-117s struck a command bunker in the opening attack.

This is useful because Chapter 13’s NATO dispute over defensive assistance to Turkey occurred immediately before the Iraq war became an air war. The image should therefore be used as a **chronological consequence marker**, not as evidence about the February NATO bargaining and not as an illustration of Operation Display Deterrence.

### Claim boundary

Safe: “On March 20, 2003, a U.S. F-117 returned from an opening Operation Iraqi Freedom strike; this federal photograph records the air war that followed the alliance’s February dispute over defensive preparations for Turkey.”

Do **not** say or imply that this aircraft flew from Turkey, participated in Display Deterrence, was enabled by the NATO decision, or represents the Patriot/AWACS deployment. The Air Force caption places the landing only in the “Arabian Gulf region.”

## Proposed manuscript integration

Use only if Chapter 13 needs a single photographic hinge between alliance procedure and the war that followed. Place after the February decision/Display Deterrence chronology and before prose that moves fully into the invasion.

**Caption:** *An F-117 Nighthawk lands in the Arabian Gulf region after an opening Operation Iraqi Freedom strike, March 20, 2003. The photograph records the air war that followed the February NATO dispute over defensive preparations for Turkey; it does not depict Operation Display Deterrence. Department of Defense photo by Staff Sgt. Derrick C. Goode.*

**Credit line:** `Department of Defense photo / Staff Sgt. Derrick C. Goode. Public domain. VIRIN 030320-F-0365G-001.`

**Alt text:** `An F-117 Nighthawk on the runway after returning from an opening Operation Iraqi Freedom strike on March 20, 2003, photographed at an airfield in the Arabian Gulf region.`

### Print / PDF / EPUB treatment

- Keep image, caption, and credit together; avoid a page turn between image and caption.
- Use the original landscape frame unless production testing establishes a crop that does not remove aircraft context.
- Do not overlay chapter titles, pull quotes, flags, NATO marks, or baseball graphics.
- In EPUB, put alt text on the image and keep the source/credit as selectable text immediately after the caption.
- If rendered in grayscale, check aircraft/background separation at final trim size; do not use aggressive contrast that makes the image look archival or older than 2003.

## Rejected near-match: Konya Air Base imagery

The Air Force has excellent item-level public-domain photographs from **Exercise Anatolian Eagle in November 2006** at Konya Air Base, including F-16 imagery by Master Sgt. Ron Przysucha. Example: https://www.af.mil/News/Photos/igphoto/2000532821/. The page explicitly marks the photograph public domain and cleared for release.

Do **not** use it for the 2003 Display Deterrence sequence. It is rights-clean but historically wrong for the event: three years late and documenting a bilateral/joint exercise rather than the NATO AWACS deployment. Its value is as a location/air-base reference during original-map design, not as documentary evidence for 2003.

This is an important production distinction: **rights clearance does not cure chronological mismatch.**

## Original figure recommendation

If no item-level 2003 Display Deterrence photograph with equally explicit rights metadata is found, prefer an original consequence strip rather than a substitute stock-like military image:

`16 Feb DPC decision → 19 Feb implementation authorized → 26 Feb first NATO AWACS patrol → 20 Mar Iraq air war begins → 3 May Display Deterrence ends`

The strip should be typographic, monochrome, and sourced in the caption/source map. Its purpose is chronology, not drama. For EPUB, render the five dated events as semantic text rather than a flattened image.

## Next search target

Highest value remains an item-level **February–May 2003** federal photograph of NATO AWACS at Konya or U.S./Dutch Patriot deployment in southeastern Turkey, with creator, exact date, identifier, release status, and explicit public-domain/reuse language. Search Air Force, Defense Visual Information Distribution Service/DoD archival holdings, Army historical collections, and NATO only where item-level commercial-publication terms are explicit. Do not infer reuse rights from a `.mil` or NATO host alone.

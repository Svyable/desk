# Red Sox Diplomacy — primary-media open routes

This file tracks strong image/video routes that are **not yet cleared or selected for local vendoring**. It exists to keep the acquisition process from quietly converting “located” into “licensed,” or “a relevant archive series exists” into “we have the exact scene.”

## 1. September 11, 2001 — Burns / Robertson / Ralston

A highly relevant photograph exists in the American Foreign Service Association's NATO-at-75 package showing Nicholas Burns with NATO Secretary General George Robertson and Gen. Joseph Ralston in the September 11 context.

**Current state:** `RESEARCH-ONLY / PERMISSION TARGET`.

The published credit is **Courtesy of Nicholas Burns**, not an explicit U.S.-government public-domain grant. Do not copy it into the book repository or treat it as cleared until the rights holder grants publication/repository permission.

The December 19, 2001 DoD photograph acquired separately is useful NATO context. It must never be substituted or captioned as the September 11 scene.

## 2. July 1994 — Clinton / Riga / Baltic-withdrawal photography

The Clinton Presidential Library finding aid identifies photo coverage of the July 5–7, 1994 trip and places Burns in the traveling White House/National Security Council context, including Riga-related meetings.

**Current state:** `ARCHIVE ROUTE / EXACT FRAME NOT YET SELECTED`.

The collection route is strong enough for targeted archival work, but no exact Burns-identifiable downloadable frame has yet been selected and rights/provenance tied to that specific frame. Do not manufacture a FIG. from a finding-aid description.

## 3. Athens — Burns / Libby Baylies Burns / embassy public-diplomacy photography

State Magazine and related federal publications contain period photographs useful to the Athens chapters, including images involving Nicholas Burns, Libby Baylies Burns, Greek participants, and the embassy/courtyard.

**Current state:** `RIGHTS CHECK REQUIRED`.

Publication on a federal website is not by itself proof that every embedded photograph is a federal employee work. Keep these as research routes until the individual image credit or an archive record establishes reuse rights.

## Acquisition rule

An asset moves from this file into `primary-media-acquisition*.json` only when all of the following are true:

1. the exact file/object has been identified;
2. its date and subject are sufficiently documented for the intended caption;
3. the rights/reuse basis is explicit enough for repository inclusion;
4. the media performs a distinct narrative job rather than duplicating a stronger object already in the book.

# Red Sox Diplomacy

*Nicholas Burns, Baseball, and the Art of Representing America*

**Sven Hardy Benson**

---

This is a work of documentary narrative nonfiction. It reconstructs Nicholas Burns’s public life from documented sources. It does not invent dialogue, private thoughts, composite characters, or undocumented scenes.

Where a source can be read online, the book links directly to it in the prose or in the chapter-keyed [Source Notes](source-notes.md). Those links are part of the documentary apparatus: they are there so a reader can follow important claims back toward the record, including competing accounts and places where the record remains incomplete.

The Boston Red Sox, Major League Baseball, and other organizations named in the text are subjects of reporting and history; their names and marks are not used to imply endorsement.

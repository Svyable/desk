# Back Matter

The working manuscript takes its title and central systemic-risk problem from the 2025 paper “Gradual Disempowerment: Systemic Existential Risks from Incremental AI Development” by Jan Kulveit, Raymond Douglas, Nora Ammann, Deger Turan, David Krueger, and David Duvenaud.

The book’s independent contribution is an extended test of that argument against labor economics, organizational delegation, consumer agency, cultural distribution, companionship, public administration, ownership, competitive pressure, reversibility, and the strongest available objections.

The research trail is maintained with the working manuscript in Sven Hardy Benson’s Desk.

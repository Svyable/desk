# Contradictions and Incentives

The strongest contradiction is methodological rather than ideological. Successful Giza discoveries used convergence: multiple muon detectors for the Big Void; muography followed by GPR, ultrasound, ERT and endoscopy for the North Face Corridor. The deep-Khafre claim currently lacks comparable independent convergence.

A second contradiction is monument drift. The 2022 SAR paper concerned Khufu; viral 2025 claims concerned Khafre. Public retellings can make evidence from one monument appear to validate another.

A third is scale. The larger the alleged engineered void, the more independent physical consequences should exist. Extreme claimed dimensions therefore increase falsifiability rather than merely increasing wonder.

Incentives run both directions. Viral media reward maximal geometry and civilizational implication. Institutional archaeology can be cautious because heritage sites demand conservation and authorization. Critics can overstate impossibility before seeing complete data. Claimants can interpret ordinary demands for replication as suppression. None of those incentives decide the geology.

The manuscript should keep personalities secondary to measurement chains.

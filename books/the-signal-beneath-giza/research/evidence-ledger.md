# Four-Class Evidence Ledger

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- Alvarez-era cosmic-ray investigation of Khafre was conducted and published.
- ScanPyramids detected the Big Void in Khufu using three muon-detector approaches.
- The North Face Corridor in Khufu is real; muography, GPR, ultrasound, ERT and direct endoscopic inspection converged.
- Biondi and Malanga published a 2022 SAR Doppler tomography paper about Khufu.
- That 2022 paper is marked retracted as of 10 August 2026.
- ScIDEP published a 2025 peer-reviewed technical paper describing development of muon telescopes for Khafre.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Giza almost certainly contains additional unmapped voids or archaeological features.
- Multimodal geophysics can detect additional structures where geometry and physical contrast permit.
- Future Khafre muography can materially constrain unknown internal density structure.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Eight engineered shafts extending roughly 648 m beneath Khafre.
- Spiral corridors around those shafts and enormous chambers beneath them.
- A connected multi-kilometre engineered underground complex beneath the Giza pyramids.
- Claims that satellite SAR has already established those structures archaeologically.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- The viral Khafre episode may become a canonical case for how inverse-problem imagery acquires false photographic authority.
- A preregistered multimodal campaign could resolve large parts of the dispute without invasive excavation.
- If structures at the claimed scale existed, their cross-physical signatures should make the hypothesis unusually falsifiable.

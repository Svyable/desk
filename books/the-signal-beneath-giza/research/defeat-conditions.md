# Defeat Conditions

The book's central methodological thesis is weakened if the disputed SAR-derived deep structures are independently recovered at matching coordinates and dimensions by well-calibrated muography, gravity, seismic or electrical methods. In that event, the story becomes one of an initially implausible inverse method making a successful prediction.

The deep-structure hypothesis is strongly weakened if preregistered independent modalities with adequate sensitivity fail to detect the density/geometry signatures predicted from the published renderings.

The claim should not be declared defeated by absence from one instrument that lacks geometric sensitivity to the target.

The claim should not be declared confirmed by another visualization produced from the same underlying data or processing family.

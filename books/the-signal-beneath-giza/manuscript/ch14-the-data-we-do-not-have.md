# The Data We Do Not Have

Missing data are not neutral when a claim is highly processed.

To reproduce a subsurface reconstruction, an independent team needs the relevant raw or minimally processed radar observations, orbit and acquisition metadata, preprocessing chain, code or a complete algorithmic specification, parameter choices, calibration exercises, uncertainty estimates and negative controls. If a model was tuned while investigators knew the geometry of famous pyramid interiors, recovery of those interiors is not a blind validation.

The best test is prediction. Give the method a structure whose internal geometry is concealed from the analysts but known to a custodian. Or make the algorithm publish coordinates before a second modality is collected.

The North Face Corridor effectively moved in this direction: muography generated a target, then different near-surface methods localized it, then a camera entered. The chain ended in photons from the room itself.

The deep-Khafre claim currently ends much earlier in the chain.

1960s–1970 — Luis Alvarez and collaborators conduct cosmic-ray work at Khafre and publish *Search for Hidden Chambers in the Pyramids* in Science.

2015 — ScanPyramids begins non-invasive investigation of Egyptian pyramids.

2017 — Three muon detector approaches confirm the ScanPyramids Big Void in Khufu.

2022 — Biondi and Malanga publish SAR Doppler tomography work concerning Khufu.

2023 — Muography precisely characterizes Khufu's North Face Corridor; GPR and ultrasonic work localize the anomaly; endoscopic inspection follows.

2025 — Multimodal GPR/UST/ERT fusion further confirms the North Face Corridor. Viral claims allege enormous structures beneath Khafre. ScIDEP publishes its independent Khafre muography project design.

2026 — The 2022 Biondi-Malanga Remote Sensing paper is marked retracted.

## Rule of evidence

A sensor anomaly is not a room. A reconstruction is not a photograph. A room is not evidence for its proposed purpose. Each promotion requires new evidence.

# The Retraction

On 10 August 2026 the journal marked the 2022 Biondi-Malanga article retracted.

A retraction does not function like a ceremonial curse. It does not prove that every idea associated with the authors is false. It changes the evidentiary status of the publication and requires the reasons and technical dispute to be reconstructed directly before relying on it.

This timing matters. The spectacular Khafre story had already circulated globally in 2025. Public belief moves on a different clock from correction. An image can reach millions before a methods dispute reaches a handful of specialists; a retraction can arrive after the claim has become part of the cultural furniture.

The epistemic asymmetry is severe. 'Eight shafts beneath Giza' is memorable. 'The methodological foundation requires independent validation and a related paper was later retracted' is not.

A serious investigation therefore has to preserve version history. What was claimed when? Which paper actually supported which claim? What changed after criticism? Which datasets were public? Retraction is not the end of the story. It is a timestamp in it.

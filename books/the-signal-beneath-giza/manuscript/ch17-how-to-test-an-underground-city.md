# How to Test an Underground City

Suppose, for the sake of experiment design, that the enormous structures are real.

Do not dig first. Predict first.

Construct a geological model of the plateau. Insert the claimed void geometry. Calculate the density deficit. Predict the gravity field at accessible points. Simulate cosmic-ray muon transmission for realistic detector positions. Model seismic travel-time and reflection signatures. Estimate electrical-resistivity contrasts under plausible fills. Compare all of these with the null model of layered, fractured limestone.

Then preregister coordinates and signatures before collecting new data.

A kilometer-scale engineered complex should not be permitted to exist in only one algorithmic universe. If it is physical, it must inconvenience several branches of physics at once.

If independent modalities converge, the claim becomes extraordinary in the useful sense. If they do not, the rendering becomes a case study in inverse problems rather than ancient engineering. Either result is worth having.

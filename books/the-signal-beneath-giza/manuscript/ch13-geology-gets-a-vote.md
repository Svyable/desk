# Geology Gets a Vote

Before archaeology, limestone.

The Giza Plateau is layered carbonate rock with joints, faults, weathering, karstic possibilities and construction cuts. Any subsurface reconstruction has to compete against natural structure. A line is not a wall merely because software can draw it as one. A low-density region is not automatically a chamber. A repeating signal can come from repeating geology, acquisition geometry or processing.

This is why the most valuable critic of a spectacular archaeological interpretation may be a geologist who does not care about pyramids.

The underground-city hypothesis predicts regular engineered boundaries that cross or modify natural stratigraphy. Geological explanations predict features aligned with bedding, fractures, dissolution and known plateau structure. High-resolution seismic, borehole information where ethically and legally possible, gravity, ERT and geological mapping can force the alternatives apart.

An ancient structure must live in the rock that actually exists.

# A Real Khafre Experiment

ScIDEP is almost too perfectly timed.

The collaboration's 2025 technical paper describes two scintillator-based muon tracking systems and a Geant4 simulation framework for Khafre. One detector can look upward from the king's burial chamber; another can operate outside. Multiple viewpoints can constrain three-dimensional density structure.

This does not mean ScIDEP can automatically confirm or exclude every alleged feature hundreds of metres below the pyramid. Muon geometry, overburden, angular acceptance and detector placement matter. But it represents the correct institutional response to a mystery: build an instrument whose output has a clear physical meaning.

The decisive future paper would not mention internet controversy in its title. It would publish density anomalies, significance, geometry, detector exposure, simulations and uncertainty. Only afterward would anyone ask whether a feature corresponds to architecture.

The boring paper is the one worth waiting for.

# The 2022 Paper

In 2022 Filippo Biondi and Corrado Malanga published a Remote Sensing paper titled *Synthetic Aperture Radar Doppler Tomography Reveals Details of Undiscovered High-Resolution Internal Structure of the Great Pyramid of Giza*.

The title itself made a formidable promise. The method addressed Khufu, not the later viral Khafre underground-complex claim. That difference was repeatedly blurred in public discussion.

A paper being peer reviewed is evidence that a manuscript passed a journal's process. It is not evidence that every physical inference in it is correct. The useful questions remain stubbornly technical: what radar product entered the analysis, what transformations were applied, how were internal features localized, what known structures were recovered blind, what false-positive rate was measured, and can an independent group reproduce the reconstruction?

Those questions became more consequential in 2026, when the paper acquired a word no viral graphic had included: retracted.

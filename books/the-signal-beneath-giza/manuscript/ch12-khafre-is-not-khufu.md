# Khafre Is Not Khufu

The internet tends to turn the Giza pyramids into one pyramid.

They are not interchangeable. The 2017 Big Void is in Khufu. The North Face Corridor is in Khufu. The 2023 GPR and ultrasound campaign at the chevron is Khufu. The dramatic 2025 subterranean-cylinder claims concern Khafre. The 2022 SAR Doppler tomography paper concerned Khufu. The new ScIDEP muography project is being designed for Khafre.

Once these names are kept straight, a strange opportunity appears. The disputed claim and an orthodox, independently developed measurement program now point at the same monument.

ScIDEP's 2025 Journal of Applied Physics paper describes scintillator muon telescopes intended to observe Khafre from the burial chamber and exterior viewpoints. At publication, the collaboration was developing and commissioning detectors; it had not announced discovery of the viral deep structures.

That is exactly what we want: a measurement program whose reason for existing is not to vindicate the viral picture.

# The Signal Beneath Giza

**Subtitle:** Muons, Radar, Hidden Voids, and the Extraordinary Claim Under Khafre

**Author:** Sven Hardy Benson

**Status:** Drafting

A forensic book about what modern instruments have actually detected in and around the Giza pyramids, what they cannot detect, and how a spectacular 2025 claim of kilometer-scale structures beneath Khafre became an experiment in archaeological epistemology.

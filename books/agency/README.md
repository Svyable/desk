*Maximizing Your Agentic Leverage in the New Economy*

Artificial intelligence is moving from answering questions to carrying out work. **Agency** argues that this changes the economic unit of leverage. When intelligence can interpret an objective, form intermediate plans, use tools, observe results, and continue, the scarce resource is no longer cognitive production alone. It becomes the ability to specify intent, grant authority, supply trustworthy context, verify consequence, redirect the system, and still own the outcome.

The book’s central claim is that **intelligence is becoming abundant; controlled consequence is becoming scarce.** The advantage goes not merely to people who use AI more, but to principals who can turn machine capability into useful agency without becoming passengers inside their own systems.

The manuscript develops four recurring concepts: **agentic leverage**, the useful distance an intention can travel through delegated machine action; **verification capacity**, the practical ceiling on safe delegation; **agency debt**, the future loss of control created when delegation outruns understanding, portability, or recovery; and the **sovereignty premium**, the price worth paying in selected places to preserve exit, continuity, and bargaining power.

## Editorial principles

1. **Agency is not activity.** Machine actions count only when they carry an intention toward a useful outcome under appropriate control.
2. **Keep the principal visible.** Delegation should preserve enough authority, evidence, and reversibility that a person or institution can still change course.
3. **Treat verification as production.** The value of cheap generation depends on whether the resulting work can be trusted economically enough to act on.
4. **Do not fetishize human clicks.** Human involvement is valuable when it supplies judgment, accountability, legitimacy, or a genuinely different failure mode — not because a checkbox happened to be touched by skin.
5. **Separate current evidence from durable mechanism.** Model scores and adoption figures are dated; concepts such as transaction cost, permission, provenance, switching cost, and decision rights are stated at a level intended to survive model turnover.
6. **Preserve counter-evidence.** Field tests include places where AI raises local productivity but fails richer standards or moves bottlenecks downstream.
7. **Write for principals, not spectators.** The book is about governing delegated intelligence in work, institutions, and life rather than watching an abstract race toward autonomy.

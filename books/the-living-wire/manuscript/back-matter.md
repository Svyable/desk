# Back Matter

The strongest claim in this manuscript is also the simplest: cable bacteria demonstrate that respiration can be physically distributed over centimetre scales through biological electrical conduction.

The weakest claims are intentionally left weak. No extraterrestrial analogue is asserted. No global methane intervention is proposed. No claim is made that neighboring bacteria universally use cable filaments as electrical utilities. Those are research directions whose status depends on experiment.

The next revision should deepen every chapter with full experimental reconstruction, especially the 2012 discovery, 2019 direct-conduction work, 2021 nickel-fiber chemistry, 2023 community interactions, and 2026 extracellular-electron-transfer study.

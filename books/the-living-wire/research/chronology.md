# Chronology

- 2012 — Long-distance electron transport by filamentous bacteria is reported in Nature.
- 2015 — Cable bacteria are demonstrated in freshwater sediments.
- 2016 — Field work shows cable bacteria controlling seasonal iron–phosphorus sediment dynamics.
- 2019 — Direct electrical measurements identify a highly conductive periplasmic fiber network.
- 2021 — Nickel-containing protein cores are implicated in efficient long-range conduction.
- 2023 — Diverse bacterial flocks are documented around cable bacteria; extracted skeletons are developed as catalytically active electrodes.
- 2025 — Protonic-conduction work appears in preprint form and requires careful peer-reviewed follow-up before strong use.
- 2026 — Electronema aureum GS is shown to perform extracellular electron transfer to insoluble acceptors under anoxic conditions with growth rates comparable to aerobic respiration in the tested experimental setup.

# Document Appendix

## Core experimental sequence

The book should be expanded around five primary experimental records rather than secondary retellings:

1. 2012 Nature — geochemical and microbiological case for centimetre-scale electron transport.
2. 2019 Nature Communications — direct electrical measurement and conductive sheath identification.
3. 2021 Nature Communications — nickel-containing conductive protein core.
4. 2023 Nature Communications — associated bacterial flocks and possible community-level electron sharing.
5. 2026 Nature Communications — extracellular electron transfer to insoluble acceptors under anoxic conditions.

For each, the publication-length pass should record sample provenance, organism/lineage, preparation method, measurement geometry, controls, quantitative result, strongest alternative explanation, and what the experiment did not establish.

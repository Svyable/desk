## Thesis

Earthquake lights occupy an unusually useful borderland between observation and explanation. Some luminous reports can be discarded as power-line arcing, transformers, weather, or retrospective story-making; some remain difficult to dismiss. Laboratory rock-electrical mechanisms make coupling between crustal stress and atmospheric electrical effects physically discussable, but no such mechanism currently converts scattered luminous observations into a validated earthquake-prediction system. The book is about preserving all three statements at once.

## Defeat conditions

The geological EQL thesis should substantially weaken if matched, prospective monitoring shows no excess of classified luminous/electrical anomalies around earthquakes once infrastructure and weather are controlled. The prediction thesis should be rejected if anomaly occurrence lacks prospective sensitivity/specificity sufficient to outperform baseline seismic forecasting or produces unacceptable false-alarm rates.

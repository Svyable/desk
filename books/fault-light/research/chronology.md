# Chronology

- 19th century — Robert Mallet catalogs earthquake phenomena including luminous reports.
- 1960s — Matsushiro earthquake swarm becomes an important modern earthquake-light case series.
- 1988 — Saguenay, Quebec earthquake; luminous observations later enter EQL literature.
- 2006 — St-Laurent, Derr and Freund publish positive-hole / earthquake-light mechanism paper.
- 6 April 2009 — Mw 6.3 L'Aquila earthquake.
- 2010 — Fidani publishes post-event L'Aquila luminous-phenomena catalog.
- 2014 — Thériault et al. publish 65-case rift-environment analysis in *Seismological Research Letters*.
- Present research problem — move from retrospective catalogs to continuously logged, multimodal, denominator-preserving observation.

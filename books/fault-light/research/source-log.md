# Source Log

## Primary / authoritative spine

1. U.S. Geological Survey, “What are earthquake lights?” https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/what-are-earthquake-lights — agency-level statement preserving the active disagreement; explicitly notes power-line arcing as an explanation for some reports.
2. Thériault, R., St-Laurent, F., Freund, F. T., & Derr, J. S. (2014), “Prevalence of Earthquake Lights Associated with Rift Environments,” *Seismological Research Letters* 85(1):159–178. https://doi.org/10.1785/0220130059 — 65-case analysis and rift/subvertical-fault association.
3. NASA publication record for Thériault et al. https://airbornescience.nasa.gov/content/Prevalence_of_Earthquake_Lights_Associated_with_Rift_Environments
4. St-Laurent, F., Derr, J. S., & Freund, F. T. (2006), “Earthquake lights and the stress-activation of positive hole charge carriers in rocks,” *Physics and Chemistry of the Earth* 31:305–312. USGS record: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/earthquake-lights-and-stress-activation-positive-hole-charge-carriers-rocks ; DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pce.2006.02.003
5. Fidani, C. (2010), “The earthquake lights (EQL) of the 6 April 2009 Aquila earthquake, in Central Italy,” *Natural Hazards and Earth System Sciences* 10:967–978. https://doi.org/10.5194/nhess-10-967-2010 — testimonial catalog; useful but not a prospective prediction dataset.
6. Witze, A. (2014), “Earthquake lights linked to rift zones,” *Nature*. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature.2014.14455 — contemporary scientific-news framing and references.

## Claim boundaries

- USGS does **not** endorse all reports as genuine EQL; it explicitly records disagreement.
- The 2014 65-case study is retrospective and does not establish forecast skill.
- Positive-hole mechanisms are proposed physical pathways, not proof that any specific historical light was caused by crustal charge.
- L'Aquila testimony is post-event collection and therefore vulnerable to reporting and memory effects.
- Power-line and transformer effects must be treated as first-order confounds, not footnotes.

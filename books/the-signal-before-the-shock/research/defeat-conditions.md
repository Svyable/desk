# Defeat Conditions and Prospective Research Protocol

A theory that can survive every earthquake has explained none.

## Defeat conditions

The book's synthesis is materially weakened if a sufficiently powered prospective program finds that:

1. non-seismic channels fail to improve proper probabilistic forecast scores over strong seismicity-only baselines across multiple independent regions;
2. claimed magnetic effects track geomagnetic/reference stations or instrument-health variables better than seismic preparation;
3. claimed ionospheric effects vanish under strictly causal preprocessing and matched global controls;
4. radon/gas anomalies are fully explained by local environmental covariates or do not spatially track future rupture regions;
5. laboratory electrical mechanisms predict field amplitudes below detection or wrong spatial/spectral signatures;
6. multimodal gains disappear after accounting for covariance among shared confounders;
7. results fail geography transfer after physically justified recalibration.

## Proposed protocol

- Fix target earthquake definitions, spatial cells and forecast horizons prospectively.
- Maintain strong ETAS/spatiotemporal seismic baselines.
- Store immutable raw streams and engineering telemetry.
- Require strictly causal transforms using no future samples.
- Timestamp every alarm/probability forecast before outcomes.
- Score continuously, including all false alarms and quiet time.
- Publish missed earthquakes as prominently as hits.
- Include low-seismicity control stations and global space-weather controls.
- Lock model versions for evaluation epochs.
- Separate exploratory data from sealed scoring data.
- Replicate any gain in a second tectonic/geological region.
- Predefine the information-gain threshold that would justify claiming a precursor contributes forecast value.

## What success would not mean

Even a statistically real precursor need not permit deterministic prediction, evacuation decisions or a universal mechanism. Scientific signal, operational forecast value and public-warning utility are three different thresholds.

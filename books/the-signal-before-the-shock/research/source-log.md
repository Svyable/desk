# Source Log

Checked for this draft September 10, 2026. Links are public and lawful. Classification below concerns what each source can establish, not prestige.

## Official / authoritative

- U.S. Geological Survey, **Are earthquakes associated with variations in the geomagnetic field?** https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/are-earthquakes-associated-variations-geomagnetic-field — Current institutional position: electromagnetic variations after earthquakes are observed; no convincing evidence of electromagnetic precursors after decades of work. **Class 1 for institutional position; not proof of impossibility.**
- USGS, Roeloffs & Langbein, **The earthquake prediction experiment at Parkfield, California** (1994). https://www.usgs.gov/publications/earthquake-prediction-experiment-parkfield-california ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1029/94RG01114 — Design/history of Parkfield and 1992 warning. **Class 1.**
- USGS, Savage, **The Parkfield prediction fallacy** (1993). https://www.usgs.gov/publications/parkfield-prediction-fallacy ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1785/BSSA0830010001 — Critique of statistical assumptions. **Class 1 as published critique.**
- USGS, Bakun et al., **Implications for prediction and hazard assessment from the 2004 Parkfield earthquake** (Nature, 2005). https://www.usgs.gov/publications/implications-prediction-and-hazard-assessment-2004-parkfield-earthquake ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04067 — Dense observations, lack of obvious precursors, implications for prediction. **Class 1.**
- USGS, Thomas, Love & Johnston, **On the reported magnetic precursor of the 1989 Loma Prieta earthquake** (2009). https://www.usgs.gov/publications/reported-magnetic-precursor-1989-loma-prieta-earthquake ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pepi.2008.11.014 — Long-record/logbook reanalysis attributes canonical signal to sensor-system malfunction. **Class 1 for reanalysis; interpretation contested historically.**
- USGS, Thomas et al., **On the reported ionospheric precursor of the 1999 Hector Mine, California earthquake** (2012). https://www.usgs.gov/publications/reported-ionospheric-precursor-1999-hector-mine-california-earthquake ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1029/2012GL051022 — Finds reported TEC anomaly consistent with normal global variation. **Class 1 for analysis.**

## Peer-reviewed primary / serious secondary

- Fraser-Smith et al., **Low-frequency magnetic field measurements near the epicenter of the Ms 7.1 Loma Prieta Earthquake**, GRL 17 (1990). https://doi.org/10.1029/GL017i009p01465 — Original ULF precursor report. **Class 1 that signal was reported; Class 3 as seismic precursor interpretation.**
- Campbell, **Natural magnetic disturbance fields, not precursors, preceding the Loma Prieta earthquake**, JGR Space Physics (2009). https://doi.org/10.1029/2008JA013932 — Argues relevant magnetic activity was regional/solar-terrestrial. **Class 1 as reanalysis.**
- Yan et al., **Statistical Study on Variations of the Ionospheric Ion Density Observed by DEMETER and Related to Seismic Activities**, JGR Space Physics (2017). https://doi.org/10.1002/2017JA024623 — Superposed-epoch statistical association, especially nighttime roughly five days before M>5 events; explicitly insufficient alone for forecasting. **Class 2 for precursor interpretation.**
- Eisenbeis & Occhipinti, **The TEC Enhancement Before Seismic Events Is an Artifact**, JGR Space Physics (2021). https://doi.org/10.1029/2020JA028733 — Demonstrates sensitivity to reference-curve choices and synthetic generation of apparent precursor. **Class 1 as methodological result; bears against specific Tohoku-style interpretation.**
- Ikuta et al., **Reanalysis of the Ionospheric Total Electron Content Anomalies Around the 2011 Tohoku-Oki and 2016 Kumamoto Earthquakes: Lack of a Clear Precursor of Large Earthquakes**, JGR Space Physics (2021). https://doi.org/10.1029/2021JA029376 — Event reanalysis emphasizing solar/methodological alternatives. **Class 1 as reanalysis.**
- Zhang et al., **Incorporating Non-Seismicity Precursors Into Earthquake Probabilistic Forecasting Model**, Geophysical Research Letters (2025). https://doi.org/10.1029/2025GL117972 — ULF magnetic + CO external excitation added to temporal ETAS around Kakioka; reported forecast gain and randomized anomaly-time test. **Class 2 until independent prospective validation.**
- Alimoradi, Rahimi & De Santis, **Detection of pre-seismic magnetic field anomalies using Swarm satellite data: a case study of the 2025 Mw7.7 Myanmar earthquake**, Scientific Reports 15 (2025). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-20901-1 — Event-centered Swarm analysis. **Class 2/3 depending on causal claim; not prospective proof.**
- **A persistent radon anomaly signal preceding the destructive 7.7 Mw earthquake in Myanmar on March 28, 2025**, Scientific Reports (2025). https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-025-10632-8 — Continuous radon record and pooled correlations. **Class 2; environmental and prospective denominators remain decisive.**

## Mechanism trail to deepen

- Friedemann Freund and collaborators on stress-activated electrical charge carriers/peroxy defects in rocks. Treat laboratory charge generation separately from any earthquake-prediction inference. **Class 1 for specific reproduced laboratory observations where verified; Class 4 for unvalidated field-scale coupling.**
- Lithosphere-atmosphere-ionosphere coupling literature. No single coupling chain is accepted as an operational precursor mechanism. **Class 4 synthesis unless individual stages have independent measurements.**

## Source discipline

Patent filings, mission names, government funding and program existence are never used as evidence that a precursor works. A published anomaly is evidence that an anomaly was reported, not that its proposed cause is established. Retrospective statistical significance is not treated as prospective forecast skill.

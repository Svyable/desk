## Research standard

Historical precedent is used to establish what happened in the precedent, not to lend truth to a modern allegation. Every modern extraordinary claim must supply its own evidence.

For alleged retrieved or reverse-engineered anomalous technology, the manuscript separates: program existence; object existence; provenance; chain of custody; measured properties; successful exploitation; derivative capability; and origin. Evidence for one rung is not automatically evidence for the next.

# Back Matter

## Evidence classes used in this book

The manuscript treats four categories separately. **Documented** means supported by official records, contemporaneous technical papers, or later peer-reviewed analysis with traceable data. **Credible but incomplete** means the claim has serious reporting or technical support but missing records, uncertain attribution, or material model dependence. **Disputed** means a claim exists but lacks adequate independent confirmation or exceeds what the cited evidence establishes. **Speculative synthesis** is the author’s attempt to connect verified facts into a larger interpretation; it is not presented as a historical fact.

## Claim boundaries

This book does not claim that ARGUS or Starfish demonstrated reliable operational control of the magnetosphere; that every satellite failure in 1962–63 was caused by Starfish; that patents or classified program names prove undisclosed capabilities; that high-altitude nuclear testing explains unrelated contemporary anomalous aerial phenomena; or that the 1962 Hawaiian effects can be scaled directly into modern grid-loss estimates without infrastructure-specific modeling.

The strongest established claim is already enough: nuclear tests intentionally and measurably altered near-Earth plasma environments, produced artificial trapped-particle populations, and imposed damaging radiation loads on some spacecraft.

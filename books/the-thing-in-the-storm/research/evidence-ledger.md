# Evidence ledger — THE THING IN THE STORM

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- Cen, Jianyong; Yuan, Ping; Xue, Simin. “Observation of the Optical and Spectral Characteristics of Ball Lightning.” *Physical Review Letters* 112, 035001 (2014). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.112.035001
  - Two slitless spectrographs observed a luminous event about 0.9 km away following a cloud-to-ground lightning strike.
  - Authors report horizontal motion, evolving size/color/intensity, and radiation from soil elements throughout the event.
  - This is evidence for this measured event, not universal validation of historical ball-lightning reports.

- Lowke, J. J. et al. “Birth of ball lightning.” *Journal of Geophysical Research: Atmospheres* (2012). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1029/2012JD017921
  - Presents detailed witness cases involving aircraft/house windows and a modeled surface-charge/discharge mechanism.
  - The calculations are a proposed mechanism; the historical events were not instrumented demonstrations of that mechanism.

- Hill, E. L. “Ball lightning as a physical phenomenon.” *Journal of Geophysical Research* 65 (1960): 1947–1952. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1029/JZ065i007p01947
  - Useful historical scientific review showing that serious physical treatment predates modern instrumental capture.

- Wu, H.-C. “Relativistic-microwave theory of ball lightning.” *Scientific Reports* 6, 28263 (2016). https://www.nature.com/articles/srep28263
  - Particle-simulation-based microwave/plasma-bubble model. Publication establishes the model and calculations, not that natural ball lightning is caused by this mechanism.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Aircraft and indoor/window reports discussed in Lowke et al. are technically interesting because they constrain mechanisms, but they remain retrospective observations without the multimodal measurements required to identify cause.
- Historical reports from scientifically trained observers should be reconstructed from primary accounts before manuscript use; expertise raises information quality but does not turn memory into instrumentation.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Literal passage of a persistent material object through intact glass.
- Claims of extreme energy release or unusually long persistence without contemporaneous calibrated measurement.
- Any assertion that one mechanism explains the full historical corpus.
- Exotic propulsion, non-human technology, paranormal agency, or weapon-system explanations. No evidence found in the opening research pass requires these hypotheses.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- **Family hypothesis:** “ball lightning” may be an appearance-based bucket containing multiple mechanisms.
- **Continuity error:** a luminous event appearing sequentially across a barrier may be perceived as one object traversing the barrier even if the physical substrate changes.
- **Distribution test:** mechanism quality should be judged partly by predicted event distributions and denominators, not merely whether a model can reproduce a luminous sphere.
- **Transfer prohibition:** one instrumented strange event can raise interest in historical reports without retroactively instrumenting them.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. Soil-linked spectrum is unusually strong evidence for a ground-strike/material mechanism in one event; indoor/aircraft reports are precisely the cases that mechanism appears least naturally suited to explain.
2. Microwave/plasma models may address barrier/aircraft phenomenology better while carrying a different burden for the measured soil lines.
3. Laboratory fireballs can establish physical possibility without establishing identity with natural events.
4. Witness heterogeneity may reflect measurement noise, multiple mechanisms, or both. Do not choose among these prematurely.

## Incentive / distortion audit

- Rare-event publication favors spectacular captures and may obscure the denominator of uneventful observation.
- Retrospective case collections select for memorable events.
- Popular accounts compress “model can explain” into “scientists solved.”
- Anomaly communities can transfer credibility from one measured case to unrelated anecdotes.
- Skeptical accounts can commit the inverse error by treating absence of mechanism as evidence that all observations are perceptual mistakes.

## Publication holds

Do not publish precise historical incidence rates until the sampling frame and denominator are traced.
Do not claim the 2012 Qinghai event is the “first proof ball lightning exists”; formulate it as a rare instrumented optical/spectral observation.
Do not claim the soil spectrum proves the Abrahamson/Dinniss model in full.
Do not describe simulations as experimental reproduction.
Do not use aircraft-window reports without tracing the underlying witness record.

- Compare competing models by predicted spectrum, RF output, energy source, lifetime, motion, barrier behavior and environmental distribution.

## Defeat conditions

The family hypothesis weakens sharply if prospective multimodal observations across diverse environments reveal a stable diagnostic signature and one mechanism predicts those observations substantially better than alternatives.

The continuity-error idea weakens if instrumented barrier events show the same bounded physical structure continuously crossing the barrier.

The testimony-skeptical posture weakens if prospectively coded witness descriptions reliably predict subsequently measured physical subclasses.

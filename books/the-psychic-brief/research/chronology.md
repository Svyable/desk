# Chronology

- **1948** — CIA historians later identify an early memorandum speculating about ESP/hypnosis-related long-distance communication.
- **1972** — CIA begins its own modern investigation of psychic phenomena/remote viewing; DIA threat interest also includes unconventional Soviet capabilities.
- **1973** — SRI parapsychology final-report briefing names Harold Puthoff, Russell Targ, Uri Geller and Ingo Swann in the declassified record.
- **1975–1978** — DIA/CIA produce or reference assessments of Soviet, Czechoslovakian and Warsaw Pact parapsychology/paraphysics research.
- **1977** — CIA ends its first research phase; work continues in Defense channels. Kenneth Kress writes his internal review, later declassified.
- **1978–1979** — INSCOM screens personnel for GRILL FLAME participation; 251 considered and 117 interviewed according to a later memo.
- **1979–1981** — Tehran-hostage and other operational tasking generates extensive session records.
- **Early 1980s** — GRILL FLAME research/operations continue; evaluation protocols and training methods are developed; CENTER LANE emerges in Army program lineage.
- **1984** — CENTER LANE records describe personnel selection and continuation rationale.
- **Mid/late 1980s** — Program lineage continues through SUN STREAK and related structures; SRI/SAIC research continues under Defense sponsorship.
- **1986–FY1995 Q1** — Later summary reports more than 200 operational tasks from military organizations.
- **1994–1995** — DIA creates explicit customer accuracy/value scoring; 40 tasks from five organizations produce roughly one hundred scores by May 1995.
- **1995** — Program transfers to CIA for review. American Institutes for Research evaluates laboratory and operational record. CIA declines to restore operational program.
- **Late 1990s–2000s** — Large body of CIA/DIA/Army records is declassified and released; CIA later describes collection as 11,985 documents totaling 89,901 pages.
- **2021** — CIA publishes an official retrospective summary emphasizing both apparent above-random experiences and lack of reliable intelligence utility.

Dates should be tightened against page-indexed primary records in expansion.

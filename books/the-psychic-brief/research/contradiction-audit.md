# Contradiction and Incentive Audit

## Contradictions to preserve

### "The government studied it" vs "therefore it worked"
Program existence is verified. Capability does not follow.

### "The program ended" vs "therefore nothing anomalous occurred"
Operational termination is strong evidence against demonstrated intelligence utility, not a complete scientific null result.

### Utts vs Hyman
Do not caricature either reviewer. Reconstruct the exact statistical and methodological claims from the AIR report. Their disagreement is itself evidence that "proved" is too coarse a word.

### Strong cases vs explicit misses
Semipalatinsk and the coordinate-error case must be printed beside null/low-correlation sessions. No best-case anthology without denominator work.

### Soviet effort vs Soviet capability
Foreign interest can motivate rational threat assessment without proving the reported psychotronic effects existed.

### Customer satisfaction vs decision value
A customer can find a report interesting without the report improving decisions prospectively.

## Incentives

**Program managers:** preserve funding, personnel, institutional mission; also avoid missing a revolutionary capability.

**Contract researchers:** scientific curiosity, contract continuity, reputational commitment; also genuine incentive to improve protocols and publish.

**Operational customers:** desire any clue in hard cases; risk of confirmation bias and hindsight matching.

**Skeptical evaluators:** incentive to enforce conventional methodological standards; possible reputational cost for endorsing anomalous claims.

**Proponents/participants after declassification:** memoir and speaking incentives; also unique access to lived program history unavailable in dry files.

**Adversary intelligence services:** possible incentive to exaggerate, conceal, misdirect or genuinely explore unconventional capabilities.

**Journalists/public:** spectacular success narratives travel farther than null sessions and scoring disputes.

## Known disinformation pathways to track

- Soviet/Eastern Bloc open literature amplified through Western threat reports.
- Deliberate adversary exaggeration cannot be assumed but must remain a live alternative.
- U.S. classification can itself inflate mythology by making mundane explanations inaccessible.
- Post-program claims of hidden continuation are structurally hard to falsify and therefore require positive evidence, not absence-of-denial reasoning.

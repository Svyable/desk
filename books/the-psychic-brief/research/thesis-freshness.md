## Distinct book-generating tension

Why did a method publicly associated with ridicule survive across CIA, DIA and Army structures for roughly two decades, even though its eventual review found it too unreliable for intelligence operations?

The answer is not assumed to be "because it worked" or "because officials were gullible." The manuscript tests the collision among intermittent apparent signal, low cost, extraordinary upside, Soviet-threat uncertainty, customer demand under information scarcity, and an evaluation problem that kept scientific effect size separate from operational usefulness.

## Boundaries

- Patents, if added later, establish filing and disclosed concept only, not efficacy.
- Participant memoirs must be treated as retrospective Class 2 unless independently anchored to contemporaneous records.
- No claim of a currently classified successor program is made absent public evidence.
- No Soviet/Russian capability claim is promoted merely because a U.S. threat report repeated it.

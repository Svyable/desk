# Back Matter

## A note on method

This manuscript treats declassified records as evidence of what an institution documented, requested, funded, assessed or claimed—not automatic proof that the underlying paranormal proposition was true. Retrospective testimony is attributed. Program existence is separated from capability. Laboratory effect is separated from mechanism. Mechanism is separated from operational utility.

The research apparatus is intentionally part of the book rather than hidden scaffolding because STARGATE is an epistemology story. Readers should be able to see where the floor changes from document to testimony to disputed claim to conjecture.

## Evidence shorthand

**Class 1 — verified/documented:** declassified government records, official program histories, contemporaneous contractual material, published primary research whose existence and reported result are directly inspectable.

**Class 2 — credible but incomplete:** serious testimony, retrospective participant accounts, customer recollections, secondary reconstructions and foreign-program reporting with meaningful but incomplete corroboration.

**Class 3 — disputed/unconfirmed:** operational success claims without reconstructable cueing/scoring chains, paranormal mechanism claims not independently established, allegations of hidden continuation without public evidence.

**Class 4 — speculative synthesis:** explanations for why the program persisted, models of reflexive Soviet/U.S. threat inflation, and hypotheses about what a future rigorous replication might reveal.

## The claim that would most change this book

A modern, preregistered, multi-laboratory remote-viewing study showing independently reproducible target information, calibrated trial-level confidence and prospective improvement in blinded analyst decisions would force a major revision. So would a comprehensive reanalysis demonstrating that the historical laboratory effect disappears under correct handling of target selection, leakage, judging and publication bias.

The manuscript is built to survive either result by changing its scientific conclusion without rewriting its institutional history.

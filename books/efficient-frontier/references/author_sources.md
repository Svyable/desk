# Author Sources

A compact reading trail for the established ideas beneath *Efficient Frontier*. These works support the technical and intellectual foundations. The book's coined language and cross-domain synthesis remain the author's framing.

## Portfolio selection and multiobjective optimization

Harry Markowitz. “Portfolio Selection.” *The Journal of Finance* 7, no. 1 (1952): 77–91. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1952.tb01525.x

Stephen Boyd and Lieven Vandenberghe. *Convex Optimization*. Cambridge University Press, 2004. https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/

Kaisa Miettinen. *Nonlinear Multiobjective Optimization*. Springer, 1998. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-1-4615-5563-6

Kaisa Miettinen. “Some Methods for Nonlinear Multi-objective Optimization.” In *Evolutionary Multi-Criterion Optimization*, Lecture Notes in Computer Science 1993. Springer, 2001. https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-44719-9_1

## Choice, uncertainty, and reversibility

Herbert A. Simon. “A Behavioral Model of Rational Choice.” *The Quarterly Journal of Economics* 69, no. 1 (1955): 99–118. https://doi.org/10.2307/1884852

Frank H. Knight. *Risk, Uncertainty and Profit*. Houghton Mifflin, 1921. Library of Congress digitized record: https://www.loc.gov/item/21012860/

Avinash K. Dixit and Robert S. Pindyck. *Investment under Uncertainty*. Princeton University Press, 1994. https://doi.org/10.2307/j.ctt7sncv

## Robust optimization

Donald Goldfarb and Garud Iyengar. “Robust Portfolio Selection Problems.” *Mathematics of Operations Research* 28, no. 1 (2003): 1–38. https://doi.org/10.1287/moor.28.1.1.14260

Aharon Ben-Tal, Laurent El Ghaoui, and Arkadi Nemirovski. *Robust Optimization*. Princeton University Press, 2009. https://doi.org/10.1515/9781400831050

## Learning, measures, and social cost

T. P. Wright. “Factors Affecting the Cost of Airplanes.” *Journal of the Aeronautical Sciences* 3, no. 4 (1936): 122–128. https://doi.org/10.2514/8.155

C. A. E. Goodhart. “Problems of Monetary Management: The U.K. Experience.” In *Monetary Theory and Practice: The U.K. Experience*. Springer. https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-1-349-17295-5

Ronald H. Coase. “The Problem of Social Cost.” *The Journal of Law and Economics* 3 (1960): 1–44. https://doi.org/10.1086/466560

## Author note

This bibliography is deliberately compact. It is a map of the conceptual neighborhood, not a claim that every adjacent field has been exhausted. A future publication-candidate pass should add claim-level sources for any new concrete case studies while preserving the distinction between established theory and the manuscript's synthesis.

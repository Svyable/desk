# Chapter Source Map

This map identifies the foundational literature beneath each chapter and the primary falsification question. Source IDs refer to `source-registry.md`.

| Chapter | Foundation | What the source supports | What would make the chapter's application fail? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — The Shape of a Good Decision | S01, S02, S03 | Efficient sets, dominance, Pareto tradeoffs, scalarization context. | A claimed dominated option is not actually feasible or becomes nondominated once a material objective/transition cost is restored. |
| 2 — Inside the Frontier | S02, S03, S04, S11 | Dominance, bounded search, transaction/coordination costs. | The supposed waste buys an omitted value, transition is too costly, or coordination makes the “better” point infeasible. |
| 3 — The Frontier Is Not the Answer | S02, S03, S04, S12 | Multiple Pareto points, preference articulation, aspiration/threshold thinking. | The choice can actually be resolved by dominance, or a claimed preference is really a hard legal/ethical/physical constraint (or vice versa). |
| 4 — The Price of Another Unit | S02, S03 | Trade-off surfaces, marginal movement along efficient regions, constraints. | The claimed marginal price is based on average rather than marginal cost, ignores a fixed threshold, or can be changed by substitution/bargaining. |
| 5 — Hidden Axes | S02, S03, S10, S11 | Objective choice, proxy/target concerns, system boundaries/external costs. | An omitted dimension is immaterial to the decision or adding it does not alter dominance, constraint status, or preference. |
| 6 — Frontier Debt | S06, S08, S11 | Intertemporal choice, irreversibility/option loss, uncertain future conditions, transferred costs. | No future stock is consumed, repayment capacity is unaffected, or the alleged borrowing is actually a durable process improvement. |
| 7 — The Counterfeit Frontier | S02, S07, S10, S11 | Model boundary, parameter sensitivity, proxy failure, social-cost boundary. | The claimed improvement survives a fair baseline, stable population, full cost boundary, uncertainty, and subsidy removal. |
| 8 — The Enough Line | S03, S04, S12 | Aspiration levels, preference methods, bounded search/satisficing context. | “Enough” protects a dominated point, lacks a mission/constraint basis, or saved resources have no higher-value competing use. |
| 9 — The Fog Around the Curve | S05, S07, S08 | Risk/uncertainty lineage, parameter/model error, uncertainty sets. | The recommendation remains stable across plausible model/parameter variation and the uncertainty does not affect action. |
| 10 — The Option Value Wedge | S06 | Irreversibility, investment timing, value of waiting and information. | Later information would not change the action, reversal is cheap, or the carrying/expiration cost exceeds the value of preserved branches. |
| 11 — Robust Frontiers | S07, S08 | Robust optimization, uncertainty structures, performance across uncertainty sets. | The uncertainty set is implausibly broad/narrow, protections fail together, or robustness sacrifices more mission value than it preserves. |
| 12 — Learning Moves the Frontier | S09, S04 | Experience effects and bounded organizational learning context. | Repetition does not improve cost/quality/speed/judgment, learning does not transfer, or the environment moves faster than capability accumulates. |
| 13 — When Teams Optimize Different Graphs | S02, S03, S11 | Multiobjective conflict, local versus system boundary, transaction/coordination costs. | The local optimization has no meaningful cross-boundary effect or added governance costs more than the externality it prevents. |
| 14 — Products Are Portfolios | S01, S02, S03 | Portfolio interaction analogy, multiple objectives, Pareto tradeoffs. | Feature interactions are negligible, enabling investments do not reduce future marginal cost, or segmentation does not map to distinct preferences. |
| 15 — Strategy as Feasible-Set Design | S03, S06, S09 | Feasible sets, option value, learning/capability accumulation. | The strategic investment does not relax a binding constraint, create reusable capability, improve with use, or expand future choices. |
| 16 — The Frontier of a Career | S03, S04, S06, S09 | Preference under constraint, bounded search, option value, learning. | The recommended “optionality” is more costly than commitment, the skill does not transfer, or household/structural constraints dominate individual choice. |
| 17 — Who Chooses the Axes | S02, S03, S11 | Objective construction, preference articulation, institutional allocation of social cost. | The analysis treats a value judgment as technical necessity or ignores the legitimate authority required to set/waive a constraint. |
| 18 — Externalities Live Off the Chart | S11 | Social cost, transaction costs, bargaining/institutional alternatives. | The alleged spillover is immaterial/already internalized, or the proposed remedy creates larger transaction/enforcement costs than the harm it addresses. |
| 19 — Frontier Politics Without Frontier Theater | S03, S05, S06, S08, S11 | Multiobjective choice, uncertainty, reversibility, robustness, social cost. | A policy is modeled as feasible but cannot be administered/durable, or distribution/transition changes the choice materially. |
| 20 — The Practice of Better Tradeoffs | S01–S12 as relevant | Synthesis of dominance, preference, uncertainty, reversibility, learning, robustness, and boundary analysis. | The framework adds process without changing a consequential decision, or becomes too cumbersome for the decision's stakes. |

## Coined-language check

Before publication, search the manuscript for each of the following phrases and confirm that none is attributed to an external source as standard terminology: `frontier debt`, `enough line`, `counterfeit frontier`, `option value wedge`, `frontier foreclosure`, `feasible-set design` (when used as the book's strategy phrase).

## Example check

Illustrative hypotheticals can remain uncited when clearly presented as examples. Any example that names a real company, person, regulation, historical event, measured statistic, or current technology condition should receive a claim note before release.

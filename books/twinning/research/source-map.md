# Twinning — Initial Source Map

Accessed 2026-09-12. This is a starting bibliography, not a claim that every source belongs in the final manuscript.

## Definitions, standards, and manufacturing

1. NIST, **Digital Twins for Advanced Manufacturing** (2023)  
   https://www.nist.gov/publications/digital-twins-advanced-manufacturing  
   Use for definition discipline, lifecycle concerns, interoperability, and trust.

2. NIST, **Digital Twins** topic overview  
   https://www.nist.gov/topics/digital-twins  
   Use as a standards-oriented map into NIST work.

3. NIST, **Digital Twins for Advanced Factory Design and Operations**  
   https://www.nist.gov/programs-projects/digital-twins-advanced-factory-design-and-operations  
   Use for factory design, operations, and standards context.

4. Siemens, **Digital Twin**  
   https://www.siemens.com/en-us/technology/digital-twin/  
   Vendor source. Use for Siemens's own framing and product examples, not as a neutral definition.

5. Siemens, **What is a digital twin?**  
   https://www.siemens.com/en-us/company/insights/what-is-a-digital-twin/  
   Vendor explainer useful for terminology comparison.

6. Microsoft Learn, **What is Azure Digital Twins?**  
   https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/digital-twins/overview  
   Use for graph-based modeling of environments and platform architecture.

## BMW / industrial planning

7. NVIDIA, **BMW Digitalized Its First Factory With NVIDIA Omniverse** (2025)  
   https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/digitalized-first-factory/  
   Corporate source. Use to locate the case and claims that require independent corroboration.

8. NVIDIA, **BMW Group Starts Global Rollout of NVIDIA Omniverse to Supercharge Its Production Network** (2023)  
   https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/bmw-group-nvidia-omniverse/  
   Corporate source for the rollout chronology and stated production-planning use cases.

9. NVIDIA, **BMW Group iFACTORY**  
   https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/lp/industries/bmw-group-ifactory/  
   Corporate case page. Useful for leads, visuals, and terminology, not final independent proof.

## Humans and health

10. Björnsson et al., **Digital Twins to Personalize Medicine** / health digital-twin literature to be traced through peer-reviewed sources.  
    Reporting task: identify the strongest original clinical or prospective studies before drafting claims.

11. NLM / PMC, **Digital Twins for Health: A Scoping Review** (2021)  
    https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7946378/  
    Use for taxonomy, state of the field, and recurring technical / ethical challenges. Update with newer reviews before final draft.

## Robotics and physical AI

12. NVIDIA, **Isaac Sim Documentation**  
    https://docs.isaacsim.omniverse.nvidia.com/4.2.0/index.html  
    Technical source for simulation workflows and terminology.

13. NVIDIA Developer Blog, **Supercharge Your Robot Development with NVIDIA Isaac Sim 5.0 and NVIDIA Isaac Lab 2.2** (2025)  
    https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/supercharge-your-robot-development-with-nvidia-isaac-sim-5-0-and-nvidia-isaac-lab-2-2/  
    Vendor source for current platform capabilities; corroborate performance claims independently.

14. NVIDIA, **Physical AI Reference Architecture**  
    https://docs.nvidia.com/physical-ai/reference-architecture/latest/index.html  
    Use for architecture vocabulary around simulation, synthetic data, training, and deployment.

15. NVIDIA, **Robot Simulations**  
    https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/glossary/robot-simulations/  
    Vendor explainer; use mainly to locate technical concepts and case leads.

## Cities, countries, infrastructure, and Earth

16. European Commission, **Destination Earth: new digital twin of the Earth will help tackle climate change and protect nature** (2022)  
    https://commission.europa.eu/news-and-media/news/destination-earth-new-digital-twin-earth-will-help-tackle-climate-change-and-protect-nature-2022-03-31_en  
    Primary institutional source for the program's launch framing.

17. European Commission Digital Strategy, **Destination Earth**  
    https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/destination-earth-new-digital-twin-earth-will-help-tackle-climate-change-and-protect-nature  
    Use to trace program goals, components, and later updates.

18. Interoperable Europe, **National Digital Twin Network Strategy: Lessons Learnt**  
    https://interoperable-europe.ec.europa.eu/collection/living-eu/support-material/national-digital-twin-network-strategy-lessons-learnt  
    Use for cross-government and interoperability questions.

19. GOV.UK, **UK's first digital twin-backed innovation clusters planned for Belfast, Glasgow and Wales**  
    https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uks-first-digital-twin-backed-innovation-clusters-planned-for-belfast-glasgow-and-wales  
    Primary government source for current UK program examples.

20. GOV.UK, **Successful Digital Twin Collaboration Technique piloted in National Digital Twin programme and Natural Digital Twin Programme**  
    https://www.gov.uk/government/news/successful-digital-twin-collaboration-technique-piloted-in-national-digital-twin-programme-and-natural-digital-twin-programme  
    Primary source for collaboration and infrastructure modeling work.

21. GOV.UK, **Parliamentary Secretary launches the National Digital Twin Programme in Cambridge**  
    https://www.gov.uk/government/news/parliamentary-secretary-launches-the-national-digital-twin-programme-in-cambridge  
    Use for the current institutional framing of the UK program.

## Source gaps to fill

- Independent reporting on BMW and other industrial deployments, including measured failures and limitations.
- Current peer-reviewed human digital-twin reviews and validated clinical examples after 2021.
- Peer-reviewed robotics work with quantified sim-to-real gaps.
- A deeply documented city twin tied to an actual public decision, not only a visualization platform.
- Legal scholarship on data rights, model ownership, liability, insurance, and contestability.
- Standards work beyond NIST, including ISO / IEC definitions where licensing and access permit citation.
- Primary history of the `digital twin` term and concept.
- Skeptical sources on category inflation and cases where a so-called twin delivered little operational value.

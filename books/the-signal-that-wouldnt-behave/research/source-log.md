## Evidence warning

CIA FOIA search results reproduce released documents but may have imperfect OCR. Exact quotations, numerical tables and names must be verified against page images before publication. Secondary transcriptions are navigation aids, not substitutes for the released scan.

For Chapter 9, preserve both halves of the quantitative record. A small estimated effect implies many individually nonsignificant studies even under the pro-effect model; conversely, the program's own null and mixed training experiments prevent that statistical fact from becoming evidence that every failure is merely underpowered. The unresolved issue is stability and causal attribution, not whether one p-value wins.

# Research README — The Signal That Wouldn't Behave

## Working thesis

Remote viewing is a control case for the difference between **anomalous statistical structure** and **deployable technology**. The strongest historical version of the story is not "CIA proved ESP" or "CIA wasted decades on obvious nonsense." Later experiments were methodologically stronger than the famous early demonstrations; the 1995 statistical review contained a genuine expert split; but AIR's operational evaluation found no adequate evidence of intelligence value. A real weak effect and a failed intelligence tool are logically compatible.

## Research rules

1. Classification markings establish provenance/security treatment, not truth.
2. Government investigation establishes institutional interest, not efficacy.
3. Operational anecdotes are not upgraded to Class 1 without contemporaneous tasking, output, ground truth and evaluation.
4. Laboratory statistical claims are kept separate from mechanism claims.
5. `Psychic functioning`, `anomalous cognition`, `remote viewing`, and `operational intelligence utility` are not treated as synonyms.
6. Soviet/Russian research claims require provenance to primary archival or scientific records where possible; U.S. intelligence summaries of foreign claims are evidence that the U.S. received/reported the claim, not necessarily that the foreign claim was true.
7. Patents, proposals, training manuals and contractor theories are evidence of filing/procedure, not efficacy.

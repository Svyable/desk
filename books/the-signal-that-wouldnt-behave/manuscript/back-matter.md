# Back Matter

## Evidence posture

This book distinguishes four classes throughout.

**Class 1 — verified / documented.** Declassified government records establish that CIA, Army and DIA sponsored remote-viewing and related psychoenergetics work; operational sessions were conducted; GRILL FLAME, CENTER LANE, SUN STREAK and STAR GATE existed; human-subject and funding decisions were documented; AIR conducted the 1995 review; the intelligence program was not continued after that review. Peer-reviewed publications establish the existence of the scientific dispute and the protocols/results those papers report.

**Class 2 — credible but incomplete.** Participant accounts of particular operational successes, reconstructions of internal decision-making, claims about which session influenced which collection action, and interpretations of Soviet/Russian program scale may be credible while remaining only partly corroborated.

**Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed.** Claims that U.S. remote viewers repeatedly produced actionable intelligence through paranormal means; claims that Soviet forces possessed a mature psychic weapon; claims that STAR GATE secretly continued after 1995 in another compartment; and claims that specific spectacular sessions prove nonlocal consciousness are not treated as established.

**Class 4 — speculative synthesis.** The book's distinction between statistical anomaly and engineering instrument; its model of low-cost strategic hedging; its claim that remote viewing may be understood as a channel-capacity problem if an anomalous effect exists; and the proposed adversarial modern experiment are interpretive constructs, offered to be tested rather than inherited as fact.

## Contradictions that remain live

The central unresolved contradiction is the 1995 expert split. Jessica Utts regarded the statistical record as sufficient to establish psychic functioning; Ray Hyman argued that the evidence did not justify that inference. Their disagreement cannot be settled by quoting either expert alone.

A second contradiction is the mismatch between some laboratory results and the operational record. The book does not resolve this by assuming either that operations were secretly better than AIR found or that all laboratory deviations must be artifacts.

A third contradiction concerns Soviet research. Declassified U.S. documents establish American concern and reporting about Soviet/Eastern Bloc parapsychology. They do not, on their own, establish the capabilities attributed in some threat reporting.

## Defeat conditions for the book's thesis

The thesis would require substantial revision if authenticated records demonstrated a mature remote-viewing operational capability with prospectively measured accuracy, calibrated confidence, repeated unique intelligence contributions, and decision consequences that survived independent audit.

It would also require revision if a modern, preregistered, multi-laboratory adversarial collaboration found no effect at a precision sufficient to exclude the effect sizes reported in the strongest later laboratory literature. That would move the scientific residual substantially toward methodological/history-of-science explanation.

Conversely, robust multi-site replication with cryptographically locked targets, prospective operator calibration and open data would strengthen the case that a real anomalous information effect survives the historical methodological controversy even if intelligence usefulness remained low.

## Research priorities

The next draft should expand every chapter beyond 3,000 words using primary material rather than thematic repetition. Highest-value work includes complete extraction of the 1995 AIR report; case-level reconstruction of operational taskings and contemporaneous evaluations; a quantitative table of later SRI/SAIC experiments and effect sizes; reconstruction of the 1988 National Research Council review; document-level Soviet-threat provenance; and a trial-by-trial distinction between training, research and operational sessions.

The decisive research product would be a dataset linking tasking date, blinding state, viewer, interviewer, target type, output length, contemporaneous score, customer evaluation, later ground truth and whether the information was novel at the time.

That dataset does not yet exist in this manuscript.

## Author's note

The purpose of this book is not to make a paranormal subject respectable by attaching intelligence acronyms to it. It is to make the evidence harder to misuse.

Secrecy is not proof. Ridicule is not refutation. A p-value is not a mechanism. A mechanism is not an operation. An operation is not a useful capability until the customer can tell when to trust it.

That hierarchy is the book.

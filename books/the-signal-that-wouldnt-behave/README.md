## Thesis

Remote viewing is most interesting neither as a proven psychic weapon nor as a stupid Cold War embarrassment. The documented U.S. program survived for more than two decades because it occupied a narrow, unstable middle: laboratory results that some competent statisticians regarded as difficult to explain by chance, protocols that improved after serious criticism, an adversarial intelligence environment that made low-probability possibilities expensive to ignore, and an operational record that never matured into a reliable intelligence instrument. The unresolved object is not a spy superpower. It is the gap between an anomalous statistical effect and a technology.

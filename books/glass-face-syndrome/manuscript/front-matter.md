*Glass Face Syndrome* is a work of cultural and technological nonfiction. The phrase “glass face syndrome” is used here as a metaphor for a learned social condition, not as a medical diagnosis. Likewise, the word “addicted” in the subtitle describes a broad pattern of dependence, compulsion, habit, and social expectation; it should not be read as a claim that ordinary smartphone use constitutes a clinically recognized addiction. Where the research is disputed, small, correlational, or mixed, the book says so.

# Evidence ledger — North Is Not Up

## Class 1 — verified / documented

### Animal magnetic navigation
Magnetoreception is experimentally established in multiple animal groups. Do not compress different taxa, tasks (compass vs map), or mechanisms into a universal receptor claim.

### European robin CRY4 magnetic sensitivity
Xu et al., *Nature* 594, 535–540 (2021), DOI 10.1038/s41586-021-03618-9. Purified European-robin CRY4 showed magnetic sensitivity in vitro and greater sensitivity than tested chicken and pigeon homologues. This is evidence for molecular magnetic sensitivity, not direct proof that CRY4 is the intact bird's complete compass.
Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03618-9

### Human EEG geomagnetic response report
Wang et al., *eNeuro* 6(2) (2019), DOI 10.1523/ENEURO.0483-18.2019. Reported alpha-ERD after specified Earth-strength field rotations, with geometry/polarity dependence and sham controls. The authors interpret this as neural transduction. It does not establish conscious perception or navigation.
Source: https://www.eneuro.org/content/6/2/ENEURO.0483-18.2019

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- CRY4 as the in-vivo avian magnetoreceptor: strong candidate, incomplete receptor-to-behavior chain.
- Human neural magnetoreception: serious peer-reviewed result with open replication/generalization burden.
- Magnetite-based human transduction: mechanistically viable interpretation of polarity sensitivity, not localized receptor proof.
- Multiple magnetic senses in one organism: plausible and supported by differing compass/map phenomena in animal literature, but must be species- and experiment-specific.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Conscious human ability to point magnetic north reliably without ordinary cues.
- Broad geomagnetic effects on mood, cognition, psychiatric state or anomalous experience absent exposure-matched replicated evidence.
- Claims that cryptochrome magnetosensitivity demonstrates quantum consciousness.
- Claims that animal magnetoreception authenticates human paranormal perception.

## Class 4 — synthesis / conjecture

- Magnetoreception may be a model for biologically processed variables that never become conscious percepts.
- The human-senses canon may systematically undercount transduced information by defining a sense through report rather than causal processing.
- Mechanism disputes in magnetoreception provide a reusable epistemic framework for anomalous cognition: phenomenon → transducer → neural signal → behavior → awareness.

## Contradiction audit

1. Human behavioral magnetic-orientation findings have a history of failed replication. The book must reconstruct Baker-era claims and negative replications rather than starting history in 2019.
2. The 2019 EEG interpretation argues polarity sensitivity against the simple radical-pair compass considered there; avian CRY4 evidence cannot simply be imported into humans.
3. In-vitro magnetic sensitivity can be genuine while physiological relevance fails. Concentration, illumination, reaction partners, orientation, temperature, noise and downstream amplification matter.
4. "Quantum" is technically appropriate for electron-spin chemistry and rhetorically hazardous. Every use should cash out into a physical variable.
5. Independent replication of the human EEG result is a publication gate, not a footnote.

## Incentive audit

- Positive magnetoreception results attract disproportionate attention because they bridge quantum physics, animal navigation and human sensory mystery.
- Null replication is less narratively attractive and must be actively searched.
- Quantum-biology framing can amplify legitimate work while inviting unrelated consciousness claims.
- Human-magnetism claims have commercial/wellness adjacency; do not allow those markets to define the scientific question.

## Defeat conditions

The central human argument weakens materially if preregistered independent laboratories using physically audited coil/sham systems fail to reproduce direction/polarity-specific neural responses at adequate power.

The CRY4-centered avian mechanism weakens materially if in-vivo perturbations show that CRY4 is unnecessary for magnetic compass behavior, or if Earth-strength sensitivity cannot be connected to physiologically plausible signaling states.

The broader thesis—that transduction and conscious perception are separable—does not depend on human magnetoreception being true, but magnetoreception ceases to be a valid flagship example if its human neural evidence collapses.

- Full Wang et al. data/code and all published independent replication attempts through 2026.
- Baker 1980s human orientation experiments and named failed replications.
- Foundational Wiltschko bird compass experiments; wavelength and RF-disruption work.
- Sea-turtle magnetic map experiments and replication literature.
- Magnetite receptor claims, retractions/corrections, contamination disputes and anatomical searches.
- 2024 Nature Communications quantum-Zeno radical-pair proposal: DOI/source audit before manuscript use.

## Research hold

Do not write "humans have magnetoreception" as settled. Prefer proposition-level language: a 2019 experiment reported a selective neural response; conscious/behavioral human magnetoreception remains unresolved.

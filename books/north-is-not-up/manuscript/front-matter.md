# North Is Not Up

## Magnetic Animals, Quantum Chemistry, and the Sense Humans May Still Have

Sven Hardy Benson

---

There are four books hiding inside this subject, and they should not be allowed to impersonate one another.

One is settled enough to be ordinary biology: animals use Earth's magnetic field.

One is a mechanism problem: how tissue converts a field of roughly tens of microtesla into chemistry and then neural information.

One is a human experiment: whether a nervous system that does not report a magnetic percept can nevertheless respond to a controlled change in geomagnetic direction.

And one is temptation.

Temptation says that if birds possess a quantum compass, humans must possess a secret faculty; that if an EEG trace moves, an ancient sense has been rediscovered; that if a molecule is field-sensitive in a tube, consciousness has acquired a new portal into physics.

This book will not make those substitutions.

A molecule is not a retina. A retina is not a brain. A brain response is not a perception. A perception is not a useful compass.

That ladder is the book.

### Evidence classes

**Class 1 — verified, documented, replicated enough to carry weight.** Primary papers, established physical chemistry, reproducible animal behavior, and directly inspectable experimental records.

**Class 2 — credible but incomplete.** Serious results whose interpretation remains under active scientific dispute, including candidate receptors and human neural-response findings.

**Class 3 — disputed or unconfirmed.** Claims of conscious human geomagnetic orientation, health/cognitive effects not supported by exposure-matched evidence, and extraordinary extensions built on magnetic vocabulary rather than causal demonstration.

**Class 4 — synthesis and open conjecture.** My argument about what magnetoreception means for the limits of conscious access. These are propositions to test, not findings to inherit.

A strange result does not become stronger because it is strange. A conventional result does not become stronger because it is comfortable. Both have to survive the apparatus.

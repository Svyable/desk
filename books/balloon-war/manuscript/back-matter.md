# Source Trail

This draft is built from public, lawfully available material. No claim of access to classified or non-public records is made.

The manuscript's analytical terms—payload test, platform laundering, recovery inversion, physical budget of cover, competence asymmetry—are authorial synthesis, not historical program terminology. Later drafting should try to break each concept against cases where payloads are dual-use, debris is incomplete, provenance is contested, or mission records contradict physical inference.

# Working Book Brief — Mind Reading Needs Permission

## Reader promise

Build an engineering ladder from demonstrated neural decoding to the strongest covert “mind reading / mind writing” claims and refuse to let evidence jump rungs. The surprising thesis is that cooperation may currently be part of the physical information channel: training, task structure and intentional signal generation are not incidental laboratory conveniences but components of performance.

## Distinct contribution

This is not *Erase, Then Write*. That book tests Cold War behavioral-control claims against disruption versus precise authorship. This book starts with modern measurable neural decoding and asks how calibration, geometry, subject-specificity, language-model priors, invasiveness and cooperation constrain what can be inferred. It uses military neurotechnology as a capability target, not proof of hidden success.

## Evidence classes

### 1 — Verified / documented

- Tang et al., *Nature Neuroscience* (2023), “Semantic reconstruction of continuous language from non-invasive brain recordings”: individualized fMRI semantic decoding; perceived and imagined material; explicit cooperation/resistance experiments. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41593-023-01304-9
- Willett et al., *Nature* (2023), “A high-performance speech neuroprosthesis”: intracortical attempted-speech decoding. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-023-06377-x
- Wairagkar et al., *Nature* (2025), “An instantaneous voice-synthesis neuroprosthesis”: intracortical closed-loop voice synthesis. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09127-3
- Card et al., *Nature Medicine* (2026), long-term independent intracortical BCI use: more than 3,800 hours of at-home use in one participant. https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04414-6
- DARPA N3 official program page: stated goal of high-performance bidirectional nonsurgical interfaces for able-bodied service members; read/write performance goals are program objectives, not proof they were achieved. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/next-generation-nonsurgical-neurotechnology
- DARPA RE-NET official history: neural-interface durability and information extraction for prosthetic control. https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/re-net-reliable-peripheral-interfaces

### 2 — Credible but incomplete

- Public N3 performer outputs and end-of-program performance need a performer-by-performer reconstruction. Program ambition cannot substitute for achieved metrics.
- Generalization across subjects, long-term drift, adversarial resistance and calibration reduction are moving research targets and need current protocol-level comparison.
- Military relevance of civilian/clinical BCI advances is plausible but must be traced through actual contracts, program documents or technical lineage rather than assumed.

### 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

- Covert remote decoding of arbitrary private semantic thought from unknown, uncalibrated, uncooperative subjects at operational stand-off distances.
- Remote installation of specific complex beliefs, memories or operator-selected behavior through a hidden bidirectional neural interface.
- Claims that public BCI limitations merely describe deliberately obsolete cover technology.

No claim in this class may be promoted because a patent exists, a military solicitation states a target, or a neighboring physical mechanism is real.

### 4 — Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- **Consent-as-channel hypothesis:** for some high-dimensional neural decoding, cooperation is not just ethical permission but an information-engineering input because it supplies labeled calibration and stabilizes task state.
- **Subtraction error:** popular accounts preserve a demonstrated output while silently subtracting the implant/scanner, calibration, task, individualized model, known vocabulary and cooperation that produced it.
- **Semantic bottleneck:** transduction may improve faster than the problem of mapping subject-specific neural dynamics onto arbitrary operator-readable meaning.

## Capability ladder

Every case should be scored separately for: signal acquisition; spatial/temporal resolution; invasiveness; stand-off distance; subject calibration; task constraint; vocabulary/content constraint; cooperation; cross-subject transfer; decoder prior contribution; real-time latency; longitudinal stability; resistance; read bandwidth; write specificity; semantic specificity.

A claim only inherits evidence from a lower rung when the missing dimensions are explicitly bridged.

## Strongest conventional model

Neural decoding is advancing rapidly but remains measurement- and task-dependent. Modern results are impressive precisely because engineering teams exploit known cortical targets, individualized training, structured attempted actions, implants or large scanners, and powerful statistical priors. Sensational “mind reading” language compresses those dependencies.

## Strongest extraordinary model

Classified work could combine better sensors, multimodal side channels, long-term passive calibration, coercive access, subject metadata and unpublished transduction methods. Public demonstrations establish that semantic information is physically inferable from neural signals in principle. Therefore the correct burden is not “impossible”; it is a traceable engineering bridge across calibration, geometry, bandwidth, specificity and generalization.

## Contradictions and incentives

- Clinical researchers benefit from emphasizing communication performance but generally publish methods and limitations; headlines benefit from compressing reconstruction into “mind reading.”
- Defense programs benefit from ambitious objectives; objectives must not be reported as achieved capabilities.
- Secrecy advocates can use every missing performance datum as evidence of hidden success. Skeptics can make the opposite error and treat absence from public literature as proof of absence.
- Language models improve decoded output while making attribution harder: what fraction of apparent semantic fidelity came from neural evidence versus linguistic prior?

## Publication holds

Do not say N3 achieved its aspirational read/write metrics until end-of-program evidence is recovered. Do not describe fMRI semantic decoding as stranger-readable or calibration-free. Do not equate attempted speech with unconstrained inner speech. Do not call stimulation “writing a thought.” Do not use patent filings as efficacy evidence. Do not imply possession of classified information.

## Defeat condition

The central thesis weakens substantially if reproducible systems demonstrate high-content semantic decoding from previously unknown subjects without individualized calibration or cooperation, under blinded conditions that quantify language-model priors and adversarial resistance. It fails more strongly if comparable stand-off systems can also write specific, durable, operator-selected semantic content with controlled false-positive and side-effect rates.

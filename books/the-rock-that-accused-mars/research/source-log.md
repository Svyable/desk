## Claim discipline

- Use McKay 1996 for what was proposed, not for proof of life.
- For the magnetite dispute, preserve the strongest version of both sides: Thomas-Keprta's claim concerns a multi-property population assay, while Golden 2004 directly challenges the morphology's exclusivity with a large 3-D comparison and an inorganic laboratory analogue.
- An abiotic analogue demonstrates a viable rival mechanism; it does not by itself prove that the historical Martian sample formed by that route.
- Use other abiotic papers as demonstrations/models of pathways, not proof that a particular pathway occurred unless the sample constraints support it.
- Use Hurowitz 2025 and NASA's own wording: **potential biosignature**.
- Do not upgrade vivianite, greigite, organics, reaction fronts, or habitability individually into a detection of life.
- Track whether multiple observations are causally coupled by one geological process before calling them independent evidence.

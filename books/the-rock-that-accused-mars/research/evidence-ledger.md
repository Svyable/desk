# Evidence Ledger

## Class 1 — Verified / documented

- ALH84001 is a Martian meteorite and contains carbonate globules, magnetite, sulfides and organic carbon compounds studied extensively since the 1990s.
- McKay et al. (1996) explicitly proposed possible relic biogenic activity from multiple lines of evidence; they did not demonstrate life.
- Thomas-Keprta et al. (2001) documented a magnetite subpopulation they argued closely resembled MV-1 bacterial magnetite.
- Multiple groups produced or modeled inorganic routes to relevant magnetite morphologies/compositions and challenged claims of biological exclusivity.
- Steele et al. (2022) reported organic synthesis in ALH84001 associated with abiotic serpentinization/carbonation chemistry.
- Perseverance investigated Cheyava Falls in July 2024 and cached the Sapphire Canyon sample.
- Hurowitz et al. (2025) reported organic-carbon-bearing Bright Angel mudstones with reaction features enriched in likely vivianite and greigite.
- NASA and the Nature paper describe these features as potential biosignatures, explicitly allowing abiotic alternatives.

## Class 2 — Credible but incomplete

- Some ALH84001 magnetites retain unusual similarity to bacterial magnetosome crystals, though biological exclusivity is contested.
- Bright Angel mineral-organic reaction fronts are compatible with microbially mediated redox chemistry analogous to terrestrial environments.
- Returned-sample isotope, molecular and microstructural measurements could materially change the biological odds.

## Class 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

- ALH84001 contains Martian microfossils.
- The relevant magnetite crystals can only be produced biologically.
- Cheyava Falls records ancient microbial metabolism.
- A public Mars result has yet established extraterrestrial life.

## Class 4 — Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- The evidentiary failure mode linking ALH84001 and Cheyava Falls is correlated biosignatures: multiple observations may descend from one abiotic reaction network.
- A causal-independence score could improve life-detection confidence frameworks.
- An explicitly funded abiotic-adversary program should accompany returned-sample biosignature analysis.

## Do not collapse these classes

The central discipline of the book is that a compelling interpretation is not a measurement, and a measurement is not a verdict.

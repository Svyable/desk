# Chapter 11 — Contamination Is a Planetary Process

ALH84001 landed on Earth. Cheyava Falls has been touched by a spacecraft built on Earth. Those are different contamination problems, but both are part of the evidence.

Meteorite studies must distinguish indigenous Martian organics from Antarctic, laboratory and handling contamination. Returned Mars samples will invert the problem: pristine Martian material will arrive inside hardware that passed through terrestrial clean rooms, launch environments, cruise, Mars surface operations, ascent, capture and Earth receiving facilities.

Contamination control is therefore not housekeeping. It is epistemology with swabs.

A future claim of Martian amino-acid chirality, lipid-like molecules or biological polymers will be only as strong as the inventory of terrestrial molecules that could have entered the sample. Witness plates, blanks, hardware archives, clean-room microbiomes and process histories are not bureaucratic accessories. They are negative controls for civilization-scale science.

There is also forward contamination. If terrestrial organisms or biological material reach Mars, they can create future false positives. Planetary protection is often framed as environmental ethics or mission safety. It is also preservation of the experiment.

The cruel possibility is that humanity could discover Earth on Mars.

ALH84001 trained investigators to ask whether a molecule entered after the meteorite arrived. Mars Sample Return must ask the same question before the sample leaves.

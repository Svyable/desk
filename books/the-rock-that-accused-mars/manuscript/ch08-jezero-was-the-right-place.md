# Chapter 8 — Jezero Was the Right Place

Perseverance did not find Cheyava Falls by wandering randomly across Mars. Jezero Crater was selected because an ancient lake and delta offered both habitability and preservation potential. That changes the prior probability before any leopard spot enters the story.

A biosignature is always conditional on environment. A structure found in fresh basalt has a different meaning from the same structure in fine-grained sediment deposited in persistent water. Clay and silt can preserve chemical and morphological records. Deltas concentrate organics. Redox gradients create energy opportunities.

The mission architecture therefore embodies a mature lesson from earlier Mars work: search where biology could have lived and where geology could have kept the receipt.

But habitability is not habitation. This distinction has been repeated so often that it risks becoming ceremonial. It should instead be quantitative. What electron donors and acceptors existed? What temperatures? What water activity? How long did conditions persist? What nutrients were limiting? What abiotic reactions would occur in the same chemical inventory?

Bright Angel is compelling precisely because the chemistry appears to involve redox transformations in sediment containing organic matter, iron, sulfur and phosphorus. On Earth, microbes exploit such gradients with enthusiasm.

Mars may have done the chemistry without them.

The strange advantage of Jezero is that both hypotheses are interesting. If biology participated, the rock may contain one of the most important records ever sampled. If not, Bright Angel reveals a Martian geochemical network capable of building unusually life-like mineral-organic associations under conditions we need to understand before interpreting any future biosignature.

An abiotic answer would not make the sample a failure. It would make the background model better. Astrobiology has historically undervalued background models because the headline belongs to life. But without a background distribution there is no anomaly, only excitement.

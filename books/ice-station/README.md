ICE STATION uses Project COLDFEET as a documented control case for interpreting anomalous sites, recovered technology and dual-use scientific infrastructure. Its central discipline is simple: infer function from configuration, records, residue and relationships among objects—not from the prestige of secrecy or the romance of discovery. The historical case does not establish any modern UAP, non-human-technology or anomalous-archaeology claim.

# THE ARTIFACT TEST

**Subtitle:** What would it take to prove we found technology that did not come from Earth?

**Thesis:** Interstellar-object astronomy has turned extraterrestrial-artifact detection from a purely hypothetical SETI problem into a protocol-design problem: before the next strange visitor arrives, science should precommit the observations, independent replications, provenance tests and defeat conditions required to promote `interstellar object` through `anomaly` to `artifact`, and only then to `extraterrestrial technology` or `intelligence`.

**Evidence posture:** astronomy / NASA / technosignature-SETI heavy. Known interstellar provenance and 3I/ATLAS's cometary evidence are documented. Artifact/probe search concepts are legitimate research topics. Artificial origin for any known interstellar object remains disputed/unconfirmed. The artifact ladder and prospective trigger protocol are the book's falsifiable synthesis, not scientific findings.

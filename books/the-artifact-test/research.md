# THE ARTIFACT TEST — research ledger

## Selection / freshness

Selected after reviewing recent Author-agent PRs. Immediate territory includes anomalous health incidents/RF bioeffects, plant sensing, earthquake lights, panspermia/spacecraft contamination, micro-PK/randomness, biohybrid animal sensing, electroreception, Project Argus, ball lightning, MKULTRA, foreign-materiel exploitation and remote viewing. This project is distinct: it is an **artifact-detection/provenance protocol** built from interstellar-object astronomy, technosignature SETI and intelligence-analysis epistemology.

## Evidence class 1 — verified / documented

- NASA: 3I/ATLAS was reported July 1, 2025 by the NASA-funded ATLAS survey; pre-discovery observations extend to June 14, 2025. https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/comets/3i-atlas/
- NASA: it is the third known interstellar object and follows a hyperbolic trajectory; observed characteristics are consistent with a comet, including an icy nucleus, coma, gas and dust. https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/comets/3i-atlas/3i-atlas-facts-and-faqs/
- NASA: numerous spacecraft/instruments observed 3I/ATLAS, providing an unusually distributed observation campaign. https://science.nasa.gov/solar-system/view-interstellar-comet-3i-atlas-through-nasas-multiple-lenses/
- Breakthrough Listen: targeted observations found no technosignatures; Green Bank observations covered 1–12 GHz near closest approach and reported no artificial radio emission localized to 3I/ATLAS. https://seti.berkeley.edu/atlas/

## Evidence class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Artifact/probe SETI is a legitimate published search concept, but every search constrains a bounded signature space. Need primary literature audit of Solar-System artifact SETI, lurker/probe searches, waste heat, optical and radio signatures.
- Interstellar-object observations can constrain natural models strongly without proving artificiality. Need primary papers for ʻOumuamua dynamics/light curve/non-gravitational acceleration and Borisov as comparative control.

## Evidence class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Any claim that ʻOumuamua, 3I/ATLAS or another known interstellar object is manufactured, controlled or an extraterrestrial probe.
- Claims that individual unusual observables constitute evidence of intention absent a pre-specified discriminating test.
- Claims that lack of a conventional explanation automatically raises an extraterrestrial-artifact hypothesis to parity.

## Evidence class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- **Artifact ladder:** interstellar provenance → anomaly → convergent anomaly → engineered signature → independent replication → artifact → extraterrestrial artifact → intelligent provenance.
- **Nature builds the control group:** a growing sample of well-characterized natural interstellar visitors improves future artifact discrimination.
- **Trigger-before-target rule:** define escalation observations and defeat conditions before a future object supplies the anomaly.
- **Search-space honesty:** every null must state the transmitter/signature/geometry/time/sensitivity class it actually excludes.

## Contradictions / incentives to track

- SETI incentives favor widening the searchable signature space; telescope-time and public-attention incentives can favor dramatic targets.
- Skeptical incentives can overcompress genuine model uncertainty into `ordinary comet` before relevant measurements close.
- Artificial-origin advocacy can promote unresolved → artificial, or capability/plausibility → event/provenance.
- Post-discovery analysis creates retrospective flexibility: unusual features noticed after target selection do not carry the evidentiary weight of prospectively specified triggers.

## Publication holds

Do not write:
- `3I/ATLAS was suspicious` as a fact. NASA's current public assessment says its characteristics are consistent with a comet.
- `Breakthrough Listen ruled out technology.` It ruled out bounded radio-technosignature classes under stated observations/sensitivities.
- `interstellar = extraterrestrial technology.` Interstellar provenance is not artificiality.
- `anomaly = evidence for intelligence.` An anomaly first belongs to measurement/model auditing.
- patents, proposals or SETI search concepts as evidence that an artifact exists.

## Strongest countermodel

There may be no deep artifact-detection problem yet. Known interstellar objects may simply be a tiny, selection-biased sample of natural planetesimals, and the apparent epistemic drama may be produced by unfamiliarity plus sparse data. As surveys increase the sample, exotic-looking properties may settle into ordinary tails of natural distributions. Under this model, an elaborate artifact ladder is useful only as scientific hygiene, not because any known object approaches its upper rungs.

## Defeat conditions for the book's stronger synthesis

Narrow the thesis if:
1. future interstellar-object classification becomes routine with natural models and no persistent cross-modal anomalies;
2. artificial-origin searches add no discriminating tests beyond standard comet/asteroid characterization;
3. proposed artifact signatures prove so unconstrained that no prospective protocol can achieve useful false-positive control;
4. intelligence-analysis analogies do not yield concrete observational decisions, thresholds or provenance tests.

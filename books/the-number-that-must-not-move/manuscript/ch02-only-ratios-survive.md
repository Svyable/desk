# 2. Only Ratios Survive

Before asking whether a constant changes, it helps to ask whether the sentence has content.

Take the speed of light. In the modern SI it is exactly 299,792,458 meters per second because the meter is defined through it. A claim that c changed yesterday is therefore incomplete unless the claimant specifies what physical comparison changed. The same trap appears with Planck's constant, Newton's constant and particle masses when they are discussed as naked numbers with units attached.

Dimensionless constants escape this trap. Alpha, approximately 1/137, measures electromagnetic coupling strength in a way no unit redefinition can erase. The proton-to-electron mass ratio compares two masses directly. Ratios of clock frequencies compare two physical oscillators. These are the proper currency of the subject.

That sounds like housekeeping. It is actually a filter against metaphysical inflation.

If alpha varies, atomic structure shifts in characteristic ways because relativistic corrections scale differently for different electronic configurations. If the electron-proton mass ratio varies, molecular rotational and vibrational transitions respond differently. If quantities connected to the QCD scale or light-quark masses vary, nuclear energies and hyperfine structure can move. A single observed spectral drift is therefore rarely 'a changing constant' by itself. It is a linear combination of sensitivities.

This is why comparisons matter more than absolute claims. A cesium clock and an optical strontium clock do not depend on the same physics. Two optical transitions in the same ion may carry opposite or strongly unequal sensitivity coefficients to alpha. A nuclear transition may depend on cancellations among MeV-scale contributions while producing an eV-scale transition energy. The experiment becomes powerful when nature gives two rulers made from different forces.

The language of constant variation is also shorthand. In a deeper theory, what varies is generally not a number floating free of dynamics. A scalar field changes, and its coupling to Standard Model sectors makes effective parameters vary. The field may be cosmological. It may constitute dark matter. It may be sourced by nearby mass. It may oscillate. It may form defects or clumps. The 'constant' is then an instrument readout.

That reframing matters. Searching for varying alpha is not numerology about 137. It is a way to search for new degrees of freedom that couple to photons, electrons, nucleons or gluonic binding energy.

The constants do not need to drift monotonically. They can oscillate. They can vary in space. They can spike transiently if Earth passes through a field structure. The correct experiment is therefore not merely a longer clock. It is a network of unlike clocks, in unlike places, with unlike sensitivities, compared across unlike timescales.

Only ratios survive the unit argument. Conveniently, ratios are exactly what precision physics has learned to measure obscenely well.

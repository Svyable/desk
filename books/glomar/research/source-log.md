# Source Log

## Primary / authoritative

1. CIA Museum, Project AZORIAN — https://www.cia.gov/legacy/museum/exhibit/project-azorian/
   - Public CIA account of K-129, 16,500-foot depth, Hughes cover, capture system, partial recovery, six recovered Soviet sailors, media exposure, and cancellation.

2. CIA, “The Exposing of Project AZORIAN,” March 17, 2020 — https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/the-exposing-of-project-azorian/
   - Useful chronology for the 1974 Hughes office burglary, February 7, 1975 Los Angeles Times story, March 18 Jack Anderson broadcast, Soviet monitoring, and termination of follow-on recovery.

3. U.S. Department of State, FRUS 1969–1976, Vol. XXXV, AZORIAN document set, Documents 183–208 — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v35/ch4
   - Core documentary spine for authorization, 40 Committee review, executive deliberation, exposure, Soviet response, and termination.

4. FRUS Document 183 — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v35/d183
   - Editorial overview: U.S. location of K-129 in August 1968; August 1970 intelligence priority; target categories; Nixon approval June 7, 1974; partial recovery; follow-on planning; Ford termination approval June 16, 1975.

5. FRUS Document 184 — https://history.state.gov/historicaldocuments/frus1969-76v35/d184
   - Helms to Kissinger, September 14, 1972. Important for technical-risk judgment, cost, public contractor commitments under cover, and concern for government credibility with classified contractors.

6. DOJ Office of Information Policy, 2026 Chief FOIA Officer Report — https://www.justice.gov/oip/united-states-department-justice-2026-chief-foia-officer-report-0
   - Current institutional definition and tracking of Glomar responses; confirms doctrine remains active in 2026.

7. DOJ OIP, Implementing FOIA's Statutory Exclusion Provisions — https://www.justice.gov/oip/blog/implementing-foias-statutory-exclusion-provisions
   - Explains why confirming record existence can itself disclose protected information and distinguishes statutory exclusions from Glomar responses.

8. DOJ OIP, Lenz v. CIA summary, September 30, 2024 — https://www.justice.gov/oip/lenz-v-cia-no-20-3327-2024-wl-4346263-ddc-sept-30-2024-lamberth-j
   - Contemporary judicial application of Glomar and official-acknowledgment limits.

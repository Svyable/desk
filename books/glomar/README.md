## Thesis

Project AZORIAN was not only a nearly impossible attempt to lift a Soviet missile submarine from three miles beneath the Pacific. It was an experiment in engineering secrecy itself: a purpose-built ship, a commercial cover story, compartmented contractors, managed press relationships, and finally a legal doctrine that taught the modern American state how to refuse even to acknowledge whether responsive records exist.

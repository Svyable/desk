# A Black Program Becomes a School

The most strategically interesting thing about CONSTANT PEG is that the secret did not remain inside the secret.

The source had to remain protected, but the lesson had to escape.

A pilot who learns how a MiG looks at close range, where it loses energy, how quickly its engine responds, and which geometry is dangerous has acquired knowledge the government wants in the broader force. Classification therefore creates a translation problem: how do you disseminate the useful consequence without exposing the source?

This is one of the expected signatures of successful reverse engineering. Breakthrough knowledge should leak downstream in sanitized form even when provenance remains hidden.

The leak may appear as changed tactics, new requirements, altered materials specifications, unusual research priorities, revised sensor design, or procurement decisions whose full rationale is classified. It need not reveal the object.

This creates both evidence and confusion. A downstream technology cannot automatically be traced to a hidden source. Human research routinely produces surprising advances. Similar ideas arise independently. Classified requirements can accelerate an existing field without introducing anything non-human.

The correct question is therefore not whether a later technology looks futuristic. It is whether there is a traceable discontinuity that requires an upstream explanation.

CONSTANT PEG gives us the ordinary version. The knowledge that escaped was not a miracle material or propulsion principle. It was tactical familiarity. Pilots became less surprised by the real enemy aircraft.

If alleged exotic exploitation has operated since the mid-twentieth century and succeeded technically, its downstream effects should be larger. Claims about gravity control or extraordinary energy systems imply entire missing branches of engineering. The absence of obvious public transformation does not disprove a compartmented breakthrough, but it forces additional questions about why operational use, accidents, replication, adversary detection, or industrial diffusion have remained so limited.

A black program that learns nothing can remain sterile.

A black program that learns something important eventually faces the problem of teaching.

# The Airplanes We Stole

| | |
|---|---|
| **Author** | Sven Hardy Benson |
| **Status** | Drafting |
| **Chapters** | 20 of 20 drafted |

**Thesis:** The United States really did acquire, hide, dismantle, fly, instrument, maintain, and operationally exploit adversary aircraft under deeply classified programs. HAVE DOUGHNUT, HAVE DRILL, HAVE FERRY, CONSTANT PEG, and the wider foreign-materiel ecosystem therefore provide a rare empirical control case for contemporary crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering allegations: genuine secret hardware programs leave characteristic traces in logistics, personnel, safety, test infrastructure, doctrine, budgets, organizational descendants, adversary behavior, and eventually archives. Those traces do not prove or disprove any non-human-technology claim by themselves, but they make the claim testable in a way folklore does not.

# Research README — The Airplanes We Stole

## Working thesis

Real secret foreign-materiel exploitation programs are a control case for alleged crash-retrieval/reverse-engineering programs. HAVE DOUGHNUT, HAVE DRILL, HAVE FERRY, CONSTANT PEG, the MiG-25 exploitation after Viktor Belenko's defection, and the institutional lineage of foreign-materiel exploitation demonstrate that deeply classified physical programs can remain hidden from the public while still leaving necessary traces in logistics, facilities, people, safety, doctrine, adversary response, and archives.

This does **not** establish that every real secret program leaves the same trace, nor that absence of one expected signature falsifies a program. It creates a comparative evidentiary baseline.

## Evidence ledger

### Class 1 — verified/declassified/documented

- The United States conducted HAVE DOUGHNUT in 1968 to exploit a MiG-21 obtained through Israel; the National Security Archive identifies a roughly 300-page DIA exploitation report.
- HAVE DRILL and HAVE FERRY exploited MiG-17 aircraft in 1969.
- The Air Force officially acknowledges CONSTANT PEG, established in 1977, and the 4477th Test and Evaluation Squadron's use of MiG-17, MiG-21 and MiG-23 aircraft.
- The National Museum of the U.S. Air Force states that the Red Eagles flew more than 15,000 sorties and trained almost 6,000 U.S. aircrew; three Red Eagles died in accidents.
- The Air Force publicly declassified CONSTANT PEG in November 2006.
- The Air Force Historical Research Agency records a Foreign Materiel Exploitation Squadron at NASIC, activated in 2008 at Wright-Patterson AFB.
- AARO's February 2024 Historical Record Report Volume I states that it found no empirical evidence that the U.S. government or private companies have reverse-engineered extraterrestrial technology and says some alleged programs were misidentified authentic sensitive programs, nonexistent programs, or proposed/disestablished efforts.
- AARO states KONA BLUE was proposed but never approved as an operational DHS SAP and never received the alleged off-world material.

### Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Detailed participant reconstructions of CONSTANT PEG acquisition channels, maintenance improvisations, individual aircraft histories, and specific tactical lessons where official summaries do not document every detail.
- The full pathway by which every Red Eagle MiG entered U.S. custody, especially aircraft sourced through shifting Cold War relationships.
- The precise institutional influence of CONSTANT PEG on later air-combat outcomes. The program clearly trained aircrew; causal attribution of later victory ratios is more difficult.
- Some historical accounts of how Belenko's MiG-25 was disassembled and examined before return to the Soviet Union, pending complete primary reconstruction.

### Class 3 — disputed/unconfirmed

- Claims that the U.S. government or contractors possess intact or partial craft of non-human origin.
- Claims of successful reverse engineering of non-human propulsion, energy, materials, or field effects.
- Specific alleged legacy program names lacking authenticated primary records.
- Claims that particular aerospace companies received alien-derived material or technology without independently verifiable chain of custody.
- Claims that every historical UAP event was produced by classified U.S. technology.

### Class 4 — speculative synthesis

- The **retrieval signature**: a proposed set of independent evidence lanes that a long-lived hidden physical exploitation program should tend to produce.
- The idea that maintenance burden is often more diagnostic than the initial recovery story because maintenance repeats.
- The hypothesis that revolutionary reverse engineering should create downstream anomalies in scientific labor, test infrastructure, industrial capability, and adversary collection even when the original object remains hidden.
- The proposal to score alleged programs against historically calibrated black-program signatures rather than against generic claims that secrecy can hide anything.

## Contradictions and incentives audit

### Government incentives

Government agencies have legitimate incentives to protect sources, methods, foreign partnerships, test capabilities, signatures, and vulnerabilities. Those same incentives can create misleading public records, delayed acknowledgment, and technically true denials that do not tell the whole story.

Agencies also have incentives to preserve institutional credibility. AARO's skeptical conclusions should therefore be tested against its documented access, methods, source handling, and subsequent disclosures rather than accepted by authority alone.

### Witness incentives and limits

Cleared witnesses may accurately know that a compartment, facility, contractor, or unusual object exists while being wrong about origin or purpose. Compartmentation is designed to produce partial knowledge. It can therefore increase both secrecy and sincere misinterpretation.

Public whistleblowers may face real professional costs. They can also acquire status, attention, community support, or narrative reinforcement after disclosure. Neither cost nor reward determines truth.

### Media and community incentives

Extraordinary claims travel farther than narrow archival corrections. Repetition can mimic corroboration when multiple accounts derive from the same small network. Skeptical communities can commit the opposite error by treating absence from public archives as proof of nonexistence even for programs known historically to have been hidden for decades.

## Defeat conditions

The book's central synthesis should be revised if:

1. authenticated records or physical evidence establish a U.S. non-human-technology recovery/exploitation program;
2. historical comparison shows that mature physical black programs often leave essentially no multi-lane trace even after many decades;
3. AARO or later official investigations are shown through primary records to have lacked access they publicly claimed to possess in a way material to their conclusions;
4. independently controlled materials analysis demonstrates manufactured isotopic, structural, or functional properties that cannot be reconciled with terrestrial origin and has a strong chain of custody to an alleged retrieval;
5. the proposed retrieval signature proves unable to distinguish acknowledged foreign-materiel programs from rumor-rich nonexistent programs.

## Research posture

Patents are evidence that a filing occurred, not that the claimed technology works. Testimony is evidence that a person made a claim, not proof of the underlying event. Classification is evidence that information is restricted, not that the most extraordinary interpretation is correct. Official denial is evidence of an institutional position, not omniscience. Surviving hardware with provenance is qualitatively stronger than narrative repetition.

# Working chronology

- **16 Aug 1966** — Iraqi Air Force Capt. Munir Redfa defects to Israel in a MiG-21, providing Israel an intact example for exploitation.
- **23 Jan–8 Apr 1968** — U.S. HAVE DOUGHNUT exploitation of the MiG-21 at Groom Lake; declassified sources describe 102 flights / 77 hours over 40 flying days.
- **1969** — HAVE DRILL / HAVE FERRY exploit MiG-17 aircraft; archival summaries describe a combined 224 sorties.
- **6 Sep 1976** — Viktor Belenko defects to Japan in a MiG-25; the aircraft is examined before return to the USSR.
- **1977** — CONSTANT PEG established; 4477th begins institutionalized exposure of U.S. aircrew to real Soviet-designed fighters.
- **1979** — Tonopah Test Range becomes the central Red Eagles operating location.
- **1980** — MiG-23 joins the CONSTANT PEG fleet according to Air Force museum history.
- **1982** — MiG-17s retired from Red Eagles service because of obsolescence/safety concerns.
- **1988** — CONSTANT PEG terminates after more than 15,000 sorties and nearly 6,000 aircrew exposures per official Air Force history.
- **1990** — 4477th TES inactivated.
- **13 Nov 2006** — U.S. Air Force publicly declassifies/acknowledges CONSTANT PEG.
- **15 Apr 2008** — Foreign Materiel Exploitation Squadron activated at NASIC, Wright-Patterson AFB, per AFHRA lineage.
- **29 Oct 2013** — National Security Archive publishes major Area 51 / secret-aircraft / Soviet-MiG document collection including HAVE DOUGHNUT and HAVE DRILL/FERRY material.
- **26 Jul 2023** — David Grusch testifies at House UAP hearing regarding alleged crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering programs; testimony does not provide public physical proof.
- **Feb/8 Mar 2024** — AARO Historical Record Report Vol. I produced/released, finding no empirical evidence of extraterrestrial reverse engineering and describing several alleged programs as misidentified, nonexistent, or disestablished/proposed efforts.

## Source rules

- Patents establish filing, not efficacy.
- Memoirs establish recollection, not automatic contemporaneous fact.
- AARO establishes an official finding, not omniscience.
- Congressional testimony establishes that testimony was given, not the underlying retrieval.
- Public surviving aircraft or authenticated program photographs establish hardware presence when provenance is documented.
- For disputed materials, require both laboratory reproducibility and chain of custody before using composition to support origin.

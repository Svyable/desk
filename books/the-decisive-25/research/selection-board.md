# Selection board — The Decisive 25

This is the working argument board for the twenty-five decade choices. It is not a finished ranking and it is not evidence that the selected person is morally superior to the finalists. The manuscript must be willing to replace a selection when the counterfactual case fails under research.

## Selection rule

For each ten-year interval, ask: **If this person disappeared from the decade, whose absence would create the largest plausible change in the path of the United States?**

Test each finalist on:

1. **Scale** — size of the change in territory, law, political participation, economic organization, technology, war, culture, or state capacity.
2. **Persistence** — whether the effect lasted, compounded, or created institutions and habits inherited by later decades.
3. **Personal leverage** — whether choices particular to the person changed timing, direction, or form, rather than merely placing the person near a transformation already underway.
4. **Counterfactual distance** — how far a plausible United States without that person moves from the history we know.

A selection loses points when the causal story requires pretending movements, institutions, allies, opponents, material conditions, or predecessor work did not exist.

## Research discipline

The board should remain uncomfortable. If every winner becomes a person most readers already admire, the selection rule has quietly collapsed into commemoration. If every winner becomes a contrarian surprise, novelty has replaced judgment.

For each chapter, the research pass should include at least:

- one primary source produced by the selected person or institution directly involved;
- one high-quality institutional or archival source establishing chronology;
- one serious secondary work capable of challenging the chapter's preferred causal story;
- one explicit counterargument naming the strongest finalist and explaining why the finalist loses;
- one falsification note stating what evidence would cause the selection to change.

The final book should preserve uncertainty where the counterfactual is genuinely close.

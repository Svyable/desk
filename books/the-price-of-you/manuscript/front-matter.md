## A note on method

Every historical claim, quotation, statistic, lawsuit, and regulatory action in this book is drawn from a cited primary or secondary source, listed in the research trail behind the manuscript. Where the evidence is thin, contested, or still unfolding — and a great deal of the last five years of pricing history is still unfolding — the text says so. No dialogue, inner thought, or scene has been invented; where a conversation is recounted, it is recounted from a source that recorded it, and that source is named.

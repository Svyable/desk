# Evidence ledger — THE CONTAMINATION PROBLEM

## Governing collision

Planetary protection + spacecraft microbiology + natural interplanetary material transfer + panspermia tests + life-detection chain of custody.

The book is not `panspermia is true`. Its question is harder: if life or convincing biological material is found elsewhere, what evidence can distinguish independent origin, ancient natural transfer, recent anthropogenic transfer and ordinary sample contamination?

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- NASA/JPL planetary protection explicitly addresses forward contamination and backward contamination. https://planetaryprotection.jpl.nasa.gov/mission-implementation
- NASA assigns mission-dependent planetary-protection categories and uses bioburden limits, cleaning, sterilization and trajectory constraints. https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/planetary-protection
- Europa Clipper was estimated at launch to carry fewer than 350,000 bacterial spores despite stringent biological-cleanliness work. https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/press-kits/europa-clipper/mission/biological-cleanliness/
- JPL maintains an archive of organisms/material recovered from spacecraft-associated environments; archived isolates include organisms showing desiccation/UVC/salt tolerance, and some isolates have been studied with Mars-relevant substrates. https://planetaryprotection.jpl.nasa.gov/biological-materials-archive
- Mars-like UV exposure experiments strongly reduce exposed bacterial endospore viability. NASA NTRS: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20040087453
- E-MIST exposed spacecraft-assembly-facility *Bacillus pumilus* SAFR-032 spores in the stratosphere; viability fell by orders of magnitude over hours and <0.001% remained viable at the final reported exposure. NASA NTRS: https://ntrs.nasa.gov/citations/20190025898
- Martian meteorites on Earth establish natural Mars→Earth material transfer. Primary geochemical/provenance papers to be extracted before quantitative manuscript claims.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Individual legs of lithopanspermia can be experimentally or theoretically plausible without demonstrating complete viable transfer and successful colonization.
- Stress resistance in spacecraft-associated isolates may matter to forward contamination, but trait-level survival must not be converted into ecological establishment.
- Molecular similarity between hypothetical extraterrestrial life and Earth life could be evidence of shared ancestry, convergence or contamination; discriminating power depends on the actual observation.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Claims that meteorites already contain confirmed extraterrestrial organisms.
- Claims that high-altitude/stratospheric microorganisms establish continuing extraterrestrial biological infall.
- Claims that any current Mars mission has established viable terrestrial colonization of Mars.
- Claims that panspermia explains the origin of terrestrial life.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis

**Sterilization selection:** contamination-control environments may enrich the recoverable population for stress-tolerant traits relevant to spacecraft survival. Must be tested against actual isolate distributions and multi-stressor fitness, not asserted from anecdotes.

**Provenance collapse:** a genuine biological observation may remain underdetermined among independent genesis, ancient natural transfer, recent human transfer and laboratory contamination.

**Planetary crime scene:** natural material exchange can make pristine provenance difficult even before human exploration; human contamination adds a second, separable provenance layer.

## Publication holds

Do not say `sterile spacecraft` where the source establishes a bounded bioburden.

Do not turn Europa Clipper's estimated spore count into viable organisms at Europa.

Do not turn UV survival into Mars colonization potential.

Do not turn a meteorite's Martian provenance into biological transfer.

Do not use a patent as evidence of efficacy.

Do not describe panspermia as established.

Do not imply that planetary-protection precautions are evidence NASA expects extraterrestrial pathogens.

## Contradictions / incentives

Planetary-protection organizations have an institutional reason to reason conservatively about contamination; their requirements establish operational concern, not probability of successful colonization.

Astrobiology has incentives in both directions: exciting life claims attract attention, while contamination objections can become a default skeptical escape hatch. Event-level chain of custody outranks either cultural incentive.

A null result in exposed-surface survival does not automatically transfer to shielded niches; a positive survival result does not automatically transfer to metabolism or reproduction.

## Strongest countermodel

The entire dramatic framing may collapse into competent laboratory hygiene plus a low-probability natural-transfer literature. Mars' surface is hostile, modern missions control bioburden, genomic forensics may make terrestrial contamination obvious, and natural transfer may be too inefficient for viable organisms to matter. If realistic end-to-end experiments and phylogenetic discrimination make provenance robust, `provenance collapse` should shrink from governing thesis to edge case.

## Defeat conditions

The book's stronger thesis weakens if: (1) realistic mission-to-habitat experiments show effectively zero viable transfer across plausible protected niches; (2) independent-origin biology would almost certainly contain molecular signatures cleanly distinguishable from terrestrial ancestry; (3) natural lithopanspermia probabilities become negligibly small across all credible transfer windows; or (4) contamination archives and sequencing make recent terrestrial contamination reliably identifiable at the relevant detection limits.

# Back Matter

## Evidence note

This manuscript treats Project Sanguine, Seafarer, and Project ELF as documented U.S. Navy communications efforts. Claims about environmental or human health effects are bounded by the available review literature. The 1997 National Research Council evaluation is especially important because it found no obvious adverse ecological effects during the monitoring program while also identifying methodological weaknesses and recommending independent assessment of further reanalysis.

## Source hierarchy

Priority order for expansion: Navy and Department of Defense records; federal court opinions and administrative records; National Academies reviews; contemporaneous IEEE and engineering literature; GAO and congressional material; state and local archives; peer-reviewed bioeffects/ecology work; then serious reporting and oral history.

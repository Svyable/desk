# Chronology

- **1950s–1960s:** Navy research explores extremely low frequency communication to submerged submarines.
- **1968:** Navy announces plans for an ELF test facility near Clam Lake, Wisconsin.
- **1969:** Wisconsin test facility becomes operational for experimentation.
- **Early 1970s:** Project Sanguine concept and environmental research proceed; full-scale development does not.
- **1977:** Project Seafarer proposed as a smaller architecture involving Wisconsin and Michigan.
- **Late 1970s–1980s:** Successive reduced concepts move toward the eventual Project ELF configuration.
- **1984:** Wisconsin/federal litigation produces major opinions over environmental review.
- **1980s:** Construction/testing of the linked Wisconsin and Michigan ELF system proceeds.
- **1989:** Two-site operational configuration enters service.
- **1997:** National Research Council publishes independent evaluation of the ecological monitoring program.
- **2004:** Navy closes the ELF system.

Dates and naming transitions will be tightened against Navy primary records in the next pass.

# Working Notes

## Freshness

Recent Author-agent lanes were Project SHAD/exposure secrecy, Navy asbestos/occupational latency, CORONA/archaeological remote sensing, fast radio bursts, and Project Seal/explosion hydrodynamics. This book deliberately shifts to strategic communications engineering, geology, environmental law, and public contestation.

## Distinct contribution

Most treatments emphasize either the gigantic antenna or alleged health danger. The stronger book is about constraint migration. Seawater creates a communications constraint; lowering frequency moves the constraint into wavelength and antenna efficiency; the antenna moves it into geology and land; land moves it into law and politics; public distrust moves it into ecological monitoring. The technical problem does not disappear. It changes institutions.

## Contradiction ledger

- **Strategic secrecy vs physical visibility:** the mission can be sensitive while the infrastructure is impossible to hide locally.
- **Large input power vs tiny radiated ELF power:** dramatic electrical infrastructure does not imply proportionally dramatic far-field exposure.
- **No obvious ecological harm vs incomplete certainty:** negative monitoring results and methodological limitations can both be true.
- **Maximalist Sanguine vs modest operational ELF:** failure of the original architecture does not imply failure of the underlying requirement.

# Why It Closed

In 2004 the Navy shut Project ELF down. The closure is important because it prevents the book from pretending that extreme infrastructure is destiny.

Communications portfolios change. Improvements in other systems, changed operational concepts, satellites, very-low-frequency capabilities, and the post-Cold War strategic environment altered the value of maintaining the ELF network. A capability once important enough to survive decades of controversy became unnecessary enough to dismantle.

This is the quieter side of military secrecy. Programs do not only hide breakthroughs. They also accumulate obsolescence. A system can be genuinely useful, controversial, expensive, and eventually redundant without any revelation that it was fraudulent all along.

The antennas came down. The physics did not change. The strategic workaround did.

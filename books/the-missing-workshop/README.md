# The Missing Workshop

**Why Ancient Technology Looks Impossible After the Workshop Disappears**

A Desk book by Sven Hardy Benson.

## Thesis

The Antikythera mechanism is not evidence for a lost industrial civilization, but it may be evidence for something historical narratives routinely erase: a small, high-skill technical ecology whose tools, jigs, timber machinery, shop records, tacit knowledge and sibling devices survived much worse than its most durable bronze output.

## Evidence posture

Primary/peer-reviewed-heavy, archaeology-forward, explicitly anti-pseudohistorical without using skepticism to flatten genuine anomalies.

The book separates:

1. documented/direct evidence,
2. credible but incomplete inference,
3. disputed attribution or function,
4. speculative synthesis.

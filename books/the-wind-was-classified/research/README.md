# Research trail

This directory keeps claim boundaries and public-source traces for *The Wind Was Classified*.

The working rule is four-way separation: documented fact; credible but incomplete reporting/inference; disputed allegation; speculative synthesis. Testimony is attributed rather than promoted into proof. Patents, where they appear in future work, are evidence of filing rather than efficacy.

The manuscript uses only lawfully available public material and does not imply access to classified records.

## Research cautions

- The 239-test figure refers to the Army's broad open-air testing inventory and should not be rewritten as 239 releases of pathogenic organisms over civilians.
- Sea-Spray exposure estimates are model/recovery-derived population estimates, not individual dosimetry.
- The Stanford cluster and Nevin death are temporally relevant but not molecularly linked to the released strain by surviving evidence identified so far.
- Project SHAD/112 is related through vulnerability-testing culture and disclosure history but differs materially from public-city simulant releases; do not merge their exposure categories.
- Zinc cadmium sulfide risk claims require dose- and formulation-specific treatment; the 1997 NRC review should anchor the toxicology section.

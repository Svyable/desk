*Deterrence in the Gray Zone*

The working thesis is that the most dangerous Taiwan crisis may begin with actions deliberately designed not to look like the beginning of a war. A customs inspection regime, coast-guard quarantine, exercise that does not end, cable disruption, cyber campaign, financial restriction, or selective maritime interdiction can impose real coercion while making every response look potentially escalatory.

The book therefore asks a different question from “When will China invade Taiwan?” It asks how states deter, identify, and answer coercion when the threshold itself is the battlefield.

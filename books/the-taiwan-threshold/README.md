# The Taiwan Threshold

*Deterrence in the Gray Zone*

The working thesis is that the most dangerous path to a Taiwan conflict is not necessarily a single decision to invade but a sequence of coercive moves below the traditional threshold of war, each individually survivable, that together make the next escalation easier and the off-ramp harder to see.

The book focuses on classification, thresholds, and decision process: quarantine, inspections, exercises, cyber operations, undersea infrastructure, financial pressure, selective seizures, and other acts whose political effect depends partly on what governments decide to call them.

The first field edition publishes the framework and opening case while the complete trade manuscript remains in development on Desk.

# The Taiwan Threshold

*Deterrence in the Gray Zone*

Sven Hardy Benson

This book does not predict an invasion date. It treats prediction itself as one of the traps surrounding Taiwan: a vivid scenario can crowd out the quieter forms of coercion that are easier to begin, harder to classify, and therefore harder to answer collectively.

The subject is the threshold between pressure and war, and the decisions made while no one is certain whether that threshold has already been crossed.

## Thesis

If an alleged recovered craft or high-quality UAP observation ever becomes physically available, the first useful question is not “what technology is this?” but “which conservation laws, fields, stresses, heat flows, clocks, spectra, and reaction products does it force us to measure?” Reverse engineering should begin as hostile metrology. The book treats non-human technology as a disputed hypothesis, not a premise, and builds a physics program capable of discovering an exotic propulsion mechanism while being equally capable of proving that there is none.

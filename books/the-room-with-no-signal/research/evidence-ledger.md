# Evidence ledger — THE ROOM WITH NO SIGNAL

Working rule: **experience is not information; statistical association is not mechanism; government interest is not validation.**

## Class 1 — verified / documented

- CIA publicly acknowledges U.S. government research into remote viewing, its transfer among agencies, the 1995 independent evaluation, subsequent decision not to restore the program, and declassification of records. Source: CIA, “Ask Molly: Did CIA Really Study Psychic Powers?” https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/ask-molly-did-cia-really-study-psychic-powers/
- Milton & Wiseman (1999) meta-analyzed 30 ganzfeld studies from seven independent laboratories and reported no significant aggregate above-chance effect: Stouffer z = 0.70, one-tailed p = .24, with reported mean effect size 0.013. Source: *Psychological Bulletin* 125(4), 387–391. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10414223/ DOI: 10.1037/0033-2909.125.4.387
- Storm & Ertel (2001) disputed the negative interpretation and reported positive results after constructing broader ganzfeld/autoganzfeld databases. Source: *Psychological Bulletin* 127(3), 424–433. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11393304/ DOI: 10.1037/0033-2909.127.3.424
- Hyman (2010) criticized Storm, Tressoldi & Di Risio’s meta-analytic treatment, arguing that exclusions/combinations created misleading consistency and emphasizing independent replication. Source: *Psychological Bulletin* 136(4), 486–490. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20565165/ DOI: 10.1037/a0019676
- Storm, Tressoldi & Di Risio replied that their procedures were standard and continued to interpret the aggregate as evidence of a communications anomaly. Source: *Psychological Bulletin* 136(4), 491–494. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20565166/ DOI: 10.1037/a0019840
- Contemporary perception/consciousness research studies the ganzfeld as a perceptual manipulation independent of paranormal claims. Pistolas & Wagemans (2025), *Neuroscience of Consciousness*: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40832455/ ; Pistolas et al. (2025), *Open Mind*: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41257264/

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Positive aggregate ganzfeld effects reported in some meta-analyses are real published statistical claims, but their evidential interpretation remains dependent on database construction, study quality, independence, participant selection, publication bias and replication assumptions. Do not flatten this into either `ganzfeld replicated` or `ganzfeld debunked`.
- CIA’s retrospective summary says the 1995 review found enough accurate remote-viewing experiences to “defy randomness” while judging the phenomenon too unreliable for intelligence use. This is an agency summary of the evaluation and must be checked against the AIR report itself before using exact wording or quantitative claims in later chapters.
- Reports that selected participants outperform unselected participants are hypothesis-generating until selection criteria, prospective status, effect stability and independent replication are reconstructed from primary studies.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- Telepathy is the mechanism responsible for any ganzfeld aggregate deviation.
- Sensory homogenization opens or amplifies a paranormal information channel.
- Particular people possess a stable, operationally useful psi faculty.
- Remote viewing and ganzfeld effects are manifestations of one mechanism.
- Neurological disruption, rTMS, meditation, altered states or reduced cortical inhibition reliably increase psi. A 2023 *Cortex* paper reports an rTMS-associated result but explicitly notes a post hoc weighting procedure; treat as a controversial experimental claim requiring independent preregistered replication, not a mechanism. PubMed: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38065765/

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

### Experience-information split
A participant’s vivid or anomalous subjective experience and measurable target-specific information are different dependent variables. Future experiments should collect both but never allow one to validate the other.

### Information budget
If an anomalous channel is claimed, ask how much target information can be transmitted under preregistered conditions. Move beyond `hit/miss` toward target-set scaling, predefined features, mutual information, repeatability and sender/no-sender contrasts. This is a proposed framework, not an established result.

### Noise-source inversion
The ganzfeld may be scientifically useful because the condition intended historically to improve a weak external/anomalous signal also increases internally generated perceptual material. That creates a direct experimental collision between anomalous-information and constructive-perception models.

### Operational compression test
A phenomenon that survives only as an aggregate statistical residual but cannot be compressed into timely, repeatable, decision-useful information may remain scientifically interesting while failing as a communication technology. This distinction should structure the government-program chapter.

## Contradictions to preserve

1. Published positive meta-analytic conclusions coexist with a prominent post-Bem/Honorton meta-analysis that failed to reproduce the main effect.
2. The literature can look more consistent or less consistent depending on study inclusion and aggregation decisions. That dependency is itself evidence about the maturity of the phenomenon.
3. Government willingness to fund remote viewing coexists with a final operational judgment of insufficient reliability/usefulness. Funding cannot be cited as proof of efficacy.
4. Ganzfeld stimulation reliably produces unusual perceptual experience as an ordinary topic of perception research. This neither proves nor disproves anomalous information transfer; it raises the burden on target-specific inference.

## Known incentive / distortion channels

- publication and file-drawer effects;
- optional inclusion/exclusion of studies;
- flexible participant-selection criteria;
- target-pool similarity and judging flexibility;
- retrospective matching of free-form reports;
- experimenter effects and expectancy;
- small-study variance;
- advocacy incentives on both pro-psi and skeptical sides;
- Cold War technological-surprise incentives that rationally reward investigation before efficacy is known;
- media incentive to convert `government investigated` into `government proved`.

## Strongest conventional countermodel

There is no anomalous information channel. Ganzfeld participants generate rich endogenous imagery under homogeneous stimulation; target pools and human judging provide opportunities for apparent correspondence; small heterogeneous studies plus selection, analytic flexibility and publication dynamics generate occasional positive aggregates; larger or more independent replications regress toward chance. Government programs persisted because the cost of investigating a potentially adversarial capability was tolerable, not because operational efficacy was established.

The manuscript must let this model win if prospective multi-lab evidence does not beat it.

## Defeat conditions for the book’s stronger anomaly hypothesis

A sufficiently powered, preregistered, multi-laboratory protocol using fixed participant-selection rules, automated target generation, blinded judging, public trial-level data, sender/no-sender arms and prespecified analyses repeatedly yields results centered on chance with confidence intervals excluding the effect sizes required by the positive literature. If that happens, the manuscript should treat the historical residual primarily as a research-ecology problem rather than a surviving communications anomaly.

Conversely, repeated above-chance target information under those conditions would not by itself prove telepathy. It would promote the anomaly from `literature-level residual` to `replicable information effect` and trigger a new causal program: leakage audits, distance/time dependence, sender necessity, target entropy, biological correlates and independent hardware/protocol replication.

## Publication holds

- Do not write `CIA proved psychic spying worked`.
- Do not write `the ganzfeld has been replicated` without specifying database, period, protocol and replication criterion.
- Do not write `the ganzfeld failed replication` as a universal claim based only on Milton & Wiseman 1999.
- Do not infer telepathy from statistical significance.
- Do not infer absence of effect from one underpowered null result.
- Do not treat patents, funding, institutional attention or classification as efficacy evidence.
- Do not merge remote viewing, ganzfeld telepathy, precognition and micro-PK into one psi mechanism.
- Do not use hallucination research to claim that target matches are explained unless the experiment actually tests that causal alternative.

## Chapter progression

The book should move from apparatus to controversy to mechanism rather than repeatedly restating the replication debate. Chapters 2–5 reconstruct the room and the historical methodological bargain; 6–9 force the replication/meta-analysis problem; 10–12 collide the paranormal literature with modern constructive perception and information theory; 13–14 use the government record to distinguish scientific anomaly from operational capability; 15 examines publication ecology; 16 specifies a genuinely adversarial experiment; 17 asks what changes if an information effect survives; 18 returns to the room with a narrower definition of what would count as surprise.

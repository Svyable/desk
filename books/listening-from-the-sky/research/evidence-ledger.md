# LISTENING FROM THE SKY — research ledger

## Governing thesis

Project MOGUL and modern balloon-borne infrasound research collide around a neglected epistemic object: **sensor position can determine which physical world is observable**. The historical program tried to exploit a proposed upper-atmosphere acoustic channel for strategic detection before the environment could be measured cleanly. Modern work has now directly demonstrated elevated acoustic ducting and cases in which balloon receivers detect known sources more clearly than ground stations. The book asks when an abandoned classified idea was wrong, when its hardware merely lost an engineering competition, and when its underlying physical premise had to wait for a later measurement regime.

This is not a Roswell book. Existing Desk work already owns MOGUL/Roswell as an explanation/secrecy problem. Here Roswell is a boundary condition: the manuscript may explain why MOGUL is culturally famous, then return to acoustics.

## Evidence classes

### 1 — Verified / documented

- U.S. Air Force historical reconstruction documents Project MOGUL as a high-priority effort involving expendable microphones for long-range low-frequency detection of explosions/missiles, telemetry, and upper-atmosphere balloon platforms. It identifies Columbia University and NYU contracts and describes later work connected to Sandstone tests.
  - https://www.dafhistory.af.mil/Portals/16/documents/AFD-101201-038.pdf
- Bowman & Lees (2015) flew a three-element acoustic array in the middle stratosphere and reported a soundscape substantially unlike simultaneous ground measurements, with both identifiable and unidentified signals and explicit platform-artifact candidates.
  - https://doi.org/10.1002/2015GL066570
- Bowman et al. (2021) recorded a known 10-ton-TNT-equivalent buried chemical explosion at 56 km horizontal range from 21.8 km altitude; the balloon environment had low background noise and the measured amplitude exceeded modeled expectation.
  - https://doi.org/10.1029/2021GL094861
- Albert et al. (2023) reported direct observation of a ground-truth airborne source propagating in an elevated acoustic duct, termed AtmoSOFAR. Their modeled channel spanned roughly 10–40 km for the event; a balloon-borne sensor recorded the rocket while distant ground stations did not provide comparable detections.
  - https://doi.org/10.1029/2023EA003149
- Modern global infrasound propagation literature establishes strong atmospheric dependence of propagation paths and detection geometry.
  - Drob, Picone & Garcés (2003), https://doi.org/10.1029/2002JD003307

### 2 — Credible but incomplete

- Exact mapping between Maurice Ewing's original atmospheric sound-channel concept, MOGUL flight altitudes, contemporaneous meteorology and the modern AtmoSOFAR duct. Similarity is physically interesting but must not become identity without primary reconstruction.
- MOGUL's operational success rate, event denominator, detection ranges and false-alarm burden. Institutional histories establish purpose and activity more readily than performance.
- Degree to which later U.S. nuclear-detection systems inherited MOGUL data, personnel, propagation models or instrumentation rather than merely replacing the balloon approach.
- Scientific continuity between classified 1940s work and modern balloon infrasound. Citation ancestry is not the same thing as technical inheritance.

### 3 — Disputed / not independently confirmed

- Claims that MOGUL's existence validates non-human explanations of Roswell. It does not.
- Claims that modern AtmoSOFAR work retroactively proves MOGUL was operationally successful. It does not.
- Claims that unidentified balloon-borne acoustic signals are evidence of secret technology, geophysical anomalies, or unknown intelligence absent source-constraining evidence.
- Any assertion that a named anomalous aerospace event should have been detected acoustically without source altitude, Mach regime, waveform, atmospheric state, receiver geometry and instrument response.

### 4 — Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- **Measurement hiatus:** classification plus difficult instrumentation may create a long interval in which a physically real effect is institutionally investigated but scientifically underobserved.
- **Sensor geography:** observability is a property of source × propagation environment × receiver position, not source alone.
- **Negative-space trap:** a sensor null has little force until the propagation path and receiver operating envelope are specified; but unknown geometry cannot be used as an unlimited escape hatch.
- **Rediscovery tax:** when an early classified program dies, later science may have to repay experimental costs already incurred but poorly integrated into public literature.

## Quantitative extraction burden

Before later chapters publish strong comparisons, reconstruct: MOGUL microphone frequency response and sensitivity; flight altitude/time; balloon-train configuration; telemetry bandwidth; source events and yields; modeled acoustic-channel altitude by date; detection/false-alarm denominator; modern sensor self-noise; balloon drift speed; wind-noise reduction; propagation loss; source-receiver geometry; channel vertical extent; ground-station sensitivity; and model residuals. Never compare source yield, received pressure, signal-to-noise ratio and detection range as though they were interchangeable measures.

## Contradictions and incentives

- The Air Force Roswell reports have an institutional incentive to resolve a famous controversy. Use them for MOGUL records they reproduce and source, not as an oracle on every disputed Roswell claim.
- Modern infrasound papers have an incentive to establish novelty. Their own statement that historical in-situ stratospheric work was sparse should be checked against older military, meteorological and geophysical literature.
- MOGUL secrecy can inflate retrospective mystique. Classification proves sensitivity, not efficacy.
- Program cancellation/supersession can make a physical premise look refuted when a platform merely lost on cost, reliability or coverage.
- Conversely, later validation of one propagation mechanism can cause retrospective success inflation: `the duct exists` is not `the old system worked`.

## Strongest countermodel

MOGUL may be an ordinary case of technological selection rather than suppressed knowledge. Balloons were operationally awkward; seismology, radionuclide collection and fixed monitoring networks offered better coverage and reliability. The atmospheric acoustic premise remained scientifically available but niche until lightweight digital sensors and modern modeling made balloon work cheap enough to revisit. A matched comparison against abandoned nonclassified sensor concepts could defeat the proposed classification-specific `measurement hiatus`.

## Defeat conditions

The book's strongest synthesis should be weakened or abandoned if: (1) primary MOGUL records show that the relevant atmospheric-channel premise was experimentally falsified rather than merely operationally superseded; (2) modern AtmoSOFAR geometry is physically non-equivalent to the historical channel in the ways the argument requires; (3) public/nonclassified atmospheric infrasound research proves substantially continuous across the supposed hiatus; or (4) matched nonclassified technologies exhibit the same dormancy/revival pattern, leaving classification with little explanatory power.

## Publication holds

Do not publish a single MOGUL 'range' or success rate until the event denominator and source geometry are recovered. Do not call AtmoSOFAR a seventy-year confirmation without distinguishing the exact old and new channel definitions. Do not use Roswell witness claims as evidence for acoustic performance. Do not infer hidden sources from unidentified modern stratospheric signals. Do not turn a ground-station non-detection into proof of privileged balloon sensing unless station sensitivity, propagation and timing are specified.

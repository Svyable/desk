# Research Notes and Source Log

## Evidence classes

### 1 — Verified / documented

- Bortolotti, N.; Curceanu, C.; Diósi, L.; Manti, S.; Piscicchia, K. “Fundamental limits on clock precision from spacetime uncertainty in quantum collapse models.” *Physical Review Research* 7, 043166 (2025). https://doi.org/10.1103/p6tj-lg8l — Primary paper. Shows, within DP and CSL collapse frameworks, an induced clock-time uncertainty associated with stochastic Newtonian-potential fluctuations; concludes effect is negligible for contemporary clocks.
- arXiv version: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.06109 — provenance and prepublication version.
- ScienceDaily/FQXi release, 11 Sep 2026: https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/09/260910225251.htm — secondary communication source; useful for quotations/provenance, not the technical authority.
- Bassi et al., “Collapse Models: A Theoretical, Experimental and Philosophical Review,” *Entropy* 25, 645 (2023): https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10138035/ — broad review and experimental bounds.
- Carlesso et al., “Present status and future challenges of non-interferometric tests of collapse models,” *Nature Physics* 18 (2022): https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-021-01489-5 — review of cold atoms, optomechanics, X rays, bulk heating and cosmological tests.
- Carlesso et al., “Experimental bounds on collapse models from gravitational wave detectors,” *Physical Review D* 94, 124036 (2016): https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.94.124036 — LIGO/LISA Pathfinder/AURIGA constraints.
- Bilardello et al., “Bounds on collapse models from cold-atom experiments,” *Physica A* 462 (2016): https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physa.2016.06.134 — cold-atom diffusion/heating constraints.

### 2 — Credible but incomplete

- The interpretation that stochastic collapse dynamics corresponds to physical gravitational-potential fluctuations. This is the framework of the primary paper, not an experimentally established property of gravity.
- Clock networks as useful future collapse probes. Plausible from the derived observable, but the paper itself concludes present and foreseeable clocks are far from the predicted level.

### 3 — Disputed / unconfirmed

- Objective collapse occurs in nature.
- Gravity is the physical origin of collapse.
- Any existing clock anomaly is caused by collapse-induced spacetime noise.
- Generic claims that “the Diósi–Penrose model has been ruled out” without specifying implementation and regularization.

### 4 — Speculative synthesis

- Cross-correlated heterogeneous clock networks could discriminate universal stochastic potential noise from clock-specific systematics.
- A joint clock + mechanical-resonator experiment could overconstrain one collapse kernel through two physically different observables.
- Precision metrology may eventually turn the measurement problem into a multi-instrument noise-spectroscopy problem.

## Contradictions and incentives

The popular incentive is to translate a conditional prediction into an ontological discovery: “time itself is uncertain.” The technical paper does not report detection. The collapse-model community has an incentive to emphasize unexplored parameter space and new tests; experimentalists have incentives to emphasize strong exclusions. Neither replaces equation-level scope checking. FQXi’s foundational mission encourages unconventional questions; funding provenance does not validate or invalidate the result.

# Back Matter

## Working chronology

- 1920s — Quantum mechanics develops its standard formalism and measurement problem.
- 1980s — Ghirardi, Rimini, and Weber formulate spontaneous localization as modified dynamics.
- 1980s–1990s — Diósi and Penrose independently develop gravity-related collapse ideas.
- 1990s onward — Continuous Spontaneous Localization becomes a major phenomenological collapse framework.
- 2010s–2020s — Matter-wave, cold-atom, mechanical, radiation, gravitational-wave, and astrophysical observations increasingly constrain collapse parameter spaces.
- 2025-04-08 — Bortolotti et al. post preprint arXiv:2504.06109.
- 2025-11-13 — Physical Review Research publishes the peer-reviewed paper.
- 2026-09-11 — ScienceDaily/FQXi coverage foregrounds the implication for fundamental clock precision.

## Defeat conditions for the book’s synthesis

The book’s strongest synthesis would be weakened if: (a) the CSL-to-gravity mapping proves physically unmotivated or inconsistent in relativistic completion; (b) viable collapse parameter space is eliminated by other experiments before clocks approach useful sensitivity; (c) clock-time noise is shown to be exactly degenerate with conventional noise under every realizable comparison; or (d) a relativistic theory demonstrates that the Newtonian-potential argument cannot be promoted to a consistent observable.

## Decisive-experiment sketch

Construct a heterogeneous optical-clock network with independent stabilized transfer, co-located and separated pairs, environmental gravimetry, and at least one mechanically sensitive collapse channel. Preregister the spatial and temporal cross-spectrum predicted by one explicit collapse kernel. Blind the signal region. Require consistency of inferred collapse parameters across clock species, baselines, and the mechanical channel. A detection must reproduce after hardware relocation. A null should be translated into parameter-space exclusion, not prose about “perfect time.”

## Note on claims

This manuscript does not claim that time fluctuations have been observed. It does not claim objective collapse is established, gravity causes collapse, consciousness causes collapse, or the 2025 calculation constitutes a complete quantum-gravity theory. Those are deliberately excluded readings.

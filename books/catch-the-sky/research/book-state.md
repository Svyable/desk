# CATCH THE SKY — working book state

## Reader promise

Follow one apparently absurd requirement—getting exposed spy-satellite film physically back from orbit—deeply enough to see how real secret systems produce evidence. The book is about logistics as epistemology: a hidden capability still has joints.

## Distinct contribution

Recent Author-agent books have used foreign-materiel exploitation, strategic sensor networks, cover ecology, balloons, environmental engineering and recovered sites as control cases. CATCH THE SKY moves to the opposite direction of travel: not recovering an enemy object or detecting an external event, but deliberately returning one’s own secret information-bearing object from space. The central object is not the satellite. It is the path by which inaccessible observation becomes usable knowledge.

## Strongest competing explanation

CORONA’s enormous physical footprint may be a period artifact. Modern digital collection can downlink information and remove the spectacular recovery chain. The book has to earn any generalization by following what replaces the bucket: antennas, spectrum, ground sites, storage, compute, tasking, access control and analytic dissemination. If the interface burden simply vanishes rather than migrates, the thesis narrows.

## Narrative movement

Part I makes the reader care about the bucket: success requires return. Part II follows failure and cover until the hidden system becomes mechanically legible. Part III follows the recovered image into strategic estimation, where `data` becomes an institutional claim. Part IV moves forward into digital reconnaissance and tests which epistemic lessons survive the disappearance of physical film. The final chapters apply the method to extraordinary classified claims without using CORONA as borrowed evidence for them.

## Voice calibration

Stay close to objects and mechanisms. Let the oddness of the history do the work. Avoid `Cold War ingenuity` uplift and retrospective triumphalism. The C-119 hook is more interesting than a paragraph announcing that the program was innovative. Preserve uncertainty around institutional causal claims. Dryness is useful when the engineering becomes ridiculous in a precise way.

## Recurring objects

- the film bucket;
- parachute and hook;
- failed recovery fragment;
- exposed film as inaccessible knowledge;
- the conversion chain from photons to presidential estimate.

Use recurrence only when the object’s meaning has changed.

## Epistemic rule

For controversial transfers, preserve four classes in notes and prose: documented fact; credible incomplete testimony/reporting; disputed allegation; speculative synthesis. Do not let classification, clearance, institutional prestige or historical precedent substitute for event-specific evidence.

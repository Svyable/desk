# Research notes — THE LOUDEST THING

## Working thesis

Use Earth as the only verified technological planet and therefore the control experiment for technosignature detectability. The surprising result is not merely that humanity is visible; different human signatures differ by enormous amounts in detection distance, duty cycle and geometry. SETI nondetections therefore constrain particular technological behaviors rather than `civilization` in the abstract.

## Evidence class 1 — verified / documented

- Sheikh et al., **Earth Detecting Earth: At what distance could Earth's constellation of technosignatures be detected with present-day technology?** Cross-wavelength comparison reports terrestrial technosignatures spanning 13 orders of magnitude in detectability; intermittent targeted planetary radar dominates detection distance. https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.02614
- Sheikh et al. 2026, *Astronomical Journal*, **A Search for Radio Technosignatures from Interstellar Object 3I/ATLAS with the Allen Telescope Array**. 1–9 GHz, >7 hr; no extraterrestrial technosignature; limits roughly 10–110 W EIRP for relevant searched signals. DOI: https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-3881/ae6651
- SETI Institute report of the same campaign records ~74 million initial detections narrowed to ~200 candidates, all traced to human interference. https://www.seti.org/news/seti-institute-looks-for-signs-of-technology-in-interstellar-visitor-3iatlas/
- NASA Exoplanet Exploration Program hosted IAUS 404, **Advancing the Search for Technosignatures**, 2–6 March 2026. This establishes technosignatures as an active astronomical research program, not validation of any candidate. https://science.nasa.gov/astrophysics/programs/exep/resources/exoplanet-meetings/iaus-404-advancing-the-search-for-technosignatures/

## Evidence class 2 — credible but incomplete / modeled

- Haqq-Misra et al. 2022, *Planetary Science Journal*, models detectability of CFC-11/CFC-12 in habitable M-dwarf atmospheres. Modeling establishes observational plausibility under assumptions, not extraterrestrial industry. https://doi.org/10.3847/PSJ/ac5404
- Kopparapu et al. 2021 models NO2 pollution as an atmospheric technosignature while explicitly facing biogenic/anthropogenic source ambiguity. https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/abd7f7
- Schwieterman et al. 2024 models artificial greenhouse gases including CF4, C2F6, C3F8, SF6 and NF3 as low-false-positive technosignatures under specified atmospheric scenarios. Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11149
- Sheikh et al. Earth-detecting-Earth framework remains partly model-based even though the technologies and receiving instruments are real. Treat its maximum distances as scenario-dependent, not universal ranges.

## Evidence class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- No 3I/ATLAS observation cited here supports artificial origin. Current radio searches are negative and ordinary astronomical observations support a natural comet-like object.
- No atmospheric technosignature has been established on an exoplanet by the sources above. Detectability papers are proposals/models.
- Do not inherit `alien megastructure`, `probe`, or `industrial civilization` from anomalous light curves, trajectories, spectra or infrared excess without case-specific reconstruction and independent exclusion of natural models.

## Evidence class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- **Visibility bias:** searches select for technological behaviors that couple efficiently to astronomical instruments, not necessarily for intelligence, complexity, longevity or social importance.
- **Geometry tax:** every technosignature occupies a constrained geometry/time/frequency/contrast window. Nondetection strength must be conditioned on that window.
- **Civilization silhouette:** the set of signatures visible at a given distance can badly misrepresent the underlying civilization because detectability is dominated by a few extreme technologies.
- **Technological self-erasure:** development can reduce some technosignatures (pollution controls, wired communications, efficiency) without reducing technological capability.
- **Nondetection is a shape:** aggregate null results become informative only after each is represented as a bounded exclusion in parameter space.

## Contradictions / pressure points

1. Planetary radar is highly detectable but intermittent and directional. A large maximum distance is not a large probability of interception.
2. Persistent atmospheric signatures may be weaker than directed radio but integrate over long periods. Compare range and duty cycle separately.
3. `Artificial greenhouse gas` can mean low known abiotic false-positive potential, not logical impossibility of unknown chemistry.
4. Technological evolution can make a civilization quieter in one channel while louder in another. Avoid one-dimensional Kardashev-style inference from any single signature.
5. Human radio-frequency interference demonstrates the confounder problem locally: the easiest technology to detect in a SETI observation may be the observer's own.

## Publication holds

- Hold any numerical `probability of detection` until beam geometry/duty cycle is reconstructed from the underlying Earth-detecting-Earth methods.
- Hold claims about 3I/ATLAS beyond the published radio constraints and natural comet-like evidence.
- Hold named historical `alien megastructure` candidates until primary papers and follow-up observations are reconstructed.
- Patents, proposals and mission concepts count as evidence of proposal/design, never efficacy or extraterrestrial presence.

## Strongest countermodel

The book could overstate Earth as a control. One technological civilization is a sample of one shaped by a specific star, biology, industrial history and communication architecture. Earth calibrates instruments and inference errors far better than it calibrates the full distribution of possible civilizations. If the manuscript begins converting `what Earth looks like` into `what technology must look like`, cut it back.

## Defeat condition

The central argument weakens if mature technosignature surveys already express their null results consistently as multidimensional exclusion regions with explicit duty-cycle, geometry, persistence and civilization-model dependence, and if the Earth control adds no consequential correction to interpretation. In that case the book should narrow from a methodological intervention to a history/science narrative about Earth's changing astronomical visibility.

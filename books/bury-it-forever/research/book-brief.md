# BURY IT FOREVER — working brief

## Reader promise

Follow one Cold War installation through three incompatible timescales: the Army's strategic planning horizon, the ice sheet's mechanical/climatic horizon, and the geological archive recovered by a drill built for the first problem. The book should leave the reader with a more physical model of secrecy: institutions can classify intentions and records; they cannot classify ice flow, waste chemistry, radionuclide decay, sediment provenance, or future melt.

## Distinct contribution

Recent Desk work includes West Ford/ARGUS environmental traces, Plowshare's commercial interface, foreign-materiel exploitation, MOGUL, Vela, remote viewing, neural decoding and biological sensing. Repository search found no Camp Century/Iceworm manuscript. The nearest conceptual neighbor is *Needle Sky*, but the governing object differs. West Ford asks what an intervention into a shared environment leaves externally observable. This book follows a **secret system that becomes an unintended archive across time**: the military installation fails as infrastructure, survives as waste, and its scientific drilling outlives the strategy by becoming evidence about Greenland itself.

## Central thesis

Camp Century exposes a failure mode in secret engineering that is easy to miss while the program is alive: the environment is not scenery. It is a participant with its own timescale. Project Iceworm treated ice as a medium that might hide and move a missile system. The same ice deformed the tunnels, helped kill the concept, preserved an extraordinary basal core, buried abandoned infrastructure, and—under a changing climate—may eventually alter the waste problem again. The strongest version of the thesis is not that nature inevitably reveals every secret. It is that any hidden physical program makes a wager about how long its environment will continue behaving as assumed.

## Strongest competing explanation

Camp Century may be too exceptional to support a general secrecy thesis. Ice is unusually mobile, climate-sensitive and scientifically legible. Many classified facilities in competent geology leave little publicly interpretable trace, and a small compartmented program can terminate cleanly without becoming an environmental archive. The book must normalize claims by scale, medium, waste stream, duration and interface. It must not turn one spectacular Arctic case into a universal law that all secrets surface.

## Evidence classes

### 1. Verified / declassified / documented

- Camp Century was built by the U.S. Army Corps of Engineers in 1959 beneath the northwestern Greenland Ice Sheet and supported year-round personnel.
- The camp used the portable PM-2A nuclear power plant; the reactor pressure vessel/core-related hardware was later removed, while substantial non-reactor infrastructure and wastes remained.
- Project Iceworm was an Army concept for a large subsurface mobile ballistic-missile network in Greenland. The concept was never realized.
- Historical scholarship based on declassified U.S. records finds that the operational Iceworm proposal was not presented to the Danish government.
- Camp Century engineering demonstrated serious deformation/maintenance problems in firn and ice.
- The 1966 Camp Century core penetrated the ice sheet and recovered basal material.
- Modern analysis of Camp Century basal sediment has found preserved plant/insect material and evidence that northwestern Greenland was ice-free during parts of the Quaternary.
- Colgan et al. (2016) modeled future surface-mass-balance change at the abandoned site and identified possible eventual remobilization of physical, chemical, biological and radiological wastes under sustained warming. This is a modeled future pathway, not a statement that the waste is presently emerging at the surface.

### 2. Credible but incomplete

- Exact boundaries between Camp Century's public scientific/engineering mission and Iceworm feasibility work require document-by-document reconstruction.
- Waste inventories and present disposition require current Danish/Greenlandic/U.S. monitoring records rather than repetition of 2016 estimates.
- The causal weight of ice mechanics versus interservice competition, Polaris/Minuteman, diplomacy and strategic doctrine in Iceworm's rejection requires primary decision records.
- Current timing of any future waste remobilization is model- and emissions-pathway-dependent and should be updated from post-2016 glaciology before publication.

### 3. Disputed / unconfirmed

- Claims that Camp Century itself operationally housed nuclear-armed missiles.
- Claims that Denmark knowingly approved Iceworm as an operational missile deployment.
- Claims that the camp's scientific work was merely a fake cover with no independent scientific value.
- Claims that climate change has already exposed the buried waste at the surface.
- Any broader extrapolation from Iceworm to unspecified contemporary underground nuclear/UAP facilities without independent evidence.

### 4. Speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- **Environmental counterparty:** a physical environment should be modeled as a counterparty to secrecy because its behavior can change independently of the institution's plans.
- **Temporal declassification:** some physical facts become easier to infer not because a document is released but because later instruments, models or environmental changes make old traces legible.
- **Archive inversion:** a facility built to exploit an environment can accidentally preserve an archive that later scientists use to understand that environment.
- **Permanence debt:** every claim that waste, infrastructure or evidence can be left safely in place embeds a forecast about future physical conditions. The longer the horizon, the larger the unpriced forecast burden.

## Quantitative extraction burden

For Camp Century: coordinates; surface elevation; distance from Thule/Pituffik; trench depth and length; personnel; annual logistics; power; PM-2A thermal/electrical output; fuel movements; waste volumes; reactor/radiological inventory; tunnel closure/deformation rates; temperature and accumulation regime; core depth; basal sediment thickness; dating constraints.

For Iceworm: proposed area; missile count; launcher/rail concept; tunnel mileage; construction rate; resupply; heat load; maintenance assumptions; expected ice deformation; launch/command architecture; strategic rationale; alternatives; rejection chronology.

For the climate/waste problem: present burial depth; horizontal ice displacement; surface mass balance; modeled transition dates under multiple emissions scenarios; contaminant inventories and decay; plausible meltwater transport; uncertainty intervals; monitoring observations after 2016.

## Contradictions to preserve

- Public camp / secret strategic plan.
- Genuine science / military feasibility.
- Ice as concealment / ice as moving material that defeats infrastructure.
- Abandonment premised on continued burial / later climate model that makes indefinite sequestration conditional.
- A military core / a climate archive.
- Declassification by paperwork / legibility created by later science.

## Publication holds

Do not say the U.S. secretly based 600 nuclear missiles under Greenland; Iceworm proposed up to roughly 600 missiles and was never realized.

Do not say Camp Century was wholly secret. Separate the publicized camp from the classified strategic planning around Iceworm.

Do not say Denmark approved Iceworm. Recover the diplomatic record and distinguish the 1951 defense framework from knowledge/approval of the missile concept.

Do not say the reactor was simply abandoned intact. The reactor plant was removed; characterize remaining radiological waste from source-specific inventories.

Do not say the waste is now melting out. Use current monitoring and updated models.

Do not use patents, later conspiracy literature, or generalized underground-base claims as evidence that Iceworm advanced beyond the documented planning/feasibility stage.

## Defeat condition

The book's broad synthesis should be narrowed if comparative cases show that environmental evolution rarely changes the detectability, safety, cost or political afterlife of secret physical systems once program scale and medium are controlled. The Camp Century case can remain a history even if the general theory fails.

## What Happened and What We Think Happened

The central events, observations and experiments in this book are documented. That does not mean every mechanism is settled or every dramatic retelling is accurate. The rocks at Racetrack Playa were directly observed moving under particular ice-and-wind conditions; the experiment does not prove every historical trail formed identically. Glass frogs can sequester much of their red blood cell mass in the liver during rest; this is not a human medical treatment. Sea slugs regenerated bodies in a reported study; the old body did not regenerate a head. A lineage of microbes lived deep in fractured rock; that does not mean Earth's deep interior contains a single simple ecosystem everywhere.

Word choices such as observed, measured, inferred, proposed, estimated and disputed are not interchangeable. The research ledger identifies each story's strongest source and the point at which the evidence becomes less certain. A future edition should correct these limits as additional studies appear.

## Sources, Rights and Scope

Individual chapters contain real links to primary papers and institutional records. A chapter-by-chapter evidence and falsification ledger is in the book's `research/` directory. Quoted language and illustrations from source papers are not automatically available for reuse; permissions and licensing must be evaluated separately for publication.

No account here represents a firsthand interview or field observation by the author. The views offered about the importance, beauty or strangeness of a result are interpretations. They are not assertions that the planet has intentions.

# Evidence ledger — EXPLAIN YOURSELF, EARTH

**Research record:** 16 November 2026 (America/Chicago). **Byline:** Sven Hardy Benson. **Form:** source-led literary nonfiction, twenty independently grounded natural phenomena. The ledger is a guide to verification, not a declaration that every sentence, link or interpretation has passed final editorial fact-check. This book is *Drafting*, no Shelf publication approved.

## Promise

Every central event, phenomenon, experiment or scientific observation **really exists**, and every chapter contains original research or an authoritative field record. That is different from saying every *explanation* is settled. Document the observed effect, the experimental or instrumental conditions, plausible mechanism, areas of disagreement and the larger ecological consequence, without assigning agency or consciousness to matter, plants or animals. No invented interviews, expedition witnesses, dialogue or quotations.

## Source and prose audit still required

1. **Original-paper pass.** Re-open every linked original paper and confirm factual numerical claims, sampling methods, experiment temperature and instrument values. Inline links are not a substitute for line-by-line fact verification. At least two narrative links in each chapter are currently present; confirm no obsolete URLs, misattributed titles or more recent corrections.
2. **Contradictions and secondary interpretation.** Dune generation/waveguide debate; exact Blood Falls discharge triggers; age of Pando; evolutionary function of slug autotomy; function and control of glass-frog red-cell storage; influence of electric force vs wind for naturally ballooning spiders; whether low-diversity Mponeng result generalizes to other fractures. Preserve source levels.
3. **Sensitive claims.** Do not turn observed animal capacities into human medical promises; do not turn biological signaling into consciousness claims; do not exaggerate natural hazards or urge readers to handle rare organisms. Rights and copyright restrictions apply to photos, figures, original wording and maps.
4. **Science ages.** Dates and census figures near publication must be checked freshly. NPS 137 is an October 2026 count reported November 3, 2026; future editions should not label it a current population without an updated search.
5. **Literary editing.** The first draft repeats explanatory sentences about the difference between an observation and a conclusion. Cut formulaic turns, improve chapter-to-chapter pacing, add grounded individuals and consequences where the documents allow, and avoid ending every chapter with an identical “nature doesn't intend it” epigram.
6. **Quantitative check.** A GitHub branch-content audit using a regex compatible with `scripts/check-book-length.py` produced **67,070 words across 20 chapters**; every chapter >=3,000. Only three chapters currently lie in the preferred 3,500–4,500 range. The full local repository validation scripts were not run in a complete checkout; do not imply they passed.
7. **Editorial status.** Source-led full-length draft, not publication-ready. Final fact verification, permissions and literary edit remain. No Shelf release, ISBN, cover, sales description or external outreach authorized.

## Intended editorial choice

The last three chapters should not be allowed to become a parade of size records. Pando is a problem of individuality and conservation, Devils Hole of geological/legal connection, *Osedax* of ecological succession and symbiosis, icefish of evolutionary loss, viruses of classification and replication, and deep microbes of the nature of habitable environments. That structural distinction is the book's conceptual strength.

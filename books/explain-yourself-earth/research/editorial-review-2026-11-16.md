## Findings and vulnerabilities

1. **Cold is not the same as frozen.** The 2025 ice worm research shows brief heat tolerance while internal freezing remains lethal. The manuscript now separates this clearly from wood frog extracellular freeze tolerance, rather than repeating the familiar myth that glacier worms instantly die in warmth.
2. **Natural phenomena have more than one evidentiary status.** Blood Falls' iron chemistry and the brine system are well grounded; the timing of all releases is not completely settled. Moving stones were directly observed in 2013–14 under thin ice; that is not a universal account of every ancient playa trail. Dune-waveguide and grain synchronization research do not yet reduce every booming dune to one universal causal formula.
3. **The biology is specific.** Glass-frog red-cell sequestration is state-dependent and does not establish a medical clot-prevention therapy; slug body regeneration is documented in certain *Elysia* species and age classes; flytrap electrical memory is a physiological response, not proof of plant consciousness; elevated external electric fields contribute to the spider experiment but do not replace wind in all natural flights.
4. **Deep time and size claims are vulnerable to inflation.** Pando's clonal identity is supported by genetic surveys while exact age/mass depend on inference; the fish's 2026 count is explicitly dated; giant viruses have huge genomes but do not autonomously grow as bacteria do; the Mponeng sample has a nearly single-species community under the original methods, not every rock ecosystem on Earth.
5. **Source review is not complete.** Reader-facing source links are present, and key recent studies and pivotal original papers were consulted, but a final publication pass must check every numerical claim, article title/DOI, source interpretation and later correction. Do not equate links with full independent fact verification.
6. **Narrative read required.** Chapters have substantive, source-specific field and experimental detail, but some repeated general statements about “the mechanism matters” and “nature did not intend the event” remain. A continuous full-book edit should cut that rhetorical repetition, vary transitions and restore pace. Four or more chapters warrant deeper primary reporting or scientific interviews only if permission and provenance permit. No invented scenes.

## Publication blockers

- Source-by-source original-study verification and corrections.
- Whole-book voice and pacing edit; independent fact and human-impact review.
- Copyright/figure permissions, rights metadata and any visual asset review.
- Full local repository validation and intentional release authorization.

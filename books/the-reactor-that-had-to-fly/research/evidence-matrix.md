## Defeat condition for the book’s central synthesis

The central thesis should be narrowed substantially if primary decision records show that Pluto’s cancellation was caused by a single exogenous administrative event unrelated to weapon-system viability or comparative strategic value, or if detailed test records overturn the premise that the Tory reactor program had genuinely cleared its central technical feasibility milestone. Neither result would erase the engineering history, but either would break the manuscript’s present causal architecture.

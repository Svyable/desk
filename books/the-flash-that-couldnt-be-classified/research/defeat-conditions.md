# Defeat conditions and next decisive work

## Nuclear-origin hypothesis

Materially strengthen with: authenticated perpetrator operational records; independent raw hydroacoustic localization; radionuclide provenance plus atmospheric transport that strongly excludes civilian/background sources; newly released contemporaneous multisensor data; or technical reconstruction showing Event 747 falls inside the known nuclear-signature distribution and outside measured non-nuclear background at a compelling likelihood ratio.

Materially weaken with: a validated spacecraft-local mechanism reproducing both bhangmeter traces at realistic event rates; sensor-health/calibration records demonstrating a matching failure mode; robust multisensor nulls showing that every plausible low-yield nuclear scenario should have been detected elsewhere; or archival evidence eliminating plausible test logistics.

## Israel/South Africa attribution

Require actor-specific operational linkage: orders, platform logs, diagnostic plans, personnel/communications records, procurement, contemporaneous intelligence from independent sources, or multiple checkable firsthand accounts. Capability, secrecy, bilateral defense ties, and geographic access are priors, not the bridge.

## Carter-cover-up hypothesis

Require evidence of intentional scientific/policy falsification: withheld decisive data with demonstrated purpose to change the panel result; instructions to force a conclusion; contemporaneous admissions that officials knowingly presented a false technical judgment; or equivalent documentary evidence. A politically convenient outcome and a high action threshold are not enough.

## Gamma-ray-burst control thesis

The analogy should be abandoned if it stops explaining a real methodological contrast. Its valid content is narrow: repeated events + independent platforms + triangulation + population evidence + follow-up produced classification closure for GRBs, while the singular Vela flash lacked comparable public closure. It is not evidence that unexplained military signals usually represent new phenomena.

## Prospective research program

1. Reconstruct Event 747’s complete sensor geometry, health, calibration, raw/processed waveform lineage, and comparator nuclear/zoo-event corpus.
2. Re-run the optical classification with modern, preregistered signal models while preserving the historical detector response.
3. Recover original hydroacoustic records and perform blind source-location inference before revealing the optical error region to the analysts.
4. Reconstruct Australian iodine-131 sampling at specimen level: location, collection date, assay method, detection limits, contemporaneous civilian sources, and atmospheric ensembles.
5. Audit the Arecibo ionospheric observation against matched solar/geomagnetic control days and historical nuclear-test perturbations.
6. Build a vessel/aircraft/island logistics chronology from South African, Israeli, U.S., British, French, Soviet/Russian, and commercial records without assuming a perpetrator.
7. Compare internal intelligence probability language with action thresholds and public statements to separate analytic disagreement from policy choice.
8. Maintain a “claim genealogy” database so repeated secondary claims do not masquerade as independent corroboration.
9. For modern anomaly systems, build synchronized independent multimodal networks and retain resolved negatives as the denominator.
10. Pre-register which observations would move each hypothesis before the next archival release is inspected.

The manuscript should be revised toward whichever hypothesis survives these tests. The unresolved status is not a protected conclusion.

# Contradictions, incentives, and disinformation audit

## Technical contradictions to preserve

- Two Vela bhangmeters responded, but their second-pulse relationship was not as internally tidy as confirmed nuclear events. Do not erase this because later nuclear advocates argue background modulation can explain it.
- The optical morphology was sufficiently nuclear-like that experienced U.S. weapons/intelligence specialists continued to assign high probability to a test. Do not erase this because the Ruina panel preferred a non-nuclear explanation.
- Contemporary corroboration was incomplete and did not produce a universally accepted second phenomenology. Do not inflate later hydroacoustic/radionuclide reconstruction into an acknowledged 1979 confirmation.
- The Ruina panel’s meteoroid/debris mechanism was a proposed explanation, not a directly observed object or experimentally reproduced reconstruction.
- South Africa’s later verified dismantlement record is meaningful counterevidence to simple national-test narratives. It does not logically exclude every hypothetical compartmented or foreign-led scenario; that residual possibility must not be treated as positive evidence.

## Institutional incentives

**Weapons laboratories / nuclear intelligence:** strong mission incentive to avoid missing clandestine tests; deep expertise in known nuclear signatures; possible tendency to overweight target resemblance and adversary capability.

**White House / State Department:** strong incentive to avoid false accusation and an Israeli/South African nonproliferation crisis; also a genuine national-security incentive not to let treaty evasion pass. Policy preference does not establish data manipulation.

**Ruina panel:** incentive and charter oriented toward independent false-positive review; freedom from some mission bias, but potentially less tacit knowledge of classified test phenomenology.

**South African government:** documented incentive to conceal a weapons program before later dismantlement/disclosure. Later denial of a 1979 test cannot be treated as independent proof; neither can concealment history be used to presume guilt.

**Israeli government:** documented policy of nuclear opacity makes acknowledgment unlikely. Opacity is context, not evidence of Event 747 participation.

**Later researchers:** incentives include solving a famous case, access to newly declassified records, reputational investment in either nuclear or non-nuclear interpretation. Peer review increases seriousness but does not remove model dependence.

**Journalists / publishers:** incentive toward perpetrator-centered certainty and “cover-up” framing. Trace claims back to documents rather than counting repetitions.

## Disinformation considerations

No specific disinformation operation is established in the public Event 747 record used by this manuscript. Nevertheless, nuclear programs, intelligence services, and proliferating states have obvious motives to conceal capabilities and mislead adversaries. Treat deliberate deception as a live analytic possibility only where concrete evidence supports it.

Do not use generic Cold War deception as a license to reverse the sign of evidence: an official denial is not automatically confirmation; a skeptical panel is not automatically a cover operation; a redaction is not evidence that the hidden text favors nuclear attribution.

## Claim genealogy hazards

- Carter diary entry → proves Carter recorded a growing belief; it does not itself prove Israeli participation.
- CIA high-probability judgment → proves a serious intelligence assessment; it does not reveal every underlying datum or establish public reproducibility.
- Conditional December 1979 attribution paper → analyzes perpetrators *assuming* nuclear origin; it is not independent proof of nuclear origin.
- Wright/De Geer 2017–2018 → later peer-reviewed advocacy based on technical/archival reconstruction; preserve as serious evidence without relabeling as official confirmation.
- National Security Archive summaries → excellent documentary navigation; cite underlying documents for exact claims when possible.

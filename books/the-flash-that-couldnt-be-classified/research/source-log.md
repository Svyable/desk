# Source log

Sources are weighted by proximity to the measurement or decision. Patents, memoirs, interviews, and later journalism are not treated as proof of technical efficacy or event attribution merely because they are vivid.

## Research cautions

The Wright/De Geer papers are later peer-reviewed reconstructions advocating the nuclear interpretation; they are not contemporaneous official confirmation. National Security Archive editorial summaries are useful navigation but the manuscript should cite the underlying declassified record for specific institutional claims. Carter diary references should be treated as evidence of Carter’s recorded belief, not an independent physical measurement. South African/Israeli cooperation, nuclear capability, and secrecy are contextual priors; none independently attributes Event 747.

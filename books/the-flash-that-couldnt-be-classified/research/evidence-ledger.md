# Evidence ledger

| Claim | Class | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Vela was created to monitor nuclear explosions associated with test-ban verification. | 1 — documented | NASA, DARPA, NNSA histories. |
| Vela detected transient gamma-ray events beginning in 1967; 1973 paper reported sixteen events of cosmic origin. | 1 — measured/published | Klebesadel, Strong & Olson; NASA histories. |
| GRBs became independently established through other spacecraft, triangulation, BATSE population statistics, afterglows and redshifts. | 1 — established science | Multiple independent missions/publications. |
| Vela 6911 recorded a double-flash optical signal on 22 Sep 1979. | 1 — documented | Declassified U.S. records and later technical literature. |
| The waveform was consistent in important respects with an atmospheric nuclear explosion. | 1/2 | Documented expert judgment and technical comparison; not equivalent to proof of nuclear origin. |
| The Ruina panel judged a non-nuclear explanation more probable. | 1 — documented institutional conclusion | Conclusion proves panel judgment, not event mechanism. |
| CIA/DIA/DOE specialists continued to favor nuclear origin, with CIA language reported at “90% plus.” | 1 — documented institutional judgment | Probability method/public basis incomplete. |
| A meteoroid/debris event near the satellite produced the flash. | 3 — unconfirmed hypothesis | Proposed mechanism; no recovered object or direct reconstruction. |
| Hydroacoustic and iodine-131 evidence supports a nuclear test. | 2 — credible later inference | Wright/De Geer peer-reviewed reconstruction; depends on historical data, propagation and provenance. |
| South Africa was preparing a Kalahari nuclear test site in 1977. | 1 — documented | Declassified U.S. records; later South African nuclear history. |
| South Africa built and later dismantled a small arsenal of gun-assembled HEU devices. | 1 — documented/verified | South African declaration and IAEA verification. |
| Israel possessed nuclear weapons capability by the relevant era and maintained nuclear opacity. | 1/2 | Strong historical/declassified convergence; Israel has not officially acknowledged arsenal. |
| Israel conducted the 1979 event. | 3 — disputed allegation | Serious historical hypothesis; no public independently dispositive proof. |
| South Africa conducted the 1979 event. | 3 — disputed allegation | Capability/context are real; event bridge incomplete. |
| Israel and South Africa jointly conducted the event. | 3 — disputed allegation | Bilateral defense ties do not independently prove joint test. |
| Carter administration knowingly suppressed a confirmed test. | 3 — disputed allegation | Policy incentives documented; deliberate falsification not established. |
| Policy consequences can alter action thresholds even when technical beliefs remain unchanged. | 4 — synthesis grounded in decision theory | General institutional inference; apply cautiously to specific actors. |
| Secrecy changes the public evidentiary ecology by delaying independent scrutiny. | 4 — synthesis | Does not imply hidden evidence supports any preferred claim. |

## Non-negotiable distinctions

- Event detection ≠ event classification.
- Event classification ≠ actor attribution.
- Actor capability ≠ participation.
- Testimony ≠ proof of the testified fact.
- Declassification ≠ verification.
- Absence of public evidence ≠ evidence of classified confirmation.
- A patent or program proposal would establish filing/planning only, not efficacy.

# MAKE A BELT — research ledger

## Selected thesis

**Shared-medium leakage:** secrecy can hide an intervention's purpose and organization more effectively than it can hide sufficiently large changes to a physical environment observed by outsiders. Operation ARGUS is the positive control because the intervention was genuinely secret, genuinely extraordinary, and physically measured.

## Evidence classes

### 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, **Operation Argus**: three high-altitude nuclear tests in the South Atlantic; Christofilos artificial-radiation-belt hypothesis; ARPA/Livermore institutional history; Task Force 88; Explorer IV; >40 worldwide ground stations; measured trapped-electron shell and radar effects. https://st.llnl.gov/news/look-back/operation-argus
- Defense Nuclear Agency, **Operation ARGUS, 1958**, DNA 6039F. Describes military interest in artificial radiation belts; unusually informal planning; dispersed/lost/destroyed records; missing film-badge records. https://www.dtra.mil/Portals/61/Documents/NTPR/2-Hist_Rpt_Atm/1958_DNA_6039F.pdf
- J. A. Van Allen, C. E. McIlwain, G. H. Ludwig, **Satellite Observations of Electrons Artificially Injected into the Geomagnetic Field**, PNAS 1959. Primary scientific measurement target for Chapter 5.
- Nicholas C. Christofilos, **The Argus Experiment**, PNAS 1959. Primary theory/experiment target for Chapters 2 and 6.
- STARFISH PRIME literature: use DTRA/DOE/NASA and contemporaneous satellite papers to reconstruct artificial belt and satellite damage; do not rely on retrospective satellite-count folklore without mission-by-mission attribution.

### 2 — credible but incomplete

- Exact chain by which test-moratorium timing accelerated ARGUS; reconstruct decision documents and dates rather than narrating motive from later summaries.
- Exact military expectation for missile/fuze effects versus communications degradation versus scientific measurement.
- Degree to which civilian/foreign observers could infer the artificial belt before public disclosure. Need dated observations and publication chronology.

### 3 — disputed / unsupported until independently established

- Claims that ARGUS or STARFISH was an operational anti-satellite weapon rather than an effects experiment.
- Claims connecting ARGUS directly to UFO events without event-specific geometry/timing evidence.
- Claims that high-altitude tests demonstrate practical weather control.
- Generic HAARP/ionospheric-heater claims borrowing nuclear-test effects without energy, coupling, geometry and observable accounting.

### 4 — speculative synthesis

- **Shared-medium leakage:** large interventions in shared physical media accumulate externally observable evidence even when organizational evidence is compartmented.
- **Detection debt:** each claimed intervention owes a predicted bundle of observables whose specificity and detectability should scale with physical coupling.
- **Second archive:** nature and third-party instruments can preserve evidence independently of the originating bureaucracy.
- **Meaning lag:** outsiders may measure a real effect before correctly identifying its source or purpose.

## Quantitative extraction burden

For each control/candidate case record: intervention energy; altitude/range; duration; affected volume; coupling mechanism; predicted particle/EM/acoustic/thermal/mechanical observables; sensor types; sensor distance; measurement cadence; background distribution; false-positive rate; persistence; geographic footprint; independent observers; publication delay; classification duration; personnel; dedicated platforms; and downstream technical consequences.

## Contradictions to preserve

- ARGUS was large enough to involve thousands of people and worldwide instrumentation, yet remained secret through execution. Large footprint does not equal immediate public knowledge.
- The later documentary record is incomplete despite the program being real. Archival gaps are not intrinsically suspicious enough to discriminate extraordinary from ordinary secret activity.
- A measured anomaly does not automatically identify its cause. Physical detectability and causal attribution are separate problems.
- STARFISH's consequences cannot simply be projected backward onto ARGUS: yield, altitude, particle population, satellite environment and geometry differ.

## Publication holds

Do not say ARGUS was designed primarily to destroy satellites without primary evidence. Do not call all high-altitude nuclear effects EMP. Do not equate artificial radiation belts with weather modification. Do not convert a program objective into achieved military capability. Do not cite a patent as evidence of efficacy. Do not use missing ARGUS records as proof of deliberate destruction without disposition evidence. Do not infer modern classified capabilities solely because a 1958 program was secret.

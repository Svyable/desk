## Secondary orientation only

Secondary summaries may be used to discover primary material but should not carry disputed technical claims. STARFISH satellite-loss counts in particular require mission-level primary attribution.

## Source discipline

A source proving that an objective was proposed does not prove that capability was achieved. A source proving that a physical effect occurred does not prove that a military application was practical. Later summaries must not silently overwrite contemporaneous uncertainty. Exact numerical claims move into manuscript prose only after primary-source extraction.

# Evidence Ledger

## Class 1 — verified / declassified / documented

- Pulsed RF can induce auditory sensations in humans; the microwave auditory effect has a substantial peer-reviewed literature.
- Thermoelastic generation of acoustic pressure in tissue is the accepted mechanism for classic microwave hearing.
- 2018 and 2019 JAMA papers described clinical and imaging findings in selected symptomatic U.S. personnel from Havana; the imaging authors stated clinical significance was uncertain.
- NASEM 2020 judged directed pulsed RF the most plausible mechanism among those considered for distinctive early cases, while emphasizing heterogeneity and uncertainty.
- The 2022 IC Experts Panel judged pulsed electromagnetic energy plausible for a subset of AHIs, while not addressing attribution.
- The 2023 ICA judged foreign adversary responsibility very unlikely for the reported AHI population.
- NIH 2024 studies found no consistent MRI-detectable brain injury or broad biological signature across the cohort studied.
- The January 2025 ICA preserved the majority negative attribution judgment while two components shifted, with low confidence, toward roughly even odds of foreign development/deployment of a relevant capability in a small subset.
- GAO documented care and registry shortcomings.
- House committees in 2026 publicly sought information about reported acquisition/testing of a possible AHI-related device.
- House Intelligence Committee leadership announced in June 2026 that DNI Gabbard had recalled prior AHI ICAs.

## Class 2 — credible but incomplete

- Serious reporting that the U.S. government acquired a foreign-made pulsed-RF device containing some Russian-origin components. Provenance, waveform, performance and causal relevance remain publicly unresolved.
- Serious reporting that a Norwegian government scientist built a device from classified information, self-exposed in 2024 and experienced neurological symptoms. No public protocol, dosimetry, medical record or independent replication is available.
- Investigative reporting alleging geographic/operational links between Russian intelligence personnel and some AHI cases. Public evidence remains circumstantial and incomplete.
- Historical reporting on military interest in weaponizing microwave hearing, including MEDUSA. Treat contracts/prototypes as development evidence, not operational proof.

## Class 3 — disputed / unconfirmed

- A Russian intelligence unit caused specific AHI events.
- The broad AHI population was produced by one weapon or one foreign campaign.
- The acquired 2026-reported device is the technology used in any AHI.
- The Norwegian self-experiment reproduces the mechanism responsible for classic AHIs.
- The recalled 2023/2025 assessments were deliberately manufactured or fraudulent; this is an official political/oversight allegation pending fuller documentary release.
- Patented RF-hearing communication systems were operationally deployed against targets.

## Class 4 — speculative synthesis / open conjecture

- A small early subset may have involved external pulsed energy while the larger administrative AHI population contains multiple unrelated causes.
- Some harmful exposure, if real, may have been incidental to surveillance rather than designed primarily as an antipersonnel weapon.
- Classification may have impeded scientific convergence by withholding the exact exposure regimes most relevant to laboratory testing.
- A real covert capability should eventually leave maintenance, procurement, training, testing, safety or device residue even if individual operations remain classified.

## Rule

A claim moves upward only when a new independent evidence lane appears. Repetition, official status, classification, patents, funding, testimony, or citation count do not by themselves promote a claim.

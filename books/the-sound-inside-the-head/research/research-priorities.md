# Research Priorities and Defeat Conditions

## Highest-priority work

1. Recover and audit the complete Frey/Guy/Chou/Lin experimental lineage with pulse widths, carrier frequencies, peak power, absorbed energy per pulse, subject geometry and hearing thresholds in one quantitative table.
2. Build an engineering model comparing classic thermoelastic microwave hearing with every publicly described AHI-relevant pulsed-energy mechanism. Keep auditory perception, pain, vestibular disruption, tissue injury and persistent symptoms as separate endpoints.
3. Obtain the fullest declassified technical annexes to the 2022 IC Experts Panel and determine whether its engineering conclusion relied on classic microwave hearing, a different interaction mechanism, or classified device information.
4. Reconstruct the 2023 and 2025 ICA sourcing/provenance to the extent later declassification permits. Separate collection facts from analytic inferences.
5. Track the 2026 DNI review and any replacement AHI assessment; update immediately if a declassified new assessment appears.
6. Obtain public technical characterization, if released, of the reported HSI-acquired foreign-made pulsed-RF device: waveform, power, antenna, provenance, biological testing, and relation or nonrelation to reported events.
7. Seek primary documentation of the reported Norwegian self-experiment; until then keep it Class 2.
8. Build a case-level matrix of early Havana, Guangzhou, and later priority cases with onset phenomenology, location, timing, objective medical findings, environmental measurements, and attribution evidence. Do not mix administrative reports with expert-panel core cases.
9. Reconcile Penn 2018/2019 and NIH 2024 at the participant/method level: overlap, selection, scan timing, control design, preregistration, outcome multiplicity, effect sizes, and clinical correlation.
10. Audit MEDUSA and RF-hearing patents using primary SBIR/patent files, but preserve the rule that development activity does not demonstrate operational efficacy.

## Decisive prospective experiment

Deploy synchronized wideband RF, acoustic, ultrasonic, power-quality and environmental sensors at selected high-risk sites with secure raw-data preservation and a preregistered event protocol. Pair any qualifying event with immediate vestibular/auditory testing and serial blood/physiological sampling. Separately characterize candidate pulsed-energy hardware in independent cleared laboratories, then design detectors around measured rather than imagined waveforms.

Key requirement: sensor data and clinical timestamps must be linked before investigators know whether the environmental trace supports the hypothesis.

## Defeat conditions

The book’s residual external-energy hypothesis should weaken substantially if:

- multiple future core events occur under instrumentation with no physically relevant RF/EM/acoustic/ultrasonic transient, and the sensors are demonstrated capable of detecting all proposed exposure regimes;
- independent laboratories fail to reproduce vestibular, neurological or injury effects from candidate devices at exposures compatible with covert field use, beyond the classic auditory phenomenon;
- proposed injurious effects require thermal or pressure doses that would create conspicuous collateral signatures inconsistent with reported events;
- improved medical studies establish a known disorder or combination of disorders that prospectively predicts the distinctive acute core phenotype better than external-energy models;
- continued high-quality foreign-technical collection finds no relevant capability or program residue after the 2026 device claims are fully investigated.

The hypothesis should strengthen substantially if:

- a candidate device is publicly or independently characterized and produces a distinctive acute phenotype under blinded, controlled conditions at operationally plausible exposures;
- a future event records the predicted waveform and spatial gradient at the same moment as core symptoms;
- a mechanism-specific acute biomarker appears after monitored exposure and is independently replicated;
- technical exploitation, procurement records, operator evidence, and event-time intelligence converge on the same foreign program.

## Anti-ratchet rule

Do not preserve the hypothesis by endlessly shrinking the subset after every null. Before each new dataset, state what result would count against external pulsed-energy causation and by how much.

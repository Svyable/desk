# Contradictions and Incentives

## Core contradictions to preserve

### Real auditory bioeffect vs uncertain injury mechanism
Microwave hearing is independently reproducible and mechanistically understood. That does not establish the more severe neurological endpoint alleged in AHIs. Do not let the weakness of the injury case erase the auditory effect, or the reality of the auditory effect authenticate injury.

### Mechanism plausibility vs attribution null
NASEM 2020 and the 2022 IC Experts Panel found pulsed RF/EM energy plausible for distinctive cases. The 2023 ICA judged a foreign campaign very unlikely. These are not logically incompatible because they answer different questions.

### Early imaging signal vs later NIH null
Penn 2019 reported group-level neuroimaging differences of uncertain clinical significance. NIH 2024 did not find a consistent MRI-detectable injury pattern. Cohorts/methods/timing differ; neither paper should be used to erase the other. A persistent common structural injury is less likely after the NIH work; transient/subgroup effects remain possible but need prospective predictions.

### Broad category vs narrow core subset
The administrative AHI population became large and heterogeneous. The 2022 panel’s core subset was narrower. A broad null can miss a rare subgroup; a narrow subgroup can also be selected in a way that bakes the hypothesis into the phenotype.

### 2023/2025 ICAs vs 2026 recall
The recalled ICAs remain historical records of prior analytic judgments. Recall lowers their authority as current consensus but does not invert every claim. HPSCI allegations of deliberate manipulation require documentary substantiation beyond the chairman’s statement.

### Acquired hardware vs causal relevance
Reported U.S. possession of a pulsed-RF device would materially strengthen capability concerns if verified. It does not show the device caused any AHI. Provenance, waveform and biological effect must be established separately.

## Incentives and failure modes

### Patients
Incentive: recognition, care, compensation, workplace protection, an explanation. Risk: etiological uncertainty can feel like disbelief; mechanism claims can become entwined with dignity and access to care.

### Intelligence agencies
Incentive: avoid technological surprise, protect sources/methods, preserve analytic credibility. Risk: compartmentation, confirmation bias, institutional defensiveness, politically sensitive conclusions, and inability to show the public decisive evidence.

### Scientists
Incentive: solve an unusual problem, secure research access/funding, publish novel findings. Risk: small selected cohorts, multiple comparisons, mechanism enthusiasm, disciplinary blind spots, and later overcorrection toward skepticism.

### Defense programs
Incentive: explore capabilities before adversaries do. Risk: research contracts and prototypes later being mistaken for proof of successful weapons.

### Journalists
Incentive: reveal hidden programs and official failure. Risk: source dependence, circular sourcing, dramatic fragments outrunning technical verification.

### Political actors
Incentive: protect constituents, oversee agencies, assign accountability, differentiate from predecessors. Risk: treating analytic disagreement as partisan morality; releasing conclusions before underlying evidence.

### Foreign intelligence services
Possible incentives include actual capability development, deliberate disinformation, strategic ambiguity, or allowing adversaries to waste resources on a phantom threat. No one incentive is presumed in the manuscript.

## Provenance rules

1. A government statement establishes that the government made the statement.
2. A patent establishes filing/disclosure, not demonstrated efficacy.
3. A research contract establishes funded interest, not successful deployment.
4. Testimony establishes what the witness says, not independent truth.
5. Anonymous-source reporting stays Class 2 unless independently corroborated.
6. A recalled assessment is not retroactively false in every detail.
7. A patient’s symptoms are real clinical data; causal interpretation remains separate.
8. An unexplained case is not positive evidence for the most exotic remaining hypothesis.

## Sources not treated as proof

- Patents involving RF hearing: evidence of filing/disclosure, not efficacy or deployment.
- Government research contracts: evidence of funded investigation, not operational success.
- Anonymous-source reporting: potentially important, but retained as Class 2 unless documents, named witnesses, or technical data independently confirm it.
- Congressional accusations of cover-up or manipulation: evidence of an official allegation and oversight dispute, not proof of the allegation by themselves.
- Victim testimony: evidence of reported experience; not by itself a mechanism or attribution finding.
- Foreign-program claims without declassified documentation: leads only.
